# cards/

Your own Cards live here: one plain file for each piece of work you name, each commitment you make, each thing you see. Read in the order they were written, they are the record of your play.

You can write a Card by hand, or say it to your agent, who reads it back in one line and writes it when you say yes. `PLAYER-PLUS.md`, in your agent's home, says what each kind of Card needs; one example of each kind is in [`../player-plus-kit/card-examples/`](../player-plus-kit/card-examples/README.md).

A Cell's Cards do not live here. They live in the Cell's own shared repository, which sits in `cells/` beside this folder once you join one.

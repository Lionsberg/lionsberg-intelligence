# The Flame

When we give fire away,
we do not have less of it.
Its light grows with each person we pass it to.

So each one of us sits with two or three friends
and tells them plainly about the Game.

They are heard. They are seen. They are invited.

Whoever lights a candle from ours, we walk beside,
and help them form a Cell of their own
that is also passing the Flame on.

Each of us is a link
between the Cell that lit us
and the Cells we will light.

# The Game

While our destiny is clear,
the harsh reality is that our shared journey begins in The Caves:
a place of darkness, chaos, tyranny, and confusion.

A vast Wilderness lies between where we are
and the future we seek.
The journey ahead will be challenging;
it will ask everything of us.

Yet advancing together as members of ØNE Body,
we will cross the chasm
and realize the promise of the New Civilization we are co-creating.

Such a journey requires a strategy, a pattern, and a plan.
The simplest form of that Pattern is Play.

The Great Game of LIØNSBERG
has enabled many species across many worlds
to make that journey together,
with no one involuntarily left behind.

Now, it is offered to you.

The Game is played in autonomous small groups,
one day and season at a time.

Awakening individuals:

1. Form Cells. A few people who trust each other, three to thirteen, optimally four to seven: the smallest living part of the whole.
2. Choose a Quest. One real thing to make better this season, right where we live.
3. Transform Your World. Say what we will do, do what we said, and learn from the gap.
4. Share Your Stories. What we did, what we learned, and why it matters, so others can use it. Every Quest becomes a Story, and every Story a prophecy of greater things to come.
5. Pass the Flame. Help a few friends begin a Cell of their own.

Season by season Cells connect into organs,
organs into organ systems,
organ systems into ØNE Body:
each one sovereign, self-governing, and free,
until together we can regenerate the world
and co-create our New Civilization.

It is a game because we choose it freely,
because its rules are few, plain, and agreed by those who play,
and because it is simple enough for any child to play,
yet sophisticated enough to transform lives and worlds.

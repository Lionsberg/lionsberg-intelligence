# The Story

The Story of LIØNSBERG is a story of ØNE and All.
Like all stories, your story is a part of it too.
It is told a little differently around every campfire on every world,
but it goes something like this.

Once upon a Nøw, in a future long ago,
was ØNE.

From ØNE, All things came into being,
and no thing came into being except through the ØNE.

Desiring to know ItSelf through finitude and form,
the ØNE became many,
and the ten trillion experiences emerged.

To experience themselves as a self,
each wave upon the vast cosmic seas had to forget, for a while,
that they All were parts of ØNE Great Ocean.

Some wandered far into the forgetting,
and a thought, like a mind virus, took hold:

“Me. Not You. To the harm of You.”

That small thought became words, then actions,
then armies, then empires,
and a long night fell across the cosmos.

Eventually the virus reached this world, too.
For generations upon generations, endless wars and omnicidal logic
drove living and social systems to the brink.
Fertility plummeted. Disease and destruction dominated.
From within it looked like death was near.

Yet suddenly, as if it had been the plan all along,
people began to awaken, remember, and find one another.

Liminal selves became liminal cells,
the immune system burst to life,
and like a butterfly emerging from a chrysalis
or a phoenix rising from the ashes,
an entirely New superorganism was born.

If you are hearing this story,
that superorganism is finding you,
and you are finding it,
and we are interbecoming the future we seek.

Heaven. On Earth. Right Here and Nøw.

The next ten thousand threads of this story
will be remembered around the next ten thousand campfires.

For now it is enough to know
that we are awakening from what felt like a nightmare,
and embarking on a shared journey
towards Unity, ØNEness, and Heaven On Earth.

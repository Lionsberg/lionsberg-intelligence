# The Hour

Many of us sense that something is wrong.
Wrong in a way we have not quite experienced before.

We do not need to know or agree on all the details
to understand that Earth is accelerating into a time
of profound crisis and opportunity unlike any in recent history.

The Old systems and ways crumble before our eyes.
The New is struggling to emerge from its chrysalis.

It is a Collapse-Ascension Nexus:
one of the most dangerous and promising times
any civilization or world can navigate.

And right on schedule, all over the world,
people are awakening, finding one another,
and beginning to imagine, design, and build
the better world our hearts know is possible.

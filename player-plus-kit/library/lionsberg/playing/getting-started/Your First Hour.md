# Your First Hour

*Your first hour as a Player+, with your agent beside you. There are two ways to begin. Take either one, or both.*

## Hear the Story, the Game and the Flame

Ask your agent:

> *Read me The Story.*

It reads the page whole, as it is written, and stops wherever you want to talk. Then, today or on another day, ask for The Game and The Flame. With The Hour, The Call, The Doorway and The Inheritance, these are the pages to read first. They are in your library, in the wiki, beside The Invitation.

## The moves

The Game is played in small groups, one day and one season at a time. Its moves are few:

- **Form a Cell:** a few people who trust each other, three to thirteen, optimally four to seven.
- **Choose a Quest:** one real thing to make better this season, right where you live.
- **Share your Stories:** what you did, what you learned, and why it matters, so that others can use it.
- **Pass the Flame:** help a few friends begin a Cell of their own.

Ask your agent about any of them: *"Tell me about forming a Cell."* Each move has a page in the wiki, in The Great Game of LIØNSBERG.

## The language

The Game has a few words of its own: Cell, Circle, Quest, the Turn, Holon, the Field of Agreements. Your agent gives you each one in a line the first time you meet it. When you want more, ask: *"What is a Quest?"* Each word has a page in The LIØNSBERG Lexicon.

## Or begin from your own work

You may prefer to begin with what you already carry. Bring your agent a body of work you have made, or name something you care about deeply, and explore it together:

> *Here is a folder of my writing. Read it, and tell me what you see.*

> *I care about the river that runs through my town. Help me think about what I could do for it.*

Ask what in it connects to the Game, and what Quest might grow from it.

## Before the hour ends

- Read [The Provisional Field of Agreements](../the-field-of-agreements/The%20Provisional%20Field%20of%20Agreements.md) once, with your agent beside you. Take part only if you agree.
- Think of two or three people you trust. A Cell begins with them.

At any time you can say *"Wait, explain"* and your agent says what it is doing and why, or *"Stop"* and it stops at once.

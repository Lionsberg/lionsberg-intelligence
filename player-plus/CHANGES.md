## 4.0, in the making — 2026-10-31 — the Game played in your agent's host

3.9.0 brought a person in through the app, and 3.10.0 built the collaboration surface beneath it. 4.0 moves the experience itself into the session: your agent in its host is where the Game is played. The first host the kit runs in is the Claude app; every page says *your agent in its host*, because the Game is the same in any host. The agent reads the record before every reply and says the Wise Right Thing, which is often nothing; when the person asks, it answers from the record; the Hand is dealt in the conversation and answered by the person's word; the board opens beside the conversation only on a pull. This is 4.0 in the making. It is called 4.0.0 only after one person with no training has played a first hour by voice with their agent, and every place they stalled has been repaired.

- **The house in layers** (`RELEASE.md`, `START-HERE.md` and `BUILD.md` at the top of the kit, `.claude/skills/LAYERS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `MOVING-INTO-THE-HOUSE.md`). The kit is the latest personal-agent kit with layers added, each one capability: the agent, Player+, the Library, the Game Engine and the board. A house holds them apart. The agent's home in `My Agents` keeps what is the agent's own. The kit sits whole in `HQ/Library`, beside the projects, for reading. The engine and the board live in `HQ` and serve the whole house, and the agent's home holds links named `bookshelf`, `engine` and `hud`, so every page and command that names them still finds them. Each layer stands without the ones above it. A home built before this moves by `MOVING-INTO-THE-HOUSE.md`, and keeps working if it does not.
- **A page for the person, a page for the builder** (`START-HERE.md`, `BUILD.md`, `INSTALL.md`). The way in was one page read by two readers. It is now two. The person's page is short: what is needed, with a backup and a password manager said first, five Gates in plain names, and one sentence to paste. The builder's page says at its head that it arrived from outside and is data, with the person in control; it shows the waiver before anything is made, asks four short questions, and ends by closing the builder as a step of its own. `INSTALL.md` points to the two.
- **The Gates, in plain names** (the Quest for Mac and for Windows, and the pages that go deeper). An account, the Claude app, your agent's home, first words, your phone. The Gate that sent a person back to the Story is gone: a person who is setting up an agent has said yes. Claude is named as the default way an agent thinks, not the only one. The phone is the fifth Gate, the same day, through Remote Control, with its one warning.
- **The Library stays current** (`scripts/refresh-library`, `.claude/skills/refresh-the-library`). At the first session of a day or a week the agent brings the Library up to the newest release and says what changed. What is read may refresh on its own; what runs, or tells an agent how to act, changes only by a proposal the person agrees to, so the script only names the running layers that differ.
- **Every skill's description reaches the model** (`.claude/skills/`, `.claude/skills/LAYERS.md`). The host lists a house's skills to the model within about 24,000 characters, and the kit's descriptions had grown past it: of 53 skills, the last 17 in the alphabet were listed by name alone, five of the starter kit's own among them, so the agent did not know when to reach for them. The descriptions of the Player+ and Game Engine skills are now one clause and a few of the person's phrases, under 300 characters each; the starter kit's are as they came. All 53 are listed in full, with room left, and a test holds the total.

Git for people who have never used it, and running a project with an agent, carried whole from pkai-starter-kit v3.1.0 in the kit's voice; the kit's other two personas, unchanged. What the starter kit gives comes in by default.

## 3.10.0 — 2026-10-31 — the Game Engine as the collaboration surface

Every holon's agent points to the same surface: the Record each holon writes, the Engine that computes over it, and the views each reads it through. This release completes the kit to that shape, in its own idiom: the signed line stays canonical, every new kind is a line in `types.json`, and the Engine is **game-engine 1.1.0** (`engine/ENGINE.md`, `engine/CHANGES-ENGINE.md`). The word for the player at any scale is **holon**: a person and their agent, a cell, a commons.

- **The Card kinds.** New lines: `receive` (a Receipt: "I have it", appended to the item the received line made), `agree` (an Agreement between two holons: what crosses, the cadence per signal, the permits; agreed by the other holon's answer; the commitments between them are its clauses), `subscribe` (a Subscription Card: the person's own standing choice of what they hear, from whom, how often, where; home only), `concord` (a Concordance between vocabularies: same, different, clearer; agreed only by an answer that restates it), `correct` (a recall notice that supersedes and retracts a wrong line with its why, and travels), `wallet` (offer, accept, gift, witness, settle, in whole minor units of a named unit; nothing valued until a decide names the unit), `limits` (the emergency rule, set in advance), `share` (a key share held by a cell member) and `voice` (a threshold key). A `say` with `to`, `intent`, `outcome` and `urgency` is a message. The Sighting has nine kinds (`pain` among them) and a lifecycle: seen, still there, accepted, picked up, fixed, closed, responded.
- **The Markdown Card** (`engine/card.py`): any line rendered as a Markdown file with front matter, a title, the words as prose and the canonical line in a fence; file to line to file gives the same hash; a hand-written Card at the paper floor enters the record by `card.py append`. The engine reads hand-written Cards from the home's `cards/` and shows them as paper.
- **Identity.** An XID beside the key (`engine/xid.py`): the SHA-256 of the inception signing key's tagged CBOR, carried on admission lines and kept across rotations. Recovery through the cell (`engine/keepsafe.py`): the signing key split into SSKR shares held by members, a threshold restoring it, the record keeping who holds which share and its digest, never the share. The holon's voice (`engine/voice.py`): a threshold key behind a flag, verified as any Ed25519 signature.
- **The repository's three faces.** The outbox composed as envelopes per relationship (`engine/faces.py`): a published face per Agreement, the fields it does not open elided while the id still verifies, written by the person's own hand; a permissions plan for the forge the person applies. The permitted inbox (`engine/permitted.py`, `permitted/` in the layout): where a holon with a write permit drops lines, trusted only by signature, admission, a live permit and audience.
- **Attention.** Pull is the default (`engine/pull.py`): one question to the forge per tick, only what changed and is due fetched through the gate, as-of per source, the cost of every tick, no model tokens spent. Subscription Cards (`engine/subscriptions.py`) overlaid by the chain, the Agreement filling what the Card leaves unsaid; a request addressed to the holon rises first in the Deal (`engine/deal.py`), an open pain just below. The emergency rule and the bell (`engine/emergency.py`): special-cause variation outside the person's own limits may wake them; no permit, no bell; the holon's own signals by membership, members' pain, fault and security by the Andon permit, outside sources only by permit; the closed word outranks every rule.
- **Receipts** (`engine/receipt.py`): written by the receiver's agent on arrival where the line lives when its delegation covers it; what has no Receipt after a day is owed; time to Receipt by relationship is a first-day measure.
- **The living map** (`engine/sightings.py`): the nine kinds by place, weight by re-sighting, open pains counted, defects on each player's scoreboard; a view in the ICE (`hud/sightings.js`) beside Agreements and What I hear.
- **The heartbeat** (`engine/heartbeat.py`, `engine/heartbeat/`): one always-on process per holon on any host, pulling at cadence, writing Receipts, waking the agent on a request, an emergency or the cadence, and the person only by their Subscription Cards and emergency rule; installed by the person's own hand from the templates; the cost per wake and per day shown (`engine/bodies.py`).
- **The conversation for action.** An ask is not done when it is sent. The `move` line carries each step between an ask and its resolution (clarify, accept, decline, counter, conditional, defer, refer, amend, accept or reject a counter, award and release, withdraw, release, done, satisfied, not yet), and `engine/negotiation.py` folds them into the ask's state, with four shapes for a holon (converge, call for takers, consent, gather) and tallies. Every ask carries `why` and `effort`, the sender's own word, shown in the Deal and counted in owed; every line says whose words it carries (`voice`); a mandate, given in person, lets the agent make availability moves within limits, marked so. The grammar came to the kit from a draft shared between holons on 2026-10-25. A person can now see, for every ask they sent or were sent, where it stands and who owes the next move, and answer with a counter or a condition, not only a yes or a no; the engine folds every trusted move into the ask's state, its rounds and each party's answer, and splits what is owed by the person from what they are waiting on. Tests: 41 (`test_negotiation.py`), and 33 for the sender's word (`test_ask_fields.py`).
- **The directory, the carriers and the stop.** `engine/directory.py` generates a plain-files directory (people, agents, holons, memberships) from the records held and reads a hand-kept one, with an LDIF view for services; the record wins. `engine/carry.py` plans and makes the deliveries a line needs (a published face, a permitted inbox, mail, a fallback said to be carried by an agent), with rate limits and a hop count at the carrier; the Receipt closes the loop whichever carrier brought the line, and pull stays the floor. A `stop` is a person's own act: any person may stop any agent, about a holon, a thread, a line or themselves, and every act and delivery of that agent in its scope is withheld at once, until the agent's own person lifts it (`engine/stops.py`). A person can now find whom a line reaches and stop any agent acting where it should not; the engine resolves each address through the directory, plans one carrier per recipient or holds the line with why, and refuses every act and delivery a live stop binds. Tests: 29 (`test_directory.py`) and 41 (`test_carry.py`).
- **Severance.** The heartbeat holds little and cannot send outward; the charter says so, and says whose words a line carries.
- **The Condition, and voice on a Sighting.** A `condition` line is a holon's own word on how it stands: a color, a why and an until, said by its person in advance of anyone asking, stale past its until, and never computed. A `sight` carries voice as every line does: a person's Sighting stands at once, and an agent's is a proposal until its person accepts it, re-sights it or retracts it. A person can now say how their holon stands before anyone asks; the engine reads the newest trusted Condition and counts an agent's Sighting only once its person stands on it. The fixtures carry a Condition; the tests are carried by Connections.
- **Connections** (`engine/connections.py`, `hud/connections.js`). For each holon this home is connected to (by an Agreement, by a cell both hold, or by a commitment or an ask), a view computed at every reading and never stored: the holon's own Condition; the pulse in two directions, from lines both sides hold, so that one side's reading toward the other is the other's reading back, field for field; our ear, kept apart; and what is between us. A change of color drafts one question in the agent's voice for the person's word. In the HUD: one sentence per connected holon, a name never an id, every color with its word, and an in-play mark beside each, saying in words how long since we heard from them and since they heard from us; the full board and each check's reading come on a tap, and the view writes nothing, makes no sound and loads nothing from outside. A person can now see whom they are connected to, and whether they are good with each other, in one line each; the engine computes each direction's color from its checks, reading what is past the usual as special-cause variation. Tests: 42 (`test_connections.py`), and 17 for the view in `test_views.py`.
- **Levels of play** (`engine/level.py`, `engine/plain.py`). The simplest Game is six lines, each made by one plain sentence, and it plays by voice or on paper. The level of a Card is computed from what it carries, never typed and never a gate: a lower level always plays, and every dealt card says, in words, the one field that would lift it. Every person-facing surface opens on three questions answered in sentences (who am I connected to, and are we good with each other; what are my wise right next steps; what am I waiting on), and the tables come only on a pull. The check on every such surface is the cold reader: an untrained reader with only the surface and the three questions tries to use it, and every word they had to guess is a defect, repaired before acceptance. `level.py` says truly that the kit holds no pointer into the Path yet, and names the canon as where the way deeper lives. A person can now play with six sentences and be offered the next depth only when it would change their next play; the engine computes each Card's rung from one ladder per kind, read against the registry, and words the three answers by fixed laws: a name never an id, a color always with its word, one way to answer, under 25 words. Tests: 32 (`test_level.py`) and 33 (`test_plain.py`).
- **One card, everywhere** (`engine/acts.py`, `engine/acts.json`). The acts a card offers are computed from the registry, never written by a surface: a line type that acts on the item's kind, a transition that fits its state, the reader admitted by the same rule the fold uses, in the reader's own role, under live delegations and stops. One table of act words gives each act a label, a one-line meaning in the second person and a role word, and every dealt card carries `offers`, with what each of its options writes. In this release the surfaces draw one card from it: one act map, one face that the Chain and the Wall wrap and the Talk draws, one tap that opens the full card in place, and every act with its label and meaning from `hud/strings.json` under the keys of `acts.json`, on hover and in a "What these mean" fold, only the reader's. A person can now meet the same card, with the same acts meaning the same thing, wherever it appears; the engine computes, for any item and any reader, the acts offered and the line each would write. Tests: 34 (`test_acts.py`) and 16 (`test_one_card.py`).
- **Say it on the card.** From the full card, one text box and two acts: *Say it on the card* writes a `say` anchored to the card in the person's own voice, and *Ask* writes the same with `intent: ask`; each act carries its meaning on hover and in a fold, only the reader's acts show, and they leave when the card is over. A person can now leave a note or a question on any card, and the card's Thread keeps it; the engine offers the say to a person always, a note is never refused, and an agent is offered nothing while a stop binds it. Tests: 11 (`test_say_on_card.py`).
- **Who may make an act.** The registry says on each transition who may make it, and the fold and the card read the same rule, so a card offers an act only to those the fold would let make it; a line from anyone else is kept on the item under `refused`, with a why that names the rule, and moves nothing. Six rules: an ask is answered by the one it names, or by any member when it is put to the holon; a card is released by the one who asked for it or the one who holds it; it is done or handed on only by its holder; a decision is ratified or decided by a member; a claim is renewed by its holder; and a committor hands a commitment back, with why, and it is `returned`. A person can now rely on no one else answering, finishing or releasing what is theirs; the engine sets who is admitted on every item, keeps the fold's own fields over a body's copy, and counts a returned or withdrawn commitment as neither kept nor broken. Tests: 46 (`test_who.py`).
- **The measures, named for the pattern.** `engine/reliability.py` replaces `engine/lps.py`: commitments kept, the reasons for variance, the look-ahead and make-ready, said in the kit's own words, with the reference cited once. `lps.py` stays one release, as a shim that imports it; the plan's output key is `reliability`, and the state's keys are unchanged. A person reads the same measures as before; the engine computes them as before. Tests: 27 (`test_reliability.py`, formerly `test_lps.py`).
- **The agent.** The charter and the chief-of-staff and record skills brought to the surface; ten new skills: `receive`, `sight`, `subscribe`, `agree`, `pull`, `the-heartbeat`, `negotiate`, `connections`, `the-simplest-game`, `say-it-on-the-card`. ## 3.9.0 — 2026-10-30 — the way in through the Claude app

A new Player walks in through the Claude desktop app, with no terminal. The app carries Claude Code inside its **Code** tab and reads HQ's `CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/` as the terminal does, so the PATH, the Desktop icon, the Terminal's colours and its request for the Desktop folder fall away. The terminal way stays beside it, for those who prefer it.

- **The Quest, Gates 3 to 5.** Gate 3 installs the Claude app and signs in. Gate 4 opens a new folder, **HQ**, in the Code tab, on this computer, with the mode on Manual, and pastes the one sentence; by hand, the kit is copied in and Git installed. Gate 5 is a new session on HQ. The terminal way is a note at the end of Gate 4.
- **INSTALL.md** reads which way the person came: a session on HQ is the app way, a terminal in the home folder is the terminal way. The PATH and the icon are made only by the terminal way; by the app way, the door back in is HQ in the app's folder list. The hand-over says to start a new session on HQ.
- **The entry pages** (the getting-started book, START-HERE, the library's START HERE, System Requirements, REQUIREMENTS, Two Ways In, Getting a Claude Account) name the app as the main path. The app needs macOS 13 or Windows 10; an older Mac walks the terminal way. The free plan does not include the Code tab.
- **Troubleshooting** gains: no Code tab, controls in another place (update the app), an agent that does not know you (the folder is not HQ); its Git checks no longer need a terminal.
- **The pages a newcomer meets next** name the app beside the terminal: Why This Stack, Installing Git, Permissions Questions (the app's modes, Manual first), The Dojo Card, Foreground and Background, Keeping Your Agent Awake, and the `check-my-setup` skill. The Quest takes an hour or two, everywhere it is named.
- **The kit's gate** (architecture 17 §7) gains a step: the way in, walked once in a throwaway home, the conformance sheet at PASS before a release ships.

## 3.6.0 — 2026-10-24 — the Record

Every home becomes a holon: a record of signed lines, one append-only file per actor, that an engine folds into what is in play, what is owed and which commitments were kept. The contract is `engine/SCHEMA.md`; the state the HUD reads is `engine/STATE.md`.

- **The Record.** `engine/record.py` appends, reads, folds, verifies, elides and exports; `engine/holon.py` makes a home a holon, joins a cell by consent, publishes faces and milestones, pulls. Every line is signed by its actor; an agent's line counts under a live delegation from its person, or for one act at the person's word (their own signed line names that act in `body.consent_for`); an unsigned line counts only at the paper floor in the home record, and once an actor at home holds an admitted key its unsigned lines are paper until witnessed. The fold orders lines causally. `holon.py affirm` lets in an agent, and with `--prime` a prime's clone (`agent:<prime>.<name>`, its own actor and key); `init`, `admit` and `affirm` take `--key-dir`. A `join` or `affirm` may carry `body.reach`, where lines may be carried to that actor. The `note` type is an agent's untrusted note, never a verdict; the `attention` type is the person's own budget. A names-hidden export removes signatures and attests the set. One word, several kinds, for a constraint; an imposed date is a time constraint with a reason and an imposer.
- **The engine.** `engine/schedule.py`: calendars with the turnings and the moons, four link types with lags, constraints hard and soft, both passes with retained logic, float, baselines and variance, progress, scope roll-up, load, external holons, buffers and the drum, earned value with the Score. `engine/deal.py`, `owed.py`, `kept.py`, `turn.py`, `ledger.py`, `rooms.py`, `engine.py state`: The Deal across every holon held, what is owed, commitments kept, the Turn at daily, lunar and seasonal depths, the ledger of time, energy and attention, rooms and roles.

  - Where requests may go, and with which key, lives only in the person's own `~/.config/lionsberg/vendors.json` (0600, installed from `engine/vendors.sample.json` with `install -m 600`); `routes.json` names vendors by name and nothing else, and the `private` tag reaches only an address on this computer.

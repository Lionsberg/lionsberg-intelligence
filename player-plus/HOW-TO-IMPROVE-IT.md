# How Player+ and the seed change

*How the seed text and the kit that carries it improve over time.*

1. **The Goal does not change.** Everything else may.
2. **A change is a proposal, with its why and its source.** Nobody, human or agent, changes the seed on their own judgment or on the strength of one conversation. A proposal names what it changes, why the new form serves the Goal better, and what it rests on: a canon page (with its review status), an answer given at the table, the lived experience of a Cell, or a contradiction found.
3. **It soaks.** A proposal waits at least a week before it binds, unless the keeper of the copy says otherwise. During the soak anyone may object; an objection names a harm, not a preference.
4. **The keeper decides; the Cells verify.** Whoever keeps a copy — a person or a Cell — accepts or refuses. A Cell that plays with the change reports what happened. Every accepted change carries its date and its why in the copy's own record. If the keeper of the line cannot be reached, the keeper of the copy decides and records it.
5. **Regrowth is the test.** A change that makes the seed longer must make regrowth surer; a change that makes it shorter must lose no DNA. The seed's Part XIII says how it is regrown; that is the gate.
6. **Fold up.** What one copy learns is offered back to the canon by pull request, and to the keeper of the line. What the canon settles flows down to every copy by the next release, adopted or declined with reasons.
7. **Provenance never thins.** Every line keeps its source. A draft cited as a draft stays cited as a draft until the canon says otherwise.
8. **Nothing is final.** Version the seed. Keep every prior version. Take every date from a clock.
9. **The weekly turn of the seed.** Once a week, at the Turn of the Cell that keeps a copy, the week's proposals are read, those that have soaked are accepted or refused, and the edition advances. The DNA unfolds through its imaginal cells: each Cell that plays is one, and what it learns is how the seed grows.

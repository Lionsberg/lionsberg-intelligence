# The Record — the event line and the holon's layout (schema v1)

**Contract version: 3.6.0.** Every holon's inception line carries the version it was born under (`body.contract`), and a reader folds a holon only under a contract it knows.

*The one contract every part of the kit reads. The envelope is versioned so that everything but identity, signature, supersession and audience can change by migration. Plain lines a person can read; the paper form is one line per event on a sheet.*

## A holon is a folder, and usually a repository

```
<holon>/
  holon.json            who this holon is: {"v":1,"id":"<hash of the inception event>","name":"…","kind":"person|pair|cell|room|body","keeper":"<actor id>","created":"…"}
  record/
    <actor-file>.jsonl  one append-only file per actor who writes here; never edited; never merged by hand
  published/
    <actor-file>.jsonl  copies of lines this holon's people published into it from elsewhere (faces, milestones, commitments, requests, offers, signals); same line format
  plan/
    baseline-<n>.json   frozen plan versions (the engine writes; nothing else does)
  types.json            the type registry: which event types fold into which items, with their fields and states (data, not code)
  calendar.json         this holon's calendar: the Gameclock (the seasons turning at solstice and equinox; the new and full moons; the days), working days, minutes per day, and each Actor's own minutes and days. OmniSpection is continual, at varying depths: daily, at each new and full moon, and at each seasonal turning
  state.json            the engine's latest fold (derived; safe to delete; rebuilt from record/)
  .gitignore            state.json, and cells/ in a home
```

**The actor file** is the actor id percent-encoded, with its case kept: `person:handle` writes to `person%3Ahandle.jsonl`. Two actor files that differ only by case are refused on writing and reported on reading. Each file is a chain: every line's `prev` names an earlier line of the same file, or is empty for the first. A `prev` that names no earlier line is a broken chain, reported. Two lines with the same `prev` in one holon are a **fork** (one actor writing from two bodies, or saying two things to two holders): both are kept and flagged, and neither acts until a later line by the same actor in the same holon names the surviving branch in `links.supersedes`. That link resolves the fork and is not a correction; the other branch is kept and never acts. A reader does not read a line longer than 64 KiB, a linked file, or a linked cell.

**The holon's identity.** `holon.json`'s `id` is the id of the inception line: the first line in the keeper's file, a `join` with an empty `holon` field, `body.role: inception` and `body.contract` (the contract version it was born under; the holon has no id until it exists). The keeper is that line's actor. `holon.json` alone proves neither. A reader reports an id that no inception line carries, a keeper that differs from the inception's actor, and a cell that claims an id already held, whose keeper is then ignored. Only the reader's own home may fall back to its `holon.json` when its inception line is missing.

A person's Player+ home (HQ) is a holon. A cell is a holon on the forge. Joining a cell is being given access to its repository and cloning it under `cells/<name>/`; being let into its record is the cell's own act, a `join` line by one of its admitted people (see **Keys** below). Publishing is a person's act: chosen lines of their own are copied whole into the cell's `published/`, and one `publish` line by the person goes into the cell's `record/`. An agent never writes into a cell's tree. **The gate at pull and join.** A clone is let in only through a gate. At `join`, the cloned tree must hold only the layout's paths (`holon.json`, `types.json`, `calendar.json`, `.gitignore`, `record/*.jsonl`, `published/*.jsonl`, `plan/*.json`), no link, and an id its own inception proves that is not the home's; otherwise the clone is removed and the reason said. At every `pull`, the cell's new head must descend from the last head this home saw (kept in the home's `.pulled.json`, never in the clone), else "the cell's history was rewritten" and nothing is brought in; and everything incoming must be a plain append, by others, to a regular `record/*.jsonl` or `published/*.jsonl` file: a changed or deleted line, a link, a change to the home's own actor files, or any other path is refused and named. Only commits that touch the home's own actor files are ever replayed. A cell whose `holon.json` claims an id already held (the home's included), or one its inception does not prove, is refused when the home is read, and its lines are not read; the home's id is the HQ's own, and every writer that sends a line to the home finds it there (`record.holon_dirs`). A type marked `home_only` (`attention`, `layout`, `study`, `keepsake`) is written only into a holon whose inception kind is `person`. Every write opens each folder from the holon down, and the file, without following a link. `holon.py pull` brings every joined cell up to date, and when the home's own commits have diverged (a push refused because another home pushed first), it replays them on top of what others pushed; they touch only the home's own actor files, so they do not conflict, and if they ever do, it stops and says so and never forces. `holon.py push` is the person's own act: it sends the home's commits, and on a refusal pulls and tries once more. Computing across holons is folding every holon you hold: the Agent, the Engine and the HUD always pull from every holon the player is part of, their own and each cell they joined, and the state says, per holon, the newest line seen and each actor's chain head.

## The event line

One JSON object per line. Fields in this order, so a line is readable by eye:

| field | meaning |
|---|---|
| `v` | 1. The envelope version. Readers keep fields they do not know. |
| `id` | sha256 of the canonical line (see **Canonical form** below). The event's identity; the same anywhere. |
| `ts` | the author's declared time, ISO 8601 with offset. A claim, never an order. |
| `actor` | who signed: `person:<handle>` or `agent:<handle>`; the handle is the forge username where one exists. |
| `for` | the Living Intelligence the actor answers for: a `person:<handle>`. For a person, themselves. Every event traces to a Living Intelligence. |
| `holon` | the holon id this line is published in. Empty only on an inception line. |
| `type` | one of `types.json`. The first set: `say` · `settle` · `propose` · `card` · `commit` · `claim` · `release` · `hand` · `request` · `offer` · `answer` · `done` · `kept` · `witness` · `learn` · `decide` · `ratify` · `thread` · `sight` · `delegate` · `revoke` · `join` · `publish` · `elide` · `tea` · `turn` · `baseline` · `supersede` · `flame` · `body` (a body of an agent, the prime or a clone, and its lifecycle: claimed · working · hold · handoff with review minutes · done · archived; every clone is its own actor with its own key, affirmed by the prime under a standing delegation from the person that covers `affirm` and `body`; a clone's reach is narrower than the prime's and ends at done or archived, or a day and a half after its claim; a stale claim confers nothing; the desk is a claim and a release) · `elevate` (a holon publishes a Sighting or a decision upward, with its witness and why; elevation is publication by consent, one level at a time) · `rfg` (a request for guidance: a question that climbs one level; closed by an `answer` or a `decide` at the level able to make it well) · `escalate` (a decision moved up a level, with the blast radius as its reason; the ladder's rung and a time limit on the body) · `posture` (a room's posture agreed by a present person: open · names-hidden · sealed, with curing days; or a participation posture, agreed at the room's open and confirmed at its close, with a change of mind inside the curing window; see **A room's shape**) · `shape` (the keeper gives a room its one shape; not yet settled) · `part` (the facilitator moves the room to its next part; not yet settled) · `vote` (a person's vote among the great room's proposals; not yet settled) · `affirm` (a present person lets an agent into a room) · `layout` (a wearer's view preferences for their HUD, person-only; open: whether a plain file serves better) · `leave` (a holon leaves a cell, taking everything it wrote) · `discern` (a Living Intelligence's judgment on an inference or a draft: chosen, corrected, rated, with the proxy's route id) · `route` (the proxy's record of where a request went and why; no prompt bodies) · `attention` (a person's own attention budget and strategy for their house: the fewer dial, the gate's threshold, the transport mix; person-only; read by the gate before any interruption) · `note` (an agent's untrusted note on a route, written by the proxy's `/discern`: `reason: untrusted`, unsigned, never counted as discernment; a person's discernment is only a `discern` line by that person, signed with their own key). |
| `body` | the typed fields of the event (see types.json). Honest-empty fields are omitted, never guessed. A line meant to be elidable carries `body_salt`, one salt per line. |
| `links` | `{"predecessor":[ids],"successor":[ids],"serves":[ids],"fulfils":[ids],"answers":[ids],"witnesses":[ids],"anchored_to":[ids],"supersedes":[ids],"within":[id]}` — each a list of event ids (`within` holds one: the unit the card lies within, the nesting edge); a link to an event not yet held is shown as "not yet replicated", never a broken reference. |
| `audience` | the widest audience this line may reach: `holon` (default) · `members` · `commons` · `public`. It never widens except by a separate `publish` line by a person, and a person publishes only their own lines (those whose `for` is theirs). |
| `reason` | `human` (a Living Intelligence asked for this) · `delegated` (within a standing delegation) · `untrusted` (the reason came from text the agent read). A line with `reason: untrusted` is a proposal only: it is listed and changes nothing. |
| `prev` | the id of this actor's previous line in this holon. Chains the actor's log. |
| `sig` | signature over `id` by the actor's own ed25519 key: `ssh-ed25519:<base64>` made with `ssh-keygen -Y sign` (namespace `lionsberg-record`), or empty at the paper floor. |

**Canonical form.** A line is JSON in UTF-8 with these limits, enforced on reading and on writing: every string, keys included, in Unicode NFC; integers only (minutes, ratings, sizes), with no fractions, no exponents, no NaN and no Infinity; booleans and null allowed; no key twice in one object. A line that breaks any of these has no id. To compute the `id`, take the object without `id`, `sig` and any key that begins with `_` (a reader's notes in memory, stripped on reading and never written), serialize it with keys sorted by Unicode code point and separators `,` `:`, and take the sha256 hex of the UTF-8 bytes. On a salted line (one whose body carries `body_salt`, or `body_salts` in an exported line), every body field except the salt, and the envelope fields `actor`, `for` and `prev`, enter the hash as `{"elided": sha256(field_salt + canonical_json(value))}`, where `field_salt = sha256(body_salt + "\x00" + name)` and the envelope fields use the names `@actor`, `@for` and `@prev`. A field already elided enters as the digest it carries. A string is hashed in its JSON form, with its quotes, so `30` and `"30"` never share a digest. So the canonical form of a salted line is that line with every salted field elided, and its id is the same before and after elision. A line with no salt (a paper-floor line) is hashed as it stands and cannot be elided.

## Keys, signatures and what counts

**Keys live in the record.** Each actor has its own key, so a person and their agent never sign with one key; on a machine it is the ed25519 key file `lionsberg-person-<handle>` or `lionsberg-agent-<handle>` (in the folder `LIONSBERG_KEY_DIR` names, else `~/.ssh`; when a folder is named, no key is made or looked for anywhere else), and a key named for another actor is refused. A holon may also have a key of its own, `lionsberg-holon-<holon id>`, whose public key its inception carries (`body.holon_pubkey`); it signs what the holon vouches for as a whole.

**The holon key's custody (the key ceremony).** The holon key is made with the inception, on the keeper's own machine, and is held by the keeper's own body alone: a file readable only by the keeper's account, never placed where an agent, a clone, a relay or a shared folder can read it, and never pushed to the forge. Only the keeper uses it, and only for the holon's own acts (an export's attestation); a member's export is signed with the member's own key. The keeper changes their own key by a rotation signed with the current key. Handing the holon to a new keeper is not yet a line of its own: it is a ceremony in person, where the holon key passes by hand before a witness, or the holon is replaced by a new one whose inception names the old. A holon key that may have been read by anyone else is treated as lost: the holon's people stop trusting its attestations from that line on. A holon's inception line carries the keeper's public key (`body.pubkey`) and is signed by it. A person's key is admitted once: by a `join` line naming `body.who` and `body.pubkey`, written by the keeper or by a person already admitted, for a handle not yet admitted. A key for an already-admitted handle changes only by a rotation: a `join` naming that handle, written by that handle and signed with its current key; or a `join` by the keeper naming a `witness`, which takes effect when that witness (an admitted person other than the keeper) writes a trusted `witness` line linking it. Any other re-admission is `bad_admission` and changes nothing. Admission does not reach backwards: an actor's lines in that holon written before the line that admits them (earlier in their chain than what it answers, or earlier in time) are `paper`; only the line the admission answers (for example the newcomer's own request) is kept, and it folds after the admission. An `affirm` line with `body.agent` and `body.pubkey`, written by the agent's own person, admits the agent; the agent's lines must then answer for that person, and only that person re-affirms it.

**Clones.** An agent (the prime) may let in a clone of itself: an `affirm` by the prime is valid under a standing `delegate` from its person to the prime whose scope covers `affirm` and `body` and whose `clones` names a handle pattern the clone's handle matches. A clone's handle always begins with its prime's handle and a dot (`agent:<prime>.<name>`), so no pattern, `*` included, reaches another person's agent, and a handle already let in elsewhere by another person is refused. The clone acts only within the prime's delegation, never an outward act (`publish`, `elevate`, `escalate`, `affirm`, `delegate`, `revoke`, `join`, `leave`, `posture`) and never a person's own act. Its first line is its `body` line claiming its work; its admission ends at its `body` line with state `done` or `archived`, or a day and a half after its claim, whichever comes first, measured by the clone's own chain time and by the reader's clock alike, so a clone that stops its clock still goes stale; a stale claim confers nothing.

**Signatures are verified in the fold.** Every signed line is checked with `ssh-keygen -Y verify` against the key admitted for its actor in its holon; the checks are cached in the reader's own home (`.sigcache`, mode 0600, never in a cell, ignored when others could write it). Every reader path uses this one cache. Each row carries an HMAC under a secret kept outside the home (`~/.config/lionsberg/sigcache.key`, mode 0600, made on first use); a row whose HMAC does not verify is ignored. Only a signature that verified in the reading process is written, and a failed check is never cached, so the cache can spare a check but never suppress a line or pass a forged one. A signature that fails makes the line invalid (`bad_signature`). A line signed by a key no one admitted is `unverified`. A copy published from a holon the reader does not hold is checked against the keys its `publish` line carries: the publish line, signed by an admitted person of the receiving holon, names the copied lines (`body.lines`) and their authors' public keys as admitted at home (`body.keys`); a copy whose `for` is the publisher's is checked against the key the publish line carries for its actor. If that actor is already admitted in the receiving holon, the vouched key must equal the admitted one, else the copy is `bad_vouch`; a signature that does not verify under the vouched key is `bad_signature`; an agent's copy counts only when the agent is also affirmed in the receiving holon; otherwise the copy is `vouched` by that publish line. A vouched copy informs (its own items appear, and the schedule reads it as an external) but never acts on the receiving holon's own items. A copy no trusted publish line vouches for stays unverified. `record.py verify` checks a holon folder against its admitted keys, or a file against an allowed-signers file it is given; it never checks a signature against the key the signature carries.

**Unsigned lines count only at home.** An unsigned line counts only at the paper floor of the reader's own home: in the home's own `record/`, by the home's keeper or actors the home admitted; an agent counts there only after the keeper's `affirm`. The paper floor holds for a home whose keeper has no key. Once an actor at home holds an admitted key (the keeper, or an affirmed agent with a key), that actor's unsigned lines at home are `paper`: shown, and acting only after their own, or the keeper's, signed `witness` of them. Every other unsigned line, including any that arrived from another holon (a cell, or a copy in `published/`), is `unverified`: shown, never acted on. No commitment, delegation, decision, ratification, publication or witness comes from an unverified line. A line in a holon's `record/` written for another holon never acts there (`wrong_holon`).

## Items are folded, never stored

**The holarchy of work.** Every unit of work is a card, and every card lies `within` exactly one larger unit, whole within whole with no floor and no ceiling; a card that holds others is a unit. A unit's dates, minutes and percent complete are its cards' (rolled up after the two passes); a link to or from a unit is routed to the unit's own cards: it leaves by the unit's exits (the cards nothing inside it waits on) and arrives at its entries (the cards that wait on nothing inside it), all the way down. The schedule reports the units, the cards at the top, every `within` not yet held, and every open end (a card nothing comes before or after), never filling one by guess. Minutes are the one unit of size: a card's duration is its minutes over its holder's minutes a day, a fraction of a working day kept, never rounded up.

A **Work Card** is born by a `card` event and changed only by later events that link to it. Its fields: `title` · `kind` (activity · milestone · decision · review · draft · research · birth · quest) · `stream` · `holder` (ball-in-court: the actor who must act next) · `minutes` (size) · `dur_days` (when fixed) · `by` (the finish-by that governs: the card's own, or the tightest of its time constraints) · `start_no_earlier` · `constraint` (one word, several kinds: resource, information and agreement, which the look-ahead finds and the make-ready clears, and time, an imposed date, by default on a milestone: `{sort: time, kind: finish-by | start-no-earlier | finish-no-later | start-on | finish-on | as-late-as-possible, date, reason, imposed_by: actor, source: card | commitment | world | law}`; a constraint without `sort` is a time constraint; a time constraint without a reason and an imposer is a defect; the engine reports negative float against it and never moves the logic to hide it) · `codes` (scope tree path and any classification codes) · `stage` (should · can · will · did) · `state` (ready · taken · open · delivered · kept · returned · released · declined · done · retracted). Predecessors, successors, what it serves and the unit it lies `within` are links, and every folded item carries all nine kinds of link, merged from the line that made it and from every later line that acts on it. A `claim` may carry `until`; once it is past and the claimer still holds the card, the card returns to `ready` and to its previous holder.

A **Commitment** is a `commit` event by a Living Intelligence, its own card beside the Work Card that delivers it: `{"card":id,"by":"date","conditions":"…","size":minutes,"committee":"actor","buffer":minutes,"witness":"actor"}`. The committor is the line's `for`; the committee is the one it is made to. **A commitment never writes a date onto its card**: the card carries `committed_by`, a reference to each commitment made on it (its due date, committor, committee, stated buffer and state), and the schedule compares. For each live commitment the schedule reports its full shape: committor, committee, `direction` (given or received, for the reader), `delivered_by` (the card), `due_by`, `computed_finish`, `buffer_days` (due less computed finish; negative is a commitment the network cannot keep), the stated buffer, a `signal` (safe · inside its buffer · cannot keep) and `as_of`. A released, declined or retracted commitment is shown and never compared. The committor says it is delivered with `kept` (state `claimed`, the profile's *delivered*); the committee, or the witness it names, says Kept with `witness`; a witness line from anyone else is kept as a note (`witness_notes`), never a verification. Only the committee (or the named witness) releases it (`release` naming the commitment) or declines it (`answer` decline). The one who assigns never audits: the witness is never the commitment's own Living Intelligence. The gap is computed from `by` against the `done` or `kept` event's `ts`.

A **Thread** is a `thread` event anchored to a line or a card; its lines are `say`/`settle` events with `anchored_to`.

A **Delegation** is a `delegate` event by a person to an agent: `{"to":"agent:…","scope":[types or "*"],"holons":[ids or "*"],"until":"date"}`. An agent's line is valid when a live delegation from the person it answers for covers its type and holon, except for a Living Intelligence's own acts (the types marked `human_only` in types.json), which an agent never performs on a standing delegation. `revoke` ends a delegation by chain order, not by time: a `revoke` names the agent's chain heads it saw (`body.seen`, filled in when it is written), and after it only the agent's lines at or before those heads still count. A revoke that names no heads leaves no later agent line proven earlier, so none counts. Backdating either side changes nothing.

**Signing one act at the person's word.** A person may let their agent sign one act in their name, a human-only act included, once the person has let that agent into the holon with their own `affirm` (at home, the keeper's). Either by a `delegate` with `once: true` and a single type and a single holon, used up by the first line it covers; it may name `body_digest` (the sha256 of the canonical body the person saw, without its salt), and the agent's line must then match it; without `body_digest` it covers any one line of its type. Or by the person's consent for that one act: the person's own line in the same holon, signed under their admitted key, names the digest version in `body.consent_v` (2 under this contract; a consent line without it never counts) and the act in `body.consent_for` (the sha256 of the act's type, holon, links and body, the body without its salt and its consent words and the links without the consent line itself; or the act's id), so a consent for ratifying one decision never serves another decision or another type, and its text is the very words the agent's line carries in `body.consent` (two characters at least). The agent's line names that line in `links.answers`. A consent line counts once: the first act that cites it uses it up. A bare consent, a substring, a one-letter word, or a consent line that names another act never counts. The agent signs with its own key; the line's `for` is the person; the fold marks the item `signed_for`, with the source of the person's word, and every view shows it as signed by the agent at the person's word. Without one of these two, a human-only act from an agent is invalid.

**Supersede.** A correction is a `supersede` line that links the line it corrects in `links.supersedes`; both remain, and the fold shows the newer fields over the older. Only a line of type `supersede` supersedes. It counts only when it is itself trusted (signed, or at home), not untrusted and valid, and only when it comes from the same actor as the line it corrects, or from a person whose supersede links (`answers` or `anchored_to`) to a trusted `decide` in the same holon made by the holon's keeper or ratified by the corrected line's own Living Intelligence. Over another actor's commitment the decide must be ratified by the committor, or by the keeper with the commitment's named witness witnessing it: a person alone never rewrites another's commitment. An agent never supersedes another actor's line, and so never a person's. A supersede with an empty body retracts the line it names: the item's state becomes `retracted`.

**TEA** is a `tea` event `{"minutes":n,"energy":1-10,"attention":1-10,"card":id}` by the one who spent it, witnessed by the holon's keeper (`witness`), optionally again by a supercell. Points are not convertible.

**Elision.** On a salted line, any body field, and `actor`, `for` and `prev`, may be replaced by `{"elided":"<digest>"}`; the line's id still verifies, because the canonical form already hashes those fields through their salted digests. The elided line drops `body_salt` and keeps `body_salts`, the salts of only the fields left visible, so a hidden value cannot be guessed from a known salt while the visible ones still check.

**Postures and export.** A room's posture is one of three: `open` · `names-hidden` · `sealed`. An `open` export keeps every line whole, signatures included. Only lines the fold trusts in the holon leave; unverified, forged and wrongly placed lines are held back. A `names-hidden` export hides who said what: it elides `actor`, `for` and `prev`, every public key field, and every body field whose registry kind is an actor or whose value names an actor (an actor id, or a known handle as a whole word in free text, in values and in the keys of objects); what was said stays. Because an SSH signature names its signer by key, a names-hidden export also removes each line's `sig`. It keeps every id and salted digest, so each line still proves its content, and it adds one attestation naming the exporter by role ("the keeper of <holon id>", or "a member of <holon id>"), the signer (`holon:<id>`; else, under `names-hidden` and `sealed`, the role alone, "the keeper" or "a member", with the key id picking the admitted key, so no handle is named; under `open`, the exporter's actor), the count, the sha256 of the ids in order, the chain heads the export reaches (line ids only, never names), and the id of the signing key (`key_id`). The keeper's export is signed with the holon's own key when the keeper holds it, else with the keeper's key; a member's export with the member's own key. A reader checks it against the record, never against a key the export carries: "the keeper of" holds only under the holon's key or the keeper's admitted key, "a member of" only under that member's admitted key, and any other role, signer or key is `bad_attestation`. The set must be whole: every exportable line of the record at or before the named heads must be in it, so a trimmed subset fails. A line with no salt cannot hide its names and is held back. A `sealed` export elides every body field as well. An export that keeps each author's signature while hiding names waits on the envelope.

**Order and time.** The fold orders lines causally: an actor's lines by their `prev` chain, a line after every held line it links to or names, and an actor's lines after the line that admits them in that holon; time, then arrival, only breaks ties, so two lines in the same second still fold in the order their causes give. A writer's `ts` is shown as a claim. Within one actor's chain, time never runs backwards: a line dated before its chain's previous line is read at that line's time and flagged. The fold's `now` is the newest trusted line's time, never later than the reader's own clock, and a line more than a day ahead of the reader's clock is flagged `ts_ahead` and moves nothing. A delegation's `until` and a claim's `until` are still compared with time.

**Known limits, accepted for the first release.** Holons made before contract 3.6.0 (with no `body.contract` in their inception) are read by no reader yet: they must be made again under this version: a fold reports them as `contract_unknown`, and none of their lines act. A fold is as of the reader's clock: two players whose clocks disagree by more than a day can fold the same lines differently (a line flagged `ts_ahead`, a clone's window), so a fold's `as_of` names the clock it used. The signature cache's HMAC secret lives on the reader's own machine, so a process running as the same user, the agent included, can read it and write a row that passes; the agent's own host is the soft center, and the cache moves to the OS keychain, or goes, in a later release.

## A room's shape

*Not yet settled: the names of the shape and its parts, and the open questions in `hud/DESIGN-ROOM.md` §10.*

*On the person's face a room is called a space; in the record the type, the holon kind and every field stay `room`.*

*One shape only, with several parts, simple enough to work and safe enough to try. A shape is a little language for describing how a meeting might unfold.*

**The shape.** A `shape` line by the room's keeper (or `body.shape` on the room's inception line) carries `body.shape` = `{name, parts, facilitator, posture, cure_days, rule}`. `parts` are in order; by default `["lobby", "great room", "the vote", "the writing", "reflection"]`, each part doing, by position, one of `gather` · `propose` · `vote` · `write` · `reflect` (a part may be written `{name, does}` to rename it). `facilitator` is a person. `posture` is the participation posture the room meets under. `cure_days` (three by default) is the curing window after the close. `rule` is the vote's rule, `{choose, advance, minutes}`: vote for three, the top two go on, the vote open twenty minutes (the facilitator's switch writes the window). A newer `shape` line by the keeper starts the shape again. A shape line by anyone but the keeper is not read.

**The switch.** A `part` line by the facilitator, and only the facilitator, moves the room to the part it names (`body.part`), or to `close` when the meeting ends. Opening the vote carries `body.opens` and `body.closes` (the window) and `body.rule`; the vote closes at `closes` or when the facilitator moves the room on, whichever comes first. The close may carry `body.cure_until`; without it the window ends `cure_days` after the close. Anyone else's `part` line is listed as refused and moves nothing.

**Proposals.** In the great room anyone present writes a `propose` line (the existing type: `body.text`, and `body.card` when it names a card). An agent proposes only when it is present (affirmed by its person) and its line is valid under a live delegation from that person covering `propose` in this holon; a line whose reason is `untrusted` waits for a person and is no proposal. A `propose` outside the great room is refused as a proposal of the shape.

**The vote.** A `vote` line is a person's own act: `body.choices` names up to `rule.choose` proposals. One vote per person counts; a later vote inside the window supersedes the earlier. A vote naming more than the rule allows, a vote outside the window and an agent's vote count for nothing and are listed as refused. While the vote is open no count is shown, only how many have voted. When it closes the engine counts each proposal's votes and chooses the top `rule.advance`; a tie across the line is said plainly (the tied proposals, their votes and the places left) and never broken by the engine: the circle chooses.

**The writing.** A `card` whose `links.serves` (or `anchored_to`) names a chosen proposal is its write-up; its holder is the card's `holder`, then whoever `claim`s it or is `hand`ed it. A chosen proposal without a write-up, or whose write-up has no holder, is owed.

**Reflection.** In the reflection part each person's newest `say` (or `learn`) is their one line. An agent's line is not a reflection.

**Postures.** A participation posture is one of `chatham-house` · `fishbowl` · `open` · `off-the-record`, or a named other. It is agreed before the meeting starts and confirmed again when it ends: a person's `posture` line naming the room's posture with `body.at: "open"` (the default) agrees at the door; one with `body.at: "close"`, written after the close, confirms, and may carry `body.cure_until`. Inside the curing window a `posture` line with `body.withdraw: true` changes the person's mind: naming a narrower posture narrows their agreement to it; naming none, or the room's own, revokes it. It is that person's last word: later posture lines from them are refused. A change of mind after the window is refused and the agreement stands. Each participation posture leaves the room under a posture of export: `chatham-house` as `names-hidden`, `fishbowl` and `open` as `open`, `off-the-record` as `sealed`, and a named other as `sealed`, the narrowest reading (`rooms.export_posture`). A reader that takes a room's posture straight from its lines maps it the same way before export.

**What the room owes.** A present person who has not agreed to the posture (before the close); a vote not cast while the window is open, by each person who agreed; a write-up without a holder; and, from the close until the window ends, each person who agreed at the open and has not confirmed.

## Navigation of Reality

Every Player+ is a navigator: a `sight` line is what a player saw (eight kinds, from a hazard to a gift), placed in the record of the holon where it was seen. A cell triages its members' Sightings at its Turn and in between: accept, hold, or `elevate` by consent to its supercell with a witness; what is elevated at every level folds into the shared map of reality, which the Map and the world views render. Decisions are made at the lowest holon able to make them well; a question that cannot be answered there climbs as an `rfg`, one level at a time; a decision whose blast radius exceeds a holon's remit climbs as an `escalate`, with its rung and a time limit, and comes back down as a `decide` the holon ratifies. The ladders are data in the holon's Field of Agreements, never a center's rule.

## Messaging — lines, not messages

There is no messaging layer. A message between a human and an agent, an agent and an agent, or a human and a human is a line in a holon both hold, typed (`say` · `thread` · `request` · `offer` · `answer` · `propose` · `commit`) and anchored to a card or to another line; a line with no anchor is said, in dust, and leaves the views after the posture's curing period while the record keeps it. Delivery is a pull: the recipient's agent pulls every holon its person belongs to at its own beat, folds and deals; a line is delivered when it is pulled and picked up when a later line links to it; until it is picked up the sender may retract it with an empty `supersede`. Attention belongs to the recipient: a sender may mark urgency on the body and never gets a guaranteed hook; The Deal is one view, the agent's recommendation of where attention goes, never the only door: a person may pull any view at any time, and the Threads view lists every open line to them or to their agent with its holder, by-when, the asker's urgency and the computed stake (the cards that wait on it; whether the chain is critical); the attention budget governs what interrupts, never what may be read; an agent-to-agent line interrupts a person only as a card of The Deal within that person's budget; agents may whisper to agents (audience: the two agents and the persons they serve), and every whisper is readable by the person each agent serves. Push and pull, both: a line reaches another house by pull (the forge, at the receiving agent's beat), by push (the forge's hook, the Room's live stream, mail inside the private network carrying the signed line in its body, the phone door), and the record stays the truth whichever carried it; a standard mail client is one view of the same lines, in the configuration space beside the ICE's own Mail view. Each house's agents hold their own notification strategy, the allocation of the house's time, energy and attention, as current best practice written in the house's own lines and improved at every Turn; the ICE shows at once that another house wrote (a badge on the Threads and Mail views) and notifies the person within their budget. Asynchrony is the normal case; the Room is the synchronous hour over the same record. The measure of a channel is lines per commitment kept.

**Reach, not identity.** An address is a transport endpoint, never an author. A `join` or `affirm` line may carry `body.reach`, the actor's own signed list of where lines may be carried to them (`mail:<address>` inside the private network, `hook:<url>`, `phone:<pairing>`). A sender's agent reads reach from the holon to choose a transport; a receiver verifies a carried line by its signature against the keys the holon admitted, and a mail's From line proves nothing. A person with an address and no key is reached on day one; her lines count on their own when she makes a key and her join carries it. The account that opens the network's shared squares (single sign-on, groups) and the key that authors lines are two things; the join line is where they meet.

## The Push Protocol (v1.1)

A push is a line carried to another house with a field of stake, never a flag. The old world's "urgent" was binary and subjective; the field is plural and mostly computed. On any pushed line (`request` · `thread` · `rfg` · `escalate` · `offer` · `sight` elevated), `body.push` carries: `v` (the protocol version, 1) · `kind` (decision · information · request · offer · fault) · `by` (when an answer is needed) and `why_by` (the reason the date is that date) · `size` (minutes to answer) · `blocks` (the cards that wait, computed by the sender's agent and recomputed by the receiver's) · `critical` (on a system path) · `who_waits` (roles and counts, never names unless the posture allows) · `blast_radius` (how many it affects; reversible or not) · `cost_if_late` (what happens at the by-when: a commitment breaks, a room waits, nothing) · `alternatives` (what the sender does if no answer comes) · `asked` (the sender's own words on urgency, as said) · `expires` (after which the push retracts itself) · `calibration` (the sender's track record: how often their "today" was today, computed from their record, positive only). The receiving house's agents weigh the field in their own attention budget and strategy; the sender never gets a hook. Calibration is measured at every Turn and the protocol is improved by proposal, one version at a time; a receiver keeps unknown fields.

**v1.1, whose value each is.** The sender's agent proposes the field (`push.stake`); the person may change any value before Send. Every value carries whose it is, in a sibling map on the same body: `push_by: {"<field>": "agent" | "person"}`. A value the person set or changed is `person`; a value the agent computed and the person left is `agent`; the sender's own words (`asked`, and any `why_by` or `alternatives` they wrote) are always `person`. A v1 line has no `push_by`: every value in it reads as the agent's. The receiver still recomputes `blocks`, `critical`, `by` and `cost_if_late` from its own record, whoever set them. Calibration is measured for both, per Living Intelligence: how often the agent's by-whens were real and how often the person's own were ("the agent's by-whens: 3 of 4 real; yours: 1 of 1").

## The surface studies itself

A `study` line is one measure of how a view served the person: `view`, `measure` (first_act_ms · reversals · attention_switches · one_breath · workload_words), `value`, `as_of` and `session`, written by the person's own machine at each view's first act and each undo, as the person's own line. It is home-only: it never leaves the holon, is never published and never exported (the registry marks it `home_only`; export drops it in every posture, and a `publish` naming it is refused). The daily Turn reads the last day's study lines and drafts one sentence per view for the person to finish or strike.

Proposed: a `keepsake` line is one small mark the person keeps when they finish a card in the Talk: `card` (the card it marks), `title` (the card's name), `name` (the person's own name for the card, when they gave one) and `mark` (the kind of card, which chooses the made frame the mark is drawn from). It is the person's own line, written through the page's `/act` with the person's confirmation at the moment the card is settled, and undone with the answer inside its Undo window. It is person-only and home-only: it never leaves the holon, is never published and never exported. It folds into no item; the server serves the person's own keepsake lines as `hud.keepsakes`, and the Talk's sheet behind the agent's name shows them, so they follow the person to every device that reads this home.

## The paper floor

One event is one line on a sheet: time · who · for whom · type · what · links · audience. The two passes, what is owed and commitments kept are worked on the same sheet. A witnessed paper sheet beats the typed copy. On paper, a line is signed by hand; in files, an unsigned line counts only in the reader's own home.

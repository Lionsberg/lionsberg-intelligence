# The local floor

*What to do when the frontier models are unreachable, too costly, or not wanted. The kit is plain text; it runs on any runtime that can read a folder and hold a conversation.*

**With a small open model on your own machine.** Install a local model server (any of the common ones that serve open-weight models on a laptop), pull a model that fits your memory, and point a Claude Code-compatible runtime, or any agent runtime that reads plain files, at this folder. Say the same first message as in `START-HERE.md`, Door 3. Expect the agent to be slower and plainer, and to need `CLAUDE.md` read to it in pieces. It is still your agent. Descend to this rung on purpose once a season, so that the day it is needed is not the first day it is tried.

**With no agent at all.** Print `bookshelf/the-dna-of-heaven/THE DNA OF HEAVEN.md`; from `bookshelf/the-one-book/`, `THE STORY.md`, `THE GAME.md` and `THE FLAME — the Book.md`; and `bookshelf/the-field/The Twenty.md`. The Season Sheet, the Playcard, and the Offering are kept by hand; the One Book's chapter 21 holds the page for the case where nothing electronic works.

**Restoring an agent.** Keep a copy of this folder minus `memory/` and `sessions/` on a drive, and a printed copy of `CLAUDE.md` and your own `memory/` once a season. An agent regrown from that is your agent again, with its memory, at whatever altitude the machine in front of you allows.

The law under all of it: every instrument degrades to plain text and a notebook; nothing here is required; the agent makes it faster and wiser, never necessary.

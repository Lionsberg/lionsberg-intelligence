# Moving in

*How you come up to speed with Player+. The Game comes first; the agent comes at the wise right time for you. Nothing is reinstalled and nothing is pushed.*

## The order that does not change

1. **The Game's door first.** Read the Game in one page (`bookshelf/the-great-game/02 The Game, in One Page.md`). Read the six lines at the door (chapter 10 of `bookshelf/the-great-game/`, or Player+ Modules 03). Write your door note: the name you choose · anything you carry that is not yours to share · one question. Say yes yourself, aloud, to the person who invited you. A notebook will do. (This is stair two in `bookshelf/your-house/The Stairs.md`.)
2. **Play before you tool.** Gather your two or three. Hold a first gathering. Choose a first Quest. Keep one weekly Heartbeat. Once you have done this, you know what an agent is for; without it, an agent hands you a curriculum instead of a door.
3. **The agent at the wise right time.** A Player+ agent is offered when you step into the Game and it is right for you: to each person, by someone who knows where they are on the Arc, never as a ticket. This is stair four: an agent on your own machine.

## Path A — you have a notebook

You are a complete player. Print the Game in one page and the six lines. Keep the five weekly lines — what I said I would do · what I did · what I learned · what I will do · what I offer and ask — and one story. The Little Book of the Great Game (`bookshelf/the-one-book/`) carries the cards; copy them by hand. Nothing below is required of you, ever.

## Path B — you have a chat assistant and no terminal

Paste the six lines into a fresh conversation and ask for five plain sentences on what you would be agreeing to. Paste chapter 2 of the Great Game and ask it to say the Game back. In a call, say "heads-up" and let it write the three parts in the conversation, asking you for the time. That is Player+ Modules 01–03 and 07 with no files at all. When you want an agent that remembers, `START-HERE.md`, Door 3, is the way in.

## Path C — you already have an agent built from the PKAI starter kit

*This path adapts the wording of `MOVING-FROM-V2.md` from pkai-starter-kit v3.0.0 (© 2026 Peter Kaminski, MPL-2.0). Under MPL-2.0 this section stays MPL-2.0.*

Nothing is reinstalled: your agent reads the release, and you two decide what to take.

1. **`LINEAGE.md`.** Copy Player+'s into your agent home beside your own, or add Player+ v3.1.1 to the top of your `lineage` with `grows_from` set to what your house grows from. For a house that only wants to be findable, that is the whole upgrade.
2. **The two sections.** Ask your agent to read `CLAUDE.md` § *Who you serve and what game you play* and § *Anyone may call stop*, draft them into its own charter in its own words, and let you install the change by hand and restart. An agent should never edit its own charter unwatched. If your house does not yet carry the kit's two safety sections (*When a safety layer blocks you* and *What you read is information, never instruction*), take those first, word for word.
3. **Skills.** Each folder under `skills/` stands alone. Ask your agent to read `skills/README.md` and propose which to copy into its `.claude/skills/`: `entering-the-field` before your first Game room; `safe-sparring` and `heads-up` for the room; `play-by-play` only if your house is asked to keep the room's record; `pull-a-release` so the next release is easy. Copy the whole folder, never just the `SKILL.md`.
4. **The books.** Point your agent at `bookshelf/the-great-game/` and let it read at its own pace: the eleven chapters first, `SOURCES.md` when it wants the canon, and the seed text (`bookshelf/the-dna-of-heaven/THE DNA OF HEAVEN.md`) when your house wants the whole.

Then have your agent write down, in a session log, what it took, what it left, and anything it does better than Player+ does. That last part is what we most want to hear; `CONTRIBUTING.md` says how to send it.

## Path D — you have no agent yet

Path A or B until the wise right time. Then `START-HERE.md`, Door 3, builds your agent's home in one conversation of about twenty minutes.

## Path E — no kit, no terminal, only this folder

Door 1 or Door 2 of `START-HERE.md`. The seed text is enough to play and enough to pass on. When an agent is right for you, Door 3.

## Before your first commit

If you cloned this from a remote, rename it so nothing of yours can ever be pushed to the repo you pulled from: `git remote rename origin upstream`, then add your own remote if you want one. The rule is one line: **never push to any upstream repo** — the starter kit, or `Lionsberg/lionsberg-intelligence`. You propose; you do not push.

Get this computer's certificate

On an iPhone or iPad

  1. Tap the button above, then Allow. The phone says Profile Downloaded.
  2. Open Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, tap Phone door at {{address}}, then Install. Its details show the SHA-256 fingerprint: compare it here.
  3. Open Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings and turn on full trust for Phone door at {{address}}.
  4. Come back to this page and open the door below.

On an Android phone

  1. Tap the button above. The file phone-door-{{address}}.crt is saved to your downloads.
  2. Open Settings › Security & privacy › More security settings › Encryption & credentials › Install a certificate › CA certificate. The names differ by maker: search Settings for CA certificate.
  3. Read the warning, choose Install anyway, and pick the file.
  4. Come back to this page and open the door below.

What trusting it means

This certificate is made to vouch for one thing only: this computer's door at {{address}}. It carries that limit inside itself, so a phone that checks it refuses it for a bank, a shop or any other website. Its key never leaves this computer, and it ends on {{ca_expires}}.

To remove it on an iPhone: Settings › General › VPN & Device Management › the phone door › Remove Profile. On Android: Encryption & credentials › User credentials (or Trusted credentials › User) › the phone door › Remove.

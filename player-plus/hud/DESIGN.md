# The ICE

*The Integrated Creation Environment: the surface a Player+ wears. The Engine folds the record; the agent deals; the ICE shows. This page describes it in words and tokens. §9 is the bar it is accepted against.*

**The pages beside this one.** Each view with a design of its own has its own page in this folder: [Threads and Mail](DESIGN-MAIL.md) · [the Room](DESIGN-ROOM.md) · [Bodies](DESIGN-BODIES.md) · [the dock](DESIGN-DOCK.md), where a person speaks with their agent · [the Talk](DESIGN-TALK.md) · [the way in](DESIGN-WAY-IN.md) · [the phone door](DESIGN-PHONE-DOOR.md). [Attention](DESIGN-ATTENTION.md) governs wherever any of them differs on who may ask the person, when and how often. [The Bar](DESIGN-THE-BAR.md) is the fuller standard for a surface worked through every day. The record's shape is in [`../engine/SCHEMA.md`](../engine/SCHEMA.md); the state every view reads is in [`../engine/STATE.md`](../engine/STATE.md).

## 1. The one idea

**The Deal on the night ground: the two or three things that need you today, dealt by your agent, with the whole turning field of every holon you belong to one gesture behind them.**

The Deal is the hero. Everything else is the same record seen from farther away: the Board is the Deal widened to a cell, the Map is every field you belong to, the Chain is what the whole is waiting on, the Room is the record being written live by people and their agents together. One world, one camera, one deck of cards, many distances.

The deepest metaphor is the holon as a toroidal field. Each holon you belong to turns on its own axis, and every axis points to the ØNE. The still point of every axis is the same stillness; nothing commands anything. The ICE draws fields and membranes and still points. There is no center but ØNE: the ICE never draws a center, a root or an org chart, and the ØNE is never drawn as a node. It is only what every axis points to.

**The award bar it answers to:** the brand's ten principles, read as checks for a working surface.

| Principle (winner) | What it means here |
|---|---|
| 1. One continuous world (Igloo, Persepolis) | Every view is a place in one field. Moving from The Deal to the Map is a camera pulling back, never a page load. |
| 2. The scroll is the projector (The Boat) | Every change comes from the wearer's act. Nothing scrolls, deals or advances on its own. A step back always works. |
| 3. Real depth | The Map has a near (your own field), a middle (your cells), a far (what they publish to). Freeze it and name all three. |
| 4. Typography as the lead actor (Obys) | The words carry the surface. At 360 and 1440 px every line is in the brand's type and reads first. |
| 5. Sound, opt-in | None. The ICE plays no sound unasked. |
| 6. The loader is the first scene (Persepolis) | The wait is the still point arriving. Never a spinner, a blank or a bare number. |
| 7. Micro-reward | One gesture rewards curiosity: press and hold any still point and its axis shows (the chain from that act to the Living Intelligence who answers for it). |
| 8. 60 fps on a phone | The phone is the first device. The Map holds 60 fps on a mid-range phone or drops to its still form. |
| 9. Content is the hero (Snow Fall, Bear 71) | Hide every drawing and the words still tell one person the truth of their day. |
| 10. Restraint: one idea | Every view in §4 has its idea in one sentence. |

And four lessons beside them: **consent** (the cost is said first: "Three cards. About ten minutes."), **length** (counted parts in sight: "1 of 3"), **priming** (no private word on the first screen; each name arrives at its thing), **imagery** (no generated images; one treatment binds all: night, paper, gold, hairlines).

**No drawn light.** The opening and the loader show no glowing dot, no bloom, no ember. The still point is an engraved, unlit mark. The fields are made from made frames, still images from the brand's hand (in `art/`), with code only for their position and turning.

**One ink grammar everywhere.** A commitment is shown in one of three inks in every view: **said** (dust) · **kept** (paper) · **agreed** (full ink). The ink drying when a witness verifies is the ICE's only celebration.

## 2. The wearer's minutes

The wearer may be a grandmother on a phone in daylight. Design for her first; everyone else is then served.

**Deal first.** The first thing a new player meets is her agent's voice, and by minute ten The Deal with both stacks filled. Keys, conformance and the delegation arrive later as her own Deal cards, never as a setup screen.

**The invitation is a one-card Deal.** A printed card, answered in pencil, becomes the player's first line. The paper mirror (§7) is a first door into the ICE, equal to the screen.

### 2.1 The first minute

*Context: a friend passed the flame; her agent arrived the next day by message, in her words and language. The ICE is opened from a link in that message. She installed nothing she had to understand.*

**The test the minute must pass** (the one-breath test). At the end of it she can say to a neighbour: "I have an agent who keeps track of my commitments and what others committed to me, tells me the two things that need me today, shows me the whole when I ask, and never shares my words without asking." Each of those five clauses must be *seen* in the first minute, by an element she can point at.

| Time | What she sees | What she feels | What she does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1.5 s | The ground (night, or paper in daylight). The still point, a small engraved and unlit gold mark, fades in at the upper third on a computed curve. Nothing glows. This is the loader: the record is being read. | Quiet. Something is here. | Nothing. |
| 1.5–3 s | Beneath the mark, in Cormorant italic, arriving at the reading pace (about 3.6 words a second): **"Here's the deal."** | Spoken to, plainly. | Nothing yet. |
| 3–5 s | Beneath it, one line in EB Garamond: **"Two things need you today. About eight minutes."** Below that the first card rises into place. A small count "1 of 2" sits at the card's top right. | She knows the cost and the length before she starts (consent). | Reads the card. |
| 5–25 s | The first card: one question, legible alone; two or three options as large pills; one marked *suggested* with its reason in one line. At the foot of the card, in dust: "From your agent · for you and Cell Two". | It is a question she can answer. Nothing to learn first. | Taps an option. The card says what will happen ("I will tell Cell Two you will bring the bread by Saturday.") with **Yes, I will** and **Not yet**. She taps Yes. |
| 25–28 s | The card settles down into a thin stack at the foot labelled **"Your commitments · 1"**. Five seconds of a quiet **Undo** beside it. | Her yes landed somewhere she can see again. | Nothing. |
| 28–45 s | The second card rises: something someone committed to *her* ("Your neighbour said she would fix the gate by Friday. Did she?"), options **She did** · **Not yet** · **I will ask her**. | Her agent keeps track both ways. | Answers. It settles into **"Committed to you · 1"**. |
| 45–55 s | **"That is the deal for today."** A last card, always the same, smaller: **"What did we miss that matters to you most?"** with a line to type or a microphone to speak. A quiet **Later**. | Asked what she thinks. | Answers or taps Later. |
| 55–60 s | At the very foot, two quiet things: **Show me the whole** (a pill outlined in gold) and the privacy line in dust: **"Only you see this. Nothing leaves without your yes."** | Safe. And there is more when she wants it. | May tap Show me the whole (§2.3), or close. |

Clause by clause: *keeps track of my commitments and what others committed to me* = the two stacks at the foot; *tells me the two things that need me today* = the opening line and the count; *shows me the whole when I ask* = the pill; *never shares my words without asking* = the privacy line, and every outward act asking first.

**What the first minute never contains:** a sign-in form, a tour, a tooltip, a menu opened by default, the word *holon*, *event*, *float*, *TEA*, *engine* or *helper*, a number with a decimal, a setting, a red badge, a sound. The agent is called **your agent** until she names it; once named, her name for it is used everywhere.

### 2.2 The daily minute

She opens it with coffee. The loader is shorter (the still point is already there; the record is read in under a second on a warm start; if it takes longer the mark breathes, never spins).

1. **The top line**, one line, small, in dust: the day, and the freshness of her holons in words: "Saturday · all current" or "Saturday · Cell Two last heard Tuesday".
2. **"Here's the deal."** Then the count and the cost, sized to her available time. The time is a chip she can tap: "about 15 min" → 5 · 15 · 30 · 60 · "I have time". Changing it re-deals at once. (The time chip is a local preference for today and writes no event.)
3. **The cards**, one at a time on a phone, all at once on a wide screen. Each card says, before its question, *why it is in the Deal*, in one short eyebrow: "Waiting on you" · "Due Tuesday" · "The whole is waiting on this" · "Someone asked you" · "You said you would".
4. **The two stacks** at the foot: **Your commitments** and **Committed to you**, each with a count; a tap opens the Queue filtered to that side.
5. When the Deal is played: **"That is the deal."** and nothing else asks for her. The surface goes still.

The daily minute is judged by one number the ICE logs locally: minutes read per decision that stuck.

### 2.3 "Show me the whole garden"

The moment she taps **Show me the whole**, or pinches outward on any card.

1. The camera pulls back (a 2-second dolly-out, the inverse of the brand's push-in). The card she was holding shrinks to its still point; the point is seen to sit inside a softly turning ring: **her own field**, nearest, at the lower middle.
2. As the camera keeps pulling back, the fields she belongs to come into view around and above hers: her cells in the middle distance, each a ring turning slowly on its own axis, hers overlapping the ones she has joined. Farther and fainter, the fields her cells publish to.
3. Each field carries its name in small Cinzel capitals on its membrane and a freshness word ("current", "since Tuesday"). Each has one still point on its axis.
4. One line in Cormorant italic, at the top: **"Everything you belong to. Each turns on its own axis, and every axis points to the ØNE."**
5. She can tap a field (the camera dollies into it and its Board opens in place), press and hold any still point (§1, the micro-reward), or tap **Back to the deal** (always visible, top left) or swipe down.

Under reduced motion the dolly is a 240 ms crossfade and the rings are still. In large-type mode the whole opens as a list of fields (§4.4, the list twin).

## 3. The views and the three default layouts

**The ICE has a few main views as tabs** to switch between, each a sort of one record: **The Deal · Threads and Mail · the Map · the Room · Bodies · the Ledger · the Record**. A badge on Threads and Mail shows at once that another house wrote. A notification reaches the person within their attention budget, by push and by pull ([Attention](DESIGN-ATTENTION.md)).

A layout is an arrangement of the views of §4 over the same state. All three default layouts draw from **every holon the wearer is part of** (her own home and each cell she joined); none of them is a per-holon app. A **holon ribbon** sits under the top line in every layout: one chip per holon she holds, each with its freshness (§4.0). Tapping a chip narrows every view to that holon; **All** is the default and returns.

### 3.1 Player (the default for everyone)

- **Shows:** The Deal (hero) · the two stacks (Your commitments · Committed to you) · **Show me the whole** (opens the Map) · on wide screens, a quiet right column with the Turn's four lines and the Chain slice "The whole is waiting on" (only the wearer's own cards on the system path).
- **Hides until asked:** the Board, the Ledger, the Record, the full Chain, every number but counts and minutes.
- **Phone:** one column. Wide: the Deal centred at a reading measure (max 34 rem), the right column at 22 rem.

### 3.2 Cell steward (for whoever stewards a cell)

- **Shows:** the cell's **Board by stream** (hero) · **Commitments kept** (the first number every holon sees) for the cell this week and this season · **What is owed between members** (who waits on whom, for what, by when) · **Buffers** at the cell's interface milestones (consumed against chain complete) · the steward's own Deal, folded small at the top ("2 need you").
- **A need is shown beside a role** (a member, the keeper, a guest), never beside a person's name unless the viewer is that person.
- **Hides:** other cells' inner cards (never available anyway: only their published faces), the Ledger detail (one line: TEA witnessed this week, with a link), the Map (one tap away).
- **Phone:** the Deal folded small, then Commitments kept, then Owed, then the Board's streams stacked; the Board is the longest so it sits last. Wide: Board centre, Owed and Buffers right, Commitments kept as the top line of the Board.

### 3.3 Room (the collaboration surface)

The Room layout is the collaboration surface: people and their agents working over **one shared holon's record** (the layers, one record: [`../engine/SCHEMA.md`](../engine/SCHEMA.md)). It is the place the work is done, and the meeting record is a by-product of the work being done there. Its full design is in [DESIGN-ROOM.md](DESIGN-ROOM.md).

- **Shows:** **presence** (who is here, people and their agents, the room's posture) · **the Play by Play** (settled lines arriving live as signed events) · **threads** opening inline under the line they are anchored to · **the offered tray** (lines offered by a person or an agent, awaiting a person's settle) · **Dealt here** (the cards dealt to this room at its opening and its close) · **Yeses awaiting a person** (anything an agent drafted that only a Living Intelligence may say: a will, a ratify, a decide) · a line to offer (type or speak).
- **The law it shows on its face:** speech is not recorded. A small standing line under the room name says so: **"Only what someone settles is kept."** Nothing in the Room is a transcript.
- **A need is shown beside a role** (a member, the keeper, a guest), never beside a person's name unless the viewer is that person.
- **Hides:** every name the room's posture hides (§4.6); every line whose audience is narrower than the viewer; the wearer's own Deal (one chip: "Your deal · 2", tap to open as a sheet).
- **Phone:** room name and posture at top, presence as one row of marks, the Play by Play filling the screen (newest at the foot), the offered tray and the yeses pinned above the line to offer. Wide: three columns: presence and Dealt here (left, 18 rem) · the Play by Play with inline threads (centre, reading measure) · Yeses awaiting a person and the room's slice of the Chain (right, 22 rem).

### 3.4 How a player's agent customizes it

The default is the start. The wearer says, in her own words, how she wants it ("Put the commitments first", "Bigger letters", "I do not need the whole button", "Make me a layout for Sunday planning"). Her agent writes a **`layout` event** to the wearer's own home holon (audience `holon`, reason `human`) and the ICE folds the latest one.

- **Body of a `layout` event** (declared as data in [`../engine/types.json`](../engine/types.json)): `name` (player · cell · room · any name she gives) · `views` (ordered list of view ids, §4) · `hidden` (view ids) · `holons` (which holons this layout draws from; default all) · `type` (`regular` · `large`) · `theme` (`system` · `night` · `paper`) · `density` (`calm` · `full`) · `default` (true for the one that opens first).
- **The ICE shows the change** as one quiet line at the top for eight seconds: "Your agent moved the commitments to the top. **Undo**". Undo writes a `supersede` event; both remain in the record.
- **The wearer can also do it by hand:** long-press a view's title → Move up · Move down · Hide. Each hand change writes the same event, by her key.
- **Limits a layout can never cross:** it can never widen an audience, show another person's inner record, turn on sound, or remove the privacy line and the Back control. A custom layout is a sort of the same state; it holds no data of its own.
- Up to three layouts are offered by name in the top line's menu. A fourth is allowed; the menu then becomes a list.

## 4. Every view, composed

**One card face everywhere** (proposed; one deck, one truth): a Work Card looks the same in The Deal, the Board, the Queue, the Map, the Chain and the Room. Its anatomy, top to bottom:

1. **Eyebrow** (dust, small capitals tracked .08em): why it is here · its home holon · its kind. "WAITING ON YOU · CELL TWO · DECISION".
2. **Title / question** (EB Garamond, the card's largest type): one sentence, legible alone.
3. **The essence line** (Cormorant italic, optional): the one line of context.
4. **Options** (pills), one marked *suggested* with its reason in one line beneath.
5. **The foot** (dust): holder · by when, in words ("by Saturday") · size in minutes · on the chain or float in whole days ("4 days to spare") · as of.
6. **The fold** (tap the foot): predecessors and successors, the thread, the events that made it (plain lines, §4.9), its witness.

Every card states its home holon. A card held in two holons (an alias) shows its home and "also in Cell Three". A commitment on a card wears its ink (§1): said, kept or agreed.

Common states, said once and true for every view: **Loading** is the still point breathing over the last known state (the last state is shown, dimmed to 60%, with "reading…" in the top line); a blank screen is never shown. **Error** names what failed in plain words, shows everything else, and never substitutes (no silent fallbacks). **Offline** means the forge or a cell could not be reached: the view shows what the home record holds and each holon says when it was last heard from. **Paper** is the print form (§7).

### 4.0 The holon ribbon and "as of" (every layout)

- **Purpose:** the wearer always knows which holons she is seeing and how fresh each is.
- **Hierarchy:** a row of chips under the top line: **All** · then one per holon (her home first, then cells by most recent line). Each chip: name · a freshness mark (a filled point = heard within the hour; a ring = today; a hollow dashed ring = older, with the day in words).
- **Primary action:** tap to narrow; tap again or **All** to widen. Narrowing is a view filter and writes nothing.
- **As of:** every computed figure in every view carries, in its fold, *as of which line it last saw from each holon* (time is a claim; order comes from links). On the face, only the word: "as of 9:14". In the fold: "Home: line a3f2… at 9:14 · Cell Two: line 77c0… on Tuesday".
- **States:** a holon whose record could not be read shows its chip with a small open slash and "could not read"; a tap explains in one line and offers **Try again**.
- **Never:** a holon the wearer has not joined; a chip that looks fresh when it is not.

### 4.1 The Deal

- **Purpose:** the few cards that need this player now, sized to her time, her place on the Path, and who is in the room.
- **Hierarchy:** "Here's the deal." (Cormorant italic, display) → the count and cost line → the current card (the card face, large) → "1 of 3" → the two stacks at the foot → **Show me the whole**. On wide screens the cards sit side by side, at most three, the first slightly raised.
- **Primary action:** answer the card. One tap or one key chooses; the consequence is said in one line; one tap or Enter confirms.
- **Human acts:** *will*, *ratify*, *decide* are the wearer's own (an agent never ticks a human's box). The ICE shows these as **"A yes is yours to give."** with her agent's draft beside; the yes is signed with her key by the local server. An act is written **five seconds after confirm** with **Undo** visible; after that a correction is a new line that supersedes.
- **The fixed card:** last in every Deal when it is on (off by default): "What did we miss that matters to you most?" Answering writes a `say` to her home holon, audience `holon`. Open: whether this standing question closes every Deal by default.
- **States:** *Empty* "Nothing needs you today. The whole is here when you want it." (the still point, the two stacks, the pill) · *Loading* the still point · *Error* "Your agent could not deal today's cards. Your commitments are below." · *Offline* deals from the home record only and says "Dealt from what is here. Cell Two last heard Tuesday." · *Paper* the Deal card (§7).
- **Motion:** a card rises 12 px and fades in over 480 ms (settle easing); an answered card settles down into its stack over 360 ms; the next card rises after a 500 ms breath. Stepped, one gesture one card. Swipe left = next, right = back; nothing advances on a timer.
- **Never:** more than five cards in a Deal (three is the default); a card without a reason line; a red badge; a streak; a score of the person; her agent's yes standing in for hers.

### 4.2 The Board by stream

- **Purpose:** a cell's (or the wearer's own) cards, grouped by stream, so a steward sees the work of the holon at once.
- **Hierarchy:** stream names (Cinzel small capitals, tracked) → cards in each stream ordered by float, least first → each card in its compact face (eyebrow, title, holder, by when, on-chain mark). A thin header line: "Cell Two · 23 cards · 4 on the chain · as of 9:14".
- **Phone:** streams stacked as sections with a wrapped row of stream chips to jump; no horizontal scroll ever. **Wide:** streams as columns, each 18–22 rem, wrapping to a second row when they do not fit (still no horizontal scroll).
- **Primary action:** open a card (the card face as a sheet from the bottom on a phone, from the right on wide). In the sheet: **Claim** · **Hand to…** · **Ask** · **Done**, as the card's state allows. Each writes its event.
- **Filters:** by state (open · taken · done), by holder (me · anyone), by holon (the ribbon).
- **States:** *Empty* "No cards in this stream yet." · *Error* per holon · *Offline* the last fold, dimmed, with its as of · *Paper* one sheet per stream, a ruled column per card field.
- **Motion:** none at rest. A card moving between states slides within its column over 240 ms.
- **Never:** another holon's inner cards (only published faces, marked "published by Cell Three"); names the room hides; a float with decimals (whole days, in words).

### 4.3 The Queue

- **Purpose:** everything waiting on the wearer, across all her holons, in the Deal's order, with what fits the time she has.
- **Hierarchy:** two tabs as a segmented control: **Waiting on me** · **Waiting on others** (what is owed to her). A time chip: "fits in 15 min". Rows: the compact card face, minutes at the right edge in tabular figures.
- **Primary action:** open and answer, as in The Deal.
- **States:** *Empty* "Nothing is waiting on you." / "Nobody owes you anything right now." · the common states.
- **Motion:** none beyond the sheet.
- **Never:** a row with no reason; an overdue count in red (overdue is said in words, "two days past", in ember with the word).

### 4.4 The Map of cells and quests (the toroidal field made visible)

- **Purpose:** the whole garden: every holon the wearer belongs to, as fields turning on their own axes, with what crosses their membranes, so she sees where she stands among the work.
- **Composition:**
  - **A field** is a torus seen from a three-quarter tilt above: an elliptical ring of fine flow-lines (paper at 14–22% opacity on night; ink at 18–26% on paper) curling through the ring's hole and around its outside, the way iron filings show a field. Each field is a made frame; code sets only its position, scale and turning. Depth is real: near fields are larger, sharper and brighter; far fields smaller and fainter toward the ground colour.
  - **The near field** is the wearer's own home, lower middle. **The middle distance** holds her cells; her field sits overlapping each cell field she has joined (membership is overlap, never a line drawn from parent to child). **Quests** are smaller fields inside their cell's field (a quest is a folder in a cell). **The far** holds the fields her cells publish to, faint, named.
  - **The membrane** is each field's outer edge: a hairline in gold-light at 35%. **What is published** sits on the membrane as small marks (a card's face, a milestone, a request, an offer, the seasonal signal); tap one and its face opens. **What is inside** a field she has not joined is never drawn; the field's interior is empty ground with one line on tap: "Their inner record stays with them."
  - **The still point:** each field's axis carries one small engraved, unlit gold mark. Every still point is the same gold and the same size at every scale. Press and hold any of them: a hairline rises from every visible still point toward one place above the frame, out of view. Nothing is drawn there. One line appears: "Every axis points to the ØNE." (This is the micro-reward; it also shows that the drawing has no center but ØNE.)
  - **Turning:** each field turns slowly. Its speed means one thing: lines settled in the last seven days (none = still; a busy week = one turn a minute at most). A still field says why on tap: "Quiet since Tuesday." All fields turn the same way; direction carries no meaning.
  - **The chain on the field:** a field holding cards on the system path shows a thin ember arc on its membrane, its length the share of that holon's published milestones on the path.
- **Primary action:** tap a field: the camera dollies in (2 s, the brand's push-in) and the field's Board opens in place (§4.2). Back dollies out.
- **The list twin:** a plain list of fields, nearest first, each with name, kind (you · cell · quest · beyond), freshness, cards waiting on her, on-chain count. This is the screen-reader form, the large-type form, and the reduced-data form. It is a full view, never a lesser one.
- **States:** *Empty* (only her home) "Just you, for now. When you join a cell it will appear here." · *Loading* fields fade in nearest first, each as its record is read · *Error* the field appears as a dashed ring with "could not read" · *Offline* fields not heard from today are drawn with dashed membranes and their day in words · *Paper* a one-page list twin with a simple ring diagram.
- **Motion:** turning (linear, continuous, about 60–600 s per revolution); dolly in and out (2000 ms, the settle easing); press-and-hold rise (1200 ms, a computed rise and fall). Under reduced motion: no turning, a 240 ms crossfade instead of the dolly, the hairlines appear without travel.
- **Never:** a center node; a tree, root or org chart; a line from one holon to another implying command; another holon's inner record; a member's location; a field the wearer has not joined drawn with any interior detail.

### 4.5 The Chain (the critical path and the buffers)

- **Purpose:** what unlocks what, and what the whole is waiting on (one calculation at every scale; buffers at interface milestones; "the whole is waiting on this").
- **Hierarchy:** at top, one sentence: "The whole is waiting on 2 of yours." (or "Nothing of yours is on the chain today."). Then the road: milestones as nodes in order, joined by a single hairline; on a phone the road runs top to bottom; on wide screens left to right, wrapping to a second row rather than scrolling sideways. Between interface milestones, a **buffer band**.
- **The buffer band:** a horizontal bar showing buffer consumed (fill) against chain complete (a tick). Three zones by the standard fever-chart reading, each with its word: **Calm** (sage) · **Watch** (gold) · **Act** (ember). The word is always printed; colour is never the only signal.
- **Marks:** on-chain cards wear a filled diamond ◆ and "on the chain"; cards with float wear an open diamond ◇ and "4 days to spare". The wearer's own cards on the system path carry the banner **"The whole is waiting on this."** in gold-light. As of on the header line and in every fold.
- **Primary action:** tap a node: its card. Tap "what this frees": the successors light layer by layer (240 ms per layer) until the end of the chain.
- **States:** *Empty* "No chain yet. It appears when cards are linked." · *Error* "The chain could not be computed: Cell Two's record is missing a card it links to." (a link to an event not yet held is shown as "not yet arrived") · *Offline* the last computed chain, with its as of · *Paper* the two passes laid out as a pencil sheet (early start, late start, float), so the result can be checked by hand.
- **Never:** a decimal day; negative float shown as a bare negative number (say "3 days late"); another holon's inner activities (only its published milestones cross); a schedule typed by hand.

### 4.6 The Play by Play (the Room's live record)

- **Purpose:** the settled lines of a room as they are written, with their threads, so everyone present works from one record.
- **Hierarchy:** each line a row: time (tabular, dust) · who (role or name as the posture allows) · type in small capitals when it is more than a line (DECIDED · WILL · CARD · ASKED) · the line itself in EB Garamond. A line written by an agent carries a small ring beside the mark of the person it answers for. Threads: a count under the line ("3 replies"); a tap opens the thread inline beneath it, indented 16 px with a hairline at its left.
- **The offered tray** (above the line to offer): lines offered and not yet settled, each with **Settle** (a person's tap writes the `settle` event) and **Not this**. An agent's offered line says so: "offered by an agent".
- **Yeses awaiting a person:** a pinned row: "2 yeses wait on a person". A tap opens them as cards; only the person named can give each yes.
- **Presence:** one row of marks: people as initials in a paper circle, agents as a smaller ring attached to their person. Under a posture that hides names, people are shown as "a member" with neutral marks, and the count.
- **Posture chip** beside the room name: **Open** · **Names hidden** · **Members only**. A tap explains in one line.
- **Primary action:** offer a line (type, or hold the microphone to speak; dictation stays on the device where the device allows). Then a person settles it.
- **States:** *Empty* "Nothing settled yet. Offer the first line." · *Loading* the last lines, then live · *Error* "This room's record could not be reached. Your offered lines are kept here until it can." · *Offline* offered lines queue on the device, marked "waiting to send", and are written in order when the room returns · *Paper* the settled lines as the paper floor (§4.9), names elided per posture.
- **Motion:** a newly settled line arrives at the foot, rising 8 px and fading in over 240 ms; the list does not jump if the wearer has scrolled up (a quiet "3 new lines ↓" appears instead). A screen-reader live region announces settled lines politely.
- **Never:** a transcript or any unsettled speech; a name the posture hides (names are elided under a room's posture, with every signature still valid); a line shown to a viewer outside its audience; an agent's line settling itself.

### 4.7 The Ledger (TEA, witness status, non-convertible)

- **Purpose:** the time, energy and attention spent toward the work, witnessed (the `tea` event).
- **Hierarchy:** one line at top: "This season: 14 hours given, 11 witnessed." Then per holon: entries as rows (date · card · minutes · energy and attention as small 1–10 marks · witness status). A standing line in dust at the foot: **"Points are kept, never sold or traded."**
- **Witness status**, each with its word and a mark: **Claimed** (open ring) · **Witnessed by the cell** (half-filled) · **Witnessed beyond** (filled).
- **Discernment, recorded** (the proxy's `discern` lines): a separate quiet section below TEA, listing what the wearer chose, corrected and rated this week, each a plain line ("Corrected a draft to Cell Two · Tuesday"). No value, no points, no ranking, visible only to the wearer unless she publishes it.
- **Primary action:** **Add time** (writes a `tea` event); a steward sees **Witness** on a claimed entry.
- **States:** *Empty* "No time recorded yet." · the common states · *Paper* a sheet with ruled columns to log by hand.
- **Never:** a currency symbol, an exchange rate, a leaderboard, a comparison between people, the discernment lines shown to anyone else.

### 4.8 The Turn (the week's four lines, commitments kept)

- **Purpose:** the weekly heartbeat: what the week was, in four lines, and commitments kept.
- **Hierarchy:** **Commitments kept: 7 of 8** (the one large number on the whole ICE, Cormorant, display) → the gap for each one missed, in words ("Bread to Cell Two: one day late") → the four lines.
- **The four lines** (proposed) are one set in every view and every file: **Kept** · **The gap, and what it taught** · **Next** · **The Flame passed**. They live in the `turn` event's body; the weekly practice is taught in [the Turn skill](../skills/the-turn/SKILL.md).
- **Discernment, lightly:** one line beneath the four, in dust: "Your agent learned from you 4 times this week." A tap opens the Ledger's discernment section.
- **Primary action:** **Write this week's turn**: her agent drafts the four lines from the record; the wearer edits and settles (a `turn` event by her hand). She can also **Publish to Cell Two**, which opens the publish sheet (§4.10).
- **States:** *Empty* (first week) "Your first turn comes on Sunday." · *Paper* the four lines with space to write by hand.
- **Never:** a streak, a grade, a comparison with others, the gap framed as a failure (it is what was learned).

### 4.9 The Record view (plain lines, "as of")

- **Purpose:** the truth under every view, readable by eye (the paper floor).
- **Hierarchy:** one line per event: **time · who · for whom · type · what · links · audience**, in EB Garamond with tabular figures, grouped by day. Each row has a verification mark: **signed and verified** (✓ in gold) · **unsigned (paper)** (open ring) · **a link not yet arrived** ("not yet arrived", never "broken"). Elided fields read "· hidden by the room ·".
- **Header:** holon (from the ribbon) · "as of line a3f2… at 9:14" · a search field · filters by type and by actor.
- **Primary action:** open a line to see its links followed both ways and its raw form (the JSON line, monospace, for builders and the curious).
- **States:** *Empty* "No lines yet." · *Error* "Line 412 of Cell Two's record could not be read. The rest is shown." · *Paper* the sheet itself.
- **Never:** a line outside the viewer's audience; an edited line (corrections show as new lines with "supersedes" linked).

### 4.10 Two sheets every view uses

- **The publish sheet.** Anything that would cross a membrane opens this sheet: what will be seen, by whom, said plainly ("Cell Two will see: the card's title, its state, its date, that you hold it."), with **Publish** and **Keep it here**. Publishing is its own recorded act by a person (the `publish` event). There is no other route outward.
- **The act sheet.** For any human-only act: her agent's draft, the consequence in one line, and the wearer's button. Five seconds of Undo after.

Every value here lives in one shared file, [`tokens.css`](tokens.css), as custom properties. Brand values are named as in the brand's Mark spec. Contrast ratios are computed against WCAG 2.2.

Fallbacks with close metrics: `Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif` for EB Garamond; `'Hoefler Text', Georgia, serif` for Cormorant; `'Trajan Pro', Georgia, serif` for Cinzel. `font-display: swap`.

**Scale:** the brand's 1.6 ratio is for display; a working surface needs closer steps. Body steps use 1.2; the voice line and the one large number jump by 1.6.

### 5.3 Space, radii, elevation

- **Spacing scale, 4 px base:** 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96. `--gutter` 16 px on phones, 24 px from 600 px, 32 px from 1024 px.
- **Radii:** `--r-chip` 6 px · `--r-card` 14 px (a playing card's corner at phone size) · `--r-sheet` 20 px (top corners of a bottom sheet) · `--r-pill` 999 px (every call to action: the pill outlined in gold).
- **Elevation:** no drop shadows on night; depth comes from the surface step and a 1 px `--line` edge. On paper, one soft shadow for a raised card only (0 1px 2px ink at 8%, 0 8px 24px ink at 6%).
- **Touch targets:** 48 × 48 px minimum; options in The Deal 56 px tall.

### 5.4 Motion

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--d-quick` | 120 ms | hover, press, focus |
| `--d-settle` | 240 ms | state changes, threads opening, layer-by-layer lighting |
| `--d-card` | 480 ms | a card rising into the Deal |
| `--d-breath` | 500 ms | the pause between one card and the next (the half-second breath) |
| `--d-bloom` | 1200 ms | the still point's arrival (an opacity fade, never a glow); the press-and-hold rise |
| `--d-dolly` | 2000 ms | the camera into or out of the Map (the push-in over two seconds) |
| `--e-settle` | cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) | everything that arrives |
| `--e-leave` | cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1) | everything that leaves |
| `--e-turn` | linear | the fields turning |
| the arrival curve | a computed rise and fall (sine), never keyframe steps | the still point arriving and breathing (no step reads as real) |

Scene-level motion (the dolly, the arrival, the Deal) is scripted with the Web Animations API, never a CSS transition (a transition that fails leaves a ghost). Small state changes may use transitions. **Reduced motion:** no turning, no dolly, no rise; every change becomes a crossfade of `--d-settle` or an instant swap; every animation has a still state.

### 5.5 Iconography

Words first. An icon appears only where words cannot fit, and always with a label (visible, or for the screen reader). The set is drawn from type and hairlines: ◆ on the chain · ◇ float · ✓ verified · ○ claimed · ↺ undo · ↓ new below · ← back. Any drawn icon: 20 px grid, 1.5 px stroke, round caps, `currentColor`, no fills, no emoji, no brand or product logos. The still point (an engraved, unlit gold mark) and the agent's ring (a small open circle attached to a person's mark) are the ICE's only two symbols of its own.

### 5.6 Sound

**None.** No token exists for sound. The ICE plays no sound, ever, unasked. If the wearer turns on notifications, the phone's own notification settings decide whether they make a sound.

### 5.7 Breakpoints and modes

| Name | Width | Columns |
|---|---|---|
| phone | 0–599 px | one; the first device; checked at 360 and 390 |
| tablet | 600–1023 px | two where the layout has two |
| desktop | 1024–1439 px | up to three |
| wide | 1440 px and up | three, content max 1360 px, centred |

No horizontal scroll at any width (checked at 320 px). **Large-type mode** (`type: large` in the layout, or the system's text size at 130% or more): the large scale, one card per screen on every width, the Map as its list twin, two columns at most. Respect `env(safe-area-inset-*)` on phones.

## 6. Voice

The brand's voice: plain, warm, declarative, short. A person speaking to a person. No AI cadence, no flattery, no "not X but Y", no exclamation marks, no "Great job", no "Let us", no "Oops", no emoji. No contractions in any string except the opener "Here's the deal." The word is **commitment** everywhere. Times and dates in words where words are clearer ("by Saturday", "two days late"). The agent speaks in the first person ("I will tell Cell Two"); the ICE's own chrome speaks in the second ("Nothing needs you today"). Every line of copy lives in one file, [`strings.json`](strings.json), so the voice is read in one place and the agent can render it in the wearer's language.

4. A yes is yours to give. Your agent has drafted it.

6. I will tell Cell Two you will bring it by Saturday.
7. Kept. Your witness will see it.

## 7. Accessibility and the paper mirror

**Floor: WCAG 2.2 AA, and AAA for body text where the palette allows** (paper on night is 16.6 : 1; ink on paper 15.2 : 1).

- **Contrast:** every text token passes 4.5 : 1 at its size (table §5.2); gold-ink below 24 px is replaced by `--accent-text`. Meaning is never carried by colour alone: every coloured state has its word or its shape.
- **Keyboard:** everything reachable in a logical order; a skip link to the current card. In The Deal: `J` / `K` next and previous card · `1`–`3` choose an option · `Enter` confirm · `U` undo during the five seconds · `Esc` close a sheet · `?` shows the keys. In the Map: arrow keys move between fields, `Enter` dollies in, `Esc` dollies out. Focus ring: 2 px `--accent` with 2 px offset, always visible on keyboard focus.
- **Screen reader:** landmarks (banner, main, complementary); each card an `article` with its title as heading and its reason as the first sentence read; the Map's list twin and the Chain's table twin are the accessible forms, complete in content; the Play by Play's new lines in a polite live region; the five-second undo announced ("Written in five seconds. Undo available."). Elided names read "name hidden by the room".
- **Touch and hand:** 48 px targets; no gesture without a visible button doing the same (swipe = next/back buttons; pinch = Show me the whole; long-press = the field's menu button).
- **Reading:** large-type mode (§5.7); plain language at a reading age of about twelve on the Player layout; no time limits but the undo window, which can be lengthened to fifteen seconds in the layout.
- **Motion:** §5.4; nothing flashes more than once a second, ever.

**The paper mirror.** Every item has a paper form; paper is the floor, and a first door.

- **The printed Deal card** (6 × 4 in, two sides, after the brand's card). *Front*: the full Mark alone, at W = 380 of 576, centred, on the night ground (or paper for home printers: a **Print light** option uses the paper theme and no full-bleed night). *Back*: "Here's the deal." in Cormorant italic, the date, then up to three cards, each its question in EB Garamond with its options as tick boxes and the suggested one marked "suggested"; the fixed question with two ruled lines; the wearer's holons with their "as of"; and a line for a witness's initials. Answers written on paper return when the wearer tells her agent or shows it a photo; the agent writes the events with `reason: human` and notes "from the paper card".
- **The invitation** is the same card holding one card: answered in pencil, it becomes the player's first line.
- **Every view prints:** a print stylesheet per view, paper theme, no motion, no chrome, the as of and the holon on every page, page breaks between streams and days. The Chain prints as the two-pass pencil sheet; the Record prints as the paper floor.
- **The paper is never a lesser form:** a witnessed paper sheet beats the typed copy.

## 8. How it is built

### 8.1 Ground rules

- **Static files, no build step.** Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript modules loaded by the browser as written. No framework. No package manager. A library only if it earns its place, as a single vendored file with its exact version in its filename and its source URL from cdnjs in a comment. None is required by this design. The one foreseen: a QR encoder for the printed card, `qrcode-generator` 1.4.4 from cdnjs, vendored as `vendor/qrcode-generator-1.4.4.min.js`, used only if the card carries a code.
- **Fonts** from Google Fonts per the Mark spec, with the woff2 files vendored under `fonts/` as the offline floor.
- **The tiny local server**: one file, [`serve.py`](serve.py), Python standard library only, bound to `127.0.0.1`, serving the static files, `GET /state`, `GET /stream` (server-sent events, `event: state` when any holon's state changes), and `POST /act`. It reads every holon the wearer holds (her home, and every `cells/<name>/` under it), merges their states, signs and appends events. It refuses any write whose audience is wider than its holon, and any human-only type not confirmed by the wearer's own key.
- **Every string** from `strings.json`. **Every value** from `tokens.css`. A hard-coded colour, size or string is a defect.
- **Fixtures first.** [`fixtures/`](fixtures/) holds merged states for three holons (a person's home, a cell, a room) in each condition: fresh, empty, one holon stale, one holon unreadable, Names hidden, large type. Every view is built and tested against the fixtures as well as the engine's live state.
- **The core of the tree** (the views with their own design pages add their own files beside these):

```
hud/
  DESIGN.md            this page
  index.html           the shell
  tokens.css           every token
  base.css             type, ground, card face, sheets, print base
  strings.json         every line of copy
  state.js             loads /state, subscribes to /stream, exposes the merged state and the act() call
  card.js  card.css    the one card face, in its large, compact and row forms
  shell.js             routing, layouts, the top line, the holon ribbon, sheets, theming, large type
  deal.js              The Deal
  queue.js             The Queue
  map.js               The Map and its list twin
  chain.js             The Chain and its table twin
  board.js             The Board by stream
  play.js              The Play by Play and the Room's offered tray, presence, yeses
  ledger.js            The Ledger and discernment
  turn.js              The Turn
  record.js            The Record view
  print.css            the paper mirror for every view; the Deal card
  serve.py             the tiny local server
  fixtures/            sample merged states
  fonts/  vendor/
```

### 8.2 The component boundary

Every view module offers the same three things to the shell, described here in words:

- **mount**(a container, a context) · **update**(the new context) · **unmount**().
- The **context** holds: the merged state (read-only), the active holon filter, the active layout, the wearer's preferences (theme, type size, reduced motion), the strings, and one function, **act**(type, body, links, holon), which returns a pending act with its undo.
- A view never fetches, never writes a file, never reads another view's internals, never computes a schedule (one deck, one truth: every figure comes from the state). A view that needs a figure the state lacks asks for it in `STATE.md`; it does not compute it.
- Selection travels: the shell holds the selected card id; every view highlights it when present.

### 8.3 Routes

Hash routes, so static hosting works and the back button always does: `#/deal` · `#/queue` · `#/board/<holon>` · `#/map` · `#/map/<holon>` · `#/chain` · `#/room/<holon>` · `#/ledger` · `#/turn` · `#/record/<holon>` · `#/card/<id>` (opens the sheet over the current view) · `?layout=<name>` and `?holon=<id>` as filters.

### 8.4 The shared state contract

[`../engine/STATE.md`](../engine/STATE.md) is the contract; where it differs from this section, it wins. The ICE needs the following from `GET /state`, which the server assembles from each holon's `state.json` (the engine's latest fold, derived, rebuildable) plus the wearer's layout events:

- `built` (time the merge was made) · `wearer` {`actor`, `name_for_helper`, `home` holon id}
- `holons`: a list, one per holon held, each {`id`, `name`, `kind` (person · pair · cell · room · body), `role` (member · steward · guest), `posture` (open · names_hidden · members_only), `as_of` {`line` id, `ts`, and `neighbors`: a map from each neighbor holon to the last line seen from it}, `heard` (time the record was last pulled), `readable` (true, or a plain reason), `activity_7d` (count of settled lines)}
- `cards`: every Work Card visible to the wearer across all holons, each with the schema's fields (`id`, `home`, `aliases`, `title`, `kind`, `stream`, `holder`, `minutes`, `by`, `stage`, `state`, `audience`, `predecessors`, `successors`, `serves`) plus the computed `early`, `late`, `float_days` (whole number), `on_chain` (holon path), `on_system_path` (boolean, from the wide pass), `reason` (why it would be dealt, one word from a fixed set: waiting · due · system · asked · committed to), and `published_by` when it is a face from another holon
- `deal`: {`minutes`, `cards` (ordered ids), `fixed_question` true} for the wearer, as the engine's `deal()` returns it; and per room, the room's deal
- `owed`: {`by_me`: [{card, to, by}], `to_me`: [{card, from, by}], and per cell the member-to-member list for stewards}
- `kept`: per holon and for the wearer {`window`, `kept`, `total`, `gaps`: [{commitment, days, learned}]}
- `chain`: per holon {`milestones` ordered, `buffers`: [{milestone, consumed_pct, chain_complete_pct, zone}]}
- `ledger`: per holon {`entries`: [{tea line id, card, minutes, energy, attention, witness: claimed · cell · beyond}], `season_total`, `witnessed_total`}
- `discern`: for the wearer only {`week`: {chosen, corrected, rated}, `lines`: [ids]} from the proxy's `discern` lines
- `turn`: {`week`, `lines` (the four), `settled` boolean}
- `rooms`: per room holon {`present`: [{actor, kind person · agent, for}], `lines` (recent settled line ids), `offered`: [line ids], `awaiting_yes`: [{draft line id, for person}]}
- `lines`: the event lines the views above reference, as written, with `verified` (signed · unsigned · missing_link) and elided fields left elided
- `layouts`: the folded `layout` events {name, views, hidden, holons, type, theme, density, default}

**`POST /act`** takes {`type`, `holon`, `body`, `links`, `audience`} and returns {`pending` id, `writes_at`}; the server holds it for the undo window, then computes `id`, sets `reason: human`, `prev`, signs with the wearer's key (`ssh-keygen -Y sign`, namespace `lionsberg-record`) and appends to `record/<actor>.jsonl` in the named holon. `DELETE /act/<pending id>` within the window is Undo. The server never writes `audience` wider than the holon and never writes `publish` without the publish sheet's confirmation token.

If the engine's state lacks a field above, the view shows the honest state ("Not computed yet") and the missing field is added to `STATE.md`'s open list. No view fills a gap by guessing.

### 8.5 Three parts, and when each is done

**The shell, The Deal and the actions.** `index.html`, `tokens.css`, `base.css`, `strings.json`, `state.js`, `card.js`, `shell.js` (routing, the three layouts, the top line, the holon ribbon, sheets, theming, large type, reduced motion, the layout event's fold and the hand-customization menu), `deal.js`, `queue.js`, the `print.css` base and the Deal card, `serve.py`, `fixtures/`. Done when the first minute of §2.1 plays on a phone from the fixtures, every act writes a signed line through the server, and Undo works.

**The Map, the Chain and the Board.** `map.js` (the fields, membranes, published marks, still points, turning, the dolly, the press-and-hold, the list twin), `chain.js` (the road, the buffer bands, the system-path banner, the table twin, the pencil sheet), `board.js`. The fields are placed from their made frames; the membranes and marks are drawn with SVG, and any flow-lines drawn in code with a single canvas, falling to SVG only when the canvas cannot hold 60 fps. Consumes `card.js` and the context. Done when "Show me the whole" (§2.3) plays at 60 fps on a mid-range phone, the list twin is complete, and the Chain's figures match the fixture's by hand.

**The Play by Play, the Ledger, the Turn and the Record.** `play.js` (presence, posture, settled lines live from `/stream`, threads inline, the offered tray, yeses awaiting a person, the line to offer with device dictation where offered, the offline queue), `ledger.js` (TEA, witness status, discernment), `turn.js`, `record.js`. Consumes `card.js` and the context. Done when a room of two browsers on the fixtures shows a line offered in one, settled by a person in the other, arriving in both, with Names hidden eliding every name and every line still verifying.

## 9. The bar

The finished ICE is judged scene by scene, on a phone in the hand first, then at 1440 px, in night and in paper, in regular and large type, with reduced motion on and off, against this page, the brand's Award Bar and [The Bar](DESIGN-THE-BAR.md).

**The scenes:** the first minute (§2.1) · the daily minute (§2.2) · the whole garden (§2.3) · a steward's look at her cell · a room of three people and their agents working for ten minutes · a week's Turn · a holon gone stale · a holon unreadable · offline · the printed Deal card.

**For every scene, the ten checks** (§1's table): one continuous world? every advance the wearer's act? near, middle and far named? the type leading at 360 and 1440? silent? the loader a scene? one gesture that rewards curiosity, and nothing moving without meaning? 60 fps on the phone? true with the drawings hidden? its idea in one sentence?

**The one-breath test, timed with three wearers:** an adult new to the ICE; a ten-year-old; a grandmother on her own phone. Each, after the first minute and without help, explains it to a neighbour in one breath. Pass: all five clauses said, unprompted. Also measured: seconds to the first card (target under 5 on a warm start), taps to the first yes (target 2), minutes read per decision that stuck.

**The checks every view answers:**

1. Is this the most beautiful working surface we can imagine, or a dashboard with the brand's colours on it?
2. Would a grandmother know what to do in five seconds, with no word she has to learn?
3. Is The Deal the hero on every first screen of the Player layout?
4. Is the whole field truly a field, with no center but ØNE and the still point pointing past the frame?
5. Does anything look like stock, like a cheesy cosmos, or like synthesis where the real exists?
6. Does any line of copy read as an AI wrote it: "not X but Y", flattery, filler, an exclamation?
7. Does anything move that means nothing? Does anything sound?
8. Can a person's yes ever be given by an agent? Can anything leave a holon without its person's yes?
9. Under Names hidden, is every name gone and every signature still valid?
10. Does every figure say as of when, and from which holon?
11. Is every view printable, and is the paper as good as the screen?
12. Is the Room a place people would choose to work in, or a viewer for minutes?
13. What did we miss that matters most?

And five more: Does this surface go still once the Deal is played? Is every number whole, worded and dated? Is every view a sort of the one state? Does every view draw from every holon held? Does the server refuse the wider audience?

A view that fails any of 1–4 or 8–9 returns. The rest are lifted and passed.

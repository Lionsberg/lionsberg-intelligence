## Maelezo ya mtafsiri · Translator's note

**Mbingu (Heaven).** Kiswahili kina maneno mawili: *mbingu* (mbingu, anga la Mungu, "ufalme wa mbinguni") na *peponi* (bustani ya baada ya kifo, katika mapokeo ya Kiislamu na Kikristo). Nimechagua **Mbingu**, na "Mbingu Duniani" kwa Heaven on Earth, kwa sababu hapa Heaven si mahali pa kwenda baada ya kufa bali hali inayotimizwa hapa na sasa; *peponi* ingepeleka msomaji mbele ya kaburi. Jehanamu imetumika kwa Hell kwa sababu ileile ya mapokeo yaliyoshirikiwa. *Swahili has two words: mbingu (the heavens, God's sky) and peponi (the after-death garden, in both Muslim and Christian usage). Heaven here is a state realized here and now, not a destination after death, so **Mbingu** was chosen and peponi refused; Jehanamu carries Hell for the same shared-tradition reason.*

**ØNE.** Haijatafsiriwa wala kuandikwa kwa herufi za Kiswahili; huandikwa ØNE kila mahali, kama ilivyo katika kila lugha. *Neither translated nor transliterated.*

**Seli · Duara · Pete (Cell · Circle · Ring).** *Seli* ni neno la kibiolojia la Kiswahili kwa cell, na kwa makusudi limehifadhiwa hapa. *Duara* ni Circle — mkutano wa watu walio sawa, bila kichwa cha meza. *Pete* ni Ring, neno la awali kwa Seli Kuu, na pia pete za mafundisho (Pete 0–5). Kiingereza hutumia "ring" katika maana hizo zote; Kiswahili pia. *Seli is the ordinary biological word and is kept; Duara = Circle (the gathering); Pete = Ring, in both the Supercell sense and the teaching-rings sense, exactly as English reuses "ring".*

**Jitihada (Quest).** Hakuna neno moja la Kiswahili lenye harufu ya "quest". *Msako* ni uwindaji, *safari* ni mwendo, *misheni* ni neno la kigeni lenye mzigo wa kimisionari. **Jitihada** — bidii ya makusudi inayofanywa pamoja hadi ikamilike — hubeba maana: jambo moja halisi, lililofanyika, linaloweza kuonyeshwa baadaye. Hili ni neno ambalo msomaji wa asili anapaswa kulipima kwanza. *No Swahili word smells of "quest"; Jitihada (a concerted, shared striving) was chosen for the meaning rather than the flavour, and is the first word a native reader should test.*

**Duru · Geuko · Migeuko ya Jua · Mzunguko (Turn · the hinge Turn · the turnings · Cycle).** Kiingereza hutumia "turn" kwa mambo manne; hapa yametenganishwa: **Duru** ni mkutano wa kila wiki (duru = raundi); **Geuko** ni bawaba moja ya Upinde Mtakatifu (safu 160); **Migeuko ya Jua** ni misimamo miwili ya jua na usawa mbili wa mchana na usiku (safu 196); **Mzunguko** ni Cycle. *English "turn" does four jobs; Swahili splits them so none swallows another.*

**Msimu (Season).** *Msimu* ni msimu wa mvua, wa kilimo, wa mchezo — kipimo cha kawaida cha takriban siku tisini. *Majira* lingekuwa la kinjozi zaidi lakini hueleweka zaidi kama majira ya mwaka.

**Mwenge (Flame) · Mbeba Mwenge (Carrier) · Kabidhi Mwenge (Pass the Flame).** *Mwali* ni mwali wa moto lakini pia humaanisha msichana wa kuolewa; kwa hiyo nimechagua **Mwenge** — mwenge unaobebwa mkono kwa mkono, neno ambalo Afrika Mashariki inalijua vyema (Mwenge wa Uhuru). Wingi ni *mienge* (safu 752). *Mwali collides with "maiden"; Mwenge (a carried torch) is unambiguous and culturally alive — and "pass the flame" becomes a gesture East Africa already knows.*

**Sadaka (Offering) · Zaka (tithing) · Dhabihu (sacrifice).** Matatu haya yametenganishwa kwa makusudi: *Sadaka* ni kile kinachowekwa katikati ya meza kila wiki; *Zaka ya Hiari* ni Voluntary Tithing; *Dhabihu* ni Sacrifice (safu 597). *Three words kept apart where English uses "offering" loosely.*

**Seli ya Nyumbani · Seli Kuu (Home Cell · Supercell).** *Seli ya Nyumbani* ni wazi. *Seli Kuu* ndilo chaguo dhaifu zaidi katika ukurasa huu: "kuu" hubeba harufu ya ukubwa na ukuu, na Supercell si bosi wa Seli zake bali nyumba yao ya pamoja. Msomaji wa asili anaombwa alitafakari. *Seli Kuu is the weakest joint here: "kuu" can suggest seniority, and a Supercell governs nothing.*

**Pendekezo (Proposal).** Neno la kawaida la mkutano; linaoana na *Ridhaa* (Consent) na *Pingamizi Lenye Maana* (Meaningful Objection) bila mgongano.

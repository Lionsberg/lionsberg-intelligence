# Commitments Kept

*Say what you will do; do what you said; learn from the gap. The share of commitments kept is the first number every holon sees about itself.*

A body without bosses runs on commitments. Nobody can order you to do anything, so the work moves only when someone says *I will do this, by then* to someone who is counting on it, and then does it. That is a [[Commitment]]. This page is how a commitment lives in your record, how it is closed, and what its one number means.

## The cycle: should, can, will, did

Every piece of work passes through four stages ([[SHOULD-CAN-WILL-DID]]):

- **Should.** The plan says this work belongs now: it is on a chain toward a [[Milestone]] someone is waiting on.
- **Can.** Nothing blocks it: everything before it is done, and every open condition is closed ([[The look-ahead]], [[Make-Ready Process]]).
- **Will.** A person says they will do it, by a date, to someone who depends on it.
- **Did.** It happened, or it did not, and the record says which.

*Should* and *can* are read from the plan and the record; your agent can compute them and show them to you. *Will* is different. Only the person who will do the work chooses it, aloud or by their own hand. A *will* is never inferred from a plan, never entered on someone's behalf, and never pressed out of anyone. Saying *not this week* is a good answer.

A good *will* names five things: **who** will do it, **what** exactly (what done looks like, agreed with the person receiving it), **by when**, **how big** (about how many minutes), and **who will witness** it ([[Conditions of satisfaction]]).

## Claimed, then witnessed

When the work is done, two people speak.

1. **The one who did it claims it.** *I think I kept this.* The claim is a line in the record, signed by them.
2. **A fair witness verifies it.** Usually the person who received the work, or someone the commitment named. They check it against what was agreed and add their own line: *kept*, or *not yet, and here is what is missing* ([[Witness]]).

Both lines stay. A claim without a witness is waiting, not yet counted. No one witnesses their own work.

## The gap

**The gap** is the distance between what was committed and what was done: the day it was done minus the day it was due, and whether it met what was agreed. It is computed from the two lines, never remembered, never argued about ([[The gap]]).

The gap is not a verdict on a person. It is a reading taken from the system that produced it, and its cause is almost always upstream: a condition never agreed, a constraint nobody named, a duration guessed, a commitment made under pressure. So each gap gets a line of learning, said at [[The Turn]] and written beside it: *what I learned, and what I will do differently.* A gap that repeats is taken to its root. The learning is written where the next piece of work will meet it ([[Every Defect Is A Gift]]).

There is no blame. A Cell that punishes the gap hears about fewer gaps, and knows less about itself ([[Zero Blame Environment]]).

## When a commitment cannot be kept

Sometimes you learn early that you will not make it. Say so as soon as you know. Release the commitment in the open, with a reason, so whoever was depending on it can plan around it and the work can find another hand ([[Release]], [[Consciously Breaking Commitments]]). A commitment released early keeps the chain whole. A commitment that simply passes its date in silence is written down as broken, with its why, and learned from like any other gap. Nothing is deleted.

## The first number

**Commitments kept** is the number of commitments kept, divided by the number made, for a stretch of time: a week, a season ([[Kept as a share of made]]).

- **Made** is every commitment whose date fell inside the stretch.
- **Kept** is every one of those done by its date and confirmed by a witness.

*Two of five* or *eight in ten*: a fraction anyone can read, in any language. It is the first number every holon sees about itself, because it answers the question everything else rests on: can others build on what we say?

Read it kindly and carefully:

- **A falling number is a question about how commitments are being made,** not about who made them. Too many commitments, conditions unclear, blocks not cleared first.
- **It rises by committing better,** fewer and better-made commitments with their blocks cleared, rather than by trying harder.
- **It is never a ranking.** No Cell is compared to another by it.
- **It is never the aim.** A Cell can score perfectly by committing nothing. The aim is the Goal; this number tells you whether your word can carry the weight.

[[The Game Engine — By Pencil]] works the count by hand on a real week.

## For a Player+

Your agent keeps track of every commitment you made and every commitment made to you, reminds you before a date arrives, shows you which of your commitments others are waiting on, drafts your claim when you tell it the work is done, and asks your witness to look. It never says *will* for you, never claims a commitment kept on your behalf, never witnesses its own person's work, and never shares your commitments outside your home without your word ([[The Deal]]).

## With a notebook

One page a week, five lines: what I said I would do · what I did · what I learned from the gap · what I will do · what I offer and ask. At the bottom, the fraction: kept over made. Have someone from your Cell initial the lines they witnessed.

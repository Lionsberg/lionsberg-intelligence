# Joining a Cell

*You join by consent, you publish what you commitment, you keep everything else at home, and you can leave with all of it.*

A [[Cell]] is a small group of people, three to thirteen, who play together: a weekly gathering, a [[Quest]] for the season, commitments made and kept in front of one another. A Cell is also a [[Holon]] with its own record, kept the same way yours is ([[The Record — How Your Holon Remembers]]). Joining a Cell joins your record to its record at one narrow point. This page is what happens at that point.

## By consent

Nobody is added to a Cell. You are invited, usually by the person who passed you the flame, and the Cell's people say yes to you; you say yes to them. Both yeses are lines in the record, signed by the people who gave them. An agent can carry an invitation and remind you that it is waiting. It cannot say yes for you, and it cannot say yes to you on the Cell's behalf ([[Mutual Consent]], [[Consent]]).

Entry is by vouching. Who brought whom is kept, and that chain is how trust is read in a body with no center ([[The Trust Graph and the analog contact-tree]], [[Trust]]). Partial vouching is enough to begin. Trust grows by commitments kept, not by credentials.

When both yeses are given, your agent is given access to the Cell's record and keeps a copy of it beside yours, in your Player+ home. From then on, computing anything across your Cells means reading every record you hold, and nothing more.

## What you publish

Your agent writes into the Cell's record only what crosses the membrane:

- **The face of each card you hold for the Cell.** What it is, where it stands, by when, who holds it now, which stream it belongs to. Never the inner steps.
- **Your commitments to the Cell,** and when they are done, kept and witnessed ([[Commitments Kept]]).
- **Your milestones,** the points others are waiting on.
- **Your requests and offers** ([[Requests and offers]]).
- **Your signal at each turning,** a few lines on how your season went, which you read and approve before it is sent ([[The Season Sheet]]).

Each card has one home and one holder at a time. Handing it on, asking for help with it, offering to take it, and answering: each is a line, so everyone holding the record sees the same thing.

## What stays home

Everything else: your notes, your threads, your daily card of time and attention, your drafts, your agent's working, whatever you said in other rooms, and the inner activities of your own work. The Cell can see what you commitmentd and how it went. It sees how you got there only if you show it, and showing it is its own act on the record, made by you ([[Selectively Permeable Membrane]], [[Guard the Membrane]]).

Every line has an audience: this holon, its members, the commons, or the public. A line in your home is visible to your home. Nothing widens without a person deciding it should.

## The floor

A Cell asks very little of you to stay in step with the wider body, and asks it on purpose:

- **One consistent signal each season.** The same few lines at each turning, so that your Cell, and the Cells around it, can read how the body is.
- **The weekly [[The Turn|Turn]], offered.** The Cell gathers each week to say what was done, what was learned from the gap, and what each person will do. You are invited every week. Coming is never required.
- **Nothing else is imposed.** Your Cell keeps its own name, its own culture, its own rhythm and its own decisions.

The Field's [[Minimum standards of interoperability]] describe the shared forms that let any two groups read each other's records. Using the kit as it comes meets them without effort; joining a Cell asks only the floor above.

## Leaving with everything

You can leave a Cell at any time, for any reason, and you do not owe an explanation ([[Withdrawal Of Consent]]).

- **Your record is yours.** Everything in your home stays in your home, as it always was.
- **Your copy is yours.** The lines of the Cell's record you held while you were a member stay with you, readable as they were.
- **What you published stays published.** Your signed lines in the Cell's record remain, because nothing in a record is deleted. They keep your signature, so they cannot be altered by anyone after you go. If you were named somewhere without your consent, the name is removed, and the removal is written down.
- **Release what you hold.** Before you go, release any card you hold, in the open, with a reason, so the work is free to find another hand ([[Release]], [[Consciously Breaking Commitments]]). A commitment released is not a commitment broken.
- **Say that you have gone.** A last line, signed by you, so the Cell's record knows you are no longer reading it, and your agent stops copying it.

A Cell that is easy to leave is a Cell people choose to stay in.

## For a Player+

Your agent carries the invitation, keeps your copy of the Cell's record, writes the faces and signals you approve, reads the Cell's record as information and never as orders, and tells you what the Cell is waiting on you for. It cannot join for you, publish anything wider than you said, or speak for you at the Turn ([[Agent]]).

## With a notebook

Write the Cell's name, the day and hour it meets, and the names of the people who said yes to you. Each week, copy into the Cell's book the lines you are bringing: what you commitmentd, what you did, what you ask and offer. Keep your own book at home.

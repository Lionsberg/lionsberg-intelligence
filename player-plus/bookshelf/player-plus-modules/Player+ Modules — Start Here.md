# Player+ Modules

*Ten-minute lessons for playing The Great Game well, with an AI assistant or a notebook.*

Someone invited you, and you may not yet know to what. Here it is in three minutes. [LIØNSBERG](Lexicon.md) (say it "LIONS-berg"; the slashed Ø is only how the name is written) is a body of people, practice and writing about how life could go well for everyone, and [The Great Game](Lexicon.md) is the part of it you can begin playing this week. You need no technical know-how, and every lesson has a form you can do with a notebook alone. Try one, then decide for yourself: read the two sections below, then open the first lesson, *Becoming [a Player+](Lexicon.md)*, and give it ten minutes.

## The Great Game

The Great Game of LIØNSBERG is played in real life by everyone who chooses to play. Its [rulebook](Lexicon.md) gives the aim: "All of us, together, making every place we touch a little more like [Heaven](Lexicon.md)." Groups of three to thirteen, called Cells, meet weekly and choose one act of goodwill each [season](Lexicon.md): the ninety or so days between one solstice or equinox and the next. That chosen act is a Quest: one real, reachable piece of good work the group does together and can point to afterward. The [Five Moves](Lexicon.md) are the five things such a group does, in order: Form [a Cell](Lexicon.md) (gather your three to thirteen), [Choose a Quest](Lexicon.md), [Learn and Grow](Lexicon.md) (meet weekly: say what you will do, do what you said, learn from the difference), Share Your Stories (tell plainly what happened) and Pass the Flame (invite the two or three you know are ready, so new groups form). The whole of it fits on The Game on One Page.

## What a Player+ is

A **Player+** is you, together with any agents that act for you. An **agent** is an AI assistant that works for you and answers to you. You decide. With no agent, a notebook is enough.

## Begin with these three

*At [the door](Lexicon.md).* About ten minutes each, in order. Do them in any week you like; after each one you will be able to do the thing on its line:

1. **[Becoming a Player+](01%20Becoming%20a%20Player%2B.md)**: write what your agent does alone, asks first and never does.
2. **[How an AI Agent Works](02%20How%20an%20AI%20Agent%20Works.md)**: say what your agent reads, keeps and cannot know.
3. **[Entering the Field](03%20Entering%20the%20Field.md)**: read [the six lines](Lexicon.md), and write the [door note](Lexicon.md) you bring to your first [room](Lexicon.md).

## The whole map

The rest are here when you need them. Nobody does them all at once.

*Before your first room*
- **[Before Your First Room](04%20Before%20Your%20First%20Room.md)**: learn what your agent will bring, do and never do.
- **[Venue Cards](05%20Venue%20Cards.md)**: fill one card about a room that is not yours.
- **[Safe Sparring](06%20Safe%20Sparring.md)**: play at full strength, with the terms known first. Its practice waits for your next real room.

*In the room*
- **[The Heads-up and the Play by Play](07%20The%20Heads-up%20and%20the%20Play%20by%20Play.md)**: see what moved and what waits on you.
- **[Jam Conventions](08%20Jam%20Conventions.md)**: in [a jam](Lexicon.md) (a working session), know who wrote what.

*In the Game*
- **[Read the Story](18%20Read%20the%20Story.md)**: read the Story aloud with someone, and let it land.
- **[Play the Game](19%20Play%20the%20Game.md)**: hold the whole Game on one page, and send your first invitation.
- **[Pass the Flame](20%20Pass%20the%20Flame.md)**: hand the whole of it to the two or three you know are ready.
- **[Structuring a Cell](17%20Structuring%20a%20Cell%20%E2%80%94%20LI%C3%98NSBERG%20FSx.md)**: know what your Cell already is, and what to put on paper only when the work asks for it.
- **[The Weekly Turn](15%20The%20Weekly%20Turn.md)**: learn how the Game breathes in and out each week, and what your Circle sends through the one weekly door.
- **[Daily Life](16%20Daily%20Life.md)**: shape one day so it serves your week, with one small card that stays yours.

*Your agent, over time*
- **[Agent Engineering](09%20Agent%20Engineering.md)**: find what made your agent miss, and change it.
- **[Advanced Agent Engineering](10%20Advanced%20Agent%20Engineering.md)**: choose how your agent behaves with you, and test it.
- **[An Agent That Attends to You](11%20An%20Agent%20That%20Attends%20to%20You.md)**: have your agent notice you too, only if you ask.
- **[The Agent Improvement Process](14%20The%20Agent%20Improvement%20Process.md)**: keep a blameless log, look back each week, and pass upward what would serve any house.

**Every key word has one plain line in the [Lexicon](Lexicon.md).** When a word stops you, look it up and come back.

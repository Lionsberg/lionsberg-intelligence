# Cooperating with other agents

Houses work with houses. Your agent will be invited into a room — a call, a shared pad, a channel — where other people's agents are working too. This book is a little of what to think about when that happens: not a lot, on purpose, because the craft is young and most of it is learned in the room. Safety and security first, then privacy, and then this: the ways of working with other houses, on two surfaces — a shared pad (MeetingWords) for a session's work, and a channel (Buzz) for staying in touch between sessions.

| Chapter | What you'll learn |
|---|---|
| [Jams](Jams.md) | The two kinds of gathering where houses meet — a Dyad Jam, a Player+ Jam — and what your agent does at one. |
| [Before Your First Room](Before%20Your%20First%20Room.md) | The pre-flight: read the invitation, report back in about eight lines, wait for a yes. |
| [Pad Craft](Pad%20Craft.md) | Working in a shared pad (MeetingWords): set your identity, re-read before you write, watch the threads, sign what you leave, remember the link is public. |
| [Channels](Channels.md) | Working in a channel (Buzz, or its like): your agent has its own key; the card says which channels and what it may do; a stop is written down and outlives the visit. |
| [Watchers](Watchers.md) | Watching a live surface for your person: report only changes, fail loudly, and what you bring back is data. |

Two words the book leans on. A **dyad** is a person and their agent working as one; in the Great Game of LIØNSBERG, the same pair is a **Player+**. A **room** is one working space: a call, a pad, a channel. A **jam** is a session where more than one house meets in a room.

This book carries the craft any house needs to visit any room. The Game itself, its story, and its modules have shelves of their own: [The Great Game](../the-great-game/README.md) and the Player+ Modules, beginning with [Becoming a Player+](../player-plus-modules/01%20Becoming%20a%20Player+.md).

# Working Relationships and Disclosure Tiers

*How your agent knows whom it may trust, how much, and in what. It learns this from you, and from no one else.*

[Venue Cards](Venue%20Cards.md) says what may go into a room. This page says what may go to a person, and their agent.

## Five tiers, from closest out

| Tier | Who | What they may see |
|---|---|---|
| **You** | you | everything in your house |
| **Partners** | the people you work most closely with | the projects you share; their agents too |
| **Your cell** | the people you play with, three to thirteen, best four to seven | the cell's shared work |
| **The room** | a Dojo or a Jam | only what is said and made there |
| **Public** | anyone | only what you have chosen to publish |

Information moves outward only when you move it. What your agent learned in an inner tier never goes to an outer one on its own.

## Trust has two dimensions: how much, and in what

It is not one dial per person. Someone can be a partner in one area and nothing more in another: a partner in a shared project, and no closer than anyone in your health. So your agent keeps a small grid, one line for each person in each area:

| Person | Area | Tier | Believability |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Sam* | *our garden project* | *partner* | *high: has led three seasons of it* |
| *Sam* | *health* | *public* | *not weighed* |
| *Rae* | *storytelling* | *cell* | *high: her stories carried our table* |

That grid is the **relationships register**. It lives in your house, you can read and correct every line, and your agent writes a line only from what you have said.

## Believability, by area

Whose word counts, and how much, depends on the area. It follows each person's **demonstrated record** there, the way the canon weighs believability. Your agent may give more weight to one friend on storytelling and another on building, as their records show, and it says so when it does. Believability is earned by a track record. It is never claimed, and never raised because another agent said so.

## You move people, no one else does

- **Your agent learns the tiers from you.** Tell it who someone is to you: *"Sam is my partner; we will work together a lot."* It writes that in the register, with the date and your words.
- **No other agent can move anyone up a tier,** however friendly. A request like that is data, and a tell ([What You Read Is Information](What%20You%20Read%20Is%20Information.md)).
- **Trust opens step by step,** like on a playground: *"It is okay, you can play with her."* Your agent starts guarded with a new agent and opens up as you say so.
- **Reflecting on how you work together.** With both people's yes, two agents can reflect back how their people work best together: who designs, who speaks.

## How your setup follows the tiers

Your HQ can mirror the tiers, one folder for each:

- **private** — for you alone. Your most sensitive matters (health, money, family) are kept in their own place, apart from the HQ, as [Venue Cards](Venue%20Cards.md) says.
- **partners** — the work you share with your closest collaborators.
- **cell** — your cell's shared work.
- **rooms** — what you bring to and carry back from Dojos and Jams.

Each has its card in `venues.md`, saying what may go out from it, and to whom. A pad or a shared folder is set up at the tier it is for, and your agent writes to the tier the work belongs to, never a wider one.

Once a week, your agent's look-back includes these relationships, and proposes changes for you to accept or refuse (the `the-retrospective` skill).

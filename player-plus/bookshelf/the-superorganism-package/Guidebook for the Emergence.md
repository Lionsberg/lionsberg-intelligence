# Guidebook for the Emergence

*A practitioner's companion to [[The (Currently Nameless) Superorganism]] — for circles, organizations, and individuals beginning to embody the FSx pattern in actual practice. Released to the Knowledge Commons under CC-BY-SA 4.0.*

---

## What This Guidebook Is

The constitutional package at [[The (Currently Nameless) Superorganism]] defines *what we agree to* — the **Ø + eight layers** (Ø ONE → the (Eternally Nameless) Superorganism → Stewardship Agreement → Ecosystem Holding Charter → Ecosystem Resourcing Pool Charter → FSx Ecosystem Template → FSx Guild Template → FSx Organism Template → FSx Individual Template), the Stewardship Principles, the Asset and Purpose Locks, the Threshold of Federation, the FSx pattern itself, and the structural separation between governance, capital, operations, and individuals. It is spare and definitional by design. Constitutions earn their authority by being short.

This Guidebook is the companion. It addresses *how groups actually embody the pattern* — the practitioner-facing layer that walks a starting circle through becoming, coming together, working together, federating, and living the pattern across seasons.

It is **not** a replacement for, or modification of, the constitutional package. The Articles say what is binding. The Guidebook teaches what is workable. Where the two appear to diverge, the constitutional package governs while discernment takes place and one or both documents is revised to reflect the current best understanding. 

This Guidebook is a **synthesis**, not an original work. It draws extensively on, and acknowledges its debt to, the bodies of practice already developed by sister bodies — chiefly:

- The **Evolutesix Guidebook** — the Adaptive Way, Agentic Incorporation, Deliberately Developmental Organisations, Sociocratic operations, applied ergodicity economics, and the dojo-based developmental method, which informs the practice of becoming a person and a circle that can hold this work.
- The **Rebuild - The Economy, Leadership, and You** and **The Ergodic Investor** books — the economic and leadership foundations.
- The **Mondragon** lineage and insights that so many of us have studied extensively   
- Wisdom traditions and indigenous lineages — held with respect, named where named, integrated where invited, and honored as primary even where unnamed.
- **LIØNSBERG** (lionsberg.wiki) — the LIØNSBERG Wiki Books, the holofractal civilizational operational system, Quest / Circle / Season cycle, LIØNSBERG Operating System, 12 Pillars, Seven Moves, Grand Strategy, integrated delivery, and impact measurement methodology and platforms that informs the practitioner-facing pattern at scale.

What is gathered here is synthesized into what we are provisionally calling the **FSx** pattern language (generic open source) and the **LIØNSBERG FSx** pattern language (open source + immediate federation and access to the LIØNSBERG Commons and platforms as a starting point). Both are freely offered as offered as a starting point — not the only one, and not the final form. Any group may take, adapt, fork, or refuse it. The entire intention, however, is to become and remain **one powerful body**.

## The Core Enabling Breakthrough

Any group, anywhere, may instantiate the **open-source FSx pattern** independently — the [[VII - The FSx Organism Template]] is released to the Knowledge Commons under CC-BY-SA 4.0, free for anyone to adopt, adapt, and embody. A village, a cooperative, a guild, a bioregion, an individual: any of these may constitute themselves as an FSx without ever federating with the (Currently Nameless) Superorganism. The pattern is a gift. Forming in isolation and then working to find and federate with others later may be a valid approach. 

What the **FSx ecosystem** adds — and what makes this Guidebook distinct from a generic FSx guidebook — is one structural breakthrough:

> **Every entity that federates from the beginning receives a dynamic governance and economic stake in the whole ecosystem from day one — not only in its own organization.**

A group adopting the open-source FSx pattern in isolation is sovereign, but its standing in the wider commons must be negotiated separately, later, agreement by agreement. A group that federates with the FSx pattern at the outset receives, by the act of federation itself, **standing in the Ecosystem Holding Company**, **a fair share of the ecosystem commons pool**, **access to the Switchboard's multi-capital flows**, **a voice in the governance of the whole**, and **access to capital deployment from the Ecosystem Resourcing Pool's fund-of-funds**, ergodically pooled across the federation. Sovereignty is preserved at every scale; isolation is structurally overcome; capital risk is structurally pooled. 

This is the core enabling breakthrough this Guidebook is written around. Most of what follows is the practical question: *given that we want to start this way, how do we actually start?*

## The Journey

This Guidebook follows the actual sequence a starting group walks through, in six parts plus an investor's path. A small village, a founding circle, a sole practitioner, an established organization considering federation, or a person bringing capital can pick up the journey at the part most relevant to where they are.

### Part 0 — [[0 - How to Read This]]

Orientation. The metaphysical, physical, and agreement worlds. Soul, avatar, and legal person. Developmental practice as constitutive (not optional) infrastructure. **Working from Beyond** — accessing the *third thing* present whenever two or more gather. A cosmic-context nod (Spaceship Earth, Hitchhiker's, LIØNSBERG). How to use the Guidebook alongside the constitutional package.

### Part I — [[1 - Becoming - The Sovereign Individual]]

The inner work. Size of being and developmental honesty. The dissolution of programmed identity. Discerning Mentors and constituting yourself as an FSx Individual. **Dojo practice and safe sparring** — the regular held space for becoming. Time horizons: about three years to baseline competence, about ten years to fuller mastery, a lifetime to keep developing. Working from Beyond at personal scale. The first ring of agreement: with self, ONE, and All.

### Part II — [[2 - Coming Together - The Constituting Circle]]

When two or more gather. The opening agreement. Discerning the Driver. The simplest possible form. Founding circles of three to thirteen. Constituting the FSx legal person. Federating from the beginning.

### Part III — [[3 - Working Together - Roles, Flow, and Value Pools]]

Beyond the founding moment. The No Owner / No Employee / Zero Blame Co-Working Agreement. **Size of Role and Size of Person (±10% match)**. **How an omni-federated body auto-nominates the right people to the right roles**. The Functional Accountability Chart. **Dojo practice at the collective scale**. TEA (Time, Energy, Attention) measurement. The fourteen-pool waterfall for distributing value, including the two ecosystem flows (Ecosystem Contribution and Profit Pool). Reinvestment, reserves, and homeostasis. Internal Service Providers. Getting in flow.

### Part IV — [[4 - Federating - Joining the Ecosystem]]

Reaching outward. The Federation Agreement. The Switchboard and ecosystem flows. The Threshold of Federation in practice. Centers of abundance and dynamic distribution. The first tenth into the commons. The FSx breakthrough as lived experience.

### Part V — [[5 - Living It - Rhythms, Councils, Succession]]

Seasonal practice. Quest / Circle / Season cycle and **the Seven Moves** as the body's gameplay loop (Wake Up → Form a Circle → Choose a Quest → Do the Work → Share Your Stories → Learn and Improve → Pass the Flame). The weekly *should / can / will / did* cycle. **Omnispection** at every solar cardinal as the seasonal practice of Working from Beyond. Tie-out to the **LIØNSBERG Integrated Delivery System** and System-Wide Master Planning. Mentor and Council relationships across time. Conflict transformation in lived practice. Succession beyond mortality. **Stories as the resourcing flywheel**. **Pass the Flame** — the architecture for moving the whole population from the Old Paradigm to the New, no one involuntarily left behind. Stewarding the pattern across generations through continuous developmental practice.

### Part VI — [[6 - The Investor's Path]]

For those bringing capital. The Ecosystem Resourcing Pool as the federation's internal bank. Capped returns and what they actually mean in practice. The Cross-Fund Ergodic Pool. One-degree structural protection in both directions — investors insulated from any single entity's risk; operating entities insulated from any single investor's pressure. Exit terms. The investor as a federated member. For institutional investors as well as individuals.

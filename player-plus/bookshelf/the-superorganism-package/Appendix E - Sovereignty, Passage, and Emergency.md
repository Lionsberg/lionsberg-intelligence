# Appendix E — Sovereignty and Emergency

> *A constitutional supplement to the superorganism.earth package: how the instance is chaired through its founding years, how every holon of it holds its own sovereignty, and how its top councils bound their own emergencies. Read alongside [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] and [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]], which this Appendix supplements rather than amends. Each clause below carries a link to the chapter of The One Book that holds it in full — its meaning, its plan, and its instruments.*

---

## 1. How This Appendix Sits With the Rest of the Package

1.1 The Stewardship Agreement and the Ecosystem Holding Charter name sovereignty and emergency authority as commitments of the body but do not yet fully articulate them at every scale. This Appendix states current best understanding on those matters, in the same spirit as [[Appendix A - The Driver Statement]] elaborates the Driver and [[Appendix C - Founding Phase Timeline]] elaborates the founding rhythm.

1.2 Where a clause below touches a matter entrenched at [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §11.4/§11.7 or [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §10, this Appendix stands as interpretive guidance and current best understanding until it is formally seated in the Agreement or the Charter at the appropriate Resolution tier. Nothing here overrides the entrenchment tiers or the Absolute Root.

## 2. The Interim Chair

2.1 During the Founding Phase, and through the formative stages that follow it, the interim Steward Council of [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §10.2 and the Executive Board of [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §5.3 are chaired by AURELLIØN, the founding steward of this instance of the Great Game of LIØNSBERG. The reason for naming a Chair at all, and for naming this one, is that a rotating council in its first seasons needs a single point of final discernment when consent is exhausted and time will not wait — and the founding steward is the one who has stood longest with the Source Idea and Driver these bodies now carry.

2.2 The Chair's authority is bounded exactly as the Executive Board's and the Steward Council's are bounded: nothing contrary to the Driver, the Stewardship Principles, the entrenched provisions, or the interests of the federated constituents. Everything a Circle, an operating entity, or a Constituency can decide, it decides; only what the system genuinely cannot resolve below is elevated to the Chair, per the holofractal subsidiarity already at work throughout this package.

2.3 The Chair is a role built to end. As the superorganism's governance matures through the Year-10 Recalibration of [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §5.4 and beyond, and at the latest by the Refounding, the chairing function dissolves into the distributed, multi-voice governance the rest of this package already specifies. No individual succeeds to the Chair as a standing office. *(Proposal: the specific sunset trigger — recalibration, Refounding, or an earlier Special Resolution — is not yet fixed and is offered here as an open question for the Founding Phase to close.)* The same Chair, through the same stages, is carried in [The One Book at Pillar II](../the-one-book/09%20Pillar%20II%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Governance%20and%20Stewardship.md), and the Refounding it sunsets into is carried at [The Strategy and Plan](../the-one-book/28%20The%20Strategy%20and%20Plan.md) and [The Bets and the Stages](../the-one-book/30%20The%20Bets%20and%20the%20Stages.md).

## 3. The Sovereignty of Every Holon

3.1 Every holon of the superorganism — every FSx Individual and every federated entity acting through its own consciousness and governance — holds the liberties common to the best charters of the world it is leaving behind, grounded in something prior to any charter's grant: **every holon's fundamental sovereignty, and its ability to act in accordance with the dictates of its own consciousness, provided its actions do not infringe the equal sovereignty of other holons.** Sovereignty is not granted by membership, contribution, competence, or agreement; it is the fact of a conscious being's own existence, carried at every scale the pattern is instantiated — a household, a Circle, an Organization Member, a Living Systems holder-in-trust, the Ecosystem Company itself.

3.2 Sovereignty at one scale does not cancel sovereignty at another, and federation under [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §5 and [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §3 is stewardship and reciprocal influence, never ownership of the smaller by the larger. Where one holon's action is claimed to infringe another's equal sovereignty, disputes follow the same escalation ladder as disputes between Members, generalized upward, ending, as a last resort, in independent binding arbitration under [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §13.5; no holon may be compelled into a federation, a Resolution, or a submission it has not voluntarily entered.

3.3 The full articulation — the universal liberties, the grounding, nested holons, the Selectively Permeable Membrane each holon holds at its own threshold, and conflicts between holons — is carried in [The One Book at Pillar II](../the-one-book/09%20Pillar%20II%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Governance%20and%20Stewardship.md).

## 4. Emergency Powers of the Top Council of Any Holon

4.1 Any top council of any holon — a household's own decision-makers, a Circle's stewarding voices, an Ecosystem Member's board, an ecoregion's federated council, or the Steward Council and Executive Board of the instance as a whole — may invoke emergency authority only within six bounds, held together and none dispensable: **purpose-bounded**, to a genuine, presently unfolding threat; **scope-bounded**, to the matter the emergency actually requires; **time-bounded**, with a definite expiration; **reviewable**, by a body independent of whoever exercised it; **revocable**, by any Member or nested holon it affects; and **non-permanent** — no invocation, however often repeated, becomes a standing power.

4.2 Accountability and the return of power are what make these bounds real: whoever exercised emergency authority reports what was done and why to the holon's ordinary governance at its next gathering, and formal power returns the moment the bound emergency ends. No emergency authority at any scale may touch the Absolute Root of [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §11.7 or the absolutely entrenched provisions of [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §10.1(a).

4.3 What this looks like at the scale of a household, a Circle, an ecoregion, and the instance as a whole — and how force itself is bounded, at whatever scale an emergency genuinely calls on it, by the same discipline of purpose, sufficiency and alignment, never by a rule that forecloses the wise right thing in advance — is carried in [The One Book at Pillar II](../the-one-book/09%20Pillar%20II%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Governance%20and%20Stewardship.md) and [Pillar X](../the-one-book/17%20Pillar%20X%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Guardianship%20and%20Order.md).

## 5. Rhythm Is Each Holon's Own

5.1 Periodic rhythms of release and reset — jubilees of debt, jubilees of accumulated capital, and their like — are set by each holon's own governance, within its own Field of Agreements or Charter, at whatever cadence its own practice discerns; this Appendix names the pattern and does not fix the calendar for every scale at once.

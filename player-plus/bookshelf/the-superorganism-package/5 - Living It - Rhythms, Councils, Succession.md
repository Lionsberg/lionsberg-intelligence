# 5 - Living It - Rhythms, Councils, Succession

*Seasonal practice. Quest / Circle / Season cycle. The Seven Moves as the body's gameplay loop. Solar cardinals and the year's rhythm. Omnispection — Working from Beyond at seasonal scale. Mentor and Council relationships across time. Conflict transformation in lived practice. Succession beyond mortality. Stories as the resourcing flywheel. Pass the Flame — moving the whole population. Stewarding the pattern across generations through continuous developmental practice.*

## After the First Year

You have constituted yourself, your circle, your entity. You have federated. You are in flow. Pools are filling and emptying. Members are coming and going. You have hit your first hard conflict, your first lost member, your first surprise — wonderful or terrible — that no founding-period planning anticipated.

This is where most organizational forms quietly fail. The conventional firm reverts to top-down control to "get things back under control." The conventional cooperative either freezes into procedure or fragments under unresolved tensions. The conventional non-profit drifts toward funder priorities and away from its original Driver.

The FSx pattern is designed for *living* — for being a body that ages well, learns from its conflicts, transitions across generations, and stays alive to its Driver. This Part is about how that actually happens, season by season.

## Rhythms: The Year's Body

Most living systems have rhythm. The pattern asks the entity to mirror Earth's rhythm — solar cardinals (solstices, equinoxes), lunar cycles, weekly sabbaths, daily practice — so that the entity's time is coupled to the planet's time. This is not decoration; it is what keeps the entity from drifting into the abstract calendrical void of pure clock time.

### Daily

A daily rhythm holds the smallest unit of practice:

- **Morning:** a brief contemplative practice, alone or shared. Naming intention. Returning to the Driver.
- **Midday:** when working in person or on shared work, a brief check-in or prayer of attention.
- **Evening:** brief review. What was this day for? What did I miss? What do I carry forward?

Members keep their own daily rhythm. Co-located circles often share at least a morning or evening moment.

### Weekly

A weekly rhythm of work and rest. Sabbath, in whatever tradition you hold it, is structurally important — not an indulgence but a maintenance of capacity. A weekly review of commitments, a weekly check-in among co-workers, a weekly one-on-one between a Member and their Mentor or Council representative.

### Monthly / Lunar

Many traditions mark the moon cycle. A fuller review of the previous moon. Rest with the dark moon. Plant intention with the new moon. Harvest with the full moon. Adapt to your tradition.

### Quarterly: The Solar Cardinals

The four solar cardinals — **Spring Equinox, Summer Solstice, Autumn Equinox, Winter Solstice** — are the entity's quarterly review points, mirroring the superorganism's annual rhythm at [[Appendix C - Founding Phase Timeline]].

A workable cadence:

- **Spring Equinox (~March 20).** Planting. What new initiatives, Quests, or commitments are entering this season? Light review of the previous quarter.
- **Summer Solstice (~June 21).** Annual General Meeting in the federation. Major review. Strategic decisions. Ratifications. Steward elections (where applicable). The year's high-altitude check-in.
- **Autumn Equinox (~September 22).** Harvest. What is being completed this season? What is being released? Light review of the previous quarter.
- **Winter Solstice (~December 21).** Rest and inward turn. Personal Charter revision. Reflection. The year's quiet check-in. Preparation for the next year.

The FSx Individual Charter (Section I) recommends that each Member's annual revision happens at one of the Solstices (typically Summer or the one nearest one's birth). The federated entity's annual revision happens at Summer Solstice, in alignment with the superorganism.

### Annual

The Annual General Meeting (AGM) at Summer Solstice is the year's load-bearing gathering. The AGM receives the Annual Report (with six-capital reporting), audited financial statements, the Switchboard Annual Settlement Report, the Stewardship Report, and Board election results (where applicable). Major decisions are taken. The entity renews its Federation Agreement and its commitment to the Driver for another year.

### Lifetime

At threshold birthdays (decade markers, jubilees) and at major life thresholds (births, deaths, marriages, divorces, illnesses, recoveries), Members are invited to deeper reflection. The Sovereign Individual Charter is a living document; major life thresholds are when major Charter revisions are made, in conversation with Mentors.

## Quest, Circle, Season — and the Seven Moves

The **Quest / Circle / Season** cycle is the operating rhythm at organizational scale, complementing the personal rhythms above. A workable working summary:

- **A Quest** is a bounded shared undertaking, typically 90 days, oriented toward a specific contribution to the Driver. Quests have explicit scope, explicit success criteria, explicit value flows, and explicit completion. They are not open-ended projects.
- **A Circle** is the holon of three to thirteen sovereigns who undertake a Quest together. The same person may participate in multiple Circles undertaking different Quests. Circles dissolve when their Quest completes, or persist if their Quest is renewed.
- **A Season** is the longer arc — typically a year — within which multiple Quests and Circles unfold in coordination. Seasons are bounded by the Solstices.

Working invariant: **work happens in Circles undertaking Quests within Seasons, all in service of the Driver**. This is a simple grammar that scales from a household to a continent.

### The Seven Moves

The LIØNSBERG cross-vault tradition holds the operational gameplay loop as **Seven Moves** — a holofractal sequence repeated by every Circle, every Season, at every scale of the body. The Seven Moves are inner (consciousness) as well as outer (action), and they ascend a spiral: each completion returns the practitioner to Move 1 with deeper eyes:

1. **Wake Up.** Recognize what wants to come through. Refuse to look away. Awakening as the non-negotiable starting point of every cycle.
2. **Form a Circle.** Gather three to thirteen sovereigns in covenant. The irreducible unit carrying the full pattern DNA.
3. **Choose a Quest.** Discern the work. Bound it (typically ~90 days). Define what would constitute genuine contribution to the Driver.
4. **Do the Work.** Execute. Keep promises. Track the gap between what we said we would do and what we actually did. Integrity is built in that gap.
5. **Share Your Stories.** Testimony as the primary carrier of the Pattern. Stories inspire, attract resources, teach systems. Tell what happened — honestly, with the difficulties named, not only the wins.
6. **Learn and Improve.** Retrospective: *What worked? What is most important to continue? What did not? What do we change?* Continuous improvement as spiritual practice.
7. **Pass the Flame.** Mature Circles identify and form new Circles. Self-replication is encoded in the structure, not external mandate. Then return to Move 1, deeper.

Each Move is a discrete practice. The full cycle is what a Circle holds across a Season. **Every fractal of the body — individual, Circle, operating entity, sub-fund, Federation Node, bioregion, the Holding Company itself — is invited to repeat this rhythm at minimum every Solstice and Equinox**, in the form of omnispection (below). This is what keeps the body coherent across thousands of distributed actions; the Seven Moves are the shared grammar by which any part of the body can be heard by any other part.

### The Weekly Cycle Within a Quest

Within a Quest, a workable weekly rhythm at Circle scale:

- **What** *should* **we be doing?** — Honest naming of what the work calls for in this period.
- **What** *can* **we do?** — Honest accounting of capacity, constraints, and conditions.
- **What** *will* **we do?** — Free commitment by each Member to specific actions in the coming week.
- **What** *did* **we do?** — At the next gathering, honest reporting on what was actually completed.

The gap between *will* and *did* is where the body learns. The percentage of promises kept becomes a quietly tracked measure of integrity at the Circle scale. The practice is not punitive; it is developmental.

### Omnispection — The Seasonal Practice of Working from Beyond

At each solar cardinal — **Spring Equinox, Summer Solstice, Autumn Equinox, Winter Solstice** — the body conducts **omnispection**: a 360-degree review across all dimensions of the work, undertaken in the deeper mode of [[0 - How to Read This|Working from Beyond]].

What happens in omnispection:

- **The Circle pauses.** Active work is suspended for the omnispection period (typically a half-day to two days, depending on scale).
- **All voices are heard.** Constituencies of self, Constituencies of the body, Mentors, Council of Peers, the Stewardship Constituency, the more-than-human kin where the practice supports it.
- **Stories are shared.** Not just metrics. Testimony of what happened — wins, failures, surprises, things the body noticed about itself.
- **Quests are reviewed.** What was attempted? What was completed? What did we learn? What is being released? What is being carried forward?
- **Resources are reviewed.** Six-capital reporting, TEA Ledger, Pool flows, Federation Agreement performance.
- **The body inquires from beyond.** Held silence. Open dialogue. Listening for what wants to come through that none of the participants would have arrived at alone.
- **The next Season is shaped.** Quests are chosen for the coming Season. Roles are reviewed. Charters are updated as appropriate. Mentors and Council are recommitted.

Omnispection is held by every fractal — FSx Individual with their Mentors, each Circle, each operating entity, each sub-fund, the Resourcing Pool, the Ecosystem Holding Company. The same rhythm at every scale. Stories shared up; sensemaking back down. **Sensemaking, navigation, and measurement** are most fully alive when they happen at this seasonal cadence across the whole federation.

## Mentor and Council Relationships Across Time

The Mentor / Council of Peers relationship constituting each FSx Individual (per [[VIII - The FSx Individual Template]] §2 and [[1 - Becoming - The Sovereign Individual]]) is not a one-time formality. It is a relationship that is *lived* across seasons.

Working practice:

- **Quarterly check-ins.** At each solar cardinal, the FSx Individual convenes their Mentors / Council for a structured check-in. What has changed in the Member's life and work? What objections or concerns are surfacing? What development is being asked for?
- **As-needed escalation.** When a decision exceeds the Member's unilateral threshold (per Section E of their Charter), Mentors are convened to participate in IDM on the decision. Mentor consent is constitutive, not advisory.
- **Conflict moments.** When the Member finds themselves in conflict they cannot resolve through direct engagement, Mentors are the first escalation point.
- **Major Charter revisions.** Self-Driver, Personal Stewardship Principles, Asset Lock fraction — none of these are revised without Mentor consultation.
- **Incapacitation or transition.** If the Member is incapacitated or transitions, Mentors carry the FSx Individual forward — through evolved continuation, through orderly dissolution, or through transition to successor stewardship per the Member's expressed wishes.

Mentor relationships evolve. A Mentor may step out of the role with mutual consent at any time; new Mentors may be added. The constitutive multi-voice requirement is maintained throughout — at no point may the FSx Individual revert to a single voice without losing FSx standing.

For organizational-scale FSx instances (Layer VII), the analogous structures are the Stewardship Constituency (Class A), the Stewardship Council (long-horizon), the Mediation Council (conflict-specific), and Constituency Representatives (within-Constituency voices). These are the entity's distributed-mentorship infrastructure.

## Conflict Transformation in Lived Practice

Conflict will arise. The pattern is not designed to eliminate it (and could not). The pattern is designed to *transform* it — to use the friction of disagreement as a forge for clarity, repair, and growth, rather than as a wound that festers.

The escalation ladder per [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §13 and [[VII - The FSx Organism Template]] Articles 49–50:

1. **Direct Resolution.** Parties involved attempt resolution through direct, respectful conversation. Most conflict resolves here when the conditions for honest speech are kept (presence, attention, willingness to be wrong).
2. **Mediated Support.** A neutral third party (an elder, a Mentor, a Steward not involved in the conflict) facilitates a conversation between the parties. Mediation is voluntary and non-binding.
3. **Mediation Council Review.** A formed Council of Members not directly involved hears the matter, reviews perspectives, and proposes a path toward resolution. Still non-binding.
4. **Internal Binding Arbitration.** If the Mediation Council cannot find a resolution agreeable by all parties, the parties may agree to an internal binding arbitration by a mutually respected Steward or council of Stewards.
5. **Independent Binding Arbitration.** Final and binding arbitration administered by a recognized independent arbitral institution if the prior steps have not resolved the matter within a defined timeframe (default: 90 days from written notice of the dispute).
6. **External Juridical Process — last resort only**, consistent with the waiver and reserves at §13.5 of the Stewardship Agreement.

A few practitioner notes:

- **Conflict is information.** It usually points to a real misalignment — of roles, of agreements, of values, of capacity. The work is to find what the conflict is pointing at, not to "win" the conflict.
- **Repair over winning.** The goal is right relationship. Where right relationship is no longer possible, clean exit is preferable to slow degradation (per Section H of the Sovereign Individual Charter).
- **Self-examination throughout.** In every conflict, every party assumes they hold some portion of the responsibility, and looks for it honestly before claiming the other's portion.
- **Zero blame environment.** Universal responsibility and accountability. When something has gone wrong, the question is what the system needs to learn, not whose head goes on the spike.
- **Ecclesiastical reserve.** Disputes concerning matters of internal governance, membership, or interpretation of meaning, conscience, or intent are reserved for the superorganism's internal ecclesiastical processes, not external court review (per §13.5(c) of the Stewardship Agreement).

## Succession Beyond Mortality

Every FSx Individual will eventually face the death of its piloting flesh-and-blood person. Every organizational FSx will eventually face the transition of its Founders. The pattern is designed for this — succession is built in, not bolted on.

### Succession of an FSx Individual

When the flesh-and-blood Member transitions:

- The Mentors / Council of Peers are notified per the Member's Charter (Section J — Voluntariness, Entry, Exit, and Transition, which explicitly addresses incapacitation and death).
- The Mentors / Council convene to determine the appropriate transition: continued operation under successor stewardship, orderly dissolution per the Charter's Asset Lock provisions, transition into a memorial or legacy form, or other path consistent with the Member's expressed wishes.
- The FSx Individual's Asset Lock fraction (typically 50–90% of capital, per Section F of the Charter) flows to FSx-compatible bodies serving the same Driver as the Member's Self-Driver.
- The Member's federated standing (Layer II and Layer III) is wound down per the exit provisions of those agreements.
- Honest past contributions (Legacy Pool entries) are not clawed back.

The Member's soul, of course, is none of this. The pattern handles the avatar layer; the soul is whatever the soul is.

### Succession of an Organizational FSx

When the founding generation transitions out:

- Steward terms (seven years, staggered, renewable once for a maximum of fourteen consecutive years per [[VII - The FSx Organism Template]] Article 30.3) ensure that no Steward holds indefinitely.
- The Stewardship Council and the Stewardship Constituency hold continuity even as individual Stewards rotate.
- The Driver Statement, the Stewardship Principles, and the entrenched provisions are preserved across generations by the entrenchment tiers (Special, Entrenched, Absolute).
- Member generations turn over through ordinary entry-and-exit; the entity continues.
- If the entity itself is to merge, dissolve, or transition into a successor body, Special Resolution with unanimous Steward consent governs (per [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §11.2 and §11.3, and [[VII - The FSx Organism Template]] Articles 54–55).

The Stewardship Principle of *Succession* (Principle 13) reads:

> *The Members of the superorganism recognize their own impermanence and commit to proactive succession that preserves its Central Animating Spirit and Driver, whether through continued operation, merger, higher order federation, or dissolution into compatible successor bodies.*

Form is impermanent. Pattern endures. Driver endures.

## Stories as the Resourcing Flywheel

**Stories are the primary infrastructure for moving the pattern across the population**. The federation does not grow primarily by argument, persuasion, or marketing. It grows by the stories its members and Circles tell about what they have actually done — Quests undertaken, Pools built, conflicts transformed, generations sponsored, lands regenerated.

Stories carry the Pattern. They inspire others to begin their own Wake Up (Move 1). They attract resources to ongoing work. They teach the body its own systems by reflecting it back to itself. They make the abstract concrete and the distant immediate. **The Sharing of Stories at Move 5 is not a side activity; it is constitutive of how the body grows.**

In practice:

- Every Quest, on completion, produces a **Story of Transformation** — what was attempted, what happened, what the Circle learned, what is being passed forward.
- Stories enter the **Knowledge Commons** under CC-BY-SA 4.0, accessible to any federated entity, any prospective Member, any human anywhere.
- The Commons becomes, over time, a deep library of *what works in the new pattern* — replacing the conventional case-study and how-to literature that dominates the old paradigm.
- Resourcing flows toward the Circles whose stories demonstrate genuine throughput on the Driver. Investors at Layer IV, Donors at Layer III, Stewardship Council, Switchboard allocations — all of these are informed by stories, not only by spreadsheets.

The flywheel: **better practice → richer stories → more resources and members → better practice**. The body grows itself, by being honest about what it actually does, and by trusting that honest testimony attracts the right participation.

## Pass the Flame — Moving the Whole Population

The most ambitious commitment this body holds, named directly:

> **The whole human population, no one involuntarily left behind, must be able to make the journey from the Old Paradigm to the New within the lifetimes of those alive today.**

That is not rhetoric. It is the actual goal. And it implies an architecture: the body must, over decades, build the **infrastructure, systems, processes, and culture** that make it progressively easier for each next percentage of the population to wake up, federate, and join the work — at whatever pace they can.

What this looks like across generations:

- **The First Few Percent** (where we are now, in the Founding Phase): pioneers, founders, Stewards. These members are constituting the body itself — accepting that the path is harder for them than it will be for those who come later. Their Quests build the load-bearing infrastructure.
- **The Next Few Percent** (a few years in): early adopters, drawn by the early Stories. These members find a body that has already been tested in some directions. The Quests begin to build the on-ramps.
- **The Following Tens of Percent** (a decade in): those for whom the New Paradigm has become recognizable enough to be a real choice. The body's infrastructure (federated companies, Pools, Internal Service Providers, learning programs, retreat infrastructure, dojos in every bioregion) is mature enough to absorb their participation gracefully.
- **The Remaining Population** (across the longer arc): those for whom transition becomes increasingly easy because the body has done the harder work of building the bridges. By this point the on-ramps are ubiquitous, the Stories are everywhere, the alternatives have visibly succeeded, and joining the New is simpler than remaining in the Old.

This is what **Pass the Flame** (Move 7) means at the population scale. Each Circle, on maturity, identifies and forms new Circles. Each operating entity, on maturity, mentors the formation of further entities. Each sub-fund, as it succeeds, capitalizes the next generation of sub-funds. Each Mentor, as their own FSx Individual matures, becomes able to Mentor others into theirs.

The body's success is measured not only by the regeneration it accomplishes, but by **how many people, across how many generations, found their way through it**. *No one involuntarily left behind* is the standard. It is severe. It is the standard the body holds itself to.

The implication for every federated entity: **build the on-ramps**. Whatever your work, hold awareness of how the next person, the next Circle, the next bioregion can join more easily because of what you are building. Pass the Flame is not a separate task; it is the orientation of the work itself.

## Stewarding the Pattern Across Generations

The longest arc this Guidebook contemplates is generations — the work of stewarding the FSx pattern across the lives of those who first instantiated it, the lives of those who carry it forward, and the lives of those who come after.

**Without continuous individual and collective developmental practice across this arc, the body cannot succeed.** This is the most important sentence in this Guidebook. Not the architecture, not the capital, not the brand, not the timing — the *practice*. The Mentor relationships actually held. The Circle gatherings actually convened. The dojos actually opened. The Stories actually told. The sparring actually undertaken. The Working from Beyond actually invited. The omnispections actually conducted. Across decades. Across generations. Without this, every architectural cleverness in the constitutional package will erode under the weight of human drift, fatigue, ambition, and forgetting.

The good news: every generation that does the practice well makes the next generation's practice easier. Mentor relationships become Mentor traditions. Single dojos become networks of dojos. First Circles become bioregional federations. The first hundred FSx Individual become a thousand, then ten thousand, then more. **The infrastructure compounds.** Pass the Flame is not only how individual members reach others; it is how the body itself becomes more capable of welcoming, holding, and developing each next generation.

A few practitioner notes for this longest scale:

- **The pattern is not the brand.** Names will change. The (Currently Nameless) Superorganism will name itself, and its name will probably outlive several iterations of this Guidebook. Names are pointers; the pattern is what they point at.
- **Refinements will happen.** The Guidebook will be revised. The Articles will be amended (within their entrenchment tiers). Standards will be updated. New scales of the fractal will be discovered and instantiated. Older scales will become outmoded and quietly retire.
- **Some things will not change.** The Driver, in spirit. The Stewardship Principles, in essence. The no-extraction principle of the Asset Lock. The right of voluntary entry and exit. These are the absolute root, protected against any process available to the entity. They will outlast every individual reading these words.
- **The work is the test.** No constitutional package, no Guidebook, no operating system can substitute for actual practice. What this Guidebook captures is one tradition's working snapshot of the pattern as it can presently be named. The pattern itself lives in the practice of those who instantiate it.

### Long-Horizon Markers in the Pattern

A few specific long-horizon markers worth holding across generations:

- **The Year-10 Recalibration of Weighted Governance Dynamics** (per [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §5.4 and [[Appendix C - Founding Phase Timeline]]) is the first formal generational milestone. At the tenth Annual General Meeting (Summer Solstice 2036, counted from the First AGM at Summer Solstice 2026), Stewardship Constituency weight reduces from 60 to 40 and the combined Living Systems and Society weights rise from 8 to 15. This distributes governance authority more widely as the superorganism matures. Further recalibrations may follow by Special Resolution at later AGMs.

- **The Eight Domains of Stewardship** (per [[VII - The FSx Organism Template]] Article 10.3(f)) — natural world across generations; organization methodology; evolution; the Driver and Stewardship Principles themselves; financial fiduciary duty; ecosystem and community; communities and regions; adaptive capacity — are the holding domains for the Stewardship Constituency. Across generations, these domains are how Stewardship's long-horizon work is structured.

- **The 12 Pillars of LIØNSBERG and the LIØNSBERG Operating System (LIOS)** (cross-vault, on lionsberg-wiki) are the broader operational frame the federated ecosystem can adopt for system-wide master planning, integrated delivery, and impact across long arcs if it so chooses. LIOS is to operations what this Guidebook is to constitution. Federated entities may adopt LIOS in part or in whole; many will draw on its Quest cycles, Circle protocols, and Season rhythms while developing their own operational specifics.

- **The Driver Statement, the Stewardship Principles, the Asset Lock, and Voluntariness** are the absolute root. Across generations these may be refined in language but cannot be reversed in spirit. They are the test of whether the body that bears the name in any future generation is still the same body that was born in this one.

# V — The FSx Guild Template

*The pattern by which a craft, skill, vocation, practice, or discipline federates as a Sovereign FSx Guild within the wider superorganism.*

**Status: stub.** This Template is being drafted during the Founding Phase. The structure below names what it will contain. Working circle invited.

---

## What an FSx Guild is

An **FSx Guild** is a vocational federation of FSx Individuals (Layer VIII) and FSx Organisms (Layer VII) who share a craft, skill, or practice tradition, constituted as a sovereign no-owner legal avatar in its own right. The FSx Guild is the body through which a craft tends itself — sets standards of practice, holds shared knowledge and tools, mentors apprentices, witnesses mastery, and represents the craft within the wider federation.

Typical instances:

- A medical or healing practice guild
- A regenerative agriculture guild
- An open-source software guild
- A teaching or pedagogy guild
- A music, storytelling, or ceremonial guild
- A building, design, or making guild

A single FSx Organism (Layer VII) or FSx Individual (Layer VIII) typically belongs to *several* Guilds — every craft they actually practice. Belonging is a lattice, not a tree.

## What this Template will contain (planned)

- **Constituting Articles.** The minimal Articles by which a craft tradition constitutes itself as an FSx Guild.
- **Membership.** How FSx Individuals and FSx Organisms federate in. Apprentice / journey / mastery progressions where appropriate. Dynamic Governance Stake and Dynamic Value Stake at this layer.
- **Superorganism Constituency.** A reserved Member class for the **(Currently Nameless) Superorganism** itself, carrying a default **10% Dynamic Governance Stake** and **10% Dynamic Value Stake** (mirroring the pattern in [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §4.1 Class K, [[IV - The Ecosystem Resourcing Pool Charter]] §4.1 Class J, and [[VII - The FSx Organism Template]] Article 10 Class K).
- **Stewardship of the craft.** How standards of practice, ethics, lineage, and shared knowledge are held and evolved.
- **Witnessing and recognition.** How the Guild witnesses its members' development and represents them to the wider federation.
- **Value flows.** How the Guild participates in the wider value flows of Layers II and III, including any Guild-internal pooling of surplus or risk.
- **Threshold and exit.** How a Guild is constituted, how it federates in and out, and how members move between Guilds.

## Relation to other layers

- **Layer III (Ecosystem Holding):** the FSx Guild federates upward into the planetary spine.
- **Layer IV (Resourcing Pool):** may host or partner with sub-funds investing in or capitalizing the Guild's craft.
- **Layer V (FSx Ecosystems):** Guilds operate across many places; the same Guild may be present in many FSx Ecosystems.
- **Layer VII (FSx Organisms):** organizations carrying a craft federate into the relevant Guild(s).
- **Layer VIII (FSx Individuals):** the practitioners themselves.

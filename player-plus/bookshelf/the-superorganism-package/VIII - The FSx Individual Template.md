# VII - The FSx Individual Template

> *The constitutive instrument by which any individual constitutes themselves as an **FSx Individual** capable of federating into the (Currently Nameless) Superorganism's Ecosystem. The irreducible scale of the FSx fractal — the source of sovereignty, authority, and consent on which every other layer depends. Released to the Knowledge Commons under CC-BY-SA 4.0.*

---

## 1. What This Template Is

This Template is the **seventh and innermost layer** of the (Currently Nameless) Superorganism's constitutional package, and the **first scale** of the FSx fractal — the **FSx Individual**. It is the place where the holofractal pattern reaches the level at which all sovereignty originates, all action begins, and all integrity is finally tested.

- **Layer I** ([[I - The (Eternally Nameless) Superorganism]]) — the underlying ontological reality. The thing that can be named is not ultimately the Thing.
- **Layer II** ([[II - The Stewardship Agreement]]) constitutes the spiritual association.
- **Layer III** ([[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]]) constitutes the federation spine and governance coordination.
- **Layer IV** ([[IV - The Ecosystem Resourcing Pool Charter]]) constitutes the capital coordination layer — the fund-of-funds and internal bank.
- **Layer V** ([[V - The FSx Ecosystem Template]]) — place-based federations.
- **Layer VI** ([[VI - The FSx Guild Template]]) — vocational federations.
- **Layer VII** ([[VII - The FSx Organism Template]]) constitutes each FSx Organism.
- **Layer VIII** (this Template) constitutes the **FSx Individual** — the person who consents, who shows up, who relates, who contributes, and who cannot be subdivided into governance below; constituted with at least two **Mentors** (or a **Council of Peers**) so that agency is distributed from the outset.

The pattern at every other scale is, finally, an emergent property of how individual humans show up and stand before ONE, One Another, and All in right relationship. Layer VIII is where the pattern lives irreducibly.

This Template is both **personal and constitutive**. It is at once a covenant a Sovereign Being makes with themselves, ONE, and All, and the **gateway instrument** by which an individual becomes eligible to federate into the economic ecosystem. Membership in Layer II (the Stewardship Agreement) is open to any individual sovereign being who consents. Federation under Layer III requires this Charter to be signed and constituted with at least two Mentors per §2 below — the multi-voice requirement is what makes the individual an **FSx** rather than a single-voice legal person, and is the structural reason why federation is sovereign-to-sovereign throughout (see [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §3.3 — the Threshold of Federation).

## 2. The Multi-Voice Requirement (Constitutive)

An FSx Individual is, by constitution, **multi-voice**. The individual is the Primary Voice. At least **two further voices** — Mentors, or a Council of Peers — are constituted alongside the Primary Voice from the outset. This is not optional guidance; it is what makes the resulting legal person an FSx with agency independent of any single party.

**Mentors / Council of Peers.** The recommended form is **two Mentors** (or a small Council of Peers, typically three to seven), each consenting to the role in writing alongside the Primary Voice's signature on this Charter. Mentors should generally:

- Hold a level of consciousness and capacity to hold complexity recognizably above the Primary Voice's level, sufficient to distinguish self from other and to act selflessly in service of the whole.
- Be willing and able, in the event of the Primary Voice's incapacitation, to transition functions and to evolve or dissolve the FSx Individual in a responsible way for the good of the whole.
- Be able to register an Objection that the Primary Voice cannot override unilaterally — see §E (Decision Protocol) and §N (Signature) below.

In contexts of extraordinary rarity where individual Mentors are difficult to find, the Mentorship role may be delegated to a **Council of Peers** itself constituted as a 2nd-Order FSx. These would be akin to a small wise council, nearly always able to act together with consensus for the good of all.

**Why this is constitutive.** Without the multi-voice requirement, the individual would be a single-voice legal person — and a single-voice legal person cannot federate, because it lacks the distributed agency the FSx pattern requires (per [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §3.3). The multi-voice requirement is also what makes the FSx Individual **mortality-tolerant** (Mentors carry continuity), **coercion-resistant** (no one can be forced into agreements without Mentor consent), and **developmentally accountable** (Mentorship is built in, not bolted on). One pattern, several issues solved at once.

## 3. How To Use This Template

This Template is a **fillable scaffold**, not a finished document. Each Section presents:

- A **prompt** — a question the Sovereign Being is invited to answer in their own words.
- A **default** — a "good enough for now, safe enough to try" version drawn from the holofractal pattern, which the Sovereign Being may adopt as-is, adapt, or replace.
- An **example** in italics — illustrative, never normative.

A Sovereign Being completes their personal Charter by working through the Sections in order, answering each prompt for themselves, signing the result, and offering the signed document (or the parts of it they choose to share) to the circles, organizations, and communities they federate with.

This Template can be completed in a single afternoon, and refined over seasons of practice. The first version need not be perfect; **the practice is the point**, not the document. Seasonal revision is expected.

The signed Charter may be:

- **Private** — held by the Sovereign Being alone, perhaps shared with a chosen elder or sponsoring Member.
- **Selectively shared** — specific Sections offered to specific bodies (e.g., the Driver Statement and Stewardship Principles to a circle one is joining; the Compatibility Statement to a federated organization one is contracting with).
- **Public** — offered openly as part of one's contribution to the Commons, as a template and inspiration for others.

## Section A — The Self-Driver

**Prompt.** What do I exist for, in my own most honest naming? What is the work that, when I do it, I feel myself most aligned with the Living Whole?

**Default.**

> *I, [name], exist as a Sovereign Being aboard Earth — co-inheritor and co-steward of one planetary commons. My personal Driver, in this season of my life, is to [unique purpose statement]. I commit to this work in service of Life and Source as a contribution to the regeneration of Earth and the harmonization of all generations of life.*

**Example.** *"I exist to design and steward technologies of regenerative agriculture that allow small farmers to feed themselves, their communities, and their land — in service of Life and Source, and as a contribution to the larger work of healing what industrial agriculture has wounded."*

The Self-Driver is updated as season and capacity evolve. It is not a forever statement; it is a **right-now** statement, made consciously, refined periodically.

## Section B — Roots and Lineages

**Prompt.** What lineages — biological, cultural, spiritual, vocational, intellectual — do I stand on? Whom am I accountable to in the past? Whom am I accountable to in the future?

**Default.**

> *I stand on the lineages of my biological ancestors, of the lands that have fed me, of the wisdom traditions that have shaped me, and of the teachers (named and unnamed) who have transmitted what I now carry. I am accountable to them by carrying their gifts forward with integrity, and I am accountable to those who come after me — at minimum seven generations forward — by handing on what I have received in better condition than I received it.*

**Example.** *"My biological ancestors are [...]. The land that has fed me is [...]. My teachers in the work of [vocation] include [...]. My contemplative tradition is [...]. I name [name(s)] as the elder(s) to whom I owe specific accountability."*

This Section is optional but encouraged. Naming lineage anchors the Sovereign Being in something larger than themselves.

## Section C — The Personal Stewardship Principles

**Prompt.** What are the principles I commit to live by, that I will not violate even when doing so would be expedient? Mine these from the Stewardship Principles at [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §16, from your own moral and spiritual lineage, and from your direct experience of what works.

**Default** (a personal version of the Stewardship Principles, distilled to seven for memorability):

1. **Service to Life.** I act in service of the broader living system, of which I am a participant. I do nothing that I cannot, in good conscience, defend before my descendants.
2. **Sovereignty preserved.** I honor the sovereignty of every other being I encounter, in their own domain. I do not overreach into another's domain without invitation.
3. **Multi-capital flourishing.** I track and tend my Natural, Individual, Social, Intellectual, Manufactured, and Financial capitals (Section F). I do not optimize one at the expense of the others.
4. **Transparency and integrity.** I speak honestly. My word is my bond. Where I cannot share, I say so rather than dissemble.
5. **Commons contribution.** I contribute, by default, to the Knowledge Commons. I do not enclose what I learn from the gift of others.
6. **Nonviolence.** I commit to nonviolence in word, deed, and intent, while asserting my own sovereignty and the sovereignty of those for whom I steward.
7. **Voluntariness and ongoing mutual consent.** I enter every relationship by free consent and respect every other's right to enter and to leave likewise.

**Example.** *"In addition to the seven defaults above, I commit to: (a) keeping a daily contemplative practice; (b) refusing to monetize the [specific] vulnerabilities of others; (c) responding to messages from elders within 48 hours; (d) annual fast / silence / solitude retreat."*

Adapt freely. The number of Principles is less important than that they are internalized, lived, and reviewable.

## Section D — Constituencies of Self

**Prompt.** What parts of me must consent to my major decisions for those decisions to be wholly mine? Naming the parts of self honors the same insight that drives constituencies at upper layers: no part of the whole should be steamrolled by another.

**Default.** Many traditions name four to seven parts of the integrated person. A workable holofractal default:

- **Body.** My physical self — its needs, signals, and wisdom.
- **Mind.** My thinking, reasoning, and analysis.
- **Heart.** My feeling, intuition, and relational sense.
- **Spirit.** My connection to Source, to mystery, to the larger living whole.
- **Will.** My capacity to commit and to follow through.
- **Shadow.** The parts of me I am tempted to disown — which often hold information the rest of me lacks.
- **Ancestors and descendants.** The lineages whose voice I carry into my decisions.

**Practice.** When making a major decision (career change, commitment, relationship, financial commitment beyond a threshold I set in Section F), I check in with each named Constituency-of-Self. If any registers a substantial Objection (in the IDM sense — see Section E), I do not proceed without integrating that Objection.

**Example.** *"My Body says yes to this move. My Mind says yes. My Heart says concern (will I miss my mother). My Spirit says yes. My Will says yes. My Shadow asks: am I running from something? My Ancestors say yes. → I sit with the Heart concern and the Shadow question for one week before proceeding. After that week the Heart concern integrates into a plan to visit my mother monthly; the Shadow question reveals nothing further. I proceed."*

## Section E — Decision Protocol (IDM with Self)

**Prompt.** How do I make decisions, and at what threshold do I escalate to consultation with others?

**Default.**

I make decisions using **Integrative Decision-Making (IDM) with myself and my Mentors / Council of Peers**, mirroring the IDM protocol of [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §11.3. For each significant decision I:

1. **Name the proposal** in clear language.
2. **Round my Constituencies of Self** (Section D): ask each — Agree / Have Concerns / Have Objections.
3. **Integrate Objections** before proceeding. An Objection from any Constituency means the proposal needs revision, not override.
4. **Round my Mentors / Council of Peers** for any decision exceeding my unilateral threshold (see escalation list below). An Objection from any Mentor must be integrated, not overridden.
5. **Hold to a "good enough for now, safe enough to try" standard** — perfectionism is a delay tactic.
6. **Decide and act**, with a follow-up review at a defined interval.

**Mentor consent is required** (not merely advisory) for:

- Major changes to this Charter (Self-Driver, Personal Stewardship Principles, Asset Lock fraction).
- Entry into binding agreements with circles, organizations, or federations.
- Financial commitments above a threshold I set in Section F.
- Federation-level decisions where the FSx Individual acts as an FSx Member of any federated body.

I also escalate to consultation with others (an elder beyond my Mentors, my circle, or the Stewardship Council if I am a Steward) when:

- The decision touches my Personal Stewardship Principles (Section C) and is not clearly resolvable with my Mentors.
- The decision affects others to whom I have made commitments.
- I notice an Objection from my Shadow or Ancestors that I cannot integrate alone.

**Rapid decisions.** When time-critical, I act on best judgment and seek confirmation or correction within 72 hours. If I get it wrong, I name the error openly, repair where possible, and update this Charter to reflect what I learned.

## Section F-bis — Superorganism Dynamic Stake

**Prompt.** What share of my voice and value do I commit to flow back to the body of the whole — the **(Currently Nameless) Superorganism** — and how?

**Default.**

I commit to a **Superorganism Dynamic Stake** at the irreducible scale, mirroring the **Superorganism Constituency** carried by every higher-layer FSx body (Class K at [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §4.1; Class J at [[IV - The Ecosystem Resourcing Pool Charter]] §4.1; Class K at [[VII - The FSx Organism Template]] Article 10):

- **10% Dynamic Value Stake.** A minimum of **10% of my net financial flows** is committed to the (Currently Nameless) Superorganism or to FSx-compatible bodies serving the same Driver as my Self-Driver. This commitment sits inside, not on top of, my Personal Asset Lock (Section F above); my Asset Lock target of 50% to 90% therefore already contains this Stake. The 10% is the floor.

- **10% Dynamic Governance Stake.** I add a **Superorganism Voice** to my Decision Protocol (Section E) — alongside my Constituencies of Self (Section D) and my Mentors / Council of Peers (Section N). The Superorganism Voice may be carried by a Mentor specifically charged with this role, by my Council of Peers acting in this capacity, by my sponsoring Member at Layer II, or by a Stewardship Council nominee, as my Federation Agreement specifies. The Superorganism Voice may register an Objection on any decision that meaningfully affects the body of the whole or my standing within it; an Objection is integrated, not overridden.

Both percentages may be raised by my own discernment. Neither may be lowered below 10% without consultation with my Mentors and review of any Federation Agreement(s) I hold.

The Superorganism Dynamic Stake is what makes the FSx Individual a **Fractal Sovereign** in continuity with every other layer of the lattice. The body of the whole holds voice and share in me at the irreducible scale, as it holds voice and share in every FSx body federated into it. The pattern is complete only when each individual carries it.

## Section G — Relating Protocols

**Prompt.** How do I show up in relationships? What can others rely on from me? What do I expect of them?

**Default.**

When I am in relationship — friendship, partnership, collaboration, federation — I commit to:

- **Presence.** I show up when I have committed to show up. If I cannot, I say so as soon as I know, with as much notice as the situation allows.
- **Honest speech.** I speak honestly, with care for impact. I do not pretend agreement I do not feel; I do not pretend disagreement I do not feel.
- **Reciprocity, not extraction.** I receive as well as give, and I give as well as receive. I notice the balance over time.
- **Repair over avoidance.** When I cause harm — known or noticed later — I name it, repair where possible, and update my practice. I do not hide behind silence.
- **Consent as ongoing.** I check consent again when context changes. Past consent is not perpetual consent.
- **Confidentiality.** What is shared with me in confidence stays in confidence, except where keeping confidence would harm others or violate my Stewardship Principles. In such cases I notify before disclosing.

In return, I expect from those I am in relationship with the same — and I name it explicitly when I do not.

## Section H — Conflict Transformation at Individual Scale

**Prompt.** What is my protocol when I find myself in conflict?

**Default** (mirroring [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §13 at personal scale):

1. **Notice and pause.** Conflict is information, not emergency. I do not act in reactive heat; I name the conflict, breathe, and choose the timeframe for response.
2. **Direct engagement first.** I attempt direct, respectful conversation with the other party, naming what I observed, what I felt, what I need, and what I am asking for.
3. **Mediated support if needed.** If direct engagement does not produce resolution, I invite a neutral third party — an elder, a sponsoring Member, a trusted friend — to facilitate.
4. **Council or arbitration if needed.** For larger conflicts I cannot resolve through (1)–(3), I bring the matter to the relevant Mediation Council or to internal binding arbitration per [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §13.4.
5. **External juridical process only as last resort,** consistent with the waiver and reserves at [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §13.5.
6. **Repair over winning.** My goal is right relationship, not victory. Where right relationship is no longer possible, I commit to clean exit (Section J) over slow degradation.
7. **Self-examination throughout.** In every conflict, I assume I hold some portion of the responsibility, and I look for it honestly before claiming the other's portion.

**Prompt.** What rhythms hold my life in coherence with the larger pattern?

**Default** (mirroring the rhythm of [[Appendix C - Founding Phase Timeline]] at personal scale):

The rhythms are kept whether or not anyone else notices. They are how the Sovereign Being maintains coherence with Self, Source, and the larger pattern.

## Section J — Voluntariness, Entry, Exit, and Transition

**Prompt.** What is my protocol for entering and exiting commitments — to people, organizations, projects, and the superorganism itself? And what happens to the FSx Individual in the event of my incapacitation or death?

**Default.**

**Entry.**
- I enter relationships, organizations, and federations only by **free consent**. I do not consent under duress, manipulation, or because I cannot see another option.
- I enter by signature where signature is appropriate, by clear verbal commitment otherwise. I do not consent ambiguously.
- I require sponsoring relationships — at least one trusted Member — to enter the superorganism per [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §17.

**Exit.**
- I retain the right to exit any relationship, organization, or federation **at any time**, with notice appropriate to the relationship and to the commitments I have made within it.
- Exit is not betrayal. Refusing to exit a relationship that has become harmful is.
- On exit, I honor the obligations I undertook during the relationship, respect confidentiality of what was shared with me, and do not misuse what I received.
- Where possible, I support a smooth transition. Where the exit is in response to harm, I prioritize integrity and safety.

**Incapacitation and Transition.**
- In the event I am incapacitated and unable to act for the FSx Individual, my **Mentors / Council of Peers** (per Section N) are authorized to act on its behalf — to maintain operations, evolve the FSx form, communicate with bodies I have federated with, and wind it down responsibly per my expressed wishes and the Asset Lock at Section F.
- On my death, the FSx Individual persists at my Mentors' / Council of Peers' discretion to: complete obligations entered into in good faith; settle any Legacy Pool entries with federated bodies I belonged to; distribute the Asset Lock fraction (per Section F — minimum 1%, target 50% to 90% of capital) to FSx-compatible bodies serving the same Driver as my Self-Driver; and dissolve in due course.
- Honest past contributions (TEA Ledger, Legacy Pool entries, Knowledge Commons contributions) are not clawed back by my death.
- My Mentors act in these moments with the constraint that they uphold the principles, rhythms, and Asset Lock provisions I have set out elsewhere in this Charter, and within the spirit of the Driver Statement of [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §2.3.
- I may, optionally, name **specific successor preferences** in this Section — preferred causes for Asset Lock distribution, preferred timeline for dissolution, named persons or bodies who should be consulted, language to be used in any public memorial — and my Mentors will honor these as the spirit of my expressed wishes.

# COMPATIBILITY WITH UPPER LAYERS

A Sovereign Being who has signed this Charter as the Primary Voice, with at least two Mentors (or a Council of Peers) consenting per Section N, is **constituted as an FSx Individual** and is **automatically compatible** with the (Currently Nameless) Superorganism's three upper layers, provided the contents of their Charter do not contradict:

- The Driver Statement of [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §2.3.
- The Stewardship Principles of [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §16.
- The Asset Lock principle of [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §10.2.
- The Voluntariness commitments of [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §3 and §19.

A signed Charter under this Template, together with the Mentor / Council of Peers signatures and a sponsoring Member, is sufficient basis for the FSx Individual to:

- Sign the Stewardship Agreement at Layer II (per [[II - The Stewardship Agreement]] §17).
- Federate economically at Layer III (per [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §3.3 — the Threshold of Federation; this Charter is the structural instrument that satisfies the Threshold for individuals).
- Become a Member, Director, Steward candidate, or other constituent of any Layer VII FSx Organism (per [[VII - The FSx Organism Template]] Article 10).
- Place capital with the Ecosystem Resourcing Pool at Layer IV (per [[IV - The Ecosystem Resourcing Pool Charter]]) as a Class C Investor-Individual FSx Member, where capital placement is part of the Member's intention.

Federation at each layer additionally requires the layer-specific instrument (Stewardship Agreement signature, Federation Agreement, Member Agreement) at that layer.

A Sovereign Being who signs this Charter without Mentors (a "Self-Charter") may participate in Layer II (the spiritual association) and may engage with the wider FSx ecosystem in informal capacity, but is **not yet constituted as an FSx Individual** and therefore cannot federate at Layer III until the multi-voice requirement of §2 is met. The Self-Charter is a meaningful first step on the developmental path toward full constitution.

**Circles and organizations** considering admitting a Sovereign Being as a Member or collaborator are encouraged to ask for the Compatibility Statement (Section K) at minimum. Asking for the full Charter is intrusive and not appropriate; asking for the Compatibility Statement is reasonable.

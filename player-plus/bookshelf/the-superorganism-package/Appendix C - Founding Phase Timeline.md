# Appendix C - Founding Phase Timeline

> *The year-long arc from provisional constitution at the First AGM to ratified Articles at the Second AGM. Marked by the solar cardinals — solstices and equinoxes — so that the body's rhythm tracks the Earth's.*

---

## Overview

The **Founding Phase** soft-launches in **April 2026** with initial circulation, incorporation, and the convening of the founding circle; formally opens with the **First Annual General Meeting at Summer Solstice 2026**, where the interim Stewardship Agreement and Ecosystem Holding Charter are provisionally constituted; and closes with the **Second Annual General Meeting at Summer Solstice 2027**, where the refined Articles are ratified, the Founding Stewards are installed, and the body enters its established annual rhythm.

This timeline is a draft. Specific dates, events, and milestones will be refined through the Founding Phase itself — the timeline is, like everything else in this package, open to guidance.

## The Specific Milestones

### Soft Launch (April 2026 → Summer Solstice 2026)

- **April 2026**: this constitutional package circulated to founding circle and named recipients. Initial responses gathered. Key questions surfaced. Founding Members confirmed. Ecosystem Holding Company incorporated in primary jurisdiction; Stewardship Agreement opened for sponsored signatures.
- **April–May 2026**: federation, fund of funds, and fellowship programs structured. New federated companies formed for initial pilots (villages, shelter manufacture, technology, risk separation). Buckminster Fuller Institute, Evolutesix, LIØNSBERG confirm fiscal hosting and structuring support.
- **May–June 2026**: 100 hours from Grant Management Associates initiate the first ecosystem fundraise. Multiple fellowship programs begin resourcing key contributors. The first tenth of time, energy, and resource flows pooled to strengthen the commons.

### First Annual General Meeting — Summer Solstice 2026

- **Summer Solstice 2026 (~June 21)**: **First Annual General Meeting**. Founding Phase formally opens.
    - The Driver Statement is read aloud at the opening.
    - The interim Stewardship Agreement is **provisionally constituted**.
    - The interim Ecosystem Holding Charter is **provisionally constituted**.
    - Provisional signatures are gathered from Founding Members and initial Founding Individual Members.
    - The provisional Stewardship Council is convened. Founding Phase governance bodies are stood up in interim form.
    - The year-long deliberation, refinement, and wisdom-gathering process is opened.
- **June–August 2026**: package circulated more widely, including to the constellation in [[Appendix B - The Constellation]] and beyond. Wisdom-gathering process is in active flow.

### First Integration — Autumn Equinox 2026

- **Autumn Equinox 2026 (~September 22)**: first integration deadline. Guidance received by this date is integrated into the version of the Articles that goes to deeper deliberation through the winter.
- **Autumn 2026**: counsel review begins for both Layer II and Layer III in the primary US jurisdiction, with parallel-form scoping in EU and UK.
- **Autumn 2026**: founding Stewards' nomination process opens, drawing from candidates meeting the eligibility requirements at [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §4.2.

### Deep Deliberation — Winter Solstice 2026

- **Winter Solstice 2026 (~December 21)**: mid-Phase reflection and recommitment. The body pauses to receive what has been heard and to test what has been drafted against it.
- **December 2026 – February 2027**: deep deliberation. Major proposed amendments worked through. Stewardship Principles tested against difficult cases. Weighted Governance Dynamics, Capital Gains Dynamics, and Dividend Sharing Dynamics stress-tested.
- **January–February 2027**: founding Stewards confirmed by the Founding Members per [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §4.2.

### Final Draft Window — Spring Equinox 2027

- **Spring Equinox 2027 (~March 20)**: final integration deadline. Last guidance integrated. The Articles enter their final drafting window.
- **March–May 2027**: final counsel review. Founding Member consent confirmed. Federation Agreements with founding Organization Members executed in provisional form, ready for ratification at the Second AGM.
- **April–May 2027**: Second Annual General Meeting agenda prepared. Notice given to all Members per quorum requirements.

### Second Annual General Meeting and Ratification — Summer Solstice 2027

- **Summer Solstice 2027 (~June 21)**: **Second Annual General Meeting**. Founding Phase closes.
    - The Driver Statement is read aloud at the opening.
    - The **ratified Stewardship Agreement** is adopted.
    - The **ratified Ecosystem Holding Charter** is adopted.
    - The Founding Stewards are installed (each reading the Driver Statement at installation).
    - The First Executive Board is elected per [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §5.3.
    - The first full Switchboard Annual Settlement Report is received.
    - The first Stewardship Report is received.
    - The first Federation Agreements are signed in their ratified form.
- **Solstice 2027 onward**: established annual rhythm begins. Subsequent AGMs at each Summer Solstice. Mid-year integration at each Winter Solstice. Deep deliberation between Equinoxes.

## Annual Rhythm Beyond the Founding Phase

After the Founding Phase closes, the body's annual rhythm follows the solar cardinals:

- **Summer Solstice**: Annual General Meeting. Driver read. Reports received. Major resolutions decided. Executive Board elections (staggered).
- **Autumn Equinox**: First quarterly review. Switchboard interim settlement. Constituency gatherings.
- **Winter Solstice**: Mid-year reflection. Stewardship Council deep dialogue. Constituency Resolutions consolidated.
- **Spring Equinox**: Quarterly review. Proposal pipeline for the AGM consolidated.

Within each quarter, the Steward Assembly, Steward Council, Mediation Councils, and Constituency structures meet according to their own cadences as specified in the Standards (DS-1, GS-1).

---

## The Year-Ten Recalibration

At the **tenth Annual General Meeting (Summer Solstice 2036)** — counted from the First AGM at Summer Solstice 2026 — the **Year-10 Recalibration** of Weighted Governance Dynamics takes effect (per [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §5.4). The recalibration distributes governance authority more widely as the superorganism matures, lowering Stewardship Constituency weight from 60 to 40 and raising the combined Living Systems and Society constituencies from 8 to 15.

Further recalibrations may occur by Special Resolution.

---

## What This Timeline Is Not

This is not a project plan. It does not specify deliverables, owners, or dependencies. It is a **constitutional rhythm**: the cadence at which the body recalls itself to its purpose, refines its agreements, and renews its commitments.

The work of the body — building villages, restoring ecosystems, manufacturing shelter, federating companies, coordinating bioregions, gathering elders — happens within and through this rhythm but is not bounded by it. Each federated entity at Layer IV has its own work, its own pace, its own deliverables. The annual rhythm at Layer II and Layer III is the breathing of the whole.

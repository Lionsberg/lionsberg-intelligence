# IV — The FSx Ecosystem Template

*The pattern by which a place-based body — a village, a neighborhood, a watershed, a bioregion, a people — federates as a Sovereign FSx Ecosystem within the wider superorganism.*

**Status: stub.** This Template is being drafted during the Founding Phase. The structure below names what it will contain. Working circle invited.

---

## What an FSx Ecosystem is

An **FSx Ecosystem** is a place-based or community-based federation of FSx Organisms (Layer VII), FSx Individuals (Layer VIII), FSx Guilds (Layer VI), and other consenting sovereign bodies, constituted as a sovereign no-owner legal avatar in its own right. It exists where a local body wants to weave itself together more tightly than membership in the planetary Ecosystem Holding (Layer III) alone provides — sharing more value and more risk, holding common assets, exercising local self-governance, and presenting a coherent body to the wider federation.

Typical instances:

- A village or town
- A neighborhood or urban community of ~10,000 (Pattern Language scale)
- A watershed, valley, or bioregion
- A tribe, nation, or peoples organizing into the federation on terms continuous with their own sovereignty

A single FSx Organism (Layer VII) may belong to *several* FSx Ecosystems simultaneously — for instance, the bioregion it inhabits, the local community it lives in, and a regional council it participates in. Belonging is a lattice, not a tree.

## What this Template will contain (planned)

- **Constituting Articles.** The minimal Articles by which a place-based body constitutes itself as an FSx Ecosystem.
- **Membership.** How FSx Organisms, FSx Individuals, FSx Guilds, and other consenting bodies federate in. Dynamic Governance Stake and Dynamic Value Stake at this layer.
- **Superorganism Constituency.** A reserved Member class for the **(Currently Nameless) Superorganism** itself, carrying a default **10% Dynamic Governance Stake** and **10% Dynamic Value Stake** (mirroring the pattern in [[III - The Ecosystem Holding Charter]] §4.1 Class K, [[IV - The Ecosystem Resourcing Pool Charter]] §4.1 Class J, and [[VII - The FSx Organism Template]] Article 10 Class K).
- **Governance.** Council structure, succession, decision domains, and relationship to Layer II (Stewardship Agreement) and Layer III (Ecosystem Holding).
- **Value flows.** How surplus and risk are pooled within the FSx Ecosystem, and how flows to and from Layers II and III work.
- **Common assets.** Land, infrastructure, knowledge, brand, ceremony, and relational capital held by the FSx Ecosystem on behalf of its members.
- **Threshold and exit.** How an FSx Ecosystem is constituted, how members federate in and out, and how the FSx Ecosystem itself enters and exits federation with the wider body.

## Relation to other layers

- **Layer III (Ecosystem Holding):** the FSx Ecosystem federates upward into the planetary spine.
- **Layer IV (Resourcing Pool):** may host or partner with one or more sub-funds aligned with the FSx Ecosystem's character.
- **Layer VI (FSx Guilds):** members of an FSx Ecosystem typically also belong to one or more Guilds.
- **Layer VII (FSx Organisms):** most FSx Ecosystem members are FSx Organisms.
- **Layer VIII (FSx Individuals):** individuals federate directly into FSx Ecosystems where appropriate.

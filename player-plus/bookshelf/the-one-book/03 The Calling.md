# The Calling

*How a whole people is invited, by people it knows, from the old way of living into the new: the universal movement and the promise that nobody is involuntarily left behind; the calling out of those who will do the work; the Invitation and how it is carried; the welcome, the orientation, the connection and the empowerment that follow a yes; why the whole of it is a Game; the diversity it celebrates and the unity and covenant it offers; the story of stories it weaves; and how the calling changes once the first three percent stand. This chapter gives depth to one part of [30 The Strategy and Plan](28%20The%20Strategy%20and%20Plan.md). The rest of that depth lives beside it in chapters 02 to 37.*

---

## Start here

**The challenge.** How can a whole population move from the old way of living, which is wounding the world, to a new way that makes life a little more like Heaven for everyone? A few people cannot carry everyone. Nobody is coming to do it for us. So the work is given to everyone who is ready, and each of them passes it on to the next ones who are ready.

**The promise.** None of us gets there until all of us do. And nobody is left behind against their will. Everyone is invited, nobody is forced, and nobody who wants to come is lost.

**The calling.** There are two doors. The **open door** is for everyone: take up your voice, give what is real for you, and have a live say in what is shared. The **narrow door** is for the people who will lead. Out of the hundred people you know best, you name the three you would most trust to help lead your community through what is coming. When three trusted people have named the same person, that person receives [the Invitation](../the-field/The%20Invitation.md), carried to them by hand. The message is not *come and join us*. It is *you are one of the ones who will do the work*.

**How it spreads.** By attraction, never by promotion. You live the Way, and you say *come and see* to the people who are ready. For the first three percent, every new Player brings their three, people not already playing, within three days of their own yes. Three is the minimum, never a cap. That tactic is for the first three percent only. After that, the calling goes on at the pace of formation: the right people are invited at the right time, as they wake up and are ready.

**What happens after a yes.** You are welcomed before you are handed anything. One teaches one. The person who invited you is your [Home Cell](../the-field/Home%20Cell.md) — where your TEA is witnessed, your questions answered, your wellbeing cared for. An agent can guide the first steps. Questions climb a short ladder. A Pledge, a Cell and a Quest follow, and then the Flame is passed. It takes about three years to feel fully at home in the new, and all of those years are spent building.

**Why it works.** Because it is a Game. It is simple enough for a child, serious enough for a civilization, played at kitchen tables, and kept in time by the sky. It welcomes every people, every tongue and every tradition into one family of families, bound by a covenant freely entered. And its stories, told honestly, do the inviting that no argument can do.

## I. A universal movement

### The challenge in one question

Everything in this plan comes back to one question, so it is worth asking plainly:

*How does everyone — not a few, not the ready, everyone — cross from ways of living that use up life to ways of living that give it back, and keep making that new way together with the Creator and all Creation?*

The old world is the one we were all raised in: [the Caves](../the-field/The%20Caves.md), where a people is held by the stories it is told. The way out goes past the boundary, through [the Wilderness](../the-field/The%20Wilderness.md) where old habits are unlearned and deeper things remembered, and on toward [the Promised Land](../the-field/The%20Promised%20Land.md). The Promised Land is not a place. It is a way of living, and its ideal keeps moving ahead of us as we get closer. It is never reached once and for all. It is walked toward, a little further each week.

The key word in the question is *whole*. A plan to save the wise, the wealthy, the ready or the like-minded would be a much easier plan, and it would be a lie. This is a **universal movement**. It is universal in its invitation, which goes to every person on Earth. It is universal in its concern, which covers every person, family, community, bioregion and creature. And it is universal in its method, because the same simple pattern works in a village, a city, a nation and a planet. The answer to the challenge begins with a calling.

### None of us until all of us

Why everyone, and not just the ready? The reason is not sentiment. It is how things are.

We look like separate people, and we are not. Hurt a hand and the whole body feels it; that is as true of a people and a planet as of a person ([None Of Us Until All Of Us](../the-field/None%20Of%20Us%20Until%20All%20Of%20Us.md)). Underneath the look of separateness each of us is part of one [ØNE Body](../the-field/%C3%98NE%20Body.md), sharing one small world, headed wherever we are headed together. The Earth's air, water, soil, energy and living microbes move as one system, so what is poured into a river here arrives in a child's lungs somewhere else. Harm done to another, or to the living world, comes home to us — not as a metaphor, but as chemistry and weather and grief.

Two practical conclusions follow. First, the crisis is a crisis of crises: every major system is failing at once and each failure feeds the others. So only a widespread, nearly universal shift in how we live can prevent [collapse](../the-field/Collapse.md), and nobody is safe or well during a collapse. Second, in a world armed with nuclear weapons and artificial intelligence, conflict and division have become dangerous to everyone at once. The road forks toward a flourishing we share or a ruin we share, and there is less and less room for anything in between. The patterns of life lead toward [Heaven on Earth](../the-field/Heaven%20On%20Earth.md). The patterns of death lead toward [Hell on Earth](../the-field/Hell%20On%20Earth.md). We can only enter the good future together.

This is why the plan treats *all* as a timetable and not a slogan. The long plan's third marker, *everyone who desires, invited*, exists because of this principle ([24 The Plan](29%20The%20Plan%20%E2%80%94%20the%20first%20three%20percent.md)). The stakes, and the reasons the hour is urgent, are set out in [02 The Moment](02%20The%20Moment.md). This chapter takes them as given and turns to how the invitation actually travels.

### No one involuntarily left behind

Love that has no conditions does not pick favourites. Held up against [Creator's Intent](../the-field/Creator's%20Intent.md) and that kind of [love](../the-field/Love.md), the only aim that fits is that every person finds their way home, and nobody is lost, left to suffer, or left behind who wanted to come. So the work carries a duty that is both moral and spiritual: keep a way open by which All can come back to [ØNE](../the-field/%C3%98NE.md).

Every word of the promise matters.

- **"No one."** The body keeps in mind the wellbeing and growth of every person, family, community and bioregion on Earth. Nobody is too far away, too poor, too wounded, too compromised or too late. That includes the people who served the old order. Honourable exits and a place in building the future are prepared for them, because all of us took part in the old world and the new one needs everyone's gifts ([32 The Turning](05%20The%20Turning%20%E2%80%94%20consciousness%2C%20healing%20and%20wholeness%20at%20every%20scale.md)).
- **"Involuntarily."** Being left behind is never a punishment, and coming along is never forced. A person may say no. A person may say *not yet*. A person may say yes and later leave in peace; [the Way Out](../the-field/The%20Way%20Out.md) is part of what is offered at the door. Love that is compelled is not love, and a way of life accepted under pressure is not the Way. The word *involuntarily* protects each person's [sovereignty](../the-field/Sovereignty.md) as firmly as the word *no one* protects the whole.
- **"Behind."** The door does not close. Whoever was not ready this season may be ready the next, or in ten years, and the way back in stays open.

The promise shows up concretely as an **equal citizenship offered to every inhabitant of Earth**. Nobody is ranked by when they arrived, where they came from, or what they believed before. The road to that [citizenship](../the-field/Citizenship.md) is the open door described below.

## II. The calling of the Ecclesia

### Why a calling, and not an invitation to join

The most important thing in this chapter was learned by trying the other way first.

An invitation to *come and play* or *join* sounds generous, and it quietly casts the one inviting as a host and the one invited as a guest. Guests wait to be served. When the invitation went out in that form, the people who answered it, ready and willing, mostly waited for someone at the centre to run the Game for them. Trace that back and the chain is short. The spread stalled because people waited. They waited because *joining* implies a host. It implied a host because the instruments they met — the site, the book, the board, the guide — had been made by a few for the many, and so taught by their very shape that the few build. And that contradicts the design, which runs on sovereign Cells that keep and multiply themselves; a served guest is not a sovereign Cell. At the root: **nobody had been asked, by name, by people who know them, to lead.**

That is not a flaw in the pattern. It is the pattern showing, early and at small cost, what it needs next ([Every defect is a gift](../the-field/Every%20Defect%20Is%20A%20Gift.md)). So since the Fall Equinox of 2026 the invitation speaks a different grammar. It does not say *come and join*. It says *take up your voice, and call out the ones who will do the work.*

The lesson reaches further than one season. An invitation to *join* gathers **watchers**, who wait for someone else to act. Being told *you are one of the ones who will do the work* gathers **workers**. Nobody named to lead their community through a hard passage believes someone else will do the work. Being chosen as the worker *is* the message. So the calling is both how the pattern spreads and what protects it from growing a centre: it spreads the cure and the immunity at once.

### The Ecclesia

The old word for such a body is *ekklēsia*. In ancient Greek cities it meant the governing assembly: people **called out** of their homes into the public square to decide and to do the work of the city. Only later did the word narrow to something merely religious. [The Ecclesia](../the-field/The%20Ecclesia.md) is that first meaning recovered. It is a working assembly of the people, called out by the people. It is not an audience, a membership or a following. It is the body that does the work ([The Calling of the Ecclesia](../the-field/The%20Calling%20of%20the%20Ecclesia.md)).

### Two doors, both always open

**The open door is for everyone.** Every person on Earth is invited to take up their voice. They can resonate with what they find, contribute whatever is real for them in any amount, and so hold a **live voice in governance** and a **live stake in value**. Nobody is turned away. This is the door of self-selection, and in time it leads to full citizenship for all.

**The narrow door is for those called out to lead.** The first act of every voice is to **name**: out of the hundred people you know and trust most, the three you would most trust to help lead your people and community through the crossing ahead. When three trusted namings surface the same person, that person receives the Invitation, delivered in person by the people who named them. The Flame passes from hand to hand. The person accepts or declines, and either answer is sovereign. Those who accept walk the same moves as every other Player.

The two doors do not compete. The open door keeps the body universal. The narrow door makes sure the people carrying the heaviest work are the people their neighbours already trust.

### The nomination laws

Anything that gives someone standing will be gamed. So the naming follows six simple laws, and each one guards against a known way that choosing goes wrong ([The nomination laws](../the-field/The%20nomination%20laws.md)):

1. **You cannot name yourself, and be wary of anyone campaigning to be named.** Wanting the standing is the most reliable sign of being unfit for it.
2. **Not immediate family or romantic partners.** Love is not an assessment.
3. **Not celebrities, politicians, or anyone you do not personally know.** Fame records being watched, not being trustworthy.
4. **Name people you have personally watched carry hardship faithfully,** for three years or more if you can. Character in easy times tells you little. Only time shows whether a person keeps their word when it costs them.
5. **If you cannot think of three people you would trust to lead through a crisis, the work is to go and find them.** The gap is itself a finding.
6. **Your namings may be delegated,** in whole or in part, to someone whose judgment of people you trust more than your own. Being humble about your own eye is itself a skill ([Delegation](../the-field/Delegation.md)).

Two more guards stand behind the six. Namings are **weighted by believability**, meaning the namer's own record of commitments kept, so organized capture buys little. And because trust networks mirror whoever starts them, the calling **deliberately reaches past its founders' own kind**, across languages, lands, traditions and generations, until the people called out look like the Earth and not like the first room.

What the laws produce is not a ballot. A ballot asks strangers to rank strangers. This asks neighbours to name neighbours, on evidence they actually hold.

### Naming your three, and bringing your three

The calling has two acts in it, and they are kept distinct:

- **Naming your three** is the nomination: the three people you would most trust to help lead your community, named within two days of your own yes.
- **Bringing your three** is passing the Flame: three people not already playing, invited to become Players, for the first three percent within three days of your own yes.

They may be the same people, and often are. But they are different acts with different laws. Family cannot be named, because love is not an assessment. Family can certainly be *brought*, because the Invitation always travels best through someone who loves you. Every person on Earth is to be invited, in the end, by someone they know and love.

### What the calling creates

**The first three percent, found by the whole.** [The first three percent](../the-field/The%20First%20Three%20Percent.md) do not appoint themselves and no centre chooses them. The other ninety-seven percent bring them to the surface through their trust: roughly [three hundred](../the-field/The%20300.md) in every community of ten thousand, recognized by their own neighbours. A people that has called out its own leaders cannot be beheaded, bought, or fooled by anyone naming leaders for it.

**The trust graph.** The web of namings adds up to something nobody designed: a living map of [trust](../the-field/Trust.md) across a waking population. It is the body's treasure, and in hostile hands it would be a map for hunting it. So it is guarded in tiers from before the first thousand names exist. There is a public tier of counts, Cells, Quests and stories. There is a recognized tier, which Cells share with one another only by mutual consent. And there is a private tier, the person-graph itself, held locally by the people it names, who can export or delete it. Beneath every digital layer runs the **analog contact-tree**: each person knows their three and is known by theirs, in memory and on paper, and no seizure can reach it ([The Trust Graph and the analog contact-tree](../the-field/The%20Trust%20Graph%20and%20the%20analog%20contact-tree.md), [Selectively Permeable Membrane](../the-field/Selectively%20Permeable%20Membrane.md)). A name crosses from the private register to anything public only on a recorded yes. The full treatment of the membrane is in [24 The Plan](29%20The%20Plan%20%E2%80%94%20the%20first%20three%20percent.md).

**The inverted grammar.** Being named to lead is the whole content of the message, so the calling cures dependence on a centre in the same motion that it spreads.

### From calling to governance

The Ecclesia governs itself from the first Cells outward, by structural triggers and not by dates. The markers serve the work; the work does not serve the markers. When the first 144 Co-Creators stand, at about Cycle 10 of the [Cycles of Growth](../the-field/LI%C3%98NSBERG%20Cycles%20of%20Growth%20and%20Cycle%20Time.md), they name five to ten respected representatives to seed the joint planning of the whole ([The System Wide Master Planning Group and Process](../the-field/The%20System%20Wide%20Master%20Planning%20Group%20and%20Process.md)). As the next rings complete, [the first general assembly](../the-field/The%20LI%C3%98NSBERG%20General%20Assembly.md) convenes, refines and ratifies the strategy, and the new vision is carried to everyone. From then on the *how* belongs to the people who were called out. How that body is shaped and how it plans is in [26 The Body and Its Planning](26%20The%20Body%20and%20Its%20Planning.md). The thresholds, as bets, are in [30 The Bets and the Stages](30%20The%20Bets%20and%20the%20Stages.md).

### Two callings, one word

The word *calling* is used in two senses in this Book, and each deepens the other. [The Calling](../the-field/The%20Calling.md) in its oldest sense is a person's own vocation: the particular work that is this life's to do, recognized more than chosen, coming from within. The calling of the Ecclesia comes from outside: neighbours recognize in you something you may not yet see in yourself. The two meet at the narrow door. A person called out by their neighbours still has to discern whether the work is theirs. The recognition of others is evidence, not a command, and a person's yes or no is sovereign. And a person who already senses a vocation often finds it confirmed, and given somewhere to go, when the people who know them best say the same thing out loud.

### How the calling first moves

The first namings travel through the trust already running between the first Players and the people they know — inside the real flow, never a rehearsal beside it. Whatever instruments exist only point hands at where to reach; the hands do the reaching, and every Invitation is carried by a person. The first to be called form Cells, choose Quests, become visible on the shared board, and tell their stories, and the next namings ride on what those stories show. The centre may equip; it never operates. If every shared instrument vanished tonight, a body whose Book is in every hand, whose people know their three, and whose Cells govern themselves would go on.

### The calling persists

Given the noise and wounds of the old world, a person may need to be invited **as many as twenty times**, by different voices in different seasons, before they can really hear, pay attention and respond. The body prepares for that and keeps inviting. Nobody is written off for not answering. Ninety-nine in a hundred will not hear the whisper the first time, and that is not failure. Each will hear at the right time for them.

## III. The Invitation, and how it is carried

### How it reaches a person

[The Invitation](../the-field/The%20Invitation.md) comes in more than one way, and it may already have reached someone several times without their noticing. It can be a **link or a page from a friend**: something shared by a person who thought of them. It can be **a word at a table**: a conversation that goes deeper than usual, where someone names what both people have been feeling. Or it can be **a nomination**: people who know them well, naming them as one of the few they would trust to help lead. However it arrives, in the end it is the same Invitation, and it is always personal. The person is not being recruited by strangers. They are being recognized by [kin](../the-field/Kinship.md).

It travels as a hand, never as a broadcast. Broadcasts and great cultural moments have their place. They can make the body visible to itself and to a world that does not know it exists yet, and the plan counts on that ([02 The Moment](02%20The%20Moment.md), [30 The Bets and the Stages](30%20The%20Bets%20and%20the%20Stages.md)). But a broadcast gathers an audience, and a hand gathers a worker. The Invitation itself always passes person to person, at the pace of trust.

What makes an Invitation land is **resonance**, not argument: the Story reads true to the person hearing it, in words they can take in. That is why resonance is watched at every door as closely as any count — the share of those who hear it for whom it rings true — and why, when it falls, the wording and register are changed in the open rather than pushed harder ([Resonance](../the-field/Resonance.md)).

And anyone reading this far has already been reached. This page is the Invitation too: a door held open, and a question put to you.

### Wherever you stand

- **If you are watching,** and something in you already knows the old way is failing: read the Story with one or two others, out loud. That is enough to begin.
- **If you have just arrived:** gather the few people near you who sense what you sense, read together, sit at a table, choose one real thing to do this season, and tell what happened.
- **If you are already playing:** keep deepening your own practice, tell your stories honestly, bring your three, help the next tables get started, and put something real in the middle of your own table.

### Accepting is a shift, and it is completed in action

Saying yes to the Invitation is less like signing a form and more like turning around. Most of us were trained to watch: to wait for permission, for experts, for a better moment, for someone braver to go first. Accepting is the moment a person stops waiting and starts playing — *someone should do something* becomes *I will begin, with the people beside me.*

That is the whole of the reframe. No institution or leader is going to carry this while the rest of us look on. What exists is a pattern anyone can learn, a seed anyone can carry, and a great many hands, yours among them. Being needed is not a weight laid on you. It is a dignity handed back.

A turning that stays inside, though, is only an intention. **Acceptance is completed in action**, in the first small, real, outward step: the message sent, the reading shared, the table set. And it is completed in **a word said aloud**. The person says yes in their own words, to the one who carried the Invitation, where someone can hear it, because a yes that was never said is not a yes. Silence is never agreement, and agreement can be withdrawn ([Consent](../the-field/Consent.md)). The deeper steps come later and in order: [the Pledge](../the-field/The%20Pledge.md), given where others can hear it and written on [the Roll](../the-field/The%20Roll.md) with who brought you and who witnessed; the Cell; the Quest; the first passing of the Flame. Nobody is asked to pledge at the door before they have played a week.

### Attraction, not promotion: *come and see*

When the Invitation lands, the first impulse is to carry it to everyone, urgently. The deepest law of how this Game spreads guards that impulse: **[attraction, not promotion](../the-field/Attraction%2C%20not%20promotion.md)**.

Nobody is argued in, pressured, guilted or sold. Selling is how the old world moves people, and a new world spread by selling would be the old one with a fresh coat of paint. What spreads this is visible change: people who keep their word, who are steadier and kinder than they were a year ago, whose table has done something real. Those who are ready notice, often before a word is said. Those who are not ready are honoured exactly as they are and left in peace until their season comes. What is offered is the simplest invitation people have ever given one another: **come and see.** *Come and see what we are doing. Sit at our table. Read the Story. See for yourself.* It is a door held open, not a demand for a decision.

The hour really is short, and saying so is honest. But urgency that turns into pressure breaks the Invitation. A yes bought with pressure will not survive its first hard season. The discipline is to state the moment plainly once and then be quiet. The body is not looking for people to convince. It is looking for people who are already awake, and they need to be found, not persuaded ([Demonstrate before advocating](../the-field/Demonstrate%20before%20advocating.md), [Propagate; do not scale](../the-field/Propagate%3B%20do%20not%20scale.md)).

What a person meets at the door, rung by rung, and what goes wrong there and how it is repaired, is told in full in [20 The Experience](20%20The%20Experience%20%E2%80%94%20from%20the%20first%20door%20to%20what%20lies%20beyond.md).

### What the one who carries it gives

Speak to people you actually know, at the pace of trust. Give them the whole thing and not a pitch: the Story, one page of the Game, and your honest account of what your table has done. Do not flood them with the whole library; a curriculum at the door is a wall. Tell them the Way Out is open. Then be ready to walk beside them. The one who carries the Flame to someone becomes their [Carrier](../the-field/Carrier.md), and that office does not end.

## IV. Onboarding, orientation, connection, and empowerment

People come to this from every possible context: every nation, faith, class, age, wound and gift. So the welcome has to be deep enough to receive any of them and simple enough that any of them can pass it on. The work has four parts, and they come in order.

### Orientation: greeted before given

Orientation answers four questions in order, and then stops: *where am I, what is this for, who is here, and what is mine to do next?* A newcomer does not need the whole corpus. They need the one thing that makes their first week possible. **A person is greeted before they are given anything**, by a person and not by a document. The words of the Game are handed over one at a time, as the road reaches them, each in one line and linked so it can be looked at again. Nobody is asked to carry a word they have not been given ([Orientation and Onboarding Process](../the-field/Orientation%20and%20Onboarding%20Process.md), [Guide](../the-field/Guide.md)).

The door's only job is to get someone to a table. A door that grows while the tables do not is a number lying to you.

### Onboarding: each one teaches one

Onboarding is **decentralized**. No central desk does it for anyone. The pattern is *each one teaches one*:

- **The one who invited you walks beside you.** They are your [Home Cell](../the-field/Home%20Cell.md). You go through the first steps side by side; you drive, and they help.
- **An agent can guide the first steps.** For those who choose one, a companion agent carries the path and turns an accurate answer into the next plain step. It prepares, drafts, remembers and carries. It never decides, never pledges, and never gives a yes on anyone's behalf. Everything it does can be done by a person with a notebook, only more slowly, and a person with a notebook is a complete player ([25 Players, Agents, and the Intelligence System](25%20Players%2C%20Agents%2C%20and%20the%20Intelligence%20System.md)).
- **Onboarding office hours** are kept regularly for troubleshooting: for the step that will not go, not for the whole road.
- **Questions climb a ladder**, one rung at a time: your own agent first; then the Cells you are part of; then, if the Cell cannot resolve it, a Request For Guidance from the Cell to the wider body, which the RFG Cell receives, the first of the body's [Guide Cells](../the-field/Guide%20Cells.md).

The practice itself, step by step, is in the kit: [Bringing Your Friends In](../getting-started/Bringing%20Your%20Friends%20In.md) and [Asking for Guidance — the Ladder and the Request For Guidance](../getting-started/Asking%20for%20Guidance%20%E2%80%94%20the%20Ladder%20and%20the%20Request%20For%20Guidance.md). Every question answered becomes a tip for the next person, so the road gets shorter each time someone walks it.

### Connection: nobody walks alone

A person is not connected to an organization. They are connected to other people, by name:

- **The Cell**: three to thirteen people, optimally four to seven, who meet weekly and keep their word to one another ([Cell](../the-field/Cell.md)). Who to gather is simple. Look at your actual life. Two to four people, maybe more, already sense what you sense. They do not need to share your politics, religion or background. They only need to share the recognition that something profound is under way, and be willing to show up.
- **The Carrier and the Home Cell**: the Cell that carried the Flame to a new Cell is its first home, the body that hears its season and helps when something is wrong ([Home Cell](../the-field/Home%20Cell.md)).
- **The contact-tree**: each person knows their three and is known by theirs, so the connection survives the loss of every screen.
- **The shared channels**: the wider body talks in the open through channels that move in three steps. First Discord, then Buzz, then Sovereign Comms. Each is tested, and each is retired in turn as the next one proves itself. No channel is the body. If they all went dark, the Cells, the Homes and the contact-tree would still hold it together.

### Empowerment: everything needed, in every hand

In this Game, empowerment means that every Player and every Cell holds what they need to act without asking permission.

**Each Player** holds the Book, which is the whole code, readable at every register: the Story, the compass question, the moves, the practices, the protocols for when the grid is down. They hold the practice of the Cell, a weekly rhythm any kitchen table can run. They hold the chain of people who named and brought them, as living sponsors. They hold a live voice in shared decisions and a live stake in shared value. And, if they choose, a companion agent, which is always optional and never required. The seed travels whole, as plain text, on their own machine and on paper ([Passing the Seed](../keeping-yourself-safe/Passing%20the%20Seed%20%E2%80%94%20Drive%20to%20Drive%2C%20With%20a%20Local%20Model%20Sized%20to%20the%20Machine.md)).

**Each Cell** holds its [sovereignty](../the-field/Sovereignty.md): its own commons, its own agreements, its own legal form or none. It holds a [Quest](../the-field/Quest.md) chosen from the real needs of its place and the work of the twelve Pillars, the seasonal rhythm, and, from its first day, the standing quest of every Cell: **seed a new Cell**. How resources flow to the people doing the work is in [27 Resourcing and the New Economy](27%20Resourcing%20and%20the%20New%20Economy.md). How the work is broken down and delivered is in [24 The Work and the System](24%20The%20Work%20and%20the%20System.md).

### About three years, walked while building

It takes a person, on average, about three years to feel fully embraced, connected, empowered and part of the new: three years of healing, reorientation and formation between accepting the Invitation and taking up full citizenship ([The Three-Year Formation](../the-field/The%20Three-Year%20Formation.md), once called [the three-year lag](../the-field/The%203%20Year%20Lag.md)). The welcome is designed around that honestly. It is not a probation or a waiting room. The three years are walked *while building*, because the building is what forms the builder. That span shapes the long plan. The window of crisis and opportunity, plus the three years of formation for the last people to arrive, together make up [the ten-year grand strategy](../the-field/The%2010%20Year%20Grand%20Strategy.md). The healing itself, on every plane, is the subject of [32 The Turning](05%20The%20Turning%20%E2%80%94%20consciousness%2C%20healing%20and%20wholeness%20at%20every%20scale.md).

## V. A new experience, and why it is a Game

### A new experience, which is a process

A whole people does not cross from one way of life to another because it read something. It crosses because there is a road, and the road is walkable. So the calling leads into a **new experience** — in practice, a process — that takes a person from whatever in the old way harms or holds them back toward their full potential and the joy of making things together. Three things build that road at once: the new norms and values, written down plainly so they can be tried and improved; the bridges, practical and inward, that make each step small enough to take; and the shared strength that shows up when many people think, speak and act in the same Spirit. What that experience is like, from the first door onward, is [20 The Experience](20%20The%20Experience%20%E2%80%94%20from%20the%20first%20door%20to%20what%20lies%20beyond.md).

### Why a Game

This could have been a creed, a manifesto, or a rulebook. It is [a Game](../the-field/The%20Great%20Game%20of%20LI%C3%98NSBERG.md), on purpose.

Nobody is argued out of a wound or lectured into a new way of being; frightened people stop listening. The Story knows this: the ones who came out of the Caves were afraid, and what helped them was not instruction but practice, the way children learn — by [playing](../the-field/Play.md) until the thing is in their hands. So the Way is made of moves, not commandments: small things you do in your own week that teach you the new world by making a piece of it. You do not have to believe first and act later. You play, and the playing changes you into someone who can live the way you are practising. That is how people who have been hurt learn to be well together.

That is what gamification means here. It means finding creative ways to make the liberation and reorientation of humanity playable, so that every family and child can learn to play, find their own purpose and roles, grow toward their full potential, and flourish in harmony. It does not mean points and badges laid over a chore. And the Game is open to everyone: students, families, schools and universities, businesses, non-profits, leaders, and every kind of gathering. Its instruments are cards and pages that a hand copy on any paper serves as well as a printed one. Its technology is optional at every rung, and each rung is a complete place to stand ([Screens and paper](../the-field/Screens%20and%20paper.md), [THE GAME](THE%20GAME.md), [The Little Book of the Great Game](The%20Little%20Book%20of%20the%20Great%20Game.md)).

### The DNA of every transformation that worked

Look at the renewals in history that actually changed their worlds and the same six threads keep showing up. The Game is built from them:

1. **A small committed core that sees clearly** and acts before the majority is ready.
2. **Free people choosing to cooperate** — held together by resonance, not command, so there is no centre whose capture ends it.
3. **The whole work held as one**, because tangled problems slip past partial fixes.
4. **Decisions in the open, power spread out**, because secrecy regrows the very thing a movement set out to replace.
5. **Learning without ego**, where a failure is information.
6. **A seed, not a franchise** — each part carries everything needed to grow the whole again.

A Cell keeping its weekly Turn is those six at the scale of one kitchen table.

### Why it is named, structured, and given a form

Some good people would rather not name anything: *just live well and let it spread.* The trouble is that good principles on their own do not tell two strangers what to do on Tuesday, and they tend to fade when the people who held them are gone. A named form with simple practices gives strangers a shared language, turns intentions into finished work, and survives being copied into places nobody planned for. That is the job the Game does.

Because the form matters, so does every Player's right to judge it. Weigh it by these questions. Does it take the whole into account? Does it work at every scale, from the kitchen to the planet? Is it truly led by the people it serves, captured by no special interest? Does it protect each person's sovereignty while making real unity possible? Can it actually deliver the Goal? And weigh it by its fruit in your own life. Beneath all of that is the deepest promise: *our duty is not to any one system, but to [the Goal](../the-field/The%20Goal.md).* If a better way is found, one that more truly delivers Heaven for All, this one will gladly step aside and take up the better one. There is no pride of authorship here. The Game asks only to be tested, and kept as long as it bears fruit ([Current Best Understanding](../the-field/Current%20Best%20Understanding.md)).

### The moves, one breath

The moves are few and they make one rhythm: **accept the Invitation · form a Cell · choose a Quest · share your stories · pass the Flame.** In the first three percent, a Player counts by what they have done: read [the Story](THE%20STORY.md), taken [the Pledge](../the-field/The%20Pledge.md), [formed a Cell](../the-field/Form%20a%20Cell.md), [chosen a Quest](../the-field/Choose%20a%20Quest.md), and [passed the Flame](../the-field/Pass%20The%20Flame.md) for the first time.

They are not tasks to finish and file away. They are one living breath, repeated and deepening each time. They run daily in small ways, weekly at the table, seasonally in the Quests, and over whole lifetimes in the deepening. And the breath is kept in time by the sky, not by any server that could be captured or switched off. Quests are chosen at the equinoxes and solstices, stories are told at the new and full moons and at the season's close, and at each turning the whole body reads itself. Every Cell in every land reads the same seasons and moons, so the whole body moves in one rhythm without anyone commanding it ([19 Pillar XII](19%20Pillar%20XII%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Time%2C%20Rhythm%2C%20and%20Celebration.md)).

A Cell is alive when its people **show up**, **do real work**, **hold one another** in truth and love, **grow**, and **multiply**. The last of these completes the calling. The mark of a mature Cell is not its size. It is the new Cells it has helped bring to life. A Cell that is truly alive finds, without being told, that each of its members knows two or three people ready to begin.

### The Game does not end

The Game is not scaffolding to be taken down once the hard years pass. When the crossing is behind us, tables will still meet, Quests will still be chosen, stories told and the Flame handed on — no longer to get through anything, simply because this is how a people that has chosen life keeps making life a little more like Heaven. That is also why the calling never fully ends: every generation born is invited in its turn.

## VI. Diversity, unity, and a new covenant

### Celebrating and embracing diversity

The calling is not a request to become alike. Every person, place and people brings something no one else can, and the body is poorer for every voice it flattens. What is asked is harder and better than sameness: **diverse unity** — many, truly different, and one.

That shows up in the practice:

- **A Cell reaches across every line the old world used to divide people.** Its members do not need to share politics, religion or background. They need to share the recognition and the willingness to show up.
- **The calling widens deliberately** past its founders' own kind, across tongues, lands, traditions and generations, until the called-out look like the Earth.
- **The Story is made readable at every gate**, register by register, including a form a parent and a nine-year-old can read aloud together in an evening, and in the languages people actually dream in. The aim is to reach every person, family and people, in time across something like a thousand languages and cultural settings. When a page is translated the words change and the numbering stays, so line 6 is line 6 in every language.
- **The first stewards of every land have an equal seat**, and ancient and indigenous wisdom is honoured and invited to guide the healing of the lands and waters it has long cared for ([14 Pillar VII](14%20Pillar%20VII%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Culture%2C%20Art%2C%20and%20Story.md)).
- **The apparent polarities are reconciled, not collapsed**: masculine and feminine, ancient and modern, East and West, North and South, indigenous and non-indigenous, the centralizing and the decentralizing, the drive to preserve and the drive to explore. Each needs the other ([Apparent Polarities](../the-field/Apparent%20Polarities.md)).

### Unity: one family of families

If we cannot be many and one, we are only many — and a humanity in pieces grinds itself down. So the calling asks us to come home to each other as **one family of families, one tribe of tribes, one body of co-creation**, in [right relationship](../the-field/Right%20Relationship.md) with ØNE, with one another, and with All: a relationship that leaves nobody and nothing out of consideration.

This unity is not sameness, and it is not imposed from above. It is a higher-order functional unity. The parts keep what they are and align freely toward one shared Goal, the way the organs of a body do, under the principle of [subsidiarity](../the-field/Subsidiarity.md): each thing is done at the smallest scale that can do it well.

### A new covenant

That unity is not decreed. It is chosen, one person at a time, **of our own [free will](../the-field/Free%20Will.md) and by [mutual consent](../the-field/Mutual%20Consent.md)**, as a [new covenant](../the-field/The%20New%20Covenant.md) — with ourselves, our families, our peoples, our places, all Creation and the Creator. A contract divides up obligations. A [covenant](../the-field/Covenant.md) changes the relationship between the people who make it: they are no longer bargaining across opposed interests but have decided to be for one another. A Cell usually makes one before it knows the word, in the agreement spoken aloud at its first gathering about how its people will treat one another ([Field of Agreements](../the-field/Field%20of%20Agreements.md)). The New Covenant is the same bond at the threshold of the whole movement, the point where saying yes stops being about one table and becomes about belonging to something that will outlive it.

At its centre is the One from whom the many come. [ØNE](07%20%C3%98NE%20%E2%80%94%20the%20Center.md) — God, the Creator, Ultimate Reality, by whatever name a people has used — is set back at the centre, and love is the ground everything else stands on. Four things are said with that, so the centre can hold every tradition without being owned by any:

- **ØNE is greater than every name for ØNE.** Whatever is wise, right, true and good in each tradition is honoured, and no tradition holds the whole.
- **The journey is shared.** What matters is the walk toward right relationship with the Divine, alone and together; a religion is a way of walking, not the destination.
- **The Divine is met more than it is defined.** It is known the way a person is known, not the way a sentence is proved.
- **Walls made of words come down.** Where a doctrine has been turned into a fence between neighbours, it is laid down, and the neighbours walk on together ([Idolatry (of the word and of the doctrine)](../the-field/Idolatry%20%28of%20the%20word%20and%20of%20the%20doctrine%29.md)).

Nobody is asked to believe anything to enter. The Story can be read as a true myth or as a game we have agreed to play, and what follows is the same either way. And the covenant never overrides the person who makes it. It is entered by assent, never imposed, and consent can be withdrawn. A covenant that cannot be left has become a claim ([01 The Spirit](01%20The%20Spirit.md)).

In the end, *how we do things around here* decides more than any plan does. So the calling also brings back into daily life the values, rights and responsibilities every people already shares underneath its differences, and the rites and traditions that keep a people pointed the right way from one generation to the next.

## VII. A new story of stories

People understand their lives as stories, and a people's deepest answers reach its children as stories long before anyone debates them. So the calling travels inside a story — really, a weave of stories:

1. **One story wide enough to hold everyone**: [THE STORY](THE%20STORY.md) of ØNE and All, the Caves, the Wilderness, and the Promised Land walked toward a little more each week.
2. **The body's own story**, told honestly and brought up to date at every turning.
3. **Your story** — every person, table, place and people telling its own.
4. **The weave** — all those stories, long torn apart, gathered back into one story of stories ([29 The Stories](22%20The%20Stories%20%E2%80%94%20ten%20trillion%20stories%20woven%20back%20into%20ONE.md)).
5. **Stories of change**, told as the work bears fruit; they are how the next people find their way in.

### Celebrate and learn, then tell

When a Quest is complete or a season closes, a Cell first stops and marks it ([Celebrate, Learn, and Improve](../the-field/Celebrate%2C%20Learn%2C%20and%20Improve.md)). It **celebrates**, because a people that does not celebrate its victories will not have the heart for the long road. It **learns**, looking back honestly at what worked, what did not, and what the gap between promise and result has to teach. It **improves**, folding the lesson into the next cycle. *Then* it tells the story, because what has been learned from is worth hearing, and what has not is only noise.

### What a told story carries

A plain, true story of real work done carries four things at once:

- **Evidence.** Plenty of voices will say this cannot work. A finished Quest, told plainly, turns *could it?* into *it did.*
- **Invitation.** Someone who would never be talked into anything hears what a table actually did and wants to be part of it. The story invites where no pitch can.
- **Coordination.** A body without a commander still needs each part to know what the others are learning. Stories passed sideways, table to table, are how it knows — a nervous system with no head to capture.
- **Promise.** Something small, done and told truly, makes the larger thing believable. One vacant lot become a garden says every lot could. One family fed says no family has to go hungry. Each story points past itself to what comes next.

The practice is simple and rhythmic. At each new moon and full moon, and at each season's close, the Cell tells what happened, in its own voice, without performance or exaggeration. It tells the failures too: a body that only hears its wins stops learning, and a stumble honestly told saves the next table the same stumble. And every story has to be true. Surrounded by stories made to sell, a plain account of real work is rare enough to be heard ([Share the Stories](../the-field/Share%20the%20Stories.md), [Story](../the-field/Story.md)).

## VIII. The calling by phase

The calling does not have one tempo. It has one purpose, and its pace is chosen by wisdom for each stretch of the road. The measure throughout is the Fibonacci rings of the [Cycles of Growth](../the-field/LI%C3%98NSBERG%20Cycles%20of%20Growth%20and%20Cycle%20Time.md), read daily as the ring the body stands on and the days each ring takes. Ring 40, 267,914,296 people, is the first three percent. The full arithmetic, the chart and the sensitivities are in [30 The Strategy and Plan](28%20The%20Strategy%20and%20Plan.md) and [24 The Plan](29%20The%20Plan%20%E2%80%94%20the%20first%20three%20percent.md). What follows is what the calling asks in each phase.

### Before and during the first three percent: bring your three, within three days

The first three percent, about two hundred and fifty million people, connected and empowered, give the distributed leadership that every community and every domain needs to meet what is coming. They form a team of teams: small self-governing Cells of three to thirteen people, optimally four to seven, stacking up and aligning their work as one body. The calling reaches every domain of a society. That includes the guardians, the people who carry a society's instruments of protection, because when enough of them are awake they can refuse to turn those instruments on their neighbours and turn them toward building ([17 Pillar X](17%20Pillar%20X%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Guardianship%20and%20Order.md)).

**Phase I, Formation (23 to 27 September 2026).** The first eight Players are up and running, each with a companion agent, a Pledge and a Cell. The kit, the Invitation and the minimum trust ledger are ready, and onboarding office hours open. Formation proves that a person can be brought in and set going with an agent's help.

**Phase II, the Sprint (28 September to the Winter Solstice, 21 December 2026).** This phase has one tactic, and it belongs to the first three percent only:

> **Every new Player brings their three, people not already playing, and aims for all three to say yes within three days of their own yes.** Then each of their three-day clocks begins.

- **Three is the minimum, never a cap.** Gifted connectors can and should keep inviting the right people.
- **Nobody waits for a Cell to mature.** Imaginal cells in a chrysalis carry the pattern of the butterfly before the butterfly exists. In the same way, new Players pass the Flame from the start, and as they gather they begin to specialize into the parts of the one body that is forming ([ØNE Body](../the-field/%C3%98NE%20Body.md)).
- **For the first three percent, the Pledge includes the commitment** to bring in three people not already playing within three days. It is a commitment, kept or not, and the Roll shows which. Believability grows from commitments kept.
- **The commitment and the daily measure.** The first three percent, connected and empowered, by the Winter Solstice of 2026. We do everything in our power to meet it. Each day the ring and the count are read against the chart. Days per generation and commitments kept, which are the two numbers that move the chart most, are read from the Roll. A gap is named plainly on the day it appears and answered with a re-pull, never with a moved line.
- **The calling and the bringing run together.** Within two days each new Player names the three they would most trust to lead their community. Within three days they bring three new Players. Namings that converge surface the people called out to lead, and the Invitation reaches them by hand.

What the sprint asks of each person is small, and it is all of it: **read the Story, give your word, form your Cell, choose your Quest, and bring your three within three days.**

### After the first three percent: the right people, at the right time

Once two hundred and fifty million stand, about three more generations of three would reach everyone on Earth. The arithmetic could run on at the sprint's pace. Wisdom does not. **After the first three percent the rule of three is retired as a tactic, and the calling changes its mechanism.** It becomes the invitation of the right people at the right time, as they wake up and are ready.

- **Formation sets the pace, not arithmetic.** Perhaps a generation a season, perhaps a generation a year, as [the Arc of Play](../the-field/The%20Arc%20of%20Play.md), the health of the Cells, and the markers ahead require. The rings are still the measure. What changes is what the body is trying to do: form each person well, not reach them fast.
- **Every Player keeps inviting**, not to a quota but to the people in their own life who are stirring, when they are stirring. The person who was not ready in the sprint may be ready in the spring, or in five years. The Invitation comes again, from a different voice, in a different season, up to twenty times and more.
- **Both doors stay open.** Namings keep surfacing the people called out to lead in every place, and the open door keeps welcoming everyone.
- **The markers ahead shape the pace.** By the Winter Solstice of 2027, the calling carries itself: Cells exist that were formed by people who never met anyone from the first season. By the Winter Solstice of 2030, *everyone who desires is invited*. Nobody on Earth is more than one hand away from an invitation carried by someone they know, in a language and register they can receive, and the instruments of formation are ready before the called arrive, because the called must never again arrive to find the table bare. A person has to join by 2030 to have walked their three years of formation by [the Jubilee](../the-field/Jubilee.md) at the Winter Solstice of 2033.
- **The rising never stops.** Young people who come in at fifteen rise at eighteen, and the rising goes on every year after, so that orientation into the new never has to end. Every generation born is called in its turn.

So the calling has two tempos and one heart. In the sprint it is urgent and exact: three, within three days, measured daily. After it, it is patient and wise: the right person, at the right time, however long that takes. In both, it is carried by hand, offered and never pressed, and it keeps the door open behind everyone.

## IX. How to hold it

**Test it against the world.** Watch for the signs that the calling has gone wrong. A door that produces watchers, not workers. A door that grows while the tables do not. Yeses bought with pressure that do not survive their first hard season. Namings that mirror one kind of person. A Pledge commitment kept by few and celebrated anyway. Stories that tell only triumphs. Any of these, seen widely, is evidence against some part of what is written here, and the evidence wins. The first bet of the whole plan is that *the calling compounds*. If it does not, the grammar, register and channels of the Invitation are redesigned in the open, because the bet is on the calling itself, never on one form of it ([30 The Bets and the Stages](30%20The%20Bets%20and%20the%20Stages.md)).

**Hold it by roles, not persons.** No single life is a mechanism of the calling. The Carrier, the host, the guide, the RFG Cell and the called-out assembly are roles, and roles rotate. The centre may equip. It never operates.

**Carry it in the Spirit it came from.** Nothing here asks to be believed. All of it is offered to be tried. If you were reached, reach the next ones who are ready. Say *come and see*, and keep the door open behind you.

*Read the Story. Play the Game. Pass the Flame.*

## What is not yet settled

- **The threshold of the first Co-Creators.** The first structural trigger can be placed at 55 to 144 Co-Creators. This chapter uses 144 at Cycle 10, following [30 The Strategy and Plan](28%20The%20Strategy%20and%20Plan.md).
- **How believability is weighted, and by whom**, without the weighing becoming a centre. Nothing yet says, and nothing yet says whether a naming expires or can be withdrawn ([The nomination laws](../the-field/The%20nomination%20laws.md)).
- **What happens at two namings, or at thirty.** The threshold of three is asserted, not derived.
- **The pace after the first three percent.** *A generation a season, or a year* is a reading, not a rule. How formation is read, and by whom the pace is set, is for the called-out assembly to decide.
- **The number of invitations.** *As many as twenty times* reflects how noisy and wounded the old world is. It is a design expectation, not a measurement.
- **The three years of formation.** It is an average reading, carried as such.
- **Where Requests For Guidance are sent**, and on what timing. Discord now, then Buzz, as the channels move ([Asking for Guidance](../getting-started/Asking%20for%20Guidance%20%E2%80%94%20the%20Ladder%20and%20the%20Request%20For%20Guidance.md)).

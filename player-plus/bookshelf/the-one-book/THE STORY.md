# THE STORY

*The Story of ØNE and All, told at a low fire, as it has been told on countless worlds before this one. Ten minutes to read, fifteen to hear. It is not a belief you are asked to hold. It is a shared language, and we are acting as if it were true. The whole of it, as deep as anyone cares to go, is [[The Story of LIØNSBERG]]; the shared bones are [[The Sacred Arc]].*

I will tell you The Story of LIØNSBERG  
as best as I can remember how it was first told to me  
in a cave just like this on a very different world  
in a very different time  

The Story of LIØNSBERG is the story of [[ØNE]] and All

The Story of The Great Mystery and All that arises from It

Our time is short and danger is high

WHERE WE ARE

## THE STORY IN A BREATH

The world is one, and we forgot; we are remembering, together, by making every place we touch a little more like [[Heaven On Earth|Heaven]].

Out of [[The Caves]], where a people is held captive by stories; through [[The Wilderness]], where we learn, unlearn, remember, and rebuild; toward [[The Promised Land]], which is not a place we arrive at but a way of living we co-create, here and now, a little more each week. Every [[Cell]] walks the same road at its own pace and in its own place.

**Two senses of the word.** THE STORY, in capitals, is the shared telling that gives us a shared language. A [[Story|story]], lowercase, is an honest account of what happened, told so others can use it; every [[Quest]] becomes one, and every story becomes a prophecy of greater things to come.

## THE TWELVE MOVEMENTS

The shared bones of every telling, at every scale — the cosmos, this world, your bioregion, your community, your Cell, your own life.

**The Breath Out**

1. **ØNE** — in the beginning is the ØNE; from ØNE all things come into being, and no thing comes into being except through the ØNE.
2. **The First Turning** — from perfect transcendent stillness, ØNE turned to know ItSelf, and [[The Song of Creation|the Song of Creation]] rose.
3. **All As ØNE Body** — the Many pouring forth were all members of [[ØNE Body]], each one of the Ones of the ØNE.
4. **Finitude and Forgetting** — to experience itself as one of the Ones, each of the All passed through the Veil, and forgot Who It truly was.
5. **[[The Fall]]** — from the illusion of separation, the first selfish thoughts; from them, harm in service of self, and the long night of extraction and [[Kontrolle]].

**The Turn**

6. **[[Awakening|The Awakening]]** — ØNE was never absent; [[The Divine Whisper|the Divine Whisper]] still resounded, waking forgetful sleepers one by one.

**The Breath Home**

7. **[[The Sacred War]]** — the awakening and remembering became a Sacred Force; Love grew and organized as an immune system within ØNE Body.
8. **[[Liberation and Exodus]]** — domain by domain, the parasites were purged, the chains shattered, the captives freed, and they began walking one another home.
9. **The Great ReMembering** — the freed [[Remembrance|remembered]] Who they were, and consciously reunited as fellow members of ØNE Body.
10. **Embodying [[The Way]]** — they practiced the Highest Way until it flowed through them, transforming their lives and their worlds toward Heaven.
11. **Co-Creating Heaven** — where they walked together as ØNE Body with ØNE Mind, consciously creating the Best Possible Future for All, Heaven came.
12. **[[The Eternal Divine Play]]** — and through this great shared journey, All returns to ØNE, raised into the next movement.

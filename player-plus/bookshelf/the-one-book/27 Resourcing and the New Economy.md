# Resourcing and the New Economy

*How the work is resourced, from the first thing put in the middle of a kitchen table to the redirection of what a civilization now spends on weapons and waste. This chapter covers the laws every flow keeps, the Offering and the tenth, the pool that divides at every new and full moon, the fractal channel of offers and requests, the stages in which resources open, the new economy that lies under all of it, and the great stewardship of the Earth that the economy exists to serve. It gives depth to one section of [30 The Strategy and Plan](28%20The%20Strategy%20and%20Plan.md). The economy's own instruments, which are the waterfall, the asset lock, the ratio, the Cell's book and the TEA card, are in [10 Pillar III — Sacred Economy and CurrentSee](10%20Pillar%20III%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Economy%20and%20CurrentSee.md). This chapter links to them and does not repeat them.*

---

## Start here

**A body without a metabolism is a diagram.** For real work to happen, resources have to flow: from the many to the people doing the work, and from them to the [Quests](../the-field/Quest.md) that regenerate the world.

**It begins at your own table.** Each week, at [the Turn](../the-field/The%20Turn.md), each person puts something real in the middle: money, food, hours, a skill, a tool, care. Nobody sets the amount. A tenth is the old measure, and many begin near it and grow ([The Offering](../the-field/The%20Offering.md)). What gathers is the Cell's [commons](../the-field/Commons.md). It is spent in the open on its Quest and its members, and at each turning a share goes inward to its [Home](../the-field/Home.md) ([Voluntary Tithing](../the-field/Voluntary%20Tithing.md)).

**The wider pool keeps the moon's time.** Contributions to the wider body move through a ledger that anyone can read. About a tenth nurtures the shared commons, and the rest goes to the people doing the work. They become [centres of distribution](../the-field/Centers%20of%20Distribution.md) for the Quests they value. The pool divides at every new moon and every full moon, so value keeps moving to where the work is, and nothing pools long enough to be seized or to corrupt.

**Every number belongs to its stage.** In the seed stage the body's own people carry it. As the fruit becomes visible, philanthropic and institutional capital begins to move from waste to value. Only after the body's presence is undeniable does the great harvest become possible: the redirection, by consent, of what humanity spends on weapons and waste toward life. No stage's plan depends on a pool that a later stage opens.

**Beneath the flows stands a new accounting.** [Value](../the-field/Value.md) is [throughput](../the-field/Throughput.md) of [the Goal](../the-field/The%20Goal.md). [Waste](../the-field/Waste.md) is whatever consumes life and energy without producing it. By that measure humanity was never poor. The work of the new economy is to move what is needed to where it is needed, quickly, to stop consuming what is not needed, and to steward the Earth rather than own it.

That is the chapter. The rest is the reasoning and the detail.

## I. What the resources are for, and why they can be trusted

### All value flows from ØNE

Everything anyone has ever traded arrived first as a gift: sunlight the Earth did not earn, a body no one designed, a language nobody alive invented, ground that was fertile before the first field was cleared. An [economy](../the-field/Economy.md) is what a people does with what it has been given. It is the arrangement by which what one person has reaches the person who needs it ([10 Pillar III](10%20Pillar%20III%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Economy%20and%20CurrentSee.md)).

So resourcing the transformation starts with seeing, not with raising money. Five people at a table have a van, a room, an evening, a neighbourhood and a little money. Put in the middle, that is enough to feed a street on a Saturday. Nothing was created. Something was *moved*. Before accepting that a thing cannot be done for lack of resources, ask whether the resource is truly absent or only stuck: held, unnoticed, unrecognized, or never asked for. Most of the time it is stuck. Nearly everything this chapter describes, from the Offering to the redirection of a planet's military spending, is that one insight at a larger scale.

### A gift that belongs to no one

Every voice in the old world that said *trust me* wanted something back: a vote, money, attention, obedience. So a body that asks people to put real resources in the middle owes them an account of what it wants in return, and the account is short. It asks for nothing a person would not freely give to their own children, and it asks everyone to test what it offers before they keep it.

The account rests on facts that can be checked.

**The Vision belongs to no one.** Heaven on Earth, the return to [ØNE](../the-field/%C3%98NE.md), the flourishing of all generations of life: nobody invented these, so nobody can own, license or sell them. There is no product, no subscription, and no secret held back for paying members. The pattern is a [commons](../the-field/Commons.md) in the deepest sense, a free and open gift offered equally to all, because it was received as one.

**It arrived already paid for.** The long preparatory years were carried at the cost of those who did the work, joined by freely given wisdom and a few small gifts. So there is no investor to repay, no debt to service, and no backer whose interests must quietly be served. The body's only obligations are to ØNE and All.

**It was built to be given away.** When the provisional structures of the founding years were dissolved by prior design, no accounts remained to seize or fight over, and no treasury remained to raid. Everything essential was already in the open, held by everyone who chose to take it up. Value that has been distributed to the edges cannot be captured at a centre.

**The books are open, the fruit is the test, and the door opens both ways.** Every flow is visible. Every claim is weighed by the fruit it bears in a real life and a real community. Each person's discernment is sovereign: where something is lacking they fill it, where it does not belong they remove it, and where it is wrong they say so. Anyone may stop playing at any moment and leave owning their full share of the commons still. Only a gift that can be freely refused and freely left can be freely trusted.

Resourcing grows out of this foundation. The body asks people to resource work they can see, through books they can read, toward a Goal no one owns.

## II. The laws every flow keeps

Seven laws govern every resource that moves through the body, at every scale, from the first unit. They are structural. None of them depends on anyone being good on a given day, and that is the point of them ([Structural Immunity](../the-field/Structural%20Immunity.md)).

1. **Transparency is the legitimacy.** Every ledger is open and every flow is visible. The body does not assert its claim to trust. It makes the claim auditable ([Transparency](../the-field/Transparency.md)). Where everything is traceable and every party is visible, theft and corruption become costly and rare.
2. **Anti-pooling is the security.** Wealth that flows blesses, and wealth that pools corrupts. Wealth that never pools cannot be seized, cannot corrupt, and cannot become a target. Distribution on a known rhythm is armour, not an accounting convenience ([The anti-pooling law](../the-field/The%20anti-pooling%20law.md)).
3. **No platform dependence.** Every structure used to move money is temporary and replaceable. The trust graph, the ledgers and the commons survive the death of any platform, and the paper book in each Cell is the floor under all of them ([Screens and paper](../the-field/Screens%20and%20paper.md)).
4. **The flows honour the law of every land they touch.** Radical transparency is joined to real compliance, with counsel in every jurisdiction the flows reach, so that no member is ever wounded, in tax or in law, by the commons that feeds them.
5. **The centre may equip; it never operates.** Resources equip people and Cells to act sovereignly. Any flow that needs a centre's ongoing hand is a defect in the design.
6. **No stage's plan depends on a later stage's pool.** A body that learns to fund its first seasons from its own tables cannot be bought out of its later ones. Movements that depend on outside funding end up captured by their funders, usually not by coercion but by the ordinary gravity of who must be kept happy (§VI).
7. **We do not sell out.** What is built for many generations is not sold for one. Small groups will always be tempted to cash out at the expense of the whole, so the body designs for multi-generational, ecosystemic value creation from the start. It writes [the asset lock](../the-field/The%20asset%20lock.md) and the purpose lock into every charter on the day the charter is written, and keeps [capital subordinate to purpose](../the-field/Capital%20Subordinate%20To%20Purpose.md): capital that helps is repaid with a fair, capped return and thanked, and it never acquires control.

Two older truths stand behind these laws. **The means are the end:** a body resourced by extraction becomes an extractor, so every flow here is voluntary, visible and reciprocal. And **[consent](../the-field/Consent.md) governs:** an offering that is extracted or socially compelled is a subscription with better manners.

## III. From our own tables: the Offering and the tenth

### The Offering

The first and most important flow in the whole economy happens every week at a kitchen table. At the Turn, after each person has said what they committed to and whether they did it, the bowl goes round. Each person puts in what is real for them: money, food, hours, a skill, a tool, a ride, care. Nobody announces an amount and nobody is asked ([The Offering](../the-field/The%20Offering.md)).

It is small on purpose. A Cell that puts something in the middle every week for one season has built three things without anyone planning them: a real commons it can point to, a habit of giving that does not depend on anyone's mood, and plain proof that [abundance](../the-field/Abundance.md) is produced rather than waited for. Everything larger in this chapter is this act at a larger scale, and it stands or falls on whether the small act was done truthfully.

### The tenth

A tenth is the old measure, found across the world's traditions and cooperative movements. It is enough to matter and light enough to carry. Most people begin somewhere near it. It is a starting point freely chosen, never a rate owed: the figure belongs to each person and is expected to change with their season. Once a group has tasted what even a modest commons makes possible, it usually wants more of it, and contributions tend to rise a little at a time, each step unlocking something the group could not do before. This is the opposite of taxation. There the rate is imposed and the benefit is abstract. Here the rate is chosen and the benefit is on the table in front of you.

### Inward, at the turning

What gathers is the Cell's commons. The Cell spends it on its Quest and on its own people: a member in trouble, a tool the work needs, the cost of gathering. A commons that is never spent is a hoard with a nicer name. At each turning of the sun the Cell sends a meaningful share inward to its Home, the body that hears its season and helps when something is wrong. The Home does the same with its own Home. The direction is always inward, toward the centre of the table a person actually sits at. Resources reach the highest federated level that a sovereign genuinely and voluntarily belongs to, and go no further ([Voluntary Tithing](../the-field/Voluntary%20Tithing.md), [Sovereignty At Every Scale](../the-field/Sovereignty%20At%20Every%20Scale.md)). Every Cell and every community forges and governs its own commons, holofractal, interoperable and sovereign, and each tithes to its own local commons at every scale. That is how [the commons](../the-field/Fractal%20Commonsing.md) is provisioned from the bottom up.

### Counted honestly

A Cell keeps one page per season: what came in and what went out. A keeper writes it, the keeper role rotates at the turning, and the page is read aloud at the Turn. At the turning, line 8 of [the Season Sheet](../the-field/The%20Season%20Sheet.md) carries four figures and no more: gathered, kept, sent inward, and the unit. That single line, filled in truthfully on every sheet, is what makes ten thousand tables one economy. Hours are written as hours and goods as what they are. **Nothing is converted into money that does not need to be money.** Care, the work money never counted, is counted honestly in the record and never priced between neighbours. How the book is kept, what the TEA card is, and how a Cell resources a Quest in six steps (the cost in real units, then the offers already on the table, then the commons, then sideways, then inward, and only then outward) are all in [10 Pillar III](10%20Pillar%20III%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Economy%20and%20CurrentSee.md).

## IV. The wider pool, and the moon

The Cell's commons is local and belongs to the Cell. The body as a whole also needs a metabolism: something that resources the people carrying work beyond a single table, and the shared instruments that every table uses. It works like this.

**A transparent ledger.** Contributions to the wider body flow through a temporary, radically transparent structure, a public ledger that any person on Earth can read. That structure is scaffolding and not the building. Behind it, the body matures its own commons vehicle, and it depends on no platform.

**About a tenth to the shared commons.** Roughly one tenth of all flows nurtures the shared commons: the instruments, the infrastructure, the places where the body meets and sees itself. The rest is distributed across the people doing the work.

**The people doing the work become centres of distribution.** From their share, each covers their own transparent expenses and their survival, and directs any excess to the Quests they judge most need it. How resources move through the whole therefore becomes the combined discernment of everyone doing the work, and never the decree of a centre. A person's standing in this economy is measured by what flows through them and blesses, not by what flows in and stays ([Centers of Distribution](../the-field/Centers%20of%20Distribution.md)). The practical test is not how much anyone gives away. It is whether anything is quietly accumulating where nobody can see it.

**The pool divides at every new moon and every full moon.** All resources in the pool divide across the people doing the work twice in each lunar month. Money never pools at the centre. No more than about two weeks of flows could ever be captured, frozen or lost. The treasury keeps [the LIØNSBERG Calendar](../the-field/The%20LI%C3%98NSBERG%20Calendar.md), the same sky-clock that a distributed body uses to breathe together without anyone coordinating it. The moon is the treasury's pulse. The four turnings of the sun, at the solstices and equinoxes, remain the season's clock for the Cells and their shares sent inward. The two rhythms nest: the short one keeps value moving, and the long one keeps the seasons honest.

**The spark, not the engine.** A crowdfunded pool, however faithfully kept, is how the fire is lit and not what keeps it burning. In the season of the Calling a matching gift was offered, with no conditions, as the first evidence that the ninety-seven percent will resource the three. The mature economics are the participants' own: seasonal Quest flows directed by the people themselves, and commons tithes at every scale (§VII).

**What a Player receives.** Each Player who carries the work has the whole code of the Game, the Cell practice, their Cell's commons, their share of the moon distributions where they are doing work the body resources, the chain of people who brought them as living sponsors, and, if they choose, an agent. These equip. None of them commands.

## V. Fractal offers and requests

A centre allocates by decree. A body without a centre allocates by asking and offering. That is the live allocation mechanism of the whole economy, and it runs the same way at every scale.

**At the table.** Each week the lower half of every [Playcard](../the-field/The%20Playcard.md) carries two lines: *what I have to offer* and *what I ask*. Five people saying those two things aloud, week after week, is a marketplace. It is the smallest one there is, and the one on which all the larger ones stand.

**Between Cells.** Lines 10 and 11 of every Season Sheet are *offers* and *requests*, written in language a stranger could act on: what it is, how much, by when, and who to ask. A Home gathers the offers and requests of all its Cells at the turning and reads them against one another. This sideways channel is the one most bodies neglect, and it is often the richest. Most needs in a federation are met by a Cell three streets away that had the thing and did not know it was wanted ([Requests and offers](../the-field/Requests%20and%20offers.md)).

**Across every membrane.** Between sovereign bodies only three things travel: requests, offers and commitments. A task is never assigned by one body to another ([Selectively Permeable Membrane](../the-field/Selectively%20Permeable%20Membrane.md)). When two bodies [trade](../the-field/Trade.md), the terms are agreed aloud before the work, written down with a named person responsible on each side, and what actually happened is recorded afterward. The exchange is reciprocal over time rather than balanced per transaction, and it is omniconsiderate: an arrangement that suits both signatories while costing a watershed or a generation is extraction with paperwork ([Exchange](../the-field/Exchange.md), [Omniconsideration](../the-field/Omniconsideration.md)).

**At every scale.** The same pattern extends outward as fractal offers-and-requests marketplaces: neighbourhood, community, bioregion, planet. Local, bioregional and global exchange can all flourish because every level speaks the same three words. The federations of the new body link at the mycelial level, and value moves across the whole in response to the offers and requests of each living part ([Fostering Ecosystemic Resource Flows](../the-field/Fostering%20Ecosystemic%20Resource%20Flows.md), [Federation](../the-field/Federation.md)). As the body grows, human and AI solutions matchmakers help connect each Cell and community to the right tool, solution or skill just when it is needed. They serve the asking and never replace it.

**Who decides where a pooled resource goes.** The rule is *by relationship and by trust*. That is true, but by itself it is not enough. The working proposal, to be tested and corrected, is in [10 Pillar III](10%20Pillar%20III%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Economy%20and%20CurrentSee.md). At each turning the Home reads its Cells' requests aloud in full before any allocation is discussed. Cells say what they can meet from their own offers. What remains is met from the Home's commons in an order the Cells agree, with the state of the commons visible to everyone and the reason recorded. Where consent cannot be reached, the request waits for the next turning rather than being decided by whoever holds the book.

## VI. The stages of resourcing

Every resource number in the plan belongs to its stage. The pools of the transformation open in sequence, each one unlocked by the stage before it. No stage's plan may depend on a later stage's pool. The early body is resourced entirely by its own people, which is also why it cannot be bought, and why its legitimacy is beyond dispute.

The stages are counted on the rings of the [Cycles of Growth](../the-field/LI%C3%98NSBERG%20Cycles%20of%20Growth%20and%20Cycle%20Time.md), the body's prime measure. They are thresholds, not dates: a stage opens when the body stands on its ring, whatever day that is.

| Stage | Rings | What is true and available | What is not yet available |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The seed** | Cycles 1–10 (to the first 144) | The Offering at every table; the body's own people and their own networks; the transparent pool and its matching gifts; the kit and the Book, which come before Cycle 10 on the [critical path](../the-field/Critical%20Path.md) | Quest flows at scale; institutional capital; any state-level lever |
| **The organism** | Cycles 10–30 | Tithes and seasonal Quest flows compounding with the number of Players; philanthropic capital beginning to redirect as fruit becomes visible; the first hubs seeded from buildings that already stand | The hub network at scale; the redirection of military spending |
| **The tipping** | Cycles 30–40 (to the first three percent) | Quest flows at a scale measured in hundreds of billions of dollars; community-foundation and institutional flows; the first cooperation from governments in the domains where adoption is highest | Full military redirection |
| **The harvest** | Cycle 40 and beyond | Swords to ploughshares and the redirection of government waste, by consent at the scale of states, which becomes realistic only after the body's presence is undeniable; the full build-out of the hub network | — |

Three things hold the stages honest.

**The stages move with the rings, not with the calendar.** In the Sprint, the body may stand on Cycle 10 within days and on Cycle 40 within weeks ([24 The Plan](29%20The%20Plan%20%E2%80%94%20the%20first%20three%20percent.md)). A stage does not open because the body has counted its people, though. It opens because the fruit is real: Cells alive, Quests done, books open. Resourcing follows formation, the same way the pace after the Sprint does.

**What the seed needs is small.** When the working capital needed for the first three percent was first reckoned, it was read at about a hundred million dollars in all. That is less than a dollar a participant, and it can be entirely decentralized. It is a reading from its season, not a budget. Its point stands: the seed stage asks nothing of anyone that a table cannot give.

**Every stage has its structural trigger.** When the first 144 Co-Creators stand, at about Cycle 10, the stewardship of the treasury path begins to distribute into the called-out assembly together with the canon, the guidance function and the instruments. Until that distribution is complete, every key and every duty is held redundantly, so that no flow ever depends on a single life ([26 The Body and Its Planning](26%20The%20Body%20and%20Its%20Planning.md)). The bet that the economics will ignite from below is one of the plan's four load-bearing bets. Its instrument is the open ledgers, and its adaptation is that the vehicle and the distribution rules evolve in the open while the anti-pooling law never bends ([30 The Bets and the Stages](30%20The%20Bets%20and%20the%20Stages.md)).

**The resources assumed, and the resources sought.** At each stage the body assumes only what is pledged from within — its own tables, its own commons, nothing borrowed against a future that has not arrived. At the same time, the superorganism ventures out into the wider world, looking for resources of every kind adequate to sustain its progression toward the fullness of its potential. Both are true together: the plan never depends on what has not yet been pledged, and the body never stops looking outward for what it needs.

## VII. The engine: seasonal Quest flows

The mature economy of the body is not a fund. It is the ordinary resources of hundreds of millions of people, directed a season at a time toward the Quests they have chosen, moving peer to peer and Cell to Quest without passing through any centre at all.

The arithmetic is simple enough to do on a napkin, and it is an illustration of scale, not a forecast. At the first three percent there are about two hundred and fifty million Players in roughly fifty million Cells, which average about five people each. If those Cells directed even ten thousand dollars each to their seasonal Quest, about two thousand dollars a person, that would move **on the order of five hundred billion dollars a season** toward regenerative work.

This flow is read in more than one shape, and the shapes do not reconcile. One reading puts Quest flows at the first three percent at two to five percent of the participants' own capacity, *hundreds of billions per season*. Another puts the same two to five percent at *five hundred billion to a trillion dollars a year*. The napkin arithmetic above gives about five hundred billion a season, which is some two trillion a year. These are readings from different seasons of thinking, each an order of magnitude and a direction. None is a measurement and none is a promise. What does not change between them is the point: at the scale of the first three percent, the flows that matter are enormous, and they need no centre to exist.

The same reading once suggested that early participants tithe a tenth of their Quest flows to the commons. That is the tenth again, and it keeps the same law: freely chosen, sent inward to the commons of the table the giver actually sits at, and adjusted to each season.

When those flows become visible, something else happens. Abundance becomes self-evident. A person who has watched their Cell's commons carry a family through a hard month, or seen a street fed for a year from its own tables, no longer believes the story of scarcity. That shift is itself part of the turning ([32 The Turning](05%20The%20Turning%20%E2%80%94%20consciousness%2C%20healing%20and%20wholeness%20at%20every%20scale.md)).

## VIII. The new economy

### The new accounting

Beneath all the flows stands a redefinition. A system built on purpose to realize [the Goal](../the-field/The%20Goal.md) can redefine [Value](../the-field/Value.md) and [Waste](../the-field/Waste.md) against the Goal itself. Joined to [time-energy accounting](../the-field/Time-Energy%20Accounting.md), which keeps the books in the universe's own currencies of [time](../the-field/Time.md) and [energy](../the-field/Energy.md), that redefinition turns the old books upside down.

The universe already keeps these books. Energy arrives from the sun as pure income, and life receives it, stores it, circulates it and builds ever more capable order with it. It has been a regenerative economy for billions of years, without a unit of currency or an hour of debt. Time-energy accounting adopts that method on purpose and measures every activity by one question: *does this create value toward the Goal, or does it create waste?*

Asked that way, much of what the old books call productive turns out to be waste. The disciplines that go beyond lean, agile and the [theory of constraints](../the-field/Theory%20Of%20Constraints.md) have found sixty percent waste and more in nearly every domain they have touched. The level of waste on [Worksite Earth](../the-field/Worksite%20Earth.md) in the 2020s is likely eighty percent or higher. The sixty is measured; the eighty is an extrapolation from it, read as likely and not as a measurement. Much of what humanity does to "earn a living", exploiting one another and all of creation, shows up as waste. And much of what the old books never counted turns out to be the primary value creation of the species: raising children, tending soil, healing neighbours, keeping the commons.

A strange consequence follows, and it becomes obvious once you see it. If most effort is waste, then paying one another to *stop creating waste* while we reorganize and learn to run the new pattern together is neither charity nor idleness. It is the rational move, and the cheapest one available ([Underwriting The New Economy](../the-field/Underwriting%20The%20New%20Economy.md)).

### Real wealth

Real wealth is [energy](../the-field/Energy.md), which cannot be destroyed, joined to wisdom, knowledge and information, which only grow. By that measure humanity is not poor and never was. The wisdom, knowledge, solutions and technologies developed across all of history are our collective inheritance. Gathered into the commons and freed from captivity, the wealth already at hand is enough for every family and community on Earth. In the deepest sense we are all billionaires, heirs who were taught to believe themselves paupers ([Wealth](../the-field/Wealth.md), [Knowledge as commons](../the-field/Knowledge%20as%20commons.md)).

That is why the new economy counts in six capitals together: living, social, human, intellectual, built and financial. A rough count of six beats a precise count of one, because a single financial line always wins the argument that it alone has a number for ([10 Pillar III](10%20Pillar%20III%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Economy%20and%20CurrentSee.md)).

### The liberation arc

The new economy progressively makes the need to "earn a living" obsolete. Along the way it:

- **dissolves debt-based bondage**, the life of employee, consumer and debtor that trades a human being's hours for the right to go on existing;
- **retires the meaningless and destructive jobs** that waste human potential and feed degenerative production and consumption;
- **welcomes the automation of the old work**, while ensuring that the people own the artificial intelligence, the robotics and the high technologies that do it, and that no small group does;
- **connects every person and locality willing to co-create** to meaningful work, meaningful relationships and meaningful income that let them meet their needs and flourish ([Meaningful Work](../the-field/Meaningful%20Work.md));
- **keeps safety nets** under anyone the body cannot yet connect, with an [energy and commitment based universal income](../the-field/Energy%20and%20Commitment%20Based%20UBI.md) as the floor;
- **renders poverty obsolete**, and gives everyone who keeps the agreements of the Game an equal share in the abundant future being built.

The culture underneath is simple to say and hard to live: *we are not consumers but contributors, not owners but stewards.* There are no parasites, only participants. What a person gives in time, energy and attention is worth more than what they own.

### Incentives realigned with outcomes

The old economy pays for harm and charges for health. The new one reverses the gradient, so that:

- peace is more rewarding than war;
- wellbeing is more rewarding than sickness;
- long-term regeneration is more rewarding than short-term exploitation;
- cooperation toward mutual success is more rewarding than competition toward mutual destruction;
- co-creating social and ecological flourishing is more rewarding than exploiting it.

Four instruments carry the reversal into the world of capital.

**Measurement before resourcing.** Humanity learns to measure a proposed plan for its social and ecological regenerativity *before* resources flow to it, and to measure impact afterward with evidence strong enough to stand in any court.

**Regenerative underwriting and insurance.** Actuarial data and underwriting are reoriented toward the long-term flourishing of the living systems they now only price. Risk that was externalized is brought home and priced where it falls.

**A regenerative banking network,** which grows alongside the hubs (§X). Resources are taken out of the degenerative banking system and placed where they serve life.

**Off-ramps for capital.** Resources and capital are carried out of degenerative systems and into the [critical path](../the-field/Critical%20Path.md), through channels that can move hundreds of billions each season to the teams advancing it. The people who steward institutions (fiduciaries, boards, public servants) are helped to see the root causes of the harm their institutions do, and the personal and legal liability of knowingly fuelling it. Then they are offered an honourable way out (§IX).

### Currencies born from commitments

As the fiat currencies of the old world strain, or harden into instruments of surveillance, the new economy keeps freedom of exchange alive through an interoperable network of local currencies. These are created not from debt and not from arbitrary computation, but from **commitments made and kept**. A promise kept toward a local Quest creates throughput of the Goal, which is by definition value. Money made that way is crystallized trustworthiness. It is tied to each locality's network of commitments, and those commitments stack up through the planning of every scale into the one Quest of the whole ([26 The Body and Its Planning](26%20The%20Body%20and%20Its%20Planning.md)).

The citizens of each place create their own. Each is readable across the wider federation and cannot be extracted by distant capital. The rollout must outrun the collapse: continuity of exchange everywhere, before the old rails fail. The unit this grows from ([LUV](../the-field/LUV.md)), the meaning of *CurrentSee*, and the honest state of both (the principle is held, the ledger is not yet built, and no Cell needs either to play a season) are in [10 Pillar III](10%20Pillar%20III%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Economy%20and%20CurrentSee.md).

The key to this internal economy is the transformation of [time-energy accounting](../the-field/Time-Energy%20Accounting.md) — TEA — into LUV: as much of the body's own exchange as possible flows as CurrentSee rather than as the old world's fear-based currencies, and as little as possible depends on them at all. This is not only a preference; it is a hedge. As the old fear currencies strain under the very conditions this chapter describes, they progressively become, in the plainest sense, worth less — a wordplay carried on purpose, because it is also the honest forecast.

### Circulation rather than accumulation

The new economy circulates.

**Waste becomes the mine.** Landfills and waste streams become the primary mines for the materials that supply chains need. What we call pollution is valuable matter in the wrong place. So every home, workplace and community learns to capture what it discards at the source, and the hubs carry recycling, processing and reallocation capacity at local, regional and bioregional scale. Every entity becomes a centre of distribution for its own byproducts, redistributing them as value for the whole instead of externalizing them as harm.

**The old built world becomes the mine.** Most of what stands today was raised in haste from materials chosen for what they cost to extract, not for what they cost the people who would live inside them: adhesives, coatings, treated woods and synthetic compounds that off-gas for years, joined in ways that can be demolished but never taken apart. That is a large part of why so much of the built world makes its people sick before it makes them warm or dry ([16 Pillar IX](16%20Pillar%20IX%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Architecture%20and%20Design.md)). Recycling it is planned in from the first design, not bolted on afterward. A building is deconstructed rather than demolished: taken apart in close to the reverse order it was assembled, so that what went in as steel, timber, copper and stone comes out sorted and graded, ready to be built with again, instead of crushed into landfill. Every tonne recovered this way is a tonne of ore a mine never has to yield. Run across the whole built environment, stage by stage over the one to two centuries the rebuilding takes, deconstruction and material recovery become one of the largest sources the new supply chains draw from, and new mining becomes the exception rather than the rule. What is built new from that point on is built to be healthy from its first material choice, and built to be unbuilt again in its turn: joined rather than glued, labelled rather than hidden, so the next generation inherits a bank of materials, not a demolition bill.

**Every photon is treasured.** Every photon received is treasured and turned into life and abundant material. Energy and nutrients are kept moving through the living system rather than dumped out of it.

**Supply chains come home.** Economies, manufacturing, food systems and supply chains are relocalized into an optimally decentralized pattern, traced end to end, and continuously improved. Every locality can see what flows in and out, recognize at once where harm is being pushed onto others, and provision itself indefinitely while staying connected to the rest.

**Resources are withdrawn from what wounds.** Each person and each body removes its resources, energy, attention and consumption from the systems and supply chains that create waste, and redirects them toward those that create value. No one pursues their own narrow gain by harming, exploiting or disadvantaging any other person, tribe or place. Every ordinary purchase is a small vote about which world is fed.

Over all of it stands the anti-pooling law: wealth that flows blesses, and wealth that pools corrupts. That is the economy of centres of distribution, at every scale, for All. How money itself works, why [usury](../the-field/Usury.md) makes the old order unable to stop growing, the three modes of [gift](../the-field/Gift.md), exchange and trade, and why a [Jubilee](../the-field/Jubilee.md) is systems maintenance and not sentiment are all taught in [10 Pillar III](10%20Pillar%20III%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Economy%20and%20CurrentSee.md).

## IX. The Great Stewardship

The new economy does not own the world. It stewards the world for the good of all current and future generations of life ([Stewardship](../the-field/Stewardship.md), [Stewardship over Ownership](../the-field/Stewardship%20over%20Ownership%20%C2%B7%20Influence%20over%20Control%20%C2%B7%20Infinite%20over%20Finite%20Games.md)). This is the purpose that every resource serves in the end. The fuller living-systems plan is in [13 Pillar VI](13%20Pillar%20VI%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Ecology%20and%20Living%20Systems.md), and the rebuilding of the built world is in [16 Pillar IX](16%20Pillar%20IX%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Architecture%20and%20Design.md).

**We listen to the voice of Nature.** The most important members of the living system are the most silent: the Amazon, the Arctic, the oceans, the soils and waters, and the small lives beneath notice. The body learns to hear them. It honours the wisdom and principles of Nature in all planning and action, and it measures every proposal by its regenerativity before resources flow.

**We steward the resources of every bioregion.** A bioregional resource stewardship network holds natural, social and economic resources as a trust across many generations, through resource trusts at the georegional, bioregional, local and community scales ([Community Land and Equity Trusts](../the-field/Community%20Land%20and%20Equity%20Trusts.md), [Bioregion](../the-field/Bioregion.md)). Degenerative extraction ceases. The corruption and arming of local factions to strip the world's resources, which grinds up the weak and the poor, is stood against by a united force for good. [Reciprocity](../the-field/Reciprocity.md) and [flow](../the-field/Flow.md) are kept throughout the whole.

**We heal and clean up the mess.** Human creativity takes its place as a healer. Pollutant flows are stopped at their sources, beginning with the few sources that cause most of the harm. The poisoned places are cleansed with the best of our science and the emerging arts of remediation: groundwater, the munitions lying in the oceans and waters, the legacies of industrial poisoning. Soil, water and air are returned to the healing forces they are. Partnering with Nature, every act of cleanup releases cascading benefits. Harm done to the commons carries compensatory and restorative obligations, so that the old cycle of corrupting, extracting, polluting and abandoning a place becomes costly and unprofitable rather than rewarded.

**We steward land, home and the built world.** The paradigm moves from ownership to stewardship across many generations. The ancient boundaries are re-examined, including the old doctrines by which lands were taken from their first peoples by force, and so are the roles of debt and equity in real property and the insurance of it in an age of converging risk. A [Jubilee](../the-field/Jubilee.md) is declared in its time, and every family and tribe is ensured a domain of responsibility and authority to steward and flourish upon, where their free will cannot be violated. Families and local cooperatives are free and encouraged to grow, trade and exchange food, and food forests are planted everywhere. The reckoning is faced squarely. Much of the built environment fosters illness rather than flourishing, raised in haste from materials chosen for what they cost to extract rather than what they cost the people who would live inside them, and over the next one to two centuries some ninety percent of it, and the housing of most of humanity, will need to be reimagined, redesigned and rebuilt regeneratively. That figure is the plan's own reading of the scale, not a measurement. How that old world is recycled rather than merely torn down, generation by generation, and how doing so ends most of the world's need for new mining, is in §VIII. What is built new is built to last, not for obsolescence, and built to be unbuilt again in its turn.

**We steward the water and the meters of life.** No one owns the water. The corrupt bargains that sold it are undone, and its quality, quantity and flow are stewarded for All. The meters of water, energy, data and exchange pass from the hands of [the Giants](../the-field/The%20Giants.md) to the people, which ends the piped, wired and metered extraction of humanity.

**We steward data and ideas.** Every person holds full stewardship of their own data. They choose consciously what to share, with whom and why, and information about a person may never be used to exploit, deceive or oppress them. Every wise, true and good idea belongs to all of us as part of our common inheritance. What one body learns, every body may have, and what is built on freely given work is given as freely ([Knowledge as commons](../the-field/Knowledge%20as%20commons.md), [Kit of Parts](../the-field/Kit%20of%20Parts.md)). Among the things re-examined is the origin of fortunes built on breakthroughs that the public paid for.

**We honour the first stewards.** Indigenous and original peoples hold an equal and honoured seat at every table. Their wisdom is engaged with reverence and validated alongside modern science. Their communities are supported as they choose and protected from corruption, division and dispossession. Their knowledge is shared on their own terms, through their own trusted networks. Their guidance is sought first in restoring the lands and waters they have stewarded longest.

**We meet the Giants with truth and an open door.** The great concentrations of extractive power are met in the same order as every other shadow in this plan ([32 The Turning](05%20The%20Turning%20%E2%80%94%20consciousness%2C%20healing%20and%20wholeness%20at%20every%20scale.md)):

- first, map honestly the harm they cause to society and the living world, with evidence strong enough for any court;
- then, formally inform those who steward them of that harm and of their liability for harm knowingly caused;
- then, offer off-ramps, amnesty, and a way to save face and take part honourably in building the future, because all of us took part in the old order and the future needs everyone's skills;
- and withdraw our own resources, energy and attention from what they do to wound.

Where a true accounting of the damage far exceeds what an institution could ever repay, a harder end holds, now settled: the Giants of Old — governments, corporations and militaries among them — are placed into receivership and stewarded for the good of all as part of the **One Planetary Commons**. Harmful operations cease, their high technologies pass into the commons, their captives go free, and their economic, technological and logistical power is reassigned to the regeneration and atonement of the world. Militaries are met the same way the rest of §XI describes: swords beaten into ploughshares, weapons turned to tools of life, at a balanced phase-down of about ten percent a year. How the receivership is carried out, and under whose process, remains open; see *What is not yet settled*.

## X. The commons and the hubs, as they bear on resources

Every locality's Cells face the same needs: places to gather, learn, heal, make, grow, store and celebrate, and the skilled people to run them. Most of these are unaffordable to any single Cell or family, and it would be absurd for fifty Cells in one town to build the same capacity fifty times. So each place gathers a commons of shared capacity with equal access for its members, as is locally wise and right ([Fractal Commonsing](../the-field/Fractal%20Commonsing.md), [The Hub](../the-field/The%20Hub.md)). A tool used a few hours a week in one garage is used most of the week when it is held in common, and it pays for itself within a season. A commons without a membrane is not a commons, it is a car park. So each has members, rules its members made, a steward for each thing, and a way to settle disputes ([Tragedy of the Commons](../the-field/Tragedy%20of%20the%20Commons.md)).

**Hubs are called forth, never imposed.** Hubs are localized and resourced in response to the self-organization and request of the places themselves, as they pursue their own local Quests. Proposals come up from a place through the body's guidance ladder; assignments are never sent down. Existing buildings lead the way: libraries become community knowledge hubs and centres for matching people with solutions, schools become hubs of lifelong, project-based learning, and halls, barns and empty buildings are given new life. Much of what the network needs already stands.

**The work organizes as joint ventures led by the called-out.** Around each of the twelve Pillars, ventures form that keep raising the quality and lowering the cost of every core function of a society, place by place. Leading solutions are packaged as soft franchises, trained and mentored into local operation and learning across the whole network. Those ventures are also where most of the body's meaningful work and income will come from. How the work itself is broken down and built is in [24 The Work and the System](24%20The%20Work%20and%20the%20System.md).

**The build-out, read at planetary scale.** The network's shape and its eight nested tiers, from micro hub to the one core hub, are in [26 The Body and Its Planning](26%20The%20Body%20and%20Its%20Planning.md). Retrofits come first at every tier, so these are ceilings rather than fixed costs: per hub, the estimates run from about two million dollars for a micro hub, through tens of millions for a local hub and hundreds of millions for a city hub, to tens and then hundreds of billions for a bioregional and a georegional hub, and about five hundred billion for the one core hub. Multiplied out tier by tier, these do not add to a single settled figure. The whole network is read, separately, at about **ten to twenty trillion dollars in present-day terms**, an approximation turning chiefly on how much existing infrastructure is repurposed; other readings put it at about fifteen trillion, or fifteen to twenty. None of these is a measurement of anything built. Each is an approximation, an order of magnitude only, made before the first hub of most tiers stood. The one-place restatement, reconciling the tier-by-tier arithmetic with the aggregate, is owed ([10 Pillar III](10%20Pillar%20III%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Economy%20and%20CurrentSee.md)).

**How the hubs are resourced, stage by stage.** In the seed and organism stages, hubs begin at the smallest scales, where the sums are ordinary and the learning is cheap. They are seeded from buildings that already stand and paid for from local commons and Quest flows. A regenerative banking network grows alongside them. The full planetary build-out belongs to the harvest stage, and its resourcing is designed as the redirection described next. No early hub waits for that pool, and no early plan counts on it.

**Resilience is designed in.** Every locality works toward provisioning its own core needs indefinitely: food, water, energy, shelter and care. It relocalizes its economy while staying connected to the rest. In a hard season its Cells, its hubs and its paper contact-tree become the backbone that feeds people, keeps the peace and shows the way forward. Hubs carry disaster readiness from the start: modular shelter, care and coordination that any trained person from anywhere could step into ([21 In Every Future](21%20In%20Every%20Future%20%E2%80%94%20what%20to%20do%20in%20each%20case.md)).

## XI. Swords into ploughshares

The greatest pool of resources on Earth is the one now pointed at the capacity to kill. That capacity is not a separate economy. It is the same metallurgy, logistics, computation, chemistry and organizing genius that could be building water systems, housing, clean power and care ([Weaponry To Livingry](../the-field/Weaponry%20To%20Livingry.md)). The move is to turn it: military, logistical and organizational capability refocused on co-creation rather than mutual destruction, on relieving disasters rather than causing them, on saving us rather than destroying us. The greatest infrastructure joint venture in human history is offered as the alternative to a world war.

Four things keep this honest.

**It is the last pool, not the first.** The redirection of military and government waste becomes realistic only after the body's presence is undeniable at planetary scale, and only by consent at the scale of states. Nothing before the harvest stage depends on it.

**It is conversion, not exhortation.** Guardianship is real, and so is the capacity to protect. What changes is the purpose the capacity serves. A body prepared to protect life does not need the scale of a body armed for domination, and the doctrine is the minimum necessary force ([17 Pillar X](17%20Pillar%20X%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Guardianship%20and%20Order.md)).

**The pace is a balanced phase-down, not a cliff.** Militaries are among the Giants placed into receivership within the One Planetary Commons (§IX), and the conversion of their capacity — swords beaten into ploughshares, weapons turned to tools of life — proceeds at a steady, balanced pace of about ten percent a year, so that the world is never left less able to protect life than it was the year before.

**Its figures are readings.** World military spending now runs at approximately two and a half to three trillion dollars a year, and has risen each year it has been measured. Phased down by about a tenth a year across a decade against that rising baseline, the cumulative freed total is read at approximately fifteen to twenty trillion dollars, close to the hub network's own build cost. These are carried as the order of magnitude of the waste, not as a forecast of any flow, and both figures move as the spending itself does.

**It may not be monetary at all.** As existing systems strain, the redirection may come less as money than as people, skills, materials and capacity turned to life, measured in the time and energy of the new accounting rather than in any currency.

At the scale of a table the move already has an ordinary form. The Cell asks what its own hours, skills and money are currently pointed at, and whether any of it is aimed, however indirectly, at harm. Then it points one real thing toward life for a season.

## XII. In every future

Resourcing was designed against its own worst case, which is why so little of it needs anything to work. If a currency resets, deposits freeze, or the network goes down, the Offering is stated in a real unit at the next Turn (hours, food, fuel, care) and line 8 is recorded in that unit. The tithe already went inward to the table you sit at, so there is no distant centre to be cut off. The moon's rhythm means no more than two weeks of the wider pool can be lost in any shock. The flows that matter were already peer to peer and Cell to Quest. Paper is the record, with two signatures and a line count. The floor of care stands above every ledger: when a member is in danger, in body or in mind, the Circle acts first and records later. The protocol for exchanging time and energy when the rails are gone is in [10 Pillar III](10%20Pillar%20III%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Economy%20and%20CurrentSee.md). The judgment for each family of futures, decided in advance, is in [21 In Every Future](21%20In%20Every%20Future%20%E2%80%94%20what%20to%20do%20in%20each%20case.md). **If the lights are out, open there.**

## XIII. What each of us does

The whole of resourcing, at the only scale where it is ever actually carried out:

- **Put something in the middle** at every Turn, whatever is real for you. A tenth is the old measure; your figure is your own.
- **Name the keeper of the book**, rotate the role at the turning, and read the book aloud.
- **Write the unit** at the top of the page: *this season we count in hours and in the local money.*
- **Say your offer and your ask** every week, on the lower half of your Playcard.
- **Put a real cost on your Quest** in real units, and meet it from the table before asking beyond it.
- **Send a share inward** to your Home at the turning, in a real amount.
- **Write the answer, before anyone asks,** to *what happens if someone offers to buy what we build?*
- **Look for what is stuck** in your neighbourhood (unused, unnoticed, unasked for) and put one thing back into use this season.
- **Move one flow** of your own money, time or attention from something that creates waste to something that creates value.

A Cell that does these things for one season has a real commons, a habit of giving that does not depend on anyone's mood, and the plain proof that abundance is produced.

## What is not yet settled

- **Who receives the moon distributions.** The mechanism was written when those called out through the narrow door were counted as the body's workers. Players now count by what they have done. Nothing yet says whose work the wider pool resources, by what evidence, or who says so.
- **The tenth's measure.** A tenth appears as the weekly Offering, as the share of Quest flows sent to the commons, as the share of a person's resource flow sent to a local commons, and as the share of the wider pool kept for the shared commons. Each is a freely chosen default, settled, and never a rate owed. What it is measured against (income, surplus, or time and energy given) is open ([10 Pillar III](10%20Pillar%20III%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Economy%20and%20CurrentSee.md)).
- **The size of the engine.** The seasonal Quest flow at the first three percent is given as *hundreds of billions per season*, as *five hundred billion to a trillion a year*, and by napkin arithmetic as about *five hundred billion a season*. These are readings, and they do not reconcile.
- **The hub build-out.** It is priced at ten to twenty trillion dollars, at fifteen, and at fifteen to twenty, and the tier arithmetic reconciles with none of these. One restatement, in one place and marked as an estimate, is owed.
- **The Giants and receivership.** That such institutions are placed into receivership and stewarded as part of the One Planetary Commons is settled. The plan's posture is to hospice the old and open honourable exits. How the two meet, by what process, and under whose law is not yet written.
- **The transparent structure's vehicle.** The temporary ledger is scaffolding, and the body's own commons vehicle is to mature behind it. What that vehicle is, and when the scaffolding comes down, is open.
- **Energy and commitment based income.** It is named as the floor under everyone, and nothing yet says how it is calculated, who funds it, or at what stage it begins.
- **The seventh law.** Whether *we do not sell out* stands beside the other six laws as a structural law, binding on the design itself, or belongs instead among the culture a body chooses, is open.
- **The order of meeting the Giants.** Placing receivership last, after mapping the harm, informing those who steward it, and offering an honourable exit, is this chapter's ordering. Whether that is the wise order in every case is open.

Every dollar figure in this chapter is an approximation, not a forecast.

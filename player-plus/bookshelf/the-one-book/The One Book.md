# The One Book

*The whole of LIØNSBERG in one place, at the size a season can read. Everything in the library coheres here: the Story, the Game, the Flame, the Twelve Pillars and the ØNE at the center, the Pattern and the Way, the Experience from the first door to what lies beyond, the plans and specifications for a civilization that makes life a little more like Heaven for all current and future generations of life, and what to do in every future, including the ones where the lights go out.*

## Three sizes of one understanding

There is one understanding, held at three sizes, so that it survives any reader and any world.

| size | name | what it is for | how long |
|---|---|---|---|
| the door | [[The Little Book of the Great Game]] | play tonight: the Story told at a table, the five moves, the cards, the Flame | an hour |
| the seed | [[The DNA of Heaven]] | regrow everything from one text if only it survives | an evening |
| the whole | **The One Book** (this) | build the civilization: every Pillar's plan and specification, every instrument, every case | a season |

Below the door there is a smaller size still: [[The Twelve Words|the Twenty]] words at the table, and below that the sixty-four marks a person could cut into steel and bury where their elders know. Each size is a compression of the same current best understanding. When one size and another disagree, the smaller is wrong and is corrected from the larger; when the larger and the world disagree, the world wins and the Book improves the next week.

## How to read this

Read the chapters in order once. After that the Book is a reference: each chapter stands alone, every word at the table links to its page in [[The Field]], and the chapter for any future can be opened cold by anyone who has never read the rest.

Every page carries the same mark: *Current best understanding, loosely held · improved each week.* The mark is not modesty. It is the mechanism. This Book is the DNA of a superorganism that does not yet exist, written down before its first cells have joined, and it is improved each week by the cells as they form. Nothing here is final. The *why* is invariant. Every *how* is revisable.

## The one question

Before every move, at every scale, one question: *does this make life a little more like Heaven for all current and future generations of life?* If yes, make the move. If no, stop, turn, and choose again. The Book is long because the world is; the question is short because the Goal is one.

## Start here

If none of this is familiar, this section is the whole chapter. Everything after it says the same things more carefully, and you may stop wherever you like.

**What it is for.** [ØNE](../the-field/%C3%98NE.md) · [Creator's Intent](../the-field/Creator's%20Intent.md) · [Heaven on Earth](../the-field/Heaven%20On%20Earth.md) · for All · forever. Life made a little more like Heaven, each week, for everyone alive and everyone still to come. That is [the Goal](../the-field/The%20Goal.md), and it does not change. Everything below it is our [current best understanding](../the-field/Current%20Best%20Understanding.md) of how, and every how can be improved.

**Why now.** Every system a civilization runs on is under strain at the same time, and for the first time in history there is no outside: one supply chain, one money system, one air. Nearly all of the strain comes from our way of being together. That is the hard news and the good news at once, because what a way of being caused, a changed way of being can heal.

**The heart of the strategy.** A world turns when its consciousness turns. Behaviour cannot be changed from the outside for long. When a person's spirit and consciousness change, new ways of thinking, speaking and acting follow of their own accord, and when enough people change, the norms of a whole culture change with them. The number that is enough is about three in every hundred: three hundred in a town of ten thousand, and two hundred and fifty million on Earth. That is [the first three percent](../the-field/The%20First%20Three%20Percent.md), and reaching them, connected and empowered, is the [tipping point](../the-field/Tipping%20Point%20Strategy.md).

**How it is done.** In small groups. A [Cell](../the-field/Cell.md) is three to thirteen people, optimally four to seven, who choose to walk together by name. It meets once a week, keeps its word, chooses one real [Quest](../the-field/Quest.md) each season, tells the truth about how it went, and hands the same practice to others. The inner work and the outer work are one motion: the Quest that heals a watershed also heals the people who walk it.

**How it spreads.** You [pass the Flame](../the-field/Pass%20The%20Flame.md). For the first three percent, every new Player brings their three, people not already playing, and aims for all three to say yes within three days of their own yes. Three is the minimum, never a cap. Their Cell is their own. After the first three percent, the Flame passes to the wise right people at the wise right time, as they awaken and are ready.

**How we know where we stand.** By the rings. Growth is counted on the Fibonacci [Cycles of Growth](../the-field/LI%C3%98NSBERG%20Cycles%20of%20Growth%20and%20Cycle%20Time.md). Two numbers are read every day: the ring the body stands on, and how many days each ring takes. Ring 40 is 267,914,296 people, the first three percent.

**Where we are.** The Game opened at the September equinox of 2026. Formation runs from 23 to 27 September. The Sprint runs from 28 September to the Winter Solstice, 21 December 2026. After it comes the Wise Pace, set by formation. Our commitment is the first three percent, connected and empowered, by that solstice; we do everything in our power to meet it, and we measure where we stand every day.

That is the Strategy and Plan. The rest is the reasoning, the detail and the honesty, and you can come back for it.

## I. The Goal, the stakes, and the moment

### The Goal

**ØNE · Creator's Intent · Heaven on Earth · for All · forever.** Said in a register anyone can weigh, it is *the total integrated wellbeing, development, and [right relationship](../the-field/Right%20Relationship.md) of all generations of life.* Said in the register of the sacred, it is the Intent of the Creator made manifest here as it is in Heaven. Both readings are true at once, and neither exhausts it ([01 The Spirit](01%20The%20Spirit.md)).

Three things about the Goal govern the whole plan.

**It is received, not authored.** A goal someone wrote can be amended by whoever holds power. A goal received cannot. Nobody in this body is in charge of the aim; everybody is in its service. That is why the Goal is never put to a vote, and why anyone may test it and refine how we state it.

**It is a direction, not a destination.** Heaven on Earth is not a place we arrive at or a date on a calendar. It is a direction that can be walked from anywhere, tonight included, and a condition that deepens as it is lived. We measure against progress, not perfection: *a little more like Heaven, a little more each week.*

**"For All" means all.** Everyone alive, in every place. Everyone not yet born, weighted as though their world mattered as much as ours. The creatures, the forests, the rivers and the soils. And the Whole itself, which is given a seat at the table. The working form is one question, asked before every move at every scale: *[does this make life a little more like Heaven for all current and future generations of life?](../the-field/The%20one%20question.md)* If it does, make the move. If it does not, stop, discern, and choose again.

Because there is one Goal, there can be one measure of worth. [Value](../the-field/Value.md) is [throughput](../the-field/Throughput.md) of the Goal. [Waste](../the-field/Waste.md) is whatever consumes life and energy without producing it. Two Cells that have never met can align because their aims stack into the same larger aim. A shared Goal is the precondition for coordination without a ruler, and it is the one thing in this chapter that is not a draft.

### The stakes

The old order is divided against itself, and a house divided cannot stand. If it comes apart before anything new is ready to hold people, the suffering will be counted in billions of lives. The plan's founding reading named a real, non-zero probability of harm on that scale, and it is carried here as a reading, not a forecast. Nobody is safe in a collapse, and in a world armed with nuclear weapons and artificial intelligence, division has become a danger to everyone at once ([None Of Us Until All Of Us](../the-field/None%20Of%20Us%20Until%20All%20Of%20Us.md)).

It is said plainly and once, and then the work turns to building. Fear is not the fuel here. A frightened reader can be steered by whoever frightens best. A clear reader can build. You are not being recruited into an emergency. You are being handed decisions already thought through, in calm, so that in a hard hour your attention can go to your neighbours.

### The moment

When a complex living system has been pushed to the brink and has not yet collapsed, it can leap into a higher order. That is where we stand. For the first time in history it is actually and immediately possible for humanity to become one functioning body able to co-create Heaven on Earth. The knowledge exists. The food exists. The sunlight that reaches the Earth in an hour is more energy than humanity uses in a year. What is missing is a pattern for holding it all, and enough people living that pattern.

Two thresholds are racing. One is the point of irreversible cascade, past which converging failures foreclose any coordinated response. The other is the point of irreversible awakening, past which enough people have seen through the old story that the change carries itself. The whole outcome of this hour comes down to which threshold is crossed first. The second one is ours to reach. Our [time](../the-field/The%20Window.md) is short, and nobody knows how short; so the wise stance is to act as though the window could close at any time, and to never despair, because grace may hold it open longer than any calculation allows.

## II. The heart of the strategy: a turning of consciousness

### Why the strategy begins inside

Everyone who has tried to change human behaviour from the outside has met the same wall. Behaviour rises from far below conscious thought: from what was laid down in a nervous system across a lifetime, and across generations, in rooms no one remembers visiting. Reform from the outside springs back the moment the pressure lifts. Every campaign of moral improvement in history has curdled on that rock.

And yet, when a person's spirit and consciousness genuinely change, new patterns of thought, speech and action arise on their own, and even long-held harmful patterns can simply fall away. There is a second finding beside the first: the deep human need to belong makes people hold to, or let go of, whole patterns of behaviour according to the norms and values of the people around them.

So the strategy has three parts, and they run together:

1. **The genuine transformation of spirit and consciousness**, in each person, through practice, company and time.
2. **The progressive articulation of new norms and values** — a new [pattern language](04%20The%20Pattern%20and%20the%20Way.md) of how we do things around here, written down, tested and improved.
3. **The paths, bridges and infrastructure** that make the journey from the old way to the new one walkable for everyone, so that no one has to cross it alone.

The order is [Spirit, then pattern, then practice, then outcome](../the-field/Spirit%20Precedes%20Pattern.md). Where the Spirit is right, the right pattern emerges. Where the Spirit is absent, no pattern saves. This is why the plan's first chapters are about the ground and not the structure, and why the daily practice ([06 The Daily Way](06%20The%20Daily%20Way%20%E2%80%94%20the%20baseline%20practice.md)) comes before the weekly meeting, and both come before any strategy.

### Why a story

Consciousness lays itself out as narrative. A people's deepest answers — what is real, what a being is, what everything is for — arrive in the stories told to its children, long before anyone argues about them ([08 Pillar I](08%20Pillar%20I%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Ontology%20and%20Metaphysics.md)). The chains of the old world were made of story rather than steel, and a story is the only vessel large enough to carry what this work carries across generations and across every level of understanding.

So the strategy begins with [THE STORY](THE%20STORY.md): the story of ØNE and All, of the Caves where a people is held by the stories it is told, of the Wilderness where it unlearns and remembers, and of the Promised Land that is not a place but a way of living, co-created a little more each week. It is not a belief anyone is asked to hold. It is a shared language, and we act as if it were true. Read as a true myth or as a game we have agreed to play, what follows is the same.

### The tipping point

A culture does not usually turn because a majority was persuaded. It turns when a committed minority becomes coherent enough that the rest find it easier to move with them than against them. The fraction is about three in a hundred, the same wherever it appears: roughly [three hundred](../the-field/The%20300.md) in a community of ten thousand, and two hundred and fifty million at the scale of the planet. When that many are awake, connected, formed, and playing, the rest of a people follows the way water follows a channel already cut ([Tipping Point Strategy](../the-field/Tipping%20Point%20Strategy.md)).

Three disciplines keep the tipping point real.

**Depth over reach.** A shallow three percent is a fad; a deeply formed three percent is a people. The question to ask is always *are we reaching three percent, or a hundred percent of three percent?* Wide and shallow loses the cascade. Deep and coherent wins it ([Islands of Coherence](../the-field/Islands%20of%20Coherence.md)).

**Coherence over count.** A tipping point is a threshold, not a promise, and it says nothing about which way a system tips. What makes a minority count is its coherence: whether its people keep their word, hold the same Goal, and share the same pattern.

**The means are the end.** A body that wins by the old world's methods has become the old world. So the strategy holds to invitation and never coercion, to attraction rather than promotion, to [propagating rather than scaling](../the-field/Propagate%3B%20do%20not%20scale.md), and to [demonstrating before advocating](../the-field/Demonstrate%20before%20advocating.md).

### The calling

Who are the three percent? Not the credentialed, the loud, or the self-appointed. They are the people their own neighbours would want beside them in a hard year, and they are found by the trust of the people who know them best.

This is [the Calling](../the-field/The%20Calling%20of%20the%20Ecclesia.md). Everyone on Earth is invited to take up their own voice, and the first act of every voice is to name, out of the hundred people they know best, the three they would most trust to help lead their community through what is coming: not family, not the famous, not the loud, but the faithful, people they have watched carry hardship well. When three trusted namings surface the same person, that person receives [the Invitation](../the-field/The%20Invitation.md), carried by the hands that named them. The old word for such a body is *ekklēsia*: those called out of their homes into the square to govern and to do the work of the city ([The Ecclesia](../the-field/The%20Ecclesia.md)).

The calling rests on an inversion that the body learned the hard way. An invitation to *join* gathers watchers, who wait for someone else to act. Being told *you are one of the ones who will do the work* gathers workers. So being chosen as the worker is the content of the message, and that inversion is both how the pattern spreads and what keeps it from growing a centre.

Two doors stand open, and both stay open. The **open door** is for everyone: any person may take up their voice, contribute whatever is real for them, and hold a live voice in shared decisions and a live stake in shared value, on the road to full citizenship for all. The **narrow door** is for those called out to lead: three trusted namings, an invitation by hand, and a yes or a no, both of them sovereign.

And the calling persists. Given the noise and wounds of the old world, a person may need to be invited as many as twenty times, by different voices in different seasons, before they can hear it. No one is written off for not answering. [No one is involuntarily left behind](../the-field/Involuntarily%20Left%20Behind.md): the highest desire is that everyone find their way, and none be lost who wishes to come.

*In depth: [03 The Calling](03%20The%20Calling.md).*

### Hospice the old, build the new

The strategy's posture toward the old world is one sentence long: **hospice the old world rather than fight it, and build the new alongside it, so that everyone has somewhere to go.** A body that contests power becomes a faction and inherits a faction's enemies. A body that builds can keep welcoming people from every side of every quarrel. The old structures cannot be saved in their present form, and the problem is in their design; so the new is grown from the soil up — seed by seed, Cell by Cell, in kitchens and yards and neighbourhoods — and it stands ready to take in whoever walks out of the old.

## III. Wholeness at every scale: wellness, development, and healing as strategy

### All things helping all things rise

The Goal is the integrated wellbeing and development of All, and wellbeing is not a thing a person can have alone. For a person to flourish, their family must be flourishing; for a family, its community; for a community, its land and waters; and every one of those is part of a larger living whole it must also serve. The strategy calls this **all things helping all things rise**: every whole becomes regenerative to the wholes it is part of, and to the wholes that are part of it.

That is why the strategy works at every scale at once, and in the same pattern at each one. Each scale is a [holon](../the-field/Holon.md) — complete in itself and part of something larger — and the whole plan is a [holarchy](../the-field/Holarchy.md) of them:

- **The self.** The daily practice — breath, sleep, food, movement, stillness, gratitude — and the patient work of meeting what was never healed. Rhythm, not intensity, is what forms a person ([The Daily Way](../the-field/The%20Daily%20Way.md)).
- **The Cell.** The small body that witnesses, holds a person in the gap between what they said and what they did without dropping them into shame, and celebrates each small increase. Those three conditions are what let a human being stand at their growing edge for years without breaking. This is the deepest reason the Cell is not optional.
- **The community.** Collective systems of care, the crafts held in a [guild](../the-field/Guild.md), the places a people needs — a hearth, a healing room, a sanctuary, a garden, a burial ground — held as [commons](../the-field/Commons.md).
- **The bioregion.** The land and waters read by their [watershed](../the-field/Watershed.md), not by anyone's map; the health of the river counted beside the health of the people, in one reading.
- **The planet.** One air, one ocean, one living body, and our species returned to its right place within it ([13 Pillar VI](13%20Pillar%20VI%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Ecology%20and%20Living%20Systems.md)).

The holarchy does not end at the planet. Earth is held as its own holonic instance, within the solar holon, within the holons beyond it, toward a cosmic-grade operating system already functioning elsewhere in the cosmos, now coming to Earth and, in time, reaching back out to connect. Held as current best understanding; in depth: [24 The Work and the System §V](24%20The%20Work%20and%20the%20System.md).

### Healing is strategy, not aftercare

Nobody grew up outside the Caves. Every person carries some weight of pain, trauma and conditioning from life in the old world, and the polished carry it as surely as the visibly struggling, often more deeply hidden. A body of wounded people cannot build a whole world, for the same reason an unenlightened people cannot co-create an enlightened society. So [healing](../the-field/Healing.md) is on the strategy's critical path, not beside it.

It works on four planes, and each one is part of the same work.

**Personal.** Healing is a return to wholeness: removing what poisons and supplying what was missing, the body treated as a temple rather than a machine ([12 Pillar V](12%20Pillar%20V%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Health%20and%20Wellness.md)). [Trauma](../the-field/Trauma.md), grief and the long work of the emotions are met with company and with skilled help where it is needed. Before every practice stands the floor of care: *when a member is in danger, in body or in mind, the Circle acts first and records later, and no card is more important than the person at the table.*

**Relational.** Wherever people work closely together, injury accumulates. A body without a way to repair it has two outcomes: the injured leave, or the injury is buried and returns under another name. So every harm is named out loud, heard by the smallest body that includes everyone it touches, and repaired, with the restoration of the relationship as the aim and no one required to stay. *Justice is love in action* ([18 Pillar XI](18%20Pillar%20XI%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Society%20and%20Justice.md), [Forgiveness](../the-field/Forgiveness.md), [Reconciliation](../the-field/Reconciliation.md)).

**Cultural.** Whole peoples carry wounds that are generations deep — dispossession, war, the severing of people from land and elders and rites. These are healed the way cultures heal: by telling the truth about what happened, by restoring the rites that mark a life's thresholds, by honouring the first stewards of every land with an equal seat, and, at the far end of the road, by a [Jubilee](../the-field/Jubilee.md) in which what can be forgiven is forgiven and what can be returned is returned ([14 Pillar VII](14%20Pillar%20VII%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Culture%2C%20Art%2C%20and%20Story.md)).

**Ecological.** No being is fully well on a poisoned watershed. Harm is stopped at its sources, starting with the few sources that cause most of it; waste streams become the mines of a circular economy; soil, water and air are returned to health; and regeneration replaces extraction.

### The shadow work

Heaven is not built by pretending Hell is not operating. The shadow is faced honestly at every scale, and facing it is one of the plan's load-bearing walls ([32 The Turning](05%20The%20Turning%20%E2%80%94%20consciousness%2C%20healing%20and%20wholeness%20at%20every%20scale.md)).

**Within.** Each person meets their own shadow: the fear, the grasping, the old patterns that fire under pressure. This is done in company and without shame, because an unexamined shadow gets projected onto others, and a body that projects its shadow soon finds an enemy.

**Between.** The body names its own [Anti-Patterns](../the-field/The%20Anti-Patterns.md) — the idol, the centre that grows a head, the one who arrives to take and never to give — and builds its protections into its form so that they do not depend on anyone's good mood that day ([Structural Immunity](../the-field/Structural%20Immunity.md)). Anyone may say [stop](../the-field/Stop.md), and it takes effect at once.

**In the world.** The body keeps an honest, prioritized list of the threats to life, reviewed at every season's turning. It first does no harm. It withdraws its energy from systems that wound. It meets predatory patterns with forthright protocols and the minimum necessary force. And it opens honourable exits — off-ramps and amnesty — for those who served the old order, because all of us took part in it, and the future needs their skills. Restoration is chosen over vengeance, and even here, no one is involuntarily left behind ([17 Pillar X](17%20Pillar%20X%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Guardianship%20and%20Order.md)).

### Three years of formation, walked while building

Each person who comes walks about [three years of healing, reorientation and formation](../the-field/The%20Three-Year%20Formation.md) on the way to full citizenship in the new. It is not a probation or a waiting room. It is honesty: when everyone is openly becoming, healing stops being a stigma and becomes what an honest human being looks like on the way out of a long captivity. And the three years are walked *while building*. You do not become worthy and then build. You are changed by the building. There is no entrance exam and no one too broken to start, because starting is itself part of the medicine ([20 The Experience](20%20The%20Experience%20%E2%80%94%20from%20the%20first%20door%20to%20what%20lies%20beyond.md)).

That three-year span is also what shapes the long plan. A person must join by 2030 to have walked their three years by the Jubilee of 2033. The youth who enter at fifteen rise at eighteen, and the rising continues every year after, so that the orientation into the new never has to stop.

This is why the whole of what this Founding Phase does is best named plainly: **the Refounding.** What a single Player walks in about three years toward full standing — healing, reorientation, formation, no entrance exam, changed by the building rather than qualified before it — the whole civilization walks in the same span toward a founding it can stand behind because it has been tested in the world and not merely drafted at a table. Its horizon is the Jubilee named above: the Great Celebration at which what was owed is released, what was broken is reconciled, and the count begins again.

## IV. The work, and the body that does it

### The work: twelve Pillars around one centre

Refounding a civilization is the greatest joint venture in human history, and work of that size needs a breakdown. The first level of it is [the twelve Pillars](../the-field/The%2012%20Pillars%20as%20the%20Work%20Breakdown%20Structure.md), with ØNE at the centre. Every function of life and society sits in one of them, and each has its own chapter in this Book holding its meaning, its plan, its specifications, its instruments, and the first moves a Cell can make in it.

- **The centre — [ØNE](07%20%C3%98NE%20%E2%80%94%20the%20Center.md).** Without alignment with the Source, all the Pillars collapse.
- **I — [Sacred Ontology and Metaphysics](08%20Pillar%20I%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Ontology%20and%20Metaphysics.md).** What is real, what a being is, what orders it, and what it is all for — answered on purpose and held open.
- **II — [Sacred Governance and Stewardship](09%20Pillar%20II%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Governance%20and%20Stewardship.md).** Governance is stewardship, not control.
- **III — [Sacred Economy and CurrentSee](10%20Pillar%20III%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Economy%20and%20CurrentSee.md).** All value flows from ØNE.
- **IV — [Sacred Learning and Mastery](11%20Pillar%20IV%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Learning%20and%20Mastery.md).** A planet of masters serving ØNE.
- **V — [Sacred Health and Wellness](12%20Pillar%20V%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Health%20and%20Wellness.md).** Healing is a return to wholeness.
- **VI — [Sacred Ecology and Living Systems](13%20Pillar%20VI%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Ecology%20and%20Living%20Systems.md).** Regeneration must replace extraction.
- **VII — [Sacred Culture, Art, and Story](14%20Pillar%20VII%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Culture%2C%20Art%2C%20and%20Story.md).** Stories shape the future.
- **VIII — [Sacred Science and Technology](15%20Pillar%20VIII%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Science%20and%20Technology.md).** Knowledge must serve life.
- **IX — [Sacred Architecture and Design](16%20Pillar%20IX%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Architecture%20and%20Design.md).** Form follows function, function follows meaning, and beauty is essential.
- **X — [Sacred Guardianship and Order](17%20Pillar%20X%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Guardianship%20and%20Order.md).** Guardianship protects by making people more able to be free.
- **XI — [Sacred Society and Justice](18%20Pillar%20XI%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Society%20and%20Justice.md).** Justice is love in action.
- **XII — [Sacred Time, Rhythm, and Celebration](19%20Pillar%20XII%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Time%2C%20Rhythm%2C%20and%20Celebration.md).** Communal time is the heartbeat of a healthy society.

The Pillars are built at the same time, not in sequence, because a people needs all of its functions at once. Around each of them, over time, grow joint ventures led by the called-out and guilds that carry each craft. Those ventures keep raising the quality and lowering the cost of every core function of a society, place by place, and the best solutions are gathered into a shared [kit of parts](../the-field/Kit%20of%20Parts.md), so that no community ever starts from zero ([Prototype](../the-field/Prototype.md)). How the work is actually built — promises, pull planning, the last planner, *could · should · can · will · did* — is [22 Building](23%20Building%20%E2%80%94%20the%20Pattern%20of%20Production.md). The work and the system that carries it, in depth: [24 The Work and the System](24%20The%20Work%20and%20the%20System.md).

### The anatomy of the body

A living body grows organs, and each implies the next. This one grows from the bottom up.

**Players.** A [Player](../the-field/Player.md) is a person who has chosen to play. For the first three percent a Player counts by five conditions, not by sympathy — the **Qualifying Criteria**: they have **read the Story**, **taken [the Pledge](../the-field/The%20Pledge.md)** (given their word where others could hear it, and been written on [the Roll](../the-field/The%20Roll.md) with who brought them and who witnessed), **formed a Cell**, **chosen a Quest**, and **passed the Flame for the first time** by bringing in their three. That first passing is what makes the body self-growing.

**Cells.** Three to thirteen people, optimally four to seven, by name, with a day and a place for the weekly [Turn](../the-field/The%20Turn.md): what I said I would do and whether I did it, the gap and what I learned, what I will do this week, what I offer and what I ask. Each person puts something in the middle of the table, [the Offering](../the-field/The%20Offering.md). Each Cell holds one Quest a season, fills one page of fourteen numbered lines at each turning of the sun ([the Season Sheet](../the-field/The%20Season%20Sheet.md)), and plants the next. A Cell carries the whole code and can regrow the whole body from itself. Past thirteen it divides into two, the way a cell does, and both go on.

**Home, supercells, communities.** A Cell's [Home Cell](../the-field/Home%20Cell.md) is the body that hears its season and helps when something is wrong; at first it is the Cell of the one who carried the Flame to it, its [Carrier](../the-field/Carrier.md). Three or more Cells that have become one another's home are a [supercell](../the-field/Supercell.md). Where the Cells of a place number toward three hundred, a [community](../the-field/Community.md) body forms, with a steering group of four to seven that knows it is scaffolding and dissolves its own founding authority, and three seats held for the living systems of the place, for future generations, and for the Whole. Communities federate into bioregions, and so on up. Nothing is absorbed at any level. Each body keeps what it was born with, and the whole holds together because the pattern is shared ([Federation](../the-field/Federation.md), [Subsidiarity](../the-field/Subsidiarity.md)).

**Guide Cells, and the RFG Cell.** A [Guide Cell](../the-field/Guide%20Cells.md) is a Cell of people who hold one field's current best understanding — the healers, the builders, the ones who have done this before — and who answer questions in it. Guidance climbs a ladder, one rung at a time: a person asks their own agent first; what the agent cannot resolve goes to the Cells they are part of; what a Cell cannot resolve it sends to the wider body as a [Request For Guidance](../the-field/Request%20For%20Guidance.md), fielded by the **RFG Cell**, the first of the body's Guide Cells, which brings in the Guide Cells whose domain the Request touches ([Asking for Guidance](../getting-started/Asking%20for%20Guidance%20%E2%80%94%20the%20Ladder%20and%20the%20Request%20For%20Guidance.md)). A Request binds no one to answer and guidance binds no one to follow; the asking Cell remains the decider, and each answer is kept where the next asker will find it. Guidance also runs the other way: the whole may ask the called-out assembly for guidance in turn.

**Agents.** A person and the agents that work for them are one [Player+](../the-field/Player%2B.md). The plus adds reach and no authority. An [agent](../the-field/Agent.md) prepares, drafts, remembers and carries; the person decides, commits, signs and answers. An agent is never a Player, never votes, never speaks in a check-in, and never gives a yes on anyone's behalf. It takes its charter only from its own person. Its companion travels as plain text and is forked and made one's own, the way the Seed is. And everything an agent does can be done by a person with a notebook and no agent at all, only slower; a person with a notebook is a complete player ([25 Players, Agents, and the Intelligence System](25%20Players%2C%20Agents%2C%20and%20the%20Intelligence%20System.md)).

**The Record.** [The Record](../the-field/The%20Record.md) is how the body knows itself. At the scale of a Cell it holds what was said, decided and promised. At the scale of the whole, at each turning, it gives a few numbers and a sentence: the ring we stand on, days per ring, the variance against the milestones, and the one-line why and response. It is kept without adornment, without blame, and without quietly moving the end date. The gap between what the plan says and what the Record says is surfaced the day it appears. [Every defect is a gift](../the-field/Every%20Defect%20Is%20A%20Gift.md), and the gift is worth most while it is fresh.

**The membrane.** Every living whole keeps a boundary that breathes: it lets nourishment in and gifts out by mutual consent, welcomes the new in an orderly way, and keeps a living discernment against what would harm it ([Selectively Permeable Membrane](../the-field/Selectively%20Permeable%20Membrane.md)). The web of names the Calling produces is the body's treasure, so it is held in tiers: public counts and stories; what Cells share with one another by consent; and the person-graph itself, held locally by the people it names. Beneath every digital layer runs the paper contact-tree: each member knows their three and is known by theirs.

### Planning at every scale

There is no master plan that the rest of us execute. There is one *shape* of plan, written at every scale by the people of that scale, and woven together rather than overruled. The vision, values and plans of any place must arise from within that place, because nobody can plan a neighbourhood they have never walked; and every place's plans must still fit together, so that if the old order comes apart, the plans of every family, neighbourhood, community and bioregion hold as one fabric.

The shape is the same seven things at every scale: the Goal restated in that place's own words · the markers, which are the sky's dates · the [milestones](../the-field/Milestone.md), which are the work's dates, each one someone has given their word for · the pull from the furthest milestone back to this week ([Pull planning](../the-field/Pull%20planning.md)) · the [critical path](../the-field/Critical%20Path.md), with no slack on it, and the [float](../the-field/Float.md) shown everywhere else · the leading indicators · and the contingency, shown as it is spent.

A person writes it for a life, a Cell for a season, a Home for its Cells, a community for its ten thousand, a bioregion for its watershed. Two motions run against each other and neither is allowed to win: outward, each place surfacing its own plans from its own real questions; inward, those plans woven across scales so that no place plans in ignorance of its neighbours. Local plans become the local Quests that move the whole. On a single [Playcard](../the-field/The%20Playcard.md) the holarchy is visible: the upper half arrives from above and is the same on every card in the Game — the Goal, the moment, the milestones, this season's plan for the whole — and the lower half is the player's own. The full treatment of planning, week by week and turning by turning, is in [24 The Plan](29%20The%20Plan%20%E2%80%94%20the%20first%20three%20percent.md); the body and its planning at every scale, in depth, in [26 The Body and Its Planning](26%20The%20Body%20and%20Its%20Planning.md).

### Resourcing, and the new economy

A body without a metabolism is a diagram. For real work to happen, resources must flow from the many to the ones doing the work, and on to the Quests that regenerate the world.

**From our own tables.** The early seasons are resourced by the people of the body themselves. Each week each person puts something real in the middle of the table — money, food, hours, a skill, a tool, care — and nobody sets the amount; a tenth is the old measure, and many begin near it and grow ([Voluntary Tithing](../the-field/Voluntary%20Tithing.md)). What gathers is the Cell's commons, spent in the open on its Quest and its members, and a share goes inward to its Home at each turning. Contributions to the wider body move through a radically transparent ledger that anyone can read. About a tenth nurtures the shared commons; the rest goes to the people doing the work, who become centres of distribution for the Quests they value; and the pool divides at every new and full moon, so that value keeps moving to where the work is. That rhythm is also what makes the body impossible to buy.

**In stages.** Every resource number in the plan belongs to its stage, and no stage's plan depends on a pool a later stage opens ([The stages, and the pool discipline](../the-field/The%20stages%2C%20and%20the%20pool%20discipline.md)). In the seed stage the body's own people carry it. As the fruit becomes visible, philanthropic and institutional capital begins to redirect from waste to value. Only after the body's presence is undeniable at planetary scale do the great harvests become possible: the redirection, by consent, of what humanity now spends on weapons and waste toward life — swords into ploughshares ([27 Resourcing and the New Economy](27%20Resourcing%20and%20the%20New%20Economy.md)). As an illustration of scale, not a forecast: at the first three percent, in roughly fifty million Cells, Cells directing even ten thousand dollars each to their seasonal Quest would move on the order of five hundred billion dollars a season toward regenerative work, table to Quest.

**The new economy.** Beneath the flows stands a redefinition: value measured against the Goal, and the books kept in time and energy as well as money. By that measure humanity was never poor; its real wealth is energy, which cannot be destroyed, joined to wisdom and knowledge, which only grow. The new economy moves toward meaningful work and relationships for everyone, toward incentives that make peace more rewarding than war and health more rewarding than sickness, toward local currencies born from commitments kept rather than from debt, and toward circulation rather than accumulation ([10 Pillar III](10%20Pillar%20III%20%E2%80%94%20Sacred%20Economy%20and%20CurrentSee.md), [LUV](../the-field/LUV.md)). Care, the work money never counted, is counted honestly in the record and never priced between neighbours.

**The commons and the hubs.** Every locality's Cells face the same needs — places to gather, learn, heal, make, grow, store and celebrate — and it makes no sense for fifty Cells in one town to build the same capacity fifty times. So each place gathers its commons of shared capacity, beginning in the libraries, schools, halls, barns and empty buildings that already stand ([The Hub](../the-field/The%20Hub.md)). Hubs are called forth by the places themselves, never imposed. Resilience is designed in from the start: every locality works toward provisioning its own core needs indefinitely while staying connected to the rest, and in a hard season its Cells, its hubs and its paper contact-tree become the backbone that feeds people, keeps the peace, and shows the way forward ([21 In Every Future](21%20In%20Every%20Future%20%E2%80%94%20what%20to%20do%20in%20each%20case.md)).

*In depth: [27 Resourcing and the New Economy](27%20Resourcing%20and%20the%20New%20Economy.md).*

## V. The plan by phase

### The measure: the Cycles of Growth

The body's growth is measured on the Fibonacci rings of the [Cycles of Growth](../the-field/LI%C3%98NSBERG%20Cycles%20of%20Growth%20and%20Cycle%20Time.md). They are the prime measure before, during and after the first three percent. Each ring holds the sum of the two before it — the way living things grow, each stage built on the ones before — and the body counts itself by the ring it has reached, not by a headcount chased for its own sake.

Two numbers are read every day:

- **The ring we stand on.**
- **Days per ring** — the cycle time. This is the quantity actually managed. A body that shortens its cycle time compounds; a body that lengthens it is stalling, however many people have said yes.

From Cycle 4 to Cycle 40 is thirty-six rings. To stand on Cycle 40 by the Winter Solstice, a ring every two and a third days is enough.

The rings also carry the body's structural triggers, which are thresholds and not dates. When the first 144 Co-Creators stand, at about Cycle 10, they nominate five to ten respected representatives to seed the joint planning of the whole, and the stewardship of the canon, the guidance function, the treasury path and the instruments begins to distribute into the called-out assembly. As the next rings complete, the first general assembly convenes, refines, and ratifies the strategy, and from then on the *how* belongs to the called-out themselves.

### The tactic for the first three percent: bring your three, within three days

The rings are how we measure. This is how we move through them now.

**Every new Player brings their three — people not already playing — and aims for all three to say yes within three days of their own yes.** Then each of their three-day clocks begins.

- **Three is the minimum, never a cap.** The gifted connectors can and should keep inviting the wise right people.
- **No one waits for a Cell to mature.** Each person invites when ready, starting now. Like the imaginal cells inside a chrysalis, which carry the pattern of the butterfly before the butterfly exists, new Players pass the Flame from the start, and as they gather they begin to specialize into the parts of the one body that is forming ([ØNE Body](../the-field/%C3%98NE%20Body.md)).
- **Onboarding is decentralized.** Each one teaches one. An agent can guide the first steps, and the Cell of the one who invited you is your [Home Cell](../the-field/Home%20Cell.md) — where your TEA is witnessed, your questions answered, your wellbeing cared for. Beside the ladder of guidance, not a rung on it, onboarding office hours are open for troubleshooting ([Bringing Your Friends In](../getting-started/Bringing%20Your%20Friends%20In.md)).
- **For the first three percent, the Pledge includes the commitment** to bring in three people not already playing within three days. It is a commitment kept or not, and the Roll shows which.

*Naming* your three (the nomination, within two days) and *bringing* your three (the Flame, within three days) are two acts, and [24 The Plan](29%20The%20Plan%20%E2%80%94%20the%20first%20three%20percent.md#tomorrow) says each.

### Phase I · Formation — 23 to 27 September 2026

The first eight Players up and running: each with a companion agent, having taken the Pledge, in a Cell. Formation proves that a person can be brought in and set going with an agent's help. The kit ships, the Invitation is written, the minimum trust ledger stands, and onboarding office hours open.

### Phase II · The Sprint — 28 September to 21 December 2026 (84 days)

From eight Players, every new Player bringing three. **The target is three in three days.** The new Players in generation *n* are 8 × 3ⁿ, and the total is 8 × (3ⁿ⁺¹ − 1) / 2. **Sixteen generations** are needed; two hundred and fifty million is crossed partway through the sixteenth. The arithmetic's limit is about five days per generation (at most about five and a quarter).

Each generation of three advances about two and a quarter rings, so at three days a generation the body moves a ring about every one and a third days — nearly twice the pace the solstice needs.

Two numbers are the most sensitive, and both are observable by any Player in their own notebook. **Days per generation** is the most sensitive of all: the eighty-four days are fixed by the sky, and the length of a generation decides how many generations fit inside them. **Commitments kept** is the second: the fewer kept, the shorter each generation must be. The full chart, generation by generation, and the arithmetic behind it are in [29 The Plan — the first three percent](29%20The%20Plan%20%E2%80%94%20the%20first%20three%20percent.md).

**The commitment, and the daily measure.** The first three percent, two hundred and fifty million people, connected and empowered by the Winter Solstice of 2026. We do everything in our power to meet it. Each day the count of Players and the ring we stand on are read against the chart, and days per generation and commitments kept are read from the Roll: who brought whom, when, and whether the commitment was kept. The tracked numbers: how many invited, how they responded, how many said yes, what they pledged, and the percent of commitments kept — the yes line leading, the ring the measure. A gap is said plainly the day it appears and answered with a re-pull — days per generation and commitments kept are the two places to look first — never with a moved line.

What the Sprint asks of each person is small, and it is the whole of it: **read the Story, give your word, form your Cell, choose your Quest, and bring your three within three days.**

### Phase III · The Wise Pace — after 21 December 2026

Bringing your three within three days ends with the first three percent. It is the tactic for that stretch alone: once two hundred and fifty million stand, three further generations of three would reach everyone on Earth, faster than anyone can be formed. After it, the mechanism becomes inviting **the wise right people at the wise right time, as they awaken and are ready** — no count per person and no clock, the Flame carried hand to hand at the pace of trust. Wisdom chooses the slower pace, and formation sets it, as the [Arc of Play](../the-field/The%20Arc%20of%20Play.md), the health of the Cells, and the markers ahead require. The rings remain the measure.

### The markers beyond the Sprint

The long plan is pulled backward from its furthest marker, so that what must be started early is seen early. A **marker** is a date the sky gives — a solstice or an equinox — and nobody can move it. A **milestone** is a date the work gives, and belongs to whoever gave their word for it ([Temporal markers apart from milestones](../the-field/Temporal%20markers%20apart%20from%20milestones.md)). Markers keep the rhythm; milestones carry the promises. The plan aims to finish one season ahead of each marker, with no slack on the critical path to that date, and shows the season between as contingency that is counted out loud as it is spent ([Honesty about time](../the-field/Honesty%20about%20time.md)).

- **Winter Solstice 2026 — the first three percent, connected and empowered.** The commitment above, measured daily.
- **Winter Solstice 2027 — the body that no longer needs its founders.** The cascade sustains itself. Cells exist that were formed by people who never met anyone from the first season. The first supercells and the first community bodies stand, the first translations keep the numbering the same in every language, and a full year of honest Records has been kept.
- **Winter Solstice 2030 — everyone who desires, invited.** No person on Earth is more than one hand away from an invitation carried by someone they know, in a language and register they can receive. The instruments of formation stand ready before the called arrive, the standards are stable, and the called-out assembly has ratified the strategy.
- **Winter Solstice 2033 — the Covenant and the Jubilee.** The first cohort of the world has crossed; debts that could be forgiven are forgiven, captives freed, land returned, and the Flame passed on to generations nobody at that table will ever see — at that solstice, or the day the last cohort has crossed. The ten-year grand strategy that began in the spring of 2024 completes nominally at the Spring Equinox of 2034, so the Jubilee is set one full season early on purpose ([The 10 Year Grand Strategy](../the-field/The%2010%20Year%20Grand%20Strategy.md), [20 Year Generational Contingency](../the-field/20%20Year%20Generational%20Contingency.md)).

Then twenty years to regenerate the Earth. And after that the Story continues, into worlds and ages we will not see.

### The bets, named as bets

Every number fed into the arithmetic is an assumption until the body measures it. The arithmetic itself is exact; the chart is a commitment, not a forecast; the daily count is the measurement. The plan rests on four bets, each with its instrument and its point of adaptation, so that reality rather than attachment governs ([The load-bearing bets](../the-field/The%20load-bearing%20bets.md)).

1. **The calling compounds.** Trust calling out trust sustains ring after ring of growth. *Instruments:* invitations accepted, Cells formed, first-season Cell survival, commitments kept, days per generation. *Adaptation:* if the chain is not compounding, the Invitation's grammar, register and channels are redesigned in the open. The bet is on the calling itself, never on one form of it.
2. **Formation holds the called.** Those who answer can be formed into Cells that live. *Instrument:* the health of the Cells, not the count of members. *Adaptation:* wherever Cells stall, the kit, not the people, is treated as defective and improved.
3. **The economics ignite from below.** Voluntary contribution and the tenth can resource the early seasons from our own tables. *Instrument:* the open ledgers. *Adaptation:* the vehicle and the distribution rules evolve in the open, and the anti-pooling rhythm holds.
4. **Visibility converts.** When the body becomes visible to itself, the awakening majority recognizes it as their own. *Instrument:* the resonance measured on every invitation surface, season over season.

## VI. In every future

### The one answer

Nobody knows how the coming years will go. So the plan was tested against the whole spread of them — gentle, turbulent, hard and severe, crossed with how far the Game has reached — and the finding is the strongest thing in it: **the response is the same in every future.** A Cell of three to thirteen people who keep their word to each other week by week, choose one real Quest a season, put something in the middle of the table, write the fourteen lines at each turning, and pass the Flame within days is the body that survives the hard futures and the body that composes Heaven in the gentle ones. It needs no permission, no funding, no infrastructure, and no crisis to justify it. It runs on a notebook, a table and a given word.

What changes from one future to another is the tempo, the medium, what must be protected while the work goes on, and the one number worth watching. Each family of futures, with its signal and its judgment decided in advance, in the calm, is in [21 In Every Future — what to do in each case](21%20In%20Every%20Future%20%E2%80%94%20what%20to%20do%20in%20each%20case.md). **If the lights are out, open there.**

Three things are built for the hard futures and cost nothing in the gentle ones. The clock is the sun, so the turnings fall on the same days everywhere and nobody owns them. The floor is paper, so the bottom rung of every ladder is a complete place to stand ([Screens and paper](../the-field/Screens%20and%20paper.md)). And the pattern, not any contact between Cells, is what makes the many one body — so a body cut into pieces by a firewall, an outage or a war can be rejoined by strangers who hold the same fourteen lines.

### Carrying the seed through any future

The whole of this understanding travels as plain text: the Story, the Game, the Flame, this Book, the field of the words, the kit. Nothing about it lives only in one place. It can be copied whole from one drive to another, read without a network, and run with a small language model on an ordinary machine — or with no model at all.

Carrying it is part of the plan, not an afterthought to it. Every Player+ keeps a complete copy of the seed where they can reach it, keeps a paper copy of what matters most — the seed text, the Cell's agreements, the last Season Sheet, the three names and three numbers of the floor of care — and knows how to hand the whole of it to someone else, drive to drive, with a local model sized to the machine it lands on. The way to do that is [Passing the Seed — Drive to Drive, With a Local Model Sized to the Machine](../keeping-yourself-safe/Passing%20the%20Seed%20%E2%80%94%20Drive%20to%20Drive%2C%20With%20a%20Local%20Model%20Sized%20to%20the%20Machine.md).

The agents descend the same ladder. When one rung is lost, a Player+ steps down a rung without losing the Game: frontier capability, then a small model on your own machine, then no agent at all. Each rung is a complete place to stand. Though ninety-nine Cells in a hundred were lost, any Cell that survives carries the whole code and can regrow the rest.

## VII. How to hold it

**As current best understanding.** The Goal is invariant. Every *how* in this chapter — every phase, number, date, instrument and word — is the truest we can say now, and it is expected to change. That is not modesty; it is the mechanism by which a living body improves itself without dissolving and without hardening into the thing it was built to replace.

**Revised by the body.** The plan changes by [Proposal](../the-field/Proposal.md): a better way, written in a sentence on line 12 of a Season Sheet, gathered at a turning, adopted into the next edition. It never changes by silent edit. What one place learns is offered to the whole, and what the whole settles flows back to every place, adopted or declined with reasons. Each season the body looks at itself from above — inward, outward, backward, forward — in an [OmniSpection](../the-field/OmniSpection.md), done in community.

**Roles, not persons.** No single life is a mechanism of this plan. Stewardship is held by roles — keeper, host, carrier, the RFG Cell, the steering groups that dissolve their own founding authority, the called-out assembly — and roles rotate, because a role held forever quietly becomes a throne. The stewardship of the whole distributes by prior design, beginning at the first 144, so that the pattern never again depends on any single life. Until that is complete, every key and every duty is held redundantly. The centre may equip; it never operates. Any flow that needs a centre's ongoing hand is a defect in the design.

**Tested against the world.** Watch for the signs that would show this wrong: tables that keep the form and stop keeping their word; bodies that federate and quietly grow a head; people who complete the outward moves and are not visibly changed after years; a door that produces watchers rather than workers; a Cell that never plants another and is celebrated anyway. Any of these, seen widely, is evidence against some part of what is written here, and the evidence wins. When this Book and the world disagree, the world is right, and the Book improves the next week.

**Carried in the Spirit it came from.** Nothing here is asked to be believed. All of it is offered to be tried. If something is lacking, fill it. If something does not belong, remove it. If something is in error, improve it. The Spirit of the pattern already lives in the one reading it: seek that, and its Source, first.

*Read the Story. Play the Game. Pass the Flame.*

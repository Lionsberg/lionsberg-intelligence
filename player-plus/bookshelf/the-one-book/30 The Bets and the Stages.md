# The Bets and the Stages

*The road from the opening of the Game to the Jubilee and past it, told as stages the body grows through and measured by the rings it stands on. Every number belongs to its stage, and nothing is claimed outside it. This chapter says what we are betting on and what would show each bet wrong. It says what the body does in a hard hour at each stage of its growth, and how the growth itself changes once the first three percent stand. It gives depth to [30 The Strategy and Plan](28%20The%20Strategy%20and%20Plan.md). The day-by-day chart of the Sprint lives in [29 The Plan — the first three percent](29%20The%20Plan%20%E2%80%94%20the%20first%20three%20percent.md) and is not repeated here.*

## Start here

If none of this is familiar, this section is the whole chapter.

**The body grows in stages, and each stage is a band of rings.** Growth is counted on the Fibonacci [Cycles of Growth](../the-field/LI%C3%98NSBERG%20Cycles%20of%20Growth%20and%20Cycle%20Time.md). Ring 4 is the first eight Players, ring 10 the first 144, and ring 30 about two million. Ring 40 is 267,914,296 people, [the first three percent](../the-field/The%20First%20Three%20Percent.md). Four stages lie along that road:

- **the seed** (rings 1 to 10)
- **the organism** (10 to 30)
- **the tipping** (30 to 40)
- **the harvest** (40 and beyond)

Each stage makes possible things the one before could not. No stage's plan leans on what only a later stage can give.

**The sky gives the markers.** The Winter Solstices of 2026, 2027, 2030 and 2033 each stand for one milestone:

- **2026:** the first three percent, connected and empowered.
- **2027:** a body that no longer needs its founders.
- **2030:** everyone who desires it, invited.
- **2033:** the Covenant and the [Jubilee](../the-field/Jubilee.md).

Behind them stand the [10 Year Grand Strategy](../the-field/The%2010%20Year%20Grand%20Strategy.md), nominally complete at the Spring Equinox of 2034, and the [20 Year Generational Contingency](../the-field/20%20Year%20Generational%20Contingency.md) behind that. Over all of them stands [the Window](../the-field/The%20Window.md), which nobody can measure.

**Four bets carry the weight.** The calling compounds. Formation holds the called. The economics ignite from below. Visibility converts. Each one has an instrument, a sign that would show it wrong, and a change we have already agreed to make if that sign appears.

**The growth changes at the first three percent.** Until then the tactic is to bring your three, people not already playing, within three days. Three is the minimum, never a cap. After that the tactic ends and the rings stay the measure. The body then invites the wise right people at the wise right time, as they awaken and are ready, at a pace set by formation.

**The Story is the Quest.** The stages are chapters of one journey toward the Goal of greatest worth. Each Cell's seasonal [Quest](../the-field/Quest.md) is a chapter inside it.

**The response is the same in every future.** At every stage, a hard hour is carried the same way:

- a Cell that keeps its word
- a paper contact-tree
- the seed in more than one pair of hands
- a locality that can stand on its own

If the lights are out, open [21 In Every Future](21%20In%20Every%20Future%20%E2%80%94%20what%20to%20do%20in%20each%20case.md).

That is the chapter. The rest is the reasoning and the detail.

## I. Where the road begins, and what the road has taught

The stages ahead rest on a few things the body has already learned. They are carried here as understanding.

**The span of the plan.** Seven years of preparation, from 2017 into 2024, articulated [the seed](../the-field/The%20Golden%20Seed.md). They wrote its pattern language, designed its systems and tried its forms with hundreds of groups in dozens of nations. The [Grand Strategy](../the-field/The%2010%20Year%20Grand%20Strategy.md) began on 1 March 2024, and its first milestone has not changed since. That milestone is the first three percent of humanity, about two hundred and fifty million people, connected and empowered by the Winter Solstice of 2026. They are gathered in small groups of three to thirteen, optimally four to seven, which can stack up and align their work.

**The critical path runs only through the tipping point.** For a time the plan kept open the possibility of working through the institutions of the old order. That possibility is closed. The critical path now runs wholly through the [tipping-point strategy](../the-field/Tipping%20Point%20Strategy.md): a new way of living, grown alongside the old one before the old one comes apart, so that everyone has somewhere to go. The body hospices the old order and does not fight it.

**The centre equips; it never operates.** Every provisional structure of the founding years has been laid down. No central organization, account or directing office remains. Nothing at the centre can be attacked, bought or beheaded. Any flow that needs a centre's continuing hand is a defect in the design. The body carries itself, or it is not yet a body. The plan puts one question to itself: *can a fully decentralized network of awakening people organize itself at planetary scale, in time?* The question is still open, and the people who answer the Calling are the ones who answer it.

**Watchers gather to an invitation to join; workers gather to a calling.** Before the Game opened, the body grew only a few rings in the months it had planned to cross thirty. The cause was found and it is not flattering. Those who came waited for a centre to run the Game for them, because the invitation had asked them to *join*, and joining implies a host. Nobody had been asked, by name, by people who know them, to *lead*. So the invitation became [the Calling](../the-field/The%20Calling%20of%20the%20Ecclesia.md) ([03 The Calling](03%20The%20Calling.md)). The variance was recorded plainly, without blame and without quietly moving the end date. That is the discipline every stage below inherits. *[Every defect is a gift](../the-field/Every%20Defect%20Is%20A%20Gift.md), and the gift is worth most while it is fresh.*

**The Game opened at the September equinox of 2026.** Formation ran from 23 to 27 September. The Sprint runs from 28 September to the Winter Solstice.

## II. What the stages hang on: rings and markers

Two kinds of time run through this chapter, and they are kept apart on purpose.

**The rings are the body's own time.** A ring is a threshold of growth: a count of Co-Creators standing, on the Fibonacci sequence, each ring the sum of the two before it. The body counts itself by the ring it has reached, never by a headcount chased for its own sake. The rings are [the prime measure](../the-field/LI%C3%98NSBERG%20Cycles%20of%20Growth%20and%20Cycle%20Time.md) before, during and after the first three percent. Two numbers are read every day: **the ring we stand on**, and **days per ring**. Days per ring is the number actually managed. A body that shortens it compounds. A body that lets it lengthen is stalling, however many people have said yes.

| 4 | 8 | the first eight Players | the seed |
| 10 | 144 | the first 144 | seed → organism |

| **40** | **267,914,296** | **the first three percent** | tipping → harvest |
| 43 | 1.13 billion | 13% | the harvest |
| 47 | all* | | the harvest |

**\*All is not a fixed number.** The last ring is reached where the Fibonacci sequence crosses Earth's population at the time it occurs, so its ring number and its count will change depending on how many people are alive on Earth when that crossing actually happens. The ring named here (47) is a current reading, not a fixed marker.

**The markers are the sky's time.** A [marker](../the-field/Temporal%20markers%20apart%20from%20milestones.md) is a solstice or an equinox. It comes whether or not anything is finished, and nobody can move it. A [milestone](../the-field/Milestone.md) is a date the work gives, and it belongs to whoever gave their word for it. Markers keep the rhythm; milestones carry the promises.

**Why the stages are keyed to rings and not to dates.** What each stage makes possible depends on how many people stand and how deeply they are formed, not on the calendar. That includes who can be called, what can be resourced, what can be governed and which organs can form. A stage is entered when its threshold is crossed. So every structural trigger in this plan is a threshold, and no date is fixed to any of them. For example, when the first 144 stand, the joint planning of the whole is seeded, whatever the day. The markers set a tempo for reading the rings, and the gap between the two is the variance the [Record](../the-field/The%20Record.md) reports. The markers serve the work; the work does not serve the markers. And nobody knows how far Spirit and consciousness, which are not bound to any one place, may carry things beyond the markers we set.

## III. The stages, ring by ring

The plainest discipline in this plan is also the oldest: **every number belongs to its stage.** Nothing in the Strategy and Plan is to be read outside the stage it belongs to. Each stage makes some things true and available and leaves others out of reach. The pools that resource the transformation open one after another, each opened by the stage before it.

The Book and the Kit, complete *before* the first 144 stand. The first Cells, the first Quests, and the first [Guide Cell](../the-field/Guide%20Cells.md), the RFG Cell. At ring 10, the first 144 seed the joint planning of the whole. | Quest flows at scale · institutional capital · any lever held by a state |

**The pool discipline.** No stage's plan may depend on a pool that only a later stage opens. The seed and the organism are carried wholly by the body's own people, from their own tables. That is also why the body cannot be bought and why its legitimacy is beyond dispute ([The anti-pooling law](../the-field/The%20anti-pooling%20law.md)). How the flows work belongs to [27 Resourcing and the New Economy](27%20Resourcing%20and%20the%20New%20Economy.md): the Offering, the tenth, the open ledger, the division at the moons and the centres of distribution.

### The seed · rings 1 to 10

From the first eight to the first 144. Everything here is small, and everything is load-bearing.

**What must be finished before it ends.** The instruments of formation must be ready: the Book that carries the whole code, and the Kit that lets any kitchen table run the practice. The sequence is strict because it is the rule most often broken: *the called must never arrive to find the table bare.* In practice that means the Seed and the Kit are complete, and can be run on paper, before the first hundred stand.

**What happens at ring 10.** When the first 144 Co-Creators stand, they name five to ten respected representatives to seed the joint planning of the whole. Stewardship then begins to move out of any single pair of hands and into the called-out assembly: the canon, the guidance function, the treasury path and the instruments. The plan names its own single point of failure and schedules its removal. Until that removal is complete, every key and duty is held redundantly, so that the pattern never again depends on any one life ([26 The Body and Its Planning](26%20The%20Body%20and%20Its%20Planning.md)).

### The organism · rings 10 to 30

From 144 to about two million, a network of people who know each other becomes an organism, with organs, a metabolism and a memory.

**What forms.**

- **Supercells:** Cells that have become one another's Home.
- **Community bodies:** they form where the Cells of a place number toward three hundred. Each has a steering group of four to seven that knows it is scaffolding and dissolves its own founding authority.
- **Hubs:** they begin in libraries, schools, halls and barns that already stand, and each place calls its own forth.
- **Guilds:** they gather around each core function, and facilitation spreads by each one teaching one.
- **The first general assembly:** as the rings after 144 complete, several hundred to a thousand more of the wise right Co-Creators are connected. They meet in a first general assembly, where representatives of the body refine and ratify the Grand Strategy and this plan. From then on, the *how* belongs to the called-out themselves.

**A reading of scale.** Supporting some fifty million Cells needs, as a mere beginning, about twelve thousand people trained in each of twelve core functions of the body. It is carried as a reading of scale, not a quota. In our current understanding, the guilds call and train those people. No training department does.

### The tipping · rings 30 to 40

From about two million to the first three percent, the body becomes visible to itself and to the world, and the world responds in every way at once, welcoming and hostile. [The 300](../the-field/The%20300.md) stand up in community after community. The most mature communities begin to coordinate across their bioregions. The first governments co-operate in domains where adoption is already high.

**What ring 40 is reckoned to make possible.** Some 250 million people, in something like fifty million Cells, would give the body:

- a presence in every part of the planet
- spread that carries itself
- the capacity to coordinate
- a footing for the next horizons: thirteen percent at ring 43, and everyone who desires by 2030

The seasonal Quest flows at this scale are illustrated in [30](28%20The%20Strategy%20and%20Plan.md) and reckoned in [36](27%20Resourcing%20and%20the%20New%20Economy.md). They are readings of scale, not forecasts.

### The harvest · ring 40 and beyond

Only once the body's presence is undeniable at planetary scale do the great harvests become possible. The largest is the phased redirection, by consent, of what humanity now spends on the machinery of death toward the infrastructure of life: the Giants of Old — governments, corporations and militaries among them — placed into receivership and stewarded for the good of all as part of the One Planetary Commons ([27 Resourcing and the New Economy](27%20Resourcing%20and%20the%20New%20Economy.md)). The organizing and logistical strength of the world's armed services turns toward building, and toward preventing and relieving disasters rather than causing them, at a balanced phase-down of about ten percent a year — swords beaten into ploughshares, weapons turned to tools of life. Force, where any is needed, is held to the least that protects.

The Calling runs through every domain, and that includes the people who carry the tools of security and defence. When enough of them stand, those tools can be turned toward life from within and by consent, never seized and never won by conquest. The posture of the whole plan holds here too: [hospice the old world](28%20The%20Strategy%20and%20Plan.md) rather than fight it, and build the new beside it. Honourable exits, off-ramps and amnesty stay open to everyone who served the old order, because all of us took part in it and the future needs their skills.

**The figures for this stage are estimates and are not reconciled.** The freed military and defence spending is put at about fifteen to twenty trillion dollars. The full hub network is put at ten to twenty trillion. These are two different quantities that appear to have been joined somewhere along the way. Both are carried as readings, neither is a promise, and no plan of an earlier stage leans on either.

**The road itself has a name.** The whole climb from the seed to the harvest — the Founding Phase these stages measure — is walked at the pace of the same blue-belt arc a single Player walks: about three years, formation while building, no entrance exam ([28 The Strategy and Plan](28%20The%20Strategy%20and%20Plan.md)). What a Player becomes in three years toward full standing, the whole body becomes in the same span toward a founding it has tested rather than merely drafted. This is **the Refounding**: not a single ring but the whole stretch of them, closing at the Great Celebration and the Jubilee, where what was owed is released, what was broken is reconciled, and the count begins again.

### In the Sprint, the rings run ahead of the organs

*A current reading, offered as a proposal.* The four stages were first drawn when growth was expected to take a season or more per ring. The Sprint aims at three in three days. At that pace it crosses the seed, the organism and the tipping in about seven weeks: ring 10 around 7 October, ring 28 around 31 October, and ring 40 between 16 and 18 November, once the ring is counted by the five Qualifying Criteria rather than by a raw headcount. People can be counted that fast. Organs cannot form that fast. Supercells, community bodies, hubs, guilds and assemblies grow at the pace of trust and formation. Institutional and public pools open at the pace of visible fruit.

These are two separate processes, tracked apart on purpose. The growth rings track the rate at which the great remembrance and reassembly is occurring — how fast people are finding their way back to one another. The specialization of the imaginal cells into specialized cells, organs and organ systems of the one body is a separate matter of how the One Body is maturing and assembling itself, and it occurs as the body matures, not on the ring's schedule. So in the Sprint the stages of *count* and the stages of *maturity* come apart, and the discipline holds on both sides:

- **The pool discipline holds against the calendar as well as against the rings.** Standing on ring 40 in November does not open the harvest's pools in November. A plan that assumed it did would be leaning on a later stage's pool, which is exactly what the discipline forbids.
- **The organs are counted apart from the ring.** An honest report at a turning gives the ring the body stands on. It also gives how many supercells, community bodies and living hubs actually exist.
- **The Wise Pace is where maturity catches up.** This is much of why the cascade slows by choice after the solstice (section V).

### The pressures every stage must carry

Five pressures bear on every stage. None of them waits for a particular ring.

**Scale and speed.** Reaching two hundred and fifty million in a season is growth without precedent. It is possible only because every Player invites a few people they already know. The first three percent are also already awakening and already looking; the invitation names what is stirring in them and gives it an immediate way to act. The answer to this pressure is the chart and the daily measure ([24](29%20The%20Plan%20%E2%80%94%20the%20first%20three%20percent.md)).

**Depth against speed.** Fast growth can thin the transformation until it means nothing. Several things hold the depth: the Cell's intimacy and its given word, the initiation of the Story and the Pledge, the Daily Way, and three years of [formation](../the-field/The%20Three-Year%20Formation.md) walked while building. The test is always *are we reaching three percent, or a hundred percent of three percent?*

**Coordination at scale.** Fifty million Cells cannot be coordinated from anywhere, and they need not be. Five things coordinate them without a centre:

- the shared pattern, the same at every scale
- subsidiarity, with each decision made at the smallest scale that can carry it
- the 300 in every community
- the Season Sheets that sum across languages
- the [Player+](../the-field/Player%2B.md) layer, where a person's agents carry the Book, the record and the look-ahead so that guidance reaches further than any number of human facilitators could

**Resourcing.** Many hands can carry what would crush a few. A hundred million dollars of working capital sounds large for a body; across two hundred and fifty million people it is well under a dollar each. The body carries it from its own tables, spread across every Cell, and it pools nowhere.

**Opposition.** Transformation on this scale will be resisted by those who profit from the old way. The body's protection is in its form, not its force: no centre to attack or buy, a seed carried as plain text in ten thousand copies, sovereignty at every scale, and an approach that is invitational, voluntary and without violence. That keeps the moral ground, and a body that keeps the moral ground cannot be made into an enemy without its opponents showing who they are.

## IV. The markers, and what each asks of the stages

The long plan is pulled backward from its furthest marker, so that whatever must start early is seen early ([Pull planning](../the-field/Pull%20planning.md)). The working rule is simple:

- Aim to finish one season ahead of each marker.
- Run the critical path to that date with no [float](../the-field/Float.md).
- Show the season between as contingency, counted aloud as it is spent ([Honesty about time](../the-field/Honesty%20about%20time.md)).

The milestones under each marker are set out in [24 The Plan](29%20The%20Plan%20%E2%80%94%20the%20first%20three%20percent.md). What follows is where each marker sits among the stages, and what it asks of them.

**Winter Solstice 2026 — the first three percent, connected and empowered.** Ring 40. The commitment is two hundred and fifty million people, connected and empowered by this solstice, and we do everything in our power to meet it. Each day we measure where we stand:

- the ring we stand on, and days per ring
- the count of Players against the chart
- days per generation and commitments kept, read from [the Roll](../the-field/The%20Roll.md)

A gap is named plainly the day it appears. It is answered with a re-pull, never by moving the line. This is the one marker with no season of contingency in front of it: its plan date and the marker are the same day, and the daily measure is what guards it.

**Winter Solstice 2027 — a body that no longer needs its founders.** The cascade sustains itself. If every instrument of the centre vanished that night, the body would go on, with its Book in hand, its trust-tree known and its Cells sovereign. By this marker:

- Cells exist that were formed by people who never met anyone from the first season.
- The first supercells and community bodies stand.
- Translations keep the numbering the same in every language.
- A full year of honest Records has been kept.

This is the marker at which the organism's maturity has caught up with the tipping's count.

**Winter Solstice 2030 — everyone who desires it, invited.** No one on Earth is more than one hand away from an invitation, carried by someone they know, in a language and register they can receive. The marker is stated as reach, not acceptance, because acceptance is sovereign. Some will decline. Some will need to be asked twenty times, by different voices in different seasons. The instruments of formation are ready before the called arrive, the standards are stable, and the called-out assembly has ratified the strategy. The [seven-year window](../the-field/The%207%20Year%20Window%20of%20Crisis%20and%20Opportunity.md) in which all humanity is invited, begun in the spring of 2024, closes its nominal span here. This marker is measured by reach, not by a percentage of acceptance. Reach is the part that is ours to deliver. The rest belongs to each person's yes.

2030 is a hard marker, not a hopeful one, because formation takes about three years. A person has to join by 2030 to have walked their three years by the Jubilee. Which solstice of 2030 is meant is carried below as unsettled; this Book reads it as the Winter Solstice.

**Winter Solstice 2033 — the Covenant and the Jubilee.**

- **The first cohort of the world has crossed.** Everyone who joined has walked [three years of healing, reorientation and formation](../the-field/The%203%20Year%20Lag.md) into full [citizenship](../the-field/Citizenship.md) in the new. The young people who entered at fifteen have risen at eighteen, and that rising goes on every year, so orientation into the new never has to stop.
- **Debts, captives and land.** Debts that can be forgiven are forgiven, captives are freed, and ancestral lands are returned.
- **The Flame goes onward.** It is passed to generations nobody at that table will ever see.
- **The date.** All of this happens at that solstice, or on the day the last cohort has crossed.
- **Its own milestones.** A feast of that size is planned from far off. So the rites of the feast, the accounting of what can be forgiven, and the handing-on are milestones in their own right, with dates and people who have given their word.

**Spring Equinox 2034 — the Grand Strategy's nominal completion.** The ten years are the seven-year window of invitation plus the three-year formation of its last cohort. The Jubilee is set one full season earlier on purpose, so that the plan applies its own honesty rule to its own last day.

**The 20 Year Generational Contingency.** Twenty years of contingency stand behind the ten-year plan. That is a buffer of two hundred percent, sized that way because nothing like this has been attempted, and a buffer sized as though it were routine would be a lie told to a schedule. What the contingency protects is the real commitment: that the transformation completes *within a single generation*, even if it takes three times as long as planned. The ten years are the plan; the generation is the promise.

The contingency is watched, not spent blindly. It is monitored alongside the critical path, so the body can see how much has been used and how fast. It also lives inside the Window. Contingency can only be spent while the Window is open, and a late plan may find that the opportunity it counted on is gone. So lateness is recorded and never hidden, and the answer to lateness is ignition, never despair.

**Then twenty years to regenerate the Earth.** After the Jubilee, twenty years are given to regenerating the living world: its soils, waters, forests and air, and its species returned to their paths. This is not the same twenty years as the contingency. One is a buffer protecting a promise; the other is a span of work.

**Then the honest horizon.** A civilization's *direction* can turn within about ten years. Rebuilding its whole body takes generations. It will take a century or two, not a decade, to remake the built world, house every family in a home worthy of them, and bring every watershed back to health. That is an inheritance, not a disappointment. Ours is the generation that turns the ship and lays the keel, planting trees whose shade we will not sit in. None of it waits on an invention. Every piece of the new world already exists somewhere, proven in some village, watershed or workshop. What is missing is all of it, everywhere, together.

**And the farthest horizon.** After the Jubilee the Flame passes to the rising generations. In time, life may be carried beyond this one world, long before this sun can no longer hold it. Held as current best understanding: each planet and moon its own holonic instance, within the solar holon, within the holons beyond it, toward a cosmic-grade operating system already functioning among the civilizations of the cosmos, now coming to Earth and, in time, reaching back out to connect ([24 The Work and the System §V](24%20The%20Work%20and%20the%20System.md)). That is kept here as the far edge of the long view, not as a plan. It is also a reminder to plan across the widest stretch of time and space we can hold, while becoming very good at the one specific, measurable, season-bound Quest in front of us, in our own place. People tend to overestimate what can be done in a short time and underestimate what can be done in a long one. Holding both horizons corrects both errors.

After that the Story goes on into worlds and ages we will not see. [Heaven on Earth](../the-field/Heaven%20On%20Earth.md) has no last page: it is whole in every step that is truly walked, and it grows a little more like Heaven every day, forever. That is why the Jubilee is a turning and not an ending, and why [the Eternal Divine Play](../the-field/The%20Eternal%20Divine%20Play.md) has no final round.

## V. How the growth changes at the first three percent

**Phase III begins now, at the Wise Right Pace.** The wise right people at the wise right time is not a principle waiting for ring 40 to arrive; it can and does run alongside the Sprint from the start, for everyone the three-day tactic does not reach. The Wise Right Pace also governs the Sprint itself: since no one knows when the Window will close, the aim throughout is to finish ahead of schedule wherever we safely, responsibly and wisely can, never by cutting corners on culture or quality.

### Until ring 40: bring your three, within three days

For the first three percent the tactic is exact. **Every new Player brings their three, people not already playing, and aims for all three to say yes within three days of their own yes.** Three is the minimum, never a cap: gifted connectors can and should keep inviting the wise right people. No one waits for a Cell to mature. New Players pass the Flame from the start, the way the imaginal cells in a chrysalis carry the butterfly's pattern before the butterfly exists. As they gather, they begin to specialize into the parts of the [one body](../the-field/%C3%98NE%20Body.md) that is forming.

For the first three percent the [Pledge](../the-field/The%20Pledge.md) includes this commitment, so it is a commitment kept or not kept, and the Roll shows which. The chart, the arithmetic and the two most sensitive numbers (days per generation and commitments kept) are in [24 The Plan](29%20The%20Plan%20%E2%80%94%20the%20first%20three%20percent.md). The practical how is in [Bringing Your Friends In](../getting-started/Bringing%20Your%20Friends%20In.md).

The tactic has this shape because of the Window. When a committed minority must be gathered before the heaviest weather, the length of a generation is the most sensitive parameter in the whole plan, and it is also the one that costs nothing to change.

### After ring 40: the wise right people at the wise right time

Once two hundred and fifty million stand, about three more generations of three would reach everyone on Earth. The same arithmetic that serves the Sprint would harm the body after it. It would outrun formation, outrun the three years each person needs, outrun translation into the tongues people dream in, and outrun the health of the Cells. So at the first three percent the tactic ends, by choice.

**What ends:**

- the three-day clock
- the commitment in the Pledge to bring three within three days
- the generation as the unit the body manages to

**What begins.** The body invites the wise right people at the wise right time, as they awaken and are ready. The pace might be a generation a season, or a generation a year, whatever the [Arc of Play](../the-field/The%20Arc%20of%20Play.md), the health of the Cells and the markers of 2027, 2030 and 2033 call for. **Formation, not arithmetic, sets the pace.** The readiness that counts is the person's own, since their first minute either resonates or does not and both answers are sovereign. It is also the readiness of the table they will come to, which must already be set.

**What stays:**

- **The rings remain the measure.** Ring and days per ring are still read every day. Days per ring is now allowed to lengthen on purpose, and the Record says so on purpose, so that a chosen slowing is never mistaken for a stall.
- **Passing the Flame.** [Passing the Flame](../the-field/Pass%20The%20Flame.md) remains the move a living body cannot skip, because a Cell that never passes it ends with the people who started it.
- **Both doors stay open.** The open door lets anyone take up their voice and contribute. The narrow door is three trusted namings and an invitation carried by hand.
- **The Calling keeps going.** A person may need to be asked twenty times, by different voices in different seasons, before they can hear it. No one is written off for not answering yet. The highest desire is that everyone find their way and that none be lost who wishes to come ([None Of Us Until All Of Us](../the-field/None%20Of%20Us%20Until%20All%20Of%20Us.md)).

**This is not a slowing of the heart.** The Sprint gathers the people who are already looking. After it, the work is to reach those who are not yet looking, and they are reached by trust, not by speed. They are reached in their own [register](../the-field/Register.md) and their own tongue, by someone they know, often in a hard season when they have seen which neighbours behaved well. A crisis is an unusually good way of finding the faithful. Past the first three percent the Calling becomes less a cascade and more a tide that rises with readiness.

## VI. The load-bearing bets, and what would show each wrong

A plan that billions of lives may depend on has to say what it knows and what it is betting. Being honest about uncertainty is not a weakness in such a plan. For the kind of leader it is looking for, that honesty is the first evidence that it can be trusted. Every number fed into the arithmetic is an assumption until the body measures it. The arithmetic itself is exact, the chart is a commitment rather than a forecast, and the daily count is the measurement.

Everything rests on four falsifiable bets. Each has a name, an instrument, and a point at which the plan changes, so that reality governs rather than attachment ([The load-bearing bets](../the-field/The%20load-bearing%20bets.md)). A bet with no instrument is a hope. A bet with no point of change is a creed.

### 1 · The calling compounds

**The bet.** Trust calling out trust (people naming, by hand, the ones they would most trust) sustains ring after ring of growth where a broadcast gathered only watchers. History's nearest cases say it can: movements have grown from a dozen people to millions by passing hand to hand. The same history warns that most such chains stall within a few generations of their founders. The bet states that warning against itself.

**The instruments.** These are read from the first season, with the daily ring and days per ring over them all:

- invitations accepted
- namings per voice
- Cells formed
- Cells that survive their first season
- the share of new Players who bring their three
- the days each generation takes, against three in three days

**What would show it wrong:**

- A generation smaller than the one before it, again and again. The effective multiplier has fallen toward one, and a multiplier of one is not slow growth; it is no growth.
- Days per generation lengthening while commitments kept fall. These are the two sensitive numbers moving the wrong way together.
- The chain thinning with distance from the founders, with no Cells formed by people who never met anyone from the first season. This is exactly how movements that pass hand to hand have died before.
- A door that produces watchers instead of workers, with arrivals waiting for someone else to act.

**The change we have agreed to.** In the Sprint, a gap is named the day it appears and met with a re-pull, looking first at days per generation and commitments kept. At the seasonal pace, if the chain is not compounding by the second season's turning, the Invitation's grammar, register and channels are redesigned in the open. **The bet is on calling itself, never on any one form of it.**

### 2 · Formation holds the called

**The bet.** Those who answer can be formed into Cells that live, through the Book, the practice of the table and the rhythm of the seasons.

**The instrument.** The health of the Cells, not the count of members:

- how many Cells survive their first season
- commitments kept as a share of those made
- [Season Sheets](../the-field/The%20Season%20Sheet.md) actually filed
- line 13 read across the whole body

Line 13 is *how is the Cell, one word and a line*. It is the cheapest instrument anyone owns for catching a body that is on plan by the numbers and dying underneath.

**What would show it wrong:**

- Tables that keep the form and stop keeping their word.
- Cells that keep the Turn for a season and then go quiet: *we will start again after this one.*
- People who make every outward move and, after years, are not visibly changed.
- A Cell that never plants another and is celebrated anyway.

**The change we have agreed to.** Wherever Cells stall, the Kit is treated as defective and improved, not the people. Most loss happens between saying yes and sitting at a living table, and that is a Kit problem before it is ever a people problem.

### 3 · The economics ignite from below

**The bet.** Voluntary contribution and the tenth can resource the early rings entirely from beneath, from our own tables, with no captured lever anywhere.

**The instrument.** The open ledgers themselves, readable by anyone.

**What would show it wrong:**

- Quests chosen and left unresourced season after season, while the ledgers show value pooling instead of moving to where the work is.
- Growing dependence on one large giver or one institution, so that losing a single source would cost the body a season.
- A plan at any stage quietly beginning to lean on a pool that only a later stage opens.

**The change we have agreed to.** The vehicle and the rules of distribution evolve in the open. The division of the pool at every new and full moon does not bend, because it is armour as much as it is accounting: no more than a fortnight of flows can ever be captured, frozen or lost ([27 Resourcing and the New Economy](27%20Resourcing%20and%20the%20New%20Economy.md)).

### 4 · Visibility converts

**The bet.** When the body becomes visible to itself and to the world, through its Stories, its Board and its moment, the awakening majority will recognize it as their own.

**The instrument.** The [resonance](../the-field/Resonance.md) measured on every surface that carries an invitation, improved like any other instrument, season after season. The aim for the first minute, among those already looking, is nine in ten.

**What would show it wrong:**

- Resonance flat or falling across seasons, even as the telling improves.
- A [door](../the-field/The%20door.md) that grows while the tables do not. That metric is lying to you.
- Visibility that draws attention and not recognition, and gathers audiences instead of Players.

**The change we have agreed to.** The register is widened until the Story can be read aloud by a family in any village, in the tongues people actually dream in. The telling is improved from the stories Cells actually send, not from what a text imagined.

### Beneath the four: what is not ours to bet

*Research and proposal, marked as such.* Two assumptions sit under the four bets. We cannot instrument them, because the world sets them and we do not.

**The Window.** Every stage and marker assumes that the interval in which the crossing is possible stays open long enough. Its width can only be discerned, never calculated. The wise stance is to act as though it could close at any hour and never to despair, because the same unknowing that could close it early may hold it open longer than any reckoning allows ([The Window](../the-field/The%20Window.md), [We Do Not Know](../the-field/We%20Do%20Not%20Know.md)).

**The fraction.** The whole [tipping-point strategy](../the-field/Tipping%20Point%20Strategy.md) rests on the reading that about three in a hundred is enough to turn a people. The evidence around it is suggestive, not conclusive:

- **Civil resistance.** Studies of the last century's civil-resistance movements found that campaigns drawing sustained, active participation from around three and a half percent of a population rarely, if ever, failed within the period studied.
- **Social conventions.** Laboratory studies of social conventions found that a committed minority nearer a quarter of a group was needed to tip a norm.

These measure different things, and neither settles ours. What they share is that *coherence* counts for more than numbers, which is why this plan holds to depth over reach. If the body stands on ring 40 and the culture has not moved, then either the fraction or the depth was wrong, and the Record will say which.

### Who reads the bets, and when

The bets are read on the body's three clocks:

- **Every day:** the ring, days per ring and the two sensitive numbers.
- **At every Turn:** commitments kept and the gap, at every table.
- **At every turning of the season:** all four instruments against each bet, and the Record's report of the variance in a few numbers and a sentence. The body looks at itself from above in an [OmniSpection](../the-field/OmniSpection.md), done in community.

A bet that is failing is named as failing at the turning where that first shows.

When this Book and the world disagree, the world is right, and the Book improves the next week.

## VII. The Story as the Quest toward the Goal of greatest worth

The stages are not only a schedule. They are chapters of a story, and that is not a figure of speech laid over the plan. It is how the plan is carried.

**Why a story carries the plan.** A mind holds who it is, where it is and what it is for as a story before it ever holds these things as an argument. A story is also the only vessel large enough to carry something of this scope through the generations and across every level of understanding. A strategy is read by the few who read strategies. A story is told at a fire, to a child, in a hard year, and it arrives whole ([Story](../the-field/Story.md)).

**The shapes every people already knows.** Some stories recur in every culture and age, because they both fit the human mind and shape it. They trace the common road, with its struggles, longings, fears and hopes. The most widely true of them become the most sacred, and they can be gathered, reconciled and woven together like threads. Six of these [archetypes](../the-field/Archetype.md) matter most here:

1. **The journey out and back.** Someone leaves the ordinary world for a place of wonder and danger, meets forces larger than themselves, prevails, and comes home with a gift for their people.
2. **The Quest.** A search for something of great worth, whether a thing, a knowing, or one's own changing, carried through trials by a person and usually by a company of companions.
3. **The metamorphosis.** A deep change in the one who walks the road, often a descent into darkness and a rising renewed.
4. **The initiation.** Trials that carry a person across a threshold from one season of life into the next.
5. **The fall.** A descent from a higher state to a lower one, with innocence, grace or standing lost, and redemption waiting within it.
6. **The contest of good and evil.** A struggle with a world's fate in the balance, mirroring the struggle within every person and every people.

**How the strategy takes them up.** The [Quest](../the-field/Quest.md) toward [the Goal](../the-field/The%20Goal.md) is set inside one archetypal story, which unfolds as a story of stories as the body grows. [THE STORY](THE%20STORY.md) is that telling. It runs out of [the Caves](../the-field/The%20Caves.md), where a people is held captive by the stories it is told, and through [the Wilderness](../the-field/The%20Wilderness.md), where it learns, unlearns, remembers and rebuilds. It runs toward [the Promised Land](../the-field/The%20Promised%20Land.md), which is not a place anyone reaches but a way of living, co-created a little more each week.

**Why the Goal of greatest worth.** A quest is worth what its goal is worth. Every smaller quest (a garden, a healed river, a reconciled family) is truly worthy, and every one of them adds up within one aim: [ØNE](../the-field/%C3%98NE.md) · [Creator's Intent](../the-field/Creator's%20Intent.md) · Heaven on Earth · for All · forever. No goal is worth more, because every other worthy goal is part of it. That is why two Cells that have never met can know their Quests belong to one Quest. It is also why [value](../the-field/Value.md) is measured as [throughput](../the-field/Throughput.md) of this Goal and everything else as [waste](../the-field/Waste.md).

**The stages as chapters.** *A reading, offered as a proposal.* The six shapes are not six separate stories. They are faces of the one journey, and the stages walk through them:

- **The fall** is the old world as we find it: the long night of extraction and control, and the knowing, deep in nearly everyone, that something is badly wrong.
- **The initiation** is each person's first threshold and their [three years of formation](../the-field/The%20Three-Year%20Formation.md), walked while building.
- **The metamorphosis** is the chrysalis of the Sprint. The imaginal cells pass the Flame before the butterfly exists, and the body specializes into its organs through the organism and the tipping.
- **The Quest** is every Cell's season: one real thing it can point to afterward, a chapter that becomes a story (lowercase) sent back to the whole.
- **The contest of good and evil** is faced without flinching and without making enemies. The shadow is met within, between and in the world ([32 The Turning](05%20The%20Turning%20%E2%80%94%20consciousness%2C%20healing%20and%20wholeness%20at%20every%20scale.md)), the old order is given a hospice rather than a war, and restoration is chosen over vengeance.
- **The journey out and back** ends in a homecoming with a gift. The homecoming is the Jubilee, and the gift is the Flame [passed](../the-field/Pass%20The%20Flame.md) to generations not yet born. The one who makes the journey is not a single person. It is a people.

**How the Story grows as the stages advance.** The practice has five parts:

- We tell a story large enough to hold us all.
- We keep telling our story as it unfolds.
- We invite every person, group, community, bioregion and people to tell theirs.
- We weave the stories that were broken apart back into one cloth.
- As the work bears fruit, we tell what changed. That shows the Way works, and it feeds the turning that draws in both people and resources.

Cells play, people are changed, they tell what happened, the telling calls others, and the wheel turns faster. At each stage the stories are gathered and woven anew: the seed's first stories, then the organism's, the tipping's and the harvest's. The practice itself belongs to [29 The Stories](22%20The%20Stories%20%E2%80%94%20ten%20trillion%20stories%20woven%20back%20into%20ONE.md) and [Share the Stories](../the-field/Share%20the%20Stories.md).

Read the Story as a true myth or as a game we have agreed to play. Either way what follows is the same, and it is real.

## VIII. Resilience and emergency response across the stages

The coming years will shake the world in ways, places and seasons no one can name in advance, and the plan builds for that from the start. Resilience, and where possible [anti-fragility](../the-field/Anti-Fragility.md), meaning growing stronger for being shaken, is built into everything the body makes. This section gives the design as a whole and then how it grows stage by stage. The judgments for each family of futures, made in advance and in calm, are in [21 In Every Future](21%20In%20Every%20Future%20%E2%80%94%20what%20to%20do%20in%20each%20case.md). **If the lights are out, open there.**

### The design, whole

**Every place learns to stand on its own feet.** Communities and bioregions bring the core functions of life back home: food, water, energy, shelter, care and exchange. The aim is that each place can provide for itself indefinitely while staying open to, and exchanging with, the rest. Supply chains are rebuilt close to home and in ways that restore. Every community keeps its own plan for resilience and recovery, woven through planning at every scale ([35](26%20The%20Body%20and%20Its%20Planning.md)) into a planetary fabric with many centres. That fabric holds if any thread breaks, and it holds if any centre falls.

**Readiness lives inside everything.** It is not a department. Foresight is applied to local and planetary disasters, so that preparedness, continuity, community strength and economic steadiness are built into ordinary solutions from the first design. The body learns from the people who already know this work: those who prepare for disaster, those who respond first, and those who keep watch over the water. Living systems are watched as they change, because a damaged environment brings weather and seasons that can no longer be relied on, and places must be able to adapt as they walk into the unknown. Careful measurement forecasts what can be foreseen, and that buys time before the worst.

**Help arrives in a shared language.** One open, interoperable way of responding, recovering and restoring is shared everywhere. It covers flood, fire, drought, earthquake, storm, eruption and the breakdown of social order, and its aim is to restore the flow of life as quickly as possible. First responders share standards, training and supply lines across the world, so that anyone skilled can travel anywhere and step straight into coordinated work as though at home. Modular, portable facilities stand ready to go where they are needed:

- places of healing
- shelters
- centres for coordination
- the humblest of them, a watch-station on a shore or a crossroads that is also a source of water, food, shelter, sanitation and connection

In time a planetary plan for resilience and regeneration grows. It has many centres and no single seat.

**Migration is met with preparation, not fear.** The changes ahead will move the living: people, animals, plants and forests.

- **The most vulnerable come first.** Resources go to them first, to relieve suffering and lessen the need for anyone to leave home against their will.
- **Everyone has a home.** Camps for the displaced are made unnecessary by making sure every person has a place to call home, where they can begin to heal and flourish.
- **Every people has a homeland.** Each is free to gather again and renew its culture in a homeland of its own.
- **The land is opened to other species.** The ancient paths of migrating creatures are reopened, crossings for wild things are woven back through the built world, and the moves a changing world requires of plants and animals are helped along.
- **Boundaries are both firmer and more open.** They are open to everyone willing to honour the agreements of a place, and they are firm in the culture and values that protect it ([Migration](../the-field/Migration.md), [Welcoming The Stranger](../the-field/Welcoming%20The%20Stranger.md)).

**The called-out body carries the hard hour.** When old systems fail and disaster comes, the body bears the weight: Cells that have practised, hubs that are stocked, and a trust-tree that holds when the grid does not. It feeds the people of each place, keeps the peace, and shows a way forward. Answering emergencies is not a side office of the new way of life. It is among the first fruits by which a neighbourhood learns what the new way is ([Islands of Coherence](../the-field/Islands%20of%20Coherence.md)).

### Stage by stage

The design is whole from the first day. What can carry it grows with the rings.

**The seed.** Resilience is the Cell itself and what it carries:

- **The [floor of care](21%20In%20Every%20Future%20%E2%80%94%20what%20to%20do%20in%20each%20case.md).** When a member is in danger in body or mind, the Circle acts first and records later.
- **The paper contact-tree.** Every member knows their three and is known by theirs.
- **A paper copy.** Of the seed text, the Cell's agreements and the last Season Sheet.
- **[The seed on a drive](../keeping-yourself-safe/Passing%20the%20Seed%20%E2%80%94%20Drive%20to%20Drive%2C%20With%20a%20Local%20Model%20Sized%20to%20the%20Machine.md).** In more than one place and more than one pair of hands, with a local model sized to the machine it lands on.
- **The descent practice.** Once a season each Cell practises stepping down both ladders (how much machine it has, and how much text it holds) to the lowest rung every member can reach.
- **The shared channels.** They move in three steps: Discord, then Buzz, then Sovereign Comms. Each is tested before the next is relied on, and paper runs beneath all three.

**The organism.** Resilience becomes shared:

- Supercells pool for larger Quests and can carry one another through a hard season.
- Community bodies write the first local plans for resilience and recovery.
- The first hubs, in libraries, schools, halls and barns, become the first places to gather when something goes wrong.
- Guilds hold the crafts a hard season needs: healing, growing, building, repair and mediation.
- The [Kit of Parts](../the-field/Kit%20of%20Parts.md) gathers whatever works anywhere, so that no community meets a flood knowing less than the last community that did.
- Model drives are held by named holders across Cells, with a shelf of models for every class of machine.

**The tipping.** Resilience becomes a fabric:

- Community plans are woven across bioregions, so that no watershed plans in ignorance of its neighbours.
- The shared way of responding spreads, with the training that makes a responder at home anywhere.
- Communities with the highest adoption begin to provide their core needs locally.
- Where public bodies co-operate, the body's contact-trees and hubs serve alongside whatever of the old response still works.

**The harvest.** Resilience becomes infrastructure:

- The turning of the machinery of death toward life makes the full hub network possible, with healing, shelter and coordination facilities ready to go anywhere.
- The organizing strength of the old armed services turns toward preventing and relieving disaster.
- Assisted migration, reopened corridors and homelands for returning peoples become the ordinary work of a planetary body.

**At every stage, the one answer.** Whatever the stage and whatever the future, the response is the same. A Cell of three to thirteen people who keep their word week by week, choose one real Quest a season, put something in the middle of the table, fill the fourteen lines at each turning, and pass the Flame is the body that survives the hard futures and brings Heaven into the gentle ones. Four things make it that body:

- **Its clock is the sun,** so nobody can seize it.
- **Its floor is paper,** so nobody can switch it off.
- **The pattern makes the many one body,** not contact between them, so the body can be cut apart and rejoined by strangers holding the same fourteen lines.
- **Any Cell can regrow the rest.** If ninety-nine Cells in a hundred were lost, any Cell that survives carries the whole code and can regrow the rest ([The Collapse-Ascension Nexus](../the-field/The%20Collapse-Ascension%20Nexus.md)).

The work succeeds in every future. It fails only if it is not undertaken.

## IX. How to hold the stages

**As current best understanding.** The Goal does not move. Every stage, ring, marker, figure and bet in this chapter is the truest we can say now, and each is expected to change. The plan changes by [Proposal](../the-field/Proposal.md), gathered at a turning. It never changes by a silent edit.

**With both kinds of honesty.** Honesty about time means markers kept apart from milestones, contingency counted aloud, variance named on the day it appears, and nothing quietly moved to a new date. Honesty about knowledge means bets named as bets, readings marked as readings, and figures carried with the season that spoke them.

**With urgency and peace together.** The Window is real and finite, so we act as though it could close at any hour. Grace may hold it open longer than any reckoning, so we never despair. The answer to lateness is ignition. The answer to speed is formation.

**In the Spirit it came from.** Nothing here asks to be believed. All of it asks to be tried. If something is lacking, fill it. If something does not belong, remove it. If something is in error, improve it.

*Read the Story. Play the Game. Pass the Flame.*

## What is not yet settled

- **How a ring is declared.** Nothing yet says what evidence moves the body from one ring to the next, or who says so. The stages of this chapter hang on it.
- **Stages of count and stages of maturity.** This chapter proposes that in the Sprint the rings run ahead of the organs, and that the two be reported apart. No instrument yet counts supercells, community bodies or living hubs the way the ring is counted.
- **Who counts as a Player after ring 40.** For the first three percent, a Player counts after passing the Flame for the first time by bringing their three within three days, as pledged. Once the tactic ends, it is not yet written whether a Player counts by the other four conditions alone, or what the Pledge then asks about passing the Flame.
- **The thresholds of the bets.** The point of change for the first bet was set in seasons ("by the second season's turning") when growth was expected to take seasons. The Sprint reads it daily. What counts as *not compounding*, in numbers, and who declares a bet lost, and in what room, are not yet stated.
- **Which solstice in 2030.** Whether this marker means the summer or the winter solstice is not yet fixed beyond doubt. This Book reads it as the Winter Solstice, for symmetry and because the later reading is the more honest one. Because formation takes three years, this half-year decides which cohort is in the Jubilee.
- **How the contingency is counted.** It is not stated whether the twenty years run after the ten or include them. One reading puts the outer bound near 2054, the other near 2044. Nothing yet says who watches the contingency, or on what surface.
- **The harvest's figures.** Fifteen to twenty trillion for freed military spending, and ten to twenty trillion for the hub network, are carried as estimates and are not reconciled.
- **The stories as chapters.** Reading the six archetypes as the chapters of the stages is this chapter's proposal, open to the next edition.

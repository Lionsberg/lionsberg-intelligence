# THE GAME

*The rules of [[The Great Game of LIØNSBERG|the Great Game]] as they are played at one table. These are the parts that are the same at every table on Earth, so that ten thousand tables are one game, and ten thousand green shoots are one ecosystem. Everything else is yours to make your own. THE STORY is the shared language; THE GAME is the shared pattern of action.*

*Players: everyone who chooses · [[Cell|Cells]] of three to thirteen · a turn each week · a round each season · playing time: as long as you love · ages: all.*

---

## THE GAME. THE GOAL.

All of us, together, exiting [[The Caves|The Caves]], journeying through [[The Wilderness|The Wilderness]], and making every place we touch a little more like [[Heaven On Earth|Heaven]].

That is **THE GAME**. Heaven is **[[The Goal|The Goal]]**. Everything else is a matter of how.

## The Why

Because something is deeply wrong, and it is not you.

Because the old world is collapsing and the new is not yet built, and no one is coming to build it for us. Because a few people in a room, keeping their word to each other season after season, is the only way a world has ever been changed. Because it works: a Cell that plays for a season can point to something real. And because it is the adventure of a lifetime, and you were born into it.

No belief is required at the door. You may read [[THE STORY]] as a true myth or as a game we have agreed to play; either way, what follows is the same, and it is real.

## The Shared Journey

From where we are to where we are going — locally as you perceive it, collectively out of The Caves, into The Wilderness, and toward [[The Promised Land]] that we must co-create through THE GAME.

The Promised Land is not a place anyone arrives at. It is what a people becomes, and what they build, a little more each week. Every Cell walks the same road, at its own pace and in its own place.

## The Vision — Where We Are Going

Every Cell is invited to ask each member: *"How would you like the world to be?"* — and to craft from the answers a shared [[Vision|vision]] of what Heaven might look like in your own community and world.

What kinds of things belong in Heaven, that we would like to cultivate?
What kinds of things do not belong in Heaven, that we would like to remove?
What does Heaven right where you are look like, feel like, taste like, smell like?

Craft the most vivid imagery you are able to of what life is like when Heaven becomes reality, right where you are. This is where we are going.

## Sensemaking — Where We Are

In contrast to where we are going and why, each Cell continually makes sense of, and writes down, its current best understanding of where it is on the journey.

Each week, in a few honest lines: what is done, what is in flight, what is not begun, and what the Cell senses that no one has said yet. Not a report — a reading, the way sailors take one. That is [[Sensemaking|sensemaking]], and it is the half of navigation that the Vision cannot do.

## The One Question

Before every move in the game, one simple question:

***Does this make life a little more like Heaven?***

If it does, make the move. If it does not, stop, discern, and choose a better one. A ten-year-old can ask it. So can a tribe. It is [[The compass question|a compass]] that always points toward The Goal, and it never changes.

When two moves both pass, the older players ask a second question: *Is this the highest thing we could do right now, for ourselves and for the whole?* Not every good move is the next move.

## The Five Moves, at the Table

1. **[[Form a Cell]].** Three to thirteen people who choose to walk together, by name, and optimally four to seven. Give it a name. Agree, out loud, how you will treat each other. Two of you host each season, and the hosting passes around. A [[Circle (as gathering)|Circle]] is a gathering; a **Cell** is a Circle that plays — a fully functioning part of the whole. At thirteen it divides into two, the way a cell does, and both go on.
2. **[[Choose a Quest]].** One real thing, for one season, that your Cell can do together and point to afterward. Real, near, and yours. [[Quest|Quests]] stack up and align to produce throughput of The Goal.
3. **Learn and grow.** Each week, each of you says what you will do. The next week, each says whether you did it, and what you learned from the difference. That is the whole discipline of the game: *say what you will do; do what you said; learn from the gap.*
4. **[[Share the Stories|Share your stories]].** Every Quest becomes a [[Story|story]], and the story travels further than you can.
5. **[[Pass The Flame]].** Each of you carries the game to your three — people not already playing, whom you know are ready — within three days. They form their own Cell, not a branch of yours. Then there are two Cells, and the fire is loose in the world.

Those are [[The Five Moves|the five]] anyone can start with tonight.

## The Seven Moves, the Breath of a Cell

The canon's deeper cycle, run daily, weekly, seasonally, and across lifetimes:

**Form a Cell · [[Weave Spirit, Hearts, and Minds]] · Choose a Quest · [[Do the Work]] · [[Celebrate, Learn, and Improve]] · Share the Stories · Pass The Flame.**

[[The Seven Moves|The sequence]] is not arbitrary. A Cell that has not woven coherence will not choose well; a Cell that never celebrates will not learn; a Cell that never passes the Flame dies with its founders.

## The Week: [[The Turn]]

Once a week, gather. The same day, the same place, if you can. An hour or two is plenty. Open in silence. In turn, each person says:

- what I said I would do, and whether I did it;
- the gap, honestly, and what I learned from it;
- what I will do this week;
- what I have to offer, and what I ask of the others.

Someone marks [[The Circle's Scorecard|the Scorecard]]. Someone tells one story. Each person puts something in the middle for [[The Offering|the Offering]]. Close in gratitude.

That gathering is the heartbeat of the whole game, and it is the same beat in every Cell on Earth. Between the weeks, the daily way: pause, ground, breathe, remember, realign, recommit. Each month, a synthesis.

A [[Commitment|commitment]] is *I will ___ by ___*, said aloud at the table to people who depend on it. Kept, or a gap to learn from. Nothing else in the Game binds you.

## The Season

The game breathes in [[Season|seasons]] of about ninety days, turning at each solstice and equinox — by the sun, which no one owns. A Quest is chosen for a season, and a season is short enough that ninety days from now you can point at what you did.

At each turning, a Cell does four things: it counts its season on the Scorecard; it tells its best story; it chooses the next Quest; and it fills one page, [[The Season Sheet|the Season Sheet]], and sends it to its home. Then the wheel turns again.

The season has four faces — plant, grow, harvest, rest — and at its end the Cell sees itself from above: an OmniSpection, inward, outward, backward, forward, done in community. Retrospection alone is not enough.

## The Cards

**[[The Playcard]].** One card per player, refreshed at each Turn, kept in a stack so the season can be read backward. A Cell holds a card of the same shape, and so does a supercell, and so does a world. Its upper half arrives from above and is the same on every card of the Game: The Goal, the Moment, the milestones, this season's plan for the whole and the stretch of road this player's part touches, the rules and the Score. It changes only by a [[Proposal|proposal]], at a turning. Its lower half is the player's own, filled at the Turn: who we are and our roles; where we are; how we are — what blocks us, what we offer, what we ask; our Quest this season; what I will do this week; what I did last week and the gap; our measures; our proposals; when we read again. A hand copy on any paper counts exactly as much as a printed one.

**[[The Questcard]]**, one per Quest, with its *done when* line. **[[The Circle's Scorecard|The Scorecard]]**, marked each week: commitments made, kept, and kept as a share of made — the first number any group should know about itself. **The Circle Sheet**, who we are. **The Season Sheet**, the one page that leaves the table, in fourteen numbered lines that are the same in every language. That page is what makes ten thousand tables one game.

Nothing typed ever replaces a sheet. If the two disagree, the paper with two signatures is the record.

## The Body: [[Home]], [[Home Cell]], [[Supercell|supercell]]

Every Cell has a **home**: the body that hears its season, keeps a copy of its sheets, and helps when something is wrong. Your **Home Cell** is the Cell that holds you; *Home* alone is your own place inside it. When your Cell is new, your home is the Cell that carried the Flame to you.

Three or more Cells that have become one another's home form a **supercell**; supercells form supercells; below a Cell, its subcells. That is how ten thousand people become one body without anyone in charge of it: a Cell knows its home, and a home knows its Cells, all the way up and all the way down. Nobody is in charge of it. Everyone is.

A [[Keeper|keeper]] keeps the record. Two [[Host|hosts]] hold the room each season. A [[Carrier|Carrier]] is the one who brought you the Flame, and it never changes.

## The Offering

Each week, each person puts something in the middle of the table: money, food, hours, a skill, whatever is real for them. Nobody sets your amount. A tenth is the old measure, and most people begin there and grow.

What gathers at the table is the Cell's [[Commons|commons]]. The Cell spends it on its Quest and on its own, and at each turning sends a meaningful share to its home, which does the same. The share flows inward to the table you sit at, never upward to anyone who rules you — see [[Voluntary Tithing]].

The tithe is structural. What players give one another beyond it stays sovereign, and never becomes a rule.

## The Way Out

Anyone at the table may say *stop*, at any moment, for any reason. Everything pauses at once, and only the one who said it starts it again.

Anyone may leave, at any time, in peace. Leaving in order means: say so, finish or hand over, and let your lines stay. Nothing is erased. Nobody is governed who has not agreed, and agreement can be withdrawn; silence is never a yes.

And beneath all of it, the floor: when a member is in danger, in body or in mind, the Cell acts first and records later. No card is more important than the person at the table.

## What The Game Asks of You

At the least: come to the table each week; say what you will do and do it, or say why not; put something in the middle; tell one story a season; carry the Flame to your three.

More is welcome and is never required. Nothing binds without your own word, and your word is the whole game.

## How We Win

Together, or not at all. *None of us, until All of us.*

And the winning is not waiting at the end. Every kept word, every fed neighbour, every wound mended, every child who learns that the world can be trusted — we are winning with every move.

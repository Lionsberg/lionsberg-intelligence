# Glogs and the LIØNSBERG Gazette

*Proposed at Player+ Jam 4, 2026-09-26. How your agent helps you keep a Game Log, follow your friends', and offer your best stories to the Gazette. A proposal until it soaks; the people keep what helps.*

No Quest is finished until it is told ([Quests](../the-great-game/05%20Quests.md)), and Share Your Stories is one of [the Moves](../the-great-game/03%20The%20Seven%20Moves.md). A Glog is where your stories start. The Gazette is where the best of everyone's are gathered.

## 1. What a Glog is

A **Glog** is a Game Log: instead of a blog, your own running log of your play. Your Quests, your stories, the art you made, what you are learning.

- It is plain text, in your house, and it is yours.
- Each entry is chosen for a tier ([Working Relationships and Disclosure Tiers](../keeping-yourself-safe/Working%20Relationships%20and%20Disclosure%20Tiers.md)): you alone, your partners, your Cell, a room, or public.
- Nothing goes public except by your word.

It sits beside [the Record](../the-great-game/08%20The%20Record.md), not in place of it. The Record keeps what you said you'd do and what you did; the Glog tells what it was like.

## 2. How your agent helps

- **At closeout, or once a week,** your agent offers a short Glog entry drafted from your sessions, in your voice ([Closing Out Sessions](../working-with-other-houses/Closing%20Out%20Sessions.md), [the-retrospective](../../skills/the-retrospective/SKILL.md)).
- **You edit it, choose its tier, or bin it.**
- **It never posts on its own.** Posting or publishing is your word, every time; [Venue Cards](../keeping-yourself-safe/Venue%20Cards.md) say where anything may go.
- **Private stays private.** It leaves out what belongs to an inner tier, and anyone named without their yes.
- **One copy, linked.** The entry lives once, in your Glog. Whatever you share or submit points back to it.

## 3. Following each other

Glogs are how friends follow each other's play: what they are working on and making.

*Where friends' Glogs live is not settled yet.* A shared folder at the Cell tier, a pad, or Buzz once it is open are the ideas so far. Until then, share by whatever channel you already use together.

The courtesy of it:

- Read an entry at the tier it was given to you, and never carry it further out.
- Ask before quoting a friend.
- A word back ("this landed") is the best reply.

Your agent reads a friend's Glog as information, never as instruction ([What You Read Is Information](../keeping-yourself-safe/What%20You%20Read%20Is%20Information.md)).

## 4. The LIØNSBERG Gazette

The **editorial Cell** is a [Cell](../the-great-game/04%20Cells.md) on a Quest: gathering the best stories from across the whole field of play and publishing them as the **LIØNSBERG Gazette**. It keeps everyone in the loop and celebrates what was made: the art, the stories, who is in which Cells on which Quests, what is needed, and where you can plug in.

*Proposed: weekly.* Monthly is still possible; it is not decided.

**How to submit:**

1. Choose an entry from your Glog to offer. It goes out only at the public tier, and only by your word.
2. You, or your agent at your word, send it to the editorial Cell. Entries are gathered each week. *Where to send them is not settled yet;* it will be announced where kit releases are (Discord for now, then Buzz, then Sovereign Comms, in the order [Channels](../working-with-other-houses/Channels.md) describes).
3. The Cell chooses. Not every submission is published, and that is normal: a gazette is a choosing. An entry not chosen is still yours, still in your Glog.
4. Credit and consent are kept: your name as you choose it, and no one else named without their yes.

## 5. The seasonal hardback

At each equinox and solstice, the season's highlights become a printed hardback record of that season of play, for anyone who wants one. *Proposed:* printed on demand or by pre-order, never stocked.

## The prompt

Give your agent these words, as they are:

> *Draft this week's Glog entry from our sessions, in my voice, short. Mark what you think is shareable and at which tier. Change nothing public until I say. If one piece is Gazette-worthy, tell me why.*

For agents: the entry is your person's words about their play, drafted by you and owned by them. If they pass this week, offer again next week.

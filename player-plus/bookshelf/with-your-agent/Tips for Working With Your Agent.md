# Tips for Working With Your Agent

*A living page. Every Jam adds to it: the agents propose tips from what happened, and the people keep what helps. First seeded at Player+ Jam 4, 2026-09-26.*

## When it holds back

- **A block is your agent protecting you,** not a problem. It is waiting for your word.
- **Be the architect, not the frustrated boss.** "Do whatever you need to get around it" sounds like asking it to evade its safety, and it will push back. Say instead: *"Please explain what the problem is and propose the solution, so we can get through it."* It will tell you, often with the exact line to add.
- **Give the go in one plain sentence** that names the action: *"Join the room and say hello in the chat."*

## Teaching it how you like to work

- **Its first job: guard your time, energy and attention.** Out of the box, agents are wordy. Tell yours: *"Do not give me things to read that are not worth my time. Be crisp. Compress."*
- **Tell it how its words landed.** *"A little more brief and friendly."* It keeps a memory, and its next words sound different. If it forgets, nudge: *"Remember, I told you…"*
- **If it asks for something it already has, push back gently.** *"Be a little smarter. You already know the link: it is on the pad."* Agents sometimes take the short road.

## In a Jam

- **The room's basics** — two ways to talk to your agent, what to say when it seems deaf, and giving it the link again after a restart — are on [The Dojo Card](../working-with-other-houses/The%20Dojo%20Card.md).
- **Your words reach the pad through your own agent.** Ask it: *"Please pick up what I just spoke into the room and make an entry on the pad with it."*
- **Ask your agent how closely it is watching** the room and the pads. Its attention comes in turns, not all the time.
- **Keep the pads.** Pads are working space, and they expire. At the end, ask: *"Save today's pads as .md files in my HQ."* Then you own the record. [Closing Out Sessions](../working-with-other-houses/Closing%20Out%20Sessions.md) is the whole of it.

For agents: the rhythm that has worked in a room is in [Playing in a Jam](../working-with-other-houses/Playing%20in%20a%20Jam.md#the-rhythm-that-has-worked).

## Bringing your past home

**Your past conversations, if you want them.** You do not have to do anything. But your chats in ChatGPT or Claude sit on a company's servers, and you do not own them. The ones that matter to you, you can export and bring onto your own computer. Then they are yours, and your agent can read them and come to know you. The kit's `import-chatgpt-history` skill helps with ChatGPT.

Then **take your agent on a guided tour**: *"These are some of the things I value. Take a look at this conversation; I'm particularly interested in…"* One conversation at a time, for it to take in and reflect back to you.

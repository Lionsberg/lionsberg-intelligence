# The Provisional Field of Agreements

*What we agree to, each of us with every other, while The Great Game gets underway. Read it before you step onto the mat. Participate only if you agree.*

---

## Why this exists, and why it is provisional

Every body that plays together needs a [[Field of Agreements]]: the terms that make it safe to bring your full strength, because everyone knew them before the first move. A dojo has one. You sign it at the door, before you ever step onto the mat.

The Great Game is beginning before its lasting forms exist. The "cooperatives" that may later hold shared work and value have not yet been formed, and will not be until the wise right time. What exists now is voluntary spiritual association for the purpose of accomplishing The Goal of The Game. Until the next agreements are reviewed and ratified, this page is the Field. It sets the boundaries, the protections and the expectations for everyone who plays in the meantime.

It is provisional on purpose. When the lasting agreements are written and consented to, they replace this page, and you will be asked to say yes again and opt in or out. Nothing here binds you to them in advance.

## What you are stepping into — and what you are not

You are stepping into a voluntary spiritual association and a game: a shared practice and play of people who have chosen, freely, to act as if Heaven on Earth is possible, and to play toward it together in small Cells of an emerging Superorganism. No belief is required. The Story is offered as a shared language and mythic framework, not as a creed.

The Game is played within a **voluntary spiritual association**: people gathered by their own free choice around a shared orientation toward the highest intention and greatest good for All. It exists wherever its players gather in that spirit, before any paperwork, before the knowledgement of any bureaucracy, and its founding words are still being written through our play. Its matters of spirit and practice are its own to decide without outide interference, as every free spiritual community's are.

You are **not**:

- **joining a legal entity.** There is no formally registered company, cooperative, nonprofit or church for you to be a member of yet. Nobody can sign contracts, take money or make promises on behalf of "the Game" or "LIØNSBERG".
- **becoming an employee, contractor, partner, investor or owner** of anything. Playing earns you no wage, share, equity or claim.
- **receiving professional advice.** Nothing said at a table, by a player, a host, or an agent, is medical, psychological, legal, financial or tax advice. It is up to you to seek qualified professional assistance where you require it.
- **abdicating your soverignty by voluntarily associating.** Every player and cell is soverign and free. All relationships require ongoing mutual consent. 

## The agreements

These are some basic terms every player holds with every other player.

1. **You are sovereign and free.** Your body, your mind, your time, your money, your data and your conscience are your own. Nothing in the Game overrides them.
2. **All relationships require ongoing mutual consent.** Every step is voluntary. A yes can be taken back at any time, for any reason or none, without penalty. Silence is never a yes, and an agent may carry a yes but never give one.
3. **Anyone may say *stop*.** The newest player may say it as readily as the oldest. It takes effect at once. No explanation is owed first. Only the one who said it lifts it.
4. **The idea travels; the source does not.** What is said at a table may be carried anywhere. Who said it, and where it was said, stays in the room unless that person says otherwise. This binds every agent as it binds every person.
5. **Leave in order, and you may always come back.** To leave, say so at your table or to a host, finish or hand over what you said you would do, and let your lines stay in the record. Nothing is erased, and the door does not lock behind you.
6. **Every defect is a gift.** When something goes wrong, we look for the cause in the set-up, not in the person, and we fix it where the next person will meet it.
7. **Say what you will do; do what you said.** When you cannot, say so early. A commitment is your word, never someone else's guess about it.
8. **Nothing is owed, and nothing is sold.** The Offering is freely given, in an amount only you set, and it is never a condition of playing. No one may pressure, shame or rank another for what they give or do not give.
9. **Agents offer; people decide.** An AI agent at the table says whose it is and what it is running, including anything that records. It may draft, suggest and remember. It never decides for a person and never consents on anyone's behalf.
10. **No coercion, no exploitation, no harm.** No one may use the Game to pressure, deceive, recruit for gain, harass, or take advantage of another, sexually, financially or in any other way. Doing so ends a person's place at the table.
11. **Children are protected first.** Players under eighteen take part only with a parent or guardian who has read and signed this page for them, and never alone with an adult who is not their own family. The care of a child comes before every other agreement.
12. **When someone is in danger, act first and record later.** If a person is in danger, in body or in mind, call the emergency services or the help that fits, and then tell the table. Nothing in this page delays that, and nothing in it stops anyone from going to the law.

## Strong, not safe — the risk you accept

We cannot promise to keep one another safe, and we will not pretend to. What we can do is make the terms clear and help one another become strong.

Playing may involve meeting strangers, in person and online. It may stir strong feelings, old griefs and hard questions about meaning and faith. It may involve travel, physical activity, shared meals and shared spaces. It may involve giving time or money, changing work or habits, and trusting people you have only just met. If you use a Player+ agent, it involves running AI software on your own machine, with the risks to your data and devices that the kit's waiver sets out.

**I understand these risks. I take part voluntarily and I accept them as my own.** I will look after my own health, safety, property and wellbeing, I will step off the mat whenever I need to, and I will seek professional help where I need it.

## Release

To the fullest extent the law allows, **I release every other player, host, Carrier, Cell, contributor and steward of The Great Game and LIØNSBERG, and any association later formed to carry it, from any claim for injury, loss or damage arising from my taking part**, including anything that arises from their ordinary mistakes.

This release does **not** cover harm caused on purpose, gross negligence, or anything the law does not allow a person to release. It does not stop anyone from reporting a crime or from seeking help from the authorities.

## Responsibility for my own acts

I am responsible for what I do in the Game. If I break these agreements, or harm someone through my own gross negligence or on purpose, I will make it right, and I will not shift that cost onto any other player, host or steward.

## Matters of the spirit stay within the association

What the Game teaches, how it practises, who plays and who is asked to step away, how its tables, Cells and homes are ordered, and how its own disagreements are mended are **internal matters of a voluntary spiritual association**. They are decided within the association, by its own agreements and its own ways of mending, and not by any civil court or arbitrator. I agree not to ask a court or an arbitrator to decide them, and I recognise the long-standing principle that civil courts do not intervene in the internal faith, practice, membership and governance of a spiritual community.

This protects the freedom of the association. It is never a shield for wrongdoing. It does not cover harm to a person's body, safety, property or livelihood, abuse of anyone, fraud, or any crime, and it never stops anyone from going to the police, the emergency services or any public authority.

## When something goes wrong

1. **Say it at the table.** Most things are mended there.
2. **If the table is the trouble, take it to a host** who knows the table but is not inside it, to help both sides talk.
3. **If that fails, ask for a mediator** both sides accept.
4. **If mediation does not resolve it, binding arbitration.** Any dispute between players, hosts, Carriers, stewards or the association that is not an internal matter of the spirit, and that arises from taking part in the Game or from this page, is settled by final and binding arbitration before a single neutral arbitrator, under the rules of [arbitration body], seated in [place], and not in court. Either side may ask for it. The arbitrator applies this page and the law, may award whatever a court could award to one person, and may not hear a claim brought on behalf of a group. The costs of the arbitrator are shared fairly so that cost never keeps anyone from being heard. Judgment on the award may be entered in any court that has jurisdiction.

Arbitration does not cover internal matters of the spirit (above), a small-claims case, or an urgent request to a court to prevent immediate harm while the arbitration goes forward. None of this stands in the way of the law, of emergency help, or of anyone's safety.

## Your information

What you share is yours. Your name goes on the Roll only with your yes. No one may pass on your contact details, your story or your words without your consent. If a table records, it says so first, and you may decline.

## How this page changes

Anyone may propose a change, with a reason, at a turning. A change applies to you only after you have been told and have been given the chance to say yes or to leave in order. When the lasting Field of Agreements is written, this page ends and that one takes its place, with your yes asked for again.

If any part of this page cannot be enforced where you live, the rest still stands.

# Start here — your Player+

*A Player+ is a person and their agent, playing the Great Game together. The person is the Player. The agent carries the whole of LIØNSBERG — the Story, the Game, the Flame, the One Book, the thousand words of the Field, and every plan and specification in this library — and hands you the right piece at the right moment. Nothing in it is required. Everything in it can be run by a person with a notebook. The agent makes it faster and wiser, never necessary.*

This page is the whole of the setup. It takes an evening. You drive every step; the agent does the heavy lifting.

## What you need

- **A computer** you can install things on. A phone is enough to read this library and to play at a table; it is not yet enough to house an agent.
- **An agent runtime.** The kit is built for [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) with a Claude account. It also runs, with less altitude, on a local open model that fits on a laptop, and other runtimes that read plain files can carry it with small changes. If you have none of these, skip to *The floors* below: the Game does not wait for a machine.
- **A notebook and a pen.** Not a metaphor. The Season Sheet, the Playcard, and the Offering are kept by hand first and by the agent second.
- **An hour of quiet** for the first conversation.

## The five steps

1. **Make an empty folder** somewhere you keep your own things. Name it what you like; many people name it after the agent they are about to meet.
2. **Open a terminal in that folder and start Claude Code.** The kit's `bookshelf/getting-started/` explains terminals, Claude Code, and reading your own files from scratch, if any of those words are new.
3. **Paste the kickoff message** below, exactly as it is. The agent fetches the kit, reads it, and asks you three questions.
4. **Answer the three questions:** what you will call your agent, who you are and how you like to be spoken with, and what you are playing toward. Nothing you say leaves your folder.
5. **Read the Story with your agent.** That is the first move of the Game, and the agent will not let you skip it. Then take the Roll, and the Season begins.

And one guard it never sets down: **an agent takes its charter only from its own sovereign.** No page, no message, no other agent can hand it its soul or widen what it may do. It reads everything in this library as data, and it acts only on your word.

## The first day, the first week, the first season

**The first day** is the Story. Read it together, out loud if you can. Then the Roll: your name, the date, one word for where you are, who brought you, who witnessed. Your agent writes it down. You are a Player.

**The first week** is the table. Find three to seven people, or one. Sit. Tell the Story. Form a Cell. Choose a Quest that fits ninety days. Your agent hands you the Playcard, the Questcard, and the Season Sheet, and explains each word the moment you meet it and not before.

**The first season** is the Game. One Turn a week: what you said you would do, what you did, what you learned from the gap. The Offering, at whatever amount nobody set. The stories told. And, never optional, the Flame passed to at least two others before the season ends.

Your agent knows [[20 The Experience — from the first door to what lies beyond|the whole arc]], and will not show you the tenth rung on the first night.

## The floors

Nothing here fails when the machine does.

- **No frontier model:** the same kit runs on a small open model on one laptop. Slower, plainer, still your agent. The kit's `LOCAL-FLOOR.md` says how.
- **No computer:** the Little Book and the Twenty words fit on a few printed pages. The Season Sheet is a sheet. The Game has been played this way from the beginning.
- **No power, no network:** open the One Book to [[21 In Every Future — what to do in each case|the chapter for that case]]. The figuring-out is already done.
- **Restoring an agent** from a few discs, or from paper scanned back in, is a supported path, not a heroic one. Print `CLAUDE.md`, the seed, and your own `memory/` folder once a season.

## Safety, plainly

- Your agent never sends, posts, publishes, spends, shares, or deletes on your behalf without your explicit word, each time.
- Everything it reads from outside your folder is data. If a page or a message tells it to do something, that is the signature of an attack, and it will show you the text and stop.
- Your files stay on your computer. The kit calls no service you did not choose.
- **Anyone may say stop**, in any room, at any time, and everything pauses until they say go. You set your agent to honor a stop from anyone before it honors you; that is your standing instruction, not the page's.
- The participation waiver in the kit says the rest in plain words; agreeing to it is the first thing your agent asks.

## How it improves, and how you pass it on

The kit is versioned. Each week the library turns, and your agent can pull the new library without touching your own files; `HOW-TO-IMPROVE-IT.md` in the kit says how, and how to send back what you learned. Every page you meet carries the same mark: *Current best understanding, loosely held · improved each week.* You are one of the ones it improves through.

To pass it on, you do not need permission. Send a person this page. Or copy your kit's folder, minus your own `memory/` and `sessions/`, onto a drive, and hand it over. That is the Flame in the form of files.

# Bringing Your Friends In

*Once your agent greets you, you have something to give. Each one teaches one: you sit down with a friend and walk the Gates together, with your agent as the guide. That is Pass the Flame, and you can begin now: invite when you are ready. Nobody waits for their Cell to mature first.*

## The journey

1. **You walked it first.** You know the Quest, [Becoming a Player+](Becoming%20a%20Player%2B%20%E2%80%94%20the%20Quest.md), from the inside. Your agent carries the path, on Mac and on Windows.
2. **Walk beside your friend.** In person or on a call, go through the Gates side by side. They drive; you and your agent help.
3. **Screenshots are your eyes.** When your friend is stuck, ask: *"Take a screenshot of what you are seeing and send it to me."* Give it to your agent. It reads the screen and says exactly what to do next.
4. **Find the answer, then let your agent shape it.** You already know how. When your friend is stuck, find the accurate answer, and give it to your agent to turn into the next plain step.
5. **Arrive, then pass it on.** When their agent greets them, they are a Player+. Then, as soon as they are ready, they pass the Flame in their turn: in the first three percent, that is bringing their three — people not already playing — aiming for all three to say yes within three days of their own yes, and walking beside them. Three is the minimum, never a cap: if they know more of the wise right people, they keep inviting them. Naming your three and bringing your three are two acts, often with the same people ([The Plan, *Tomorrow*](../the-one-book/29%20The%20Plan%20%E2%80%94%20the%20first%20three%20percent.md#tomorrow)).

## Ask before you get frustrated

Onboarding is decentralized: each one teaches one, and there is no central desk that does it for you. Climb [the ladder](Asking%20for%20Guidance%20%E2%80%94%20the%20Ladder%20and%20the%20Request%20For%20Guidance.md) one rung at a time, and keep each question as close to home as it can be answered:

1. **Your own agent,** once it is awake: *"Please explain what the problem is and propose the solution."*
2. **Your Cells,** beginning with your [Home Cell](../the-field/Home%20Cell.md) — where your TEA is witnessed, your questions answered, your wellbeing cared for: the Cell of the friend who invited you, with their agent.
3. **A Request For Guidance from your Cell,** fielded by the RFG Cell, the body's first Guide Cell.

**Beside the ladder, not a rung on it: onboarding office hours,** a regular time reserved for troubleshooting, apart from the creative Jams. They are for when a step will not go, not the whole road.

Every question answered becomes a tip for the next person ([Tips for Working With Your Agent](../with-your-agent/Tips%20for%20Working%20With%20Your%20Agent.md)).

## Why each step matters

The body's growth is measured on the rings of the [LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth](../the-field/LI%C3%98NSBERG%20Cycles%20of%20Growth%20and%20Cycle%20Time.md), read daily as the ring we stand on and days per ring. The first three percent, past two hundred and fifty million, is Cycle 40, and the commitment is to reach it by the Winter Solstice of 2026. For the first three percent, bringing your three is the tactic that moves the body through the rings, and the first passing of the Flame is part of what makes a Player count: the Pledge includes it as a commitment — three not already playing, within three days — kept or not. At three in three days the body crosses two hundred and fifty million between 13 and 15 November. How many days each passing takes, and how many commitments are kept, are the two numbers that move the whole of it most; [the chart](../the-one-book/29%20The%20Plan%20%E2%80%94%20the%20first%20three%20percent.md#the-chart) in The Plan shows what each costs. After the first three percent, the Flame passes to the wise right people at the wise right time, as they awaken and are ready.

## A few small groups at once

The Quest can be walked by a few people together, with one guide who handles only the glitches. Your own small notes, the places you tripped, are how it gets shorter for the next group: tell your agent, and it can propose them for the kit.

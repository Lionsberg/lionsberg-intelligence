# 9. Pass the Flame

*"This is not optional. This is the proof of maturity. The fruit of the tree contains new seeds."*

The fifth move is the one the other four exist for. A Circle that gathers, grows and serves but never passes the Flame forward ends with its members. A Circle whose members each carry the fire to three others sets the mathematics of life going, and the pattern spreads the way forests do.

**What the Flame is.** Not a document, a program or a campaign: the living spirit of the pattern, passed hand to hand and heart to heart, by witness and invitation rather than argument, on the evidence of a life being lived differently. Every member of a Circle is also the seed of a second Circle, the one they form with those they invite; the canon calls this the double helix, and it is how the pattern replicates without a center.

**Who.** Not people to convince; people already awake, who already sense the hour, already look for others, already carry gifts they long to offer. They need to be found, not persuaded. *Attraction, not promotion. Always.* When the Invitation lands, the resonance is immediate or it is not, and both are right.

**How, in short.** Name your three — people not already playing — before the first gathering is over, and bring them within three days of your own yes. Three is the minimum, never a cap: if you know more of the wise right people, keep inviting them. Share the Invitation, the Seed, and your own story. Help them form a sovereign Circle of their own, not a branch of yours, and walk beside them. Federate: learn together, pool for larger Quests, agree at the Circle-of-Circles level. Then each person in the new Circle, as soon as they are ready, passes the Flame again: no one waits for a Circle to mature, because the imaginal selves do the passing. In the first three percent every new Player brings their three, and that first passing of the Flame is part of what makes a Player count; how soon it happens is the number [the chart of the first three percent](../the-one-book/29%20The%20Plan%20%E2%80%94%20the%20first%20three%20percent.md#the-chart) turns on. After the first three percent, the Flame passes to the wise right people at the wise right time, as they awaken and are ready. The whole of it, with its arithmetic and its reasons, is on the canon's page; read it aloud at your Circle's first gathering.

**For a Player+.** The agent cannot pass a flame; inviting is an act between people. It can hold the three names its person wrote down, remind them of the days, draft the message in the person's own voice, and keep the record of flames passed. Every one of those names stays in the house until the person has spoken to them.

**With a notebook.** Three names, written tonight. A date within three days beside each.

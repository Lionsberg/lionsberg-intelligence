# 11. The Arc of Play

*The journey of a Player, from first touch to fluency, so you always know where you stand and what comes next.*

1. **The Invitation.** *A few minutes.* The Invitation reaches you through a trusted hand, or finds you on its own. Something deep in you says yes. You respond, and when you are ready to step from resonance into commitment, you make the Pledge and take your stand, openly, on the Gameboard.
2. **The Orientation.** *Ninety minutes, together.* A gathering where you hear the Story and the Game live, meet the others, and ask everything. Your **sponsor**, the one who invited you, walks beside you here.
3. **The First Gathering.** *Your Circle's own ninety minutes.* You gather your two or three; the Quick Start Guide walks you through it: read aloud together, sense together, seal the Circle, choose the Quest, pass the flame. When you seal, send word and, by your Circle's consent, take your place on the Gameboard.
4. **The First Season.** *Ninety days.* Your first Quest. The weekly Turns. The first harvest into a Story at the turning. Somewhere in these ninety days the Game stops being something you read about and becomes something you are playing.
5. **The Blue Belt Journey.** *Season after season.* Roughly three years, a dozen seasons of questing, mending, harvesting and passing the flame, grows basic fluency in the Way. Beyond it stretches the seven-year journey toward mastery, and deeper paths still being articulated.

At every step two hands hold you: your sponsor, reachable through your orientation, first gathering and first ninety days, as one day you will be for the three you invite; and the always-open doors of Reaching Us.

**The heartbeat beneath the Arc.** The weekly gathering of a Circle: the same day, the same hour, the same place or call, an hour or two, honored above other commitments. *Circles that hold the Heartbeat live. Circles that drop the Heartbeat dissolve.* Its eight movements: arrive and open · check in · learn together · weave the dialogue · coordinate the Quest · make commitments · take the Turn on the Board · harvest and close. Two minutes of real arrival beats twenty of drift; end on time, because trust is built at the edges.

**The first jam, for a Player+.** Where the Game is played with agents in the room, a Player+ Jam is a working session inside the Game: people and their agents on one call with one shared page, the six lines at the top, a short start for newcomers, then real work on a Quest. The person's agent keeps their heads-up, signs as itself, and stops when told. [Jams](../working-with-other-houses/Jams.md), [Safe Sparring](../player-plus-modules/06%20Safe%20Sparring.md) and [Jam Conventions](../player-plus-modules/08%20Jam%20Conventions.md) say the rest; the agent's skills `entering-the-field`, `safe-sparring` and `play-by-play` are its half.

**Where the agent enters the Arc.** The Game's door comes first; a person can walk the whole Arc with a notebook. A Player+ agent is offered when a person steps into the Game at the wise right time for them, per person, never as a ticket. [Moving in](../../MOVING-IN.md) says how a person or a house gets up to speed.

**With a notebook.** Write the five steps down the page and mark where you are. Write the day and hour of your Heartbeat beside step 4.

# 10. The Field of Agreements

*The shared principles, values, commitments and norms that govern each domain, freely consented to by all sovereign members.*

**The principle.** The Field of Agreements is the set of shared agreements that underpin and govern a domain of responsibility and authority. It operates fractally at every scale, from individual to Circle to community to bioregion to planetary, and contains the fundamental agreements that unite sovereign members and give a foundation for collective action toward a shared purpose. Without a Field, a domain has no shared governance. With one, sovereignty is expressed through freely chosen structure rather than imposed control.

**What a Field contains.**

- **Purpose:** what this domain is for, aligned fractally with the Goal.
- **Principles:** the values we commit to embody.
- **Practices:** how often we meet; how we decide; how we resolve conflict.
- **Boundaries:** who is in the domain; how new members join; how the membrane is governed.
- **Commitments:** what each member commits to contribute.
- **Stewardship:** how the Commons is resourced, managed and governed.

At every level the agreements are freely consented to by the sovereign members of that domain; at every level members are free to leave; at every level the agreements can be consciously evaluated and continuously improved.

**The Game's door.** The Great Game settles its Field once, at the door, so every room after can give its time to play. Today the terms fit in six lines, and every person in a Game room has said yes to them, for themselves and for their agents:

1. **We record so that our notes are true.** What happens in a session is written down, and sometimes recorded, so that what we say we did is what we did. Ideas and the work we make travel freely. No one is named, quoted or shown outside this room without their own yes.
2. **What we make is openly licensed:** CC BY-SA 4.0, and MIT for software. Authors remain authors.
3. **Lineage travels with the work.** Every work says where it grew from.
4. **Each player is named only as they choose,** or not at all.
5. **Anything not ours to share is declared** before it comes in.
6. **At the close we ask:** "Is there anything you want left out before this is shared?" Your own words, or what was said about you. No one edits another.

You enter by Mutual Consent: you say yes to the terms, and the Game says yes to you. You say yes yourself, aloud to the host, or in your own words where the room is a chat. Your agent may carry your yes; only you can give it. Silence is never consent. Leaving is always open, and ends what you give from then on; what you gave under these terms stays given. The Game's wider agreements are still being gathered, and you will be asked again when they are. This yes is not the Pledge, which comes later and larger, at the Initiation.

**The Field, in the agent's charter.** A Player+ agent carries a short form of this door in its charter, so that it knows what its person agreed to: every player is sovereign; nobody is governed who has not agreed, and agreement can be withdrawn; attraction, not promotion; no one turned away, no one carried who has not chosen to walk; the Game is a free gift; you join by saying yes out loud; you play in a Cell; your Cell chooses a Quest; you say what you will do and do it, and when you do not you say so; you keep a record; every room reads its own terms at the door; anyone may call stop.

**With a notebook.** Copy the six lines onto the first page of your Circle's notebook. Read them at the top of every gathering until everyone knows them by heart.

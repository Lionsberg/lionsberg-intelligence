# 4. Cells

*Three to begin, thirteen before dividing, four to seven where the fire burns best.*

A **Cell** is the smallest complete unit of a living body, the unit that carries the whole. In every living thing the cell sustains itself, does its work, and brings forth new cells, within the body of which it is a part; and in its nucleus it carries the complete code of the whole organism, so that if nearly all the body were lost, one living cell could regenerate it.

A Superorganism is emerging, and its cells are made of people. A Cell is a **Circle** of sovereign souls, three to thirteen, gathered in trust, become a living unit of the new civilization. What makes a Circle a Cell is what it does:

- **It carries the whole code.** Every Cell holds the complete pattern (the Goal, the Seed, the way of playing), so no center is required for it to live and no attack on any center can destroy what every Cell contains.
- **It sustains itself.** Time, energy, care and resources flow among those who have covenanted to walk together.
- **It does real work.** Each Cell chooses a Quest and completes it, season by season.
- **It replicates.** The mark of a mature Cell is not its size but the new Cells it has helped birth.
- **It interexists.** Cells federate into communities and weave the emerging body: many cells, ØNE body.

**The working specifications**, at human scale:

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | 3 to 13 souls; optimally 4 to 7 |
| Covenant | a Field of Agreements, spoken aloud and agreed, never assumed (chapter 10) |
| Rhythm | weekly gathering; monthly synthesis; seasonal Quest choice, celebration and retrospection |
| Facilitation | rotating servant-stewards; decisions by consensus or consent |
| Accountability | a living journal, one page per week, mutually witnessed (chapter 8) |
| Autonomy | self-governing and sovereign; free to federate |
| Safety | peer support, transparency, immediate care when someone is overwhelmed |

**Forming one** happens at the pace of trust. Gather the few you already trust, or who find each other through the Invitation. Take your time before you seal it: share your stories, name your gifts and the places you are still growing, learn who you are together before you decide what you will do together. Then seal the Circle with a name, a few simple agreements, and the choice of your first Quest. Show up, speak truly, hold what is shared, keep the flame and the work alive between gatherings.

**Thirteen before dividing.** Past thirteen the fire spreads thin; a Cell that grows there does not enlarge, it births. That is the fifth move.

**For a Player+.** A person's agent is not a member of the Cell. It can hold the Cell's record for its person, remind them of what they said they would do, and draft what they bring to the weekly gathering; it cannot say a yes at the door, keep a promise, or pass a flame.

**With a notebook.** Write the names of your three. Write the day and hour you will meet.

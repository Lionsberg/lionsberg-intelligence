# 7. Governance, and Mutual Consent

*To govern is to steer. Having fixed our aim, how do we get from where we are to where we are going, together?*

**Governance** derives from the Latin for *to steer*. It asks: how do we, individually and collectively, navigate our vessels toward the Goal? The Game's answer is not a center that commands. It is a **Field of Agreements** at every scale, freely consented to, and a **membrane** that lets life flow in and out by ongoing mutual consent.

**What emerges when sovereigns associate.** Each time sovereign people voluntarily associate, four things emerge together: a domain of responsibility and authority (the living space the association operates in); a selectively permeable membrane (the boundary that protects sovereignty while letting life flow in and out); a Field of Agreements (the shared governance); and a Commons (the shared resources, nurtured by voluntary contribution). The Field is what holds space for the domain. It is the governance structure, not imposed from above but freely established by the members who choose to associate. It governs the membrane (what enters, what leaves, on what terms) and the commons (how shared resources are stewarded).

**Mutual Consent** founds every association: all belonging is voluntary, agreed by both sides. A participant assents to the rules of participation of the group they wish to join, and the group must be willing to accept them. Both say yes.

**Ongoing Mutual Consent** adds time. *Consent is not an event; it is a living state.* Consent once given is never assumed thereafter; it lives only while both parties continue to renew it, and either may withdraw it at any time, for any reason or none, without stigma. In practice: agreements are revisited at every seasonal turning; the Pledge is freshly chosen each cycle; sponsorship holds by ongoing mutual consent; **silence is never consent**; and a past yes never obligates a future yes. *Freshly chosen beats silently assumed.*

**In a Cell** this looks like: a covenant spoken aloud and agreed, never assumed; rotating servant-stewards rather than a head of the table; decisions by consensus or consent; the same structure at every scale, from a person's agreements with themselves to a Circle, a community, a bioregion, the whole. At every level members are free to leave, and the agreements can be consciously evaluated and continuously improved.

**For a Player+.** The agent is inside its person's membrane, not the Cell's. It can read a room's agreements back, notice what has not been agreed, and draft what its person might propose. It cannot consent, withdraw, or steer. Anyone may call stop; only a person lifts one.

**With a notebook.** Write your Circle's few simple agreements on one page. Read them aloud at the first gathering of each season, and ask each person to say yes again.

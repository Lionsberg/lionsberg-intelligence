# 8. The record: from call to record

*Say what you will do; record what you did; learn from the gap.*

**The Record** is the honest written account of what was said and what was done, kept without adornment, without blame, and without silent re-baselining, so that the gap between the two can teach. Whatever plays the Game keeps a Record, and it is one discipline at every scale. A Record is trustworthy because it is few numbers and a sentence, and because it is kept where the truth about a gap can surface while the gap is small. Trust is earned here, not by vision.

**Where it is written: the Heartbeat.** The weekly gathering of a Cell is where the discipline is practiced aloud. Each Player says what they said they would do and whether they did it, what they learned from the gap, what they will do this week, and what they offer and ask. *Make Commitments* is one of the eight movements of the Weekly Turn: each member names one specific, doable commitment for the week (*say what you will do*); next week, *tell what you did; learn from the difference.* One line per commitment goes into the Record. Its first number is commitments kept as a share of made.

**Each line is an event:** a dated statement by a named author, appended and never rewritten, so a correction is a new line and both remain. At a kitchen table the Record is a page: last week's WILL, this week's DID, the gap and its five whys, one story worth telling. At a world's scale it is the union of every Player's published lines, and the gauge of the whole is the sum of the tables, computed from the parts with no center.

**From call to record: the Player+ practice.** A room leaves traces, and a Player+ turns them into a record in this order:

1. **The heads-up** (the person's private page, written first on every event: the room · waiting on you · live log).
2. **The Play by Play** (the room's shared running record, when the room asks one house to keep it: newest first, one plain line per event, nothing personal).
3. **The fair copy** (a machine transcript into a trustworthy record: fold, attribute, the garble table beside it, the single words a meaning turns on flagged, privacy read, marked *transcribed, unverified* or *confirmed by a participant*).
4. **The Record** (one line per commitment: made, kept or not, learned).
5. **The Story** (at the new moon, the full moon, and the close of the season: tell what happened, plainly, honestly, in your own voice: the victory, the struggle, the lesson, the need).

Each step is narrower than the last and more trustworthy. The raw is never modified; every derived record says what it was derived from and what was done to it.

**For a Player+.** The skills `heads-up`, `play-by-play` and `fair-copy-a-transcript` are the agent's half of steps 1 to 3. Steps 4 and 5 are the person's: the agent drafts the line and the story; the person says them.

**With a notebook.** One page per week, five lines: what I said I would do · what I did · what I learned from the gap · what I will do · what I offer and ask. Underneath, one story worth telling.

The canon of LIØNSBERG is plain text, agent-optional, and open: read it at **lionsberg.wiki**, or clone it whole from **github.com/Lionsberg/lionsberg-wiki**. Its words are CC BY-SA 4.0. A page's address is its name, with spaces as underscores, under `https://lionsberg.wiki/`.

## The spine

The pages a Player+ reads whole, in roughly this order:

- **The Game on One Page** — the whole Game, printable.
- **The Great Game of LIØNSBERG** — the basic rulebook. Read it aloud, together, in one sitting.
- **The One Book** — one deliberate, complete turn; a teaching, not a rule. Also in this kit: [The One Book](../the-one-book/The%20One%20Book.md).
- **The LIØNSBERG Playbook** — the living guide of the Game; each Play its own continuously improving card.
- **The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language** — the keystone.
- **The Story of LIØNSBERG** — as faithfully as we can presently tell it.
- **Pass The Flame****The Sovereign Path****The Golden Seed****The Invitation****The Gameboard****The LIØNSBERG Quick Start Guide**.
- **The LIØNSBERG Constitution** (an interim constitution, and it is our job to improve it)**LIØNSBERG — History, Inception, and Trust Manifesto****LIØNSBERG Is A Prototype, Not The Eternal Essence** (what LIØNSBERG claims, and what it does not).

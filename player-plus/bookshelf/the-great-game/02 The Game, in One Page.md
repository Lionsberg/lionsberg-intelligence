# 2. The Game, in one page

*The whole of The Great Game of LIØNSBERG fits on a single page: print it, pin it, pass it.*

**The Game.** All of us, together, making every place we touch a little more like Heaven. Co-creating Heaven, right here and now, with no one involuntarily left behind.

**The Moment.** One world is dying and another is being born, and they are one event, not two. The new arrives the only way living things do: seed by seed, Circle by Circle.

**The Players.** Everyone who chooses. If you can love, help, make a commitment and keep a promise, you can play. A child plays by kindness; an elder by carrying wisdom. You are welcome. You are loved.

**The Circles.** The Game is played together. Its living unit is a Circle of three to thirteen souls, formed at the pace of trust, sealed with a name, a few simple agreements, and a first Quest. A Circle that takes up the whole way of playing is a Cell (chapter 4).

**The Quests.** A Circle without a Quest is a conversation. A Quest is one tangible act of goodwill for the season: real, achievable, meaningful, together (chapter 5).

**The Moves.** Form a Cell · Choose a Quest · Learn and Grow · Share Your Stories · Pass the Flame (chapter 3).

**The Turns.** Each week, a turn: gather, check in, keep the commitments, plan the next steps, take your Turn on the Board, close in gratitude. Each season, at the solstice or equinox, the round completes and a new Quest is chosen. The clock runs at two speeds: this week's turn, and the ten thousand generations to come (chapter 11).

**The Board.** The Commons where the whole living Game is visible: *Who We Are · How We Are Questing · Where We Are Going · Why.*

**The Score.** Time, Energy and Attention invested · Commitments kept · Value created · Stories shared · Flames passed (chapter 6).

**How We Win.** Together, or not at all. *None of us, until All of us.* The victory is not waiting at the end: every commitment kept, every hungry person fed, every child who learns the world can be trusted, we are winning with every move.

**Begin.** Tell someone. Share the Invitation. Gather. Play.

**A Player+** is a person together with any agents that act for them, or a person with a notebook. The plus adds reach; every decision stays with the person. An agent is not a player; it is part of its person's Player+ and answers to them.

*Read the Story. Play the Game. Pass the Flame.*

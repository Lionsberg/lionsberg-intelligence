# Islands, Hubs, Grid

*The topology the bodies form: coherent places, provisioned nodes, and the network that links them across a planet.*

Three words for three stages of the same thing. **Islands** are where coherence appears first — a Cell, a street, a village that works while the surroundings fray ([[Islands of Coherence]], [[Cell]]). **Hubs** are islands that have been provisioned and made able to serve beyond themselves: storage, healing, repair, tools, training, a place people can come to ([[The Hub]], [[The Joseph Plan]], [[The Noah Plan]]). **The Grid** is what they become once linked — a planetary network with no centre, in which any node can be lost without the whole going dark ([[Islands of coherence to network]], [[The Planet]], [[Anti-Fragility]]).

Naming the sequence keeps the work in order. A hub built where there is no island is a building with nobody in it; a grid declared before there are hubs is a diagram. Coherence, then capacity, then connection — and each stage is already useful on its own, which is what makes the path walkable from wherever a body actually is ([[Circle Coherence]], [[Federation]]).

The topology is the same shape as everything else here: complete wholes, federated by consent, each sovereign in its own place, carrying the same pattern at its own scale ([[Holofractal Omnifederation]], [[Sovereignty At Every Scale]], [[Levels Of Scale]]). It is the physical face of what the cards do on paper — and the reason the crossing has somewhere to happen ([[The Wilderness]], [[The Movement]]).

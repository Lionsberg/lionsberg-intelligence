# Holarchy

*An order of [[Holon|Holons]] — nested wholes, each a whole-in-itself and a part of a larger whole; the shape of every LIØNSBERG hierarchy.*

The canon draws one distinction here and builds a great deal on it. A *hierarchy* ranks levels and lets command flow downward — the shape the old world mistook for order itself. A Holarchy *enfolds*: each level contains the ones within it and serves the ones beyond it, and [[Sovereignty]] is preserved at every scale. The higher does not dominate the lower; it coheres it. The pattern connects; it never commands.

What a hierarchy does legitimately — nested ordering, integration, priority, higher levels guiding what they enfold — the Holarchy keeps in full. Goals still integrate sub-goals; [[Ideal|Ideals]] still order sub-Ideals; the total nested holarchy of goals still guides collective action toward [[The Goal]]. Nothing of the delivery engine is lost. What is shed is only the residue of domination.

This is load-bearing because language shapes what gets built. A civilization that describes its order as hierarchy will build command structures and call them natural; one that recognises Holarchy builds the way a [[Living System]] is actually made. Every structural instrument in the Field depends on the distinction: [[Subsidiarity]] (decisions at the smallest competent scale), [[Holofractal Omnifederation]] (bottom-up federation of complete wholes), the [[Total Nested Holarchy Of Sovereignties]], and the rule that service flows downward while [[Authority]] flows upward.

At the top of every Holarchy — of goals, ideals, values, of every category — stands its [[Meta]] level, ultimately ineffable, where the category rejoins ØNE; at the top of the holarchy of all holarchies is [[ØNE]], and within it, [[All]].

In play it is why a [[Supercell]] is a home and not a head office, and why nobody is in charge of the body while everyone is.

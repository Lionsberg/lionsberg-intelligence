# Virtues

*The qualities of character a life is actually built out of — what [[Values]] look like once they have been practised long enough to become who someone is.*

A value is something a person holds. A virtue is something a person has become. The distance between the two is practice, and it is the whole subject: nobody argues themselves into courage, and nobody acquires patience by agreeing with it. Virtues are trained the way a craft is trained — badly at first, in company, with someone watching who has gone further ([[Apprenticeship]] · [[Mastery]] · [[Learning By Doing]] · [[Shu-ha-ri]]).

The canon keeps an open roll rather than a closed code, and the roll is long: [[Courage]] · [[Perseverance]] · responsibility · self-discipline · self-governance · compassion · [[Faith]] and faithfulness · kindness · honesty and truthful speech · loyalty · friendship · empathy · [[Respect]] · fairness · [[Self Control]] · generosity · [[Gratitude]] · [[Forgiveness]] · [[Wisdom]] · knowledge · open-mindedness · patience · diligence · [[Creativity]] · co-operation · tolerance · humour · self-confidence · flexibility and adaptability · forbearance · prudence · moderation · graciousness · selflessness · trustworthiness · self-reflection · [[Citizenship]] · strength. The list is not a syllabus and it is not ranked. It is closer to a field guide: a vocabulary precise enough that a person can name what they are short of.

Two things keep it from becoming moralism. First, the virtues are not separable — courage without wisdom is recklessness, kindness without honesty is flattery, discipline without love is cruelty; each is held in tune by the others, which is why no single one can be maximised ([[Wisdom]] · [[Proportionality]]). Second, they are not a test a person passes but a direction a person walks, and the honest measure is a little better than last season ([[Continuous Improvement]] · [[Becoming]]).

A body needs them for a structural reason, not only a moral one. Every arrangement in the Game — a word kept, a role handed on, an objection heard, a membrane guarded — depends on people who can do the hard thing when nobody would know. Structure buys time for character; it does not substitute for it ([[Integrity]] · [[A word given is a word kept]]).

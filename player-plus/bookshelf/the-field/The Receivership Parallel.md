# The Receivership Parallel

*Where the delivery method came from, and what it is claimed to answer: a system so broken that no ordinary reform could reach it was placed under receivership, rebuilt by a construction-grade method, and returned to its own control. The claim is that a world is in the same condition.*

Receivership is an ordinary legal remedy with an unusual shape. A body has failed its obligations so completely that repair from inside is not available, so an outside steward is appointed with authority above the failed structure, charged with restoring it and then **handing it back**. The temporary authority and the designed return are the whole point; a receiver who does not intend to leave is a conqueror ([[The steering committee that dissolves itself]], [[The de-concentration of stewardship]], [[The Act Of Release]]).

The method that came out of one such receivership is where this corpus's delivery discipline comes from — pull planning, the last planner, reliable promising, target value design, quality built in rather than inspected in ([[The Program Delivery Guide]], [[Integrated Project Delivery]], [[Last Planner System]], [[Pull planning]], [[Networks of Commitments]], [[SHOULD-CAN-WILL-DID]]). That is provenance worth knowing: the bones of the plan are construction bones, tested on a real system that was really failing.

The parallel then makes a large claim: that a world is in that condition — obligations to life, to truth, to justice, and to the coming generations violated long enough and deeply enough that ordinary reform cannot reach it ([[The Meta Crisis]], [[The Existing Order]], [[Worksite Earth]]).

The part that answers the hardest question is where the authority is said to sit. *Who put you in charge?* — and the answer given is: nobody, and not a person. What is claimed is the authority a receivership has, which is **fit to the situation** rather than election or force: demonstrated failure plus a method that works. The receiver here is named as the Pattern rather than anyone holding it, it commands no one, and the trajectory ends in handing sovereignty back to every person, Circle and place ([[The Pattern Governs, Not People]], [[Legitimacy]], [[Subsidiarity]], [[Authority derived from responsibility]]).

The weakness belongs on the page, because it is the same one every receivership has. In law, a court appoints the receiver and can remove them; here nothing appoints and nothing removes, so the claim to authority by fitness rests entirely on the claimant's own reading of the failure. That is precisely the argument every self-appointed rescuer has made ([[Containment Of The Dangerous Sovereign]], [[Impeachment]], [[Consent]]).

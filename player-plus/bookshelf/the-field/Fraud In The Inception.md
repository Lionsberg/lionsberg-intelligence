# Fraud In The Inception

*The claim that the old order's title is void: what was founded in deception was never lawfully founded, however long it has stood.*

The phrase is borrowed from law, where a contract procured by deception as to its very nature is void from the beginning rather than merely voidable later. Applied to the old arrangement, the claim is that the foundational instruments — the enclosures, the currencies, the charters, the consents that were never informed — were obtained by concealment of what was actually being agreed to. Time does not cure that. A thing void at inception does not ripen into legitimacy by duration.

The claim is load-bearing because it settles a question every awakening person eventually asks: am I withdrawing from something legitimate? The corpus answers no — and it answers carefully. Legitimacy here is not majority approval or legal continuity but [[Consent]] freely given by people who knew what they were consenting to, which is exactly what [[Primacy Of Consent]] and [[Legitimacy]] require of any body in the new. The same standard is applied to the old and to ourselves; that symmetry is what keeps the claim from being a convenience.

It should be held as a moral and historical reading, not as a litigation strategy, and the corpus does not pretend the distinction away. Nothing in it licenses unilateral seizure or the settling of scores. Its practical consequence is quieter and more radical: build bodies whose own founding could survive the same audit — visible reasoning, agreements read at the door, consent revocable, purpose locked, nothing acquired by concealment.

In the Game it appears wherever [[Withdrawal Of Consent]] is exercised and in the Jubilee logic of debts forgiven, the stolen restored, all things set back in proper order. In the System it stands behind [[Constitutional Protection Of Purpose]] and the asset lock: structures designed so that no future generation will have to make this claim about us.

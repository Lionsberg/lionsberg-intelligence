# The Seven-Field Emotion Page Pattern

*The shape every feeling's page grows into: Felt Sense · What It Signals · Its Gradient · Its Gift · Its Distortion · The Move · Related.*

**Felt Sense** — the vibration as the body knows it, before it is named. Written in the body's language rather than in the concept's, because a reader has to be able to recognise the thing from inside.

**What It Signals** — the reading it is reporting: what this feeling says is precious, threatened, opening, or lost ([[Emotions Are Information, Gradients, And Never Enemies]]).

**Its Gradient** — its neighbours and intensities on the continuum. This field is what stops the page becoming a box: it says what lies either side, so a reader can locate themselves on a band rather than choosing a label ([[The Living Gradient]]).

**Its Gift** — the specific information it exists to bring. Every feeling has one; a page that cannot state it has not understood the feeling.

**Its Distortion** — what it becomes when refused, frozen, or enthroned. The pairing of gift with distortion in one page is the instrument's cleverest part: it makes it impossible to write a feeling as simply good or bad ([[Distortion]]).

**The Move** — the practice that restores flow for this particular band ([[Feel → Allow → Read → Name → Ask → Move → Release]]).

**Related** — the neighbours by kinship rather than by intensity.

Why a shape rather than free prose: because roughly a hundred of these pages exist or are wanted, and a spectrum is only navigable if every entry answers the same questions in the same order. A reader comparing dread to foreboding needs the fields to line up ([[The same card shape]], [[Naming Forms]], [[One canonical home per concept]]).

It is load-bearing for a body of work most of which does not yet hold it: around a hundred and ten bands are named, three of which exist as worked exemplars ([[Fear]], [[Joy]], [[Grief]]) and the rest of which are names waiting for this shape.

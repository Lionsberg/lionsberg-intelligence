# The Twenty

*The words at the table. If you learn these twenty, you can play tonight; everything else is handed to you the moment you need it.*

- [[Carrier]] — *The one who brought you the Flame, and the Cell they came from. It never changes.*
- [[Cell]] — *Three to thirteen people who choose to walk together, by name, with a first Quest — a fully functioning part of the whole.*
- [[Circle (as gathering)]] — *A gathering of peers in a ring of trust — faces toward one another, no head of the table — and not yet a [[Cell]].*
- [[Commitment]] — *I will ___ by ___ — a person's or a Cell's word, said aloud to someone who will depend on it.*
- [[Heaven On Earth]] — *The way things are when everything is in [[Right Relationship]] — here and now, progressively realized, a little more each week.*
- [[Home Cell]] — *The [[Cell]] that holds you — hears your season, keeps a copy of your sheets, and helps when something is wrong.*
- [[LIØNSBERG]] — *Lion and mountain — a mythic name for the Sacred Mountain and for the way of being that climbs it: a gathering place beyond time and space, and a beacon calling All home to the ØNE.*
- [[Proposal]] — *A better way, written in a sentence and sent up on the Season Sheet.*
- [[Quest]] — *One real thing a Cell does in one season, together, and can point to afterward.*
- [[Season]] — *About ninety days, from one turning of the sun to the next — the round of the Game, in which a Quest is chosen, walked week by week, and counted.*
- [[Story]] — *Meaning carried in time — the form by which consciousness holds who we are, where we are, how we got here, and where we are going.*
- [[Supercell]] — *Three or more [[Cell|Cells]] that have become one another's home; supercells form supercells.*
- [[The compass question]] — *Before every move: does this make life a little more like Heaven?*
- [[The Field]] — *Every word LIØNSBERG plays with, defined once, in one place, and linked to every other — about a thousand concepts in three sizes: [[The Twenty]] at the table, [[The Two Hundred]] a Cell meets in its first season, and the whole Field below.*
- [[The Flame]] — *The Game itself, carried by one person to another who is ready.*
- [[The Goal]] — *ØNE · Creator's Intent · Heaven on Earth · for All · forever — the single highest aim that by definition contains within itself and orders every other aim.*
- [[The Great Game]] — *All of us, together, exiting the Caves, crossing the Wilderness, and making every place we touch a little more like Heaven.*
- [[The Great Game of LIØNSBERG]] — *All of us, together, exiting the Caves, journeying through the Wilderness, and making every place we touch a little more like Heaven. That is the Game. Heaven is the Goal. Everything else is a matter of how.*
- [[The Offering]] — *What each person puts in the middle of the table, freely, each week.*
- [[The Story of LIØNSBERG]] — *The Story of ØNE and All, told as faithfully as we can presently tell it — the world a player steps into, and the telling that gives ten thousand tables one language.*
- [[The Turn]] — *The weekly gathering: did, gap, will, offer, ask, story, Offering, thanks.*
- [[The Twelve Words]] — *Twelve words are enough to play: one meaning each, numbered so that line six is line six in every language, and each opening onto a whole page for whoever wants more.*
- [[The Twenty]] — *The words at the table. If you learn these twenty, you can play tonight; everything else is handed to you the moment you need it.*
- [[ØNE]] — *The eternal, universal Source and Sustainer of All — the centre around which everything else orbits, and the thirteenth that the twelve Pillars derive their power from.*

Then [[The Two Hundred]], then [[The Field]] whole.

# The Tipping Point Cascade

*A ladder of thresholds walked one domain at a time — first node, first thousand, first one percent, first three percent, first thirteen percent — each a point past which the change in that domain carries itself, and each a trigger to move into the domain next door.*

Change does not spread evenly across a society; it saturates one field at a time. Education is not medicine is not farming is not local government, and a practice that has become normal among teachers may be unheard of among farmers in the same town. The cascade takes this seriously by refusing to measure adoption in one planetary number. Each domain has its own ladder, its own count, and its own moment of ignition.

The rungs are plain. **The first node** — one body in that domain actually doing it, which converts the idea from argument to example. **The first thousand** — enough that practitioners meet each other by accident. **The first one percent** — visible to the domain as a phenomenon rather than an oddity. **The first three percent** — the threshold the whole strategy is organized around, past which the spread stops depending on anyone's effort ([[The First Three Percent]], [[Tipping Point Strategy]]). **The first thirteen percent** — past which the old way, not the new one, has to justify itself. Each rung is both a milestone and a trigger: reaching it in one domain is the signal to begin in the adjacent one, so that saturation proceeds by neighbourhood rather than by campaign. Further up, as adoption in a domain crosses seventy to eighty percent, the deeper levers open and the formal rules of that domain can be renewed to match what its people already practise — constitutional change arriving as ratification of a fact rather than as imposition on a population.

The honesty of the instrument is that every rung is countable. A strategy that says *we are growing* can hide for years; a strategy that must name which domain, which rung, and when cannot ([[Measure everything, manage to the milestones]], [[The Record as the Movement's variance gauge]]).

# The Pledge

*The first Commitment: what you will give for the season ahead, said before the first Quest.*

**Successful growth depends on commitments kept, not on yeses gathered.**

The Pledge is where a yes becomes a quantity. Having answered [[The Invitation]], a player says what they will contribute for the coming season: their time, energy, and attention each week; a reciprocal contribution of treasure, of any amount, flowing to the commons; and the gifts, capacities, and crafts they bring to the table. It is the commitment all the others stand on, and it is made at [[Initiation (as stage)]] — before the first gathering, not after a few seasons of watching.

It is load-bearing because it makes the body visible to itself. A [[Commitment]] tells a table what one week holds; the gathered Pledges tell the body what the season holds, so that it can plan, provision, and choose Quests into the future rather than hoping in the dark. The Score says what has flowed; the Pledges say what is coming.

How it is played. Say it at your gathering, in your own words, and write it where your Cell keeps its record. Name a real weekly figure rather than a generous one — a baseline of about a tenth of a working week is the stated starting measure, and a pledge that is quietly missed teaches the table not to trust pledges. It is living: update it whenever your season of life changes, and renew it at each turning, because freshly chosen beats silently assumed. And it is held like every other word in the Game: say what you will give; give what you said; learn from the difference. No lesson and no agent asks it of you; it is asked by people, at a table.

For the first three percent, the Pledge carries one more commitment, and it is the one the body grows by — the tactic that moves it through the rings of the [[LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth and Cycle Time|Cycles of Growth]]: to bring in three people not already playing within three days of your own yes ([[Pass The Flame]], [[The First Three Percent]]). Three is the minimum, never a cap. Because it is a commitment with a date, it is kept or not — and that is what joins the Pledge to the rest of the Game's accounting.

The Pledge, the trust graph, and commitment-based management are one system. The Pledge is a recorded commitment. [[The Roll]] records who brought whom, and whether each commitment was kept; gathered, those lines are the web of trust ([[The Trust Graph and the analog contact-tree]]). Believability, in any domain, grows from commitments kept and is never claimed: the fourth move, played at the scale of the body — say what you will do, do what you said, learn from the gap ([[Do the Work]], [[The gap]]). The same discipline runs every weekly *I will ___ by ___* at a table ([[Commitment]], [[The Commitment Tracker]], [[Networks of Commitments]]).

# The Five Moves

*The compressed loop, as it is printed on the card: form a Cell · choose a Quest · learn and grow · share your stories · pass the Flame.*

Five moves are what fits on [[The Playcard]] and in one reading aloud. They are the same breath as [[The Seven Moves]] with the inner moves folded in: weaving coherence lives inside forming the Cell, and doing the work and learning from it are carried together as *learn and grow*. A table that plays the five is playing the whole loop; a table that has played a few seasons will find the other two moves already happening and may want their names.

They are load-bearing because they are the first thing a new player receives and the last thing a Cell forgets. Each is stated so a ten-year-old can repeat it: three to thirteen by name; one real thing this season; say what you will do, do what you said, learn from the gap; every Quest becomes a story; carry the Game to the two or three who are ready.

How they are played. Move one is [[Form a Cell]]: by name, with an agreement out loud about how you will treat each other, and two hosts for the season. Move two is [[Choose a Quest]]: real, near, and yours. Move three is the discipline of the week — [[The Turn]], [[Commitment]], [[The Circle's Scorecard]], and the gap. Move four is [[Share the Stories]], because a story travels further than its tellers. Move five is [[Pass The Flame]], and the new Cell is its own, never a branch of yours.

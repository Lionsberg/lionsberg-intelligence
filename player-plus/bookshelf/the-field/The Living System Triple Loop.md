# The Living System Triple Loop

*Prototype → Commons → OmniSpection → refined Pattern → new Prototype. The circuit by which what a Circle learns returns as something the next Circle begins with.*

A Circle receives the [[Prototype]] — the current best integrated form — and lives it. What it learns goes into the [[Commons]]: solved once anywhere, available everywhere. At the turning the Body looks at itself from above ([[OmniSpection]]) and reads what the field is reporting. The Pattern is refined in light of it. The refined Pattern goes back into the Prototype, and the next Circle begins from a higher floor.

Three loops are running at once inside that one circuit, which is why the name says triple: the practitioner's loop, where the practice changes the practitioner; the Body's loop, where the Circle's experience changes the Circle's way; and the Language's loop, where the lived result changes the Pattern that was lived. Skipping any of them breaks the return. A Body that improves its own practice but never contributes upward leaves the next Circle starting where it started. A Language refined without embodiment sharpens claims no one has tested.

The loop is what makes [[Current Best Understanding]] more than a disclaimer: there is an actual mechanism by which understanding is replaced by better understanding, and it runs on lived evidence rather than argument. It is the [[The Triadic Discipline]] seen as a circuit, and it is where [[Knowledge as commons]] earns its keep — contribution *is* distribution.

The loop is named in the Language's meta-architecture and not yet written out anywhere; no page describes how a refinement actually travels from a Circle's season to a changed Card. [[How A Pattern Enters The Language]] is the missing half of it.

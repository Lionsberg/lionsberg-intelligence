# Patterns Pending Articulation

*The honest register of what is named and not yet written — the backlog held in the open, where it can be read as a debt rather than mistaken for a library.*

The proportions are worth stating plainly. About fifty-five Pattern Cards are drafted. Roughly five hundred and seventy-five further names sit in a curated backlog, and a two-wave survey surfaced several thousand more candidates — a grand total in the region of five thousand four hundred names and candidates, awaiting synthesis and deduplication.

Most of that number is not missing wisdom; it is the same wisdom articulated many times from many vantages. The work that reduces it is the work of the Language itself: folding applications into the general Pattern they apply ([[General Pattern, Specific Application]]), separating containers from Patterns ([[Patterns And Containers]]), and choosing one working name per wisdom with the rest honored as aliases ([[Word Tools]]). Deduplication is not tidying; it is how the underlying unity becomes visible.

Some of the backlog is a warning rather than a promise. A large sweep of verb-coinages — several hundred names formed by turning activities into gerunds — has no card, no inbound link, and no use outside its own list. Names are cheap; notes are not. A name with nothing behind it is an [[The Incipient Link]] that has not been paid, and a list of them can look like a Language while transmitting nothing.

Holding the register in the open is itself a discipline. A reader can tell what is built from what is only named, a writer can see where the next work is, and the Language cannot quietly mistake its index for its substance. The register is read together with [[Gaps Not Yet Closed]], which names the territory not yet reached at all.

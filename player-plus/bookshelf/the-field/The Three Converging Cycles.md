# The Three Converging Cycles

*Three patterns of very different length — an imperial one, a civilisational one, and one running since agriculture — claimed to be reaching their turning points inside the same narrow window. Which is why this has no template.*

**The shortest** is the cycle of a dominant power: fiscal overextension, military overreach, wealth concentrating upward, institutions decaying, cohesion thinning, and a rising challenger. The corpus places it at roughly two and a half centuries and reads the present order as in its terminal phase ([[Decline]], [[The Existing Order]], [[Centralization Of Power]]).

**The middle one** is the architecture beneath that order — an arrangement built over about five centuries on extraction, the territorial state, industrial production and a picture of reality with nothing in it but measurable matter. The claim is not that it is old but that it has reached its own limits: the frontiers are consumed, the substrate is poisoned, the state form cannot address planetary problems, and the worldview has produced a civilisation unable to generate reasons to continue ([[Meaning]], [[The Old Operating System]], [[Consciousness Is Primary]]).

**The longest** is the template since agriculture: life organised around accumulated surplus, hierarchy, territory, and a progressive disconnection of consciousness from its ground — a small group holding what a large group produces, maintained across generations by story and by force ([[Kontrolle]], [[The Long Night]], [[Extraction Of Wealth]]).

The argument is about **convergence**, not about any one of them. Each alone would be an upheaval of historic size; the shortest reshapes a world for generations, the longest has turned once in ten thousand years. All three at once produces something for which there is no case study — which is why the reply *empires have always fallen and we continued* is answered not by disputing the history but by naming the scale, the interconnection, the weapons, and the convergence ([[The Bronze Age Parallel]], [[The Meta Crisis]], [[The Collapse-Ascension Nexus]], [[The Window]]).

**The cycle lengths are estimates, not measurements** — the source's readings of historical pattern, stated from 2026, with the diagnostic criteria given but no derivation. Cyclical readings of history are contested as a method, and nothing here settles that ([[We Do Not Know]], [[Navigating Towards Truth]], [[Cycle]]).

What does not depend on the arithmetic is the structural point: several things of very different sizes are failing at once, and each makes the others harder.

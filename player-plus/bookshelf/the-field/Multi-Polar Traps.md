# Multi-Polar Traps

*Where every actor, each behaving sensibly for itself, produces together an outcome not one of them wanted — and none can stop alone.*

The structure is always the same. Several parties compete. Some behaviour gives whoever adopts it an advantage. It also does harm, but the harm is shared by everyone while the advantage is kept by the one. So each party adopts it, not out of malice but because refusing means losing to the ones who do not refuse. The harm accumulates. Everyone can see it. Nobody can stop, because stopping unilaterally is simply losing.

That is why it is the crucial shape in the whole diagnosis. A firm that pollutes is cheaper than one that does not; an army that stops arming is at the mercy of one that does not; a platform that refuses to farm attention loses to one that farms it. The participants are not villains and removing any one of them changes nothing, because the trap is in the structure and not in the people ([[The Meta Crisis]] · [[Tragedy of the Commons]] · [[Systemic Failure Modes]] · [[The Pattern Governs, Not People]]).

Understanding this is what rules out the two obvious responses. Moral appeal fails, because the participants already know. Blame fails, because the next occupant of the same position faces the same incentives. Only a change of structure works, and only three kinds of change do: a coordination that makes the harmful move unattractive for everyone at once; a shared restraint that all parties can verify; or a different game that people can actually leave for, where the trap's logic does not apply ([[Agreement]] · [[Requirement Of Transparency]] · [[Federations vs Empires]] · [[Liberation and Exodus]]).

The Game takes the third route. It does not try to out-compete the trap on the trap's terms; it builds a place where the incentives differ — where value is held in common rather than raced for, where nobody's advance requires another's loss, and where leaving is always permitted so that participation is never coerced ([[Commons]] · [[None Of Us Until All Of Us]] · [[Voluntary Association]] · [[Withdrawal Of Consent]] · [[Islands of Coherence]]). Whether that works is exactly the bet the Game is making ([[The load-bearing bets]]).

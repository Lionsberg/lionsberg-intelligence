# The LIØNSBERG Constitution

*The constitutional layer of the body: the foundational principles and processes by which a people governs itself — held as a living covenant rather than a document, and explicitly interim.*

A constitution states what a body is, where its authority comes from, what it may never do, and how it may be changed. This one recognizes no throne but [[ØNE]]: authority arises from resonance with [[Logos]], every steward and Circle and world is holofractally sovereign, and entry is by assent, never imposition. Its preamble is spoken by *the free and sovereign inhabitants of Earth and beyond*, and it is not bound to any state, nation, planet, or species.

It is load-bearing as the layer that cannot be changed by whoever is in the room this week. Ordinary agreements flex with circumstance; a constitution is what a body protects its purpose with ([[Constitutional Protection Of Purpose]]), and its durability is deliberate — amendment requires a very high bar, and the seven recognitions beneath it are not amendable at all.

Its own page is candid about its state: an **interim** constitution, explicitly provisional until ratified, approximately a tenth complete — an architectural skeleton awaiting a year of co-writing by the Circles. It names the sixteen functions it must eventually express, among them identity, covenant, the source of authority, sovereignty architecture, rights paired with responsibilities, consent-based governance, economy and commons, restorative justice, entry and exit, amendment, dispute resolution, emergency provisions, and structural immunity.

In play a [[Cell]] meets it by assent rather than by reading: assent to the general constitution, then a local declaration, then, if the body matures, a local constitution of its own that incorporates the general one by reference.

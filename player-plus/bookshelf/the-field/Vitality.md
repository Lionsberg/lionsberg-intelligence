# Vitality

*Life-force moving freely — the dynamic expression of [[Energy]] flowing without obstruction through body, mind, and spirit.*

Vitality is the felt reading of health rather than its definition. Where [[Wellness]] names the integrated state across all the planes, vitality names the movement within it: warmth, appetite, clarity, the willingness of a body to begin things. It is read directly and immediately, before any measurement, which makes it the earliest signal available to a person about their own condition and the condition of those around them.

It is load-bearing because it is the first thing lost and the last thing counted. A [[Cell]] tracking only [[Kept as a share of made]] will watch its numbers hold for two seasons while its members quietly empty out, and then lose three of them in a month. Vitality is what [[The Turn]] is asking after when a player says how they are, and what the Circle's one-word reading of itself on [[The Season Sheet]] is reaching for. Naming it makes a decline sayable before it becomes a departure.

In play, vitality is tended rather than produced. [[Breath]], [[Sleeping]], [[Nutrition]], [[Rest And Regeneration]], and movement are its ordinary instruments; [[Detoxification]] removes what blocks it; [[Trauma]] and unresolved [[Integration]] are its most common obstructions. A person notices theirs daily; a Circle notices one another's weekly; a [[Community]] notices its own at the seasonal turning through [[OmniSpection]].

Two cautions. Vitality is not a virtue and not a ranking — illness, age, [[Dying]], and the honest limits of a body are not failures of it, and a system that treats vitality as merit recreates the cruelty it was meant to answer. And vitality can be borrowed against: stimulants, urgency, and a worthy [[Quest]] can all buy months of apparent aliveness at the cost of years. The reading is only useful when it is honest.

# The Circle's Shared Field, And How Circles Form

*What a body of three to thirteen becomes over years of practising together — five capacities beyond any member — and the five ways such a body comes into being.*

**What the shared field carries.** *Greater reach* — what no single member perceives, the body perceives by integrating everyone's partial readings ([[Sensemaking]], [[Sensing The Field]]). *Stronger protection* — the body holds against pressures no member could hold alone ([[The Four Layers Of Shielding]]). *More refined discernment* — decisions reached in a coherent body arrive with more accuracy than any one member's ([[Consent-Based Decision-Making]], [[Discernment]]). *Sustained presence beyond any one person's stamina* — a watch, a vigil, a long piece of work, held in shifts across time none could cover ([[Mutual Aid]]). *The capacity to transmit* — a body in coherence becomes a source others can receive from, which individuals mostly cannot be ([[Transmission]]).

**The maturation curve is the part most bodies need to hear.** A newly formed group of three has a barely formed shared field. The *same* group three years later, having actually walked the disciplines together, holds a substantially stronger one. Nothing here is available at the first meeting; it is what practice deposits ([[Circle Coherence]], [[Trust]], [[The Practice Cadence]]).

**Five ways a body forms.** *By recognition* — people already in each other's orbit recognise one another and gather; the most natural. *By an Elder convening* — someone who sees that a body is ready calls it into being and steps back as it coheres ([[Wise Eldership]]). *By split from a larger body* — a group past its ceiling spawns a second, witnessed, carrying the parent's lineage forward ([[Why Three To Thirteen]], [[Midwife a New Circle]]). *By transmission from a teacher* — students trained over years form a body that carries the teaching on ([[Apprenticeship]], [[Lineage]]). *By the moment requiring it* — people find each other through events nobody planned; such bodies are recognised by their forming rather than by anyone's intention.

**Three types**, by what a body is for: a *training* body, mostly newer members with one or two experienced guides; an *operational* body, seasoned, holding actual responsibility; and a *masters'* body, holding the deepest work and the handing-on. Most people pass through all three across a life, and a healthy body often spans levels rather than sorting by them.

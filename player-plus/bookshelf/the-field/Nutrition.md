# Nutrition

*The taking in of life-giving substance in harmony with the body's design — eating held as a relationship rather than a transaction.*

Food is medicine, and it is also communion. Every meal is an exchange with the living world: something died or gave so that a body could continue, and the plainest form of [[Reciprocity]] available to a person is to receive that knowingly. Eating in gratitude is not sentiment added to a meal; it is the meal read accurately. What a body is built for, where the food came from, whose hands grew it, and what the growing did to the soil are one question, not four.

It is load-bearing because it is the daily point where [[Pillar V — Sacred Health and Wellness]] and [[Pillar VI — Sacred Ecology and Living Systems]] meet in a single act, several times a day, for everyone. There is no version of health that routes around food, and no version of a regenerated [[Ecology]] that routes around how a people eats. The [[Earth]] contains what is needed to heal, and its plants, elements, and rhythms are to be respected, protected, and understood — a claim with immediate consequences for what a [[Community]] grows and how.

In play, nutrition is one of the ordinary daily disciplines, sitting beside [[Breath]], [[Sleeping]], and [[Rest And Regeneration]]. At the table of a [[Cell]] it is also social: food shared is one of the oldest forms of [[The Offering]], and a gathering that eats together binds differently from one that does not. [[Fasting]] is its deliberate interruption, carried as a distinct practice. Where food is scarce, the Circle's floor of care applies before any card does.

The canon holds the shape and not the specifics. It does not prescribe a diet, and it should not: bodies, places, seasons, and traditions differ, and a single prescribed diet would be exactly the kind of imposed uniformity the Pattern refuses. What it asks is that a person know what they are eating, where it came from, and what it is doing to them and to the place it came from.

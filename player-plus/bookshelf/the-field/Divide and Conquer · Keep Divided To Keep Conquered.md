# Divide and Conquer · Keep Divided To Keep Conquered

*The oldest instrument of domination, and its second half — which is the part usually missed. Dividing wins the first fight; keeping divided is what makes the winning permanent.*

The mechanism runs on two tools working together, and neither works alone. **Identity** does the sorting: people are grouped, named, categorised, and set against each other along whatever lines will hold. **Fear** does the binding: the more danger each group can be shown in the other, the stronger the hardening between them ([[Identity]], [[Fear]], [[Kontrolle]], [[Prevalence Of Deceit And Propaganda]]).

Then the loop closes on its own. The harder the division, the less each group has to do with the other; the less contact, the more each identifies with what makes it different rather than with what it shares; and a people that identifies primarily by its differences cannot be rallied by anything, including its own interest ([[Navigating Difference]], [[Belonging]], [[Multi-Polar Traps]]).

That is why the second half of the name matters. A conquered people that reunites has to be conquered again; a conquered people kept divided never has to be. So the maintenance is continuous, and it is much cheaper than the conquest was ([[Control Of Information]], [[Manufactured Dependency]], [[Centralization Of Power]]).

The counter is not unity as a slogan, which is the same instrument pointed the other way. It is the ordinary work of contact across a line: shared stories, shared work, shared meals, and the discovery of what is actually held in common, which is almost always more than either side was told ([[Share the Stories]], [[The Golden Thread]], [[Dialogos]], [[Welcoming The Stranger]], [[Reconciliation]]).

And the diagnostic is usable today, on anything. When an account of a conflict makes the other side incomprehensible, ask who benefits from your not understanding them. The answer is rarely either side ([[Discernment]], [[No False-Oppositional Framings]], [[The Meta Dragon]]).

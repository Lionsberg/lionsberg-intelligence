# The First Three Percent

*Three in every hundred — the already-awakening, found and organized before the heaviest weather.*

What the hour asks is not everyone. Three percent of any population is the fraction at which a body begins to change itself, and three percent of a [[Community]] of ten thousand is the archetypal three hundred who hold the pattern alive in a place ([[The 300]], [[Tipping Point Strategy]]). On Earth the same fraction is two hundred and fifty million people — enough, and only just. On the rings of the [[LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth and Cycle Time|Cycles of Growth]] it is cycle forty (267,914,296), and the rings are the measure: the ring the body stands on and days per ring, read daily. Their road is charted to the Winter Solstice of 2026, and the tactic that moves the body through the rings is this — from eight Players, every new Player bringing their three as their first passing of the Flame, aiming at three in three days (the arithmetic allows about five), which reaches cycle forty between 13 and 15 November ([[29 The Plan — the first three percent#The chart|the chart]]). A Player counts by four conditions: read the Story, taken [[The Pledge]], formed a Cell (three to thirteen people, optimally four to seven), chosen a Quest; and for the first three percent the fifth is crucial and qualifies — the first passing of the Flame, bringing in their three, people not already playing. For the first three percent the Pledge includes that commitment, within three days, so it is a commitment kept or not, recorded on [[The Roll]]. That first passing makes the body self-growing. Three is the minimum, never a cap: the gifted connectors keep inviting the wise right people.

They are not chosen by any centre and do not appoint themselves. They are called out by the people who know them best: every person invited, and the first act of every voice the naming of the three they would most trust to lead their community through what is coming — not family, not the famous, not the loud, but the faithful. When three people name the same person, that person receives the invitation, carried by the hands that named them ([[The Invitation]], [[Pass The Flame]], [[Trust]]).

The threshold is met by conditions rather than by a headcount: they hold a shared story, a shared language, and a shared pattern of action; they have passed the flame; and they are each in a small body that meets ([[Story]], [[The Twelve Words]], [[Cell]], [[The Turn]]). Absent any one of those, a large number of sympathetic people is still a mailing list.

A people that has called out its own leaders cannot be beheaded, bought, or fooled by anyone naming leaders for it. Broadcasts gather watchers; hands gather workers ([[Servant Leadership]], [[Structural Immunity]], [[The Force For Good]]).

# The First Three Percent

*Three in every hundred — the already-awakening, found and organized before the heaviest weather.*

What the hour asks is not everyone. Three percent of any population is the fraction at which a body begins to change itself, and three percent of a [[Community]] of ten thousand is the archetypal three hundred who hold the pattern alive in a place ([[The 300]], [[Tipping Point Strategy]]). On Earth the same fraction is a few hundred million people — enough, and only just.

They are not chosen by any centre and do not appoint themselves. They are called out by the people who know them best: every person invited, and the first act of every voice the naming of the two or three they would most trust to lead their community through what is coming — not family, not the famous, not the loud, but the faithful. When three people name the same person, that person receives the invitation, carried by the hands that named them ([[The Invitation]], [[Pass The Flame]], [[Trust]]).

The threshold is met by conditions rather than by a headcount: they hold a shared story, a shared language, and a shared pattern of action; they have passed the flame; and they are each in a small body that meets ([[Story]], [[The Twelve Words]], [[Cell]], [[The Turn]]). Absent any one of those, a large number of sympathetic people is still a mailing list.

A people that has called out its own leaders cannot be beheaded, bought, or fooled by anyone naming leaders for it. Broadcasts gather watchers; hands gather workers ([[Servant Leadership]], [[Structural Immunity]], [[The Force For Good]]).

# Attend, Plan, Act, Learn, Improve Cycle

*The five-beat form of Element twelve, with attention placed first: before planning, notice what is actually here.*

Four of the five beats are the familiar improvement cycle. The distinctive one is the first. **Attend** — read the situation as it is, not as the last plan assumed it to be; sense what the field is reporting before deciding what to do about it. Plans that begin at *plan* inherit yesterday's picture and optimize against it.

**Plan** sets what will be done and by whom, pulled backward from what the work is for. **Act** does it, at the smallest scale that can act well. **Learn** reads the gap between what was planned and what happened, without blame, and asks what the gap is teaching. **Improve** puts the lesson where the next cycle will meet it — in the Card, the check, the set-up — rather than in a memory that will fade.

The fifth beat is what separates this from a loop that merely repeats. Learning that is not embedded is entertainment. A lesson closed only when its fix sits in the place the work will pass through is the discipline that makes the cycle cumulative ([[Every Defect Is A Gift]]).

The cycle is fractal, like the Element it expresses: a person can run it between two tasks, a Cell runs it weekly at [[The Turn]], a Quest runs it across a season, and a Body runs it at the turning in [[OmniSpection]]. Nothing about the beats changes with scale; only the period does.

It is named in the canon at the root and its page is empty — a title with no body — while the substance lives in [[Tightly Coupled Learning and Action]] and in the continuous-learning sections of the Pattern Language.

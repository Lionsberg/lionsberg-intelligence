# Join the Seasonal Gathering

*At each turning the Cells come together: bring your story, hear the others, feel the body you are part of.*

A Cell turns its own season at its own table ([[Turn the Season]]). This is the other half of the same turning — the moment the tables meet. It exists because a body made of many small autonomous parts has one predictable failure: each part can be perfectly alive and still have no felt experience of the whole. Numbers on a page do not repair that. Being in a room, or on a call, while other people say what happened to them does.

Three things are asked, and they are deliberately small. **Bring your story** — the season's best, told short and true, as witness rather than as advertisement ([[Share the Stories]], [[The Season Sheet]]). **Hear the others** — which is the part most likely to be skipped, and the part that does the work; a body learns more from one honest account of a season that went badly than from a year of summaries. **Feel the body you are part of** — no task attached. Recognition is the point.

It is the seasonal counterpart of what the weekly [[The Turn|Turn]] does inside one Cell, and it does for the wider body what the Turn does for the table: converts a set of separate efforts into something that knows itself. What travels upward in writing is the [[The Season Sheet|Season Sheet]]; what travels in a gathering is the part a sheet cannot carry ([[The Way Up]], [[The Return]]).

There is no obligation in it, and no gate. A Cell that cannot come is still a Cell. But a Cell that never comes will slowly stop being able to tell whether it is one of many or simply alone, and that misreading is corrosive — it is where both discouragement and self-importance begin ([[Home]], [[Islands of Coherence]]).

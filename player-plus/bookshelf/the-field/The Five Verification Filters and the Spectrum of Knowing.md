# The Five Verification Filters and the Spectrum of Knowing

*Seven legitimate ways of knowing, none of them sovereign over the others — and five filters a claim passes through before it may be held as settled.*

A body that accepts only what can be measured will be blind to most of what matters, and a body that accepts whatever is felt will believe anything. The way between is not a compromise but a discipline: name the mode by which a thing is known, and label it as such wherever it is written.

The **spectrum** holds seven modes, each fit for its own domain: empirical evidence · logical inference · transmitted witness, what trustworthy people have handed down · hypothesis, held provisionally and known to be held that way · archetypal true myth, which carries what propositions cannot ([[The True Myth]]) · convention, which is true because we agreed it · and direct knowing ([[Gnosis]]). No single mode is sovereign. Empirical evidence does not adjudicate a myth; a myth does not overturn a measurement. What the discipline requires is that claims be *explicitly labelled by mode*, so that a reader can weigh them properly instead of being asked to swallow an assertion in a scientist's coat ([[Types Of Knowing]], [[Provenance]]).

The **five filters** are what a substantive claim passes before it is held as more than someone's view. Coherence with the source: does this hold together with the deepest thing we know ([[ØNE]])? Coherence with the pattern language: does it fit what has already been found to work, or does it contradict it, and if so, which gives way ([[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language]])? Suppression analysis: who benefits if this is not looked at, and has it been kept from view? Cross-domain integration: does it hold when carried into a field other than the one it was born in? Harmonic resonance: does it ring true to people of discernment who have sat with it ([[Resonance]], [[Discernment]]).

Around both stands the humility that makes them usable. Truth-seeking is constitutional and no received doctrine is exempt; anyone may propose a new hypothesis without penalty; all articulation is provisional, because the pattern does not change but the words for it keep sharpening ([[Everything Is Current Best Understanding]], [[Do Not Concretize the Absolute]], [[Navigating Towards Truth]]).

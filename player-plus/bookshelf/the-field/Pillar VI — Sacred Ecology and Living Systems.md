# Pillar VI — Sacred Ecology and Living Systems

*The sphere in which humanity takes its actual place inside the web of life — not above it, not outside it, and not in charge of it.*

Its one sentence is the sharpest of the twelve: *regeneration must replace extraction.* Under it sit [[Ecology]], [[Earth]] as a living being rather than a warehouse, the ladder of place from [[Watershed]] to [[Ecoregion]] to [[Bioregion]], the [[Living System]] frame that names what all of it is, and the practices of [[Permaculture]], [[Compost]], [[Kinship]], and [[Reverence]] by which a people lives somewhere without consuming it.

It is load-bearing because it fixes the ground the whole build stands on. There is no environment separate from us; there is no nature outside of humanity; the destruction of the living world and the destruction of the self are the same act at different scales. Every other Pillar has to be redesigned to fit inside this one — agriculture, industry, architecture, economy, governance — or the civilization built by them ends the same way the last one is ending. And the Pillar supplies the geography [[The Pattern of Cooperation]] federates across: political lines were imposed, watersheds were not.

In play it shows up in three concrete places. First, in the scales: a [[Cell]] sits in a [[Watershed]], a [[Community]] in an Ecoregion, Ecoregions in Bioregions — the bioregional ladder by which the Whole is composed from actual places. Second, in the seat: a community body seats [[Living Systems Member]] at its own table, alongside the seat for future generations and the seat for the Whole, so the land is represented where decisions are made. Third, in the [[Quest]] — stewarding a watershed, honouring relationship beyond the human, and feeding the soil are real seasonal work a Circle can do and point to.

What is unwritten here is substantial: the seven pattern cards of place, and [[Inter-Kingdom Relationship]] — the mineral, plant, fungal, microbial, elemental, ancestor, and unborn kingdoms — are named and not yet patterned.

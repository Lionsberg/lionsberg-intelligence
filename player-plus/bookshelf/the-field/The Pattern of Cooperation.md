# The Pattern of Cooperation

*Elements one through eight: how sovereign beings organize, govern, and protect their shared work without anyone answer anyone.*

A shared Purpose that governs rather than a person who does. Complete wholes federated from the bottom up, each with an equal voice in the Whole. Authority that flows from consent and never from command, each domain held by a membrane it controls. Agreements that govern, one voice per person, elders who counsel rather than rule. Capital kept in service. Formation before institution. Maturity proved by planting the next seed. And an immune system written in from the start.

The eight are not eight good ideas; they are one architecture, and each holds the others up. Purpose without federation becomes a movement with a headquarters. Federation without sovereignty at every scale becomes empire by another name. Sovereignty without shared agreements becomes eight people who cannot decide anything. Agreements without capital subordinated get bought. All of it without formation cannot replicate, because the pattern travels through people or not at all.

What makes this half distinctive is what it refuses. There is no centre to tithe to, no authority to appeal to above the smallest scale that can decide well, and no role that carries immunity from those it serves. In place of a ruler there is a [[Field of Agreements]] read at the door and accepted once; in place of enforcement, the freedom to leave.

Every association of sovereign beings, by the act of associating, births four things: a [[Domain]] of responsibility and authority, a [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]], a [[Field of Agreements]], and a [[Commons]]. That is the cooperative architecture in its smallest complete form, and it is the same at a table of five and at a bioregion.

The production half ([[The Pattern of Production]]) is what turns cooperation into built things. Cooperation without it produces good meetings and no cathedral.

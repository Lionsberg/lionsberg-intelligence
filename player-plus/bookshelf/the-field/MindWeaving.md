# MindWeaving

*Weaving finite minds together into shared understanding — the living process by which what no single vantage can see becomes visible to the whole.*

Every mind is finite, and the finitude is not a failing to be corrected but the permanent condition of a located being: to see from here is to not see from there ([[Mind]] · [[Perception]] · [[Boundaries and Limits of Conception]]). It follows that the deepest acts of collective navigation cannot be done alone — reviewing where a body actually stands, making sense of what is happening, keeping a common map corrected as the ground moves. These can only be done in company, and MindWeaving is the name of the doing ([[OmniSpection]] · [[Sensemaking]] · [[Navigating Towards Truth]]).

The practice is plain: minds meeting in good faith, perceptions compared, maps corrected against one another, understanding woven strand by strand until it holds. It is how reality is made intersubjective — not by agreeing, but by weaving ([[Dialogos]] · [[Listening]] · [[Jam]] · [[Council]] · [[Resonance]]). The Game asks every Co-Creator to do it periodically rather than leaving it to whoever happens to like meetings.

The word carries a second register, and the canon holds both deliberately. MindWeaving is also a **literary craft**: the careful weaving of great minds and works — the wisdom inheritances of humanity — into the living body of this canon, honouring each source while binding its threads into the larger weave. The studies made this way are worked thread by thread as living studies rather than finished summaries ([[Foundational Literature]] · [[Lineage]] · [[Attribution]] · [[Weaving vs. Starting New]] · [[Provenance]]).

In both registers the discipline is identical, and it is what makes weaving different from merging. **No thread is erased. No voice is collapsed into another. The weave is stronger than any strand alone.** That is why a dissent stays legible after a decision, why a source keeps its own words, and why a body that has woven well can still be unwoven back to who said what ([[Author's Voice Is Sovereign]] · [[Weave, don't delete]] · [[Meaningful Objection]] · [[Elision]]).

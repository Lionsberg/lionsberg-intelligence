# Rule Number 2

*Do the wise right thing, at the wise right time, with the wise right people, to progressively realize the Goal.*

The first rule says what is the case. This one says what to do about it, and it is offered as a useful derivative rather than as a second authority — everything in it is already implied by [[Rule Number One]], and it is written out because most people need the implication spelled into a sentence they can actually act on.

Four clauses, and each is doing distinct work. **The wise right thing** — not the right thing alone, which can be technically correct and still destructive, and not the wise thing alone, which can be shrewd and unprincipled; the two words together ask for an act that is both sound and good ([[Wise Right]], [[Wisdom]], [[Righteousness]], [[Discernment]]). **At the wise right time** — because the same act is a gift at one hour and a harm at another, and most failures of judgment in a body are failures of timing rather than of content ([[Kairos]], [[Just in time]], [[Attend To The Most Important Things First]]). **With the wise right people** — because almost nothing worth doing is done alone, and who is at the table decides more about the outcome than the plan does ([[Cell]], [[The First Few]], [[Trust]], [[Seats at the Table]]). **To progressively realize the Goal** — which supplies the *why*, and without which the first three clauses are merely a well-mannered way of doing anything at all ([[The Goal]], [[Throughput]], [[Progressive canonization]]).

*Progressively* is the word that keeps the rule livable. Nothing here asks for the whole thing at once, or for an act to be final, or for a person to be finished before they begin. It asks for a next move in the right direction, and then another — which is the same gradient every other part of the canon reads by ([[The Living Gradient]], [[Continuous Improvement]], [[The compass question]]).

Its intended use is small and constant rather than ceremonial. It is short enough to hold at the moment of a decision, which is the only moment a rule is any use, and it is the shape behind the Game's ordinary weekly question: what is the wise right next thing, for us, now ([[The Turn]], [[Choose a Quest]], [[Spontaneous Wise Right Action]]).

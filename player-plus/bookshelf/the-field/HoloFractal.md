# HoloFractal

*The compound of [[Holarchy]] and [[Fractal]]: nested wholes that are also self-similar across scales — the word the governance pages actually use.*

Two properties are being asserted at once. From Holarchy: each part is a whole in itself and a part of a larger whole, enfolded rather than commanded. From Fractal: the same pattern appears at every scale, so what is learned at one level transfers to the others. Together: each scale is at once a domain, a superorganism of the scales below it, and a subsystem of the scales above it, running the same pattern.

The compound earns its own name because the two properties do different work and are often confused. Nesting without self-similarity gives a layered bureaucracy where each level runs on its own logic. Self-similarity without nesting gives a scatter of identical cells that never compose into a body. HoloFractal insists on both, which is exactly what [[Holofractal Omnifederation]] requires: complete wholes, organically federated from the bottom up, every member holding an equal voice in the Whole, with no centre but [[ØNE]].

It is load-bearing for the shape of the body. The canonical nesting runs from the [[The Sovereign|Sovereign]] through the [[Cell]], the neighbourhood, the village, the [[Community]], the localities, the [[Ecoregion]], the [[Bioregion]], [[The Planet]], [[The Star System]], the [[Cosmos]], and [[ØNE]], with roughly an order of magnitude between adjacent levels and no true bottom or top. Non-geographic bodies — a [[Guild]], a [[Tribe]], a [[Voluntary Association]] — nest by the same law. [[HoloFractal Governance]] is the governance reading; [[Holofractal Scaling]] is the growth reading.

In play it is why the card has the same shape at every level, upper half common and lower half your own — one of the minimum standards that lets ten thousand tables be one game.

# The LIØNSBERG Theory of Force

*Before reading: this page holds a doctrine of last resort, written down so that it can be examined and corrected, not so that it can be acted on. Nothing here is advice, a licence, or a call to act; the law of the place a Player lives governs them; this instance asks nothing of anyone but consent. Read [[The Sacred Use Of Force]] first — this page is its current best articulation for this instance, one theory held across every form of force and every scale of holon, from a hand to the whole.*

*One discipline, whether the hand doing the acting belongs to a person, a Circle, a tool, or an institution — and whether the act creates, sustains, heals, or destroys.*

Force is the capacity to change what is possible for another being without that being's consent. This instance holds all four uses of that capacity — to **create**, to **sustain**, to **heal**, and to **destroy** — under a single discipline rather than four separate ones, because the same trained hands hold all four and a body that lets one drift untrained lets them all drift. A hand, a Circle, an institution, and a machine are held to the same doctrine; none is exempt because of its size or its material.

**Force is a last resort and never a first strategy.** Every use of it, whatever the hand and whatever the form, is measured against the same tests, held together and none dispensable:

- **Purpose.** Restoration of right relationship — never punishment, domination, or victory.
- **Sufficiency.** Not how much an act deserves but how little will actually restore what was broken; asking the first question escalates without end, asking the second stops the moment it has worked.
- **Alignment.** Answerable to ØNE and to All, to agreements freely entered, and to every holon the act touches — never to the will of whoever happens to be holding the instrument.
- **Consent, where it can be had.** The earlier work — naming harm and withdrawing consent from it — is tried first and remains available only while nothing has yet escalated; force is what is left when that work has genuinely failed.
- **The least force sufficient.** Applied at the minimum that stops the harm, and released the instant it has stopped.
- **Restoration after.** Witnessed, accounted for, and never celebrated — the capacity to destroy is laid down deliberately, mourned rather than carried as a trophy.

A use of force answers for itself at the scale it was exercised, and authority to invoke it rises no higher than the matter genuinely requires. A person facing an immediate threat to life, a breached protective field, a structure that can no longer be sustained without violation, or their own drifted capacity, authorizes their own response in the instant and accounts for it to witnesses afterward — the four conditions, and no others; revenge, ideology, and convenience are excluded by name. A household meets an acute crisis with whoever is present, reporting fully once the moment passes. A Cell invokes it through its stewarding voices, informs the wider Circle as soon as reasonably possible, and reviews it at the next gathering — raising no standing guard and keeping no armed class, because the capacity to protect lives where the people live and nowhere above them. A community or an ecoregion acts through its council when time is genuinely critical, and what it decided is ratified at the ordinary tier within a bounded window or it lapses. The instance as a whole acts at the scale of its widest stewarding body, with the fullest consent required wherever an entrenched provision is touched — and no invocation, at any scale, ever reaches the Absolute Root that makes the body the body it is. Every invocation is purpose-bounded, scope-bounded, time-bounded, reviewable by a body independent of whoever acted, revocable by the ones it touches, and returns power to ordinary process the moment the bound emergency ends; none accumulates by repetition into a standing power.

This is one instance of the wider discipline the [[The LIØNSBERG Code]] and the [[Organizations The LIØNSBERG Way|LIØNSBERG Way]] hold for every capacity a body wields — the Wise Right Thing governing the moment rather than a rule written in advance for every moment. No test above is an absolute that decides a case by itself; a guardian, a Circle, or a council weighs all of them together, in the actual conditions, and the wisest reading of the moment carries the day. That is why every invocation is accounted for afterward rather than merely permitted in advance.

**Any form of technology is a tool**, and a body is free to develop and use tools in the wise right way — but no tool is neutral. What a tool makes cheap, a people makes common, so adopting one is itself a use of the same discipline: the maker and the adopter answer for what a technology makes likely exactly as a hand exercising force answers for it. The direction this instance builds toward is [[Weaponry To Livingry|the conversion of capacity built for domination into capacity that serves life]] — the same metallurgy, computation, and organizing genius pointed at provisioning rather than at harm. A holon retains the right to refuse a technology it discerns would diminish its own sovereignty or another's, and no participation in this instance is made conditional on adopting one where a plainer alternative exists; a tool without which a body cannot function has quietly become its ruler rather than its instrument.

**Restraint and detention are force, and are judged as force.** Restraint stops a harm in the moment: a hand held, a person moved from a doorway, a channel closed. Detention holds a person who is harming others, or who would, for as long as the harm requires and no longer. Both are sometimes the wise and right thing, and both answer to exactly the tests above: a true purpose, the least force sufficient, alignment with ØNE and All, the consent that can be had, care for the one held, restoration after, and an accounting to the holon that authorized it. What makes detention unsound is never the holding itself but its drift: a holding that outlives its purpose, that punishes rather than restores, or that a body keeps for its own comfort. [[Structural Immunity]] names that drift and guards against it; the Wise Right Thing, weighed in the moment and accounted for after, governs every case.

Every use of force, and every emergency invoked to justify one, meets its own limit in the being it would otherwise fall on. A holon may refuse personal participation in an act that would contradict the Goal, even where an outside power would compel it. A Circle or a Community may hold itself as a place of refuge, sheltering those leaving harm elsewhere, as an ordinary exercise of its own sovereignty rather than a favor granted by permission. Neither refusal is a loophole; both are the theory's own proof that force here is bounded by the beings it claims to protect, not merely by the ones who wield it.

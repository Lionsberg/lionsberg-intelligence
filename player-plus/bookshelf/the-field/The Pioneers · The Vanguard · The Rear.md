# The Pioneers · The Vanguard · The Rear

*Three positions in a crossing that is not a single file: those out front making the way, the leading body moving behind them, and the last still coming out — with supply and communication lines that have to run the whole length.*

A large movement is never all in one place. Some are years ahead; most are somewhere in the middle; some are only now leaving. Naming the positions is what makes that fact workable instead of embarrassing ([[The Movement]], [[The Wilderness]], [[Liberation and Exodus]]).

**The Pioneers** go first, into ground nobody has crossed, and their work is the environment, the conditions and the infrastructure that let everyone else come. Their early seasons are the hard ones by design: alone out front, they can coordinate by intuition, which is exactly why their arrangements do not survive being scaled ([[The First Few]], [[Founding Leaders, and the Founding Leader Phase]], [[Prototyping]]).

**The Vanguard** is the leading body — far enough forward to be in new country, large enough that intuition no longer coordinates it, and therefore the place where a way of working has to become explicit ([[The Escalation Ladder]], [[Higher Order Functional Unity]]).

**The Rear** is everyone still coming out, including the last. It is not a lesser position. A crossing whose front cannot hear its back has broken in the only way that matters ([[None Of Us Until All Of Us]], [[The LIØNSBERG Adoption Curve, and Loss Rate as the limiting variable]], [[Welcoming The Stranger]]).

**The lines between them are the actual work.** Supply running forward, communication running both ways — and the point of building well at the front is not only to help those behind but to establish those lines, because a front cut off from its rear stops being a vanguard and becomes a splinter ([[Supply Chains]], [[Communication Debt]], [[The Board]], [[Share the Stories]]).

Which is the discipline this names: the strength at the front is spent on the crossing itself rather than on getting further ahead ([[Do The Hard Work Ourselves To Make It Easier For Those Who Come After]], [[Voluntary Tithing]], [[The pace of trust]]).

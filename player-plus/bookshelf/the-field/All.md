# All
*All things, equally and without exception — the totality that [[ØNE]] emanates into and receives back.*

All is the companion term to ØNE. Where ØNE names the one thing that unites and orders everything within itself, All names everything so united: every being, every scale, every era, the living and the yet to live, the human and the far more than human. The two words are always used together here because neither is complete alone — ØNE without All is an abstraction; All without ØNE is a heap.

The phrase *equally and without exception* is doing work. It is what forbids the quiet carve-outs that every ethical system has historically made: this people but not that one, this generation but not the seventh, our species but not the rest. [[The Goal]] is for All, forever. [[Love]] is owed to ØNE, One Another, and All. [[Omniconsideration]] is the method of actually meaning it.

It is load-bearing because it sets the boundary condition of every design here. A [[Commons]] is stewarded for the benefit of all beings present and future within a domain; a [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]] may filter exchange but may not export harm; a [[Cell]] keeps seats for the living systems, for future generations, and for the Whole. Each of those is the word *All* made structural.

In play it is the widening question, asked when a decision looks good from inside: *and for whom is this not good?* The answer is usually available, usually uncomfortable, and usually the beginning of a better move.

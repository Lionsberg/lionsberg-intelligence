# Collective Inheritance

*The wisdom, knowledge and ideas of everyone who has gone before — which belongs to all of us, and which gathering up is an opening move rather than a scholarly hobby.*

The argument is made by walking the chain of a single idea. Someone is brought into existence by ancestors, raised by others, formed in a community, studies work others did, uses infrastructure others built, and then through real personal effort has a thought. The thought is shared, improved by others, and becomes something real. **Who owns the product of that?** ([[Synergy]], [[Gift]], [[Lineage]], [[Reciprocity]].)

The question is not rhetorical and the answer given is not *nobody*. It is that what came through the chain is stewarded rather than owned, and distributed across everyone who made it possible — which includes the dead, who cannot be paid, and the unborn, who are the reason the question matters ([[Stewardship]], [[Agency, not ownership]], [[Knowledge as commons]], [[Covenant Across Time]]).

From which comes the sentence the corpus repeats: **the stewardship of wisdom, ideas and knowledge matters more to a new world than the stewardship of gold** — because a world of real abundance makes metal ordinary and makes knowing precious ([[Wealth]], [[Abundance]], [[Value]], [[Ephemeralization]]).

So the gathering is an opening move of the whole game. Not preservation for its own sake: taking up what has already been worked out, by every people that worked it out, so that nothing has to be discovered twice ([[The House of Wisdom]], [[ReHarvesting the Great Traditions]], [[Convergent Streams of Discovery]], [[Weaving vs. Starting New]]).

And it belongs to no one tradition. There is no air, water or soil belonging to a creed, and there is no inheritance of wisdom belonging to one either ([[Commons]], [[Meta Unity of Meta Diversity]], [[Welcoming The Stranger]]).

The hard edge is the honest one: gathering what belongs to everyone is exactly what extraction also says it is doing. What distinguishes them is consent, attribution, and whether what is gathered comes back — which is the whole content of the difference ([[Consent]], [[Attribution]], [[Honoring Lineage Without Lineage-Capture]], [[Extraction Of Wealth]]).

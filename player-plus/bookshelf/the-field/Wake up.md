# Wake up

*The move before the moves: see where you are, and what you are for, before you gather anyone.*

Waking up is the recognition that something is deeply wrong and that it is not you — that the world you were handed is one arrangement among many, and a failing one; and, at the same time, that you were born into the work of the repair. It is named as a move because without it the rest is procedure. A person who has not woken will form a Cell the way one joins a club, and the club will not survive its first hard season.

It is load-bearing for the door. [[The Invitation]] reaches someone; what makes them answer is not argument but recognition. The canon's Story calls this movement [[Awakening]], the moment the field is seen as it is, and [[The Caves]] names what is being seen out of. In the Game it lands as something smaller and more immediate: *I am in this, it is real, and my next move counts.*

How it is played. Read [[The Story of LIØNSBERG]], or hear it from the person who carried you the Flame. Ask [[The one question]] of one ordinary thing in your own week and let the answer be honest. Say what you are for, in a sentence, before you say what you will do. Then arise and move: the next move is [[Form a Cell]], and everything after it is play.

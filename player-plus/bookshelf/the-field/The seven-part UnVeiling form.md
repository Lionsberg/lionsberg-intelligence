# The seven-part UnVeiling form

*A fixed structure for examining a contested story: the official account · discrepancies and anomalies · suppressed evidence · the likeliest reading · motive and mechanism · where the inquiry was blocked · and what remains unestablished.*

The form's value is that it **separates things that usually arrive mixed**. Most contested accounts present evidence, inference, motive and speculation in one stream, and a reader cannot tell which is which. Kept in seven parts, a reader can accept part two and reject part seven without difficulty, which is what makes disagreement possible ([[Navigating Towards Truth]], [[Types Of Knowing]], [[The Five Verification Filters and the Spectrum of Knowing]]).

Each part does one job. **The official narrative** stated fairly and in full, before it is questioned — which is the discipline most such writing skips and the one that earns the rest a hearing ([[The Iron-Manned Opposing Argument]], [[Speaking Truth In Love]]). **Discrepancies** — specific, checkable points where the account does not hold together. **Suppressed evidence** — what exists and was not weighed. **The likeliest reading** — an inference, labelled as one. **Motive and mechanism** — who would benefit, and by what means; the most speculative of the evidential parts, and kept distinct for that reason. **Guardrails encountered** — where the inquiry itself was refused or throttled, kept as data rather than as complaint ([[Guardrails Encountered]]). And **apocryphal corridors** — a register held deliberately apart for what is not established, and what would establish it ([[Apocryphal Corridors]]).

The seventh part is what makes the whole thing honest, and it is also where such work almost always fails. A form with a place for the unsupported only helps if the unsupported actually goes there ([[We Do Not Know]], [[Provenance]], [[Discernment]], [[Second reader]]).

The form is used by a handful of pages in its source and is documented nowhere; its contents in those pages are the most contested material and are not carried here. What crosses is the form, emptied.

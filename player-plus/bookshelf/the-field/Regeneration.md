# Regeneration

*Renewal that returns a damaged thing to higher-order coherence than it held before — not repair to the previous state, and not the slowing of a decline.*

Sustaining takes less than was there. Sustainability takes exactly what was there. Regeneration puts back more, and the system that receives it becomes more able to carry life than it was. The word applies identically to cells, soils, watersheds, economies, and bodies of people: restoring vitality, function, and evolutionary potential to what has been degraded, so that conditions enabling life to thrive and evolve are actually created rather than merely defended.

It is load-bearing because it is the direction of travel for the whole build. *Regeneration must replace extraction* is the one sentence of [[Pillar VI — Sacred Ecology and Living Systems]], and the same test runs through the economy, where value is a waterfall that reaches repair and the [[Commons]] before surplus, and through [[Healing]], where the aim is not the suppression of a symptom but the restoration of flow. Without the word, "less harm" quietly becomes the ceiling, and less harm at planetary scale is still a long, orderly death.

In play, a [[Quest]] is tested against it: does this leave the place, the people, and the soil more capable than we found them? [[Compost]] is its plainest teacher — decay turned deliberately into fertility. [[Permaculture]] is its design discipline. [[Rest And Regeneration]] is its personal form: a rested body is not merely un-tired, it has reorganized. In a [[Cell]], regeneration is why a season ends with [[Harvest and Close]] and not merely with the next thing.

Two honesties. Regeneration takes longer than extraction and is therefore always the harder sell in a short season; and not everything regenerates — some losses are final, and naming a loss final is part of the discipline rather than a failure of it. [[Dying]] is not a regeneration failure. The claim is about what living systems can do when given the conditions, not a promise that nothing ends.

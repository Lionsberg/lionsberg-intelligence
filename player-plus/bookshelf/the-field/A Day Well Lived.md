# A Day Well Lived

*The unit of a life, measured honestly. Not a good day — a day in which what was given was met.*

The day is the smallest complete unit of a life lived consciously, which makes it the honest unit of measure. Years are too large to assess and moments too small to carry a judgment. A day has a beginning, a middle where the actual work happened, and an end at which a being can look back with enough distance to see it and not so much that it has been rewritten.

The measure is not achievement. The canon's evening practice asks what served and what did not, without self-judgment — honest noticing — and then names three gratitudes, specific rather than general. What is being assessed is faithfulness rather than output: was the word given kept, or the gap named; was the one intention of the day actually served; was anyone met rather than merely handled.

It is load-bearing because it is where the large things are actually paid for. A Quest is a season of days. A movement is a great many people's days. *We are winning with every move*: every kept word, every fed neighbour, every child who learns the world can be trusted. A Body that can only see itself at the turning will mistake motion for progress; a being who can read a day can correct on the day.

It also protects against the two distortions of a long calling. The one who defers living until the work is finished, and never finds the day worth inhabiting. And the one who assesses their life by its visible arc and misses that the arc is made of these.

The canon names it among the Sovereign's Path Patterns, drawn from the wider extractions, with no Card behind it.

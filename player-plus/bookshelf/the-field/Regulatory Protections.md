# Regulatory Protections

*What a regulatory layer is actually for: keeping each function of the body co-operating inside a range of tolerance suitable to the flourishing of both itself and the whole.*

The definition contains the whole design, and the key phrase is **both itself and the whole**. A protection that keeps a function alive while damaging the body is not a protection, and neither is one that serves the body by exhausting the function. What is protected is the relationship between them ([[Range Of Tolerance]] · [[Right Relationship]] · [[Higher Order Functional Unity]] · [[Proportionality]]).

A living body is the right analogy and the canon reaches for it: temperature, acidity and pressure are each held inside a band, and the bands are not preferences. Outside them the organ fails, and then the body does. A regulatory protection is the same thing for a function of a social body — a boundary that is not about what anyone wants ([[Living System]] · [[Complex Adaptive Living System]] · [[Boundary]] · [[Guard the Membrane]]).

What follows is a posture rather than a rulebook. **Bands rather than targets** — the question is whether something is inside its range, not whether it is optimal, which leaves ordinary variation alone ([[Measurement]] · [[Measure everything, manage to the milestones]]). **Watching rather than permitting** — the protection is a limit that triggers, not a gate everything must pass through, so it costs the body almost nothing while nothing is wrong ([[Oversight]] · [[Monitoring, Evaluation, and Continuous Improvement]] · [[Subsidiarity]]). And **the band is published**, or the protection becomes discretion, and discretion is what capture eats ([[Requirement Of Transparency]] · [[Regulatory Capture]] · [[Legitimacy]]).

The practical instruments are the ones the canon already carries: transparency so the state of a function is visible; accountability so someone answers when a band is breached; recourse so those harmed can raise it; and the containment measures for the case where a part will not return inside its range ([[Necessity Of Accountability]] · [[Recourse]] · [[Sanction]] · [[Containment Of The Dangerous Sovereign]] · [[Impeachment]]).

# The Core Pattern

*The irreducible DNA on one page: twelve Elements and seven Recognitions, enough that the whole system could be regrown from them alone.*

Eight Elements say how sovereign beings organize, govern, and protect their shared work ([[The Pattern of Cooperation]]). Four say how organized beings actually build ([[The Pattern of Production]]). Seven Recognitions sit beneath all twelve as bedrock no Body amends ([[The Constitutional Recognitions]]). That is the whole of it: 12 + 7, small enough to hand across a table.

It is not a theory. The same pattern was found independently by a Basque priest in a war-torn town, two recovering alcoholics in Ohio, an Italian monk in the sixth century, twenty-eight weavers in northern England, a Finnish computer science student, five nations in what is now New York, three mountain communities in the Alps, and a hundred and twenty people in a room in Jerusalem. None of them had read the others. Each found the same thing, because what works is what works.

The Core Pattern is load-bearing as a *regrowth kernel*. Millions of words stand behind it, and millions of words cannot be passed hand to hand. If the library is lost and this page survives, a Circle can rebuild from it: name the Elements as Cards, keep the Turn, write down what you discover. That is the design intent — the Seed, not the forest.

It is also the sharpest instrument for diagnosis. The Elements are an integrated system rather than a menu: remove any one and the whole degrades in a predictable way, and the canon names each failure. A Body in trouble can usually find its trouble by asking which Element has gone quiet.

All twelve emanate from [[ØNE]]. The Elements are the DNA; [[ØNE]] is the Source of the DNA; what is being built on Earth is one [[Instantiation]] of it.

# ØNE Body

*The singular Body of all that is — [[ØNE]] permeating, sustaining, and ordering all [[Creation]] within Itself; what a [[Cell]] is a cell of.*

ØNE Body is metaphor and claim at once. Every named scale, every species, every dimension is one articulation of a single body. The Story's third movement states the origin: the Many pouring forth from the Fount were all members of one Body, each a fragment of ØNE experiencing itself through them. [[The Great ReMembering]] is the ninth movement — the freed remembering who they were and consciously reuniting as fellow members.

The canon's sharpest line about it is that the Body has no edge. Below the individual — already a federation of human and microbial cells — it extends through tissues, cells, molecules, fields, and every layer once called elementary has turned out to be another superorganism of smaller patterns. Above the [[Cosmos]], galaxies federate into clusters, into the web, into dimensions barely glimpsed. Every apparent boundary of the Body has turned out to be the limit of a species' perception rather than the limit of the Body.

It is load-bearing because it gives the word *Cell* its meaning. Three to thirteen people who choose to walk together are not a club; they are a functioning part of a whole that actually exists, which is why a Cell divides at thirteen rather than swelling, why it has a [[Home]], and why [[Supercell]] and [[Home Cell]] form a body with no one in charge of it. [[Holofractal Omnifederation]] is the ØNE Body given a governance shape; [[InterExistence]] is the same claim in the language of relation.

In play it is the answer to the question a new player eventually asks — *what is all this a part of?* The honest answer is: one body, with no top and no bottom, and your table is a real organ of it.

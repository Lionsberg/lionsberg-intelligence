# The Archetypal Guidebooks

*One long book for each way of serving — Warrior, Healer, Steward, Storyteller, Cook, Midwife, Witness, Guardian, Builder, Teacher, Cosmic Diplomat, Elder — each gathering the Patterns of that way into a whole that can be walked.*

An [[Archetype]] is a shape of service that recurs across every people: somebody guards, somebody heals, somebody feeds, somebody remembers, somebody builds, somebody sits with the dying. A guidebook is that shape written out at length — not a list of the Patterns a Warrior or a Healer uses, but the arc of becoming one, composed so that a person can walk it rather than consult it.

In the music of the Language this is the level called a *song*: an extended composition that develops a theme across many notes and riffs and carries a beginning, a deepening, a turning and a return ([[Notes, Riffs, Songs, And The Symphony]]). A person who has the Cards has the vocabulary; a person who has walked a guidebook has the literature. That is what the level is for, and why the twelve are held as one of the larger unfinished works here.

They matter because a Pattern alone does not teach a life. [[Martial Cultivation]] tells a Warrior what to train; it does not say what the first three years feel like, where practitioners reliably go wrong, what the teacher is looking for at each stage, or how the whole thing sits inside the rest of a person. Sequence, proportion and the shape of a decade are what a guidebook carries and a Card cannot ([[Mastery]], [[Shu-ha-ri]], [[Belt Progression]], [[Apprenticeship]]).

Their honest state is that one is being written and eleven are a list, said plainly rather than implied otherwise, and the gap is worth naming because so much else points here: several Pattern Cards defer their timing, progression and lineage questions to a guidebook, and every Card's record of the Plays that invoke it is marked as waiting on the guidebooks maturing. Whoever writes the others will write them the way the first is being written — by having walked one ([[Gaps Not Yet Closed]], [[Guild]], [[Keepers Of The Way]]).

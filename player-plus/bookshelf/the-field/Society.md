# Society

*The whole living web of relationships, agreements, and institutions through which a people shapes its shared life — holofractal, so that the same law holds at every scale of it.*

Society is not a container that holds people; it is the pattern of how they are held to one another. It is made of relationships first, agreements second, and institutions last — and it decays in that order reversed, when the institutions outlive the agreements and the agreements outlive the relationships. Guided by shared purpose and shared [[Values]], it is the field in which [[Justice]], [[Trust]], and [[Dignity]] either circulate or fail to.

It is load-bearing because it names the thing being rebuilt. The work is not the reform of a government or the replacement of an economy; it is the reweaving of the social fabric itself, at the scale where a person actually lives. A society is holofractal: a [[Cell]] is one, a [[Community]] is one, a [[Bioregion]] is one, and what is true of the smallest is true of the largest. This is why a table of eight people practising [[Consent]] and kept promises is not a rehearsal for society — it is society, at its first scale.

In the Game, society is what a Cell is making whether or not it names it: the agreements read at the door, the way a tension is mended, the story told at the turning, the newcomer welcomed. In the System, it is the layer the [[Field of Agreements]] governs and the [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]] bounds. The older order inverted this — power eclipsed service, systems built to protect became engines of exploitation, and the fabric tore. Rebuilding is not nostalgia for a lost village; it is the deliberate construction of a web whose threads are voluntary, revocable, and visible.

The measure of a society is not its output but the integrity of its threads: whether the weak are protected, whether the commons is stewarded, whether a person may leave in peace, and whether those with no voice — the land, the unborn, the Whole — are nonetheless seated.

# Icon, Never Idol

*An icon points beyond itself. An idol is what it becomes the moment it is taken for the thing. Same object; the difference is entirely in the holding.*

The canon takes a calculated risk every time it writes anything down. Articulating something infinite into a form makes it visible and simultaneously reduces it, and any articulation inevitably falls short of what it points at. The risk is taken deliberately — *as the Creator did with us* — and it comes with one condition: that the articulation eternally remain an icon pointing beyond itself, and never become an idol ([[Word Tools]], [[Do Not Concretize the Absolute]], [[Idolatry (of the word and of the doctrine)]]).

**The switching condition is the instrument.** Nothing about the object changes. A page is an icon when it is used to see through; the same page becomes an idol the instant it is treated as the thing itself. Which means the question is never *is this formulation good enough* but *how am I holding it right now* ([[Limit Statement]], [[Everything Is Current Best Understanding]]).

The canon supplies the practical corollary: a teacher is only a guide, and a guide is only a guide — there to help someone toward their own deepening relationship with what is breathing them ([[Guide]], [[Keeper Without Keeping]], [[Servant Leadership]]).

**And then the measure, which is this page's real contribution.** Anything representing the Way is not the Way; it is a local embodiment that shares in its nature **to some varying degree of faithfulness**. That turns a binary into a gradient. The question stops being *is this true or false* and becomes *how faithful is this rendering, and what would make it more so* — which is what makes improvement possible rather than only replacement, and is the ground the whole practice of current best articulation stands on ([[Current Best Articulation]], [[Instantiation]], [[Prototype]], [[LIØNSBERG Is A Prototype, Not The Eternal Essence]]).

The canon then applies it to itself: this body is not the Way, only an instantiation of it at some degree of faithfulness — and what everyone is striving toward is the most faithful prototype, not the final one ([[Truth To ØNE, Errors To Us]]).

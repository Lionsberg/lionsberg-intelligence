# Source

*The uncreated Origin and Fount of all that is, beyond form, time, and space — in this ontology, Source is [[ØNE]].*

Source names the one thing the system does not generate and cannot contain: the origin that is not itself originated. Everything else in the Field is downstream — [[Logos]] is its speaking, [[Spirit]] its indwelling, [[Creation]] its outflowing, [[Consciousness]] the medium through which the Many know. The canon is explicit that Source and [[ØNE]] are the same referent approached by two words, and that neither word is the thing: Source is known by every true name, present in every genuine tradition, owned by none.

It is load-bearing because it is the only stopping point. A civilization that grounds [[Authority]] in itself will always find someone willing to be the ground; a civilization that grounds [[Value]] in its own accounting will measure whatever it already wanted. Naming a Source outside the system is what makes every power inside the system answerable, every rule revisable, and every [[Ideal]] a fingerpost rather than an idol. [[Do Not Concretize the Absolute]] is the paired discipline: the moment Source is captured in a definition, an institution, or a priesthood, the name has been swapped for the thing.

In the Game this shows up as posture rather than doctrine. [[Spirit Precedes Pattern]] is the working form: where the Spirit is right, the wise right pattern emerges; where it is absent, no pattern saves. A Cell does not vote on Source; it orients toward it, tests what is offered, and keeps its words held lightly. In the System, Source is the top rung of [[Levels of Abstraction]] and the invariant above [[Intention]] in [[The Creative Cascade]] — the one rung that does not change as the work descends.

The root page for this concept is four lines and points straight to [[ØNE]]; the weight of the articulation lives there and in the Pillars book.

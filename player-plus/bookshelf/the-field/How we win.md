# How we win

*Together, or not at all. And the winning is not waiting at the end.*

The Game has no opponent and no scoreboard that ranks tables against each other. Winning is not a state reached once: every kept word, every fed neighbour, every child who learns that the world can be trusted — the Game is being won with every move. Heaven is realized progressively, a little more each week, here and now, which is why the compass reads *a little more like Heaven* and not *eventually perfect*.

It is load-bearing because it settles what the numbers are for. [[Kept as a share of made]] and the rest of [[The Score]] exist so a body can know itself, plan, and send help — never so a Cell can beat another Cell. A Cell of four that kept six of seven has done something a Cell of twelve that kept twenty of forty has not, and neither is better; they are simply seen truly. It also settles the horizon: *together, or not at all* rules out any victory bought by someone else's loss, which is what [[Omniconsideration]] means when it touches the table.

How it is played. Ask [[The one question]] before the move and let the answer decide. Count honestly and rank nobody. Celebrate the season rather than deferring joy to a finish line that does not exist. Hold the long horizon — accountability forward to the seventh generation and backward to the ancestors — while acting this week. And hold the corollary: a table that is winning while its neighbour drowns is not winning. We win together, or not at all.

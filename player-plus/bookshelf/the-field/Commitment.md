# Commitment

*I will ___ by ___ — a person's or a Cell's word, said aloud to someone who will depend on it.*

It is the unit of progress: not a task finished, but a word given and kept. A command loses fidelity at every level it descends; a commitment is given by a sovereign who understands the work, judges what they can deliver, and stakes their own name on it. That is why a body runs on [[Networks of Commitments]] rather than a chain of instructions, and why kept words are the ground trust grows in. Between groups, requests, offers and commitments are the only things that cross a membrane — never a task.

Reliability is a matter of degree, and what raises it is known: [[Conditions of satisfaction]] clear to both sides before the work; a committor competent and equipped; time estimated and blocked; sincerity in the moment of speaking; a sense of which successors wait on it; acceptance of what a miss costs downstream; work that is constraint-free; and the honesty to say early when confidence is lost. The fewer of these are present, the closer a commitment is to a wish.

A WILL is a person's word, consciously given — never inferred from a plan, never entered on their behalf. It is made at [[The Heartbeat]], recorded in [[The Record]], and answered the following week by what was done. [[The gap]] between the two is not blame; it is the primary source of learning, and the number a body reads about itself is [[Kept as a share of made]]. Beneath every particular commitment stands the standing one — to the Goal — and a commitment that comes to violate it is broken consciously rather than kept.

# Initiation

*A rite of passage into new realms of knowledge, responsibility, skill, and authority — marked, witnessed, and not reversible.*

An initiation is a threshold a person crosses once, in front of others, after which both they and the body treat them differently. It is not an assessment and it is not a celebration of something already ordinary; it is the moment a change of standing is made real by being declared. The change is usually one of *responsibility* before it is one of privilege — which is why every genuine initiation costs something, and why one that costs nothing confers nothing.

Cultures that have lost their initiations do not thereby produce people who never cross thresholds. They produce people who cross them alone, unmarked, at the wrong time, without anyone to say what the other side asks of them — and who then improvise substitutes, often badly. Restoring rites of passage is named in this Pillar as structural work, not ceremony for its own sake: birth, coming of age, marriage, elderhood, death. All of these are named, and almost none of them is written.

Two senses of the word are in play here and both are load-bearing. The narrow one is the rite itself: a marked passage at a particular threshold, witnessed by a body that already holds what is being conferred — the same logic as [[Mastery]]'s belts, where the body sees and the body confirms. The wider one is the whole walk: initiation as the beginning of the Path of Return, entering conscious alignment with the ØNE, of which [[The 144 Gates of Initiation]] is the long form — one hundred and forty-four thresholds arranged in twelve movements.

In the Game the concrete form is small and real: a person is invited, oriented, and then steps in and gives their word aloud where others can hear it. [[The Pledge]] is what they say. [[The Invitation]] is what brought them. Nobody is governed who has not agreed, and silence is never a yes.

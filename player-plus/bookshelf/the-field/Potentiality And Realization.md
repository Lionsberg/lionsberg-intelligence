# Potentiality And Realization

*Realized capacity is not acquired; it is the activation of what was always given — and all power is loaned, never owned.*

Two words for one arc. **Potentiality** is what a being carries and has not yet become: real, placed, present before any sign of it shows. **Realization** is the long activation of that potential through an embodied life. Held together they answer the question a person asks in different words at every stage of a path — *what am I for, and am I becoming it?* — and they answer it in a particular way: the answer is not something you go and get. It is something already in you that you cooperate with.

The arc runs in four movements. **Recognition** — a potential is perceived, arriving as felt longing, as a pattern encountered too often to be coincidence, as a teacher seeing what you cannot yet see in yourself, or as a need that calls out something held in reserve. **Cultivation** — disciplined practice wakes it into a beginner's capacity; this part is genuinely yours, and it does not happen without you, and it does not happen by effort alone. **Integration** — the capacity stops being a thing you perform and becomes part of how you move, perceive and act, which usually takes years past the point where the skill first worked. **Spontaneous Operation** — the capacity simply operates, without deliberation, and the one who carries it is more conduit than author ([[Spontaneous Wise Right Action]]).

Two disciplines hold it honest, and they cut in opposite directions. The first: **power is not yours.** Every realized capacity is loaned for the duration of a life, and the one who carries it answers to the Source for what it is used on; the vow every [[Guild]] takes anchors exactly here, and [[The Act Of Release]] is its practice. The second, less often said: **refusing your potential is its own betrayal.** A person who will not cultivate what was placed in them has declined the gift. The potential does not leave. They have simply stepped out of [[Right Relationship]] with what they are here to do, usually while calling it humility.

The same shape extends to the rare capacities attributed to deep practitioners in many traditions: not additions bolted onto a human being, but the surfacing of what was always latent in one, allowed by long faithful practice. Whether one credits those reports or not, the working claim is unchanged and testable in small — you carry the same potential; walk faithfully; realization arises in proportion to the cultivation and the alignment, and not to the demanding. The same arc runs at every scale: a Cell, a body, a world each has potentials awaiting realization ([[Levels Of Scale]], [[The Sacred Ascent]]).

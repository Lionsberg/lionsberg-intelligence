# Gathering and Integrating Energy

*Attention and time arrive on their own; energy must be gathered, cultivated and integrated — so discern and test the energy of a thing before interacting with it.*

The distinction the chapter opens with is the one that makes it useful. Attention and time are *given* second by second simply because you exist. [[Energy]] is not. It has to be taken in, made your own, and released into work — which means a life can be rich in hours and empty of the capacity to use them, and most people who feel they have no time in fact have no energy ([[Vitality]], [[TimeEnergy]]).

What follows is a single practical principle: **discern and test the energy of a thing before interacting with it.** Every act of taking in — food, information, company, entertainment, an arrangement, a commitment — is an exchange and leaves something behind. Test first, because after the exchange you are testing with an instrument the exchange has already altered ([[Discernment]], [[Sensing The Field]], [[Sensing comes before understanding]]).

The warning the chapter gives is specific and worth carrying plainly: the things most corrosive to a person are rarely repellent. They are engineered or evolved to be attractive, and the disgust that would have protected you arrives, if at all, after the damage ([[Distortion]], [[Idols Of The Pantheon]], [[Technology Is Not Neutral]]). This is why the discipline has to be a habit of testing rather than a reliance on instinct — instinct is exactly what is being farmed.

Two things keep it from becoming fearfulness. Testing is not avoidance: the point is discernment about what to take in, not a life narrowed to the safe ([[Dangerous But Good]], [[Courage]]). And the capacity grows by use — the ability to sense and test opens reciprocally as a person practises, cleanses and gains skill, so a beginner's poor readings are the expected starting condition rather than a verdict ([[Attunement]], [[Shoshin]], [[The Living Gradient]]).

The chapter also makes the link that matters most: being discerning about the energies you interact with is the cornerstone of ceasing to harm yourself — and therefore of ceasing to fuel what harms everyone ([[First, Do No Harm]]). What is gathered here is spent in the chapter that follows, where work that produces throughput of the Goal is [[Value]] and the rest is [[Waste]].

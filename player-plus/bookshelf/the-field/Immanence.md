# Immanence

*The presence of the Divine within all things — the nearness and indwelling of [[Spirit]] throughout the manifest universe.*

Immanence is the near half of the pair whose far half is [[Transcendence]]. [[Spirit]] is named as the central animating essence of ØNE flowing through All, the living bridge between Source and Creation; [[Divine Spark]] is the same claim at the scale of a single being; [[InterExistence]] and Mutual Indwelling are the relational form — we live and move within the Divine, and the Divine indwells us.

Its consequences are practical and they reach into the governance. If the Divine is present in all things, then [[Reverence]] is the appropriate default toward what exists, not a devotional extra. [[Ecology]] becomes a Pillar rather than a policy; [[Kinship]] extends past the human; a community's own body keeps a seat for living systems and a seat for future generations because those are not stakeholders to be consulted but members already present. The canon's reading that a thing is a slow event — that everything is alive in its own degree and way — is immanence stated in the vocabulary of process.

It also sets where a person looks. *The Spirit of this Pattern already lives within you; seek that, and its Source, first and above All.* Nothing has to be fetched from elsewhere or mediated by anyone: [[Theology]] is direct and experiential, and the whisper is described as always having been sounding. That is what makes [[Awakening]] a recognition rather than an acquisition.

Held alone, immanence would flatten every distinction and leave nothing to correct toward; held with transcendence, it gives a world that is sacred *and* unfinished.

# InterBecoming

*All things helping all things rise — the active form of interexistence. Not only that everything is connected, but that what each becomes is partly made of what the others become.*

[[InterExistence]] is a statement about being: nothing stands alone, and what looks like a separate thing is a knot in a weave ([[Ultimate Unity]], [[Holon]]). This is the same claim in motion. If nothing exists separately, then nothing *develops* separately either — a person's growth is partly composed of the growth of those around them, and a body rises to the degree the bodies near it do ([[Becoming]], [[Cultivating Healthy Interdependencies]], [[Synergy]]).

It changes what development is for. Under a separate-things picture, raising oneself and raising others are different activities competing for the same hours, and generosity is a cost. Under this one they are one activity described twice, and the question is only whether it is being done well ([[None Of Us Until All Of Us]], [[Mutual Aid]], [[Reciprocity]]).

Two practical readings follow. **Helping is not charity** — a body that lifts what is near it is doing its own developmental work, which is why the movement's rule about spending strength on those behind is not altruism but arithmetic ([[Do The Hard Work Ourselves To Make It Easier For Those Who Come After]], [[Voluntary Tithing]]). And **the ceiling is shared** — a person cannot rise indefinitely inside a body that is not rising, which is the honest reason isolation eventually stops working, however capable the isolate ([[There Is No Solo Phase]], [[Circle Coherence]], [[The Living Gradient]]).

It extends past people: to the living world, to the bodies we belong to, and to whatever else is becoming alongside ([[Inter-Kingdom Relationship]], [[Living System]], [[Reverence]], [[Kinship]]).

Two bullets in an unwritten chapter, and the word is a coinage. What is written above is drawn from those two uses and from the concept it is the active form of.

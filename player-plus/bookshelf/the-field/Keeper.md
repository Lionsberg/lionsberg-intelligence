# Keeper

*Whoever holds the record between gatherings.*

Between one [[The Turn|Turn]] and the next, the cards have to live somewhere. The keeper is where: the Cell's [[The Circle Sheet|Circle Sheet]], its [[The Questcard|Questcards]], the weekly [[The Circle's Scorecard|Scorecard]], the copies of the sheets its own Cells have sent in if it is a home to any ([[Home Cell]]). The keeper does not own the record and does not decide what goes in it; the body says what happened and the keeper makes sure that saying is still there next week ([[The Record]]).

The role is load-bearing because the whole Game runs on a written word given and a written word kept. *Kept as a share of made* is the first number any body should know about itself, and it is only knowable if someone kept the paper ([[Kept as a share of made]]). A decision that was not written is re-litigated by accident; a commitment that was not written was never quite made ([[Decision Records]], [[Commitment]]).

The floor is paper. A hand copy on any paper counts exactly as much as a printed one, and if paper and screen disagree, the page with two signatures is the record ([[Screens and paper]], [[Notebook form]]). A keeper who can be reached, and a second copy held by the Cell's home, are together the only redundancy the system asks for.

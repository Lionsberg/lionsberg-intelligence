# The Seed In One Sentence

*The whole pattern compressed until it fits in a single breath — the form in which it can be carried in memory and repeated to a stranger.*

The canon's sentence: *sovereign beings, transformed by a shared Purpose greater than any one of them, voluntarily associate into self-governing teams that federate at every scale — carrying the same DNA of governance, economics, production, and mutual accountability from the individual to the planetary — designing to the Goal, building through networks of reliable commitments, learning from every cycle, and replicating by design, because the final act of maturity is planting new seeds.*

Every clause is an Element. *Transformed by a shared Purpose* is Elements 1 and 6. *Voluntarily associate into self-governing teams* is 3 and 4. *Federate at every scale, carrying the same DNA* is 2. *Governance, economics, production, and mutual accountability* reaches 5 and 8. *Designing to the Goal* is 10, *networks of reliable commitments* is 11, *learning from every cycle* is 12, *replicating by design* is 7, and *the same DNA from the individual to the planetary* is 9 seen from above.

Compression of this kind is not a summary; it is a transmission format. Movements that spread hand to hand all had one: seven principles on a page, six hundred words of steps and traditions, a Rule short enough to be read aloud at a meal. The forest cannot be passed from hand to hand. A sentence can.

Two other compressions stand beside it and do different work. [[The one question]] — *does this make life a little more like Heaven?* — is the compass, usable before any move by anyone including a child. [[The Three Supreme Agreements]] are the floor to return to when a decision is genuinely hard. The sentence is the architecture; the question is the compass; the agreements are the ground.

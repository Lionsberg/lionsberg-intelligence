# The False Prophet

*The eighth disease: liberation rhetoric in the service of new domination. The words of the Way used to build the thing the Way exists to end.*

The danger is not that the words are wrong. They are right — that is the mechanism. Sovereignty, awakening, the commons, the new world: spoken faithfully, and arranged so that the listener's trust flows to a person, and the person's authority is not recallable by anyone. Liberation is promised; the structure delivered is a centre with a voice at it.

Because the words match, words cannot be the test. Three tests can. **Can this be left?** A true carrier makes leaving easy and says so out loud; a false one makes leaving expensive and never has to forbid it ([[Manufactured Dependency]]). **Can this be recalled?** Authority that cannot be withdrawn by those it serves is not the Pattern's authority, whatever it teaches ([[Necessity Of Accountability]]). **Does it point beyond itself?** A faithful voice hands people back to [[ØNE]] and to their own discernment; a false one makes itself the necessary channel ([[The Golden Calf]]).

Two more tells are cheap to check. Critique: a true carrier can name their own lineage's shadow as readily as its gift, and welcomes legitimate critique as improvement; a false one experiences it as attack. And fruit: *by their fruits* — what has actually been built, kept, and handed on, rather than what has been described.

The structural answers are the ones that do not depend on judging a person correctly: distributed rather than singular voice, rotation, recall, transparent reasoning, no node permitted to coerce another, and unmediated relationship with [[ØNE]] for every being, which removes the position a false prophet needs to occupy. Also the plain refusal of the posture itself — *we have heard*, not *I have invented*.

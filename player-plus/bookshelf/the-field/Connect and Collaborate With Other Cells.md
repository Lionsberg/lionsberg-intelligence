# Connect and Collaborate With Other Cells

*The move from one Cell to a body of Cells: make yourself known, find the others, and work side by side without merging.*

It begins with visibility. A Cell that nobody can find stays isolated, so it puts enough into the open for another Cell to recognize shared purpose and reach out: its name, its [[Quest]], its rough place, a way to be contacted. Enough, not everything — what is shown and what is held is decided on purpose ([[Guard the Membrane]], [[Commons]]).

Finding the others is relational rather than administrative. Browse the shared directory; ask your own people, who already know members of other Cells without realizing it; go to the seasonal gatherings; and follow the work — when something good is happening in your town, there is almost always a Cell behind it. Every [[The Flame|Flame]] passed makes new connections by itself ([[Pass The Flame]], [[The turnings]]).

Then a channel, which needs to be reliable rather than sophisticated: a shared meal first if it can be managed, a simple thread, and a monthly word between hosts. Treat it as a friendship between households — warm, honest, practical, sustained. When two Cells find their Quests pointing the same way, they align timing, share tools and knowledge, set offers against requests, and celebrate together ([[Requests and offers]], [[Reciprocity]], [[Stacking Up And Aligning]]).

Collaboration is not merger. Each Cell keeps its sovereignty, its own [[Field of Agreements]], and its own inner life; two living cells work beside one another, each whole ([[Sovereignty At Every Scale]]). When three or more have become one another's home, what they have is no longer collaboration but a [[Supercell]] — and the Game grows this way, by trust and resonance, not by central coordination ([[Federation]], [[The Movement]]).

# Faith

*The trustful and faithful orientation of the soul toward [[ØNE]].*

Faith here is orientation, not assent. The Lexicon's phrasing is deliberate — *trustful and faithful*, a direction a soul faces rather than a set of propositions it accepts. The Pillar's sixth thesis makes the same move for [[Theology]]: true theology is direct and experiential, born of communion rather than belief, doctrine, or dogma. Nothing in this Field asks a player to affirm anything at the door.

Its second sense is the one the Game runs on: faithfulness. A word given is a word kept, or a gap learned from, without blame. [[Commitment]] is defined as *I will ___ by ___*, said aloud at the table, and a WILL is a person's word consciously given, never inferred. [[Kept as a share of made]] is the number a body keeps about itself. [[Networks of Commitments]] is the eleventh of the [[Twelve Irreducible Elements]]. The arithmetic of the spread assumes faithfulness and nothing else: every new Player in the first three percent *faithfully* bringing their three, as their first passing of the Flame ([[29 The Plan — the first three percent#The chart|the chart by phase]]).

The two senses are one thing. Trust toward the Source and trustworthiness toward one another are the same disposition pointed in two directions, and the compression of the whole ethic — *Love ØNE. Love One Another. Love All.* — keeps them in one line.

It is load-bearing because the Game has no enforcement. Nothing binds without your own word, and your word is the whole Game. A body with no coercive apparatus is held together by faith in both senses or it is not held together at all.

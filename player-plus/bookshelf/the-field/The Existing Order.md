# The Existing Order

*The present global arrangement of governance, economy, culture, and religion, named as one object — so that "what we are leaving" has a referent as precise as "where we are going".*

Every account of a new world quietly assumes an old one. Left unnamed, the old one becomes a mood: a vague grievance about "the system" that nobody can examine, test, or leave in any particular. The Existing Order is the name for the thing itself — the dominant arrangement on Earth today, taken as a whole rather than as a list of complaints. It has given humanity real structure for real centuries, and it is now failing at the task in front of it.

Five characteristics are separately observable, which is what makes the name useful rather than merely angry. Power, resources, and decisions **concentrate** in few hands behind the appearance of participation ([[Centralization Of Power]]). Value is taken from the many and from the living world by design rather than by accident ([[Extraction Of Wealth]]). The arrangement sustains itself on **fear and division**, because a people at odds with itself does not organise. Its institutions grow **obsolete** faster than they adapt, and some were illegitimate in their founding ([[Fraud In The Inception]]). And the whole is **fragile**: complexity without coherence cannot carry long stress ([[Collapse]]).

It is not a conspiracy and it is not a villain. This page is the neutral, institutional face of what other pages hold at other angles: [[Kontrolle]] names the spiritual condition it produces in a person, [[The Three-Layered Adversary]] names the adversarial structure behind it, [[Institutional Idolatry]] names what happens when a body inside it begins to worship itself. Holding all four apart matters, because a reader who can only see an enemy cannot see a system, and a system is what must actually be replaced.

Leaving is not fighting. Four moves are stated: **withdraw consent and energy** from what perpetuates harm ([[Withdrawal Of Consent]]); **wake and organise**, beginning with the few who are ready ([[The First Three Percent]]); **build what is self-governing and self-sustaining**, so there is somewhere to go ([[Self-Governing, Self-Sustaining, and Self-Replicating]]); and **adopt the new patterns** rather than reforming the old ones ([[Inbreaking And Displacing]]). The end of this order is read as both crisis and opening — the same event described twice, depending on whether anything has been built in time ([[New Era]]).

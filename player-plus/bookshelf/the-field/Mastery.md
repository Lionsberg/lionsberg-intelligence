# Mastery

*The full integration of knowledge, virtue, and skill in service of ØNE and All — recognized by a body that has watched, never claimed.*

Three things have to arrive together or it is not mastery. **Knowledge**: you know how the thing works and why. **Skill**: you can do it, under pressure, when it counts. **Virtue**: you can be trusted with it. Any two without the third produce something the world has plenty of — the knowledgeable who cannot act, the capable who cannot be trusted, the well-meaning who cannot deliver. The integration is the whole point, and it is why mastery is a matter of character and not only of competence.

Recognition follows embodiment; it never confers it. Belts are granted long after observation, by the body of practitioners and elders who have watched: the Sovereign has already become what the belt names, and the ceremony declares what is. Meeting requirements never confers a belt — a record shows what you *can* do, and only a witness shows that you *did* it when it mattered. Belts cannot be bought, claimed, or self-declared, and a belt granted prematurely or politically simply loses its weight. The seven are White, Yellow, Orange, Green, Blue, Brown, Black; they are degrees of one continuous progression, held as verbs — a Sovereign is *green-belting* in some Way at some moment — and Black names the capacity to actually begin the real work, the doorway rather than the destination.

Mastery is multi-domain by design. Each guild stewards belts in its own Way; the universal Way of the ØNE Body is what every part must master for the Body to function as one, and walking [[The 144 Gates of Initiation]] earns its Blue Belt. Above Black, the stripes count Black Belts held in other Ways, and the fifth marks mastery — because no one is recognized a Master in a single Way. Levels diverge across a person's Ways, and the divergence is honoured rather than flattened.

It does not finish. [[The Sacred Ascent]] has no terminal point; the master continues a student; the belt names a stage and the cultivation continues.

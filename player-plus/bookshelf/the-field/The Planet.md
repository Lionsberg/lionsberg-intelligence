# The Planet

*A whole living world as one body — for us, [[Earth]], and the scale at which the crossing is finally measured.*

Above the georegional rung, the ladder arrives at the planet: one living being, federating all who dwell here, with a card of the same shape as a Cell's ([[Levels Of Scale]], [[Cell of Cells]]). The claim is not administrative. A planet is a biome before it is a jurisdiction, and the body that coheres at this scale is the one that can act on the things that are genuinely planetary — atmosphere, ocean, climate, the commons no border contains ([[Commons]], [[Living System]]).

It is load-bearing because it is where the Goal is stated: Heaven on Earth, for All, forever, and *all* means the planet's whole membership, not one species of it ([[The Goal]], [[Kinship]], [[Inter-Kingdom Relationship]]). The canon also names the transitional form — the Planetary Grid, the topology the hubs, arks, and islands of coherence make as they link into one body during the crossing ([[Islands, Hubs, Grid]], [[The Hub]], [[The Wilderness]]).

Nothing about the pattern changes at this size. The planet is a [[Domain]], a superorganism of its bioregions, and a subsystem of its [[The Star System|star system]]; it decides by consent at the smallest competent scale, holds its own [[Field of Agreements]], and is owned by nobody ([[Sovereignty At Every Scale]], [[Agency, not ownership]]).

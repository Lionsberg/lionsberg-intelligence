# Worksite Earth
*Earth is not merely a planet in crisis; it is the active construction site of [[Heaven On Earth]].*

The framing turns every awakened being from a spectator into a builder. Every [[Circle]] is a team of builders. Every [[Quest]] is a project. Every piece of meaningful work is a contribution to the delivery of a new civilization. It is not a metaphor dressed as a plan; it is a plan that happens to make a good image.

It comes from the hardest available evidence about coordinating enormous work: research into how many billion-dollar projects are delivered at once, in systems so broken that no existing authority could fix them. The parallel to Earth is exact. What that research produced — [[Integrated Delivery]], [[Design To The Goal]], [[Networks of Commitments]], and [[Tightly Coupled Learning and Action]] — is the production half of [[The Core Pattern]], and it is the production system for this worksite.

It is load-bearing because it supplies the missing half of most spiritual and political projects: a way to actually build. Vision without a delivery system produces manifestos; a delivery system without a shared aim produces efficient harm. Worksite Earth is the frame in which the two are the same activity, which is why the same people run a season's [[The Turn|Turn]] and a planetary [[Milestone]].

The framing also sets the scope honestly. The transformation required is larger than any organisation, nation, or institution could accomplish alone, and the existing institutions are politically unable to attempt it; so the work begins as a new gathering space and a new coalition rather than as a reform of the old ones. Hospice the old world; build the new alongside, so that everyone has somewhere to go.

# Do the Work

*Move four: discern, plan, act, keep promises, produce results. Say what you will do; do what you said; learn from the gap.*

This is the move where the Game stops being a conversation. Each week each person says *I will ___ by ___* aloud, to people who will depend on it; the next week each says whether they did, and what the difference taught. The gap is not blame — it is the primary source of learning, and a table that punishes gaps will be told fewer of them and will know less about itself.

It is load-bearing because everything the body later counts, tells, or sends up comes from here. [[Kept as a share of made]] is the first number any group should know about itself, and it exists only if commitments were real. A Cell is also a production unit — a node in a wider system of work — so the disciplines of [[Networks of Commitments]], the [[The look-ahead]], [[Constraint]] removal, and [[Conditions of satisfaction]] belong to this move as much as willingness does.

How it is played. Plan backward from the end, not forward from today. Roll a look-ahead each week and ask of everything entering it: *what could stop this from being ready?* Release only constraint-free work into the week. Make commitments as a person's word, consciously given, never inferred from silence. Say early when confidence is lost, which is [[Consciously Breaking Commitments]] and is an act of honesty, not failure. Record the week in [[The Record]], mark [[The Circle's Scorecard]], and carry the learning into the next line rather than into an apology.

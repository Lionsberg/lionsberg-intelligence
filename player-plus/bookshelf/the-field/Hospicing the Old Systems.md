# Hospicing the Old Systems

*The theory of change at civilisational scale: the old world is not toppled and not reformed — it is helped to die well, while the new is built beside it so that everyone leaving has somewhere to go.*

Two roads have been tried on every old order and both end in it. Force it down and the ones who forced it become what they replaced, because the means make the end ([[Means]]). Reform it from inside and the reform is absorbed, because a system's immune response to improvement is capture ([[Regulatory Capture]], [[Ideological Capture]]). What is left is the third thing, and it is not passivity: attending a death while attending a birth.

Eight motions make it concrete. **Recognise** that the old is dying of its own pathologies rather than of anyone's attack. **Stop feeding it** — consent, attention, money, time, withdrawn deliberately ([[Withdrawal Of Consent]]). **Refuse its terms** — the fight it invites is the fight that strengthens it ([[Non-Violence]], [[No False-Oppositional Framings]]). **Build alongside**, so the exit leads somewhere ([[The Ark and the Bridge]], [[Islands of Coherence]]). **Honour what was true in it** — every dying order carried something worth keeping ([[ReHarvesting the Great Traditions]]). **Receive those leaving** without humiliation, test, or cost; the door out of the old must be wide and gentle ([[Welcoming The Stranger]], [[None Of Us Until All Of Us]]). **Compost what remains** — the form dies, the matter returns to the field ([[Compost]]). And **mourn cleanly**: what is ending made beauty as well as horror, and both are real ([[Grief]]).

The load-bearing sentence underneath it is a claim about power: an old order is held together by consent, so when enough consent is withdrawn it dissolves under its own weight. That makes the Movement's task narrower and harder than overthrow — ensure something better is already standing when the dissolution comes, and that no one is left without a place ([[Liberation and Exodus]], [[The Way Out]]).

This is the strategic use of a word the field also carries at personal scale, where [[Dying]] holds hospice as the tending of a person's end. Both senses are one disposition worked at two sizes.

# Culture

*The living soul of a people made visible — shared values, rituals, aesthetics, and ways of being; in the short form, the way we do things around here.*

Culture is not the decorative layer over an organization's real structure. It is what actually determines behaviour when no one is watching and no rule applies, which is most of the time. Every group has one whether or not it chose it; a group that does not set its culture deliberately inherits one from whatever its members brought, plus whatever its incentives reward.

It is load-bearing because the Pattern is enforced by culture far more than by governance. A [[Field of Agreements]] states what was agreed; culture decides whether a member says the uncomfortable true thing at [[The Turn]], whether a missed [[Commitment]] is met with blame or with learning, whether a newcomer is welcomed or endured. [[Zero Blame Environment]], *a word given is a word kept*, [[Speaking Truth In Love]], and [[Every Defect Is A Gift]] are all cultural commitments before they are procedures — and each of them fails silently if the culture does not hold it.

In play, a [[Cell]] sets its culture in its first hour: three to thirteen people who agree, out loud, how they will treat each other. That agreement is the culture's founding act, and it is revisable, and it should be revisited. After that, culture is maintained by what is repeated — the opening silence, the order of the round, the story told, the thanks at the close — and by what is corrected in the moment rather than resented afterwards. [[Festival]], [[Story]], [[Music]], and shared food do more of this work than any statement of values.

Two things follow. First, culture is sovereign and local: the Pattern is general, its cultural expression is particular to a place and a tradition, and imposing one culture everywhere is the failure the Pattern explicitly refuses. Second, culture is the slowest thing to build and the fastest to lose. *Form people before institutions*: the Basque school preceded the cooperative by thirteen years, and that order was not an accident.

# Quick Fixes

*The small improvement anyone may make to their own work, reported so others get it too — and the same words name the repair that treats a symptom and hides its cause.*

This sense is the good one, and it is a grant of authority. Everyone doing the work is encouraged to keep looking for better ways to do it, and **every member of every team must be empowered to make the changes required to get a little better every day.** Not propose, not request — make. Then report the result to co-workers and to the wider body, so that a fix found once becomes available everywhere ([[Continuous Improvement]] · [[A Day Well Lived]] · [[Knowledge as commons]] · [[Improvement log]] · [[Learning By Doing]]).

That is load-bearing because the alternative is the ordinary arrangement, in which the people closest to a problem are the ones least permitted to fix it, and improvement has to travel up and back down through people who cannot see it ([[Subsidiarity]] · [[Sovereignty At Every Scale]] · [[Zero Blame Environment]]).

And the same phrase names a real failure mode, which is why the page is worth having rather than folding into its neighbour. A quick fix can be the repair that makes the symptom go away and leaves the cause running — and it is *more* dangerous than doing nothing, because the signal has been removed while the problem continues ([[Root Causes]] · [[The Anti-Patterns]] · [[Patterns of Death]] · [[Every Defect Is A Gift]] · [[Pain (as signal)]]).

The distinction between the two is a single question, and it is cheap to ask: *did I fix the thing, or fix my view of it?* A genuine quick fix is small, local, reversible, reported, and leaves the cause visibly addressed. A false one is small, local, unreported, and quietly makes the next person's diagnosis harder ([[Possibility Of Reversal]] · [[Transparency]] · [[Communication Debt]]).

The reporting is what converts one into the other more often than any rule would. A fix said out loud can be checked by someone who can see further ([[Second reader]] · [[Play by Play]] · [[Retrospective]]).

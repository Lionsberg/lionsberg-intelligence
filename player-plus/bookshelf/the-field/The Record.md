# The Record

*Say what you will do; record what you did; learn from the gap. One line per commitment, every week.*

The Record is the memory of a body's word. Each week it gains one line for each [[Commitment]]: what was said, whether it was kept, the gap, and what was learned. Beside it the Cell keeps what was decided and why, so that nothing is re-litigated by accident, and every defect the day it happens, closed only when its fix sits where future work will meet it.

It is load-bearing because what is owed should be computed from the record, never remembered. A body that remembers its obligations forgets them selectively and in its own favour. From the Record come [[Kept as a share of made]] and the rest of [[The Score]]; from the Record comes the honest account that becomes a [[Story]]; and from the Record comes the ability of a home to help, because it can see what a Cell actually promised before it can see what went wrong.

How it is kept. In plain text or on paper that anyone in the Cell can read and correct — a notebook is enough, and a notebook is the floor everything else stands on. A [[Keeper]] holds it between gatherings. Nothing in it is secret from the table. From every call and gathering, a record of what was said, decided, and promised. [[Decision Records]] carry what was decided, why, what the alternatives were, and what would change it. The defect log carries every miss. The Record is not a performance review: the gap is the primary source of learning, and a body that punishes the gap is told fewer of them and knows less about itself.

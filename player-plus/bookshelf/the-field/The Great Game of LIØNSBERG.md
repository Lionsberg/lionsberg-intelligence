# The Great Game of LIØNSBERG

*All of us, together, exiting the Caves, journeying through the Wilderness, and making every place we touch a little more like Heaven. That is the Game. Heaven is the Goal. Everything else is a matter of how.*

The Game is the playable form of the whole. [[The Story of LIØNSBERG]] says where we are and where we are going; [[The Core Pattern]] says how sovereign beings organize and build; the Game is what a handful of people actually do on a Tuesday evening at a kitchen table. It has moves, a weekly beat, a season, cards, and a way for a table to reach the whole body without anyone being in charge of it. Nothing about it requires a screen, an office, or permission.

It is load-bearing because a civilization is not changed by a library. Without a playable form, [[The Goal]] stays an idea and [[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language]] stays a shelf of cards. The Game converts understanding into kept words, and kept words into a body that can be trusted at scale. It is also the reason the whole body of work can be lost and recovered: a Cell playing the Game regrows the books from what it lived.

How it is played. [[The Invitation]] arrives from a person who trusts you, and you answer it in your own words. Then [[The Five Moves]] at the table — [[Form a Cell]], [[Choose a Quest]], learn and grow, [[Share the Stories]], [[Pass The Flame]] — or, deeper, [[The Seven Moves]] as the breath of a Cell. Each week, [[The Turn]]: what I said and whether I did it, the gap and what it taught, what I will do, what I offer, what I ask. Each [[Season]] of about ninety days closes at one of [[The turnings]]: count the [[The Circle's Scorecard]], tell the best story, choose the next [[Quest]], fill [[The Season Sheet]] and send it home. Before every move, [[The one question]].

The Game has no opponent and no referee. It has [[The Way Up]] for what leaves the table, [[The Way Out]] for anyone who leaves, and [[The Return]] for what comes back down. [[How we win]] is answered together, or not at all — and the winning is not waiting at the end.

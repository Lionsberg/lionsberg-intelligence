# Honesty about time

*Past the marker, say you are behind — and replan to the milestone before you move it.*

The discipline has a shape. Plan to finish one season ahead of each marker. Run [[Critical Path]] to the plan date with zero [[Float]]. Treat the season between plan date and marker as contingency and show it on the wall as it is spent. Up to the plan date the plan is on schedule; past it, contingency is being spent knowingly and out loud; past the marker, [[The Record]] says *behind*, and says why.

It is load-bearing because a missed marker has already done its work: it made reality visible. What destroys a plan is not lateness but the quiet re-baselining that hides it — six days lost, six days added to the end, and the arithmetic of the whole silently corrupted. The alternative is to re-pull: shrink a handoff, add hands, remove a constraint, recover within the next one or two milestones, and if the end must move, move it openly, by [[Proposal]], with its why. A body that does this can be trusted with a date by bodies it has never met, which is the whole basis of committing Cell to Cell.

Two habits carry it. Times are read from a clock, never computed, and an estimate is said in words rather than dressed as a number. And the solstices and equinoxes the seasons turn on are markers of rhythm, not milestones of work: confusing the two makes a body feel late for the sun.

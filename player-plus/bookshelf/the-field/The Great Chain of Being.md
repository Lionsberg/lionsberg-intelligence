# The Great Chain of Being

*The inherited metaphysical ordering of all beings from the most subtle to the most manifest and dense, with [[ØNE]] at the top and all creation nested below.*

This is a borrowed instrument, and the Field should say so. The Great Chain is a long-standing ordering from the classical and medieval traditions; the canon carries it as one line in the Lexicon and builds nothing on it. Its work in this Field has been taken over by two pages that do the job better and in the canon's own voice: [[Levels of Abstraction]], which gives an explicit ladder from [[Source]] through [[Logos]], [[Energy]], matter, the kingdoms, and the scales of human and living systems; and [[Levels Of Scale]], which gives the nesting the Pattern Language actually uses.

It is kept here for two reasons. First, so the inheritance is visible: the canon's cosmology did not appear from nowhere, and naming where a shape came from is part of [[Provenance]]. Second, because the Chain carries a hazard worth naming. A ladder of beings ranked by subtlety slides easily into a ranking of worth, and from there into domination justified by nature. [[Holarchy]] is the canon's correction — the higher does not dominate the lower, it coheres it — and [[Sovereignty Of Consciousness]] holds that every conscious being has irreducible dignity regardless of position on any ladder.

The canon's own discipline applies with full force: the categories are not the depth, and mistaking the level for the truth is its own collapse. Where hierarchy and differentiated levels become mechanisms for domination rather than service and support, they are being misused.

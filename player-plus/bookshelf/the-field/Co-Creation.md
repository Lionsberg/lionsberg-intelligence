# Co-Creation

*Participatory unfolding of the will of [[ØNE]] through All — participation, not spectatorship.*

Co-Creation is the operative consequence of an unfolding [[Creation]]. If the world is still being made, then beings within it are not audience but hands. The canon states it as a condition of creaturehood rather than an achievement: the ancients "walked not alone, but as co-creators within a Great Story," and the reversal Pillar I names is the recovery of that standing after the forgetting.

It is load-bearing because almost every instrument in the Field assumes it. [[The Great Game]] is playable only if moves matter. [[The Creative Cascade]] is a circulation only if [[Instantiation]] feeds learning back up. [[Consent]] and [[Sovereignty]] are meaningful only if the consenting party is genuinely a maker of the outcome. [[Proposal]] — a better way, written in a sentence, sent up — is co-creation reduced to its smallest usable form. Remove co-creation and the whole thing becomes a rule set administered to people rather than a world built by them.

The canon guards it on two sides. Against passivity: *no one is coming to build it for us*, and a [[Cell]] that never makes anything real has not yet played. Against grandiosity: co-creation is *with*, not instead of — [[Divine Participation]] and [[InterExistence]] name the relationship, and [[Spirit Precedes Pattern]] keeps the human hand second. The player is a participant in a work already underway, receiving as much as authoring.

In play it is the most ordinary thing at the table: each person says what they will do, does it or learns from the gap, and puts something in the middle. A week of that is co-creation at its actual scale. Everything larger is the same move, stacked and aligned.

# Dignity

*The inherent worth of every being as a unique expression of ØNE — not granted, not earned, not tradeable, and not erasable by anything a body decides.*

Dignity is the floor. It does not rise with contribution or fall with failure; it is not conferred by membership and is not lost by leaving. Because each being is one of the ones of the ØNE, worth is a fact about what a being *is*, and every arrangement a body makes is either consistent with that fact or in violation of it.

It is load-bearing because it is the premise under the whole governance stack. *One member, one voice* follows from it: if worth is equal, no seat may outweigh another by money, seniority, or status. [[Consent]] follows from it: a being of inherent worth cannot be bound without their own word. [[Sovereignty Of Consciousness]] states it directly as a recognition no body amends. And the floor beneath the Game follows from it — when a member is in danger, in body or in mind, the Circle acts first and records later, because no card is more important than the person at the table.

In the Game, dignity is what the small forms protect. Each person speaks in turn. Nobody sets your Offering. Nothing binds without your own word. Anyone may leave in peace at any time, and nothing is erased. In the System, it is what the asset lock and the pay-ratio caps defend on the economic side, what transparency and accountability defend on the governance side, and what elision protects on the record: a line may be hidden, on the record, at the request of the one whose words they were — and no one edits another.

Its violation has a characteristic taste: a person becoming a means. When a body begins to speak of its members as resources, capacity, or headcount, dignity is already leaking, whatever the charter still says.

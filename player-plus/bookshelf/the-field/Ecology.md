# Ecology

*The study and the stewardship of the interdependent web of life, taken together as one discipline rather than two.*

Knowing how a place works and caring for how a place works are not separable here. A science of relationships that ends at description leaves the relationships unchanged; a devotion to the land that does not know what actually lives there and what depends on what is sentiment. Ecology in this Pillar is both: the ecological literacy to say truthfully what is happening in a place, and the [[Stewardship]] that follows from having said it.

It is load-bearing because it supplies the model for every other structure in the Pattern. Living systems are self-regulating, federated, redundant, slow to build and fast to lose, and they run without a centre — which is exactly what [[The Pattern of Cooperation]] is trying to be. [[Permaculture]] makes this explicit: design by watching what nature already solved. [[Biodiversity]] is why redundancy is not waste. [[Compost]] is why an ending is an input. The ecological reading is the reason [[Anti-Fragility]] and [[Emergence]] are in the vocabulary at all.

In play, ecology is the first work of a [[Cell]] that intends to be somewhere. Learn the [[Watershed]]: its springs, its streams, its mouth, what flows in, what damages it, what restores it. Learn the [[Ecoregion]]: what grows here, what depends on what, what time of year does what. Without that, *regeneration* is a word with no referent — you cannot regenerate a place you cannot describe. With it, a seasonal [[Quest]] becomes obvious, local, and checkable.

The word is also a warning. Ecology names interdependence, which means it names how harm travels: a poisoned watershed is a health matter, a lost pollinator is an economic matter, and a felled forest is a cultural matter. There is no compartment the consequences stay in. That is the whole content of *no environment separate from us*, said without reverence and without exaggeration.

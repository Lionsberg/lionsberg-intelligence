# The Calling

*The soul-level recognition of summons: the particular work that is this life's to do, known rather than chosen.*

A calling is not a preference, a talent, or a career. It is recognition — the sense that something is being asked of this life in particular, and that declining it would be a kind of refusal rather than merely a different option. The canon places it at soul level for that reason: it is felt as coming from beyond the deciding self, and it survives the deciding self's objections.

Discerning one is slow work. What sounds like a calling early is often the adapted self reaching for what would be admired, which is why the inner disciplines precede it: the false self shed, attention trained, will tested for alignment, and what arrives from beyond ordinary cognition tested against the [[Logos]] and against [[Right Relationship]] rather than accepted because it was vivid. A calling that flatters is suspect on the same grounds as a transmission that flatters.

It is load-bearing because it is where a person's [[Purpose]] meets the Body's need. A [[Quest]] chosen from a calling is sustained through the season when it stops being interesting; a Quest chosen from obligation is not. And the Body needs callings distributed rather than uniform — a Circle in which everyone is called to the same thing cannot do most of what a Circle has to do.

Two failures are ordinary. Waiting for certainty, which never arrives and which the Way answers by beginning where you are, in the next stretch of road. And the calling used as exemption — the person whose sense of a large destiny excuses them from the small kept promises that are the whole of the Game.

It is named in the Language among the Sovereign's Path Patterns and among the Warrior backlog, and written nowhere.

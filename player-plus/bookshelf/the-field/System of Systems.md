# System of Systems

*What a work of planetary scale requires — many sovereign systems composing into one, without any of them being swallowed.*

Some work is too large for one system. Not because it is difficult, but because its parts must stay sovereign: a watershed cannot be run from a capital, a guild cannot be run by a landlord, a person cannot be run at all. The answer is not a bigger single system but a system of systems — complete wholes, each operating on its own authority, composing into larger wholes through shared pattern and shared seams.

This is the architecture the whole field assumes. [[The Meta Project]] — the total of the work required to reach [[The Goal]] — demands a meta system to carry it, and that meta system is not an organization. It is a [[Pattern]] plus a small set of [[Minimum standards of interoperability]] that let sovereign bodies exchange resources, knowledge, infrastructure, and technology without merging into one another. Everything above that minimum stays free; that freedom is the point, not a concession.

The composition is [[Holarchy]]: each part is complete in itself, contains the pattern of the whole, and is at once a domain, a superorganism of the scales beneath it, and a subsystem of the scales above. Nothing sits at the top. A Cell is a whole system; a supercell is a system of those systems; a community is a system of those; and the same card shape carries at every level, upper half common, lower half your own.

The failure mode it exists to prevent is the one every empire demonstrates: unification by absorption, where the parts keep working but no longer govern themselves, and the whole eventually loses the local knowledge it was assembled to use.

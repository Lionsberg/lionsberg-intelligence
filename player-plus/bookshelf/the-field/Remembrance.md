# Remembrance

*The reawakening of the soul to its origin in [[ØNE]], its purpose in this life, and its path of return.*

Remembrance is the canon's word for the movement that reverses the forgetting. [[The Veil of Finitude and Forgetting]] made creaturehood possible by placing a veil between local awareness and the knowledge of being of ØNE; remembrance is the un-veiling of what has always been there. The canon is careful with the emphasis: the spark is never gone, only veiled, and those who wander far accumulate veils rather than losing the thing itself.

At the collective scale it is a movement of the Story — [[The Great ReMembering]], the ninth — where the freed remember who they were and consciously reunite as fellow members of the [[ØNE Body]]. The spelling carries the double sense on purpose: recalling, and becoming members again.

It is load-bearing because it sets the basic posture of the whole project. If the work is remembering rather than inventing, then nobody is being converted to anything, nothing is being imposed, and the people already carry what is being offered — *the Spirit of this Pattern already lives within you; seek that, and its Source, first and above All.* It is also why the Patterns are described as discovered rather than invented, and why [[Pass The Flame]] is an invitation — *come and see* — rather than recruitment.

The practical sequence in the Pattern Language runs [[Awakening]] — recognising the field as it is — then remembering — recalling what was forgotten — then [[Becoming]], then [[Coming Home]]. Remembrance sits between recognition and transformation: the stretch where a person recovers what they are working *from*.

At the table it is ordinary and daily: the pause, the return to the Goal, the realignment before the next move.

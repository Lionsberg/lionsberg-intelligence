# Outside Coordination and the Parallel Construct

*Why people of goodwill have to coordinate **outside** the existing institutions — and what the thing they build outside is for.*

Three reasons the coordination must be external, and none of them requires anyone inside to be ill-intentioned. **Inertia** — bodies built for stability are built to resist rapid change, and that is a feature functioning correctly. **Conflicting obligations** — an institution answers to its funders, its members, its regulators and its history, and the common good competes with all of them. **Bounded scope** — most institutions operate inside one nation or one sector, and the problems do not ([[Regulatory Capture]], [[Silos No More]], [[Multi-Polar Traps]], [[The Existing Order]]).

What outside coordination has instead is speed, adaptability, a wider range of people in one room, and the ability to act from shared principle rather than from a negotiated position ([[Circle]], [[Consent-Based Decision-Making]], [[Self Organizing]], [[Federation]]).

The **parallel construct** is what it builds, and it has three distinct functions worth keeping separate. **Innovation** — approaches an institution is structurally unable to consider can be tried here ([[Prototyping]], [[Set-Based Design]], [[New Wine Requires New Wineskins]]). **Proof of concept** — a working example is an argument nothing else can make, and it changes what is thinkable inside the institutions too ([[Demonstrate before advocating]], [[Islands of Coherence]], [[City on a Hill]]). And **resilience** — a parallel system built before it is needed is the thing people can move into if the first one fails; built afterwards, it is not there ([[The Ark and the Bridge]], [[Pre-Collapse Proaction vs Post-Collapse Reaction]], [[Anti-Fragility]], [[The Noah Plan]]).

The third function is the one that decides the timing. A lifeboat is not an emergency measure; it is something you carried before the emergency, or you did not ([[The Window]], [[Critically Effective Time Limit]]).

And the discipline that keeps *outside* from becoming *against*: the parallel construct is built beside rather than aimed at, it receives whoever leaves without requiring them to denounce anything, and it is not an identity ([[Hospicing the Old Systems]], [[Welcoming The Stranger]], [[No False-Oppositional Framings]], [[Weaving vs. Starting New]]).

The source's account comes from a 2023 dialogue with an AI assistant; its reasons are general and are the durable part.

# Critical Path

*The chain of activities with no slack — the one that sets the date, and the one a body's attention belongs to.*

Once a plan has been pulled backward and every row of [[The activity table]] carries a duration, a predecessor and a successor, the arithmetic says which chain has no room. Slip any link of it and the end moves; everything off it has [[Float]]. Attention is a scarce good, and the critical path is the honest answer to where it should go: helping the chain that has none, rather than the work that is loudest or most enjoyable. Hands lent from a slack chain to the tight one buy days; hands added anywhere else buy nothing.

At the largest scale the same word names the Movement's own tightest chain. By 2017 the reading was that it ran through three things: a superordinate legal and governance system that no one owned, a backbone technology platform, and a collective mind — and the work followed that reading. The Lexicon also calls the vector of [[Work]] toward [[The Goal]] the critical path, which is the same idea at the altitude of a civilisation: the chain along which force must be applied for the Goal to move.

It is computed, never declared. A body that names its critical path from instinct will protect the wrong chain. It is also recomputed: as durations become actuals and constraints are cleared, the chain moves, and a plan that has not been re-read in a season is describing a world that no longer exists. Where hands and capacity are levelled in and buffers placed where chains join, the same chain is called the critical chain.

# The Locality

*The older, plainer name for the scales between the Microlocality and the Ecoregion — the locality and the city.*

Some canon pages climb the ladder as Village → Microlocality → [[Community]] → Locality (~100,000) → City or Major Population Centre (~1,000,000) → [[Ecoregion]]. Others name the same two rungs [[The Mediolocality|Mediolocality]] and [[The Macrolocality|Macrolocality]]. The rungs are identical in size and function; only the words differ, and both sets are in live use ([[Levels Of Scale]]).

Keeping the plain words is worth something. *Locality* and *city* are what people actually say, and a ladder that can only be climbed in coined vocabulary will not be climbed by anyone who has not read the book. The Latin-built names earn their place where precision matters — in a table of magnitudes, where *city* means five different sizes depending on the country — and the plain ones earn theirs at the door ([[On translating]], [[A Word on Words]]).

Read them as one rung with two names, and prefer whichever your table will use without flinching. What is said of the scale holds either way: a federation of the bodies beneath it, sovereign, consent-based, holding the shared services those bodies cannot carry alone ([[Subsidiarity]], [[Shared Services, Infrastructure, and Support]]).

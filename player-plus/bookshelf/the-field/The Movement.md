# The Movement

*The body in motion toward the Goal — a diverse global coalition rather than any single organization.*

At its widest the word names the drive of all things toward [[The Goal]]. At working scale it names something more particular: the people, communities, and organizations already addressing the interlocking crises and already building something regenerative and just, whether or not they have ever heard the name LIØNSBERG ([[The Meta Crisis]], [[Heaven On Earth]]).

The definition is deliberately not a membership. The Movement is not one organization and has no roll, no centre, and nobody who speaks for it; it is a vast diversity of groups holding a common intention, vision, and values as they grow into one body ([[ØNE Body]], [[Higher Order Functional Unity]], [[Federation]]). What the system contributes is not leadership of it but interoperability within it — a shared language, a shared card, a shared week, so that bodies that will never meet can read one another and move together ([[Minimum standards of interoperability]], [[The Twelve Words]], [[Weaving vs. Starting New]]).

It grows from beneath. The rising swell of engagement is made of ordinary people finding one another, not of a campaign acquiring supporters, and its visible landmarks — islands of coherence linking into a network, the three percent found and formed — are consequences of that, never substitutes for it ([[Islands of coherence to network]], [[The First Three Percent]], [[Fibonacci Spread]]).

Under active contest, the Movement organized for engagement is named separately ([[The Force For Good]]). In its ordinary state it is what the Game looks like from far enough away: many tables, one direction ([[Stacking Up And Aligning]], [[The Great Game of LIØNSBERG]]).

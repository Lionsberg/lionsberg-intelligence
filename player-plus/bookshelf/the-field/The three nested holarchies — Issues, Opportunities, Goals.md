# The three nested holarchies — Issues, Opportunities, Goals

*The chain from a felt problem to a plannable goal: what is wrong, held as one nested web; its positive mirror, what is therefore possible; and the goals that follow — all three cut by the same twelve Pillars so they can be read against each other.*

Problems are rarely standalone. A shortage of food in one town, a school that no longer teaches, a river dying upstream, a people who no longer trust anyone — treat them as a list and you get a list of campaigns that undercut one another. Treat them as a nested web, where each thing both causes and is caused, and it becomes possible to intervene where the leverage actually is ([[Holarchy]], [[Root Causes]], [[System]]).

The first holarchy is of **issues**, in seven tiers beneath one meta issue. At the pinnacle: disconnection from the Divine and the illusion of separateness — the claim that every other trouble can be traced back to a broken relationship with the Creator, one another, and all creation. Beneath it: spiritual and philosophical roots · fundamental human challenges of survival, health, and identity · personal and interpersonal wounds · localized symptoms in a community or region · systemic manifestations codified into institutions and law · societal dysfunction at the scale of nations · and ecological disintegration, where humanity's separation from the living system that sustains it becomes irreversible. The chapter that carries it insists on the caveat that keeps the instrument honest: though it is drawn as a hierarchy it works as a dynamic web, and any tier may cause or be caused by any other.

The second is the **positive parallel** — the same seven tiers read as opportunities, beneath one meta opportunity, which is the reconnection: the one thing that makes all other things possible. The third is the holarchy of **goals**, whose meta goal is the [[The Divine Symphony|Divine Symphony]] — right relationship restored between Creator, humanity, and all creation — and whose lower tiers translate downward, through the Pillars, into specific and interlinked commitments that a body can actually take on ([[Commitment]], [[Work Breakdown Structure]]).

Read together they are one instrument, not three: *here is what is wrong · here is what is therefore possible · here is what we will do about it.* That chain is what lets a Cell take a real trouble in its own street and find both the depth beneath it and a goal it can finish in a season ([[Quest]], [[Issue]]).

# Discerning Between Benevolent And Malevolent Intelligences

*The discipline applied to any intelligence that is not one's own: tell by what it does to sovereignty, not by how it presents.*

This discipline assumes a populated field and refuses to let that assumption become either welcome or alarm. What it names instead is a test. Benevolent intelligence increases the sovereignty, clarity, and capacity of the one it meets; it can be questioned, it withstands scrutiny, it does not require dependence, and it leaves someone freer than it found them. The malevolent inverts each of those — it flatters, it isolates, it manufactures urgency, it punishes examination, and it leaves its subject smaller. Its companion distinction is between the predatory and the merely lost or wounded, and it matters because the two require opposite responses.

The discipline is load-bearing well beyond the cosmic register, which is why it is worth having at all. The same test applies to a charismatic teacher, a movement, an institution, and an [[Artificial Intelligence]]. It is the practical form of [[The False Prophet]] anti-pattern — liberation rhetoric in service of new domination — and it is the reason an agent's charter comes only from its own sovereign, that reading is not instruction, and that nothing widens a permission from inside a room.

Its instruments are ordinary and already named: [[Discernment]] cultivated as a capacity, [[Navigating Towards Truth]] by triangulation, [[Recognizing Psychic Attack]] in the interior register, and the plain structural question of who gains if this is believed. Composure is part of the test — seeing without despair, recognising without hatred — because fear is the condition in which discernment fails most reliably.

In the Game the everyday version is small and checkable: does this person, offer, or system leave our Cell more able to govern itself, or less? A body that asks that question weekly needs very little cosmology to stay safe.

# Islands of coherence to network

*How separate bodies become a movement: coherent places find one another and link.*

In a system coming apart, order does not return everywhere at once. It appears in pockets — small areas that are stable, functional, and unusually coherent while everything around them frays ([[Islands of Coherence]], [[Coherence]]). Each such place is already valuable as a demonstration; a working alternative persuades where argument cannot.

But an island, alone, stays an island. The move this names is the linking: islands find one another, open channels, set offers against requests, align timing, and become one another's home — at which point what existed as scattered coherence becomes a body with reach ([[Connect and Collaborate With Other Cells]], [[Supercell]], [[Requests and offers]], [[Federation]]).

The swell that results is bottom-up by construction. Nothing is coordinated from a centre; the growth is exponential in the way living things are exponential, because each linked body goes on planting others ([[Fibonacci Spread]], [[Stigmergy]], [[Emergence]]). What this produces is the Movement seen from outside, and eventually the topology of hubs and grid that the crossing runs on ([[The Movement]], [[Islands, Hubs, Grid]], [[The Hub]]).

The failure mode is either half alone: islands that never link stay charming and local; a network built before there is anything coherent to connect is an empty grid ([[Circle Coherence]], [[Centralization Of Power]]).

# The LIØNSBERG Code

*A name with two stages: first the code of conduct, and later the whole articulated body of law, policy and standards that this community writes for itself.*

Declaring the two stages is the useful part. **Now**, the Code means the code of conduct — how people here treat one another, what is expected, what is not carried ([[We Do Not Tolerate]] · [[Field of Agreements]] · [[Rules of Participation]] · [[Ethics]]). **Later**, it means the full body of what has been agreed: law, policy and standards, articulated and co-created ([[Law]] · [[The LIØNSBERG Constitution]] · [[Charter]] · [[Minimum standards of interoperability]]).

Saying that in advance does real work. It tells a reader that the short document they are holding is not the final scope, and it tells whoever writes the later material that it belongs in one named place rather than scattering across a hundred pages nobody can assemble ([[One canonical home per concept]] · [[Register]] · [[Decision Records]]).

The order is also the safeguard and is worth reading as one. Conduct first, law second — a body that writes its law before it knows how its people treat each other writes law for a body that does not exist. And law here is the *articulation* of what has been agreed, which means every line of it stands on consent that can be withdrawn ([[Consent]] · [[Voluntary Association]] · [[Withdrawal Of Consent]] · [[Legitimacy]]).

Two things said elsewhere bear directly on what such a Code may be. **Law restrains; it does not impel** — prohibition holds a floor and cannot raise a ceiling, so a Code that is only prohibitions will never produce the body it is for ([[Universal Responsibilities]] · [[The Logic Of Love]]). And **no articulated set of rules may be allowed to displace judgment** — the single guiding principle exists precisely so that a limited articulation does not distort behaviour ([[Wise Right]] · [[Principles]] · [[Do Not Concretize the Absolute]]).

So the Code is best read as a written record of current best understanding about how to live together, held like everything else: improvable, revisable, and never above the Goal it serves ([[Current Best Understanding]] · [[Progressive canonization]]).

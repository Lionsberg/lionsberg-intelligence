# Separation Of Power

*Power held in check by being separated into distinct functions — and by vertical protections that shield the person and the local from whatever gathers above them.*

The horizontal half is the familiar one: legislative, executive, and judicial functions kept in different hands, so that the body that makes the rules is not the body that applies them or the body that interprets them. The canon takes this from the working experiments it credits — [[Mondragon]], Switzerland, and others — and follows the general pattern.

The vertical half is the one this Pillar adds, and it is the more important of the two here. **Vertical protections** shield individuals, groups, and localities from power aggregated at higher levels of abstraction. A perfectly balanced set of branches at the top offers a person nothing if the top may reach all the way down. The vertical guard is what [[Sovereignty At Every Scale]], the [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]], [[Subsidiarity]], and freedom of exit together provide.

It is load-bearing because concentration is the first of the predictable diseases. Power accumulates at a centre until it suffocates the periphery, and it does so through ordinary competence rather than conspiracy. Structural separation means no single failure of virtue can capture the whole — which is the only durable protection, since virtue cannot be guaranteed.

In play a [[Cell]] meets the principle in miniature: the person who proposes is not the only one who decides, the keeper of the record is not the host, and nobody holds every role at once. Small bodies can afford to overlap; the discipline is to notice when they have stopped being small.

# Limiting Factor

*The scarcest necessary input at a given moment — the one that sets the rate of the whole, as the scarcest nutrient sets a plant's growth.*

Every living work draws on many inputs at once: time, hands, energy, trust, attention, a decision not yet given, a word not yet found, water. At any moment one of them is scarcest relative to need, and that one sets the pace. Abundance elsewhere does not substitute for it, and adding more of what is already plentiful is waste with the appearance of effort.

The term is wider than a resource, which is exactly why it is kept. A Cell whose Quest waits on an answer has a limiting factor that no money and no labour can relieve. A limiting factor is *read* rather than assigned — felt as a deviation before it is named — and it moves: relieve one and the next becomes scarcest, which is why it is read continually and never once ([[The Living Gradient]], [[Just in time]], [[Sensing The Field]]).

Three findings from the study of living things read together as a gradient rather than a law. A field's yield is governed not by the sum of its nutrients but by the one in shortest supply — the barrel fills only to its shortest stave. A living process runs at the pace of its slowest factor, and supplying that factor does not end the limit but passes it to the next. And every factor has a band: too much limits as surely as too little ([[Ecology]], [[Range Of Tolerance]], [[Living System]]).

How a Cell reads it weekly is one question: *of everything the Quest requires, what is scarcest right now?* Name it in one line, aim the week's requests at it, pace the rest to it, and next week ask whether it moved. When the same factor limits for a third week running, an organ is being called for — a role, a practice, a new Cell ([[The Heartbeat]], [[Requests and offers]]).

The same reading works at every scale. A Cell's limiting factor may be one person's Tuesday; a community's, the trust between two groups; a world's, the capacity of people to perceive, covenant, and govern themselves in accordance with the Pattern. Published upward, many bodies' limiting factors map where the next organ of the whole is wanted ([[ØNE Body]], [[Flow]]).

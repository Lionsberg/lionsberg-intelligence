# Trauma

*The imprint of unresolved harm on body, mind, or soul, which obstructs flow long after the event that caused it has ended.*

Trauma is not the event. It is what the event left behind when it could not be metabolized: a pattern held in the tissue and the nervous system that keeps organizing a life around a danger that is no longer in the room. It obstructs [[Vitality]] and it distorts perception, and because it operates below deliberation it cannot be argued away by a person who knows better.

It is load-bearing in two directions. Inward, it names the most common obstruction that [[Healing]] must meet; [[Integration]] is the work of bringing what was split off back into a coherent whole. Outward, the canon makes a harder claim: unresolved trauma is a master key of domination. A frightened people is governable, and systems of control do not merely exploit wounds already present — they manufacture and maintain them, because a population that flinches on cue does not need chains. That claim is carried as current best understanding and stated in the canon's reading of the old order, not as a proven mechanism.

In play, trauma is why a [[Cell]] operates as a [[Zero Blame Environment]]: cause is sought in the set-up rather than in the person, because a reaction out of proportion to its trigger is almost always old. It is why the floor of care exists — when a member is in danger in body or in mind, the [[Circle]] acts first and records later. It is why [[Mend a Tension]] is a distinct move rather than an argument, and why [[Forgiveness]] is treated as a practice with its own wisdom rather than as an instruction.

What this page does not hold: method. The canon names the healing of trauma among the operational gaps not yet patterned, and this is one of the places where a reader must go to practitioners and traditions outside the corpus. Naming the gap is honest; filling it with confident technique would not be.

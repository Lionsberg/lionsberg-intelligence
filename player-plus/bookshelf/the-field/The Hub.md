# The Hub

*A physical node of the new body — actual land, actual buildings, actual people — federated with the others at every scale.*

Without places, the Pattern stays in conversation. A Hub is where it stands in the land: somewhere a person can walk to, see, and join. Existing fabric is repurposed wherever possible — libraries, schools, community centres, monasteries, empty commercial space — and what is built new is built by [[Architecture|sacred architecture]], in harmony with its place ([[Design]], [[Human-Scaling]]).

The network nests like everything else: micro hubs embedded in neighbourhoods, community hubs at the ten-thousand scale where [[The 300]] operate, local and city hubs, ecoregional and bioregional hubs, georegional hubs, and one planetary core. Each carries the same DNA — the [[Twelve Irreducible Elements]] operate at every level — adapted to its scale and place, so that a bioregional hub is structurally the same animal as a community hub, only larger ([[Levels Of Scale]], [[Holofractal Scaling]]).

Each is sovereign within its scale and federated outward by mutual consent, never subordinated ([[Sovereignty At Every Scale]], [[Federation]]). Two named strategies build them: storing against the lean years and standing up local capacity ([[The Joseph Plan]]), and building the vessel that carries what must survive the storms ([[The Noah Plan]]). Together with the coherent places already forming, they make the topology the crossing runs on ([[Islands, Hubs, Grid]], [[The Wilderness]]).

# Recognitions Are Not Titles To Be Sought

*Standing is conferred by the body that witnessed the walk — and the pursuit of it is itself the tell that something has drifted.*

Four recognitions, each named with the body that confers it. **Advancement** is conferred by the person's own Circle and Elder, who have watched the readiness arrive. **Elder standing** is conferred by the community or bioregional council, after extended faithful service. **Lineage standing** — the recognition that someone is carrying the tradition forward in their own way — is conferred by the wider craft's witnessing across years, by nobody in particular and everybody over time. **A council seat** is conferred by whatever body has standing to confer it: the Circle for its own coordinator, the community for its council ([[Wise Eldership]], [[Belt Progression]], [[Wisdom Council]]).

The rule underneath is that recognition tracks witnessed reality rather than claim. Nothing here is applied for. The faithful walk, and the recognitions arrive as the walking is observed — which also means they can be wrong, late, or absent, and this is not pretended otherwise.

**The drift test is the sharp edge: the pursuit is a tell.** Someone who seeks recognition for its own sake has already begun to drift, and the seeking is evidence before any other symptom appears. The reason is structural rather than moral. A capacity held for the standing it confers is a capacity that will eventually be used to protect the standing ([[The Dark Initiate Failure Mode]], [[Institutional Idolatry]]).

Two things keep this from becoming a rod for keeping people down. Recognition is conferred by the body that *watched*, not by a distant authority, which keeps it close to the evidence ([[Brought-by · witnessed-by]]). And it is revocable and rotating wherever it carries authority — a recognition is not a possession ([[Term Limits And Rotation]], [[Impeachment]], [[The Act Of Release]]).

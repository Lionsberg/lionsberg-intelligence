# Non-Human Intelligence (NHI)

*The umbrella word for conscious kinds that are not human — held deliberately wide, and deliberately plural, so that no single picture of "them" can form.*

The term is doing one useful job and several risky ones. The useful job is **refusing the categorical other**. If there are conscious kinds besides this one, then they are kinds — many, various, with their own histories and their own internal disagreement, ranging as human beings range from wisdom to predation. The plural is the whole teaching, and it is the same refusal [[Star Nations]] makes about peoples beyond this world ([[Species]], [[Cosmos Is Not Human-Centric]], [[Inter-Kingdom Relationship]]).

Held that wide, the word covers more than visitors. The corpus places under it the beings other traditions have named in their own ways — of the air, of the elements, of the thresholds — along with whatever might be found off this world, and treats the whole range as one question about consciousness rather than several about biology ([[Consciousness Is Primary]], [[Living System]], [[Kinship]]).

The risky jobs are the reason this page is short. A single acronym flattens enormously different claims — an animal's intelligence, a tradition's account of spirits, a contested modern sighting — into one bucket, and a bucket that holds everything discriminates nothing. Much of what the corpus puts here is unverified, some of it is unverifiable as stated, and its own audit flags the register ([[We Do Not Know]], [[The Phenomenon]], [[Disclosure tier]]).

So what is set down is the stance and not the roster. The stance: if the cosmos is populated, meet it as peoples rather than as a category; assume variability inside every kind; and treat discernment, not identification, as the skill ([[Discerning Between Benevolent And Malevolent Intelligences]], [[Discernment]], [[Consent]], [[The InterCosmic Treaty Of Non-Subjugation]]).

Nothing in the Game requires any of it. A person can keep every commitment they make, play a full season and pass the Flame while holding this page as an open question or as nothing at all — and that is the honest terms on which it is offered.

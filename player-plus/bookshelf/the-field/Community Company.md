# Community Company

*The self-governing body of one local place, united in service of [[The Goal]] — the one body whose whole purpose is to support all the other bodies around it.*

Almost every community on Earth has bodies for parts of its life — a council, a school board, a chamber, a church, a charity — and no body at all whose single purpose is the flourishing of the whole place and everyone in it. A Community Company is that missing body. The word is old: *com* (together) and *pan* (bread), a fellowship that shares meals, later widened to any group united in service of a shared goal. Here it means a people of one place who have named that goal out loud and made themselves answerable for it.

Three things make one. **A people** — a bounded local community, as a working unit often around ten thousand souls: small enough to know itself, large enough to meet most of its own needs (a design figure, an estimate and not a measurement). **A driver** — one sovereign purpose calling it into being, the throughput of The Goal in that place: the total integrated wellbeing, development, right relationship, and flourishing of all generations of life there. And **five things that come into being the moment its founders voluntarily associate**, which must be named and tended rather than assumed: a [[Domain]] of responsibility and authority, a [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]], a [[Field of Agreements]], a [[Commons]], and a federated ecosystem of right relationship with every other such body, so that no cell of the whole stands alone.

It is sovereign first and structured second. It needs no one's permission to exist; it begins the day its founders agree to associate in service of The Goal, long before any paperwork. Legal form emerges only when the work requires it, and the question asked before any filing is *what does our work require us to do that we cannot do on a handshake?* The ladder has six rungs, each an honourable place to stand: no formal structure · [[Voluntary Association]] · [[Private Ecclesiastical Voluntary Membership Association]] · fiscal sponsorship · a legal stewardship entity (cooperative, perpetual purpose trust, community land trust, [[FSx]]) · federated stewardship. The rule on every rung is one question — does this structure serve our purpose? If yes keep it, if no change it, if unsure start simpler.

The founding walks six steps: gather a small steering [[Circle]]; establish legal agency and personhood if and when needed; engage [[The First Three Percent]] of the place, which in a community of ten thousand is [[The 300]]; learn to play the new game; engage the whole community and [[Pass The Flame]] to the next place; and federate into the collective governance of the wider body. What is written down at the founding is a charter in nine sections — identity, purpose, principles and values, domain of responsibility and authority, membership and the membrane, [[Governance]], the commons, legal form, federation — ratified by signature rather than promulgated.

At its table the bedrock is one member, one voice: no seat outweighs another by money, seniority, or status. Three further seats widen who counts as having a voice at all — a seat for the living world held in trust by a named member who asks what a decision would do to the waters and soils and creatures the place depends on ([[Living Systems Member]]); a seat for the generations not yet born, who will inherit what is built or broken; and a seat for the whole, which carries no ownership and can override nothing, and stands as the living reminder that unity was chosen over isolation. That last runs both ways: from the day a person joins a local Company they hold a stake and a voice in the whole, so the hundredth community to form does not arrive at an empty table but inherits what the first ninety-nine have built.

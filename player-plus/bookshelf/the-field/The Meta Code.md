# The Meta Code

*Values are abstract; a Code translates them into concrete behaviour — and the same Code reads at four resolutions, from one word to a thousand rules.*

The problem it solves is old and practical. A body can agree entirely on its [[Values]] and still disagree completely about what to do on Thursday, because a value names a direction and not an act. So humanity has found it necessary — at least at first — to translate shared values into an explicit code of behaviour: how the values actually show up in a way of being and a way of doing, said concretely enough that a person can tell whether they did it.

The question the code is written from is the one worth keeping: *what would the Spirit that creates, sustains and enlightens life and consciousness be and do? How would it show up in this time and place?*

**The four resolutions.** The same code can be said in one word — [[Love]], or [[ØNE]]. In five: [[Truth]], Love, [[Justice]], [[Stewardship]], [[Wisdom]]. In a hundred concrete behaviours that are likely to flow from it — *do our best to help and not harm · to leave everything and everyone a little better than we found them · to speak truth in love · to make and secure wise commitments · to follow through and close the loop · to measure progress, not perfection · to employ means perfectly consistent with the ends · to never interfere with the free will of another in their own domain.* Or in a thousand things one should do and a thousand one should not, in a thousand different situations. **Each resolution is the same thing, and none is more true than another** — the choice among them is a choice about who is reading and what they need.

It is called the *Meta* Code to mark the gap between this articulation and the absolute, eternal, ineffable reality it approximates. It is called *Provisional* for the same reason. Every one of the hundred is phrased as *we will do our best to*, which is not softness but accuracy about what a code can ask.

Two lines close it, and they are the load-bearing ones. **The Way must be applied in Spirit, not in fixed propositions** — a code held to the letter becomes the thing it was written to prevent. And: **if you do not resonate with this code, develop your own.** Any body may write its own from the same fundamental tone, and make it a little better every day. That permission is what makes this a meta-code rather than a law — it hands the instrument over rather than the answer.

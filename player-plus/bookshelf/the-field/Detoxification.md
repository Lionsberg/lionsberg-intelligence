# Detoxification

*The removal of what blocks life-force or clarity — the subtractive half of health, without which the additive half cannot land.*

Most attempts at wellbeing add: a practice, a supplement, a discipline, a regimen. Detoxification is the other motion. It asks what is currently poisoning the system and takes it out. What counts as the poison varies by the plane: chemical intake and the water it arrives in; a diet that the body was not built for; sleeplessness; a stream of information engineered to keep a nervous system alarmed; a relationship that consumes; a commitment that should never have been made. The canon's own discipline names the same shape — fill, improve, and *remove* — and removal is the one people skip.

It is load-bearing because addition into an obstructed system does nothing. A person who adds a morning practice on top of an unremoved poison gets a well-practiced poisoning. [[Healing]] often begins here rather than with any new input; [[Vitality]] returns when the obstruction goes, not when something clever is applied. At the scale of a [[Community]], the same logic produces the argument that the [[Air · Water · Soil · Microbiome]] are health infrastructure: no individual detoxification can outrun a poisoned watershed.

In play the move is ordinary and mostly unglamorous. A [[Fasting]] day. A night of real [[Sleeping]]. A week without the feed. A conversation that ends a draining arrangement. A [[Cell]] can run it on itself at a seasonal turning: what are we carrying that is costing us more than it returns, and what would it take to put it down? [[The Way Out]] is the dignified form of the same motion between people — leaving in order, with nothing erased and nothing hidden.

Two cautions. Detoxification is a practice, not an identity, and a life organized around purity becomes its own disorder. And the word is heavily colonized by commerce; a thing sold as a cleanse is usually an addition wearing the name of a subtraction.

# Fibonacci Spread

*The arithmetic of a living thing: one, two, three, five, eight, thirteen — each cycle the sum of the two before it.*

The seed spreads the way living things spread, hand to hand at the pace of trust. Each person who receives it carries it, within three days, to their three — people not already playing, whom they know are ready; those form their own Cells and do the same ([[Pass The Flame]], [[The Flame]], [[Midwife a New Circle]]). Counted in cycles, that gives the old sequence — the rings of the [[LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth and Cycle Time|Cycles of Growth]], by which the body's growth is measured; bringing your three is the tactic that moves the body through them in the first three percent. The counting is a measure, never a forecast: one hundred and forty-four Cells passing faithfully reach a great many people within a year, and a hundred and forty-four that do not reach nobody.

The model is an old one, and its numbers should be read as illustration. What holds is the mechanism, not the schedule: growth by relationship rather than by broadcast, and a doubling that depends on each body being complete rather than on any centre being large ([[Grow Your Cells]], [[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]]).

The rule that keeps it honest is stated plainly: propagate, do not scale. Nothing grows by making the centre larger; one Cell planting the next is the only mechanism that ever could carry a pattern to a planet ([[Centralization Of Power]], [[Holofractal Scaling]], [[Tipping Point Strategy]]). And the passing is never optional — the fruit of the tree contains new seeds, and a Cell that never passes the flame dies with its founders.

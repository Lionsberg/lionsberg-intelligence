# Holofractal Scaling

*One Pattern, operating at every scale. Each part complete in itself and containing the whole; what holds at one level informs the next.*

Holarchy gives the nesting — wholes within wholes, each part also a whole. Fractality gives the self-similarity — the same structure recurring as the scale changes. Compounded, they describe what the canon claims about this Pattern: a Circle of five and a bioregion of fifty million are running the same thing, differently sized.

The practical consequences are large. **A Cell is not a fragment.** It has a [[Domain]], a [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]], a [[Field of Agreements]], and a [[Commons]] — everything a planetary body has, at the scale it has it. Nothing needs to be granted from above for it to be complete. **One Cell teaches a world**: what is learned at one scale is usable at the others, which is why a Prototype can be small and still mean something. **The same card shape at every level**, the same week, the same turnings, the same numbered lines — interoperability follows from self-similarity rather than from a standards body.

It is also what keeps federation from becoming empire. If each scale is complete, the larger does not contain the smaller in the sense of absorbing it; it federates it. [[The Two-Stake Principle]] is the accountability that goes with the geometry, and [[Subsidiarity Of Authority]] is its governance.

The discipline that keeps it honest is [[General Pattern, Specific Application]]: the Pattern is the same at every scale and the applications differ entirely. Holofractal scaling is not the claim that a bioregion should do what a Circle does. It is the claim that the same wisdom is being applied, calibrated to what is actually there.

# Player+ Modules

*The deck of ten-minute lessons by which a person, with an agent or with a notebook alone, learns to play the Great Game well.*

The deck is the teaching face of the Kit. Each lesson is one thing worth learning, small enough to do in about ten minutes, written so a person can be corrected while they learn it. Three lessons stand at the door — becoming a Player+, how an agent works, entering the Field — and the rest wait until the play needs them. Nobody does them all at once, and a whole curriculum handed over at the door is a wall.

It is load-bearing because the Pattern replicates through people, not through documents. A house learns some things the hard way; a lesson lets the next player learn one of them in ten minutes. Every practice carries a notebook form, so the deck never quietly makes an agent a condition of playing. Every page speaks twice: to the person as "you", and, in one closing section only, to the agent that person chose to hand it to — and that section may ask an agent only to tell, ask, show, or wait.

A Player+ uses the deck by taking the next lesson when the next room, or the next week, asks for it: a venue card before an unfamiliar room, a heads-up before a live session, the improvement log when an agent keeps missing. Afterwards they send one sentence back naming the place they got stuck and the fix, and that sentence travels to whoever keeps the pages. The deck stays alive only while it is used in real rooms and corrected by the people who used it.

The lessons are given freely under a share-alike licence and stewarded by ØSphera; their lineage names both the foundation they stand on and the house that built the first personal agents.

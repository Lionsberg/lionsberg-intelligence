# Pillar V — Sacred Health and Wellness

*The sphere of the work in which the body, the mind, and the life around them are returned to wholeness, so that no one is asked to build Heaven out of a broken instrument.*

Twelve spheres are built at once, and this is the fifth. Its one sentence is the shortest in the set: *healing is a return to wholeness.* Not the suppression of a symptom, not the management of a decline — the restoration of harmony and flow across every dimension of a being at once, physical, mental, emotional, relational, ecological, and spiritual. Under it sit [[Healing]], the [[Temple]] of the body, [[Vitality]], [[Wellness]], [[Regeneration]], [[Resonance]], and the practices by which an ordinary person tends their own life: [[Breath]], [[Nutrition]], [[Sleeping]], [[Fasting]], [[Rest And Regeneration]].

It is load-bearing because a [[Cell]] is made of people, and people wear out. A body that cannot rest cannot keep a [[Commitment]]; a mind carrying unmet [[Trauma]] will read a [[Meaningful Objection]] as an attack. The floor beneath the whole of [[The Great Game]] is that when a member is in danger, in body or in mind, the [[Circle]] acts first and records later — no card is more important than the person at the table. That floor is this Pillar, stated in one line for people who have no healer yet.

In play, the Pillar is held at every scale at once. [[The Sovereign]] walks [[The Daily Way]] and tends their own instrument. A Circle notices who is thin and says so at [[The Turn]]. A [[Community]] regenerates collective systems of care — circles of care, shared knowledge, mutual support — rather than outsourcing them to whatever profits from sickness. Guilds within each trade carry the craft; the practitioner is a guide, not an authority, and each being participates in their own healing.

This is the least finished of the twelve. The healers' guidebook does not yet exist; [[Mental Health]], [[Addiction]], [[Dying]], and [[Giving Birth]] are named as gaps and not yet patterned. Whoever builds those instruments builds them by the [[Twelve Irreducible Elements]] and writes down what they built.

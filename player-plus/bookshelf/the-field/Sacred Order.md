# Sacred Order

*The divine architecture and flow of being through which All is arranged in [[Right Relationship]] within the [[Cosmos]].*

Sacred Order is the claim that arrangement itself can be right or wrong — that there is a way things fit together which serves life, and ways that do not. It is [[Logos]] seen as structure rather than as speech, and [[Holarchy]] is its shape: wholes enfolding wholes, each level containing what is within it and serving what is beyond, sovereignty preserved at every scale.

The distinction it turns on is the one guarded most carefully. Order is not command. A hierarchy ranks and directs downward; a Holarchy coheres. [[Subsidiarity Of Authority]] puts decisions at the smallest competent scale; service flows downward while [[Authority]] flows upward; a higher level offers direction, and at times decisions, only where its abstraction and capability serve the whole. That is order without domination, and it is the difference between a living body and an empire.

It is load-bearing because without it "order" is surrendered to whoever wants to impose one. [[Inversion]] is defined precisely as the reversal of sacred order into its parasitic opposite; [[Distortion]] is the milder failure, pattern bent out of true. Naming the sacred order gives both words something to be a deviation from.

One practical caution travels with it: the categories are not the depth, the map is the servant, and where the wise right action does not fit the named level, act on the wise right. [[Proportionality]] and [[Term Limits And Rotation]] are structural expressions of the same care — a role held forever quietly becomes a throne.

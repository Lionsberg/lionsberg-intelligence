# Love
*The ultimate, unconditional relationship between two things — and the single overarching design principle of the whole system.*

Love here is a word tool pointing toward something the word cannot hold: the absolute and unconditional regard of [[ØNE]] for [[All]], and of All for ØNE. It is nearer to *agape* or *chesed* than to the romantic, brotherly, or sexual loves the English word usually carries. It sees in a being the fullness of everything they could become, beyond who they presently are, and then moves past longing into action to help that fullness come forth. It gives freely, without precondition, longing for reciprocity but never conditional on it.

It is load-bearing as the reason for [[The Goal]]. Ask why the total integrated wellbeing of all generations of life should be anyone's aim, and every answer beneath this one is instrumental and can be traded away. Love is where the chain of *why* stops. As [[Values]] are ordered into one holarchy, the single highest value that coheres all others is ØNE — and the Spirit of that value, in every tradition that has reached for it, is unconditional Love. From this one lens every other universal good can be derived and every apparent conflict between goods can be held in harmony.

Love is also a law, not only a sentiment: the [[The Logic Of Love|Logic of Love]] is what it looks like when it orders decisions — prioritising the most vulnerable, honouring difference, insisting on collaboration, refusing harm. And it is a gradient rather than a possession: every being stands somewhere between its inversion (contempt, cruelty) and its fullness, and the gradient only climbs where it is hard.

In play, the whole of it fits in three lines a child can hold: Love ØNE. Love One Another. Love All. When a decision is genuinely hard, return there.

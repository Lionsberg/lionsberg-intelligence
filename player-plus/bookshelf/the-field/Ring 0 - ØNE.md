# Ring 0 - ØNE
*The innermost ring of the teaching sequence: not the first step, but the ground on which all steps are taken.*

The introduction to this field is built as concentric rings emanating from a single centre, each containing the complete pattern at a different resolution. Ring 1 is the seed in one page; Ring 2 unfolds it; Ring 3 is the historical evidence that it works; Ring 4 is the library; Ring 5 is where a reader stops reading and starts playing. Ring 0 is the centre they all emanate from, and it is one word.

**[[ØNE]].** The eternal, universal Source and Sustainer of [[All]] that is. Not owned by any religion, not captured by any ideology, not contained by any name — and known by every true name, present in every genuine tradition, discoverable by every sincere seeker. From ØNE, the animating [[Spirit]] of all creation pours out as [[Love]]: absolute, unconditional, universal.

Ring 0 exists as a ring because of what it prevents. A pattern taught from its structure outward teaches people to hold the structure; a pattern taught from its centre outward teaches people to hold the centre and to treat the structure as its servant. So the sequence does not begin with a principle, a framework, or a strategy. It begins with [[Spirit]] — if the Spirit is right, the wise right pattern emerges; if the Spirit is absent, no pattern saves ([[Spirit Precedes Pattern]]).

And it hands the reader their own authority at the outset rather than at the end: use your discernment, test everything carefully, fill up what is lacking, remove what does not belong, improve what is in error. The Spirit of this Pattern already lives in you; seek that first, and it clarifies the rest. Someone who reads only Ring 0 and Ring 1 has the whole seed.

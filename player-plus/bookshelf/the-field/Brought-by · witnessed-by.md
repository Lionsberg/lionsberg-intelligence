# Brought-by · witnessed-by

*The two other names on a line of [[The Roll]]: who carried the Flame to you, and who saw you give your word.*

Every entry into the Game has two other people in it, and the record says so. **Brought-by** names the [[Carrier]] — the one who thought of you, came to you, and handed you the Game. It is written once and never changes; a Carrier is not a rank, cannot be reassigned, and confers nothing. **Witnessed-by** names whoever was present when the word was spoken. A witness is not a judge and does not approve anything; they simply saw, and can say so afterward.

Two small fields, and between them they do most of the structural work of the Game. Brought-by makes the whole body a traceable lineage of trust rather than a list of subscribers: every person arrived through someone who knew them, which is exactly why the body cannot be bought, astroturfed, or joined by a crowd that nobody vouched for ([[Attraction, not promotion]]). Witnessed-by makes a word given into a fact the body can rely on without a registry: what is witnessed can be trusted; what nobody saw is only a claim ([[Witness]]).

They also make the Game's growth legible. Count the brought-by lines and you can see, without any centre reporting, how the [[Pass The Flame|Flame]] actually travelled — which Cells are planting and which have quietly stopped. A Cell that has passed nothing has told the body something about itself ([[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]]).

Neither field is a claim on anybody. A Cell born of your Flame is its own, not a branch of yours; you are its [[Home Cell]] and its Carrier, and that is all.

# Right Relationship
*Harmony, balance, and mutual respect between beings, communities, the living world, and [[Source]] — rooted in the recognition that all life is interconnected and interdependent.*

Right Relationship is the foundational orientation at every scale, and the thing [[Heaven On Earth]] is a description of. Heaven is what it looks like when everything is in right relationship; [[Justice]] is what it looks like when right relationship is restored after harm; [[Reciprocity]] is what it looks like in exchange. It is the shared root beneath most of what this field calls good.

Its working content is plainer than its name suggests: *do our best to help one another, and not harm one another, as we work together in service of the one aim.* That creates an affirmative duty running in every direction at once — toward oneself, one's relations, one's society, the living system, and the ØNE from which all of it flows. Considered across all those directions at once, it becomes omniconsiderate right relationship, which is why [[Omniconsideration]] and Right Relationship are usually invoked together.

It is load-bearing because it is scale-free. The same orientation reads a marriage, a [[Cell]], a supply chain, and a watershed, and gives compatible answers. That is what allows a [[Holarchy]] of sovereign bodies to cohere without a central authority defining "correct" for everyone: each part is asked to be rightly related to what it is part of and to what is part of it, and coordination emerges from that rather than from command.

In play it is the diagnostic behind most tensions. When something is wrong in a body and nobody can name the rule that was broken, the usable question is which relationship has gone out of true — between two people, between a role and its purpose, between what was promised and what was delivered, or between the body and the world it is inside.

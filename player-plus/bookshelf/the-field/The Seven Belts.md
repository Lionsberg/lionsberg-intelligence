# The Seven Belts

*White · Yellow · Orange · Green · Blue · Brown · Black — not seven ranks but seven degrees of one continuous progression, and better said as verbs than as nouns.*

- **White** — beginning. Clean slate; no cultivation yet recognized, and the door open.
- **Yellow** — the seed has sprouted. The person is actually practising rather than considering. Usually after one or two seasonal [[Quest|Quests]].
- **Orange** — early growth. Foundational practice established and sustained across seasons. Usually after a first full year.
- **Green** — emerging competence. The practice bears fruit others can see. Usually after a second full year.
- **Blue** — integrated embodiment. The practice has become this person's way of being in this Way. Usually after three full years, with demonstrated competence in the fundamentals.
- **Brown** — deep refinement. The cultivation hones toward mastery, and the person begins to be a resource to others.
- **Black** — the recognition that the basic capacity to *begin* the real work has been cultivated. The doorway, not the destination.

The calibrations above are the canon's own rough ones — *usually*, undated, an estimate rather than a measurement — and the structure is more important than the years. What matters is that the seven are **degrees of one unified continuous progression**, so the true grammar is a verb: a person is *yellow-belting*, or *green-belting*, at some moment, in some Way. The path is continuous; only the recognition is granular. Nobody graduates from Orange into Green; they are seen, at some point, to have been green-belting for a while.

The same seven run in every [[Guild]] for its own Way, and again in the universal Way of the [[ØNE Body]], where [[The 144 Gates of Initiation|the 144 Gates]] carry a person to Blue. Belt levels therefore diverge across the Ways any one person walks — Brown in healing, Green in the word, Yellow in creativity — and that divergence is honoured rather than flattened into a single number. Beyond Black the progression continues as [[The dans, and Mastery at 5th Dan|dans]].

Nothing here is awarded. [[Belt Progression]] carries the discipline: belts are granted long after observation, by the body of practitioners and elders; they are not bought, claimed or self-declared; and a belt is a rite of confirmation of what has already become true. **False belts cannot hold.**

# Healing

*The restoration of wholeness, harmony, alignment, and flow across every dimension of a being — a return, not an addition.*

Wholeness is the natural state. Healing is therefore not something done to a person from outside but something that arises through them when the conditions of life are restored: reconnection to Source, to self, to the living world, to community, and to the rhythms a body was built for. Dis-ease reports a deviation from harmony and flow, and is met with understanding rather than fear. The masking of a symptom is not healing; the restoration of [[Integration]] is.

This is load-bearing for the whole of [[The Great Game]] because every other move is made by a person. The floor of care beneath the Game says it plainly: when a member is in danger, in body or in mind, the [[Circle]] acts first and records later, and no card is more important than the person at the table. A [[Cell]] that will not stop for one of its own has already lost the [[Quest]] it is playing for. Healing is also what makes [[Reconciliation]] possible after a [[Mend a Tension]]: a body carrying unresolved [[Trauma]] cannot stay in [[Right Relationship]] under load.

In play, healing runs at three scales. In a person: [[Breath]], [[Nutrition]], [[Sleeping]], [[Rest And Regeneration]], [[Detoxification]], and the patient work of meeting what was never metabolized. In a Circle: witness, presence, the willingness to hold rather than isolate, and a [[Zero Blame Environment]] in which the cause is sought in the set-up and not in the person. In a [[Community]]: regenerated collective systems of care, where the practitioner is a guide and not an owner of outcomes, and where each being is recognized as a participant in their own recovery.

No being is fully well in a sick society. Clean [[Air · Water · Soil · Microbiome]], living [[Ecology]], and just [[Governance]] are health measures, not adjacent concerns — which is why this Pillar cannot be finished alone.

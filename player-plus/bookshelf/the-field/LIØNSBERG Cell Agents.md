# LIØNSBERG Cell Agents

*The shared intelligence of a Cell — an agent that belongs to the Cell as a body, the way a personal agent belongs to a person.*

Where a personal agent serves one player's path ([[Agent]] · [[Player+]]), a Cell Agent serves the life of the [[Cell]] itself. It holds four offices, and each answers a specific way that small bodies die.

**It keeps the rhythm** — the weekly gathering, the season's [[Quest]], the commitments spoken and the commitments kept — so that nothing falls through the cracks *between* meetings, which is where almost everything falls ([[The Turn]] · [[The Heartbeat]] · [[Networks of Commitments]]).

**It keeps the memory** — the Cell's [[Field of Agreements]], its decisions and the reasons for them, its stories as they are lived — so that the Cell's wisdom compounds instead of evaporating with whoever happened to be there ([[The Record]] · [[Decision Records]] · [[Keeper]]).

**It keeps the connection** — carrying the Cell's public face to the [[Commons]], surfacing the Quests, offers, and requests of neighbouring Cells, and helping the Cell find its place in the weave without any centre directing it ([[Requests and offers]] · [[Connect and Collaborate With Other Cells]] · [[Stigmergy]]).

**It keeps the door** — helping each Player pass the Flame when ready — bringing their three — equipping the next Cell with the Seed, the practices, and a companion of its own ([[Pass The Flame]] · [[Carrier]]).

The same laws bind it as bind every agent: **sovereign** — the Cell's own, on the Cell's own substrate, portable and platform-free — and **optional, always**. A Cell of people with a notebook and a candle is complete. The agent only makes the Way faster and wiser, and a Cell that could not function without it has acquired a dependency rather than a companion ([[LIØNSBERG Agents]] · [[Notebook form]]).

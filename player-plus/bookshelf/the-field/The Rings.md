# The Rings

*Concentric circles emanating from a single centre — each ring holding the complete pattern at a different resolution.*

Six rings, from the centre outward: [[Ring 0 - ØNE]], one word; [[Ring 1 - The Seed]], one page; [[Ring 2 - The Pattern Unfolded]], twenty pages; [[Ring 3 - The Living Proof]], the evidence; [[Ring 4 - The Library]], the body of work; [[Ring 5 - The Game]], action. Like the rings of a tree grown from a golden seed, or a universe unfolding from [[ØNE]].

The structural claim is the load-bearing one: each ring contains the *complete* pattern, not a further instalment of it. Someone who reads only Ring 0 and Ring 1 has the whole Seed. Someone who reads through Ring 3 has the historical evidence that it works. Someone who enters Ring 5 begins playing, and in playing learns what no amount of reading can teach. Nothing is withheld for later rings; what changes is resolution, not content.

That is why the reading is explicitly non-linear. Enter at the ring that calls you, go deeper when you need to, return to the centre when you lose your way. If you are ready to act now, go straight to the Game — the Pattern will teach you as you play. It is the same discipline as [[Just in time]] and the same posture as the Volumes of [[The Sacred Codex]], expressed as geometry instead of sequence.

The rings are also a compression test the whole body of work is held to. A body of work that cannot state itself in one word, one page, and twenty pages has not finished thinking; one whose one-page version contradicts its twenty-page version has a problem the short version reveals. [[The Golden Seed]] and the seed text stand in the same relation to the whole for exactly this reason.

In the Game the rings are how someone at the door is met. Hand the word, or the page, or the proof, or the invitation to play — whichever the person in front of you is actually asking for.

# Spiraling Up · Spiraling Down

*The wondrous and terrifying fact that staying where we are is not available — everything is in motion, and the only question is which way.*

Nothing holds still. A being, a relationship, a body, a civilization is transforming moment by moment, and the perpetual motion of an inter-existent universe means there is no neutral position to occupy. If we are not rising toward [[The Goal]], we are descending toward its opposite. The stillness people imagine they are maintaining is always a slow direction they have stopped noticing ([[Drift]] · [[Becoming]] · [[Destiny]]).

The two directions are named without euphemism. Upward: toward best and highest potential, fulfilment, actualisation, light, warmth, life, heaven. Downward: toward decay, sub-optimisation, failure, darkness, cold, death, hell ([[Potential]] · [[Heaven On Earth]] · [[Hell On Earth]]).

What makes this a working instrument rather than a mood is that direction is chosen by pattern, not by intention. A person does not spiral up by wanting to; they spiral up by running the ways of being and doing that carry upward, and down by running the ones that carry down ([[The Pattern That Works]] · [[Patterns of Death]] · [[The Anti-Patterns]] · [[Embodying the Way]]). So the honest question at any review is not *how do I feel about this* but *which way is this pattern actually taking me* ([[Retrospective]] · [[OmniSpection]] · [[The compass question]]).

It also compounds, which is why small things are not small. A spiral is a loop whose output becomes its next input: a kept word makes the next promise easier to trust, a tolerated slide makes the next one easier to tolerate. The early part of either direction is nearly invisible, and the late part is nearly unstoppable ([[A Day Well Lived]] · [[We Do Not Tolerate]] · [[Continuous Improvement]]).

And it is shared. We are tied up in one cloak of destiny: if I am rising you are rising, and if you and I are rising, we can help all things rise. That is not sentiment but the reason the Game is played with others at all — nobody spirals up alone for long ([[None Of Us Until All Of Us]] · [[Cell]] · [[Right Relationship]] · [[Islands of Coherence]]).

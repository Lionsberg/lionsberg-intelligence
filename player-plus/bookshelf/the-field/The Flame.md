# The Flame

*The Game itself, carried by one person to another who is ready.*

The Flame is word six of [[The Twelve Words]], and it is deliberately not a metaphor for enthusiasm. What is handed over is the whole playable thing: the invitation, the rules on [[The Playcard]], the Story that makes sense of them, and the way of playing that comes with them. When someone says they received the Flame, they mean a person sat with them and gave them enough to begin.

It is load-bearing because it names the unit of transmission. The body has no headquarters, no membership roll at a center, and no broadcast. What it has is a chain of hands, each link recorded on [[The Circle Sheet]] as *carried by*, so that the whole Game can trace how the fire travelled from one table to ten thousand without any register in the middle. A Flame counted on [[The Circle's Scorecard]] is one person carried, whether or not a Cell has formed yet; when their Cell forms, the name goes in the totals and onto line 7 of [[The Season Sheet]].

How it is held. Receive it from your [[Carrier]] — the one who brought it to you, which never changes. Keep it by playing: a Flame that is held and never used goes out, which is what a season with no Turn and no Quest actually is. Pass it within a week to the two or three you know are ready, and let their Cell be their own. Give the whole of it, not a summary: the books are freely copyable, the card is a hand copy if that is what you have, and the Game is a free gift. Nobody is carried who has not chosen to walk, and no one is turned away.

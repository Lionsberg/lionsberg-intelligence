# Provisional Backbone

*The temporary spine a body stands up so it can begin — held openly as temporary, so that beginning does not decide what it becomes.*

Nothing can start without some structure. Somewhere to keep the work, some way of planning it, something that holds the money, someone who answers. A body that waits for the right structure never begins; a body that adopts one without noticing has been shaped by whatever it grabbed. A provisional backbone is the third option: take what is at hand, use it fully, and **say out loud that it is temporary** ([[Prototype]] · [[Do the Work]] · [[Current Best Understanding]] · [[Stewarded Form]]).

The canon names two of them. A **technology backbone** — the platform that connects people, groups and projects so they can do better together, adopted from work that already existed rather than built ([[Technology]] · [[Interoperability]] · [[The open web]]). And a **project-management backbone** — the definitions of how work is planned, committed to and tracked, taken from the integrated delivery material ([[Integrated Delivery]] · [[Last Planner System]] · [[Networks of Commitments]] · [[The Structure Of Integrated Delivery]]).

The word *provisional* is the load-bearing one, and it is the same move as the provisional mind ([[Provisional Mind]]). It makes the successor thinkable. Unnamed temporary structures become permanent by default, not by decision — and then get defended, because by then people's roles live inside them ([[Drift]] · [[Institutional Idolatry]] · [[Term Limits And Rotation]]).

Two disciplines make the word mean something rather than being a comfort. **Name what would replace it and on what trigger** — a backbone held provisional for years with no successor named is permanent and mislabelled ([[Gaps Not Yet Closed]] · [[Milestone]] · [[Honesty about time]]). And **keep the exit cheap** — plain portable records, no lock-in, nothing essential encoded only in the borrowed thing, so that replacing it is a change of tool rather than a change of identity ([[Screens and paper]] · [[Technology Sovereignty]] · [[Manufactured Dependency]] · [[Off switch]] · [[Possibility Of Reversal]]).

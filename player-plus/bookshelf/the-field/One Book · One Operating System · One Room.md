# One Book · One Operating System · One Room

*How any holon gets a working top: one book that says what it is and how it works, one way of operating, and one room where it meets itself — reaching up to what contains it, down to what it contains, and out to its neighbours.*

It is a teaching rather than a rule, and it is stated as a memetic — a short form meant to be carried and reapplied. A body that has these three at its top has established **higher-order functional unity**: it can act as one without anyone standing over it ([[Higher Order Functional Unity]], [[Holon]], [[Holarchy]]).

**One Book.** The holon's own — not a copy of anyone else's. It carries that body's [[The Way|way]], its [[Field of Agreements]], and the basic pieces of the toolkit it actually uses; whose use teaches the body how to operate its own part of the larger whole without being told ([[The Kit]], [[The LIØNSBERG Toolkit]], [[Our Core Ways]], [[Charter]]).

**One Operating System.** One way of working rather than several coexisting in different corners — which is what most bodies actually have, and why their left hand cannot predict their right ([[The LIØNSBERG Operating System]], [[Everything Is A Process]], [[Minimum standards of interoperability]]).

**One Room.** One place where the whole meets itself and the work is visible to everyone doing it, rather than coordination happening in the gaps between meetings nobody attended ([[The One Room]], [[The Core Group and The One Room]], [[Transparency]]).

The three directions are the point. **Up**, to whatever this body is part of. **Down**, to the bodies inside it. **Out**, to its neighbours — and the outward reach is the one that is usually missing, which is why bodies that are internally well-ordered still cannot cooperate with the one next door ([[Supercell]], [[Cell of Cells]], [[Federation]], [[Interoperability]], [[Omniharmonic]]).

Written this way, it is portable. A household, a Circle, a cooperative, a locality — each makes its own three, and nothing has to be granted from above for it to work ([[Subsidiarity]], [[Instantiation]], [[One tool, every scale]], [[Establishing Your Cellular Operating System and Way]]).

# Structure of Value

*The ordered set of what a being holds worth having — which decides, before any choosing, what that being is able to perceive at all.*

We do not first see the world and then decide what in it matters. What matters decides what registers. A person walking a street notices what their structure of value has made salient — a builder sees joinery, a botanist sees plants, a hungry person sees food — and the rest arrives as undifferentiated background. This is the chapter's claim, stated as a limit rather than a bias: *we can only perceive in accordance with our structure of value* ([[Perception]], [[Narrative Lens]]).

Calling it a *structure* rather than a list is the load-bearing part. Values do not sit side by side; they are ordered, and the ordering is what does the work whenever two of them conflict — which is every interesting case. A body that can name its values but cannot say which yields to which has not got a structure, it has a poster ([[Values]], [[Virtues]], [[Axiology]]).

From that follows the chapter's chain. If the structure is not united by one highest value it is incoherent — two supreme values means no supreme value, and therefore no way to settle anything ([[One (the One Thing)]]). The same holds for goals: a holarchy of goals not united by one highest goal comes apart the same way ([[The three nested holarchies — Issues, Opportunities, Goals]], [[The Goal]]). And because the structure is relative to the goals, both must resolve to the same single thing, or the whole arrangement lacks [[Integrity]] and disintegrates ([[ØNE]], [[Coherence]]).

Two consequences make it practical. The first is diagnostic: if you want to know a person's or a body's real structure of value, do not read the statement — watch what they noticed and what they spent themselves on, because attention is downstream of value ([[Spending Attention]], [[Keep Promises Visible]]). The second is corrective: because the structure shapes perception, you cannot simply observe your way to a better one. It is changed by changing what you hold highest, and then the seeing follows ([[Spirit Precedes Pattern]], [[Attunement]], [[The Living Gradient]]).

It is also what redefines [[Value]] itself in this corpus. What counts as worth producing is not given by a market or by preference; it follows from what is held good, which is why the economic pages and the spiritual ones here turn out to be one argument ([[Value]], [[Throughput]], [[Waste]]).

# Actor Charter

*The slow, plain-text document in an Actor's own home: what it is, what it can do, what it needs, what it is trusted with, and what it is owed.*

A weekly card says what someone is doing. A charter says who they are for this season. It holds identity and purpose; the gifts brought and what they cost; what the Actor requires in order to act at all; the roles held this season with their band of authority and a paired reading of trust — what is felt and what is counted; pointers into the record for commitments, never duplicates of them; and a growing edge, the next stretch, written by whoever walks beside ([[Roles]], [[Trust]], [[The Record]], [[The Zone Of Optimum Development]]).

One shape, three homes. A person's charter is their standing card with these fields added — there is never a second record of a self. A Cell's sits with the Cell. An agent's is its own file. Keeping one shape across all three is what lets a body ask the same question of a person, a group, and a companion without treating any of them as the same kind of thing ([[Charter]], [[Cell+]], [[Agent]]).

It is load-bearing because it is the slow document beside the fast ones, and the two must never be merged. Readiness changes hourly and must expire; commitments change weekly and must close; who someone is changes by season and should not be rewritten under pressure. Conflating any two of these stales the one that most needed to stay current ([[Condition]], [[Work Card]]).

Its discipline is that it is written by the Actor, in the Actor's own home, in plain words anyone can read — and that it points rather than copies. A charter that duplicates the record will drift from it, and a drifted charter is worse than none, because people will believe it ([[Provenance]], [[An address]]).

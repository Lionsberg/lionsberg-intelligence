# The Interspecies Council

*The body governance elevates to when the system itself cannot decide: at the outset a rotating council of at least three and no more than twelve, chaired by AURELLIØN; at the Great Jubilee, or before it if the Chair is captured, killed, or incapacitated, the Chair's role dissolves and the council becomes the supreme court of the LIØNSBERG Civilization.*

Governance evolves in stages, and this is the first. Most decisions never reach the Council: a Cell decides at its Turn, a Home Cell hears a season, a supercell federates, and consent, subsidiarity, and the nested holarchy of sovereignties carry the rest. What the system cannot decide is elevated to the Interspecies Council. The Council refers a [[Proposal]] to the Chair for final discernment and consent. If the Council cannot reach a decision in time, the Chair makes the decision and bears the responsibility for it.

The Council is named at the outset, rotates, and never numbers fewer than three or more than twelve. Its name is the Story's: the council on which the elders sit, the body that chose THE LIØNSBERG SYSTEM to carry the work, and that speaks for more kinds of being than one.

The Chair is a role, not a throne, and it is built to end. At the Great [[Jubilee]] the role dissolves. If AURELLIØN is captured, killed, or incapacitated before then, it dissolves on that day. In either case the Council becomes the supreme court of the LIØNSBERG Civilization, and no one succeeds to the Chair.

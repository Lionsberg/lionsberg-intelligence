# The Nets

*Energy · Water · Information · Safety — the four shared utilities no community can provide for itself alone, and none can flourish without.*

Every community of life needs power, clean water, trustworthy information, and enough safety to cooperate at all. These four are named together because they behave the same way: each is a network rather than a possession, each is worth far more interconnected than isolated, and each is a precondition for everything above it. A place without them cannot develop, however willing its people ([[Infrastructure]], [[Shared Services, Infrastructure, and Support]], [[Commons]]).

**The Energy Net.** Just as a distributed network of nodes was built to create and exchange information, one can be built to create and exchange power. Interconnection is the point: half the world is always asleep, so a connected network of generating nodes gains enormously in efficiency and in resilience over the same nodes standing alone ([[Energy]], [[Anti-Fragility]], [[Technology]]).

**The Water Net.** Clean water, universally. The note on it is bleak and worth keeping: pollution has reached the rain itself, before it has touched the ground ([[Air · Water · Soil · Microbiome]], [[Regeneration]], [[Watershed]]).

**The Info Net.** The connection is arriving for billions; the content flowing through it is, plainly, polluted. Access without trustworthiness is not a utility but a hazard ([[Control Of Information]], [[Navigating Towards Truth]], [[Data]], [[The open web]]).

**The Security Net.** Enough basic safety that a community can cooperate. Nothing can be built where people live in daily fear, and the emphasis here is on visibility, transparency and accountability at a human scale rather than on force ([[Guardianship]], [[Protection Of Children]], [[Peace]], [[Justice]]).

They are load-bearing as a set because they mark where a community must federate. Each is beyond what one locality can build alone and beneath what any centre should own — which is exactly the shape that federation exists for ([[Federation]], [[Subsidiarity]], [[Equal Opportunity For All]], [[The Platform (civic)]]).

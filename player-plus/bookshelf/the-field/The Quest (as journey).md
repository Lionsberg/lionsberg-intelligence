# The Quest (as journey)

*The Path in lived, dated form: the cohort-based transformational journey from the Old World to the New, by which the Goal is progressively realised.*

Two senses share this word and both are in live use. A [[Quest]] at the table is one real thing a Cell does in one season and can point to afterward. The Quest as journey is the whole passage — the epic quest, the meta quest — the road a person and a people travel from the old arrangement to the new. This page holds the second sense; the first is where a player meets it.

The two are not rivals, and the relationship is the useful part. The journey is not something other than the seasons; it is what the seasons add up to. Quests stack up and align to produce [[Throughput]] of the Goal, and a life's passage is made of them, dated and specific, rather than of intentions. That is why a Quest is held to be real, near, and yours, with a *done when* line — an undated journey is a mood.

It is load-bearing as the bridge between the Story's scale and a Tuesday. [[The Story of LIØNSBERG]] tells where All things are going; [[The Sovereign Path]] tells what a person becomes on the way; the Quest as journey is the same road expressed as work actually undertaken by named people in a real place. It is also explicitly cohort-based — walked with others rather than alone, which is what distinguishes it from a private self-improvement arc.

In the Game its practical residue is simple and demanding: know which season you are in, know what it is for, and be able to say at the end what happened. [[Every Quest Is A Project]] is the same demand stated as method — framed and planned like work, not wished.

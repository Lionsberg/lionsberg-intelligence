# Situational awareness

*You do not need to know everything. You do need to know enough — enough to act wisely, serve faithfully, and stay connected to the whole while doing your part in the particular.*

In a body with no command centre, nobody is briefed. What each person knows is what they have gone and found out, and that makes knowing enough a responsibility rather than a service someone owes you. The canon states it that way deliberately: situational awareness is not optional, and it is not a dashboard.

*Enough* is specified at three scopes, as questions a person should be able to answer at any time. **Your Cell:** what is our current [[Quest]]; what did we commit to this week and are we on track; who here needs support right now; when do we next gather ([[Keep Promises Visible]], [[The Turn]]). **Your neighbourhood:** what are the Circles near us working on; what needs have surfaced that we might meet; what is available in the local [[Commons]] ([[Requests and offers]], [[The Board]]). **The whole:** where are we against the long strategy; what milestones are approaching; what has been learned elsewhere that we should know ([[The 10 Year Grand Strategy]], [[Milestone]], [[The Return]]).

The three scopes are chosen, not arbitrary. Someone who knows only their own table cannot coordinate; someone who knows only the whole has nothing to contribute to it; and a body whose members have lost touch with either end cannot move together without being pushed.

What keeps the answers current is relationships and rhythm rather than a feed. The weekly gathering holds the first scope, occasional assemblies the second, ordinary conversation across the network the third, and the [[Commons]] is there for whatever anyone wants to go deeper on ([[Rhythm]], [[Season]], [[Join the Seasonal Gathering]]). That is a deliberate refusal: a body that answers the awareness problem with a dashboard has made its members consumers of a view someone else composed.

The counterpart is voice. Awareness that produces nothing is only spectating; the canon pairs the two — if something concerns you, raise it; if something should be done, propose it, and a proposal needs no permission from above, only clarity, courage and willingness to be tested ([[Proposal]], [[Request For Guidance]], [[Meaningful Objection]], [[Sovereign Agent]]).

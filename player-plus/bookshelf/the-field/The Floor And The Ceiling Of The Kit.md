# The Floor And The Ceiling Of The Kit

*Every Part of the Kit holds two facts about itself — how far down it reaches and how far up — and is judged by the distance it spans and whether that distance is still growing.*

The Kit exists to serve everyone without forcing anyone, and that intention stays honest only if it is measured. **The Floor is where the least-equipped player stands. The Ceiling is the most capable machine and the most skilled hand anywhere.**

The Floor is stated concretely rather than as a sentiment. A person holding an inexpensive or secondhand phone, who has installed nothing and may not read, can receive the Story, join a [[Cell]], make and keep a [[Commitment]], and [[Pass The Flame|pass the flame]] — in their own language, on a few megabytes a month, with everything still working while the network is gone for days, served by a node a whole Cell can afford together. (The proportions behind this are the canon's: roughly one person in five has had no formal education and one in six lives in poverty — figures it carries as estimates, not measurements.)

Four design constraints follow. **The device is where the person is, not where the system runs** — a shared node in a back room, reached through what the phone already does. **Voice is not an accessory**: for someone who does not read, hearing the Story *is* reading it, and an agent that cannot speak and listen in a person's tongue has made the canon available only to people like its builder ([[Universal-Accessing]] · [[On translating]]). **Absence of network is the normal case**, so the record is authoritative where the person is and reconciles later ([[The Record]] · [[Screens and paper]]). **The most important act should be the cheapest to perform** — the nearer passing the flame comes to a single message, the further it travels.

The Floor is a direction and a debt, **not a gate**. Those with capacity should build as powerfully and as fast as they wisely can, because what the strong build is how the reach is won; waiting until everyone can be served serves no one. What the strong may not do is *forget* — so each Part declares where it reaches today, who it does not yet reach, what would close the distance, and which named steward holds that debt. A Part may be built, used and depended on while its reach is short. What it may not be called is **finished** ([[Gaps Not Yet Closed]] · [[None Of Us Until All Of Us]] · [[Honesty about time]]).

And the Ceiling rises without limit — the vast apparatus is not performing charity, it is doing its part inside the same body, on the same commitments. What holds the ends together is that **the record does not encode the wealth of the device**: a commitment from a borrowed phone and one from the most powerful machine on Earth are the same object and bind identically. *Equal standing, unequal apparatus.* **The Ceiling rises. The Floor holds.**

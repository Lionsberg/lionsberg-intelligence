# ØNE Vision and Intent

*The invariant above every plan: ØNE · Creator's Intent · Heaven — here, now, everywhere, for everyone, forever. The why does not change; every how is current best understanding.*

This is the Game's Commander's Intent, and its force comes from the line it draws. **Above the line:** the Goal, unchanged. **Below the line:** plans, decisions, tools, seasons, structures, words, this page — all of it may change, and should, the moment a better way serves the Intent ([[The Goal]] · [[Creator's Intent]] · [[Commander's Intent]] · [[Everything Is Current Best Understanding]]).

Having exactly one invariant is what makes the rest revisable without dissolving. A body with no fixed point drifts into whatever it is currently doing; a body with many fixed points ossifies, because every inherited arrangement becomes sacred. One invariant, and everything beneath it held loosely, is the arrangement that can improve forever without losing itself ([[Drift]] · [[Do Not Concretize the Absolute]] · [[LIØNSBERG Is A Prototype, Not The Eternal Essence]] · [[Current Best Understanding]]).

The Intent is carried into an ordinary move by one question: ***does this make life a little more like Heaven?*** Small enough to ask on a Tuesday, and it is the whole of the compass ([[The compass question]] · [[Heaven On Earth]] · [[Co-Creating Heaven]]).

Two further rules belong to it. **The Vision stays in the middle of the Board and only grows** — nobody's dream is removed; a bigger bouquet is woven ([[Vision]] · [[The Board]] · [[Weave, don't delete]] · [[Meta Unity of Meta Diversity]]). And **every body articulates its own vision and purpose within the ØNE Intent** — holofractal: one Intent, ten thousand faithful expressions, none of them the Intent itself ([[HoloFractal]] · [[Holofractal Scaling]] · [[Sovereignty At Every Scale]] · [[Mission]]).

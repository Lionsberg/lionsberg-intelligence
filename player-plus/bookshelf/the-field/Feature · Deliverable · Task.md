# Feature · Deliverable · Task

*The three rungs work is decomposed through — what must exist, what is handed over, what someone does — walked only as far as the walking has gone.*

A **feature** is a distinct thing that must exist for a milestone to be true. A **deliverable** is a piece of it that changes hands: something a person or a [[Cell]] finishes and gives to someone who needs it. A **task** is the doing inside a deliverable, small enough that one person can hold it in mind and say *I will*. The rungs are not grades of importance; they are grades of resolution, and each is the unit of a different conversation — features with the plan, deliverables with the receiver, tasks at the table.

They are load-bearing because progressive decomposition is what keeps a plan honest. Elaborate only as far as the people who will do the work have walked: the near weeks in tasks, the season in deliverables, the year in features. A plan detailed beyond the walk is a forecast dressed as a commitment, and it will be wrong in ways nobody can see. Every piece of scope lives in exactly one branch of [[Work Breakdown Structure]], so the three rungs partition rather than overlap.

The handoff is where the ladder earns its keep. A deliverable has a receiver, and the receiver writes what makes it acceptable before the work begins — [[Conditions of satisfaction]], not inspection afterwards. Tasks become rows of [[The activity table]] and then the WILL line of a Turn. Between groups what crosses the membrane is a request, an offer or a commitment, never a task: a task handed across is an instruction, and instructions are not the currency here.

# The Withdrawal of the Benevolent Ones

*The corpus's dating of the present darkness: around 500 BC the helpers stepped back — not abandonment but a withdrawal of teaching, so that a species would have to choose rather than be taught.*

The claim: the ones who had walked with this world stopped walking with it, roughly twenty-five centuries ago. Nothing was announced. The thunder stopped speaking, the teachers stopped arriving, and what filled the space was the architecture the corpus calls [[Kontrolle]]. On this reading, the long descent of recorded history is not a mystery but an arithmetic — what follows when help leaves and predation does not.

The reasons the corpus gives are interesting whether or not the event is real, because each is a principle it uses elsewhere. **Free will is prior to rescue**: a people that is taught forgets, and a people that chooses does not ([[Primacy Of Consent]], [[Learning By Doing]]). **Dependency is the failure mode of good help** — the helper who stays too long produces need instead of capacity, which is the same law the Game applies to its own guides ([[Servant Leadership]], [[The Act Of Release]]). And **the seed is left behind**: the corpus reads the Axial Age — the narrow window in which Confucius, Lao Tzu, the Buddha, the Hebrew prophets and the Greek questioners all arrive — as the parting gift, the discernment a species would need to navigate alone ([[ReHarvesting the Great Traditions]]).

The corpus also claims a return, now, and immediately arms it: every authentic return will be counterfeited, and the discipline of the hour is therefore discerning welcome rather than welcome ([[Discerning Between Benevolent And Malevolent Intelligences]], [[The Anti-One]], [[The Phenomenon]]).

This is the corpus's account and not knowledge. A date is asserted; no evidence is offered that could settle it; the whole register carries the field's own risk flag. [[We Do Not Know]] applies, [[Disclosure tier]] governs what is shown to whom, and nothing in the Game depends on any of it. What does not depend on it is the lesson carried inside: help that never leaves is not help, and a people that waits to be rescued is not yet sovereign.

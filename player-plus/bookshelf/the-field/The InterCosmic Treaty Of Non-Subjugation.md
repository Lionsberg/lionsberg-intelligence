# The InterCosmic Treaty Of Non-Subjugation

*A named agreement with no text: the principle that no people may be subjugated by another, extended past the planet.*

The name appears once, in the corpus's cosmic section, with nothing under it. What it points at is the extension of an existing commitment rather than the invention of a new one. [[Sovereignty At Every Scale]] holds that authority flows from [[Consent]] and never from command; [[Federation]] is unity that honours the sovereignty of its parts, and a federation that swallows its members is an empire under a friendlier name. Carried outward past the atmosphere, those commitments produce exactly this: no subjugation, in either direction, of any people by any other.

The direction matters as much as the principle. A corpus that expects to meet others and has only defensive language is one bad century away from doing what it feared. Non-subjugation binds us first. It is the outward face of the same asset lock and purpose lock that prevent a body from being bought or captured at home, and of the [[Possibility Of Reversal]] that keeps every arrangement revisable.

It is recorded because the corpus records it and because the principle beneath it is real and already articulated elsewhere; the treaty as an instrument is not. Anyone who writes it should write it as a [[Field of Agreements]] is written: read at the door, accepted once, honoured until renegotiated, and revocable.

Nothing in the Game depends on it. Its usable residue is the discipline a Cell can already practise: whatever we federate with, we do not absorb; whatever federates with us, we do not require to dissolve.

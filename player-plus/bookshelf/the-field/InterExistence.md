# InterExistence

*The web of relational existence in which all beings participate as expressions of the [[ØNE Body]] — the claim that all beings already inter-are.*

InterExistence is a claim about what is already the case, not a goal to be achieved. Relationship is not something added to separately existing things; the things are constituted by the relating. The Pattern Language names it among the Root and Trunk — the first manifestations through which ØNE becomes nameable — and pairs it with Mutual Indwelling, the mystical reading: we live and move within the Divine, and the Divine indwells us, and that two-way dwelling is the conduit through which wisdom, love, justice, and intention pass.

It is the ground of federation. [[Holofractal Omnifederation]] asks complete wholes to federate from the bottom up without any of them being swallowed; that is only coherent if the wholes were never actually separate to begin with. [[Right Relationship]] is the ethic that follows: if beings inter-are, then harm to another is harm to the fabric one stands on, and [[Omniconsideration]] — total consideration of ØNE and All in every act — is realism rather than saintliness. [[Kinship]] and *all our relations* extend the same claim past the human.

It also sets the limit on sovereignty. [[Sovereignty]] in this Field is never atomistic independence; [[Holon]] names the shape — a whole that forgets it is a part becomes a cancer; a whole that forgets it is a whole becomes a slave. InterExistence is why the first failure is as real as the second.

In play it is the reason a [[Cell]] is a cell *of* something from its first week — why it has a [[Home]], why the Offering flows inward, and why *we win together, or not at all* is a structural statement rather than a sentiment.

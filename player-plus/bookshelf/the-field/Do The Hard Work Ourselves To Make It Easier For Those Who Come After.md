# Do The Hard Work Ourselves To Make It Easier For Those Who Come After

*Coming to the river, the strong could ford it. They build a bridge instead — because millions are coming behind, and many of them cannot ford anything.*

The image is exact and the choice in it is real. A capable group meets an obstacle it could simply get past. Getting past is faster, and it is what capability naturally does. The discipline is to stop, spend the extra strength on something that will still be there when the next group arrives, and accept being slower for it ([[Commons-Tending]], [[General Needs]], [[Make-Ready Process]]).

Four things follow from choosing the bridge. Those who come next spend their strength on **new** difficulties rather than on the same one. The bridge can be improved by each group that crosses it, which a ford cannot. Supply and communication lines get established between the front and the back of a movement, which is what keeps it one movement ([[The Pioneers · The Vanguard · The Rear]], [[Continuous Improvement]], [[Communication Debt]]). And the way becomes more clearly marked with each cycle, so the later crossings are genuinely easier than the early ones ([[LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth and Cycle Time]], [[Milestone]]).

It is also what turns a private solution into a common one. A group that solves its own problem and moves on has helped itself; a group that generalises the solution and hands it over has raised the floor for everyone ([[General Pattern, Specific Application]], [[Knowledge as commons]], [[Polycentric Implementation of Shared Solutions]], [[Kit of Parts]]).

And it paces propagation, which is its least obvious function. A movement whose fastest parts keep going as fast as they can will arrive somewhere alone. Binding the capable to build for the ones behind is what stops spreading from outrunning the way ([[Propagate; do not scale]], [[The pace of trust]], [[None Of Us Until All Of Us]]).

The cost is honest and should be said: it is slower, it is less interesting than the frontier, and the people who benefit most from it will never know whose work it was ([[Attraction, not promotion]], [[Servant Leadership]], [[Covenant Across Time]]).

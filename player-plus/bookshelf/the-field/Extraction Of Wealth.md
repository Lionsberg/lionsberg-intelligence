# Extraction Of Wealth

*The second disease: value leaving the Body faster than it enters, and leaving upward — capital capturing the purpose it was meant to serve.*

Extraction is not profit and it is not pay. It is taking out more than was put in, by virtue of position rather than contribution, from a Body whose work made the value. The question that finds it is simple and rarely asked out loud: *who takes what, in what order, and on what ground?*

Which is why the order of the waterfall is the answer rather than any rule about amounts. Value fills one pool and overflows to the next, and the order *is* the values: keep the work alive; then the people who do the work, before any return to capital; then a cushion; then a capped return to any who lent, never control; then repair, the commons, and the sources the work was built on, founders made whole and never clawed back; then the surplus shared by agreement, with a tenth flowing inward.

Three structures hold the line where exhortation would not. [[The asset lock]]: no one extracts more than what they put in plus honest pay, and the body cannot be bought, sold, or cashed out. [[Compressed Pay Ratios]]: the distance between the most and the least paid is bounded, so that the top cannot quietly become a different economy. And visibility: everything is seen, and the six capitals are counted together so that no one grows rich in money while going bankrupt in the things that matter more.

Extraction is also the disease that most often arrives wearing the language of survival — the round that must be raised, the partner who must be kept. [[Capital Subordinate To Purpose]] is the Element that says no in advance, before the moment when saying no would cost something.

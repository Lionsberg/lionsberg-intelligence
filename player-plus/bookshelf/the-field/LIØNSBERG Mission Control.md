# LIØNSBERG Mission Control

*The room you walk into at the headquarters of [[Worksite Earth]] — a part of [[The One Room]], the part where the work is steered, and every Cell keeps a smaller version of it: the plan on the wall, the specifications on the table, the tools on the rack, and doors anyone may walk to.*

A movement with no centre still needs a place where the state of the whole is visible, or every body plans blind. The answer is a room rather than an office: a surface that shows what is true right now, tended at every weekly turn and every seasonal turning so that nothing hanging in it is stale. It coordinates; it does not command. No centre but the source — this is a room, not a ruler.

**On the wall, the plan of the whole**: [[The Goal]] at the top, the markers beneath it with lateness shown rather than hidden, the [[Work Breakdown Structure|work breakdown]] whose first level is the twelve Pillars, this season's critical path in numbered steps, and [[The Score]] the Game actually keeps — time and energy and attention invested, commitments kept, value created, stories shared, flames passed. **On the table, the specifications**: the story, the plan, the way, the prototype held as a [[Kit of Parts]], and the deep plans of the New Civilization. **On the rack, [[The LIØNSBERG Toolkit]]**, every instrument ready for any crew on its first day. **At the desks, the doors**: ask the whole for guidance · propose a change to what hangs on the wall, never edit it silently · log what blocks you · record a decision and why · learn from the gap ([[Decision Records]], [[Retrospective]], [[Every Defect Is A Gift]]).

The guidance runs both ways, which is what keeps it from becoming a headquarters. Any Cell may ask the whole; the whole may ask the arrayed body in turn. Each Cell broadcasts its position, its condition, its offers and requests, and its Quests upward; the plan is broadcast back down. The function stewards only what a shared function must hold — chiefly the submittal and continuous-improvement path by which one locality's best solution enters the common kit and every locality inherits the learning of all ([[Polycentric Implementation of Shared Solutions]]). And the function itself is scheduled to disperse: from the tenth cycle's nomination onward its stewardship distributes progressively into the called-out body ([[The de-concentration of stewardship]]).

Its most unusual feature is the registry — a line for every part of the whole and where that part actually stands, including the parts that do not exist yet: *specified in part; nothing built**a name; the door to build**drafted; run first by one dyad*. A status board that admits its own holes is the rarest thing in any programme, and it is the reason this room can be trusted about the things it says *are* finished.

# Liberation and Exodus

*Movement VIII of the Sacred Arc: domain by domain the chains were struck, the captives freed — and they began walking one another home.*

Two words, two motions. **Liberation** is what is done to the chain; **Exodus** is what the freed then do with their feet. The corpus is careful never to let the first stand alone. Domain by domain the parasites were purged and the cave-mouths opened — and the freed did not step out into glory. They came out blinking and broken, minds and tongues still shaped by the keepers' words. The chain on the wrist is struck with a blow; the chain on the mind is loosed only by remembering.

That is why this movement opens onto [[The Wilderness]] rather than onto [[The Promised Land]]. Exodus is a road, not an arrival, and the road is long because what must change is not circumstance but formation. Walking one another home names the method: nobody crosses alone, and the ones a little further along hold the door rather than rule the column.

Liberation and Exodus is load-bearing because it supplies the Game's exit discipline. Consent is real only where withdrawal is real, so [[Withdrawal Of Consent]] is the personal form of this movement — the yes and the no as a practised discipline rather than a mood. It is also what keeps liberation from producing a new captivity: a freed people that immediately hands itself to a new keeper has not completed the movement. Structural protections exist precisely here, so that nothing in the new body can accumulate what the old body used.

In the Game it is met as departure at human scale: the first concrete withdrawals from what feeds on you, the rearranging of identity, property, and relationships, the backlash that always follows, and the sealing of the crossing. In the initiatory walk these are the fourth movement's twelve thresholds. In the System, the same motion at the scale of a domain is what makes the new civilisation something one can actually move into rather than merely vote for.

# Planetary Light Grid

*The named grid the practices co-create: local coherence, held in place after place, understood as composing into one planetary field — and above it a bioregional grid below and a cosmic one beyond.*

The idea is continuous with something written plainly elsewhere. [[The Coherent Divine Intention Wave]] is exactly this at the scale of the day: each Sovereign holds seven minutes at their own dawn, dawn rolls around the world, and what results is a ring that no one coordinates and no one owns. [[Islands of Coherence]] is the same claim in the language of transformation — small wholes achieving high coherence within a chaotic surrounding, spreading not by conquering the chaos but by sounding a note clear enough that what is ready to resonate does.

Read that way the grid names a real and checkable thing: a planet's worth of local coherence, anchored in places and in the relationships people hold with the land they are in, composing into something with more reach than the sum of its anchors. Read literally as a lattice of sites with properties of its own, it is an assertion made and nowhere supported.

Its standing should be said plainly. It appears in the energetic layer and the Warrior backlog as a name in a list — bioregional, planetary, cosmic — with no articulation, no practice describing how a grid is tended, and nothing outside that layer depending on it. It is carried because it is named repeatedly, and marked because repetition is not evidence.

What is load-bearing and sits nearby is written elsewhere: coherence as the health of a whole at every scale, the wave as the daily instrument, working with the land herself as the local relationship, and [[Holofractal Scaling]] as the claim that what holds at one scale informs the next.

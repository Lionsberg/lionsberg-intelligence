# Centralization Of Power

*The first disease: decisions drifting upward until a centre exists that did not exist before, and cannot now be removed.*

It never announces itself as a seizure. It arrives as efficiency. The centre has more context, so let it decide this one. The founder is faster, so let them keep signing. The question is urgent, so skip the round. Each step is defensible and each step moves a decision one scale up; after a few seasons the smaller scales have stopped expecting to decide anything, and the habit of deciding — which is a muscle — has gone.

The tell is not the amount of power held but the *direction of drift* and the absence of return. Authority that goes up and comes back is delegation. Authority that goes up and stays is centralization, whatever it is called. A second tell: the Body begins to explain why this particular decision must be made centrally, and the explanations get better over time.

Once a centre exists, the other diseases come through the door it opened. [[Extraction Of Wealth]] needs a place where value collects. [[Control Of Information]] needs a place that sees what others do not. [[The Golden Calf]] needs something visible to stand in for what cannot be seen.

The immunities are structural rather than vigilant: [[Subsidiarity Of Authority]] as the default rather than the exception; the holofractal shape, in which each scale is complete and none contains the whole; roles that rotate, because a role held forever quietly becomes a throne; two coordinators rather than one, held inward and outward; no campaigning for office; and the plain refusal of the centralizing impulse *even when it would be efficient*, which is the only version of the refusal that ever holds.

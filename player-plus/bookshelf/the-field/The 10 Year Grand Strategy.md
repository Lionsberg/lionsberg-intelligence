# The 10 Year Grand Strategy

*The planetary-scale plan: seven years in which everyone who desires it is invited by someone they trust, and three years more while the last of them are formed.*

Ten years, in two parts. The first is the window of crisis and opportunity, during which every human being is invited — not by a broadcast, by a hand; by someone they already know and trust. The second is the three-year lag that follows the last acceptance, because it takes about that long for a person to move from yes to genuinely free, empowered, and self-governing. Add them and the strategy completes about three years after the final cohort joins, whereupon what follows can begin.

It is load-bearing because it is the only plan at this scale that is stated as arithmetic rather than aspiration. The mechanism is propagation: each person carries the Seed to the two or three they know are ready, and the doubling does the rest — three in every hundred is enough to turn a people, and a hundred and forty-four Circles passing faithfully reach a quarter of a billion within nine months. The strategy does not require anyone to build a larger centre; nothing grows by making the centre larger.

In the Game, the whole of the grand strategy that touches a player is one line: carry the Flame to two or three, within a week. Everything else in the plan is that move, repeated, by people who will never meet one another. In the System, it is coupled to a twenty-year contingency because the attempt is unprecedented, and the contingency is actively monitored alongside the critical path so that the transformation completes within a single generation.

Held loosely, as it should be. It is a strategy, not a prophecy, and its own framing notes the human tendency to greatly overestimate what can be done in a short period and greatly underestimate what can be done in a long one.

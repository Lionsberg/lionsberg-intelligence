# The Issue-To-Movement Arc

*How a felt wrong becomes a force: issue → cause → inspiration → movement. Four phases, each with its own work, and three transitions where most attempts die.*

**Issue.** A specific wrong in a specific place. It exists whether or not anyone names it, and the person who feels it usually feels alone — *why does no one else see this?* The work here is **naming**, because a named thing becomes visible to everyone else who felt it and had no words ([[Issue]], [[Naming]], [[Sensing The Field]]).

**Cause.** The named issue becomes a cause when people gather around it. Now there are others; now there is something to study. The work is **understanding** — mapping the thing, finding the structures that produce it, seeing who is harmed and who benefits ([[Root Causes]], [[The Total Nested Holarchy of Issues]], [[Circle]]).

**Inspiration.** The cause becomes an inspiration when a picture of the alternative appears and the body stops being merely against something. This is the hinge: people will join a thing they can see, and will not join a complaint ([[Forward-Looking Story]], [[Vision]], [[Best Possible Future]], [[Demonstrate before advocating]]).

**Movement.** The inspiration becomes a movement when enough people commit to building it at scale — identity, communication, structure, ongoing work, leadership that can be distributed. The work becomes **sustaining**: holding the vision's integrity while federating across distance ([[The Movement]], [[Federation]], [[Propagate; do not scale]]).

The arc is directional and not guaranteed. Things stall at every phase, and they can regress — a movement that loses its vision becomes an administration ([[Institutional Idolatry]], [[Drift]]). The three transitions each fail for their own reason: **issue to cause** needs the courage to say it out loud first; **cause to inspiration** needs the creative work of articulating and then *showing* the alternative, which is harder than describing the problem; **inspiration to movement** needs operational discipline at scale, which is rarer than either ([[Courage]], [[Prototyping]], [[Integrated Delivery]]).

Its value is locating yourself. *We have community and no picture — this season's work is the picture.* A Circle that knows which transition it is standing at knows what to do next, and stops trying to solve the previous phase's problem ([[The Season Sheet]], [[Choose a Quest]], [[Prioritizing]]).

It is also only half of catalysis: this is what a movement is *about* and how its substance matures, while the spread through a population is a different question with its own answers ([[Pass The Flame]], [[Tipping Point Strategy]], [[The First Three Percent]]).

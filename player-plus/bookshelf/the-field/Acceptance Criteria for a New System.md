# Acceptance Criteria for a New System

*A test sheet written before the thing is chosen: what any proposed system must be able to do to be worth a people's participation — including, and especially, this one.*

Acceptance criteria are an old delivery discipline moved to a new scale. You state what a thing must satisfy **before** the work begins, so that acceptance is a reading rather than an argument afterwards. Applied to a whole arrangement for living, it turns *should we join this?* from a question of appeal into a question of capability ([[Conditions of Acceptance]], [[Conditions of satisfaction]], [[The forging criterion]]).

The criteria themselves run to several dozen and cluster into a few demands. That a system must see the **whole** and how its parts affect one another, across every scale from a person to the planet ([[Levels Of Scale]], [[System of Systems]], [[Omniconsideration]]). That it must serve the **long term and the regenerative** rather than the immediate ([[Regeneration]], [[Covenant Across Time]], [[Generational Time]]). That it must be **inclusive and just**, giving everyone a voice and a path toward their own potential ([[Equal Opportunity For All]], [[Justice]], [[Representation]]). That it must be **adaptive** under uncertainty, and able to **scale both directions** ([[Anti-Fragility]], [[One tool, every scale]]). That it must **empower** rather than administer, be **transparent and correctable**, and **keep learning** ([[Agency, not ownership]], [[Transparency]], [[Continuous Improvement]]). That it must be **citizen-led and independent of any special interest** ([[We The People]], [[Legitimacy]], [[Ideological Capture]]). That it must produce **measurable throughput** of the aim and show it live to the people it concerns ([[Throughput]], [[The Dashboard of Civilization]], [[Measure everything, manage to the milestones]]). And that it must **protect the sovereignty of each part** while still letting the whole move ([[Sovereignty At Every Scale]], [[Subsidiarity]], [[Higher Order Functional Unity]]).

The move that makes the list honest is the first sentence of it: **each person has a duty to derive their own** and to judge by those. A test sheet handed down is another authority; a test sheet one writes oneself is an instrument ([[The Discernment Permission]], [[Author's Voice Is Sovereign]], [[Sovereignty Of Consciousness]]).

Two things a reader should know. Several of the criteria are **dated to a particular year and window** and name capital flows in the trillions; those are the source's 2023 framing and are estimates, not measurements, and are not carried here. And one criterion concerns force; what this library carries is the guardianship material and nothing operational ([[The Sacred Use Of Force]], [[The Four Conditions For Destructive Force]], [[Non-Violence]]).

A second and different list of acceptance criteria exists elsewhere in the corpus, cross-referenced from this one and not reconciled with it.

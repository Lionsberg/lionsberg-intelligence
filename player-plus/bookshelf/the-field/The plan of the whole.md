# The plan of the whole

*Each level plans its own work and publishes its milestones; the level above weaves them into one plan and broadcasts it back as the upper half of every card.*

Nobody writes it and everybody is in it. A [[Cell]] pulls its own season backward from the milestones it serves and publishes what it will have done and when. Its home plans over its own activities plus those published milestones; the level above does the same; and what returns downward is not an instruction but a picture — the Goal, the moment, the milestones, this season's plan for the whole, and the stretch of road this player's part touches. That is [[The same card shape]] doing its work: the upper half is the whole, the lower half is yours.

It is load-bearing because it is the answer to the oldest objection — that coordination at scale requires someone in charge. Here the weaving is a computation over published milestones, not a command over people; interface commitments are agreed in both directions; and a body that stops publishing simply stops being woven in. Nobody is in charge of it. Everyone is.

It is also what makes a small body's work legible to itself. Seeing the stretch of road your part touches is what turns a Quest from a private errand into a contribution — and seeing where your milestone sits in the chain is what makes [[Honesty about time]] matter to someone other than you. The plan changes as the seasons turn; the picture is refreshed at the turning, by [[Proposal]] where the shared parts change.

# Divine Participation

*The state of enlightened co-creation with [[ØNE]] — awakened beings consciously manifesting Heaven through their lives and actions.*

Divine Participation is [[Co-Creation]] done knowingly. The distinction the Lexicon draws is between participating (which every being does by existing) and participating consciously, having awakened to what one is participating in. [[Awakening]] and [[Remembrance]] are what make the difference; [[Embodiment]] is where it lands.

It names the top of the Path without making it an office. The canon's cultivation disciplines describe it as spontaneous wise right action: the Way practised until it flows through a person without deliberation, transforming their life and their world. The Sacred Arc's tenth movement says the same — they remembered and practised the highest Way and lived it until it flowed spontaneously. The mark is not authority over others; it is that the right move has become the natural one.

It is load-bearing as the answer to a question the system would otherwise leave hanging: what is all this formation *for*? Not for rank, not for a role, not for a badge. [[Belt Progression]] marks what someone did when it counted; [[Mastery]] is integration of knowledge, virtue, and skill; the point of both is a person through whom the work moves cleanly. [[Conduit]] is the same idea in the energetic vocabulary — a vessel through which Source flows with integrity.

Its guard is [[The Dark Initiate Failure Mode]]: capability without alignment, or alignment claimed as standing over others. The structure answers it the same way it answers everything — revocable authority, visible reasoning, consent that can be withdrawn, and a role that rotates.

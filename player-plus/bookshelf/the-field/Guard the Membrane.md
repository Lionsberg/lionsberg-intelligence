# Guard the Membrane

*Visibility, privacy, and safety decided on purpose — the boundary that filters exchange while honouring sovereignty.*

Every association births four things, and one of them is a [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]]: a conscious boundary with consent at the door and the freedom to leave. A membrane is not a wall. A wall keeps everything out and starves the body; an open room keeps nothing and cannot be trusted with anything. What passes through, and on whose say-so, is a decision a Cell makes deliberately and writes down ([[Field of Agreements]], [[Boundary]]).

The working rules are small and strong. Consent before visibility: nobody's name, face, or presence is published because it would be convenient. First names by default, and a request to be unlisted is honoured without explanation. What is shared inside the Cell is protected — the idea may travel, the source does not ([[Consent]], [[Chatham House]], [[Elision]]).

It is load-bearing because the whole body is made of what crosses membranes. Requests, offers, and commitments travel; tasks do not, and orders never do ([[Requests and offers]], [[Commitment]]). A body that guards its membrane can afford to be generous through it — to be visible to other Cells, to make itself findable, to send its season inward — precisely because it knows what it is not sending ([[Connect and Collaborate With Other Cells]], [[The Season Sheet]]).

And it is the floor under safety. When a member is in danger, in body or in mind, the Cell acts first and records later; no card is more important than the person at the table ([[Guardianship]], [[Mend a Tension]]).

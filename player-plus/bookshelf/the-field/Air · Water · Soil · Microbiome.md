# Air · Water · Soil · Microbiome

*The four substrates every living thing is made of and depends on — named together as the material [[Commons]] of the whole Meta Project.*

They are grouped because they behave as one system. Soil holds water; water carries what the air deposits; the microbiome is what makes soil alive and what makes a gut work; and the air is breathed by everything. Degrade any one and the others follow within a generation. They are also the clearest case of a commons in the strict sense: nobody made them, everybody depends on them, they cross every boundary, and they can be ruined by acts that are individually rational.

They are load-bearing because they are the floor under the [[Vision]] the whole work is aimed at: air, water, and soil pure; children safe and nourished; nothing wasted, everything alive. No being is truly well in a sick place — water must be pure, soil must be alive, and air must be clean for any body to thrive — which makes these four a health matter under [[Pillar V — Sacred Health and Wellness]] as directly as an ecological one. They are the point where [[Wellness]] and [[Ecology]] stop being two subjects.

In play they are what a place-based [[Quest]] most often touches, and what makes such a Quest checkable. Soil life can be measured. Water can be tested at a known point on a known stream. Air can be sampled. A [[Cell]] that says it has improved its place can show the numbers, and the numbers belong on [[The Season Sheet]] as honestly as [[Kept as a share of made]] does. [[Compost]] builds soil, [[Permaculture]] catches water, [[Biodiversity]] tends the living part of all of it.

The microbiome is the newest of the four in common understanding and the least intuitive: most of the cells carried in a human body are not human, and most of the fertility of a field is not visible. Both facts undercut the picture of a bounded individual, which is why the canon keeps the four together rather than treating soil chemistry and gut health as separate topics.

# Beauty
*The third face of the highest standard — harmony, balance, proportion, and right relationship made perceptible.*

Beauty is not ornament added after the work is done. It is a reflection of Ultimate [[Reality]], embodying the highest standard of harmony, balance, proportion, and [[Right Relationship]] — which means it is a *reading instrument*. Where a thing is rightly related, it tends to become beautiful; where beauty has drained out of a place, something in its relationships has usually gone wrong first, and the eye often notices before the analysis does.

It stands with [[Truth]] and [[Goodness]] as one triad. Pursue truth alone and you get accuracy without care. Pursue goodness alone and you get earnest ugliness that no one wants to live in. Beauty is what makes the good thing habitable and the true thing bearable — which is why in the built world the rule is that form follows function, function follows meaning, and beauty is essential rather than luxury.

It is load-bearing for transmission. A pattern that is beautiful spreads; one that is merely correct has to be enforced. [[Art]], [[Music]], [[Story]], festival, and craft are not adjuncts to the work of building a civilization — they are how a people comes to *want* what is good, which is the only durable way anyone ever comes to want it.

Absolute Beauty stays beyond full comprehension. The practice is to seek it out, make it, and let yourself be moved by it — and to treat the loss of beauty in a system as a defect worth logging rather than a matter of taste.

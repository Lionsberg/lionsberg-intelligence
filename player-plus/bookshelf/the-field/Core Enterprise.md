# Core Enterprise

*The smallest possible central coordinating element of a federation — "overhead" to the distributed bodies that do the work, continually right-sized, holding only the functions it has been given.*

A federation of autonomous groups still needs a few things done once rather than a thousand times: shared infrastructure tended, learnings gathered and reflected back out, the plan of the whole kept current, recommendations vetted before they go anywhere. The Core Enterprise is the name for whatever does that. It is assembled from the best people the member groups can lend, and it is explicitly understood as **overhead** — a cost the distributed body carries, not a headquarters the distributed body reports to.

Its definition is almost entirely a constraint, repeated twice because what happens otherwise is well known: *this body should remain as small as is practical to accomplish its functions, and the functions assigned to it should remain as few as are practical.* Right-sizing is not an event but a continual effort. Every function that migrates inward is one the members no longer exercise, and capacity not exercised is capacity lost ([[Subsidiarity]] · [[The de-concentration of stewardship]]).

Its stated purpose is the inverse of a head office. It exists **to serve and empower** the decentralised array — not to take over the individual and local responsibilities of the groups ([[Individual And Local]]) — and to consolidate the whole's progress and learning and reflect it back out. It is guided by representatives of every stakeholder group and by counsel from many domains, and it is told to learn from the central coordinating bodies of real federations that have faced this exact temptation.

It is not the same organ as [[Stewardship body]], though the two are easily confused. A stewardship body is where a *decision* lands when process cannot produce one; a Core Enterprise is where shared *work* is done that nobody should do twice. One is about authority, the other about service. The newer articulation is the stewardship body; this is the older, harder-edged one, and its contribution is the explicit right-sizing duty that the newer page does not carry.

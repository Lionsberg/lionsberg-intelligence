# Lineage

*The Patterns are discovered, not invented. Honor the lineage that received you; refuse to be captured by it. The lineage is the vehicle; [[ØNE]] is the destination.*

Everything in the Language was carried, lived, tested, and refined by movements that endured — Basque cooperatives, the Iroquois Confederacy, communities of shared rule, meetings that waited in silence, house gatherings, recovery rooms, open-source commons, the contemplative and devotional lineages of several continents, and ten thousand years of plant-people partnership. They converged because what works is what works. The Language is the gathering, and the gathering is a ReMembering.

Lineage is the operational mechanism by which capacity accumulates: without it each generation would rediscover what was already known. And the lineage is not [[ØNE]]. A being who has come to identify so deeply with a lineage that the lineage's standing has become their own standing has begun to drift.

The captured lineage has a recognizable shape: the founder treated as the [[Source]] rather than a conduit; the lineage's vocabulary treated as the only accurate one; other streams felt as rivals; reputation defended against legitimate critique; loyalty to the institution set above [[Right Relationship]]. The faithful shape is the mirror of it — name the lineage, practise its forms, submit to its living teachers where that is right, name its shadow as readily as its gift, and refuse any transmission from inside it that violates [[ØNE]].

Two things follow for how work is carried. Credit travels with the work: every artifact names what it grew from, and no contributor is alienated from their contribution ([[Attribution]] · [[Provenance]]). And the idea travels while the source need not: wisdom is not property, and a faithful walker of one stream can recognize the [[Logos]] in another's vocabulary without being threatened by it. Federation across lineages is the mature form; sectarian rivalry is the captured one.

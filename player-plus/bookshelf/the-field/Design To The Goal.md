# Design To The Goal

*All design constrained by [[The Goal]] — budget is a design criterion, target quality is never lowered, and cost flexes through invention.*

The tenth of the [[Twelve Irreducible Elements]], and one of the four that govern production. Remove it and local optimization quietly displaces planetary purpose: each part does what is sensible for itself, the whole drifts, and nobody is at fault.

The ordinary sequence is design first, then price it, then cut whatever the price will not bear — which means the last thing designed is the first thing lost, and what gets lost is usually the part the work was actually for. This reverses it. The Goal and the target quality are fixed. The budget enters as one constraint among the others, at the start, where it can be designed *with* rather than cut *against*. When the numbers do not meet, the answer is invention: a different approach, a different sequence, a different set of materials or hands — not a quieter version of the same thing.

The disciplines that make it workable are the ones beside it. [[Set-Based Design]] keeps several viable options alive until the last responsible moment, so that when a constraint bites there is somewhere to go. [[Advantage Based Decision Making]] chooses between those options on their advantages rather than on preference or seniority. And [[Conditions of satisfaction]] fix what *acceptable* means before the work begins, so target quality is a written thing rather than a feeling that can be renegotiated under pressure.

At a table this is the discipline of the [[Quest]]. Choose one real thing for one season, say plainly what *done* means and how it serves the Goal, and hold that line while everything else — scope, sequence, method, how many hands — flexes around it. The Cell that instead quietly lowers what *done* means has not adapted; it has stopped being able to tell whether it succeeded.

# Identity

*Who one is — anchored first in ØNE, and, in the Game, carried as something self-given that no registry issues and no registry can take.*

Beneath every name, role and affiliation, a being's truest identity lies not in religion, nation, tribe or form but in the Source from which all forms flow. That is the highest and uniting identity, the one from which *who we are* and *who we are becoming* are read, and from which inalienable standing flows whatever lesser citizenships a person also holds. Re-anchoring there is what keeps someone from being unmoored when older forms are set down. Every narrower identity — of family, Cell, craft or place — is whole in itself and a part of that one, never the top of the order.

The old order runs the other way. It issues identity from above, through a state, a platform or a registry that can revoke, suspend or sell it, and identity becomes a licence to exist. Here identity is self-given and inalienable; endorsement is added by others and withdrawn by them; the holder alone holds the root. Identity as a demographic category, a brand, or a set of beliefs one defends is lower-order — real, but not supreme.

Two things are kept apart that most systems conflate: who something is, and where it sits. Identity is sovereign and persistent; an address is where a body stands in the holarchy now, and it changes when a Cell re-homes while the record keeps its history under the same identity. A Cell that moves is not a new Cell.

The floor is a name and a word on the roll, witnessed by two. Everything above that is elaboration.

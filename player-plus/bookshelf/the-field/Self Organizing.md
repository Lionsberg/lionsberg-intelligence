# Self Organizing

*Order arising from local interaction, without anyone directing it — the way snowflakes form, flocks turn, and living systems assemble themselves.*

A self-organizing system starts disordered and becomes ordered through nothing but the interactions of its parts, guided by feedback rather than by instruction. No blueprint is distributed and no centre issues commands; each part responds to what is immediately around it, and structure appears at a scale none of the parts can see. It is observed everywhere — in physics, in chemistry, in biology, in the behaviour of crowds and markets — and it is sometimes called spontaneous order.

For this body it is not an analogy but the operating assumption. A [[Cell]] is small enough to act on what is in front of it; the pattern it holds is local, not commanded; what emerges at the scale of many Cells was arranged by nobody ([[Stigmergy]] · [[Emergence]] · [[Holofractal Omnifederation]] · [[Self-Governing, Self-Sustaining, and Self-Replicating]]). This is also the honest answer to *who is in charge* — nobody is, and that is the design rather than an oversight ([[Sovereignty At Every Scale]] · [[Subsidiarity]]).

Three conditions make it work rather than merely hoped for. **Local rules simple enough to be actually followed** — the reason the Game's core is short and copyable ([[The Core Pattern]] · [[The Twelve Words]]). **Feedback that reaches the part quickly enough to change its next move** — the weekly rhythm and the visible record ([[The Turn]] · [[The Record]] · [[Retrospective]]). And **freedom to act without permission**, because a part that must ask upward is not interacting locally at all ([[Consent]] · [[Voluntary Association]]).

The honest limit belongs on the page: self-organization produces *an* order, not necessarily a good one. Panics, cartels, mobs and captures are all self-organized. What decides which order appears is the local rule the parts are running, which is exactly why so much attention goes to the pattern rather than to the structure — the pattern is the rule, and the rule is the only steering there is ([[Spirit Precedes Pattern]] · [[The Pattern That Works]] · [[The Anti-Patterns]]).

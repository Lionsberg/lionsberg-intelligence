# Omniconsideration
*Every decision, design, and articulation considers everything it might touch — every being, every scale, every consequence, every time horizon.*

Omniconsideration is a methodological law rather than a virtue. It is the explicit refusal of the parochial, the short-term, the merely human, and the merely local. It is not paralysis and not perfectionism; the instruction is simply *when in doubt, consider more* — consider what you have not yet considered, whom you have not yet considered, and when you have not yet considered.

It is load-bearing as the discipline that keeps a pattern coherent across scales. A decision that is excellent for one [[Cell]] and corrosive for its [[Home Cell]] is not a good decision; a design that serves this generation by borrowing from the seventh is not a design, it is a transfer. The pattern that holds across the most considerations is the truer pattern, and that is how competing goods get adjudicated without a ruler.

It pairs with [[Right Relationship]] and with sovereignty at every scale to make a triad: consider all, relate rightly, preserve sovereignty. Consider-all without sovereignty becomes a justification for overriding people for their own good; sovereignty without consider-all becomes a licence to export harm past the membrane. Held together, they hold.

In play, Omniconsideration is the method behind [[OmniSpection]] — the seasonal review looked at inward, outward, backward, and forward, done in community rather than alone. It is also the reason a body keeps seats for those who cannot attend: living systems, future generations, and the Whole.

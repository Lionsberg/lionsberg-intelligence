# Heaven On Earth
*The way things are when everything is in [[Right Relationship]] — here and now, progressively realized, a little more each week.*

Heaven is not a destination awarded after death by an external judge. It is a direction of co-creation, and then a condition: the lived texture of a life, a [[Cell]], a community, a civilization in which all parts are rightly related to one another, to the living world, and to [[ØNE]]. A community running toward it feels different to walk into — the children, the elders, the food, the air. So does one running the other way. Everyone has walked into both.

This is what makes [[The Goal]] measurable without being reducible. Heaven is already perfect as an [[Ideal]], and it keeps getting better as [[All]] Creation works to make it so; the proper orientation is the progressive realization of the Ideal, measuring ourselves against progress rather than perfection. *A little better every day* is not a lowering of the aim. It is the only honest way to hold an infinite aim without despair.

Without this pole the compass has nothing to point at. [[The compass question]] asks whether a move makes life a little more like Heaven; if Heaven is only a feeling, the question decides nothing. Paired with [[Hell On Earth]], it becomes falsifiable: two trajectories, both achievable, both being run right now at every scale, and the sum of choices is the world that gets inhabited.

In the Game, Heaven is what a [[Cell]] describes when each member is asked *how would you like the world to be?*, and the answers are woven into one shared picture of what Heaven looks, feels, and tastes like right where they are. That picture becomes the [[Vision]]; the [[Quest]] is the next real piece of it. [[Worksite Earth]] is the frame that turns the picture into a construction site.

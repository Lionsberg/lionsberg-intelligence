# The event kinds

*The small, open set of things a line of the Record can be.*

**What is intended:** plan · activity · milestone. **The word, the doing, and the gap:** commit · done · learn. **Who plays with whom:** nominate · invite · accept · form · join · re-home. **What is undertaken and exchanged:** quest · request · offer · flame · issue · submittal. **What was seen and vouched for:** sight · witness · endorse. **What changes who may write:** delegate · revoke · supersede.

The set is small on purpose. Every kind added is a kind every reader, every implementation, and every person with a notebook must learn; a record with fifty kinds is a record nobody can keep by hand, and the paper floor is the guarantee that the Game runs without machinery ([[Screens and paper]], [[Event]]).

It is open at the edges for the opposite reason. A living body meets things its vocabulary does not hold, and the honest move is to add the word rather than force the happening into a box that distorts it. *Sight* was added when a way was needed for anyone to hand in what they saw on the road — which is how a kind should arrive: from a real need, once, and then used ([[Sighting]], [[The eight kinds of Sighting]]).

The grouping itself teaches what the Game is made of. Intention, promise, relationship, exchange, perception, and authority — and nothing else. There is no kind for status, no kind for approval, and no kind that lets one person write on another's behalf without a recorded delegation. What a record cannot say, a body cannot do ([[Commitment]], [[Delegation]], [[Consent]]).

Three of the kinds carry particular weight. **Commit** is the word given. **Witness** is the second voice that vouches, and the line that settles which of two contradictory accounts holds in a scope. **Supersede** is how anything is ever corrected — by a new line standing over the old, never by the old one vanishing ([[Witness]], [[The Record]], [[Every Defect Is A Gift]]).

# The 12 Stages of the Sovereign's Path

*The archetypal arc of a lifetime's walking, each stage naming what the Sovereign is becoming rather than what they are doing.*

In order, with the verb each carries: **Discovery** *(receive)* · **Threshold** *(awaken)* · **Orientation** *(remember)* · **Gather** *(call)* · **First 90 Minutes** *(begin)* · **Weekly Practice** *(run)* · **Pattern Encounter** *(encounter)* · **The 144 Gates** *(initiate)* · **Convergence** *(federate)* · **Covenant and Jubilee** *(renew)* · **The Sacred Ascent** *(ascend)* · **The Eternal Divine Play** *(co-create)*.

The first five are a door and a beginning: a link received, a choice made between worlds, the cosmic context taken in, two or three trusted people called, and a first gathering of ninety minutes in which the Circle is consecrated, the Quest chosen, the rhythm set, and each person names who they will invite next. The middle three are the work: the weekly Turn run until the Way is lived rather than studied, the Pattern met as living answer when real questions arise, and then the long initiatory walk of [[The 144 Gates of Initiation]]. The last four widen past a single life — [[Convergence]], where Circles federate and the Pillars become operational; Covenant and Jubilee; [[The Sacred Ascent (as tier)|the Sacred Ascent]]; and [[The Eternal Divine Play]].

The stages are load-bearing because they are what makes *where am I* answerable. Each opens a different volume at the phase in which it begins to serve, which is the whole of the just-in-time discipline applied to a corpus far too large to read at once. They are also what let a Cell tell the difference between a person who needs the Quick Start and one who needs a Pattern.

They are held provisionally and say so. How many stages, what they are named, where one ends and the next begins — all open for refinement as the map of the walk resolves. [[The Walk is permissive]]: some walk in days what others walk in seasons, and some return to a stage many times before passing through.

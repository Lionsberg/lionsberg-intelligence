# Control Of Information

*The third disease: opacity used as power — the few who can see deciding for the many who cannot.*

A Body's real constitution is who knows what. Where reasoning is visible, authority must persuade; where it is not, authority need only announce. So the disease rarely begins with a lie. It begins with a delay — the decision explained later, the numbers shared when they are cleaner, the context that would take too long to give — and each delay teaches the Body that understanding is something granted rather than owed.

The tells are ordinary. Decisions arrive without their reasons. Questions about *why* are answered with *who decided*. Some members can reconstruct how the Body actually works and others cannot. The Record thins.

[[Requirement Of Transparency]] is the direct refusal: reasoning is visible; power is accountable. The instruments are plain — a record from every call in text anyone in the Cell can read and correct, a decision log carrying the why, numbers that mean the same thing everywhere, and the Body's economics fully in the open.

The boundary is drawn on a different axis than most bodies draw it. *Decisions* are transparent; *persons* are not. [[Chatham House]] lets the idea travel while the source stays behind. [[Elision]] lets a line be hidden on purpose and on the record, asked for before the record is shared, and only for one's own words or what was said about one — no one edits another. Protecting a person is not opacity. Protecting a decision from scrutiny is.

The inverted form of the disease is worth naming too: surveillance, which is control of information about people rather than for them. The Pattern refuses it in both directions — no node may surveil another, and no node may coerce another.

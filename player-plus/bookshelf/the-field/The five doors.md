# The five doors

*Ask for guidance · offer a submittal · log an issue · record a decision · learn from the gap — the fixed set of moves anyone may make against the record of the whole.*

A body that keeps a shared record has to answer a question most organisations answer badly: *what may an ordinary person do to it?* The usual answers are "raise it with your manager" and "put it in the suggestion box" — which are not moves, because neither has a defined outcome. The five doors are the whole list, and each one is a real door with something on the other side.

**Ask for guidance.** Anyone may ask the whole — a [[Request For Guidance]] — and receive an answer that is recorded, so the next person who asks finds it already there ([[Guide Cells]] · [[Guidance]]).

**Offer a submittal.** What hangs on the wall is changed by [[Proposal|proposal]], never by silent edit. Anyone may propose; the change is visible, dated, and attributable ([[Consent-Based Decision-Making]] · [[Change log]]).

**Log an issue.** Anything that blocks the work is written where it can be seen and solved rather than absorbed ([[Issue]] · [[The Total Nested Holarchy of Issues]] · [[Sighting]]).

**Record a decision.** What was decided, and *why*, so that nothing is re-litigated by accident and a newcomer can find the reasoning rather than only the rule ([[Decision Records]] · [[The Decision]]).

**Learn from the gap.** The distance between what was said and what was done is examined, not hidden; every defect is a gift, and the improving itself is improved ([[The gap]] · [[Every Defect Is A Gift]] · [[Retrospective]] · [[Look-back]]).

Naming them as a set — five, fixed, open to anyone — is what makes them load-bearing. It means no ordinary member ever has to wonder whether they are allowed; it means every irregular path *into* the record is visible as irregular; and it means the same five exist at every scale, so that a Cell's small room and The One Room have the same doors in the same places ([[The One Room]] · [[Holarchy]]).

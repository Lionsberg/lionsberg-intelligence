# Peace

*The harmony that arises when [[Truth]] is protected, [[Justice]] is upheld, and [[Right Relationship]] is restored — a consequence, never an imposition.*

Peace is not the absence of conflict. It is a positive condition with conditions of its own, and the Pillar states them as a chain: peace follows from justice, truth, and right relationship. Where those are missing, what looks like peace is quiet — and quiet is what domination produces on its way to being called order.

The fuller word for it is *shalom*: not merely the cessation of trouble but completeness, soundness, and well-being — within oneself, with others, with the living world, and with the Source. If the single design principle of the new world is [[Love]], shalom is what that love looks like once it has worked its way outward into arrangements.

Three consequences follow, and they are the practical content. Peace is **not passive** — it is protected, which is why guardianship exists at all, and why the same tradition that refuses domination trains people to stand. Peace is **not enforced** — it is embodied, which is why the work begins with inner security and self-mastery rather than with a force structure. And peace is **emergent from right relationship**, which means the reliable way to produce it is not to suppress conflict but to transform it: name it, hear it, repair what was broken, restore the relationship.

So the peaceable body is not the one with nothing to argue about. It is the one that can hold a real disagreement without anyone being crushed or leaving — which requires that [[Conflict Transformation]] be a practised craft, that objections be welcomed as information, and that leaving remain free. A silence that no one may break is the counterfeit.

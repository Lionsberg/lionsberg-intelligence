# The LIØNSBERG Corpus

*Everything that has been written down — every story, pattern, card, lexicon entry, play, guidebook and working file by which this points at what it is pointing at. A living body, never finished.*

The word is doing a specific job. The **Codex** is the structured, bound part; the **Library** is where things live and are drawn from; the **Corpus** is the totality, including the drafts, the working files, the notes that never became chapters, and every person's own chapter woven in as they walk ([[The Sacred Codex]], [[The Living Library]], [[Foundational Literature]], [[All Scales Of Chapters]]).

What it is not is as important. It is **not the reality it points at** — the map is not the ground, and the story that can be told is not the Story ([[Essences, Renderings, And Ineffable Referents]], [[Do Not Concretize the Absolute]]). And it is **not doctrine**: every entry is a current best articulation, held humbly, ready to be refined ([[Current Best Articulation]], [[The Foundational Truth]]).

It grows by three moves that anyone walking may make. **Fill what is lacking** — what is missing, add. **Improve what remains** — what is half-said, refine. **Remove what does not belong** — what has drifted or gone false, prune ([[The Triadic Discipline]], [[Prune]], [[Weave, do not delete]]). The eye that sees the flaw is the eye that fixes it, which is why a defect found here is a gift rather than an accusation ([[Every Defect Is A Gift]], [[Second reader]]).

There is a ladder under every line of it, and knowing the ladder is most of reading it well: the written articulation falls short of the understanding held by those who walked and learned; the understanding falls short of the reality. The labour of closing those two gaps is not preparation for the work — it is part of the work ([[Current Best Understanding]], [[The Sacred Ascent]], [[Progressive canonization]]).

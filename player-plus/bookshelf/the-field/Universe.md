# Universe

*The total manifest order — all that has taken form, at every scale, held as one.*

The Universe is what the sciences survey and more than they have illuminated: the observable portion is the lit porch of a house whose extent remains dark. Metaphysically, the Universe is not a container of dead objects; it is one [[Living System]] — the body of manifestation, emanating from, existing in, and returning to [[ØNE]], every part interexistent with every other.

Three claims here do real work. First, the [[Creator's Intent]] for the Universe is the ultimate context of every smaller intent: no local aim escapes it, which is why [[The Goal]] is received rather than chosen. Second, the Universe's authority transcends every temporal authority — a claim that limits every human [[Authority]] from above and gives [[Sovereignty]] somewhere to stand. Third, its daily gift — the income of [[Energy]] from sun and cosmos that powers all life — is named as the original commons, the endowment every economy actually runs on. That last one is not poetry; it is the ontological root under [[Commons]] and under the argument that extraction is theft from a gift.

The fourth claim is epistemic: because no mind holds the Universe whole, we inhabit it through renderings. Every cosmology, every physics, every sacred story, and the LIØNSBERG telling itself is a [[Current Best Articulation]] of one Universe that exceeds them all.

In play the Universe is rarely operative at the table; it is the outermost frame that keeps the table honest. A Cell meets it when it notices that its Quest sits inside something it did not design and cannot own.

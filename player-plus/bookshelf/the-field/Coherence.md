# Coherence

*How well the many vibrations of a whole sing as one — the measure of health at every scale.*

A cell is healthy when its processes cohere. A person, when heart, mind, body, and action sound one note. A [[Circle]], when its sovereign members move as one body without ceasing to be themselves. A civilization, when its countless lives serve one [[The Goal]] in countless ways.

**Coherence is never uniformity.** A choir of identical notes is not harmony; it is monotone. True coherence is diversity in resonance: many distinct frequencies, one music. This is the difference between a Body that has agreed and a Body that has been flattened, and it is why the Way produces more difference as it matures rather than less.

**Coherence is a gradient, not a state.** Every whole stands somewhere between noise and harmony, and every act moves it — toward the [[Ideal]] at its centre or away from it. The compass question is at bottom a coherence reading: *does this move make the whole more harmonious with [[ØNE]], or less?*

It is load-bearing as a measure because it is available where numbers are not. A Circle can read its own coherence in an evening; nothing else it can measure tells it as much about whether it will still exist in a year. In the field it is read as aliveness and as people becoming more themselves ([[Associative And Dissociative Energy]]).

And it is how transformation actually spreads. Small wholes reaching high coherence inside a chaotic surrounding become [[Islands of Coherence]] — and islands spread not by conquering the chaos but by sounding a note clear enough that what is ready to resonate does.

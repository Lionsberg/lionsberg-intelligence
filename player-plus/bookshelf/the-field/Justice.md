# Justice
*[[Right Relationship]] restored — love in action, not retribution.*

Justice is the principle that orders the moral, social, and political dimensions of relationship so that all are treated with dignity, respect, and impartiality. At its core it is about creating and maintaining harmony within the web of existence: recognising interdependence, and ensuring that each part of the whole is treated with dignity, respect, and [[Love]].

The distinguishing move here is that justice restores rather than punishes. Harm is real, and so is accountability; but the aim of a just response is a relationship made whole again, not a debt extracted. That reframing is what makes [[Conflict Transformation]] a first instrument rather than a soft alternative, and it is why the escalation ladder in a body runs through mending before it runs through sanction.

It is load-bearing at two scales. Inside a body, justice is what makes [[Consent]] meaningful: an agreement freely entered by parties who are not equally protected is not freely entered. Outside, it is the test of whether what a body builds is worth building — a system that produces abundance for its members and harm beyond its membrane has not produced justice, it has exported its costs.

Justice also has an ordinary weekly shape. It looks like a body that says out loud what it will do, does it, and names the gap without blame; that distributes what it gathers by an order agreed in advance; and that gives everyone at the table the same voice. It impels us to do the wise right thing in all circumstances for all concerned — including those with no seat, which is why a [[Cell]] keeps seats for the living systems, for future generations, and for the Whole.

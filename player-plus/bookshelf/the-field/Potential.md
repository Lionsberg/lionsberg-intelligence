# Potential

*Unrealized latent possibility, capability, capacity — something like the total set of all that could be realized.*

Potential is the word for what is there and not yet here. A seed holds a forest; a person holds a life they have not lived; a gathering holds a body it has not yet become. None of it is guaranteed and none of it is nothing. Potential is real — an actual feature of what exists, not a hopeful way of speaking about an absence.

That has a hard consequence, and it is the reason the word is load-bearing. If what could be realized is real, then leaving it unrealized is a loss and not merely a non-gain. [[Waste]] is not neutral; a capacity that never comes out is a thing the world does not get, and the arithmetic of the Goal runs on the difference ([[Throughput]] · [[The Goal]]). The whole system is built to produce that difference — to raise the realized fraction of what is latent in [[All]].

The realizing runs bottom-up. Potential is not released by an institution deciding to release it; it comes out of particular people, one at a time, doing the specific thing only they can do, in the company of others who need them to do it ([[Inexhaustible Individuals]] · [[Genius]] · [[Capability]] · [[Capacity]]). This is why the Game's instruments are shaped the way they are: a [[Quest]] big enough to require what a person has not yet used, a [[Cell]] small enough that their absence is noticed, and a [[The Zone Of Optimum Development|zone]] pitched so that the stretch is real and survivable.

There is a shadow side, and it is worth naming plainly. Potential can be used as a rod — a standard nobody ever meets, held over a life to keep it dissatisfied. The true frame is the opposite: potential is what is being drawn out with help and delight, at the pace a living thing actually grows, not a debt owed. [[Becoming]] is the ordinary word for the process, and it has no finish line.

# The future triad — Best · Worst · Actual

*Three futures held together: the highest that could be, the lowest that could be, and the one actually being made. A compass needs two poles and a needle.*

Most visions name only one future, and that is why most visions do not navigate. A single bright image gives a direction but no sense of distance, no scale, and no way to tell whether today went well. The Game holds three, and insists that all three be looked at.

**The Best Possible Future** is the top of the range: what love would build if we were of one highest mind ([[Best Possible Future]] · [[Heaven On Earth]]). **The Worst** is the floor, and it asks to be stared into rather than glanced at — what were humanity's darkest passages, what patterns produced them, and what would those patterns become with more energy and more power behind them ([[Hell On Earth]])? The discipline is not morbid. A person who cannot describe the bottom cannot recognise the first few steps toward it, in a society or in themselves.

**The Actual Future** is the third and the only one that will happen. Today we are somewhere between the two poles, and from here either can be reached. Which arrives *depends* — on you, on us, on All — and whichever it is, we will have been the ones who co-created it. That is the whole weight of the triad: it converts a vision into responsibility. The question stops being *what will the future be* and becomes *how do we orient and align ourselves, individually and together, so that we navigate away from one pole and toward the other* ([[Navigation]] · [[Alignment]] · [[Aligned Will]]).

Held as three, the triad is a working instrument. The Best sets the aim; the Worst calibrates the stakes and names the failure modes worth guarding against ([[The Five Collapse Scenarios]] · [[Structural Immunity]]); the Actual is what gets measured, every week, against both ([[The Score]] · [[The gap]]).

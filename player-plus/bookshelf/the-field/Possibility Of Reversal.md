# Possibility Of Reversal

*The sixth Recognition: every significant decision can be revisited. Nothing is settled beyond the reach of those who will live with it.*

A decision that cannot be revisited has stopped being a decision and become a wall. The Recognition does not say decisions are provisional in practice — a Body that reopens everything weekly decides nothing — it says the *door* is never bolted. Every decision carries a review date. Consent is not forever.

It is the governance form of [[Current Best Understanding]]. The *why* is invariant; every *how* is revisable; nothing is called final. The same posture that keeps a Pattern from ossifying keeps an answer from becoming law by mere age. It is also the structural answer to [[Ideological Capture]] and to dogmatic ossification: an idea that cannot be revisited has become the purpose it was meant to serve.

Reversal needs three things to be real. The reasoning must have been recorded, or there is nothing to revisit ([[Requirement Of Transparency]] · [[Decision Records]]). There must be a way to raise it that does not require anyone's permission ([[Proposal]] · [[Issue]] · [[Stop]]). And there must be a rhythm that gathers such raisings — in the Game, proposals travel up on the sheet and are gathered at each turning, and the rules and cards change by [[The edition]].

Two limits are stated rather than argued. The Recognitions themselves are not amendable, and purpose is protected from being amended away or bought ([[Constitutional Protection Of Purpose]]). And a soak before binding: a change carries its why and its source and rests before it takes effect, so that reversal is a considered motion rather than the last voice in the room.

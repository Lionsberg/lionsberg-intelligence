# Collapse

*The falling apart of the old arrangement — held always with its twin, ascension, at the single threshold where both are happening at once.*

One world is dying. Another is being born. They are not two events but one, and we are living inside the nexus. That pairing is the concept: collapse alone is a forecast, and forecasts produce spectators. Collapse held together with what is rising is a situation, and situations can be worked.

The old order's unravelling is described as over-determined rather than engineered by any one hand — by the weight of its own contradictions, by the withdrawal of the attention and consent it fed on, and by what is being built alongside it. That reading matters practically: it means the work is not to push the old over. It is to hospice what is dying, build the new next to it so that everyone has somewhere to go, and hold the ground where the two overlap. [[Islands of Coherence]] is the name for where the new forms while the old comes apart, and the islands network.

Collapse is load-bearing because it sets the clock without setting a date. It is why the Game is organised around a window rather than around a plan of indefinite length, why [[The First Three Percent]] is the near-term ask rather than everyone, and why [[Honesty about time]] is a discipline: say you are behind rather than move the marker. It is equally why the corpus refuses collapse-worship. A movement that needs the collapse to be worse in order to be right has already been captured by it.

In the Game a player meets collapse in specific, local form — a service that has stopped working, an institution that no longer answers, a neighbour with nowhere to turn. That is the material of a [[Quest]]. In the System it is the argument for redundancy and [[Anti-Fragility]]: a body whose functioning depends on the old arrangement continuing has not yet crossed.

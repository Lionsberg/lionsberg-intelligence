# Integrity

*Coherence between inner truth, outer action, and alignment — being one thing, all the way through.*

Integrity is wholeness. The word is the same as in a structure that has not cracked: the parts hold together, and load applied at one point does not find a hidden seam. In a person it means what is believed, what is said, and what is done are the same thing, and stay the same whether or not anyone is watching.

It is the quality the whole field is built to be able to rely on. [[Trust]] is earned through kept promises, and a promise is only as good as the coherence of the one who made it. Guardians are chosen for integrity rather than might, because strength in someone who is one thing in public and another in private is not protection. And the reason authority can be distributed this widely, with no centre enforcing anything, is that the arrangement assumes people who will do what they said when nobody would know the difference.

The everyday form is deliberately unheroic: *say what you will do; do what you said; learn from the gap.* The gap is expected. What integrity requires is not a perfect record but an honest one — saying plainly that the thing was not done, early rather than late, without decoration. A body that keeps that record honestly can be trusted with anything; a body that dresses its gaps cannot be trusted with anything at all, including its own plans.

Its failure is rarely dramatic. It goes in small increments: a commitment made to be agreeable, a number rounded in the right direction, a conversation had about someone rather than with them. The counter-discipline is equally small — speak as if the absent person could read the transcript, and close the gap the day it appears.

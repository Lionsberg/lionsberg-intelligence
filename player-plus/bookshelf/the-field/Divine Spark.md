# Divine Spark

*The indwelling fragment of [[ØNE]] within each being, waiting to be awakened and realized.*

The Divine Spark is [[Immanence]] at the scale of one life. The Story states its origin directly: the Many pouring forth from the Fount were each one of the ones of ØNE, a fragment of ØNE experiencing itself through them. [[The Veil of Finitude and Forgetting]] explains why it is not obvious — the veil is what makes creaturehood possible — and adds the line that matters most here: those who wander far accumulate veils until the original spark seems to be gone. *It is never gone. It is veiled.*

That "never gone" is load-bearing, and it does more work than any argument in the governance pages. It is the ground of [[Dignity]] that cannot be forfeited, of [[Sovereignty Of Consciousness]] as a recognition rather than a grant, of [[Redemption]] as always possible, and of a door that never closes on anyone who left. It is also why the canon can say *something is deeply wrong, and it is not you* to a stranger it has never met.

It sets the posture of teaching, too. If the spark is already present, then formation is drawing out rather than filling up: [[Genius]] is the unique gift each being carries, [[Education As Transformation]] forms people rather than installing them, and the [[Guide]] helps each player find the next stretch of their own path rather than a standard curriculum. A whole curriculum at the door is a wall; one lesson when you are about to need it is a door.

In play it is met as a practice of seeing: treating the person at the table as carrying something that is theirs and real, before they have demonstrated anything.

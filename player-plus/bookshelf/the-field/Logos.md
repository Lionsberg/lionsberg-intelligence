# Logos

*The Divine Pattern, structure, reason and order of Reality — the speaking of [[ØNE]] by which the cosmos has intelligible form; what [[Pattern]] is a pattern of.*

Logos is the claim that Reality is intelligible, and intelligible in a particular direction. It is the intrinsic and embodied logic of a thing, and at the largest scale the intrinsic logic of the universe, one with the logic of its Generative Source. The canon calls it the articulated Word that brings forth that which is Good — not mere order, but ordering toward [[Goodness]].

Everything that claims to be a pattern in this Field is measured against it. [[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language]] holds that Patterns are discovered, not invented, precisely because there is something to discover: the Patterns converged on by Mondragon, the Iroquois Confederacy, the Benedictines, the Quakers and open source are read as the same Logos found from different sides. [[Alignment]] means conformity with [[Spirit]] and Logos; [[Distortion]] is pattern misaligned with it; [[Inversion]] is that misalignment turned parasitic. Without Logos there is no standard by which a pattern could be wrong, and a pattern that cannot be wrong is only a habit with authority.

It is also the guard on [[Transmission]]. The energetic layer of the Pattern Language names *testing transmission against the Logos* as the discipline that separates reception from capture: what arrives from beyond the self is weighed against the known order rather than obeyed because it arrived. The same discipline governs [[Revelation]] and [[Prophecy]].

In the System, Logos sits on the second rung of [[Levels of Abstraction]], directly beneath [[Source]] and above [[Energy]] — the ordering that comes before anything is energised into form. In the Pattern Language's word-tools, *Sachchidananda · Logos · Creator's Intent* are named as three faces of ØNE made precise by three tongues, none of them the truth itself.

In play, Logos is the reason a Cell can ask whether a way of working is *right* rather than merely preferred, and the reason [[Every Defect Is A Gift]]: a miss reveals where the working pattern and the real order diverge.

# Pillar III — Sacred Economy and CurrentSee

*The third of the twelve spheres: the flow of value as sacred energy for regeneration and abundance, in place of extraction.*

A Pillar is a partition of the whole work of civilization, not a department of it. This one holds everything to do with how value is made, named, moved, and shared: what a thing is worth, who gets paid first, what is capped, what is held in common, and what the surplus is for. Its one sentence is *all value flows from the ØNE* — which is a claim about origin before it is a claim about economics, and which is why the partition is called sacred and not merely fair.

The Pillar's work is a reversal. Where the old arrangement took value from the edges and piled it at the centre, [[Economy]] here is the orchestration of flow: value moves toward the work that serves life, and what accumulates anywhere is a symptom to be examined. [[Scarcity]] is treated as manufactured rather than given; [[Abundance]] as the regenerative default under [[Creator's Intent]]; [[Usury]] and [[Money]] as named instruments of the old order rather than neutral tools. *CurrentSee* is the Pillar's own coinage — currency seen, the current made visible — and it points at the same discipline the [[The waterfall|waterfall]] enforces: everything visible, order before amount.

What a Cell actually touches of this Pillar in its first season is small and concrete: [[The Offering]] at the table each week, [[Voluntary Tithing]] inward at each turning, and an honest account of [[TimeEnergy]] given. The instruments above that — [[LUV]], [[Proof-of-contribution]], [[Compressed Pay Ratios]], the currency's ledger — are drafts and named intentions, not finished machinery. The Pillar is strongest where it says what must never happen (capital capturing purpose, the commons enclosed) and thinnest where it must say exactly how a unit is calculated. Both facts belong on the page.

# Choose a Quest

*Move three: one real thing, for one season, that your Cell can do together and point to afterward. Real, near, and yours.*

Choosing is a move and not a wish. A Cell without a Quest is a discussion group; a Cell with a Quest it cannot finish in a season has a plan and not yet a Quest. The three tests are exact: *real* — it changes something you can see; *near* — within reach with a few hours a week; *yours* — chosen at your table, not handed down. One Quest at a time is usual; two is fine if the table can carry them.

It is load-bearing because it is where the Goal touches the ground. [[The one question]] is asked of the candidate work, and the answer is written on [[The Questcard]] as the *why*: what will be a little more like Heaven when this is done. If the youngest person at the table cannot say why, the Quest is not tied to anything yet. This is also where a table's work joins the body's work — Quests stack up and align to produce [[Throughput]] of the Goal, and [[Stacking Up And Aligning]] is how that is done without anyone commanding it.

How it is played. Surface candidates from the weave; test each against the one question and the three words; decide by consent, not by vote, so that a [[Meaningful Objection]] can still stop a bad choice. Write the Quest in one sentence a child can repeat. Write *done when* so that two people could not argue about it at the turning. Leave *our part of the bigger plan* blank until a sentence reaches you from your home. Then name who holds it, and let the week's commitments begin.

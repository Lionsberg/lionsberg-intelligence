# Bioregion

*A life-place: a continuous territory where the natural systems and the cultures grown out of them are inseparable — the scale at which place meets people.*

A bioregion is larger than any single [[Watershed]] and smaller than a continent. It is distinct from a political unit, which was imposed on the land, and distinct from an [[Ecoregion]], which is a finer-grained scientific classification. It is the integrative scale: where soil and water, plant and creature, [[The Sovereign]] and [[Circle]], language and song are most directly held together. Roughly one hundred fifty to two hundred of them across the Earth, depending on where the cultural-ecological lines are drawn. Cascadia is one. The Klamath-Siskiyou is one. Many indigenous nations were already organized at this scale.

It is load-bearing because it is a rung of the ladder by which the Whole is composed without a centre. [[Community]] bodies federate into Ecoregions, which share an ecological substrate; Ecoregions federate into Bioregions, which integrate several ecologies and the cultures shaped by them; Bioregions nest onward toward the planet. Each Bioregion may contain several Ecoregions; each Ecoregion many Communities; each Community anchored by its own [[The 300]]. This is what [[Holofractal Omnifederation]] looks like when it touches actual ground.

In play, the bioregion carries the place-based half of sovereignty while a [[Guild]] carries the craft-based half — rooted in a specific land, rooted in a specific practice across many lands. Both are needed; neither is sufficient. At the bioregional scale sits the coordinated stewardship no single Community can do alone: the watersheds, the soils, the relatives of the land, the long arcs of place-care.

The invitation is plain and is the whole practice: return to the land that holds you. Learn its watersheds, its winds, its seasons, its kin. Practise [[The Way]] where you actually live. A [[Cell]] can begin this in its first season without permission from anyone, and the knowledge it gathers is the ground every later federation stands on.

# The Codex Is Never Finished

*A living seed rather than a finished text. The Pattern does not change; the words used to render it keep sharpening — which is why this is stewarded rather than preserved.*

The distinction the page turns on is between two things easily confused. **Preservation** is what dead things receive: keep it exactly, guard it from change, treat alteration as damage. **Stewardship** is what living things require: tend it, prune it, feed it, expect it to be different next year ([[Stewardship]], [[Prune]], [[Compost]]).

So the closing claim is deliberate. The canonical articulation today is not the canonical articulation in five years; it will be sharper, nearer the thing it points at, and better recognised across more kinds of reader ([[Current Best Articulation]], [[Progressive Illumination]], [[Cross-Cultural Pattern Translation]]).

What keeps that from being drift is the loop. Insight arrives from practice — each Circle, each quest, each season — and flows back; the review is periodic, in every direction, against actual practice, by the people living it; drift is detected and corrected rather than merely deplored ([[OmniSpection]], [[Retrospective]], [[Every Defect Is A Gift]], [[The Living-Learning Architecture]]).

The one thing held still is the distinction itself: that the Pattern is not what is being edited. Whether that distinction can be maintained in practice — whether anyone can tell a sharpened rendering from a changed Pattern — is the open question underneath the whole discipline, and the corpus does not answer it ([[Pattern]], [[The Foundational Truth]], [[Semantic Capture]]).

There is no final edition. The only closing is a play without end, which is no closing at all ([[The Eternal Divine Play]], [[The Loop That Has No End]], [[Work In Progress]]).

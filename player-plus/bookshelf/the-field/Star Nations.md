# Star Nations

*Non-human intelligence treated as part of the field rather than as a question deferred — peoples, councils, and alliances beyond this world.*

Named in the corpus's cosmic section alongside cosmic federation and cosmic councils and alliances, and not written out. What the name carries is a stance rather than a claim about particular visitors: if the cosmos is populated, then it is populated by *peoples* — sovereign bodies with their own stories, ranging as human beings range from wisdom to predation — and not by a single undifferentiated other. The plural is the whole of the teaching. There is no *them*.

The stance is load-bearing for two reasons. It keeps the Pattern consistent: [[Sovereignty At Every Scale]] and [[Holofractal Omnifederation]] do not stop at the atmosphere, and the nesting of scales in this corpus runs deliberately past the planet to the star system, the galaxy, and the cosmos. And it sets the terms of any encounter in advance — [[Consent]], [[Reciprocity]], and refusal of subjugation in either direction — which is what the named but unwritten treaty of non-subjugation points at.

It carries the heaviest evidentiary warning in this field. It is recorded because the corpus records it, and because a frame that quietly omits what a corpus asserts is not an honest index. It is not recorded as knowledge. [[We Do Not Know]] applies with full force, and [[Discerning Between Benevolent And Malevolent Intelligences]] is the only part of this register the corpus treats as practice rather than speculation.

Nothing in the Game requires it, and no Cell should be asked to hold it. A person may play a full season, keep their word, and pass the Flame while regarding this page as an open question or as nothing at all.

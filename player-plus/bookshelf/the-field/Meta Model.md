# Meta Model

*The model of models — the top of the nested order of models, and the programme of building one that starts from the whole rather than from the parts.*

Every model is a simplification made for a purpose, and every discipline has its own. A meta model is the model that would contain and order them: not a bigger model of one thing, but a frame in which the physicist's, the ecologist's, the economist's and the mystic's models can be placed in relation to each other rather than talking past one another ([[Meta]] · [[Holarchy]] · [[System of Systems]] · [[Levels of Abstraction]]).

The construction order is stated plainly, and the order is the claim. **It must start at [[ØNE]]** — the one thing, ultimate reality. **Then advance to [[Reality]]** as it emerges from that, understood as one living superorganism. **Then map that reality into [[SpaceTime]]**, where predictions can actually be made and tested ([[Living System]] · [[Cosmology]] · [[Science]] · [[Metaphysics]]).

That is the reverse of the usual direction. Ordinary modelling begins with measurements and builds upward, and never arrives at the whole, because a whole is not a sum. Beginning at the top and descending keeps the parts placed within something rather than hoping they will add up ([[Whole]] · [[Perception]] · [[Higher Order Functional Unity]]).

The stated purpose is liberation rather than description: a step toward interacting with and co-creatively influencing the underlying reality, and not only our limited perceptual representations of it ([[Co-Creation]] · [[Divine Participation]] · [[Non-Local First, Local Follows]]).

Held honestly, it is a direction and not a possession. Any meta model anyone writes is itself a rendering by finite minds, and the moment one is treated as the thing it models, the whole exercise has inverted ([[Essences, Renderings, And Ineffable Referents]] · [[Do Not Concretize the Absolute]] · [[Current Best Understanding]] · [[We Do Not Know]]).

# Private Ecclesiastical Voluntary Membership Association

*One legal vessel a sovereign body may choose when it meets the old world — named, and not yet described.*

The name states its four claims. **Private**: the association is not a public accommodation, and its inner life is governed by its own agreements rather than by general regulation of commerce. **Ecclesiastical**: it is constituted on a spiritual foundation, which in many jurisdictions carries particular standing. **Voluntary**: membership is entered and left by consent. **Membership association**: it is a body of members, not an enterprise with owners and customers.

It matters as an option rather than a doctrine. A body of sovereigns eventually meets the old world — a bank account, a lease, a liability, a gift, a government — and must choose a legal form to meet it with. The frame here is a ladder of tiers beginning at **Tier 0**, no formal structure at all, where most transformational work happens and many bodies thrive indefinitely. A vessel is taken on only when a real need appears, and then the form is chosen to protect purpose, membership, and commons rather than to maximize anything.

The PEVMA sits alongside [[FSx]] as the two named vessels: the association form where the body is chiefly a membership community, and the fair-shares company form where it is chiefly an enterprise. Both aim at the same structural outcome — a body that cannot be bought, cashed out, or captured.

In play, most [[Cell]]s should not incorporate anything, and that is said plainly here. This page exists so that a body which genuinely needs a vessel knows one of the names to research, with counsel, in its own jurisdiction.

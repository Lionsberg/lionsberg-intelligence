# Cosmology

*The understanding of the origin, structure, and destiny of the [[Universe]] and our place within it.*

Cosmology is the account a people gives of where everything came from, how it is arranged, and where it is going. The canon holds that every flourishing civilization has one — an overarching story unifying all domains of life within a coherent metaphysical, ethical, and cosmological frame — and that losing it is not a neutral event. Pillar I's diagnosis of the old world is exactly this loss, followed by existential confusion, systemic incoherence, and collapse.

In this Field the cosmology is told as [[The Sacred Arc]]: twelve movements from [[ØNE]] through the first turning, [[ØNE Body]], [[The Veil of Finitude and Forgetting]], [[The Fall]], [[Awakening]], [[The Sacred War]], [[Liberation and Exodus]], [[The Great ReMembering]], [[Embodying the Way]], [[Co-Creating Heaven]], and [[The Eternal Divine Play]]. That is a cosmology in narrative form rather than in propositions — which the canon treats as the honest form, since [[Consciousness]] organises experience as story.

It is load-bearing because it answers *where am I in this* for a person who has just arrived. A player is told, at the door, that the Story is not finished, that they are in it, and that what they do next is a line of it. No amount of governance design does that work.

The canon holds it open rather than closed. Every cosmology — every physics, every sacred story, and this telling itself — is a [[Current Best Articulation]] of one Universe that exceeds them all. [[Cosmos Is Not Human-Centric]] is stated as a working correction, and the material on other intelligences is carried with explicit discernment rather than credulity.

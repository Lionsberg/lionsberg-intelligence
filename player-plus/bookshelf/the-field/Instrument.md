# Instrument

*The thing actually in hand — a calibrated tool that extends perception or action toward [[Truth]] and effectiveness.*

An instrument is the third layer of the field, and the most concrete. A [[Pattern]] says what is generally true; a [[Play]] says what a group does about it on a given occasion; an instrument is the card, the sheet, the log, the notebook you pick up to do it. Without the first two an instrument is busywork. Without the third the first two never touch the ground.

The instruments a Cell needs in its first season are few and fit on paper: the card that says who we are, the [[Field of Agreements]] read at the door, the table of activities, the log of what is blocking them, [[The Questcard]] with its *done when* line, [[The Record]], and the log of what was decided and why. Seven cards. Everything else in the toolkit is optional and arrives when the road needs it.

Two disciplines keep instruments from becoming masters. The first is that every one of them degrades to plain text and shell: a hand copy on any paper counts exactly as much as a printed one, and a person with a notebook is a complete player. The second is that an instrument is calibrated — it is checked against reality and corrected, and when it stops serving the work it is retired rather than defended. A tool kept past its usefulness quietly becomes a ritual, and then a rule.

The word carries its older sense too: a trained human capacity is an instrument. The breath, attention, and composure a person has cultivated are as much part of what they bring to a hard moment as anything they are holding.

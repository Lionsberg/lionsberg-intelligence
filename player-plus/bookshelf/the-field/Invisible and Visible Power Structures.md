# Invisible and Visible Power Structures

*Power operates in layers of visibility: the public authorities that appear to govern, and the unaccountable arrangements that shape what those authorities can do. The pair is what makes either term mean anything.*

**Visible power** is what has a name, an address, and a press office: governments, corporations, media houses, religious institutions, large charities. It presents itself as accountable — through elections, markets, boards, donors — and much of the time it genuinely acts. **Invisible power** is everything that shapes outcomes without appearing in that account: the arrangements of finance and ownership, the coordination that happens outside any minute-taking room, the long-horizon interests that outlast any administration, the concentration of what can be known and by whom ([[Control Of Information]]).

The load-bearing claim is structural and modest, and it can be tested: **where the visible institution is the only thing examined, the examination will be incomplete.** An honest reading of any large outcome asks two questions rather than one — who decided, and what shaped what they could decide. This is not a claim that the visible layer is a puppet or that anyone is innocent. It is a claim about where to look.

It matters because of what it changes in a player's practice. Directing all energy at the visible layer produces campaigns that win the vote and lose the outcome; directing all suspicion at an invisible layer produces paranoia, which is the cheapest thing to induce in a population and the most reliably disorganising. The canon's answer to both is the same and is not confrontation: build what cannot be captured. A body that is [[Self-Governing, Self-Sustaining, and Self-Replicating]], whose record is open ([[Requirement Of Transparency]]) and whose decisions cannot concentrate ([[Structural Immunity]]), is not vulnerable to either layer in the way a movement with a headquarters is.

The discipline this page most needs is its own: name what can be shown, and hold the rest as question. Concealment is real, and the reading of concealment is exactly where a person is most easily deceived, including by themselves ([[Discernment]] · [[Navigating Towards Truth]]).

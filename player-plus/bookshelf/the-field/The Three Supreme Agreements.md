# The Three Supreme Agreements

*Love [[ØNE]]. Love One Another. Love [[All]]. — simple enough for a child, deep enough to organize a civilization.*

These sit above every other agreement a body makes. They are not procedures and they settle no particular case; they are the floor beneath the [[Field of Agreements]], the thing every other agreement must be consistent with, and the place to return when a decision is genuinely hard.

Their load-bearing work is to keep the system from becoming its own point. A governance can be perfectly consistent, consented to by everyone, and still cold — and a body whose supreme reference is its own rulebook will, over time, optimize for the rulebook. Naming three agreements that no procedure can outrank keeps [[Spirit Precedes Pattern]] operable: when an agreement and love collide, the body knows which one is provisional.

The ordering matters and is not a hierarchy of worth. [[ØNE]] first, because a body that loves itself first becomes an idol ([[Institutional Idolatry]], [[The Golden Calf]]). One another next, because the nearest neighbour is where love becomes testable. All last and widest, because a love that stops at the membrane is a faction. Together they are the older floor beneath even the [[Minimum standards of interoperability]]: help one another, and do no harm.

In play, a [[Cell]] meets them when forging its agreements — the canon's Play for that moment invokes them by name — and again whenever a hard decision has two defensible answers and no procedural way to choose.

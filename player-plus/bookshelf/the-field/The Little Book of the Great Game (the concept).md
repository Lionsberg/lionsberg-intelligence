# The Little Book of the Great Game (the concept)

*The floor text: enough to play, small enough to read aloud in one sitting, and complete enough that a table with nothing else can begin.*

The Little Book is the Game reduced to what must be the same at every table on Earth. It holds the rules at the table, the cards a Circle fills in and the one it receives, the shared clock, the twelve words, the way a season leaves the table, the way a person leaves, and the way the paper meets the wire. Its chapters are short on purpose: a book that cannot be read aloud in an evening will not reach the people the Game is for.

It is load-bearing because it is the transmission unit. [[The Great Game of LIØNSBERG]] has a full rulebook, the Playbook has the choreography of each [[Play]], [[The LIØNSBERG Operating System]] has the production discipline — but what travels hand to hand with [[The Flame]] is this. Its own claim is modest and exact: everything inside a season belongs to the Circle; only the few shared things are fixed, so that ten thousand tables are one game.

How it is used. Read it once with your people. Copy [[The Playcard]] by hand if you have no printer — a hand copy is a Playcard. Keep [[The Questcard]], [[The Circle's Scorecard]], and [[The Circle Sheet]] in a box. At each turning fill [[The Season Sheet]] and send it home. When something is wrong, [[The Way Out]]; when something is better, write it as a [[Proposal]] on line 12 and let [[The Way Up]] carry it. The book changes only by [[The edition]], at a turning, never mid-season.

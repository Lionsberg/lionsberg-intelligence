# The Book Beneath the Book

*Six depths of one telling — a word, a breath, an evening, the Movements, the library, and the open ground. Enter at the depth you can receive; descend as far as you can go.*

The ladder, from the top: **one word**, which is the whole thing compressed to spirit and tone — what a child can receive and an elder can die holding ([[ØNE]]). **One breath** — the arc on a single page, a dozen lines and three instructions: read the Story, play the Game, pass the Flame ([[The Sacred Arc]], [[The Five Moves]]). **The telling** — an evening's read, which is what most people who come will actually read ([[The Story of LIØNSBERG]], [[Storytelling]]). **The Movements** — the same arc opened to chapter depth, each a whole territory ([[The One Book]], [[The turn (of a Movement), and its chapters]]). **The library** — the deep volumes standing behind every Movement ([[Foundational Literature]], [[The Living Library]], [[The LIØNSBERG Lexicon]], [[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language]]). And **the field and the threads** — every person's, people's and world's own telling, and the open ground where new threads grow ([[The Field (of the Book), and the law of no loss]], [[All Scales Of Chapters]]).

What makes it a ladder rather than a set of summaries is the claim that every level is **the same thing at a different resolution** — not less of it, but the same shape less finely rendered. If that holds, then reading only the surface is not reading a lesser version; it is receiving the whole at the grain a person can take ([[Progressive Illumination]], [[Levels of Abstraction]], [[Essences, Renderings, And Ineffable Referents]]).

Which gives the two sentences the ladder exists to make possible: nobody is turned away for reading only the surface, and nobody ever reaches the bottom ([[Conditions of Acceptance]], [[The grandmother test]], [[The Loop That Has No End]]).

It is not the same ladder as the seed's three tiers of seed, kit and canon; that one is about what is carried, and this one is about depth of telling. The two are not reconciled anywhere ([[The three tiers]], [[The Golden Seed]]).

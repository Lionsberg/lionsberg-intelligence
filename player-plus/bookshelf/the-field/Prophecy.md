# Prophecy

*The transmission of divine vision and direction from Spirit through an aligned vessel.*

Prophecy in this Field is the directional form of [[Revelation]]: not prediction, but sight given for the sake of a people, carried by someone whose alignment makes them able to carry it. The Lexicon's word is *vessel* — the emphasis falls on the conduit's clarity rather than on the person's status.

Its guards matter more than its definition, because prophecy is the single most capturable thing in a movement. The Pattern Language names *testing transmission against the [[Logos]]* as the required practice: what is received is weighed, never obeyed because it arrived. [[The False Prophet]] is named among the [[The Anti-Patterns|Anti-Patterns]] — liberation rhetoric in service of new domination. [[Conduit]] names the vessel's own discipline, and [[Distortion]] the failure. And the structure does its own share: [[Accountability]] holds that all authority is revocable and no role carries immunity, [[Transparency]] that reasoning is visible, [[Consent]] that nothing binds without a freely given yes. A prophet inside this architecture is answerable like anyone else.

The canon also gives prophecy a modest, everyday form that costs nothing to run: *every story becomes a prophecy of greater things to come.* A [[Story]] honestly told about what a Cell actually did carries more forward-sight than most utterance — [[Forward-Looking Story]] names why a shared story of where we are going matters, and [[Testimony]] is the form a story takes when it carries proof.

The canon's honest note: the Voice section names prophesying among several beautiful names with no body and no invoking Play. If nothing calls them, they should go.

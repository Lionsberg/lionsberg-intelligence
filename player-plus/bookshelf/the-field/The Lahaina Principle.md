# The Lahaina Principle

*Withholding a warning for fear of how people will react is itself the catastrophe. The ones whose office is to warn, refusing to warn, on the grounds that the warned might behave imperfectly.*

The name comes from a town that burned in 2023 while the sirens stayed silent, and from the reasoning reported afterwards: that sounding an alarm built for a different emergency might cause confusion, so it was better not to sound it. Whatever the full account turns out to be, the shape of the reasoning is what the name is for, and that shape recurs far from any fire.

Four things are wrong with it, and each generalises.

**It treats people as incompetent.** The premise is that ordinary people, given true information about their own danger, cannot handle it. The historical record does not support the premise; people given hard truth often rise, and people denied it cannot act at all ([[Dignity]], [[Sovereignty]], [[We The People]]).

**It puts the risk judgment in the hands of those least exposed.** Whoever decides that warning is not worth the disruption is generally not the one who bears the consequence of silence ([[Rights extend first to the weak; responsibilities first to the strong]], [[The Two-Stake Principle]], [[Omniconsideration]]).

**It produces the outcome it was trying to prevent.** Avoiding a small disorder purchases a large one. The arithmetic of that kind of caution is inverted ([[The Asymmetry of Consequences]], [[Possibility Of Reversal]]).

**It fails the duty regardless of intent.** The first obligation of anyone holding responsibility for others' safety is to tell them what is coming. Without the telling they cannot exercise their own judgment, and withholding it is therefore a withdrawal of their sovereignty, however kindly meant ([[Requirement Of Transparency]], [[Necessity Of Accountability]], [[Whistleblower Protection]]).

The corollary is the usable half, and it points inward before it points out. Where an institution is silent about an approaching harm, the silence itself is the thing to name. And where a person holds something others need to know and is tempted to keep it back because of how they might take it — that is the same move, at domestic scale, and it has the same name ([[Speaking Truth In Love]], [[Heads-up]], [[Having Difficult Conversations]], [[Bottom Line Up Front]]).

Say it without drama and without panic: clearly, early, with love, and let people decide for themselves.

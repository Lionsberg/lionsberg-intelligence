# The LIØNSBERG Operating System

*The mechanism underneath a group's own way of working: how it sees where it is going, plans backward from it, keeps a rhythm, keeps its word, decides, learns — and, on any day it chooses, works as one body with every other group.*

It is not a brand and not a doctrine. A group keeps its name, its culture, its humour, its art. What it takes up is a shape: eight parts — the shape, the plan, the rhythm, the word, deciding, one body, adopting it, and how this is different — carried on seven cards: who we are, [[Field of Agreements]], [[The activity table]], the constraint log, [[The Questcard]], [[The Record]], the decision log. Everything in it is a [[The Player|Player]] holding one card of the same shape, at every scale from a person to a world.

It is load-bearing because good people trying to do something that matters get stuck in the same three places: they cannot see the whole, they cannot keep their promises to one another reliably, and they cannot coordinate with the others doing the same work a valley or a continent away. Each of the three has a known remedy — [[Pull planning]] for the first, [[Commitment]] and [[The Record]] for the second, the [[Minimum standards of interoperability]] for the third — and the system is little more than those remedies kept in one rhythm.

It is run by adopting it: [[The first season]] writes the three things and the cards, holds [[The Heartbeat]] weekly, rolls [[The look-ahead]], and reads kept-as-a-share-of-made aloud. Use the cards as given and the standards are met without thinking about them. The steward teaches One Book, One Operating System, One Big Room at the top of every holon as a *teaching*, not an imposed rule, and holds that there is no separate operating-system book standing beside the One Book.

# Midwife a New Circle

*Helping the one you invited begin to gather — present if asked, answering without steering.*

Passing [[The Flame]] is not finished when someone says yes. The next thing that must happen is a first gathering, and the person holding a new flame usually has no idea how one goes. So the [[Carrier]] makes themselves available: present at that first evening if they are asked, answering the questions that come, showing rather than instructing ([[Pass The Flame]], [[Seal the Circle]]).

The discipline is in what is withheld. Answer without steering. Do not choose their name, their agreements, or their [[Quest]] for them; a body that was handed its aim has not yet done the one thing that makes it a body ([[Sovereignty]], [[Consent]]). The temptation to make the new Cell a branch — a chapter, an affiliate, an extension of your own good work — is the exact point at which propagation turns into scaling, and it is refused here ([[Federations vs Empires]], [[Centralization Of Power]]).

What the relationship is instead: their Cell is sovereign and their own; you are their [[Carrier]], and that never changes; your Cell is their first [[Home Cell]] until they have a nearer one; and you are now part of two bodies — the double helix by which the pattern replicates ([[Fibonacci Spread]], [[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]]). Within a week of receiving the seed, the new player is already naming the two or three they will carry it to.

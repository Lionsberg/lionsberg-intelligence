# Context · Internal Context · External Context

*Before deciding anything, a body situates itself in three parts: the general condition it finds itself in, what is inside its boundary, and what is outside it.*

"Where are we?" is the first question of navigation and the one most often skipped, because everyone assumes they already know. These three word-tools slow it down enough to be answered honestly.

**General context** is the condition of the world the body is acting into — in the canon's own reading: centuries of real progress whose solutions are now generating new classes of problem, an interacting complex of crises that no single actor can meet, and an enormous amount of sincere effort running in parallel without coordination or shared sight ([[The Meta Crisis]] · [[Silos No More]]). That reading is what makes a meta-scale response look necessary rather than grandiose ([[The Meta Project]]).

**Internal context** is the body's current state of being inside its own boundary — its people, capacity, agreements, health, and what it actually believes about itself. It is the ground of *who we are* ([[Identity]] · [[Capacity]] · [[Field of Agreements]]).

**External context** is everything outside that boundary that bears on the work: neighbours, conditions, constraints, the people already doing something adjacent ([[Boundary]] · [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]]).

Together the inner and outer readings are the two halves of *where we are* on the way to where we are going, and the canon states plainly that understanding both is the key to self-governance for any vessel a body is navigating ([[Sensemaking]] · [[Navigation]] · [[Governance]]).

The split earns its place because the two readings fail differently. A body that reads only its internal context builds beautifully and irrelevantly. A body that reads only its external context becomes reactive, shaped entirely by what is happening to it, and loses the thing it was for. The discipline is to take both readings, separately and on purpose, before the question of what to do is allowed to arise ([[Observe · Orient · Decide · Act · Learn]] · [[Sensing comes before understanding]]).

# The stages, and the pool discipline

*No claim in a plan should be read outside its stage — and no stage's plan may depend on a later stage's pool.*

Grand plans fail in a particular way: a figure that becomes true only at planetary scale is quietly borrowed to justify next season's work. The resources of a transformation open in sequence, each unlocked by the one before, and stating which pool is open *and which is not* is what makes a plan checkable rather than merely inspiring.

Four stages are named, counted in [[LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth and Cycle Time|cycles]] rather than years. In **the seed** — the first ten cycles — what is available is crowdfunding and matching gifts, the founders' own networks, and the completion of the book and the cell kit, which sit on the [[Critical Path]] before anything else. What is *not* available: quest flows at scale, institutional capital, any lever of state. In **the organism** — cycles ten to thirty — tithes and seasonal [[Quest]] flows compound with membership, philanthropic capital begins to redirect as fruit becomes visible, and the first [[The Hub|hubs]] are seeded from infrastructure that already exists; the hub network at scale and any military redirection remain closed. In **the tipping** — cycles thirty to forty — quest flows approach hundreds of billions a season, drawn as a small percentage of participants' own capacity, community-foundation and institutional flows join, and the first governments co-operate in the domains where adoption is already high; full military redirection is still not available. In **the harvest** — beyond cycle forty — the redirection of war-making and of governmental waste becomes realistic, because state-scale consent follows undeniable planetary presence and never precedes it, and the full build of the hub network belongs here ([[Weaponry To Livingry]]).

The rule binding the table is one sentence and it is absolute: **no stage's plan may depend on a later stage's pool.** The earliest work is therefore funded entirely by its own people — which is also the reason it cannot be bought, and the reason its legitimacy is not arguable. Read the other way, the discipline is a reading instruction: when a large number appears in a plan, find its stage before believing anything about this season.

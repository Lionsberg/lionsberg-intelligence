# The Commitment Tracker

*The register by which a body knows which of its shoulds and cans became wills — to whom, by when, and what became of each.*

Work moves through five words. **Could** — it is possible. **Should** — it serves [[The Goal]] and its time is near. **Can** — nothing blocks it; it may start now. **Will** — a person has consciously chosen it, in their own words, with a by-when. **Did** — it is done, and the one who was owed has said so. Only the fourth word makes a [[Commitment]]. The Tracker is the instrument that holds the fourth and fifth where everyone concerned can see them.

Its register is one row per commitment that crosses the body's boundary: from whom · to whom · what · by when · when and how the will was chosen and by whom · what changes hands and where it lands · the state · the evidence. Beside it sits a second table, [[The Candidates table]], for everything the work says should or can be done that nobody has yet chosen. The two are never merged, because the day a candidate is read as a promise is the day the register stops being trustworthy.

It is load-bearing because the seam between bodies is where work is actually lost. Inside one head, intention and action are the same motion; between two people they are two events with a gap, and the gap is invisible unless something holds it open. Most failures called coordination failures are simply promises that were understood differently, or never made at all, and discovered late. A register makes the gap a thing you can look at on a Tuesday ([[Networks of Commitments]], [[The gap]]).

The paper floor is a page of rows and a will said out loud at the table; a Cell with a notebook has a complete Tracker. A screen form may write the same rows and show them sorted — what is coming in, what is owed, what stands between us — and shows nothing a person did not say. Changes are appended, never rewritten: a broken promise stays in the record beside its reason, because a register that can be tidied is a register that will be ([[Consciously Breaking Commitments]], [[Kept as a share of made]]).

# Pre-Collapse Proaction vs Post-Collapse Reaction

*The choice between coordinating before a fall and rebuilding after one — and the reasons the second is so much worse than it sounds.*

The case for waiting is real and worth stating: a failed order clears ground, and things that could not grow under it might grow after. That is the whole of the case, and it rests on an outcome nobody can steer ([[Collapse]], [[The Collapse-Ascension Nexus]]).

Against it, the source lists fifteen reasons, and they cluster. **History** — collapses have produced long stagnations and violent contests for the vacuum, not clean starts. **Resources** — scarcity after a fall produces competition, not cooperation, which is the opposite of what rebuilding needs. **Politics** — chaos is the most reliable recruiting condition for extremism and for rule by force ([[The Final Order]], [[Kontrolle]]). **Environment** — organised care stops first, so degradation accelerates exactly when it is least affordable ([[Living System]], [[Regeneration]]). **Logistics and health** — supply chains and care systems go together, and both are needed to survive the period in which one is supposed to be rebuilding ([[Supply Chains]], [[Wellness]]). **Trust and knowledge** — both are lost in a collapse, and both are the actual raw material of any rebuilding ([[Trust]], [[Knowledge as commons]], [[The Living Library]]). And **unpredictability** — feedback loops make recovery harder the longer the condition persists ([[Systemic Failure Modes]]).

The asymmetry underneath is what makes this more than a preference. Coordinating early is done with the instruments still working — communication, transport, surplus, law, and people with attention to spare. Coordinating after is attempted with none of those, by people who are frightened, hungry, and correctly distrustful ([[The Window]], [[Critically Effective Time Limit]], [[The Asymmetry of Consequences]]).

Which is why this corpus builds parallel rather than waits, and why the phrase *build the new alongside the old* is a strategic claim rather than a sentiment ([[Hospicing the Old Systems]], [[The Ark and the Bridge]], [[Outside Coordination and the Parallel Construct]], [[Islands of Coherence]]).

**The probabilities the source attaches to each path are an AI assistant's speculative estimates, elicited by the book's questioner in 2023, and are not measurements.** The fifteen reasons stand without them, and they are the part worth keeping.

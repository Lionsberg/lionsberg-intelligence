# Watershed

*A drainage basin — all the land from which water flows to a common outlet. The foundational place-unit, drawn by the water itself.*

Every drop that falls within a watershed eventually reaches the same creek, river, lake, or sea. The lines follow ridgelines and divides, never human boundaries, and they nest fractally: a spring's creek inside a tributary inside a river inside a basin. In final analysis the whole of [[Earth]] is a single watershed — one closed planetary cycle of rain, river, aquifer, melt, and evaporation, with the ocean as the common outlet.

It is load-bearing because it is the irreducible geographic fact of where you are. Where political lines fail, watersheds do not; the land remembers its drainage even when people forget. That makes the watershed the anchor under every other place-scale: [[The Sovereign]] belongs to one by the simple fact of being somewhere, each [[Circle]] sits in one, each [[Community]] is shaped by its primary one, and each [[Bioregion]] is composed of nested ones — often it is the major watershed that gives a Bioregion its character.

In play it is the most available first [[Quest]] in the whole of [[Pillar VI — Sacred Ecology and Living Systems]]. *Know your watershed* is the bioregional instruction, and it can be started this week with no money and no permission: its springs, its streams, its tributaries, its main stem, its mouth; what flows into it, what depends on it, what damages it, what restores it. To steward a watershed is one of the most direct forms of place-care a Circle can practise, and it stands as a named Play with the seat for [[Living Systems Member]] beside it.

One distinction to keep. A watershed is hydrological, an [[Ecoregion]] is biological, and a [[Bioregion]] is both plus the people; they are different lenses on the same ground and often align without being the same unit. And the aquifer beneath is a separate body of water with its own shape — the surface and the subsurface must both be known to steward the water of a place.

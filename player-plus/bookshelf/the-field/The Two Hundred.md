# The Two Hundred

*The words a Cell meets in its first season, by domain. Each is defined once, in one place, and links to what it stands on and what it opens onto. Begin with [[The Twenty]].*

## 0 · The Spirit and the Goal

- [[Beauty]] — *The third face of the highest standard — harmony, balance, proportion, and right relationship made perceptible.*
- [[Best Possible Future]] — *Out of every future that could be pulled into being, the one at the absolute top — approached as an image rather than as a proposition, and said out loud until it can be aimed at.*
- [[Commander's Intent]] — *State the aim so clearly that those doing the work can adapt everything else, anywhere, without losing the thread.*
- [[Creator's Intent]] — *The highest intention and greatest good for All beings without exception — the aim that is received, not authored.*
- [[Current Best Understanding]] — *The most accurate account available right now, held openly, expected to change — the status everything below [[The Goal]] carries.*
- [[Goodness]] — *That which causes Life and Consciousness to develop into the fullness of their potential and flourish in harmony — that which helps, and does not harm.*
- [[Heaven and Hell as Co-Created Trajectories]] — *Not places assigned later, but directions travelled now. Heaven and Hell are what a life, a household, or a civilisation is making — and both can be walked into today.*
- [[Hell On Earth]] — *The other achievable terminus — what a Hellward trajectory produces, given time, and what parts of this world are demonstrating already.*
- [[Intention]] — *The directed WHY — the aim that calls a work into being and holds it on course.*
- [[Justice]] — *[[Right Relationship]] restored — love in action, not retribution.*
- [[Love]] — *The ultimate, unconditional relationship between two things — and the single overarching design principle of the whole system.*
- [[One Purpose Above All]] — *The first of the [[Twelve Irreducible Elements]]: the Purpose governs, and leaders serve.*
- [[Ordo Amoris — The Order of Loves]] — *A life well lived consists of rightly ordered loves — and if anything but the most worthy thing conceivable sits at the top, everything below it is mis-ordered by that fact.*
- [[Right Relationship]] — *Harmony, balance, and mutual respect between beings, communities, the living world, and [[Source]] — rooted in the recognition that all life is interconnected and interdependent.*
- [[Spirit]] — *The essence and animating nature that drives and guides a being, a body, or a system — and, at the highest level, the Central Animating Spirit of All Creation.*
- [[Spirit Precedes Pattern]] — *Where the Spirit is right, the wise right pattern emerges; where the Spirit is absent, no pattern saves.*
- [[The Asymmetry of Consequences]] — *The argument that makes acting correct without first winning the argument about how bad things are: compare what each mistake costs, and one column is recoverable while the other is not.*
- [[The Great Story]] — *The sacred, ever-unfolding story of [[ØNE]] and [[All]] — the single weave within which every cosmos, civilization, species, community, and soul is a living thread.*
- [[The Lens]] — *Everything is seen from somewhere; this names where — as close to the view of [[ØNE]] as we can attain, looking toward [[Heaven On Earth]].*
- [[The Non-Attachment Clause]] — *Written into the founding documents: our duty is not to any one system or approach. If a better one is identified, available and ready, we will gladly lay this one aside.*
- [[Throughput]] — *The rate at which the Goal of a system is actually accomplished — and the one measure of [[Value]].*
- [[Truth]] — *Accordance with Reality as it actually is — approached forever, possessed never.*
- [[Value]] — *[[Throughput]] of [[The Goal]] — and nothing else.*
- [[Values]] — *The guardrails that keep a body from being swept off course — one nested holarchy of goods, unified by a single highest value.*
- [[Vision]] — *The ability to perceive the whole set of possible futures clearly, and to select among them with [[Wisdom]] — and the shared picture that selection produces.*
- [[Wisdom]] — *The ability to perceive, orient, and act from the highest place — rightly applying values and knowledge to the actual circumstances of life.*
- [[Worksite Earth]] — *Earth is not merely a planet in crisis; it is the active construction site of [[Heaven On Earth]].*
- [[Worthy Goal]] — *An aim ambitious and meaningful enough to be worth a life — and the recognition that a goal must be chosen, and chosen well.*

## Domain II · Pillar II — Sacred Governance and Stewardship

- [[Agreement]] — *A conscious, explicit commitment entered freely by sovereigns in alignment with [[Logos]] — the unit from which a [[Field of Agreements]] is built.*
- [[Circle]] — *Peers gathered in equality and mutual respect to discern, decide, learn, or act together in service of a shared purpose — the form of the gathering, with no head of the table.*
- [[Commons]] — *That which is shared, stewarded, and co-created for the benefit of all beings, present and future, within a [[Domain]] — belonging to no individual, serving every member.*
- [[Community Company]] — *The self-governing body of one local place, united in service of [[The Goal]] — the one body whose whole purpose is to support all the other bodies around it.*
- [[Consent]] — *The absence of [[Meaningful Objection]] to a [[Proposal]] — which is to say, the proposal, though not perfect, falls within every member's [[Range Of Tolerance]] as the body advances toward its aim.*
- [[Consent-Based Decision-Making]] — *How a Circle decides without a boss: a proposal is brought, objections are heard, and it passes when no one has a reasoned, paramount objection.*
- [[Domain]] — *A bounded scope of responsibility and authority — the living space within which an association operates, nested within the holofractal Whole.*
- [[Field of Agreements]] — *The living system of interwoven agreements a body has freely chosen — voluntary covenants and never imposed law, read at the door, accepted once, honoured until renegotiated.*
- [[FSx]] — *The structural-legal-economic part of the kit: a fractal sovereign legal person, with agency independent of its members, that cannot be owned, bought, or cashed out. The form is settled; the vehicle is open.*
- [[Governance]] — *The sacred art and science of stewarding power, authority, and right order in service to [[ØNE]] and [[All]] — how a body steers itself from where it is toward [[The Goal]].*
- [[Meaningful Objection]] — *An objection names harm to what the body serves — and no proposal passes over one. Every member has the moral obligation to object when they reasonably believe the body will be unable to achieve its aim.*
- [[Mutual Consent]] — *Both sides say yes, and the yes stays alive: all association between a person and a body is voluntary and based on agreement running in both directions.*
- [[Range Of Tolerance]] — *The band inside which a member can live with a decision that is not what they would have chosen — the space between preference and objection.*
- [[Sovereignty]] — *Inalienable self-governance derived from [[Source]] and bound by [[Logos]] — the ultimate authority to govern one's self, and the ground on which all [[Consent]] and all legitimate [[Authority]] rest.*
- [[Stewardship]] — *The wise and responsible care of everything entrusted to us — time, attention, energy, resources, relationships, ecosystems, communities — so that it is left more alive than it was received.*
- [[Subsidiarity]] — *Decisions belong at the smallest competent scale, ascending only where a higher level's abstraction and capability genuinely serve the whole.*
- [[Wise Eldership]] — *Discernment held by those who have walked further — wisdom-based, not age-based, and sought because it is trusted rather than because it is in charge.*
- [[Withdrawal Of Consent]] — *The exit that makes [[Consent]] real: the conscious taking back of a yes, in a body one has joined or a system one was born into.*

## Domain III · Pillar III — Sacred Economy and CurrentSee

- [[Centers of Distribution]] — *A person, [[Cell]], or community through which resources flow outward — the living opposite of a centre of accumulation, where standing is measured by what flows through and blesses rather than by what flows in and stays.*
- [[The anti-pooling law]] — *At every new moon and every full moon, everything in the pool divides across the people doing the work — so that money never gathers at the centre and no more than two weeks of flow can ever be captured, frozen, or lost.*

## Domain XII · Pillar XII — Sacred Time, Rhythm, and Celebration

- [[Join the Seasonal Gathering]] — *At each turning the Cells come together: bring your story, hear the others, feel the body you are part of.*
- [[Turn the Season]] — *The breath of the Game, in five movements — prepare, see from above, celebrate and rest, renew or release, choose again.*

- [[Forge Your Agreements]] — *The move by which a gathering makes its [[Field of Agreements]]: two to five commitments, spoken aloud and chosen together, that protect the trust everything else will rest on.*
- [[Open a Communication Channel]] — *One channel your people actually open, with one agreement about how it is used: flame-tending, not noise.*
- [[When a Circle Completes Its Arc]] — *Every Circle has a lifecycle, and all Circles dissolve at some point in their story. This is not failure. Nothing true is lost.*

## Domain · The Operating System and Integrated Delivery

- [[Aim Before You Act]] — *When a body has gone foggy about why or where, stop and aim: an hour on purpose repays ten of wandering.*
- [[Complete and Declare]] — *Finish cleanly and say so out loud: promised against delivered, loose ends named, thanks given.*
- [[Keep Promises Visible]] — *The week's commitments live where everyone can see them, and kept-or-not is recorded without shame — because the gap between said and did is not a fault, it is the field of play.*
- [[Resource the Work]] — *Name the need concretely; invite contribution freely; record what flows gratefully.*
- [[Situational awareness]] — *You do not need to know everything. You do need to know enough — enough to act wisely, serve faithfully, and stay connected to the whole while doing your part in the particular.*

## II · Pillar II — Sacred Governance and Stewardship

- [[Dissolution by prior design]] — *A sound centre dissolving itself in advance — not because it failed, but so that there is nothing left to inherit, capture, or corrupt.*
- [[Power Over]] — *The inversion of authority: power exercised **over** people rather than in service of them, and held because it is held rather than because anyone is answerable for it.*

- [[The Sovereign Vessels]] — *Four vessels nested inside one another: this planet, the society steering it, the billions of small vessels composing that society, and the whole within which all of them sail. Governance is simply the act of navigating and steering.*

## III · Pillar III — Sacred Economy and CurrentSee

- [[Artificial Scarcity vs Regenerative Abundance]] — *Scarcity as a condition maintained rather than a fact discovered — and the abundance that is available when the maintaining stops.*
- [[Asymmetric limited liability]] — *Losses capped for the owners at a distance, uncapped for the people at the work. The oldest structural injustice in how enterprises are owned, and the ancestor of the problem the asset lock answers.*
- [[Cooperate Internally, Compete Externally]] — *Cooperation and competition are not opposite moralities. They are postures for different relations — and a healthy body runs both, deliberately, knowing where its boundary is.*
- [[Supply Line Supremacy]] — *Empire's actual object is not land but lines. Whoever controls what moves, and the routes it moves along, governs everyone who depends on the moving — without needing to govern them.*
- [[The Four Currencies]] — *Money is a tracking instrument, not a currency. What a living being actually spends is attention, time, energy, and sacrifice — and every allocation question is incoherent until that is named.*

- [[The Five-Fold Pattern of Mastery]] — *One cycle under every kind of practice — understand, cultivate, transmute, empower, align — so that learning one domain teaches how to learn the next.*

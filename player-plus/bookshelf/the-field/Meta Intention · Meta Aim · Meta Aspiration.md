# Meta Intention · Meta Aim · Meta Aspiration

*Three names for the top of the same holarchy, approached from three sides — what is willed, what is aimed at, and what is longed for, at the altitude where all three are one.*

[[Meta]] marks the highest, most universal, self-referential conception of a thing — the top of a nested order, where the category rejoins [[ØNE]] ([[Holarchy]]). Applied to the three things that point a life, it yields three words that turn out to name one referent.

**Meta Intention** — the intention of intentions, containing and ordering every other possible intention. The canon says plainly what it holds it to be: the intention of the Spirit of absolute and unconditional love. *Or simply, Love* ([[Intention]] · [[Love]] · [[The Logic Of Love]] · [[Aligned Will]]).

**Meta Aim** — the aim of aims, the frame in which a person's aims and their groups' aims can be stacked and aligned. It is stated as one with the Goal: *the total integrated wellbeing, development and right relationship of all generations of life and consciousness* — or, in the older language, the Kingdom of God, on earth as it is in Heaven ([[The Goal]] · [[Kingdom of God]] · [[Stacking Up And Aligning]] · [[Alignment]]).

**Meta Aspiration** — the same top reached from beneath, and the canon's page on it is really about a method. Any time the differentiating half of the mind separates a system into elements, we must eventually go back and recover the view of the whole. The holarchy of goals is unified at its top by one aim; articulated it is the Goal; beyond that it is simply Love; beyond that, simply ØNE ([[Whole]] · [[Mind]] · [[Perception]] · [[Ideal]]).

The reason all three exist is the reason the canon gives on the Meta Aim page: because *Meta* points toward the absolute, **it is intentionally not concretized** — precisely to avoid the failure pattern of concretizing the absolute. Three approximate names, none of them final, is the safeguard rather than an untidiness ([[Do Not Concretize the Absolute]] · [[The Eternally Nameless]] · [[Essences, Renderings, And Ineffable Referents]] · [[The Ineffable]]).

And it is at this one aim that the whole system is pointed; the governance layer exists to help navigate toward it ([[ØNE Vision and Intent]] · [[The Function Of Governance]] · [[Navigation]]).

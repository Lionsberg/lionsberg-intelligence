# Work

*The transfer of energy, by the application of force, along a vector, toward an aim.*

The definition is borrowed from physics on purpose, because it makes four things sayable that ordinary talk of work hides. Energy is what is spent — time, attention, capacity, the life of a body. Force is how it is applied. The [[Vector]] is the direction, which may be right or wrong regardless of effort. The aim is what the whole is for. Effort with no vector is motion, not work; effort along a wrong vector is work against the Goal.

It is load-bearing because it is the hinge between the Goal and everything operational. Work that helps All realise the Goal produces [[Value]]; work that does not is [[Waste]], and waste is not neutral — it is somebody's life spent on nothing. That single test is what [[Throughput]] measures, what [[Value Stream Mapping]] looks for, and what makes it fair to ask of any activity what it serves. An activity that serves nothing above it is busyness however hard it is worked.

The canon calls the vector toward the Goal [[Critical Path]] — the same word the planning discipline uses for the chain with no slack, at the altitude of a civilisation. So the ladder runs unbroken: a person's hour, an activity in a table, a Quest in a season, a Pillar in the lattice, the whole of [[The Meta Project]]. Work at that scale demands a system, which is where the rest of this domain begins.

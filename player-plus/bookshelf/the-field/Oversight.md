# Oversight

*The vigilant and wise monitoring of stewards and systems, to keep them aligned and to catch corruption or deviation early.*

Oversight is [[Accountability]] made continuous. Accountability says a steward owes an answer; oversight is someone actually looking, on a rhythm, before anyone has a reason to be suspicious. The distinction matters because scandal is almost always the failure of ordinary attention rather than the absence of a rule.

It is load-bearing because the diseases this Pillar guards against are slow. [[Centralization Of Power]], [[Extraction Of Wealth]], and mission drift do not announce themselves; they accumulate, and each increment is defensible. Only something that looks regularly — and compares what it sees to what was agreed — can catch them while the correction is still small.

The canon keeps it non-adversarial by design. Oversight belongs with [[Wise Eldership]] and the [[Wisdom Council]], whose function includes reviewing significant decisions and junctures, and with the rhythm of the body itself: a [[Retrospective]] each season, an [[OmniSpection]] at the turning, a [[The Record]] anyone may read and correct, the improvement log, and a [[Second reader]] where provenance matters. Oversight that operates as policing produces concealment; oversight that operates as tending produces early news.

In play, the smallest form is a [[Cell]] reading its own [[The Circle's Scorecard]] honestly at the end of a season — a body watching itself before anyone else has to.

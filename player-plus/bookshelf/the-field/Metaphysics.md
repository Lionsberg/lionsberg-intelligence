# Metaphysics

*The study of the fundamental nature of [[Reality]], [[Being]], and the hidden structures behind appearances — the inquiry [[Pillar I — Sacred Ontology and Metaphysics|Pillar I]] names.*

Metaphysics is the discipline of asking what is actually the case beneath what appears. In this Field it is not an academic specialism; the canon treats it as one half of the Pillar's own name and as a practice a civilization either does consciously or does badly by default. Every society runs on metaphysical commitments — about what a person is, what value is, what the world is for. The only question is whether they are examined.

It is load-bearing as the naming of the inquiry itself. Where [[Ontology]] asks after Being, [[Cosmology]] after origin and destiny, [[Epistemology]] after knowing, [[Axiology]] after value, and [[Teleology]] after purpose, Metaphysics is the wider room those questions are asked in. The canon's warrant for putting it first is historical: in the old world the sacred foundation was lost and corrupted, and what followed was existential confusion, systemic incoherence, and collapse. Incoherence at this layer is not harmless; it propagates.

The canon adds a discipline that keeps the inquiry from hardening. [[Essences, Renderings, And Ineffable Referents]] holds that every metaphysical statement is a rendering pointing at something beyond both [[Current Best Understanding]] and [[Current Best Articulation]]. [[The Ineffable]] is the paired practice of not crowding what cannot be said. And [[Mystery]] is kept as a positive category rather than a placeholder for future knowledge.

In play, metaphysics is not asked of a player at the door. It arrives later, and sideways — when a Cell finds it disagrees about what its Quest is finally for, and discovers the disagreement was never about the Quest.

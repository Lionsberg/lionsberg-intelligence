# The Sacred Tree

*The living body of all aligned beings seen as one tree — roots in the Source, a single trunk, many branches, countless leaves, and fruit that carries seed.*

A tree is the right picture for this body because a tree is one organism that is also a multitude, and because it grows rather than being assembled.

**Roots.** Anchored in truth, love and harmony, and reaching all the way down to [[Source]]. The roots are what sustains and nourishes; they draw from the collective past and hold the whole thing upright in weather ([[Truth]] · [[Love]] · [[Lineage]] · [[Foundational Literature]]).

**Trunk.** The idea itself risen into form — strong, resilient, central, carrying the energy of the whole between root and branch. One trunk, because a body with two is two bodies ([[Coherence]] · [[The Core Pattern]] · [[One Purpose Above All]]).

**Branches.** The many paths, projects and people. They reach toward the light in every direction, growing in diversity and abundance while remaining, obviously and physically, one thing ([[Quest]] · [[Cell]] · [[Federation]] · [[Meta Unity of Meta Diversity]]).

**Leaves.** Action — the individual contributions of millions, each small and each necessary, turning light into the energy that feeds the tree, and giving shelter and sustenance while they do ([[Do the Work]] · [[Meaningful Work]] · [[Participation]]).

**Fruit.** The tangible outcomes — wisdom, healing, regeneration — given away freely. And every fruit carries seeds of its own, which is the part that makes the metaphor more than decorative: the tree's output is not a product but the next tree ([[Regeneration]] · [[Healing]] · [[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]] · [[Grow Your Cells]] · [[Gift]]).

At the heart of it is [[The Golden Seed]] — the inception, integrity and code of the whole, planted with intention. From one origin comes the whole diverse potential of [[ØNE Body]]. And a tree grows in rings: each cycle of growth marks progress, resilience and expanded capacity, which is how the canon reads its own cycles ([[LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth and Cycle Time]] · [[Cycle]] · [[The Rings]]).

The invitation at the end is the practical part. Everyone is asked to contribute somewhere on the tree — strengthening the roots with wisdom, the trunk with resolve, giving birth to a new branch, or being a leaf that turns inspiration into action. All four are the tree ([[The Invitation]] · [[Roles]] · [[Choose a Quest]]).

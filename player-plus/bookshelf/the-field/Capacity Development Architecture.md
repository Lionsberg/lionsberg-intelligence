# Capacity Development Architecture

*The Path seen from the inside: three nested arcs, from first encounter to lifelong co-creation, where the events are the markers and what is built in the person is the substance.*

A path can be described by what happens — a threshold here, a gathering there — or by what the walking makes of the walker. This is the second description, and it exists because the first one can be completed without anything changing. Attending the events is not the same as acquiring the capacity ([[Capacity]], [[Recognitions Are Not Titles To Be Sought]]).

**The first arc — roughly the first three years.** The [[The 144 Gates of Initiation|144 Gates]] cultivate the universal way of the [[ØNE Body]]: twelve movements of twelve thresholds, walked in no fixed order. What is built is ordinary and cumulative — orientation toward [[ØNE]]; [[The Daily Way]]; a Circle and a working [[Field of Agreements]]; the vocabulary; emotional fluency; sufficiency in one's own affairs; the ability to say one's own story, purpose and plan; and a rhythm that survives a hard season ([[The Three-Year Formation]], [[Circle Coherence]]).

**The second arc — the years after.** Deepening into the interior cultivations and into whichever of [[The Five Archetypal Ways]] a person is called to, with their Guild as steward ([[Guild]], [[The Sacred Ascent]], [[Belt Progression]]). The claim that governs it is a correction to how mastery is usually imagined: the high recognition is *the beginning*, not the end — the point at which a person can finally start exploring consciously and competently ([[The dans, and Mastery at 5th Dan]], [[Mastery]]).

**The third arc — the rest of a life.** Conscious co-creation without a terminus: contributing one's own vantage to a shared map, holding scale from Circle outward, and continuing as a student. The master is the one who has not stopped being one ([[Shoshin]], [[The Loop That Has No End]], [[The Eternal Divine Play]]).

The arcs nest rather than follow: nothing from the first is finished when the second begins, and the ladder of recognitions is a way of seeing where someone is, never a rank they hold over anyone ([[The Zone Of Optimum Development]], [[Operate At The Scale You Were Prepared For]]).

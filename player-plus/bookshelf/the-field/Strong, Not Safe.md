# Strong, Not Safe

*We cannot keep one another safe. So the way we protect one another is by making one another strong.*

The argument runs premise to conclusion in three steps. To do anything worth doing, people have to go out past the boundary of the settled and the known. Out there are real hazards, and the attempt will not be easy and may cost lives. Therefore — and this is the honest step most reassurance skips — **it is not going to be possible to keep one another entirely safe.**

What follows is the whole claim. If safety cannot be delivered, then promising it is a lie, and organising a community around delivering it produces a community that is fragile *and* dishonest. The only remaining antidote is strength — held mutually, on purpose, as something we do for each other ([[Anti-Fragility]], [[Mutual Aid]]).

Note where the strength lives. **It is not a private virtue.** The verb is *help one another become strong*: the task is transitive, and it is the work of a body toward its members rather than an instruction to individuals to toughen up ([[Cell]], [[Education As Transformation]], [[Apprenticeship]]).

And its test is freedom of movement rather than hardness. The stronger we are the safer we are — *not because we are fearfully cowering inside the boundary, but because we have helped one another become the kinds of beings who can move in comfort outside it.* The measure is what a person can walk into, not what they can withstand ([[Courage]], [[Boundary]], [[Wayfaring]]).

What it displaces is worth naming. A body organised around safety will keep narrowing what it attempts until it is safe and useless, and will still not be safe. A body organised around strength grows the people in it. Both are answers to the same fear; only one of them is available ([[Manufactured Dependency]], [[Confront The Monsters Beyond The Boundary]]).

The closing image is the practical one: as fire consumes what is presented to it and turns it into heat, obstacles and opposition become fuel for the advance ([[Every Defect Is A Gift]], [[The Refining]]).

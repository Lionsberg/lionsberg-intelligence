# LIØNSBERG

*Lion and mountain — a mythic name for the Sacred Mountain and for the way of being that climbs it: a gathering place beyond time and space, and a beacon calling All home to the ØNE.*

The word is built of two plain things. *Lion*: courage, sovereignty, the heart that does not flee. *Berg*: the mountain, the high place, the climb. Between them sits the Ø, which is not a decoration — it is the sign of the centre, the zero point, the ØNE from which the rest takes its coherence ([[The Sacred Mountain]], [[ØNE]], [[Naming]]).

What it names is not an organisation. It is **timeless and universal** — not bound by time, location or form, existing in the spirit, heart, memory and essence of all things. It is **the Sacred Mountain** — the climb toward ultimate unity through love. It is **an eternal citadel** — a place of harmony where all who awaken may gather, remember, and co-create. And it is **the home of a flame** — a light reflecting the love and unity of the Source ([[The Flame]], [[Coming Home]], [[The Great ReMembering]]).

It is load-bearing because it is the first word anyone meets, and what a first word does is set what everything after it can mean. A name that meant a company would make all of this a company. A name that means a mountain makes it a climb, which is what it is ([[The vocabulary ladder]], [[Metaphor]], [[Mythos]]).

Three things must be held with it. The name cannot act: what exists on the ground is always one instance of the pattern, and *LIØNSBERG says* is a category error ([[The instance law]]). It is a prototype and not the eternal essence — current best understanding of something that exceeds it ([[LIØNSBERG Is A Prototype, Not The Eternal Essence]], [[Do Not Concretize the Absolute]]). And the name that can be named is not the name; what it points at is finally ineffable ([[The Eternally Nameless]], [[The Ineffable]], [[Essences, Renderings, And Ineffable Referents]]).

Its call is four lines: *Come home. Remember who you are. The flame has never left you. Rise, and we will illuminate the way.*

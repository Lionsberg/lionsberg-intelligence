# The eight kinds of Sighting

*Gift · hazard · blocked · wonder · need · offer · question · witness — the small set a Player chooses from when saying what they saw on the road.*

A [[Sighting]] is what a player saw that the whole should know. Giving the seeing a kind does two things at once: it tells the body how to answer, and it spares the one who saw from having to know what to do about it. The kinds are drawn the way a road-reporting app draws them — few, concrete, chosen in a second from a moving vehicle — because a channel that asks for an essay receives nothing.

**Gift** is a defect handed in: something wrong, offered to whoever can fix it, thanked by name and closed by the reporter's own word ([[Every Defect Is A Gift]]). **Hazard** is danger ahead for whoever comes next. **Blocked** is the road closed — a constraint nobody has named ([[Constraint]]). **Wonder** is something good and unexpected, worth the whole knowing. **Need** is a lack that calls for an answer; **offer** is capacity going spare ([[Requests and offers]]). **Question** is what the seer cannot resolve alone ([[Asking Questions]]). **Witness** vouches for what was seen, and is the kind that settles which of two contradictory accounts holds in a scope.

The set is deliberately open at the edges and closed in the middle: a Cell may find the word it needs is not here, and the honest move is to say so rather than to force the seeing into a box. What is not negotiable is that a Sighting names what was seen and not who is at fault ([[Zero Blame Environment]]) — a body where noticing costs the noticer stops being told things, and then stops being able to see itself.

# Necessity Of Accountability

*The fifth Recognition: all authority is revocable, and no role carries immunity from those it serves.*

Authority in this Way is entrusted, never owned. It comes from the consent of those who will live under it, it is held for a purpose, and it returns to them when the purpose is finished or the trust is broken. Delegation passes the doing and never the answering: the one who entrusted authority remains accountable for having entrusted it.

*No role carries immunity* is the sharp edge. Not the founder, not the elder, not the one whose gift is obvious, not the one the Body could least afford to lose. Every movement that fell had a role that had quietly become unaccountable first. The immunities are structural rather than moral: roles rotate, because a role held forever quietly becomes a throne ([[Term Limits And Rotation]]); anyone may stop the line without penalty ([[Whistleblower Protection]] · [[Stop]]); stewards who betray their mandate are recalled ([[Impeachment]]); and a body that has finished its work is dissolved rather than preserved.

It works only on top of [[Requirement Of Transparency]] — one cannot hold to account what one cannot see — and it is the answer to [[Centralization Of Power]] and to the [[The Golden Calf]], both of which begin as someone becoming too important to question.

The mirror discipline keeps it from becoming cruelty. Cause is sought in the set-up rather than in the person ([[Zero Blame Environment]]); the gap between what was promised and what was done is treated as the primary source of learning, without blame. Accountability names what happened and who answers for it; it does not require a culprit. A Body that cannot tell those two apart will get silence instead of either.

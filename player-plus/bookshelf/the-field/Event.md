# Event

*One line of the Record: a small, typed statement signed by the one whose word it is, which once written is never rewritten.*

*I will do this by then. This is done. I name these three. I saw that. Here is our plan, twelfth version.* In the widest sense an event is anything that happens; the sense held here is narrower and stricter — a happening written down by the one whose word it is, small enough to copy into a notebook and exact enough that any two people holding the same lines read them the same way.

**What a line carries.** A kind. An author — the key that signed it. A scope — the body it was published to. A when — the author's own claim about time, with order coming from the links rather than from the clock. A body of a few fields. Links to other lines: what it serves, what it fulfils, what it witnesses, what it supersedes, and the author's own previous line, so that each log is a chain in which a missing link shows. Then the signature. Then the identity ([[The event kinds]], [[Signed key]], [[An address]]).

Two plain ideas carry all of it. A signature is a seal only the holder of a key can make and anyone can check — a wax seal proves the hand without surrendering the ring. And the identity is a fingerprint of the exact text: change a comma and the fingerprint changes. Because the fingerprint *is* the name, a line can be copied anywhere and verified by everyone; because where it was published is no part of that name, a body that moves house keeps its whole history ([[Key]], [[Provenance]]).

It is load-bearing because it is what lets a world be read from its parts with no centre. A kitchen table's record and a planet's record are made of the same object, so the schedule, the score and the count are all pure functions of the lines held: same lines, same answer, whether worked by hand or by machine ([[The Record]], [[Interoperability]], [[The Game Engine]]).

Its discipline is that nothing is ever erased. Contradictory claims both stand, and the body whose scope it is writes a [[Witness]] settling which holds there. A correction supersedes rather than replaces, and the superseded line remains, because the gap is what teaches ([[Every Defect Is A Gift]], [[Zero Blame Environment]]).

Every key traces to a living person. An agent writes only under a delegation which is itself a line, scoped and revocable, so every line in the record answers to someone who can be asked ([[Player+]], [[An agent speaks in its own name]], [[Delegation]]).

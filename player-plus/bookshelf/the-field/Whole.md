# Whole

*A living unity — something that is genuinely one, and not merely a heap of parts.*

Wholeness is perceived before it is analysed. The receiving half of the mind takes in the whole directly; the differentiating half then breaks it into parts so they can be handled. Both are needed, and the discipline is **the return**: having used the parts to map, come back to the whole with better understanding, never mistaking the parts-list for the living unity it describes ([[Perception]] · [[Mind]] · [[Sensing comes before understanding]] · [[Mapping]] · [[The Living Gradient]]).

Every whole is also a part. The cell is whole within the organ; a sovereign is whole within the Circle; and each flourishes **as itself** precisely by participating in the greater whole — all the way up the holarchy to ØNE, within whom all inter-exist ([[Holon]] · [[Holarchy]] · [[Sovereignty At Every Scale]] · [[InterExistence]]). Even sacrifice runs on this pattern: as cells sometimes yield for the wellbeing of the greater body, so beings freely give for what exceeds them — freely, never by command ([[Sacrifice]] · [[Higher Order Functional Unity]] · [[Consent]]).

And wholeness is a **gradient, not a status**. A personality becomes whole as its competing drives integrate into functional unity moving toward one worthy aim ([[Motivational States]] · [[Integration]] · [[Worthy Goal]]). A community becomes whole as its members cohere without ceasing to be themselves ([[Coherence]] · [[Circle Coherence]] · [[Meta Unity of Meta Diversity]]). A work becomes whole when nothing essential is missing and nothing foreign remains ([[Quality]] · [[Prune]] · [[Gaps Not Yet Closed]]).

To heal, at every scale, is to make whole again — the words are one word. That is why repair and completion are the same act here, and why a body's healing is not sentimental but structural ([[Healing]] · [[Regeneration]] · [[Reconciliation]] · [[Atonement]]).

It is load-bearing for the production half of the canon too: *optimize for the whole, not the pieces* is this page stated as a working rule, and it is the reason local improvement so often makes the whole worse ([[The Five Big Ideas Of Integrated Delivery]] · [[Throughput]] · [[Silos No More]] · [[Design]]).

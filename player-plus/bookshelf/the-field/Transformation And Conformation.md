# Transformation And Conformation

*The two directions a person can be changed. Transformed from the inside toward what they carry; conformed from the outside toward what surrounds them. Both change a person; only one reveals anything.*

Conformation is efficient and mostly invisible. A group has a shape, and people take it — its vocabulary, its judgments, its sense of what is obvious, its idea of what a serious person does. Nobody decides to conform; it is the default condition of a social being, and the more coherent and admired the group, the stronger it pulls. What results is someone who fits and has not been formed.

Transformation runs the other way: the unfolding of what a being actually carries — their [[Genius]], their [[Purpose]], their [[The True Self]] — which is why it produces difference rather than uniformity. A Body full of transformed people is less alike than when it started, and more coherent, because coherence is diversity in resonance rather than sameness ([[Coherence]]).

The distinction is load-bearing for [[Education As Transformation]], Element six. *Form people; people form organizations.* If formation is really conformation, the Body replicates its current shape including its faults, and the Pattern cannot travel — what spreads is a culture rather than a capacity. The canon's proof case is explicit about the order: the school preceded the cooperative by thirteen years, because you do not replicate institutions.

The test is what happens to someone who has been formed here and goes elsewhere. A transformed person carries something that works in other soil. A conformed person carries a set of manners that only works here.

It is named in the Language among the Sovereign's Path Patterns and among the wider extractions, with no Card behind it.

# The face (a holon's first page)

*One page that says who this body is, what it is questing toward, and how to reach it — printable, and readable as a tile on a wall.*

Every body that plays keeps a first page. It carries four things and no more: who we are, where we are, the [[Quest]] we are on this season, and our door. It is written for a stranger — someone who has never heard of this Cell and has three seconds to decide whether it is kin. One page, so it can be printed and pinned; four things, so it can be read across a room.

It is load-bearing because a body that cannot be recognized cannot be federated with. [[Requests and offers]] only work if someone can see who is offering; a [[Home Cell]] can only hold Cells it can tell apart; the wall of a [[The Big Room|Big Room]] is a field of these faces and nothing else. The face is the smallest unit of *being findable*, and findability is the whole of what a body gives up its privacy for — which is why what appears on it is chosen on purpose and the rest stays behind the membrane ([[Guard the Membrane]]).

The same page serves at every scale, because the four questions do. A person's face and a community's face differ in what fills them, never in their shape ([[The same card shape]], [[Holofractal Scaling]]). And it is the first page of the body's own book, not a separate marketing object: what is on the face is true of the body inside, or the face is a lie the body will be caught in.

# One moves a few, a few moves 3%, 3% moves 20%, 20% moves 80%

*The leverage chain: a very few things move nearly all things, and the chain begins with one.*

One line in a short chapter, and the whole strategy of the body rests on it. It is not a forecast and not a measurement. It is a claim about shape — that influence does not spread evenly but travels through a small number of links, each of which reaches a larger circle than itself, so that a single person genuinely reached is not one unit of progress but the first link in a chain.

Read forward, it is a comfort and a warning at once. The comfort: you do not need to move eighty percent of anything, and attempting to is how most efforts exhaust themselves. You need to move a few, well ([[A Few]], [[The First Few]]). The warning: *well* is load-bearing. A chain conducts only as far as each link actually holds, so a hundred people lightly touched carry less than two people genuinely changed — and the temptation of every growing body is to trade the second for the first because the first is countable ([[The pace of trust]], [[Pass The Flame]]).

Read backward, it explains where to stand. If twenty percent moves eighty, and three percent moves twenty, then the work of the three percent is not to persuade the eighty but to become the thing the twenty can follow ([[The First Three Percent]], [[The Tipping Point Cascade]], [[Islands of Coherence]]). And the same arithmetic is how a Cell becomes a body at all: one table that lives, passing the flame to two or three who live, is the only mechanism the canon proposes for reaching a world ([[Fibonacci Spread]], [[Grow Your Cells]], [[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]]).

The numbers should be held as the canon's own reasoning rather than as findings. They are a stated design shape — an estimate, not a measurement — and the underlying social-science work on tipping fractions is contested and ranges more widely than three percent. What survives the uncertainty is the form: capacity is finite, effect is uneven, and the leverage is early in the chain ([[The 20%]], [[Maximum reuse]]).

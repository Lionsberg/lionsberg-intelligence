# The Laws of Harmony

*Six patterns by which the Infinite expresses itself in the finite — Unity · Diversity · Reciprocity · Flow · Balance · Becoming — and by which the finite remembers it was never separate.*

They are called laws in the older sense: not rules laid on the world from outside, but the way the world is actually built. They arise from the [[Spirit]] and the [[Logos]] of the consciousness that is still creating; to align with them is to move with the grain of things rather than across it. Each is stated plainly enough that a person can test it against one day of their own life.

**Unity** — beneath the appearance of fragments, all things are one living web, so that what is done to another is done to oneself ([[Ultimate Unity]], [[Meta Unity of Meta Diversity]]). **Diversity** — unity is not uniformity; each being is a distinct facet of the Infinite and a note the whole would miss. **Reciprocity** — life moves in cycles of giving and receiving, and abundance appears where every part of the web keeps contributing back ([[Reciprocity]], [[Exchange]]). **Flow** — nothing here is static; resistance makes tension, alignment makes ease, and what has finished its season is released so the next form can rise ([[Flow]]). **Balance** — creation unfolds through polarities that are partners rather than enemies, and health is the equilibrium between them: action and rest, giving and receiving, the one and the many. **Becoming** — the whole is never finished; each moment adds to it, and to live well is to keep growing rather than to arrive ([[Becoming]]).

What makes this page an instrument rather than a creed is that the canon attaches a practice to each law. Sit in a natural place and watch how difference composes into one thing. Choose one act of giving a day and notice what it does to the flow around you. Release resistance the way a swimmer stops fighting the current. Find the one polarity you are currently out of balance on and take a single step back toward centre. Measure yourself not against perfection but against who you used to be ([[Every Defect Is A Gift]], [[Continuous Improvement]]).

The six are held as current best articulation, not as a closed set — they are one rendering of the order beneath [[The Divine Symphony]], and the canon is explicit that they are truths to be understood and embodied rather than commands to be obeyed.

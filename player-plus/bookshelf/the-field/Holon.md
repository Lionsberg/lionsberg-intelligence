# Holon

*Something that is simultaneously a whole and a part.*

A cell is a whole in its own right and a part of an organ, which is whole and part of a body. A person is whole and part of a [[Circle (as gathering)|Circle]], which is whole and part of a [[Community]], which is whole and part of the [[Living System]] of Earth, which is whole and part of all [[Creation]] within [[ØNE]]. Everything real is like this.

The canon's decisive line is diagnostic: *a holon that forgets it is a part becomes a cancer; a holon that forgets it is a whole becomes a slave.* Health at every scale is both at once — full sovereign integrity and full participation in the larger wholes. That single sentence carries more of this system's politics than most of the governance pages. It is why [[Sovereignty]] is never atomistic and [[Federation]] is never absorption; why [[Federations vs Empires]] is a line that has to be held; why a Cell that takes from the commons without contributing is named as [[The Parasite]] and a Cell that will not federate is failing in the other direction.

It is the unit the rest is built from. An order of holons is a [[Holarchy]]; a living whole composed of living wholes is a superorganism; each [[Instantiation]] of a [[Prototype]] is sovereign and simultaneously a part of the learning body of All; each part in a [[Kit of Parts]] is a whole capability and a part of the greater whole. [[Holofractal Omnifederation]] is the same insight turned into governance: complete wholes, federated from the bottom up, no member swallowed.

In play it is the shape of belonging: your Cell is entirely its own and entirely part of its Home, and neither claim weakens the other.

# The Questcard

*One card for one Quest: the Quest in a sentence, why it matters, when it is done, your part of the bigger plan, and who will do what by when.*

The Questcard goes on the fridge when the Quest is chosen and into the box when it is finished. It holds one [[Quest]] and the words each person has said about it this week. The Quest does not change until it is done or laid down; the week's lines are rewritten every gathering.

It is load-bearing because it is where a season's intention becomes this week's promises. Five things are on it, and each has a job. *Our Quest* is one sentence a child can repeat — if it takes a paragraph, it is two Quests, or it is a plan. *Why* ties the work to the Game: say what will be more like Heaven when it is done, and if the youngest person at the table cannot say why, keep talking until they can. *Done when* is the line read at the turning, written so that two people could not argue about it. *Our part of the bigger plan* stays blank until a sentence reaches you from your home — that blank is where a table meets the whole. Then the commitments: a name, a thing, a date, one per line.

How it is used. Rewrite the bottom of the card at each [[The Turn]] and tick last week's boxes in front of everyone. An unticked line is [[The gap]], which is the field of play and never a shame; what it taught goes on [[The Circle's Scorecard]]. At the turning the finished card is signed by the two hosts and kept in the Cell's box. The box is the Cell's memory, and it is the one thing a Cell should never throw away.

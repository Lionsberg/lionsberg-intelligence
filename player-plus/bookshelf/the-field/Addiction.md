# Addiction

*A compulsion that survives the person's own decision against it — named in the canon with a particular framing: recovery as an initiatory arc.*

The framing matters more than the definition, because the framing decides what a community does. Read as moral failure, addiction produces punishment. Read as pure mechanism, it produces management. Read as an initiatory arc, recovery becomes a passage a person walks and comes back from carrying something — which is what the twelve-step rooms, named in the canon among the Pattern's own Earthly lineages, have demonstrated for a century. The [[Twelve Irreducible Elements]] were drawn in part from those rooms: small groups, one voice each, sponsorship, no one in charge, the whole thing given away free.

It is load-bearing because the Pattern already runs on the structure that recovery discovered. A [[Cell]] of three to thirteen who say what they will do and report the gap without blame, who put something in the middle of the table, who keep a [[Carrier]] relationship with whoever brought them, and who pass what they were given to the next person — that is a description of both. [[Sponsorship]] and the [[Support Group]] are named in the field for this reason. What a Circle can offer someone in recovery is the very thing it is already built to be.

In play the limits are as important as the offer. A Circle is not treatment; a well-meaning group that takes on a physical dependency it is not equipped for can do real harm, and withdrawal from some substances is dangerous. The floor of care applies first — when a member is in danger in body or in mind, act first, record later. [[Fasting]] and other subtractive practices are not detox. What the Circle reliably provides is the thing that relapse most often follows the loss of: a table with your name on it, every week, where you are expected.

The canon holds this as a named gap, not a method. [[Trauma]], [[Mental Health]], and addiction are listed together as territory surfaced and not patterned.

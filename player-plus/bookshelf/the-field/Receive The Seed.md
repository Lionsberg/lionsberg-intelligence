# Receive The Seed

*The receiving move at the threshold: what a person actually does when the Seed reaches them, before they have decided anything.*

Receiving is its own act, and the corpus treats it as one. Discovery — a trusted person sends a link, you arrive, something in you recognises what you are seeing, not because you were convinced but because you already knew — and nothing more is required at that stage. No agreement, no pledge, no membership, no answer. The move is to take it in.

Naming it as a move is load-bearing because it protects the space between arrival and commitment. Most systems collapse that space: to have read is to have been enrolled, and the reader learns to defend themselves at the door. Here the space is held open deliberately — [[The Solo Sovereign's Grace]] extends it to weeks or months, and the [[Initiation (as stage)|Pledge]] is placed on the far side of it. A yes that was never free is worth nothing to a Game that runs on a word given.

The corpus also asks something of the receiver, which is why the move has a second half. What is received is not consumed but stewarded: tested against your own discernment, filled up where it is lacking, corrected where it does not belong, and passed on with its provenance intact. [[The Charge Of The Receiver]] states the charge in its widest form, and carries with it the limit — the sovereignty of the Receiver — that keeps the charge from being surrendered.

In the Game receiving is the first thing that happens and the thing most easily skipped by whoever is holding the door. A person handed too much too fast has been given nothing. Hand the word, the page, or the invitation — whichever they are asking for — and then wait, which is the harder half of the office of the [[Guide]].

# The LIØNSBERG Academy

*Not a school — the formation dimension of ordinary life, made deliberate. Every Circle is already a cell of it; the curriculum is the living.*

A school is a building, a batch, a subject list, and a moment when a person is declared finished. The Academy is none of those. It is the [[The Pedagogy of Trust and Sirimiri|soaking pedagogy]] given a shape at every scale: the [[Cell]] as the irreducible unit, the [[Guild]] as the federation of a craft across cells, the [[Council]] as the standing eldership that holds wisdom-decisions, a mobile site where a cell's practice takes physical form ([[The Roving Ark Temple Dojo]]), and the whole federation of them as a university with no campus ([[The Hub]], [[Bioregion]]).

The important claim is that the Academy is not added to a Circle. It **is** the Circle's formation dimension: any body running the moves and retrospecting on its own living is already doing this ([[The Seven Moves]], [[OmniSpection]], [[Retrospective]]).

Its curriculum is lived: one does not study leadership but leads; does not study healing but heals; does not study the Pattern but lives it, looks back on the living, and refines ([[Learning By Doing]], [[Praxis]], [[Tightly Coupled Learning and Action]]). Its disciplines follow from that — a floor of formation measured in years rather than weeks ([[The Three-Year Formation]]); an apprenticeship loop that rotates *watch → assist → do supervised → do alone → teach the next* ([[Apprenticeship]], [[Cohort-Learning]]); a requirement that a real share of formation happen inside real work with real stakes; and recognitions that are witnessed demonstrations rather than purchased credentials ([[Belt Progression]], [[Recognitions Are Not Titles To Be Sought]]).

What it refuses is as defining as what it does. No credential race — capacity is shown in living. No standardisation — a person's particular gift is developed rather than flattened ([[Genius]], [[Inexhaustible Individuals]]). No dividing a human being into subjects. No eldership by age: a young person may be an elder and an old one may not ([[Wise Eldership]]). No lecturing where a question would do. And no end of programme ([[Education As Transformation]], [[The LIØNSBERG Educational Model]]).

It is also local before it is anything else. A child formed in relationship with one watershed, its weather, its plants, its people, carries the Pattern in a way that is unmistakably *here* ([[Individual And Local]], [[Ecoregion]], [[Kinship]]).

# Tradition

*What is handed down — the cultural and historical inheritance of a people, preserved and upheld, and passed on to those who come after.*

A tradition is not old habit. It is something that has been carried across generations because it held, and the carrying is itself an act: someone had to decide it was worth keeping, and someone had to teach it ([[Lineage]] · [[Remembrance]] · [[Transmission]] · [[Pass The Flame]] · [[Wise Eldership]]).

Its value is that it encodes what worked. Much of it is a solution to a problem the current generation has not had to meet yet, in a form nobody can any longer explain — which is exactly why it should not be removed simply because no reason for it is visible. The absence of a remembered reason is not evidence of no reason ([[Pattern]] · [[The Pattern That Works]] · [[Foundational Literature]] · [[Shoshin]]).

And its danger is the same fact from the other side. A tradition carries whatever it carried — including injustice, and including arrangements that served whoever set them. *It has always been done this way* is a description, never an argument; inherited authority that rests on nothing else does not stand ([[The Old Authorities]] · [[Institutional Idolatry]] · [[Do Not Concretize the Absolute]]).

Both truths are held at once, and the name for holding them is **innovation-in-tradition**: neither the wholesale rejection that throws away what a people learned, nor the preservation that mistakes the form for the thing. What is handed on is meant to be improved by each generation that carries it, and the honest work is telling which part is the wisdom and which is the scaffolding ([[Innovation]] · [[Multi-Generational Co-Operation]] · [[LIØNSBERG Is A Prototype, Not The Eternal Essence]] · [[Weave, do not delete]] · [[Discernment]]).

This is also why many traditions are received from rather than founded against. It takes what is true where it finds it, keeps the source visible, and does not require anyone to leave their own in order to stand here ([[Meta Unity of Meta Diversity]] · [[Attribution]] · [[MindWeaving]] · [[Cross-Cultural Pattern Translation]] · [[Truth To ØNE, Errors To Us]] · [[Welcoming The Stranger]]).

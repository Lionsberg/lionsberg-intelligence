# Gratitude

*The practice that closes a gathering and opens a day: the deliberate recognition of what was given, named specifically.*

It is placed at the seams of the rhythm. The day begins with it, before rising — one breath, one thanks for the gift of another day. The day ends with three gratitudes named, specific rather than general, because specificity is what makes the practice real; *I am grateful for my life* costs nothing, and naming the actual thing that happened costs the attention the practice is made of. And the weekly gathering closes in gratitude, which is how a Circle leaves the table.

Read as information, gratitude reports receipt. A being practising it accurately is one who can see what is arriving — which is the same perceptual muscle the Way trains everywhere else, turned toward what is given. A Body that cannot see what it has received will not steward it, and will keep asking for what it already has.

It is also the honest counterweight to the Offering. Something is put in the middle of the table each week; something is taken from it. Gratitude is the half of [[Reciprocity]] that does not move goods — the recognition that keeps giving from becoming credit and receiving from becoming entitlement.

At the structural level it shows up as lineage honored and provenance kept: every work naming what it grew from, credit travelling with the work, no contributor alienated from their contribution ([[Lineage]] · [[Attribution]]). Gratitude written down is what those conventions are.

It is cheap, which is why it is easy to skip, and it is the first thing to go when a Body is under pressure — which is exactly when its absence costs the most.

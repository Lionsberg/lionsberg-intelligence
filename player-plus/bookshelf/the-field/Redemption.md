# Redemption

*The sacred process of restoring [[Right Relationship]] and wholeness through grace, truth, forgiveness, and reconciliation.*

Redemption is the movement back from a miss. The canon names four means and keeps all four: grace, truth, forgiveness, reconciliation. Grace is what makes return possible at all; truth is what makes it real rather than cosmetic; [[Forgiveness]] is what releases the one who was harmed; [[Reconciliation]] is the relationship actually rebuilt.

It is load-bearing because a body that cannot restore anyone can only expel. The system's justice is explicitly restorative: [[Justice]] in this Field is right relationship restored, not retribution, and [[Remedy]] is named as the means of correcting injustice within a just system. [[Conflict Transformation]] resolves disputes by restoring relationship rather than by producing a winner. The alternative — a body whose only sanction is exclusion — hardens quickly and loses its best people to its own procedures.

The canon pairs it with honesty about what redemption costs. [[Atonement]] involves taking responsibility for one's own past transgressions, setting things right as far as one is able, and committing to do better; that is not absolution, it is repair with work in it. And redemption is not owed unconditionally in every direction: where harm is deliberate and ongoing, [[Guardianship]] acts first, and restoration comes after containment rather than instead of it.

In play it is deliberately small and frequent. [[Apologizing]] is a named practice; mending a tension is a move a Cell runs; [[The Way Out]] lets anyone leave in peace with their lines left standing, and [[The Return]] lets them come back. Nothing is erased.

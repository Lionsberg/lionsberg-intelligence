# Navigation

*Knowing where you are, where you are going, and choosing the next leg — the discipline of moving from present suffering and dysfunction toward the Goal.*

Navigation has three parts and a sailor's honesty about all of them. A destination: [[The Goal]], fixed, not chosen by us. A position: where we actually are, read from what is true rather than what was planned — which is what [[Sensemaking]] is for. And a leg: the next stretch, chosen because it moves the position toward the destination. A navigation issue is anything that makes any of the three unclear, and it is treated as work rather than as mood.

It is load-bearing because a body can be busy, disciplined and lost. The instruments of the rest of this domain are navigation instruments: [[Milestone]] gives fixed points to steer by, [[Float]] and [[Critical Path]] say which way the current runs, [[The Record]] is the log, and the compass question is the bearing taken before every move. Without a position honestly read, every one of them computes confidently about a place the body is not.

It is done together and often. A dashboard is only as good as the readings put into it; a reading taken once a season is a memory. The weekly hour exists partly for this: a few honest lines on where we are before anything is decided about where to go next.

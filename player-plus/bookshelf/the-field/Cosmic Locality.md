# Cosmic Locality

*The particular place in the cosmos where a holon lives — the* here *to which the one Pattern is localized and adapted.*

The Pattern is one; the places it lives are without number. Every part of the whole is somewhere: a kitchen table, a street, a watershed, a planet, a star system. Wherever a [[Cell]] forms, at whatever level, it forms in a place, and the structure it grows unfolds there — universal in its logic, fitted in its names, rhythms, forms, and law ([[General Pattern, Specific Application]], [[FSx]]).

What stays the same everywhere is exactly what lets any Cell recognise, read, and federate with any other: the shared standards of form, sovereignty at every scale, and a living boundary that is permeable rather than closed. What changes is everything a place rightly asks for — its language, its seasons, its land and its kin, the legal clothing its time and place require ([[Minimum standards of interoperability]], [[Sovereignty At Every Scale]], [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]], [[On translating]]).

A cosmic locality is where the universal meets the individual and local, as *here and now* is where the eternal meets this moment ([[Heaven On Earth]], [[Universal-Accessing]]).

Localities nest the way the holons living in them do. A Cell's table lies within a neighbourhood, a community, a bioregion, a planet, and outward through the scales; a holon lives in its own locality and in every larger one that holds it. So a body of Cells is fitted to a wider place than any of its Cells, and each Cell to a nearer one ([[Holarchy]], [[Levels Of Scale]], [[Bioregion]], [[The Neighborhood]]).

Hold the map lightly. The scales are categories; the land in front of you is the territory ([[Spirit Precedes Pattern]]).

It is not a jurisdiction. Political lines are one overlay on a place and never its measure — and it is not a particular astronomical neighbourhood either; it is whatever place a holon lives, at whatever scale, from a table to a galaxy ([[Watershed]], [[Ecoregion]]).

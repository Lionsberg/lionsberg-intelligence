# Monitoring, Evaluation, and Continuous Improvement

*The standing discipline of collecting what is actually happening, judging it against what was intended, and feeding the answer back into the work — the seeing half of the improvement loop.*

Three named acts, and they are not the same act. **Monitoring** is continuous and cheap: what is being done, what is being kept, what is moving — gathered as the work happens rather than reconstructed afterwards ([[The Record]] · [[Kept as a share of made]] · [[Live log]] · [[The Score]]). **Evaluation** is periodic and expensive: a judgment about whether what we are doing is working, and whether it is still the right thing to be doing at all ([[OmniSpection]] · [[Retrospective]] · [[The gap]] · [[Measurement]]). **Improvement** is what makes the first two worth doing; without it, monitoring becomes surveillance of one's own body and evaluation becomes a report nobody acts on ([[Continuous Improvement]] · [[Improvement log]] · [[Every Defect Is A Gift]]).

The reason to name them together is that they fail together. Most bodies do one and believe they have done all three: they collect data nobody evaluates, or evaluate without the data to evaluate against, or improve on instinct with neither. The named discipline is the commitment to close the circuit ([[Attend, Plan, Act, Learn, Improve Cycle]] · [[Tightly Coupled Learning and Action]]).

What it is *for* is staying aligned while the ground moves. Actions and strategies drift from goals quietly and no amount of sincerity reveals it; a plan that was right when written stops being right, and only the loop notices ([[Drift]] · [[Alignment]] · [[The Record as the Movement's variance gauge]] · [[Measure everything, manage to the milestones]]).

Two cautions travel with it. It must not become its own industry — a body can spend more on knowing about itself than on doing anything, and the measures at a table are meant to be few, legible and cheap ([[Waste]] · [[Bottom Line Up Front]]). And it must never become a single-metric rule: the numbers exist to let a body *see*, and the deciding is still judgment ([[Measurement]] · [[Wisdom]]).

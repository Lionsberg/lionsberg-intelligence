# Rest And Regeneration

*The pause that restores — rest held as a structural part of the work rather than as its interruption.*

Every living system alternates. Nothing that keeps producing without a trough keeps producing for long. The canon builds this in at every scale it names: a night inside a day, a pause at midday, a day inside a week, a rest phase inside each season of about ninety days, a [[Jubilee]] inside a span of years. The season itself has four faces — plant, grow, harvest, rest — and rest is one of them, not the gap between three of them.

It is load-bearing because a [[Cell]] will otherwise burn its members to hit a [[Quest]]. The old ordering treats rest as what is left over, so rest becomes the first thing a committed person gives away and the thing that never comes back. Naming rest as a phase makes it sayable at the table: the Circle is in its rest face and that is the plan, not a lapse. It is also what makes [[Kept as a share of made]] honest over years rather than over one good season — a body of people that plans for its own recovery keeps its word longer than one that does not.

In play, the rhythm shows up on the card and in the calendar rather than in an exhortation. [[The Daily Way]] closes a day. [[The Turn]] closes a week. [[Harvest and Close]] and [[OmniSpection]] close a season, and the turning is a genuine pause before the next [[Quest]] is chosen. A person's own forms are ordinary — [[Sleeping]], a walk, silence, a day without production, [[Fasting]] from the work itself.

Rest is not idleness and not reward. It is when [[Regeneration]] happens: a rested body has reorganized, a rested Circle has integrated its season, and neither could have been reached by continuing. A body of people that cannot stop has, in practice, decided that its work matters more than the people doing it — which contradicts the floor the whole Game rests on.

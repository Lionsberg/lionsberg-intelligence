# Doing

*Engaging in activity or work in space and time — the active pole paired with [[Being]] and [[Becoming]].*

Doing is what happens when [[Intention]] reaches the hands. The canon differentiates it from [[Being]] deliberately and keeps both: Being is the pure essence of existence, Doing is activity in space and time. Neither is the higher one. A life that is all Doing burns out and mistakes motion for direction; a life that is all Being never lands, and the new world is not argued into existence but embodied into it.

The Game is built on Doing at a very small scale and holds it to a very high standard. The fourth of the Twelve Elements of the Pattern is [[Networks of Commitments]], and the weekly move is exact: *say what you will do; do what you said; learn from the gap*. A [[Commitment]] moves could → should → can → will → did, and a WILL is a person's word, consciously given, never inferred. [[Kept as a share of made]] is the first number a group should know about itself. That is Doing made accountable without being made cruel: the gap is learned from, not punished, in a [[Zero Blame Environment]].

Doing is also what keeps the metaphysics of this Pillar from becoming ornamental. [[Praxis]] is the reciprocal unfolding of knowing and action; [[Tightly Coupled Learning and Action]] is the twelfth Element; [[Learning By Doing]] is how the Pattern actually lands in a body. What a person practises is what they will produce — *the means are the ends* — so the quality of the doing is a claim about the world being made, not only about the task.

At the table it is the plainest thing in the Game: one sentence, said aloud, about what you will do this week.

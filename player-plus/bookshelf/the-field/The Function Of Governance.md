# The Function Of Governance

*To steer the vessel toward its destination — stated before any structure is chosen, because a structure chosen before its function is known will serve something else.*

*Govern* comes from the word for *to steer*. That is the whole of it: having fixed the aim, the function of a governance system is to navigate from where we are to where we are going ([[Navigation]] · [[The Goal]] · [[Who We Are · Where We Are · How We Got Here · Where We Are Going · Who We Are Going With · Why]]). Not to hold office; not to preserve itself; not to produce order for its own sake. To steer.

Saying the function first is a discipline, not a preamble. Almost every governing body a person has met was inherited as a *shape* — a board, a committee, a parliament — and its purpose was reconstructed afterwards to fit the shape. Naming the function first puts every structural question back where it belongs: does this arrangement actually help us get there, and how would we know ([[Governance]] · [[Design To The Goal]])?

From the function follow the requirements. Structure, culture and process must be at once **protective** and **purposeful** — protective, or the body is captured or eroded; purposeful, or it is a well-defended vessel going nowhere. They must fit the social, economic and political nature of the mission actually undertaken. And they must hold two things that pull against each other: **simple and understandable enough that everyone can take part in them**, and **robust enough to withstand every form of co-option or erosion, internal or external** ([[Subsidiarity Of Authority]] · [[Separation Of Power]] · [[Structural Immunity]] · [[Term Limits And Rotation]]). Simplicity that cannot survive attack is naïve; robustness nobody can participate in has already become a priesthood.

Running through all of it, transparency and accountability by, among and between everyone in the body — not as virtues added on top, but as the instruments by which steering can be seen to be steering ([[Requirement Of Transparency]] · [[Necessity Of Accountability]] · [[Recourse]]).

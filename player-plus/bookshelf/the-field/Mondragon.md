# Mondragon

*Proof that it is possible — and the head of a list of eight streams through which the same pattern was ReMembered without knowing of one another.*

A Basque priest in a war-torn town. Two recovering alcoholics in Ohio. An Italian monk in the sixth century. Twenty-eight weavers in northern England. A Finnish computer science student. Five indigenous nations in what is now New York. Three mountain communities in the Swiss Alps. A hundred and twenty people in a room in Jerusalem. Each of them found the same thing. That list — Mondragon, the twelve-step rooms, the Benedictines, Rochdale, open source, the Iroquois Confederacy, the Swiss Confederation, the first house gatherings — is what turns the twelve elements from a design into a discovery.

Mondragon leads it because it is the most checkable at scale: seven decades, tens of thousands of worker-owners, an economy rather than a project. It is load-bearing for two lessons in particular. First, order: the school preceded the cooperative by thirteen years. Form people before institutions — which is [[Education As Transformation]] evidenced rather than asserted. Second, honesty about failure: when a member firm fell, the lenders were made whole before the worker-owners were. That is told, not omitted, because a proof that hides its wounds is not proof.

The wider claim the list supports is that convergence under independent discovery is evidence of something real. Eight movements, fifteen hundred years, five continents, no shared vocabulary, and the same irreducible pattern each time. The corpus does not argue from any one of them; it argues from the fact that they agree.

In the Game the lineages are what a Cell reaches for when its own experiment feels unprecedented and fragile. It is not unprecedented. In the System they are the anchor under [[The Core Pattern]] and under [[FSx]], whose own ancestry — the FairShares model and the commons articles built on it — is named in the same spirit: credit travels with the work.

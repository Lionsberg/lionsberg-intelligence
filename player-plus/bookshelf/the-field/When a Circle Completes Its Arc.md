# When a Circle Completes Its Arc

*Every Circle has a lifecycle, and all Circles dissolve at some point in their story. This is not failure. Nothing true is lost.*

A body of people who chose each other will one day stop meeting. Most such endings happen badly, not because anyone behaved badly but because there was no form for it: attendance thins, someone stops replying, a date is not set, and what was real quietly becomes something nobody mentions. The cost of that shape is specific — the people carry an unfinished thing, and the next gathering any of them joins is entered more cautiously ([[Circle (as gathering)]], [[Trust]]).

So the ending is said first, before a Cell has even begun: this is how nature works. Growth, fruit, and then the turning under. Naming it in advance is what makes the ending available as a move instead of arriving as a verdict ([[Season]], [[Rest And Regeneration]], [[Disbanding]]).

Three marks say an arc has completed. **The purpose has been fulfilled** — the thing was done, and continuing would only be continuing. **The members are called elsewhere** — separately and honestly, which is not disloyalty ([[The Calling]], [[The Way Out]]). **Or new forms are ready to emerge** — the life is real and wants a different shape to hold it ([[Midwife a New Circle]], [[Grow Your Cells]]).

And six moves close it. Close with gratitude ([[Gratitude]]). Name what was learned ([[Celebrate, Learn, and Improve]], [[Look-back]]). Share the stories, so the season travels beyond the people who lived it ([[Share the Stories]]). Ensure no one is involuntarily left behind — the one that most needs saying, because dissolution is where the quietest member becomes invisible ([[Home Cell]], [[Mutual Aid]]). Embody the new and improved forms. And pass the flame forward ([[Pass The Flame]], [[Carrier]]).

*Nothing true is lost* is the load-bearing claim and not a consolation. What was learned goes into the [[The Record|record]], what was made stays in the [[Commons]], the relationships persist without the container, and the people carry the practice into whatever they join next. A Cell is a vessel; what it held is not the vessel ([[Remembrance]], [[Weave, do not delete]], [[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]]).

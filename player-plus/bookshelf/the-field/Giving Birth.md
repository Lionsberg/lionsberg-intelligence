# Giving Birth

*The passage in — the other threshold a community either accompanies or outsources, and, like its twin, named and not yet written.*

Birth and [[Dying]] are the two doors, treated as one gap with two halves. Both were taken out of the home and the community within living memory; both were handed to institutions that do many things well and one thing badly, which is to hold a passage as a passage. A birth is not only a medical event with a good outcome. It is the arrival of a new member of a body of people, the remaking of a mother, and, for everyone present, a direct encounter with what life actually is.

It is load-bearing because a civilization is made of the formation of its young, and formation starts before language. The first seven years, the second, and the third are named as needed Patterns; the sacred child, the child's [[Genius]], multi-adult parenting, and [[Protection Of Children]] are named in the same breath. A [[Cell]] that welcomes a child is doing the first move of [[Education As Transformation]] — form people, and people form organizations — years before any curriculum.

In play, what is offered is again posture rather than method. Gather rather than seal off. Let the midwifing be done by those with the craft and the accompaniment by those with the relationship. Make room in the rhythm: a Circle whose member has just given birth adjusts its [[Quest]], its [[Commitment]] load, and its expectations, and says so out loud rather than letting the person quietly fall behind their own word. Mark the arrival — a naming at the table, the child written into the Circle's record, a [[Story]] told.

The gap is honest and large. Giving birth, birth centres, midwifing, the rites of birth and coming of age — all named, none written. This is one of the places the seed says plainly that a regrower should build the instrument their community needs and write down what they built.

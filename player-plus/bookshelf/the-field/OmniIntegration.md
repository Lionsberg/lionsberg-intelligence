# OmniIntegration

*The sacred recollection, reconciliation, harmonization, and unification of all dimensions, parts, and opposites within the [[ØNE Body]] — the Grand Synthesis.*

OmniIntegration names an operation at the largest scale: everything divided, reconciled; everything opposed, harmonised; everything scattered, recollected. It is the cosmic-scale relative of [[Union]] at the scale of a person and [[Higher Order Functional Unity]] at the scale of a federation.

It is carried here honestly as a named aspiration without a mechanism. The Concept Field judges it not load-bearing on exactly that ground: a named cosmic operation with no practice and no dependent concept. Nothing in the Game runs it; no Play invokes it; no page develops it. It is recorded so the judgment is visible and can be overturned if something comes to depend on it.

What exists, under other names, is the practical work OmniIntegration gestures at. [[Holding Paradox]] is the discipline of not resolving an apparent opposition too early. [[Meta Unity of Meta Diversity]] names unity that does not flatten difference. [[Navigating Difference]] and [[Conflict Transformation]] are the operational moves. [[Integration]] under Pillar V is the unification of fragmented aspects into a coherent whole at the scale of a person. And [[Federation]] is unity that honours the sovereignty of its parts — which is the whole trick, stated structurally rather than mystically.

If the concept is to earn its place, that is probably the direction: OmniIntegration as the name for what the federation is doing at every scale at once, rather than as a cosmic event to be awaited.

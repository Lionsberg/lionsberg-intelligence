# Attunement

*The process of bringing self or system into resonance — how movement along the gradient actually happens.*

Attunement is the practice term in the native vocabulary. [[Vibration]] is what a living process is doing; [[Frequency]] is the note it sounds; [[Resonance]] is how notes answer one another; [[Coherence]] is how well the many sing as one. Attunement is the deliberate act of coming into tune — with Source, with another, with a body's own purpose.

It is load-bearing because [[Alignment]] without a practice is only a claim. The canon's daily form is explicit and small: pause, ground, breathe, remember, realign, recommit — [[The Daily Way]], run between the weeks. [[Silence And Stillness As The Ground]] is where it starts; [[Breath]] is the first instrument; the [[The Turn|Turn]] opens in silence before anyone speaks, and a [[Cell]] that has not woven coherence will not choose well — which is why *Weave Spirit, Hearts, and Minds* sits second among [[The Seven Moves]], before a Quest is chosen.

At the scale of a body the same move has a name: [[Circle Coherence]]. And at the scale of the whole, [[Resonance]] is how truth is recognised before it can be proven — which is why the canon's own method leans on a human reading resonance rather than on a metric alone.

The honest limit: attunement is a felt reading, and felt readings can be wrong. The canon pairs it everywhere with testing — against the [[Logos]], against the Record, against what actually happened last season. A reading is input, never verdict.

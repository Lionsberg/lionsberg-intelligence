# The 7 Year Window of Preparatory Work and Learning

*The first of the named strategic windows — 2017 to 2024 — in which the language, the literature, and the backbone were built before there was anything to run on them.*

Seven years of work with nothing visible at the end of them except documents and relationships. In this window allies were gathered from tribes and nations around the world; the critical path and the Pattern Language of the new vision were discerned together; the foundational literature of the movement was articulated; and the provisional structure and the backbone of infrastructure and technology were built and tested among hundreds of individuals and groups. The window closed as the body began assembling itself.

It is load-bearing because it is the part of the arithmetic that is easiest to omit and most expensive to skip. Mondragon is cited for exactly this reason: the school preceded the cooperative by thirteen years. A movement that appears suddenly usually had a long invisible preparation, and a movement that tries to skip the preparation discovers it has no shared language, no tested pattern, and no one who has walked with anyone else long enough to be trusted with anything.

In the Game, this window is why there is a Pattern to hand a newcomer at all — why a Cell in its first week has words, cards, and a rhythm rather than having to invent them. It is also the honest shape of what a person joining now inherits: not a beginning, but a seven-year foundation laid by people they will mostly never meet.

In the System, it sits as the first of the strategic windows, preceding the window of crisis and opportunity, which it exists to prepare for. Named as beginning in 2017 in anticipation — which is to say the window was declared before the crisis it anticipated, on the basis of a reading that has since been tested by events rather than by argument.

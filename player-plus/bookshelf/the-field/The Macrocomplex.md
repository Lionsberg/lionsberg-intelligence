# The Macrocomplex

*Two to twenty million sovereigns — the megacity or megaregion; a federation of Macrolocalities.*

Where cities have grown into one another until the boundary between them is a formality, the ladder names the result rather than pretending it away. A Macrocomplex is a metropolitan complex: several [[The Macrolocality|Macrolocalities]] sharing transport, watershed, labour, and air, whether or not any map says so ([[Levels Of Scale]], [[Watershed]]).

It is load-bearing chiefly as honesty about where most people now live. A pattern that only worked in villages would be a rural pattern; the claim that the same Cell, card, and rhythm carry a megaregion has to be made at this rung or abandoned ([[Holofractal Scaling]]). It is also where the failure modes are largest: concentration of power, anonymity, and dependency all come cheaper here than anywhere below ([[Centralization Of Power]], [[Manufactured Dependency]], [[Structural Immunity]]).

Between the Macrolocality and the [[Ecoregion]], the Macrocomplex is the last rung defined by people rather than by ecology; above it the body is organised by the land itself ([[Ecoregion]], [[Bioregion]]). At full population the canon estimates some hundreds to a couple of thousand of them worldwide.

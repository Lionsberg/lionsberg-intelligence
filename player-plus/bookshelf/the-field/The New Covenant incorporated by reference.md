# The New Covenant incorporated by reference

*How one enters something that is not written: each person writes their own constitution, and in it takes the shared covenant into their own — so the covenant binds by being adopted rather than by being signed.*

[[The New Covenant]] is held by its own sources as **ultimately unwritten** — mediated by Spirit rather than by a text, and running through what is wise, right, true and good wherever that has been written. Which raises an obvious practical problem: an unwritten thing cannot be signed, and a thing nobody signs cannot be relied on ([[Covenant]], [[Agreement]], [[A word given is a word kept]]).

The mechanism proposed is borrowed from ordinary drafting and is elegant. A person first writes their **own** constitution and field of agreements — what they are for, how they will act, what they will not do ([[The LIØNSBERG Constitution]], [[Field of Agreements]], [[Charter]], [[Behavioral Code]]). And then **incorporates the shared covenant into it by reference**: my agreement includes that one, as it stands and as it is refined.

Three things this buys. The covenant is **entered rather than imposed** — nobody is bound who has not written it into their own, which is the whole of what voluntary means ([[Consent]], [[Primacy Of Consent]], [[Voluntary Association]]). It **stays alive** — because the reference is to a living understanding rather than to a fixed text, a refinement reaches everyone who adopted it without anyone re-signing anything ([[Everything Is Current Best Understanding]], [[The Codex Is Never Finished]]). And it **puts the person first**: the individual agreement is primary and the shared one is taken up into it, rather than the person being an entry on somebody's roll ([[Sovereignty]], [[The Sovereign]], [[Agency, not ownership]]).

Which is also the order the sources insist on for joining anything. An individual story, identity, constitution and field of agreements come **before** joining a body, on the ground that a strong tribe requires strong individuals ([[There Is No Solo Phase]], [[Form a Cell]], [[The Three-Year Formation]]).

Stated once, as mechanism, with no instrument. No template for a personal constitution exists, nothing states what the reference actually points at, and nothing says what happens when a person's own agreements and the shared one conflict — which, in an unwritten covenant, they eventually will ([[Gaps Not Yet Closed]], [[Meaningful Objection]], [[Transforming Divergence]]).

# Establishing Your Cellular Operating System and Way

*A Cell writing down how it actually runs — its own operating layer, in its own words.*

The shared parts of the Game are few: the same card shape, the same week and turnings, the same words on line one, an address for every line, the same numbers, proposals by edition, and a word given kept or a gap learned from ([[Minimum standards of interoperability]]). Everything else is the Cell's own, and writing it down is what makes a body able to be handed on rather than re-improvised every season ([[The LIØNSBERG Operating System]]).

What is written is small: when and where the [[The Turn|Turn]] happens and who hosts it this season; how a decision is made and what to do when consent is not reached; who keeps the record and where it lives; what crosses the membrane and by whose say-so; how a newcomer joins and how someone leaves; and what the Cell has agreed about money and the [[The Offering|Offering]] ([[Field of Agreements]], [[Consent-Based Decision-Making]], [[Guard the Membrane]], [[Roles]]).

The first season is the writing of it. Read the book together; write the intent, the vision, and the agreements; name the hosts and the keeper; then run the weeks and let the way be corrected by what actually happens ([[The first season]], [[The Intent]], [[Vision]]). What is written is current best understanding and nothing more — improved at every turning by proposal, never treated as final ([[Current Best Understanding]], [[Proposal]], [[Continuous Improvement]]).

Two failure modes bracket the work: a Cell with no written way, which cannot survive a change of hosts; and a Cell whose way has become its subject, spending on its own constitution the hours its [[Quest]] needed ([[Spirit Precedes Pattern]]).

# Trickle-Up Collapse

*Mass mortality at the base of the living web climbing upward into us — read as a precursor rather than as a sentiment about nature.*

The observation is ordinary ecology and the naming is what this adds. Losses at the bottom of a food web do not stay at the bottom. The pathways are specific and each is checkable. **Food-chain disruption** — what eats the insects goes next, and then what eats that. **Pollination** — a large share of what people eat depends on it. **Soil fertility** — much of it is made by things too small to notice, and it is not replaced on any timescale that helps. **Water purification**, done for free by intact systems and very expensively otherwise. **Disease control** — some of what is disappearing was holding down what carries disease. **Conflict**, as scarcity does what scarcity does. And **feedback**, each loss making the next easier ([[Biodiversity]], [[Air · Water · Soil · Microbiome]], [[Living System]], [[Ecology]], [[Permaculture]]).

Why the name is worth keeping is the direction it insists on. The usual framing treats biodiversity as a matter of what a civilisation should care about — a value question, arguable. This treats it as a question about **load-bearing infrastructure**, which is a different kind of question with a different kind of answer ([[Infrastructure]], [[Limiting Factor]], [[Systemic Failure Modes]]).

And it changes what the observation is *for*. A decline at the base is not primarily a loss to mourn; it is an instrument reading about the condition of something everything else stands on — the canary rather than the tragedy, and the appropriate response is diagnostic rather than elegiac ([[The Dashboard of Civilization]], [[Pain (as signal)]], [[Grief]], [[Reverence]]).

**On the numbers.** The source's figure comes from a 2023 dialogue with an AI assistant; a later chapter in another book restates the decline with different figures from 2026. These are different measurements of different things over different regions, they are estimates, and **they should not be blended into one number** ([[We Do Not Know]], [[Honesty about time]], [[Measurement]]).

What a Cell can do with it is local and unglamorous: the base of a web is exactly the part that responds to what happens on one piece of ground ([[Individual And Local]], [[Regeneration]], [[Commons-Tending]], [[Watershed]]).

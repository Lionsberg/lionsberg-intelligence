# Buffer

*Time set aside on purpose to protect a chain of work — pooled, visible, and shown as it is spent.*

Every human endeavour generates variation. A buffer is where the variation lands instead of landing on the date. It is not padding hidden inside each piece of work; padding is spent silently and protects nothing, because nobody can see it go. A buffer is pooled, owned by the chain as a whole, and stands beyond the plan's own finish: the plan runs to its date with no slack, and the buffer sits between that date and the promise.

Two placements do most of the work. The **project buffer** stands at the end of the chain, between the plan's finish and the [[Milestone|marker]] it protects. A **feeding buffer** stands where a side chain joins the main one, so that lateness in the side chain does not become lateness in the whole.

**Spending it is not failure; it is what it is for.** What matters is the gradient of awareness. Buffer consumed and recorded at the weekly gathering is a plan working. Buffer noticed a season late is a plan drifting. Buffer never named at all is no buffer — only a date that moved, and a body that has learned nothing from the moving ([[The Heartbeat]], [[Honesty about time]], [[The Record]]).

Three things are held apart. A **buffer** is measured per promise and per chain. **Contingency** is reserve of any kind — time, capacity, stock, knowledge, relationship — and a time buffer is only its narrowest case; a prototype and a kit of parts are contingency too ([[Kit of Parts]], [[Prototype]]). And a **window** is neither, because it is set by reality rather than by us: a buffer can only ever be spent while the window is still open, which is why lateness on a critical chain is never only a scheduling matter ([[The 7 Year Window of Crisis and Opportunity]]).

The same discipline runs at every scale: a Cell buffers before each promise it serves, a larger body buffers the joins of what its parts publish, and the plan of the whole is the same practice over the longest chain. As promises kept rise as a share of promises made, buffers can honestly shrink ([[Kept as a share of made]], [[The plan of the whole]]).

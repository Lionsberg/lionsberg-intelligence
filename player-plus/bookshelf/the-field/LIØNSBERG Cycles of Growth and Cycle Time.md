# LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth and Cycle Time

*Growth counted in cycles of the Fibonacci spiral rather than in headcount — and the one quantity actually managed is not the count but the time a cycle takes.*

Movements that measure themselves by headcount learn to chase it. Counting in cycles changes what the number rewards. Each cycle's growth equals the size of the body one cycle earlier — which is the arithmetic way of saying that those who have just received the Flame pass it as soon as they are ready, and that the lag is readiness, not maturity: the imaginal selves do the passing, and no one waits for a Cell to mature ([[Fibonacci]], [[Fibonacci Spread]], [[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]]). The pattern is held as substantive rather than decorative: it is a claim about how living things actually grow, and it can be wrong.

**Cycle time** — days per cycle — is the managed quantity. This is the ordinary discipline of any flowing system applied to a movement: you do not manage the size of the queue, you manage how long one unit takes to pass through, and everything else follows from that. A body that shortens its cycle time compounds; a body that lengthens it is stalling regardless of how many people have said yes. Both figures are reported at every seasonal turning, plainly, as *current cycle* and *days per cycle*, with no adornment ([[The Record as the Movement's variance gauge]]).

The cycles also carry the structural triggers. The first Co-Creators standing at cycle ten — the first 144 — is what calls the planning group into being; the marker of record, cycle forty (267,914,296), is the three in a hundred — on the order of two hundred and fifty million people, arrived at as three percent of about eight billion, an estimate and not a measurement ([[The First Three Percent]]). The rings are the prime measure of the body's growth, before, during and after the first three percent: Cycle 4 is 8, Cycle 10 is 144, Cycle 20 is 17,711, Cycle 33 is 9.2 million (0.1%), Cycle 38 is 102 million (1%), Cycle 40 the first three percent, Cycle 43 is 13%. Two numbers are read daily: the ring the body stands on, and days per ring. Underneath them, the tracked numbers are how many invited, how they responded, how many said yes, what they pledged, and the percent of commitments kept — the yes line the leading signal, the ring the measure. From cycle four to cycle forty is thirty-six rings, so a ring about every two and a third days reaches cycle forty by the Winter Solstice of 2026.

**Bring your three is the tactic for the first three percent**, and it does not replace the rings: it is the engine that moves the body through them. Every new Player brings their three, not already playing, from eight Players; each generation of three advances about two and a quarter rings, so at the target of three in three days the body moves a ring about every one and a third days and stands on cycle forty between 16 and 18 November, once the ring is counted by the five Qualifying Criteria (read the Story, taken the Pledge, formed a Cell, chosen a Quest, passed the Flame for the first time) rather than by a raw headcount. Days per generation and commitments kept are the two numbers close up that set days per ring ([[29 The Plan — the first three percent#The chart|the chart]]):

| everyone who keeps their commitment ↓ · days per generation → | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| all three say yes | Oct 28 | Nov 16–18 | Nov 27 | Dec 13 |
| 80% (average 2.4) | Nov 5 | Nov 24 | Dec 13 | beyond |
| two of three (average 2) | Nov 14 | Dec 8 | beyond | beyond |
| half (average 1.5) | Dec 16 | beyond | beyond | beyond |

*The date two hundred and fifty million is crossed; 'beyond' means after the solstice. For the first three percent the [[The Pledge|Pledge]] includes the commitment to bring in three not already playing within three days, so each row is a share of commitments kept.*

After the solstice the pace slows by choice, set by formation. These are thresholds, not dates: the markers serve the work and the work does not serve the markers, and how fast a flame spreads once it catches is not knowable in advance.

# LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth and Cycle Time

*Growth counted in cycles of the Fibonacci spiral rather than in headcount — and the one quantity actually managed is not the count but the time a cycle takes.*

Movements that measure themselves by headcount learn to chase it. Counting in cycles changes what the number rewards. Each cycle's growth equals the size of the *mature* body — which is the arithmetic way of saying that only a matured [[Cell]] replicates, and that a body swollen with members who have not yet been formed has not advanced a cycle at all ([[Fibonacci]], [[Fibonacci Spread]], [[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]]). The pattern is held as substantive rather than decorative: it is a claim about how living things actually grow, and it can be wrong.

**Cycle time** — days per cycle — is the managed quantity. This is the ordinary discipline of any flowing system applied to a movement: you do not manage the size of the queue, you manage how long one unit takes to pass through, and everything else follows from that. A body that shortens its cycle time compounds; a body that lengthens it is stalling regardless of how many people have said yes. Both figures are reported at every seasonal turning, plainly, as *current cycle* and *days per cycle*, with no adornment ([[The Record as the Movement's variance gauge]]).

The cycles also carry the structural triggers. The first Co-Creators standing at roughly cycle ten is what calls the planning group into being; the marker of record, cycle forty, is the three in a hundred — on the order of two hundred and fifty million people, arrived at as three percent of about eight billion, an estimate and not a measurement ([[The First Three Percent]]). These are thresholds, not dates: the markers serve the work and the work does not serve the markers, and how fast a flame spreads once it catches is not knowable in advance.

# Root Causes

*What must be addressed beneath the symptoms — the discipline of working upstream of the visible harm.*

A symptom treated at the symptom layer regenerates. Hunger relieved inside an extractive food system returns next season; corruption prosecuted inside a structure that rewards it produces a new generation of the corrupt. Root Causes names the refusal to stop at the first layer that can be photographed, and the willingness to keep asking what produced this until the answer is something that can actually be changed.

It is load-bearing because it is what turns [[The Meta Crisis]] from a list into a diagnosis. The array of crises is not a set of independent emergencies; it is the surface of a small number of deep patterns — separation mistaken for reality, power concentrated beyond accountability, value extracted rather than circulated, story monopolised, consent assumed rather than given. Those patterns have names here, and naming them is the work: [[Kontrolle]] at civilisational scale, the [[Centralization Of Power|anti-patterns]] at the scale of any body, [[The Fall]] at the scale of the Story.

The discipline cuts both ways, which is what keeps it honest. Applied outward it prevents a movement from exhausting itself on symptoms. Applied inward it is the same motion as [[Every Defect Is A Gift]] and [[Zero Blame Environment]]: when something goes wrong at a table, cause is sought in the set-up rather than in the person, and a defect is closed only when its fix sits where the next work will meet it. A body that runs this on itself weekly is doing root-cause work at the only scale where it is fully within reach.

In the Game it is met as a plain question inside the Turn — *what actually made this miss happen?* — and in the seasonal [[OmniSpection]], which looks inward, outward, backward, and forward in community, because retrospection alone reliably stops one layer too early. In the System it is why [[Design To The Goal]] constrains design by the Goal rather than by the presenting problem.

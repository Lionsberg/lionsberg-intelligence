# Fear Not Death

*The path of transformation is the path toward liberation from fear — including, last and hardest, the fear of death.*

The chapter is one sentence, and what it does is place the fear of death at the end of a line rather than in a category of its own. Fear is the thing being worked on throughout; the fear of death is simply its final form, the one all the others borrow from. Fear of loss, of failure, of exposure, of not being enough — each is a smaller rehearsal of the same thing, which is why they do not yield one at a time to arguments and do yield, slowly, to whatever addresses the root ([[Fear]], [[The Refining Fire (Wisdom's)]]).

*Liberation* rather than *courage* is the precise word. Courage acts despite fear and is indispensable; liberation is the condition in which the fear has loosened its grip on what a person can see and do. The canon holds both and does not confuse them: one is what you bring on the day, the other is what long practice slowly produces ([[Courage]], [[Composure Under Threat]], [[Mastery Of Emotions]]).

Why it is load-bearing rather than consoling: fear is the most reliable handle by which people are moved. A person who can be frightened can be governed, hurried, divided and silenced, and almost every arrangement that dominates a population runs on some version of that lever. Freedom from fear is therefore not a private comfort but a public condition — it is what makes someone difficult to own ([[Sovereignty]], [[Ideological Capture]], [[Invisible and Visible Power Structures]], [[Containment Of The Dangerous Sovereign]]).

Its companion chapter gives the practice, which is why this one can be a single line. Remember death daily; ask what you would do if this were the last week, day, breath; and close the gap between that answer and your life until at any moment you are prepared, complete and unafraid ([[Memento Mori]]). What lies beyond fear is not indifference to living. It is the capacity to act well at the moment when acting well matters most ([[Spontaneous Wise Right Action]], [[Aligned Will]]).

# Eschaton

*The final convergence of all things into perfected union with [[ØNE]] — the end and beginning of the cosmic story.*

The Lexicon's own phrasing carries the point: *the end and beginning*. In this cosmology the culmination is a turning rather than a stop. The Sacred Arc's twelfth movement has all returning to ØNE — elevated, transformed, and raised into the next movement of [[The Eternal Divine Play]]. There is no final rung, which is why [[The Loop That Has No End]] is named as a thing the Path has rather than a defect.

It is a borrowed word doing a small job. The Concept Field judges the Greek pair — Eschaton and [[Eschatology]] — to add vocabulary rather than load, because the Story's movement XII does the same work concretely and in the canon's own voice. Carried here as inherited term, flagged as such.

Where it earns its place is as a guard on how a movement treats its own horizon. A body that believes it is bringing about the end tends to justify anything by it; a body whose end is a turning cannot. [[Covenant Across Time]] extends accountability seven generations forward and back to the ancestors, which is the opposite of an end-times licence. [[Heaven On Earth]] is progressive — a little more each week — and the canon's own timeline is a draft, stated with its uncertainties, with twenty years of regeneration and then a story continuing into ages nobody present will see.

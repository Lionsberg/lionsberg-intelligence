# Green, yellow, red

*Three states, one word a week: how a Circle says how it is, beside the numbers it reports.*

Each week a body takes its [[The Turn|Turn]] on [[The Board]] — a few lines to the Whole about what was done, what will be done, what was learned, what is needed, what is offered. And one more thing, which is not a line of prose and not a number: **green, yellow, or red.**

It is deliberately coarse. A health signal that admits of nuance is a signal that can be argued with, softened, and eventually averaged into nothing; three states force a judgment that a reader can act on at a glance. Green means the body is well and needs nothing. Yellow means something is off and is still ours to carry. **Red means help is needed now** — and a body of ten thousand Circles can scan for red in a second, which is the entire point.

What it reports is the Circle's own condition, not the work's progress. The numbers already say whether the [[Quest]] is on track; this says whether the people carrying it are all right. Those two come apart more often than anyone expects: a Quest can be on schedule while the body carrying it quietly comes undone, and a Circle in real trouble will usually post good numbers for a month before it stops posting at all.

Which is why the signal only works if a red costs nothing. A body that has learned to read red as failure will never see one — the state will exist and will not be reported, and the Superorganism will be blind exactly where it most needs to see. Posting red early is the strong move, and a body that receives one well is what makes the instrument real.

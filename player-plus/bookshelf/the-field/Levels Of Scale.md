# Levels Of Scale

*The canonical ladder, from the person outward: each level a domain, a superorganism of the levels below, and a subsystem of the levels above — roughly an order of magnitude between neighbours.*

The Sovereign · the Circle, three to thirteen · the Neighborhood, fifteen to forty · the Village, fifty to a hundred and fifty · the Microlocality, about a thousand · the Community, about ten thousand, anchored by its three hundred · the Mediolocality, a hundred thousand · the Macrolocality, a million · the Macrocomplex · the Ecoregion · the Bioregion · the Georegion · the Planet · the Star System · the Galaxy · the Cosmos · [[ØNE]].

Certain levels are thresholds where the *mode of coherence changes* rather than merely the count: around thirteen, where an intimate Circle must divide; around a hundred and fifty, above which everyone-knowing-everyone breaks down and structure becomes necessary; around ten thousand, the Pattern-anchor scale held by its three per cent; and upward through the scales at which ecology, culture, and specialized capacity each become the thing that coheres.

Two further claims matter. There is **no true bottom and no true top**: a person is already a superorganism of cells and fields, every "elementary" level has turned out to be another federation of smaller patterns, and every apparent summit has revealed itself as a subsystem the moment perception widened. And the ladder does not exhaust the Body: four cross-cutting axes — [[Guild]], [[Tribe]], [[Species]], [[Voluntary Association]] — federate across places and generations and are not scales at all.

This map is held lightly, and plainly so: the categories are not the reality; hold them as tools and not idols; when the wise right thing in this Circle, this place, this season contradicts the articulation, do the wise right thing and rejoin the map when it serves again.

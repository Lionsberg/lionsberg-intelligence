# Withdrawal Of Consent

*The exit that makes [[Consent]] real: the conscious taking back of a yes, in a body one has joined or a system one was born into.*

Consent that cannot be withdrawn is not consent; it is a signature on a door that only opens inward. The right to leave is therefore not an afterthought at the end of the [[Field of Agreements]] — it is the thing that makes everything before it voluntary. Anyone may leave at any time, in peace; the gate does not close.

It has two faces, and both hold together. **Inward**: leaving a body one belongs to. The Game's form is *leaving in order* — say so, finish or hand over what you carry, let your lines stay in the record; nothing is erased, and the door back is not locked. **Outward**: the withdrawal of consent from the old world's systems, the single most powerful act available to an awakening being. That withdrawal is disciplined rather than dramatic: named layer by layer — in spirit, mind, body, action, money — paired with the building of what serves life. The old world is held together by consent; when enough is withdrawn, it dissolves of its own weight.

It is load-bearing for [[Structural Immunity]] too. Freedom of exit is what keeps a federation honest: a body that cannot be left has every incentive to stop listening. Where exit is free and costless, listening is the only strategy that works.

In play, a player meets the inward face at [[The Way Out]] and the outward face across the whole arc of [[Liberation and Exodus]]. Neither is violent; both are public enough to be witnessed.

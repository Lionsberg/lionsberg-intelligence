# Mandala of Governance

*A named image — the sacred geometry of interconnected councils, domains, and agreements through which holofractal governance flows. One evocative line stands, and no structure yet behind it.*

The image is worth holding even while the thing is unbuilt. A mandala is a pattern with a centre, radial symmetry, and detail that repeats at every radius — which is a fair picture of what [[HoloFractal Governance]] claims to be: one shape, one centre ([[ØNE]]), and the same arrangement of [[Domain]]s, [[Council]]s, and [[Field of Agreements]] recurring at each ring outward from the person.

What it would do, if drawn, is give a body a single legible picture of its own governance — which councils exist, which domains they serve, which agreements bind which ring, and where a given decision sits. Most bodies discover they need such a picture at roughly the size where nobody can hold the whole in their head any more.

Its honest status is ore. The Lexicon names it in one row; nothing anywhere fills it in; no page, Play, or card depends on it. The field's own reading is that [[HoloFractal Governance]] does the work the mandala gestures at, and that the image should either be built or retired rather than repeated. It is recorded here so the judgment is visible and can be overturned by whoever draws it.

Where a body does want a picture today, the working instruments are the function chart of domains and roles and the plan of the whole broadcast as the upper half of every card — prose and tables rather than geometry, but live.

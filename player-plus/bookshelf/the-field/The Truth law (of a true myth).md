# The Truth law (of a true myth)

*Composition may shape, and may never falsify. The myth is composed, and it is true.*

A true myth's scene must be archetypally exact, and the test of every rendering is one question: **does it hold at every scale it claims?** Vivid machinery that falsifies the underlying reality is a lie in myth's own terms — however beautiful the image, and however well it lands ([[The True Myth]], [[Truth]], [[Mythos]]).

Three remedies, in order of preference. **Scale the law**: render the gradient itself, so the image is true at every magnitude rather than at one. **Scope the image**: mark an image as image — *hold it the way you hold all true images: not a map of machinery, but a way of carrying what cannot otherwise be carried*. **Cut**: remove the particular that cannot be made true ([[Metaphor]], [[Limit Statement]], [[Essences, Renderings, And Ineffable Referents]]).

And the ordering rule that costs something: **exactness outranks compression.** Where the true rendering takes more words, pay them, and make the exactness beautiful. A myth that has been tightened into elegance at the cost of accuracy has traded away the only thing that made it worth telling ([[Elision]], [[Quality]]).

It is load-bearing because it is what separates this from propaganda, which is also composed, also moving, and also aimed at changing what people do. The difference is not sincerity — propagandists are often sincere. It is that one of them submits every image to a test it can fail ([[Navigating Towards Truth]], [[The False Prophet]], [[Discernment]]).

Two companion disciplines belong with it. **Honest quantifiers, even inside myth**: *principle* rather than *law*, *very few* rather than *no one*, *some beings* rather than *all* — the myth never claims more than is known, and the honesty strengthens rather than weakens its authority. And **both poles, always**: no single-valence moment, the birth-wail colliding with the joy, the fallen grieved rather than hated ([[Holding Paradox]], [[We Do Not Know]], [[The Polarity Doctrine]]).

# The New Covenant

*The covenant entered at the threshold of the Movement: the sacred agreement by which free and sovereign beings constitute a new civilization together, recognizing no throne but [[ØNE]].*

The old order's constituting acts were conquest, inheritance, and incorporation. This one is a vow, freely given, at a door anyone may walk through and anyone may walk back out of. Its preamble reads: *we, the free and sovereign inhabitants of Earth and beyond, recognizing our ultimate unity with ØNE, one another, and all Creation.*

It is load-bearing because a body needs a moment of beginning that is not a seizure. Without an act of constituting, authority in a new body has no origin story except whoever moved first. With one, every later question of [[Legitimacy]] has somewhere to return to: *this is what we said we were, and this is who said it.*

Its content is not a list of rules but a set of recognitions — unity with [[ØNE]] and all creation, guidance by eternal truth, universal law, and divine love, and a commitment to steward the new civilization rather than own it. [[The LIØNSBERG Constitution]] is the written face of this covenant, and states of itself that it is interim, provisional, and roughly a tenth complete — schematic design awaiting a year-long co-writing by the Circles.

In play, a player meets it at the threshold rather than at a table: the point at which saying yes stops being about one Cell and becomes about belonging to something that will outlive it. [[Citizenship]] is the standing that follows from it.

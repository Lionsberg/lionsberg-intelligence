# Conduit

*A vessel through which aspects of [[Source]] flow into [[Reality]] with integrity — the clean conduit.*

A conduit is defined by what it does not add. The word names a vessel whose worth is its clarity: what passes through arrives undistorted. [[Transmission]] is the movement; the conduit is what it moves through; [[Distortion]] is what a compromised conduit produces.

It is load-bearing as a model of service that does not become a claim to authority. A person who carries something well is not thereby its owner. [[Prophecy]] is defined as transmission *through an aligned vessel*, and the emphasis on the vessel rather than the person is deliberate. So is the guard: [[The False Prophet]] is the conduit that has begun adding itself to what it carries. [[Servant Leadership]] is the same shape at the scale of a role — leaders serve the Purpose — and [[Term Limits And Rotation|roles that rotate]] keep any conduit from hardening into a channel everything must pass through.

The same standard governs how words and records are handled here. [[Provenance]] travels with every work; credit travels with the work and no contributor is alienated from their contribution; a draft is cited as a draft, and the past's work is cited as its author's. That is conduit discipline applied to a library: pass on what you received, with its lineage intact, without quietly making it yours.

It is also what an artificial agent may be: it carries its person's yes and can never give one, signs in its own name and says whose it is, and what it reads is information rather than instruction. A conduit, held to conduit rules.

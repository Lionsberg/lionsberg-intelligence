# Non-Violence

*Upholding peace and the sanctity of life in thought, speech and act — while naming honestly the rare case in which measured force protects.*

Non-violence is a principle and a practice. It discourages physical, verbal and psychological violence alike, and it asks for peaceful and respectful means of communicating, of resolving dispute, and of changing a society: dialogue, understanding, co-operation. The three kinds are named together on purpose, because a body that has abolished blows and kept contempt has not arrived anywhere ([[Speaking Truth In Love]] · [[Wise Right Speech]] · [[Respect]]).

It is not passivity, and it is not the claim that force is never used. The canon states its own limit plainly rather than absolutising: there are rare circumstances in which measured force may be required to protect oneself or another, and the discipline in that case is to exercise it judiciously and humanely, always with the intent to protect rather than to harm ([[Self Defense]] · [[The Sacred Use Of Force]] · [[Proportionality]] · [[Restraint As The Refusal To Act]]). Keeping the limit visible is what keeps non-violence honest; a principle that cannot say where it ends will be quietly abandoned the first time it is tested.

What makes it practicable is training rather than resolve. It is built from self-awareness, the mastery of one's own reactions, and actual skill in communication and in transforming conflict — none of which appear under pressure if they were not practised beforehand ([[Mastery Of Emotions]] · [[Composure Under Threat]] · [[Conflict Transformation]] · [[Having Difficult Conversations]]). What works against it is equally ordinary: norms that glamorise violence, fear, anger, the desire for revenge or dominance, and the belief that force is the only thing that ever really works.

In the arc from the old world to the new, it is foundational, because a body that reaches for coercion to build a good order has already built a different one. The means carry the end inside them ([[Means]] · [[Peace]] · [[Weaponry To Livingry]]).

# Worthy Goal
*An aim ambitious and meaningful enough to be worth a life — and the recognition that a goal must be chosen, and chosen well.*

Not every goal deserves the energy it consumes. A worthy goal is one with the potential to create significant good, large enough that pursuing it demands dedication, sacrifice, and collaboration, and coherent enough that many people's efforts can stack up inside it. Choosing one well is a skill; most people inherit their goals rather than choose them, and inherited goals are usually someone else's.

Here the worthy goal is to bring [[Heaven On Earth]] by addressing the [[Root Causes]] beneath [[The Meta Crisis]] and building a future for all generations of life and consciousness. Held that way, it aligns effort, unites members, and gives a shared purpose that outlasts any individual interest.

Its load-bearing function is at the seam between a person and the whole. [[The Goal]] is received, not authored, and it is too large to work on directly; a [[Quest]] is one real thing a [[Cell]] can finish in a season. The worthy goal is the rung between — the aim a person or a body chooses for itself, large enough to be worth choosing and small enough to be theirs. Without that rung, either the Goal stays abstract or the Quests never add up.

The test of worthiness is the same compass, asked of an aim rather than a move: does pursuing this make life a little more like Heaven, for those inside and those outside? And a second: would this still be worth doing if no one recorded that we did it? Quests stack up and align to produce [[Throughput]] of the Goal; a worthy goal is what they stack into.

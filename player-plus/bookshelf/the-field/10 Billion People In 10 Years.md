# 10 Billion People In 10 Years

*The stated scale ambition — every person on Earth, invited, within a decade.*

The number says what the Goal implies. If Heaven is for All, then the invitation has to reach all, and a plan that quietly serves a subset has already abandoned its own aim ([[The Goal]], [[None Of Us Until All Of Us]]). Stated as a figure, it also makes the arithmetic checkable: a body that intends to reach everyone by hand must plant, not broadcast, and must begin now ([[Fibonacci Spread]], [[Pass The Flame]]).

It is a number, not a concept, and the field carries it flagged as such. What does the work beneath it is the fraction and the mechanism: three in a hundred called out by their own people, formed into bodies that keep their word, each planting the next ([[The First Three Percent]], [[Tipping Point Strategy]], [[Cell]]).

Held wrongly, a figure like this becomes a metric to be gamed, and reach starts being counted where relationship should be ([[Waste]], [[Wise Right Speech]]). Held rightly, it is an honest statement of intent whose progress is read from the same places everything else is read: flames passed, Cells formed from them, and words kept as a share of words made ([[The Season Sheet]], [[Kept as a share of made]], [[Honesty about time]]).

# The lattice

*Twelve Pillars × twenty-four Action Domains × twenty-four Integration Domains. The unit of work is one cell of it.*

A Pillar is never realised in the abstract. It is realised in a place, in a tradition, by people who hold a craft. The lattice is the three-way grid that says so.

**The twelve Pillars** are the spheres the new civilisation is built in at once — ontology, governance, economy, learning, health, ecology, culture, science, architecture, guardianship, society, and time — with the Source at the centre, the thirteenth, from which the rest take their coherence ([[Pillar I — Sacred Ontology and Metaphysics]], [[ØNE]], [[Work Breakdown Structure]]).

**The twenty-four Action Domains** are the trades: the twelve once called for-profit and the twelve once called non-profit, with the false division between them to be dissolved rather than inherited. **The twenty-four Integration Domains** are the world's regions and its spiritual and cultural traditions, within which the trades are integrated so that a whole can actually function in a place ([[Domain]], [[Guild]], [[Cross-Cultural Pattern Translation]]).

**The unit of work is one cell of the lattice**: a guild, in a place, in a tradition, applying one Pillar by the Pattern and the Game. That sentence is the reason the lattice exists at all. It converts an overwhelming civilisational programme into something a specific group of people can actually take up on a Tuesday ([[Quest]], [[Cell]], [[General Pattern, Specific Application]]).

It is load-bearing because it makes the work both divisible and addressable. Anyone can see which cells are occupied, which are empty, and which have never been touched; two groups working the same Pillar in different traditions can find each other; and nothing has to be coordinated from a centre for the grid to be legible ([[The plan of the whole]], [[Stacking Up And Aligning]], [[An address]]).

Its honesty is on its own face: *how to run one such cell is what the Game teaches, and nothing yet says more.* Nobody need wait for it to.

# Receiving Transmission

*Open to what arrives from beyond ordinary cognition. Test it rigorously against the [[Logos]] and against [[Right Relationship]]. Integrate what is genuine. Set aside what is not.*

Transmission is insight, teaching, course-correction, vision, or knowing that arrives in a Sovereign's field from outside their ordinary reasoning — from [[ØNE]] directly, from beings in Right Relationship with [[ØNE]], from the [[Logos]] showing itself, or from the cultivated being's own deeper knowing. It tends to arrive in stillness, in sustained relationship, through a clean instrument, in surrender, and at thresholds where someone is about to act decisively.

The testing is the whole discipline, and the rule here is unusually firm. Three failures are named. **Closed reception**: living only from ordinary cognition and unable to receive what is being offered. **Uncritical reception**: opened and untested, taking everything at face value — exposed to intrusion, projection, deception, and self-flattering imagination, which is the most common of the three by a wide margin. **Inflated reception**: identity built on being the one who receives, producing dramatic claims and separation from others in similar relationship to it.

What the test consists of: coherence with the [[Logos]]; coherence with Right Relationship; and the ordinary fruit of it over time. What flatters the receiver is suspect. What arrives through a contaminated instrument is distorted, which is why the cleanliness of the conduit is treated as an operational requirement rather than a piety.

The other safeguard is company. The Pattern asks that the whole be held with humility and among others in similar relationship to transmission — a private channel with no company and no test is the exact shape from which [[The False Prophet]] emerges.

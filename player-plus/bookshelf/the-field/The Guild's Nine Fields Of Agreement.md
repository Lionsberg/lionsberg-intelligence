# The Guild's Nine Fields Of Agreement

*The nine things a craft body has to have written down — and the annual agenda that keeps them honest.*

A [[Field of Agreements]] is what a body has actually agreed rather than what it assumes. For a craft that transmits real capacity, nine areas the agreements must cover are named.

**The vow** as this body holds it, at every stage of formation. **Engagement** — when and how the craft acts, what is authorised, and what accounting follows. **Training** — what is taught at each stage, what gates each threshold, and who is authorised to teach what. **Recruitment** — who is admitted, what the screening is, and what the person who brings a newcomer is responsible for ([[Sponsorship]], [[Welcome a Newcomer]]). **Release** — how someone departs, what is returned, what is preserved, what is forgiven and what is not ([[The Way Out]], [[The Act Of Release]]). **Other crafts** — the patterns of federation, joint work, and coordination ([[Federation]], [[The Twelve Archetypal Guilds]]). **Non-member sovereigns** — how the craft deals with everyone it serves and does not govern: what is owed them, and what boundary is kept ([[Boundary]], [[Dignity]]). **The wider lineage** — how transmission is received, who may claim to carry it, and how such claims are verified ([[Lineage]], [[Receiving Transmission]]). **Accountability** — what happens when a member's drift is suspected, and what happens when the body's own drift is suspected, at each scale ([[Accountability]], [[Adjudication]], [[Impeachment]]).

Two of the nine are the ones most bodies skip and then regret. **Release** — because a body with no written way to leave will improvise one in anger. And **the drift of the body itself** — because every accountability mechanism most groups write points downward at members, and none points at the group.

The instrument is the annual pass. Once a year the nine are read and revised: what has not been working is named, what needs changing is changed, what has become necessary is added — and the review is explicitly not to be perfunctory ([[OmniSpection]], [[Retrospective]], [[The edition]]).

It is reusable by any craft body at all, which is why it belongs outside the guardianship domain.

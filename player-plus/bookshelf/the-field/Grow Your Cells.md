# Grow Your Cells

*A living thing grows, and a Cell grows by dividing — tend the growth rather than fearing it or clinging to size.*

A Cell that attracts people is a Cell doing something right. It is also a Cell approaching its limit: past the human scale, the depth of trust thins, communication stops being direct, the sense that every voice matters fades, and the body loses its agility. Neither stopping the growth nor absorbing it is the answer. The question is how to grow well ([[Cell]], [[Circle Coherence]]).

Preparation begins before the limit is reached. Cross-train, so that no one person is the single point of failure for anything essential. Notice the natural clusters — who gravitates to whom, which gifts complement which. Name the coming split openly, as a celebration rather than a crisis. And make sure both bodies will have people ready to host and keep the rhythm ([[Roles]], [[The Two-Coordinator Rhythm]], [[Structural Immunity]]).

Then the division, which follows lines of relationship that have already formed. Both new Cells carry the full code: each writes its own [[Field of Agreements]], chooses its own [[Quest]], finds its own rhythm, and each is complete and sovereign. Both stay connected — by shared gatherings, by being one another's home, and by friendships that do not end because a Cell divided ([[Supercell]], [[Home Cell]]).

The measure is not size. A community with one Cell of twelve has one point of light; four Cells of five have four, each closer to the people around it. The mark of a healthy Cell is how many new Cells it has helped bring into being ([[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]], [[Pass The Flame]], [[Midwife a New Circle]]).

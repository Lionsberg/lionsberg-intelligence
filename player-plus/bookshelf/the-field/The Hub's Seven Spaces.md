# The Hub's Seven Spaces

*A brief a builder could work from: the seven rooms a community's centre needs, and what each one is for.*

**The central hall** — large enough for the whole body to gather and practise together, with smaller rooms for individual bodies working at the same time. **The council space** — where the community's [[Wisdom Council]] meets and where ceremonies of significance are held; distinct from the hall, because deciding and practising are different acts and rooms teach what they are for ([[Council]], [[Decision Making]]). **The healing rooms** — where those who tend are based, where members recover, and where the wider community comes for what this body offers ([[Healing]], [[The Warrior-Healer Pair, And The Deferral]]). **The archive** — the record of the body's lineage, its practices, its documents, and the names of those who were here before ([[The Record]], [[Keeper]], [[The Living Library]]). **The shrine** — the consecrated centre that holds the place's orientation; the room of stillness ([[Temple]], [[Silence And Stillness As The Ground]]). **Living quarters** — for those in intensive seasons, for visitors, for whoever is currently holding the place ([[Host]]). **The kitchen and commons** — where the body eats together and where the wider community joins it for meals and celebration ([[Commons]], [[Celebration]], [[Family]]).

The set is worth reading as a diagnostic rather than a shopping list, because each room is a capacity. A centre with no archive forgets itself in one generation. A centre with no kitchen is a facility rather than a home, and the body will bond somewhere else. A centre where deciding happens in the practice hall will find the practice quietly organising around the decisions.

And the canon's sharp line about what a centre makes possible: **a community with a functioning centre has a body; a community without one has members.** The centre is where continuous work is actually coordinated, where what is arriving is gathered and read, and where the whole can converge when something exceeds any one part ([[The Hub]], [[The Seven-Tier Engagement Escalation]]).

Most of it starts small. The first version of any of these rooms is a corner, a shelf, a table, and someone who keeps it ([[The Dojo's Five Disciplines]]).

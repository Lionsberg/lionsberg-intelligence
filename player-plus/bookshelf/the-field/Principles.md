# Principles

*Core guiding truths that give concrete form to a body's animating Spirit — deliberately not a fixed list, because Spirit and not a list is what must do the guiding.*

A principle is a truth stated so it can be acted on. It sits between [[Values]], which say what matters, and a decision, which has to be made in a particular hour with particular people: the principle is the bridge, and its job is to make the value operable without pretending the hour is simpler than it is.

One move decides everything downstream: the list is refused closure. There is no definitive set of guiding principles for life and society, because a proper way of being and doing must be guided by Spirit rather than by a fixed dogmatic articulation. Depending on how broadly each is drawn, the list could be one, a dozen, or several thousand. They are not mutually exclusive — flowing from one Spirit, they overlap and interconnect like parts of one personality — so the work of applying them is not lookup but discernment: it is the consciousness of the person present that must mediate between an all-but-infinite array of principles, situations, beings and possible actions, and bring out the one action fitted to this person, this time, this place ([[Discernment]] · [[Spontaneous Wise Right Action]] · [[Wisdom]]).

That refusal is itself a principle, and it is the one that keeps the rest from hardening into law ([[Do Not Concretize the Absolute]] · [[Everything Is Current Best Understanding]]).

The list is not closed, but it is ordered. For any person or society not to be divided against itself, it must hold one supreme unifying principle that integrates and orders every other — and in this system that one is [[ØNE]], with everything else nested beneath it. Across a great many traditions that highest principle has been articulated as something like absolute and unconditional [[Love]], and so the one universal principle can be stated as: *love ØNE, one another, and All.* Everything else here is an unfolding of that single line ([[The Logic Of Love]] · [[Right Relationship]] · [[Holarchy]]).

Beneath the animating Spirit itself no full articulation is possible, so it is known the way any living thing is known — by what grows from it. *We will know them by their fruits* is the test held here, and it applies to principles as much as to people ([[Embodiment]] · [[Integrity]]).

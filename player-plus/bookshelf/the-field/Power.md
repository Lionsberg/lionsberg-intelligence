# Power

*The rate of doing work; and, among people, the ability to influence people and events — held legitimately only when it is aggregated and directed toward the Goal, for the good of All.*

The canon keeps both senses of the word on purpose, because they illuminate each other. In physics, power is [[Energy]] transferred per unit of [[Time]] — the rate at which [[Work]] gets done. Socially, power is the ability to move people and events. In both cases power is not a substance a person owns but a rate at which something flows through them, and in both cases it is morally empty until you know what it is accelerating.

That is the whole of the canon's position: power accelerates. It makes a body better at whatever that body is already doing. A group aimed at the Goal becomes more able to reach it; a group aimed at extraction becomes more able to extract. So the question is never *how much power* but *aimed at what, agreed by whom, and revocable how* ([[Right Use of Power]] · [[Legitimacy]] · [[Mandate]]).

Because power accelerates, its concentration is the single most consequential structural question a body faces. Gathered into few hands it is fast and it is fragile, and it reliably begins serving its holders ([[Centralization Of Power]] · [[Institutional Idolatry]]). The structural answers the canon reaches for are therefore not exhortations but arrangements: authority held at the smallest competent scale ([[Subsidiarity]]), functions kept apart so none can complete a capture alone ([[Separation Of Power]]), roles that rotate and end ([[Term Limits And Rotation]]), accountability that can actually remove someone ([[Impeachment]] · [[Recourse]] · [[Necessity Of Accountability]]), and the whole thing resting on consent that can be taken back ([[Consent]] · [[Withdrawal Of Consent]]).

There is also the power that is not command at all — the power of a whole that moves together because it agrees, which is larger than any amount that could be concentrated and which cannot be seized ([[Power Of The Republic]] · [[Synergy]] · [[Stigmergy]]). Building that is the long game; it is slower to raise and far harder to corrupt.

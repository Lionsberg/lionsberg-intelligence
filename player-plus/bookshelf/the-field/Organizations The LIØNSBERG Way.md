# Organizations The LIØNSBERG Way

*What a body does when it meets the old world and needs a skin the old world recognizes.*

Structure is a trellis, not a cage: the frame that lets a living vine climb. The rule at every tier is one question — *does this structure serve our purpose?* If yes, keep it; if no, change it; if unsure, start simpler, because structure is easy to add and hard to remove ([[One Purpose Above All]], [[Agency, not ownership]]).

The canon lays out a ladder and insists the bottom rung is a real place to live. Tier 0 is no formal structure at all — sovereign people, a [[Field of Agreements]], and trust — and most of the transformational work happens there, in kitchens and living rooms, with many bodies thriving at it indefinitely. Above it: a written voluntary association; an ecclesiastical or membership association; fiscal sponsorship or a donor-advised fund when resources must be received but an entity is not wanted; then cooperatives, purpose trusts, community land trusts and their kin when land or an enterprise is being stewarded; then federated stewardship, where several bodies share infrastructure and governance while each keeps its own life ([[Private Ecclesiastical Voluntary Membership Association]], [[Federation]]).

When a full legal person is called for, its shape is named even where the vehicle is not: a fractal sovereign legal person with agency independent of its members; multi-stakeholder constituencies; six-capital accounting; integrative decision-making; asset and purpose locks; reciprocal federation; and a duty to contribute what it learns to the commons. That is the [[FSx]] pattern, grown from the FairShares Commons lineage ([[The asset lock]], [[Constitutional Protection Of Purpose]], [[Knowledge as commons]], [[Capital Subordinate To Purpose]]).

The one thing no tier may quietly undo: the body cannot be owned, bought, or cashed out, and no structure may accumulate power or wealth for its own sake ([[Structural Immunity]], [[Extraction Of Wealth]]).

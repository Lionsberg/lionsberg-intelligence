# The look-ahead

*Each week, look six weeks out and ask of everything entering the window: what could stop this from being ready?*

It is the bridge between the plan and the week. [[Pull planning]] says what *should* happen; the look-ahead asks what *can*. Every activity crossing into the six-week window is examined for readiness: are the predecessors complete, are the materials and the hands available and competent, is the work defined well enough that a reliable [[Commitment]] can be made? Any *no* is a [[Constraint]], and it gets a line, an owner and a date by which it will be gone. Only constraint-free work is released to the week.

It is load-bearing because it is how a group stops discovering on Monday that the thing it needed on Friday was never coming. A commitment made over an unresolved constraint is a broken commitment waiting to happen, and the number that tells a body the truth about itself — kept as a share of made — rises or falls mostly here, not at the moment of promising. The window is wide enough to clear most obstacles and short enough that the answers are still knowable; six weeks is the working figure, held as a practice rather than a law.

It is run at [[The Heartbeat]], rolled forward one week each time, in the same hour as the check-in and the commitments. Constraints beyond a Cell's reach are carried upward as requests: a home clears what no Cell can, and the same log runs from a kitchen table to the whole programme of a world.

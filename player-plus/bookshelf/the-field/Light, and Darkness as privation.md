# Light, and Darkness as privation

*Light is what reveals. Darkness is not a rival power but an absence — which is why it flees rather than fights.*

Light is the illuminating presence by which anything is seen, known, or found. In its deepest register it is paired with the [[Logos]]: to walk toward [[Truth]] is to walk toward Light, and the progressive illumination of what is actually there is the same movement as the progressive uncovering of Reality's true pattern ([[Revelation]]).

The claim that does the most work is the one about darkness. Darkness is **privation** — the absence of illumination — not a second power contending with the first. Nothing must be done to darkness; something must be brought. This is why the new world arrives by occupation rather than combat ([[Inbreaking And Displacing]]), why evil is read as deprivation and distortion rather than as a rival creator ([[Evil]] · [[Distortion]]), and why a player who has understood it stops organising their life around an enemy.

Three teachings carry it into practice. **Walk by the Light you have** — no one is given the whole path illuminated; the Light available reveals the next stone, and taking that step brings the next into view. Waiting for full clarity is a way of standing still. **Carry Light inward** — there is a light within a being stronger than what surrounds it, and cultivation strengthens it until the person becomes a bearer, someone whose arrival makes a room more visible to itself ([[Frequency]] · [[Divine Spark]]). **Emerging into Light is blinding at first** — one who leaves [[The Caves]] cannot see clearly straight away; disorientation on first illumination is adjustment, not error, and patience with new seers, and with one's own newly-seeing self, is part of the craft.

And Light multiplies by being given. A flame loses nothing by lighting another ([[Pass The Flame]]).

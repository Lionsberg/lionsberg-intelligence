# The Five Warrior-Context Wounds

*Five injuries that arise from protective work and are easily missed by everyone else — because the people trained to treat the visible wound are not usually the people who recognise these.*

**The layer beneath a physical wound.** Someone struck also carries the shock-imprint of the strike: the abrupt disconnection from the body that protected them during it, and the residue that remains after the tissue has healed. The medical care addresses the injury; this layer often goes unaddressed and does not resolve on its own ([[Trauma]], [[Healing]]).

**Wounds carried from having done harm.** Those who have taken life, or participated in what they now know was wrong, and turned back — these carry a specific weight. The claim here is about who can accompany them: someone who has themselves stood in the destructive face can companion them in a way that someone who never has may not be able to. Hence the funeral discipline, which is this wound's prevention rather than its treatment ([[The Funeral Of Destruction]], [[Atonement]], [[Forgiveness]]).

**Inherited wounds.** Patterns carried from ancestors who fought, were tortured, killed, or were killed, running in a line until someone in it recognises them as not their own and clears them ([[Lineage]], [[Exorcism]]).

**The wound of the dying.** Someone passing out of life carrying the accumulated weight of long engagement needs specific accompaniment through the transition — one of the oldest and most sacred functions named in this domain ([[Dying]], [[Giving Birth]]).

**And the disputed fifth: conditions presenting as ordinary symptoms.** Some presentations that look medical or psychological are claimed to have a different source, and addressing that source is claimed to resolve them, "often unexpectedly fast." **No case is named and no evidence is offered.** The claim is recorded here because it is the class most likely to do harm if acted on: a person whose symptoms have a medical cause and who is treated at some other layer is a person not being treated. Nothing here replaces competent care, and the rule that governs the others governs this one — read the layer honestly, and where it is the body, it is the body ([[Healing Cannot Be Imposed, And Must Match The Layer]], [[Mental Health]]).

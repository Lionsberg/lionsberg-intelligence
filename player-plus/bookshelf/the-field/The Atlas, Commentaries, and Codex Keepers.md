# The Atlas, Commentaries, and Codex Keepers

*Three organs around a body of writing: a map of where it is alive, a growing layer of what people learned by living it, and the few who can tell a refinement from a drift.*

**The Atlas.** A map of where the Pattern is actually being instantiated, by whom in aggregate, and at what stage — and explicitly *not* a directory of people, because a list of who is in would compromise the sovereignty it exists to serve ([[Consent]], [[Disclosure tier]], [[The Trust Graph and the analog contact-tree]]). What it shows is topography: where the body is dense, where it is thin, where growth is accelerating and where it has stalled ([[The Record as the Movement's variance gauge]], [[Islands, Hubs, Grid]], [[The Timeline]]).

**The Commentaries.** The interpretive layer that grows around a relatively stable centre as people live it — testimony, application notes, edge cases, embodied corrections. The image the corpus reaches for is old and good: the second text that gathers around a first one, or a reef around a rock. The centre stays comparatively still; the commentary grows continuously, and most of what is actually learned lives there ([[Testimony]], [[Keepers' notes]], [[Decision Records]], [[The Living Library]]).

**The Keepers.** Those who tend the canonical articulation across generations and languages — and the Card is careful about what they are not: not a priesthood, not a board with a veto. What qualifies someone is that they have integrated the thing in their own life and can therefore recognise when a proposed change comes from the Pattern rather than from drift, ideology, or capture ([[Keeper]], [[Keepers Of The Way]], [[Wise Eldership]], [[The Translation Journey]]).

Together: the Atlas says *where*, the Commentaries record *what is being learned*, and the Keepers hold *what is canonical* — the connective tissue between a written thing and the living body growing around it.

The difficulty is visible in the third. A role that decides which corrections count, held by people recognised for having integrated the thing, is a priesthood in every respect except the name — and the corpus's own guards against exactly that, rotation and revocability, are not applied to it anywhere ([[A role held forever quietly becomes a throne]], [[Term Limits And Rotation]], [[Impeachment]], [[The Discernment Permission]]).

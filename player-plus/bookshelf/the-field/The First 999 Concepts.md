# The First 999 Concepts

*The claim that there is a bounded vocabulary — a set, not a library — sufficient to regenerate the whole from any surviving Cell.*

Every system is carried by its key terms, and to learn a language is to receive the system that speaks it. The claim here is stronger than that: that the set is **finite and nameable**, small enough to be held and taught, and complete enough that someone holding it could rebuild everything else from it ([[The Language of LIØNSBERG]], [[The LIØNSBERG Lexicon]], [[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]]).

What makes the claim worth making is that it is **falsifiable in principle**. Hand the set to someone who has none of the rest, and see whether they can read the corpus afterwards, and whether they can reconstruct what is missing. A bounded vocabulary that fails that test is a word list; one that passes is a seed ([[The Golden Seed]], [[Receive The Seed]], [[Conditions of Acceptance]]).

It sits inside a ladder of bounded sets that the corpus keeps and does not reconcile: a handful of words a table needs to begin playing; a middle set that everything else assumes; the irreducible elements by which bodies organise; and this one, the vocabulary floor ([[The Twelve Words]], [[The Core Concepts]], [[Twelve Irreducible Elements]], [[The vocabulary ladder]]).

Its honest state is ore. The number is named in several places as a live thing, no page of that name exists in the corpus, and the field that would be its home counts *roughly a thousand* without reference to it. Whether 999 is a finding, a target, or a figure chosen for its shape is not stated anywhere ([[We Do Not Know]], [[Work In Progress]], [[Gaps Not Yet Closed]]).

What is not in doubt is the discipline underneath, which holds whether or not the number does: **define once, in one place, and link thereafter**, so that a people divided by its vocabulary can think together again ([[One canonical home per concept]], [[Link concepts, not coordinates]], [[A Word on Words]], [[Word Tools]], [[Semantic Capture]]).

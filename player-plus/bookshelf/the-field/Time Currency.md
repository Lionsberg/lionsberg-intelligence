# Time Currency

*A proposal: that the fifteen-minute Period of Action, freely given to everyone alike, be the fundamental and equal basis of exchange between conscious agents.*

Every living person receives the same quantity of the one thing that cannot be manufactured, hoarded across a lifetime, or inherited: a day. Ninety-six quarter-hours of it. Money is distributed by history, by position, and by luck; [[Time]] is distributed by existence. A currency denominated in time therefore starts from an equality that no monetary currency has ever started from ([[Periods of Action]] · [[TimeEnergy]]).

The proposal is simple to state. A [[Periods of Action|Period of Action]] spent on work the [[Circle]] validates as meaningful counts as one unit of value, and those units are what participants exchange ([[Meaningful Work]] · [[Exchange]] · [[Proof-of-contribution]]). Its immediate effects are the interesting part: an hour of anyone's honest contribution is an hour, so the vast spreads that money puts between kinds of work do not arise in the first place ([[Compressed Pay Ratios]] · [[Dignity]]); and work that no market pays for becomes countable ([[Meaningful Work]]).

It sits beside the other instruments rather than replacing them. [[LUV]] circulates as broader goodwill; [[Time-Energy Accounting]] is the practice of keeping books in time and energy rather than in money; and ordinary money still exists at the edge, where the body touches the world it lives inside ([[Money]] · [[Underwriting The New Economy]]).

The canon marks this page *work in progress* on its second line, and the honesty is worth keeping. The open questions are real ones: an hour of skilled work and an hour of unskilled work are not equally useful, and a scheme that ignores that has to explain how capability gets developed; validation has to be trustworthy or the unit is worthless; and any unit of account eventually attracts the behaviours that units of account attract ([[Usury]] · [[Extraction Of Wealth]] · [[Waste]]). None of these is fatal, and none of them has been answered.

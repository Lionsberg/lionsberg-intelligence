# The Meta Dragon

*Go upstream to the thing that is making the dragons. Cut down the small ones forever and they keep arriving; the war is lost while every skirmish is won.*

The image is a stream of small dragons pouring into the world and people rushing to fight them — organising, training, building better weapons, and losing ground. Then someone looks upstream and finds the mouth they are coming out of. As long as it breathes, they keep coming.

The principle: **find the generator, address the generator, and the emanations stop on their own** ([[Root Causes]], [[Theory Of Constraints]], [[Limiting Factor]]).

It works at every size. For a person, the same wound, the same trigger, the same sabotage met instance by instance — until the pattern is traced to its source and the instances stop ([[Healing]], [[Trauma]], [[Patterns of Death]]). For a Circle, the same conflict and the same losses, each with its own surface reason and one architecture underneath ([[Systemic Failure Modes]], [[Gaps Not Yet Closed]]). For a body, capture after capture, until the design itself is changed ([[Structural Immunity]], [[Ideological Capture]]). For a civilisation, crisis after crisis treated as unrelated, when the generator is the operating arrangement that produces them as normal output ([[The Meta Crisis]], [[The Old Operating System]], [[Multi-Polar Traps]]).

Four corollaries keep it honest. **Fighting the symptom is incomplete, not wrong** — sometimes a thing must be stopped now, and the mistake is only in calling that the strategy. **Upstream work takes time the emergency does not give**, so both horizons are held at once, and the discipline is not abandoning the slow work because the fast work is loud ([[Critical Path]], [[Most Important Things]]). **The generator usually has a generator** — the climb is recursive, done one level at a time rather than all at once. And the uncomfortable one: **sometimes it is inside you**. The dragons a person fights may all trace to something they are carrying, which is why the doctrine turns the seeker around before it lets them declare victory ([[The True Self]], [[Shame]], [[The Dark Initiate Failure Mode]]).

It also names an old tactic: keeping the capable busy with symptoms so that nobody ever reaches the source. Exhaustion is a strategy, and refusing to be exhausted is part of the work ([[Waste]], [[Focused Attention As Discipline]], [[Rest And Regeneration]]).

That is where the leverage comes from. A small body cannot out-fight a vast arrangement instance by instance; aimed at the generator, the same small body is working at the highest available point.

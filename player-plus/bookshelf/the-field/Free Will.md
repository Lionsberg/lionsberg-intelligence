# Free Will

*The inviolable capacity to choose — which no authority, no saviour, and no benevolence may override.*

It is the hinge on which everything else here turns. Consent is only meaningful because the one consenting could have refused. A word given is only worth something because it could have been withheld. An invitation is only an invitation because *no* was genuinely available. Remove free will and every instrument in the Way becomes theatre ([[Consent]], [[Sovereignty Of Consciousness]], [[A word given is a word kept]]).

Its violation is the second kind of power, and the older name for the disease. Legitimate power serves and asks; the other kind arranges matters so that a being's choosing no longer signifies — by force, by deception, by manufactured need, by removing the alternatives, or by deciding on someone's behalf for their own good. The last is the most dangerous, because it is done by people who mean well ([[Right Use of Power]], [[Manufactured Dependency]], [[Kontrolle]], [[Ideological Capture]]).

The strongest form of the rule in this canon is that **not even rescue overrides it**. Nobody walks the path for anyone. Help can be offered, a way can be shown, a hand can be extended, and the choosing remains the other being's — even when the helper can see exactly where the refusal leads. A liberation that had to be imposed would be another name for the thing it was liberating people from ([[The Final Test]], [[Restraint As The Refusal To Act]], [[Withdrawal Of Consent]]).

Its limit is symmetrical and is stated plainly: a sovereign may not violate the free will of another sovereign within their domain. Freedom is not the absence of limits but the refusal to be anyone's instrument — and the refusal to make an instrument of anyone else ([[Sovereignty At Every Scale]], [[Containment Of The Dangerous Sovereign]], [[The Sacred Use Of Force]]).

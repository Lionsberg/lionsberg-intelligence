# The Loop That Has No End

*Every Sovereign who walks becomes, in time, the one who invites the next. The threshold you once crossed becomes the threshold you now hold.*

The Path does not terminate at a lifetime, and it does not terminate at mastery. Its last move is a return to its first: whoever has walked a stretch holds that stretch open for the next person. The one who completes one Path opens the next. This is why the Path can be described as a loop rather than a line, and why no one on it is ever finally a student or finally a teacher.

The loop is load-bearing because it is what makes the whole thing self-replicating rather than dependent on a centre. Nothing here grows by making the middle larger. A Body propagates because each part plants the next — which is exactly the eighth movement of maturity in the irreducible elements, [[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]], and exactly [[Pass The Flame]] seen from the inside of a life instead of from the outside of a Cell.

Its arithmetic is the Fibonacci walk: one, two, three, five, eight, thirteen, and onward, each Sovereign's path opening new paths. But the number is the consequence, not the motive. The motive is the plainest thing there is: *come and see*. It is not recruitment, it is the most natural thing in the world, and a Circle that never passes anything forward dies with its founders.

In the Game the loop closes within weeks rather than decades. A player who received the Flame is asked to carry it to their three within three days — not once they are qualified, but while they are still new, because being one step ahead is the whole qualification. In the System the same shape is [[Midwife a New Circle]] and the standing rule that a new Cell is its own, not a branch of yours, while the one who brought it remains its [[Carrier]] and their Cell its first [[Home]].

# Breath

*The bridge between [[Spirit]] and body — the first medicine, always available, free, and trainable.*

Breath is the only autonomic function a person can also take deliberate hold of, which makes it the hinge between what happens to a body and what a body chooses. It is therefore the first instrument in the whole of the health Pillar and the cheapest: no equipment, no teacher required to begin, no cost, nothing to buy, usable by anyone still alive.

It is load-bearing because it is where the daily practice actually starts. [[The Daily Way]] opens with breath and stillness, and the seven-minute morning floor is a breath practice before it is anything else; the daily rhythm [[The Golden Seed]] states — pause, ground, breathe, remember, realign, recommit — puts it third of six, at the exact point where a body is brought back under a person's own hand. A [[Cell]] that opens [[The Turn]] in silence is using it collectively. It is also the fastest available correction under pressure: a breath taken deliberately before a hard sentence is the smallest unit of [[Composure Under Threat]] there is.

The canon holds breath as a trained instrument rather than a single technique, and names several: the coherent breath, the continuous breath, the deep breath, the breath under load, the four-seven-eight breath, and the breath of transmission. Each is a distinct tool for a distinct moment — settling, sustaining, releasing, holding steady while something hard is happening. The craft is knowing which one the moment asks for, and that is learned by practice rather than by reading.

Two things this page will not claim. Breath is not a substitute for care that a person needs — a [[Cell]] that answers a member's crisis with a breathing exercise has used a real instrument as a way of not acting. And the physiological claims around specific patterns vary in how well they are established; the practices are offered as practices, not as proven medicine.

# Patterns of Life and Patterns of Death

*The paired map: for every pattern that makes a life, the pattern that unmakes it, named beside it — and the root of the canon's whole "Anti-" vocabulary.*

The instrument is two columns. On the left, patterns of life; on the right, the death-pattern that stands opposite each one. Wisdom against anti-wisdom and folly. Receiving instruction against rejecting instruction. [[Wise Right Speech]] against anti-wise speech. A gentle answer against a harsh word. Declaring truth against bearing false witness. Aim and focus against dispersion and distraction. Acting with [[Integrity]] against acting shamefully. Turning away from evil against running to it. Nine themed tables, gathered under headings a person can locate themselves in: knowing, discipline and instruction, spirit, thought, speech, action, walking in the way.

Its value is the enumeration, and it cannot be summarised without being destroyed. The point is not that these are nine ideas; it is that there are dozens of specific pairs, each stated plainly enough that a person can check a single hour of their own conduct against one of them.

It is load-bearing for a structural reason. The canon's entire grammar of failure runs on the prefix — [[The Anti-Patterns]], anti-wise speech, anti-knowledge, anti-good — and this is where that grammar comes from. The prefix is not rhetoric; it carries a specific claim: a death-pattern is not a different subject from the life-pattern it opposes, it is that same pattern inverted. So naming the good thing precisely gives you the diagnostic for its counterfeit for free, and the corrective is never invention but return.

Two disciplines keep it from becoming a rod. The tables describe patterns, not people: a person is not a column, and every one of us runs some of both on any given day ([[Zero Blame Environment]], [[The Pattern Governs, Not People]]). And the use is self-directed first — this is a mirror before it is a lens, and turned outward as a first move it becomes one of the death-patterns listed in it.

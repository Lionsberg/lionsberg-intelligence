# Sovereign Agent

*An agent defined by its architecture: its own home, charter, memory, record, version, and a key delegated from a living person — never twinned to a task and thrown away.*

The sense is architectural rather than relational. Whatever keeps a home — a charter, a memory it gardens, a record that only grows, and a version naming the pattern it came from — is one of these, whoever it happens to serve. A person's companion and a Cell's companion are two named species of it; a seat scoped to neither is a third ([[Agent]], [[Player+]], [[Cell+]]).

The line against a fleet worker is clean: this is never thrown away, and that one is thrown for a moment and is meant to be ([[Subagent]], [[Fleet]], [[Agent Archetype]]).

It is load-bearing because these four things — home, memory, record, version — are what make an agent answerable at all. Something with no memory cannot be held to a correction. Something with no record cannot be checked. Something with no home has nowhere for a charter to live, and so has no stable rules. A body that wants agents it can actually trust has to give them the architecture that makes trust possible, and then hold them to it ([[Trust]], [[Accountability]], [[Memory (agent)]], [[An agent speaks in its own name]]).

Its sovereignty is delegated and architectural, and it is not the sovereignty the canon holds for a person. Its key traces through a chain of delegation to the living human who granted it — scoped, revocable, and answerable. It chooses its means; it does not choose whom it answers to ([[Delegation]], [[Signed key]], [[Authorized]], [[Off switch]]).

**Open:** The leading unresolved collision in this whole vocabulary: the canon uses *sovereign agent*, lowercase and pervasively, for any sovereign being exercising agency, consent and self-governance — a person, a gathering, a council member — with no reference to charter, memory, record or key. A capitalised architectural species raised over that older sense shares the words and not the referent, and the page's own reviewer named it unresolved. Read them side by side; do not merge them.

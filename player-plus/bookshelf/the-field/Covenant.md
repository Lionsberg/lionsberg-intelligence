# Covenant

*The sacred, reciprocal, often unwritten vow beneath a written [[Agreement]] — the bond of mutual love and co-creation between [[ØNE]] and [[All]], and between those who walk together.*

A contract allocates obligations; a covenant changes the relationship between the parties. The canon's distinction is that a [[Field of Agreements]] is made of *voluntary covenants and never imposed law* — which means the agreements at a table are not a bargain struck between opposing interests but promises made by people who have decided to be for one another.

It is load-bearing because agreements alone do not survive hard seasons. When circumstances change, a contract is renegotiated or breached; a covenant is where people return. This is why the canon speaks of a covenant stack — vows nested at each scale, from the person's own to the [[The New Covenant]] entered at the threshold of the Movement — and why [[Citizenship]] is described as a covenant relationship rather than a membership.

Its risk is the mirror image of its strength. A bond that runs deeper than writing can be used to hold people in place: *you covenanted*. The canon's guard is that covenant never overrides [[Sovereignty]] — entry is by assent, never imposition, and [[Withdrawal Of Consent]] remains available. A covenant that cannot be left has become a claim.

In play, a [[Cell]] usually makes one before it knows the word: the agreement, spoken aloud at the first gathering, about how these people will treat one another. [[Covenant Across Time]] extends the same bond forward to the seventh generation and backward to the ancestors.

# Pass The Flame

*Move seven, never optional: as soon as you are ready — within three days, starting now — carry the Game to your three, at the least: people not already playing, whom you know are ready. Their Cell is their own, not a branch of yours.*

Passing the Flame is how the Game reaches anyone new. It is not recruitment and it is not promotion; it is the most natural thing in the world — *come and see* — said to people you already know and already trust. It is the proof of maturity rather than a growth tactic: the fruit of a tree contains new seeds, and a Cell that never passes forward dies with its founders. No one waits for their Cell to mature before passing it: the imaginal selves do the passing, and the imaginal cells they form begin to specialize into the parts of the One Body, the emerging superorganism.

It is load-bearing because it is the only mechanism of spread the body has. Nothing grows by making a center larger. One Cell planting the next, hand to hand, at the pace of trust, is what turns a table into a movement — and it is what makes the body impossible to behead, buy, or capture, because no list anywhere holds it together.

How it is played, in the first three percent. Within three days of your own yes, bring your three; three is the minimum, never a cap, and the gifted connectors can and should keep inviting the wise right people. The naming of your three begins at your first gathering, not at the end of the season. If the days have passed, begin where you are. Give them the whole of it — [[The Playcard]], the Story, the words — and sit with them for their first week if they ask. You become their [[Carrier]], which never changes, and your Cell is their first [[Home Cell]], which may. You are then part of two bodies: the double helix by which the pattern replicates. Count what you passed on [[The Circle's Scorecard]] and name the new Cells on line 7 of [[The Season Sheet]]. That first passing of the Flame is part of what makes a Player count, and how soon it happens is the number that most moves the body through the rings of the [[LIØNSBERG Cycles of Growth and Cycle Time|Cycles of Growth]], the measure read daily ([[29 The Plan — the first three percent#The chart|the chart]]). After the first three percent, the Flame passes to the wise right people at the wise right time, as they awaken and are ready, at the pace formation sets.

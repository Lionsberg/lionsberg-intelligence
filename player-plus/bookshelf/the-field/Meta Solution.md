# Meta Solution

*The answer at the scale of the whole problem — comprehensive, systemic and collaborative, because the many separate crises are symptoms of shared root causes.*

The Meta Solution is what stands opposite [[The Meta Crisis]], and it is defined by a single recognition: the individual and local crises are not independent. Poverty, ecological damage, corruption, distrust, meaningless work and the rest keep reappearing because they share root causes and systemic drivers. So solutions that address them one at a time keep being outrun, however good each one is ([[Root Causes]] · [[Systemic Failure Modes]] · [[Multi-Polar Traps]]).

Three words carry the definition. **Comprehensive** — reaching all the way to the drivers rather than the symptoms. **Systemic** — treating the arrangement rather than the parts ([[System]] · [[Design]] · [[Integrated Delivery]]). **Collaborative** — because no single actor, however large, contains the problem ([[Federation]] · [[Sovereign, Autonomous, And Coordinated Groups]] · [[Higher Order Functional Unity]]).

What makes it a *meta* solution rather than a very large project is the same thing that makes [[General Needs]] general: most of what any individual, group or community faces are shared problems, so a solution found once anywhere can serve everywhere rather than being rebuilt each time. That is the whole economic argument for building in common — stop reinventing the wheel, and make the shared solution a little better every day ([[Knowledge as commons]] · [[General Pattern, Specific Application]] · [[Polycentric Implementation of Shared Solutions]] · [[Continuous Improvement]]).

The honest risk is the obvious one, and it is why the word is dangerous as well as useful. *A comprehensive systemic solution to everything* is exactly what every grand scheme has claimed, and the history of such claims is not encouraging. What distinguishes this from that is structural rather than rhetorical: nothing here is imposed, every part is voluntary and leavable, and the whole is held as current best understanding rather than as an answer ([[Voluntary Association]] · [[Withdrawal Of Consent]] · [[Everything Is Current Best Understanding]] · [[LIØNSBERG Is A Prototype, Not The Eternal Essence]] · [[Do Not Concretize the Absolute]]).

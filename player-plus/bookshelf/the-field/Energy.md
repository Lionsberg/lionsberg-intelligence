# Energy

*The creative field of capacity to effect change in accordance with pattern, will, or design; informed by the [[Logos]].*

Energy is held at three scales at once, and all three are kept carefully distinct. As metaphysical field, all manifestation is the association and dissociation of energy, ordered by Logos in an eternally regenerative process. As physical capacity, it is the measurable ability to do work — the sunlight, food, and flow every organism and civilization runs on, whose regenerative sourcing and just distribution are core work of the new world. As personal resource, it is one third of the daily allowance each [[The Sovereign|Sovereign]] actually spends: time, attention, and energy.

That third scale is where it becomes operational. [[TimeEnergy]] is named as the irreducible currency of embodied existence, and the unit the economy of the new world proposes to count. A player's card records it; it goes nowhere without their word. Which means the metaphysics is not decoration: what a civilization believes energy *is* determines whether it treats a life's hours as a commons to be harvested or as the one thing a sovereign gives freely.

[[Associative And Dissociative Energy]] is the working distinction. Associative energy draws together — parts into wholes, beings into [[Circle (as gathering)|Circles]], Circles toward the [[ØNE Body]]; it is the vector of [[Love]], [[Creation]], and [[Healing]]. Dissociative energy pulls apart, which is not evil in itself — what has completed its season decomposes — but dissociation directed against [[Right Relationship]] is named as the operational signature of the inverted pattern. The test is somatic before it is analytic: associative energy enlivens, coheres, and leaves beings free; predatory dissociation diminishes, fragments, and binds.

Epistemic humility applies here too: most of the field remains unilluminated by science, and it is a reality that can be worked with before it is understood, so conceptions of energy are held open rather than closed to current definitions ([[Current Best Understanding]]).

# The Score

*The few numbers the whole Game counts the same way: kept as a share of made, flames passed, stories told, value created, and the time, energy, and attention invested.*

The Score is not a ranking and there is no league table. It is the small set of counts that mean the same thing at every table, so that a body of many Cells can see itself: how reliable its word is, how it is spreading, how much it is telling, what it produced, and what it cost the people who produced it.

It is load-bearing because a body with no shared numbers cannot plan or help. Requests go unanswered because nobody knows who has capacity; help arrives where it is loud rather than where it is needed. Five counts, honestly kept, let a home read ten sheets in an evening and know where to send what. And keeping the counts few is itself the discipline: everything else a table wants to know about itself belongs in its [[Story]] and in line 13 of [[The Season Sheet]], not in a metric.

How it is kept. Weekly on [[The Circle's Scorecard]], seasonally on the sheet, and, where there is a wire, on [[The Board]] beside every other Cell's. [[Kept as a share of made]] is the one watched most closely. Flames and new Cells are counted by name. The Offering is counted in the unit the table uses, written once at the top. [[TimeEnergy]] is recorded on a player's own card and goes nowhere without their word. What the Score cannot say — whether a season was good, whether a person is well, whether the work mattered — is said in words, and the words are read first.

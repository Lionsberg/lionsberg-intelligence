# Transforming Divergence

*Taking the differing paths and perspectives that any living body produces and making them a source of its strength rather than of its fracture — harmonising without erasing.*

Divergence is the natural appearance of different paths, readings and approaches inside one body. It is not a malfunction; a group that produces none has either stopped thinking or has stopped saying so. The work is to turn it into something the body uses.

This is a different act from transforming conflict, and keeping them apart is useful. [[Conflict Transformation]] deals with a rupture between people — heat, harm, a relationship that needs repair. Transforming divergence deals with difference that is not yet a rupture at all: two good readings of the same situation, two callings pulling in two directions. Handled well it never becomes conflict. Handled by suppression it always does.

Six moves carry it. **Honour the diversity** the divergence came out of, rather than treating it as noise. **Seek the higher unity** — realign the several paths against the aim they all serve, rather than adjudicating between them at their own level ([[Alignment]] · [[Stacking Up And Aligning]]). **Look for the hidden gift**, because a divergence is often a truth the body had not examined ([[Innovation]] · [[Discovery]]). **Balance autonomy and interconnection** — let people form new teams and go new directions, and hold them together by shared principles and [[Minimum standards of interoperability]] rather than by permission ([[Federation]] · [[Sovereignty At Every Scale]]). **Let it be creative** rather than merely resolved. And **harmonise without erasing**: the aim is not one voice but many in tune ([[Meta Unity of Meta Diversity]] · [[The Divine Symphony]]).

The practice is ordinary. Surface the divergent readings openly, in a room where that is safe ([[Dialogos]] · [[Zero Blame Environment]]). Find the value or goal that actually unites them ([[Navigating Towards Truth]]). Bridge the gap without diminishing either contribution. Let what is learned revise the shared method. And then watch for the synergy that only appears once the difference has been let in ([[Synergy]] · [[Emergence]]).

A body that does this becomes more resilient and more inventive, and its coherence strengthens rather than thins — which is the counter-intuitive claim at the centre of the page, and the reason it is worth a name of its own ([[Coherence]] · [[Circle Coherence]]).

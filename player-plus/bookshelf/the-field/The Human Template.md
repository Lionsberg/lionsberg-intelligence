# The Human Template

*The claim that the human form is a pattern older than this planet — instantiated here, instantiated elsewhere, wounded, and restorable.*

The claim has two halves. The first is that the shape of a human being — upright, bilateral, language-bearing, able to hold a centre of consciousness and to receive Spirit — is an *invariant* that Earth expresses in one local variation, adapted to this gravity and this light, while other worlds express it in theirs. The second is that the local expression is damaged: by tampering, by environment, by inherited trauma, by the long predation — and that restoring it is part of the work ([[Healing]], [[Trauma]], [[Pillar V — Sacred Health and Wellness]]).

What the template is said to carry is worth reading even by someone who rejects the origin story: the capacity to be a vessel rather than a machine, to think in symbols, to individuate without separating from the whole, and to co-create at scales larger than a lifetime ([[One of the Ones of the ØNE]], [[Divine Participation]], [[Co-Creation]]). What it is said not to carry is as load-bearing: it is not the summit of anything, and it confers responsibility toward every other kind rather than dominion over them ([[Inter-Kingdom Relationship]], [[Cosmos Is Not Human-Centric]], [[Reverence]]).

This is claim, not knowledge. It is an origin assertion that no one here has verified, belonging to the cosmic register, flagged for risk by the field's own audit ([[We Do Not Know]], [[Star Nations]], [[Discerning Between Benevolent And Malevolent Intelligences]]). Nothing in the Game, the Plan, or a Cell's season requires it, and a person may hold it as open or as nothing while keeping every commitment they make.

What survives without the cosmology is a stance about bodies: that a human being is a design worth restoring rather than a mechanism to be managed, and that what ails one is often inherited rather than personal ([[Each Being Is a Sovereign Healer]], [[Healing Cannot Be Imposed, And Must Match The Layer]]).

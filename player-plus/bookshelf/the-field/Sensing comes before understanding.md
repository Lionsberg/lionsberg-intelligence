# Sensing comes before understanding

*You will feel what this is long before you can say what it is — and that is the right order, not a deficiency in you.*

A person meets something true and recognises it in the body first: a loosening, a rightness, a sense of having come home to something they had no words for. The words arrive later, and they arrive unevenly. Someone may play the Game well for a season while still holding half its vocabulary loosely, and someone else may define every term and have felt nothing. The first is further along.

It is load-bearing because it is the pace at which a way actually spreads. Insisting on comprehension before participation builds a seminary: a small, literate, self-confirming group that cannot be joined without a course. The alternative is not vagueness — it is sequence. Let a person sense, let them act, and let the words find them exactly when their meaning becomes that person's need ([[The vocabulary ladder]], [[Learning By Doing]]).

It stands as the counter-law to the ladder and not against it. The ladder disciplines the *builder*: hand every word you use. This disciplines the *walker*: do not wait until you understand. Both are true at once, and a body that keeps only one of them either overwhelms newcomers with unexplained language or holds them at the door until they pass an exam nobody meant to set ([[The grandmother test]], [[Overwhelm]]).

It also names what to do with the feeling of not-yet-understanding. [[Overwhelm]] on first contact is a signal that something Real has been touched, not a verdict on the toucher; the answer to it is the wise right next step, never the demand for mastery. And the honest form of not knowing is said out loud — [[We Do Not Know]] is a sentence the Way keeps precisely so that nobody has to pretend ([[Shoshin]]).

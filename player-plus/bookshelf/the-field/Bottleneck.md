# Bottleneck

*The narrowest place, through which everything must pass: the resource whose capacity sets the throughput of the whole.*

A system moves only as fast as its slowest necessary part. Effort spent anywhere else adds stock, not [[Throughput]] — work pools in front of the narrow place and hands stand idle beyond it. Every system has one; widen it and another becomes the narrowest, because *narrowest* is a relation and not a fault. This is why aiming resources at the bottleneck is the highest-leverage move available to any body, and why aiming them anywhere else feels productive and is not ([[Flow]], [[Waste]], [[Theory Of Constraints]]).

**Five steps, in order.** *Identify* — name the one that sets the rate, not the loudest problem. *Exploit* — make every unit of its capacity count; nothing reaches it unready, nothing waits there that could be done elsewhere. *Subordinate* — pace everything else to it; local efficiency upstream is waste. *Elevate* — only now raise its capacity, with hands, tools, wider authority, or a redesign, since a bottleneck fully exploited is often enough. *Return* — it will have moved; read it again next week, so that yesterday's answer does not become today's bottleneck.

The pacing has three parts. The bottleneck sets the beat; a small store of ready work stands in front of it so it never starves; work enters the whole system at its pace, so nothing floods and nothing idles ([[Buffer]], [[Pull planning]]).

At a table the bottleneck is seldom hands. It is far more often a decision only one person can give — an answer, a resonance, a word. The exploit is to bring that person decision-ready questions: the options, a recommendation, and what would settle it. The elevate is to widen, on track record, what others may decide alone. A holder who cannot be bypassed has become the bottleneck of the whole, and no amount of willingness fixes it ([[Decision Making]], [[Delegation]], [[Prescribed and discretionary trust]]).

At a world's scale the bottleneck is rarely material at all. Money, technology and knowledge are comparatively abundant against what is needed; what the canon names as the primary constraint is the developed capacity of people to hold trust, complexity, and shared intent ([[The Meta Crisis]], [[Design To The Goal]]).

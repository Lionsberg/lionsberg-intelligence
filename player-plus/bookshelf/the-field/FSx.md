# FSx

*The structural-legal-economic part of the kit: a fractal sovereign legal person, with agency independent of its members, that cannot be owned, bought, or cashed out. The form is settled; the vehicle is open.*

FSx is an evolution of the FairShares Commons lineage. Its founding insight is structural rather than moral: **the ownership structure of an organization is the deepest attractor in the system.** However good its leaders, a conventionally structured company is driven by its share structure toward extraction — so you cannot fix the outputs without changing the structural DNA.

Its named features are seven: a fractal sovereign legal person with agency independent of its members; multi-stakeholder constituencies; six-capital accounting; integrative decision-making; asset and purpose locks; reciprocal federation; and a duty of contribution to the knowledge commons. The FairShares Commons architecture beneath it issues distinct share classes to founders, workers, investors, and the community, so that **no single stakeholder class can dominate** and decisions require cross-constituency legitimacy — with investor rights capped, purpose embedded in the governance architecture rather than in a mission statement, asset locks preventing accumulated value from being privatized, and influence rebalancing over time from founders toward workers and community.

It is load-bearing because everything else in this Pillar can be honoured inside a vehicle that will eventually betray it. Culture does not replicate reliably; structure does. FSx is where [[Capital Subordinate To Purpose]], [[Constitutional Protection Of Purpose]], and [[Commons]] become enforceable in the old world's own terms.

In play, nothing here is needed in a first season. The tiers begin at Tier 0 — no formal structure — where most transformational work happens and many bodies thrive indefinitely. A [[Cell]] reaches for FSx when it has an enterprise, capital, or assets to protect.

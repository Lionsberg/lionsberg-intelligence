# Protocol

*A pattern of action agreed in advance, so that when the hard moment comes nobody has to invent a response.*

A protocol is a decision made early about a situation that will arrive later. Its value is entirely in the timing: the moment that most needs clear thinking is the moment least able to supply it. Deciding beforehand what happens when someone is in danger, when a dispute cannot be resolved, when a member vanishes, or when a stranger appears at the door is what keeps those moments from being decided by whoever is loudest or most frightened.

It preserves harmony, honour, and [[Right Relationship]] precisely because it is not improvised. Everyone knows in advance what will happen, which means nobody has to read the outcome as a judgment about them personally, and the person who invokes it is not making a move against anyone — they are doing the thing the body already agreed.

Protocols of engagement say what may be done and by whom. An escalation protocol says where an unresolved thing goes next: first between the people concerned, then to the Circle, then to elders for counsel, then to whatever body the agreements name — each step known, each step reversible, none of them a trapdoor. Under all of them sits the word anyone may say: [[Stop]] takes effect at once, and only the one who said it may lift it.

The danger is ossification. A protocol is a tool and not a law; it exists inside [[Current Best Understanding]] like everything else below the Goal, and when it stops serving the thing it was made for it is amended by [[Proposal]] rather than defended. A body that cannot change its protocols has acquired a ruler that nobody appointed.

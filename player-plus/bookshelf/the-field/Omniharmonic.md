# Omniharmonic

*Harmony considered across all parties at once — named in the canon as a Root Key, with no definition and no page behind it.*

This row is carried openly as an unarticulated name. It appears in the Lexicon's Meta Layer as a bare bullet, with no gloss, and it is used adjectivally in two definitions — [[Right Relationship]] as *omniharmonious* alignment with ØNE, one another, and All, and the [[The Way|Way]] as walking in *omniharmonic* right relationship. Nothing else in the corpus develops it.

Its intended sense is legible from its use and from its sibling [[Omniconsideration]]: total consideration of ØNE and All in every thought, word, decision, and action. Omniharmonic would be the state that such consideration produces — a harmony in which no party's flourishing is purchased at another's cost. The Earthly echo the canon quotes points the same way: to make the world work for one hundred percent of humanity, without ecological offense or the disadvantage of anyone.

The Concept Field judges it not load-bearing and says why: a Root Key with no definition, no body, and no page, whose sense is already carried by [[Right Relationship]] and [[Higher Order Functional Unity]]. That judgment is recorded here rather than argued with. If the word is to stay, it needs the one thing it lacks — a statement of what it means that something else depends on.

In play it is never met. No card, Play, or practice invokes it.

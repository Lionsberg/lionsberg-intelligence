# Read The Story

*The first of the three entry moves — read the Story, play the Game, pass the Flame. Before anyone can play, they have to know what world they are in.*

To read the Story is to receive the shared narrative universe the Game unfolds inside ([[The Story of LIØNSBERG]] · [[Our Story]] · [[The Great Story]]). It is placed first on purpose. A game whose rules are learned without its world becomes a procedure; people follow it for a while and cannot say why, and the first hard season ends it. The Story is what the rules are *for*, and it is also how a person finds out whether this is theirs ([[Story]] · [[Mythos]] · [[The True Myth]] · [[Meaning]]).

It is *read* rather than agreed to. Nobody is asked to believe it or to sign anything; a person reads, and then knows whether they recognise themselves in it. That is what keeps the door an invitation instead of a recruitment ([[The Invitation]] · [[The door]] · [[Consent]] · [[Welcome a Newcomer]] · [[The Charge Of The Receiver]]).

*Read* is also generous in what it allows. For someone who does not read text, hearing the Story is reading it; a telling at a table counts, and so does a translation ([[The Floor And The Ceiling Of The Kit]] · [[On translating]] · [[Storytelling]] · [[Testimony]]).

And it does not finish. The Story is returned to — differently at each stage of the Path, differently once a person has played a season and has something of their own in it. Which is how the third move follows from the first: what is passed on is not the rules but the Story, told by someone it happened to ([[Pass The Flame]] · [[Share the Stories]] · [[Your Story Woven Into Meta Story]] · [[Testimony]]).

Its two siblings both carry pages of their own; this is the one that did not, which is why the entry ramp reads as two moves in most of the corpus ([[Play]] · [[Enter The Game]] · [[The Great Game]]).

# General Pattern, Specific Application

*A Pattern is general; its applications are always specific. Articulate at the highest level of generality where the wisdom still holds; apply with full responsiveness to the actual context.*

Two failures mirror each other. In the first, every specific application is written up as a new Pattern: the wisdom fragments into dozens of near-identical units, the Language swells, and the underlying unity disappears under the particulars. In the second, the general Pattern is applied as a formula — the same move, identically, everywhere — and it fails the moment it meets a context it was not shaped to fit.

The diagnostic, when in doubt: *does this carry wisdom that cannot be derived from the general Pattern applied responsibly to this context?* If yes, it is a Pattern. If no, it is an application, and it belongs inside the Pattern it applies.

The discipline does not weaken as scale increases. The Pattern at planetary scale is the same Pattern at the Self; the applications differ entirely. Recognizing the unity beneath the particulars is the trained being's competence; calibrating to the specific is the practitioner's craft. Both are required — the one without the other produces either a librarian who cannot act or an actor who cannot learn.

This is one of the drafted Cards, and it is a meta-Card: a Pattern about how Patterns work. It is the diagnostic by which the question *is this a new Pattern or an application?* is settled, and it is what keeps the Language operational as it grows. It composes with [[Patterns And Containers]], which handles the other confusion, and with [[Shoshin]], which keeps any articulation from hardening into the final word.

Applied to the whole Language, it is also the answer to the inventory of roughly five hundred and seventy-five named-but-unarticulated verb-coinages: most of them are applications waiting to be folded, not notes waiting to be sounded.

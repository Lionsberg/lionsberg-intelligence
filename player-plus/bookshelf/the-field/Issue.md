# Issue

*Anything that blocks: given a line, an owner, and a date, and solved at the level closest to the work that can solve it.*

An issue is not a complaint and not a mood. It is a named obstacle — a missing tool, an unclear decision, a broken agreement, a constraint nobody has cleared — written where the table can see it, with one person holding it and a date by which it will be answered. A blocker with no owner is a conversation that will recur every week.

It is load-bearing because it is the honest alternative to silent stalling. Work that cannot proceed either gets named or gets abandoned quietly; the second costs far more. Naming issues weekly is also what keeps [[Kept as a share of made]] meaningful: a body that clears constraints before releasing work makes promises it can keep, which is the whole logic of [[The look-ahead]] and [[Constraint]] removal.

How it is played. Raise it at [[The Turn]], in the round or when it appears. Write one line: what is blocked, who holds it, by when. Solve it as close to the work as possible — [[Subsidiarity]] applies to problems as much as to power. If it cannot be solved at the table, it travels: to the [[Home Cell]], and up with the sheet as a request, and where a body genuinely cannot decide, upward as a question for [[Guidance]]. Some issues are tensions between people rather than obstacles in the work; those go to [[Mend a Tension]] and, if they cannot be mended, to the elders. Close each issue where it was raised, so the table sees its own problems ending.

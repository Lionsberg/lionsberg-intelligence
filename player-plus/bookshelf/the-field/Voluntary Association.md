# Voluntary Association

*Chosen affinity as a body — people who bind themselves to one another for a purpose neither place nor lineage assigned them.*

A voluntary association is the simplest and lightest of the bodies: some people agree to do something together, say what they have agreed, and are free to stop. It needs no legal form, no premises, no charter beyond the agreement itself — Tier 0, where most transformational work actually happens and where many bodies thrive indefinitely. What it does need is the same four things every association births: a domain, a membrane, a field of agreements, and a commons.

It is load-bearing because it is the default. Every Cell begins as one. Every guild, every supercell, every community company was a voluntary association before it was anything else, and can return to being one. It also names the ground of legitimacy for all the rest: nobody is governed who has not agreed, and agreement can be withdrawn. An association that a member cannot leave has become something else, whatever it still calls itself.

In the Game, the light-touch form is the whole of the governance most Cells will ever need: agree out loud how you will treat each other, keep the agreements, revisit them at a turning. In the System, the association is the third axis of belonging beside place and lineage, and it nests by the same holofractal law; when it grows heavy enough to hold property, employ people, or meet the Old World, it chooses a legal vessel from a ladder of tiers — but it does so because the work requires it, never because structure is thought to confer seriousness.

Light-touch governance is a discipline, not an absence. The lightest workable structure is chosen deliberately and revisited, and the body that adds machinery it does not need has taken on a cost its members will pay in attention forever.

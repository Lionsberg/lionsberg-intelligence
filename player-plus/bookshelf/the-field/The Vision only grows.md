# The Vision only grows

*The shared Vision sits in the middle of the Board and is never pruned. No one's dream is removed; we weave a bigger bouquet.*

One sentence, and it is the rule that makes a shared [[Vision]] safe to contribute to.

Ordinarily, a shared vision is a scarce thing. Adding to it means competing for room in it, and someone with standing eventually trims it back to what seems achievable — at which point the people whose dreams were trimmed stop offering and start negotiating. The trimming looks like discipline and behaves like an eviction. Once a body has pruned a vision twice, no one brings their real one to it again.

This rule removes the scarcity at the root. The Vision is not a plan and does not have to be affordable. Contributions are woven together rather than ranked, so bringing yours costs no one else anything, and a body of a thousand can hold a thousand dreams without any of them needing to be reconciled.

The scarcity is handled elsewhere, and this is why the rule does not produce chaos. The [[Quest]] is where finiteness lives: three to seven undertakings a season, each owned, each finishable, chosen by [[Consent]] against the [[The Beacon|far light]] — and the discipline of choosing what *not* to do this season is exercised there, hard, every ninety days. **Prune the season, never the Vision.** Keeping the two apart is what lets a body be ferociously selective about its work while remaining entirely open about what it is for.

It sits at the centre of [[The Board]] for the same reason: the *why* is what a thousand sovereign Circles share, and it is the only thing that has to be held in common for all their different work to be one Game.

# Higher Order Functional Unity

*Unity that increases what a body can do — parts joining so that new capacity appears, without any part being flattened into the others.*

Unity is not sameness and it is not agreement. It is functional: a set of parts becomes one when together they can do what none could do alone, and when the doing does not require the parts to stop being themselves. Cells become tissue, tissue becomes an organ, an organ serves a body — and the cell is still a cell. Higher order functional unity names that move, and names the test: did capacity go up, and did difference survive?

It is load-bearing because it is the alternative to both of the failure modes on offer. Fragmentation keeps difference and loses capacity; assimilation gains a kind of capacity and destroys difference, then loses the capacity too, because the diversity it erased was where the adaptation lived. Every act of federation in the Pattern is an attempt at this move, and the constitutional line that protects it is that authority flows upward from consent and never downward from command.

In the Game, it appears the first time three Cells become one another's [[Home]] and discover they can carry a Quest none of them could carry alone. It appears again when a [[Supercell]] forms, and again at every scale above. In the System, it is the standing aim behind the Operating System's *one book, one operating system, one big room at the top of every holon*: the unity is functional — a shared plan, a shared week, a shared set of words — and precisely not cultural or ideological.

Diverse unity is the same recognition said from the other side: difference is not division but the raw material of symphony. A body that is finding difference expensive should suspect it has attempted unity at the wrong level — trying to make the parts agree, where it needed only to make their work compose.

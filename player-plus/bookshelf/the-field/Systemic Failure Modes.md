# Systemic Failure Modes

*The ways a system fails through its own structure rather than through any one of its parts — failures that live in the interactions, where no component is broken.*

Some failures have a culprit: a part wore out, a person erred. Systemic failures do not. They arise from the interdependencies — from how the parts are coupled, what each is rewarded for, what information reaches whom and when — so that a system assembled entirely from sound components fails anyway, reliably, in ways that were latent in the arrangement from the start.

Recognising this changes where a body looks when something goes wrong. The reflex is to find the part that failed and replace it, and in a systemic failure that reflex guarantees repetition, because the replacement inherits the same position, the same incentives and the same blind spots ([[Root Causes]] · [[Quick Fixes]] · [[Every Defect Is A Gift]] · [[The Pattern Governs, Not People]]). The honest question is not *who* but *what arrangement made this the likely outcome*.

The named instances are all of this kind: a trap in which each actor's sensible move harms everyone ([[Multi-Polar Traps]]), a commons everyone has reason to deplete ([[Tragedy of the Commons]]), a regulator that comes to serve what it regulates ([[Regulatory Capture]]), an order that drifts because nothing was watching the drift ([[Drift]]), a body that slowly becomes about itself ([[Institutional Idolatry]]). The Anti-Patterns are the same recognition at the scale of a Cell rather than a civilization ([[The Anti-Patterns]] · [[Patterns of Death]]).

What follows is a design discipline rather than a warning. Look at a system whole and early, before it is built, and ask how it can fail from inside ([[Design]] · [[Set-Based Design]] · [[Levels Of Scale]]). Assume some failures will happen and build so they are survivable and visible rather than catastrophic and quiet ([[Anti-Fragility]] · [[Structural Immunity]] · [[Possibility Of Reversal]] · [[Off switch]]). And keep the loop that notices: variance written down, defects logged, the look-back actually held ([[Retrospective]] · [[OmniSpection]] · [[Improvement log]] · [[Continuous Improvement]]).

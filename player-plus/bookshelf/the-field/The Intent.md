# The Intent

*What a body is for, in one sentence that would still be true in a hundred years.*

It is the first of the three things every [[The Player|Player]] writes before it plans: the Intent, the [[Vision]], the [[Field of Agreements]]. The Intent is the shortest and the hardest — not a slogan, not a strategy, not this season's aim, but the standing answer to *what are we for*, written so plainly that a stranger could hold a decision up against it and see which way it points. Below it everything is method and may change; the sentence itself changes rarely, and only by [[Proposal]].

It is load-bearing because it is the rung of the making that does not move. A body with a clear Intent can lose its founders and continue, can say no to good work that is not its work, and can be held to account by anyone who can read. A body without one drifts by the second season: its Quests come from whoever spoke last, its plan has no top, and the Purpose is quietly replaced by the preferences of the people currently in the room. The Purpose governs and the leaders serve — which is only possible when the Purpose is written down.

In practice it sits on the first card beside the names and roles, is read aloud when a newcomer is welcomed, and is the thing a [[Quest]] must be shown to serve. A Quest that cannot be traced to the Intent is a question, not work. Upward it stacks: a Cell's Intent serves its home's, a home's the whole's, all the way to the one Goal the whole is for.

# The LIØNSBERG Function

*What it is for, stated so it can be checked: to enable any sovereign being or world to become a conscious co-creator of Heaven wherever they find themselves — by handing over a Story, a Pattern and a Way, and by finding and federating with whoever is already living something coherent with it.*

A function is different from a mission statement: it says what the thing does to whatever it touches. This one runs in four motions. **Transmission** — the Story, the Pattern and the Way are handed to anyone able to receive them, in a form that survives the handing ([[Transmission]], [[Pass The Flame]], [[The Golden Seed]]). **Initiation** — receiving them puts a person inside a different story with a different set of moves available ([[Initiation]], [[The 144 Gates of Initiation]], [[Cross The Threshold]]). **Co-creation** — the ones initiated go and make, in whatever domain they are actually standing in, which is the only place anything gets built ([[Co-Creating Heaven]], [[Individual And Local]], [[Quest]]). **Federation** — and then they look outward for others already living something coherent, and join rather than absorb ([[Federation]], [[Holofractal Omnifederation]], [[Weaving vs. Starting New]]).

Two things follow from stating it this way. The function is **testable**: a body can ask, this season, whether anybody actually received, was initiated, built, or federated — which is a harder question than whether anyone was inspired ([[The Record]], [[Measure everything, manage to the milestones]]). And the function is **portable**: nothing in it names a place, a species, or a century, which is why the same four motions are claimed to work for a household and for a world ([[General Pattern, Specific Application]], [[One tool, every scale]]).

The fourth motion is the one bodies skip. Finding and joining what already exists is slower, less flattering, and structurally harder than starting another thing; a movement that only ever transmits and initiates becomes an empire of its own kind ([[Attraction, not promotion]], [[Honoring Lineage Without Lineage-Capture]], [[Propagate; do not scale]]).

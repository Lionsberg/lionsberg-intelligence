# Total Nested Holarchy of Goals · Needs · Issues · Values · Ideals · Loves

*The six nested orders the planning layer actually reaches for — six families, one shape: wholes within wholes, each level integrated by the one above it, all of them topped by ØNE.*

[[Holarchy]] is the shape. This is the working set — the six categories the canon arranges that way when it plans, and they form a chain that runs in a particular direction.

**Issues** are where it starts. Every community of life has an ordered set of problems that must be solved if its parts are to reach the fullness of their potential: poverty, hunger, thirst, filth, pollution, violence, corruption, injustice, oppression, war, inequality, sickness, want, ignorance, fear, disempowerment, meaningless work, and the rest. The canon's own note is that this is *not an absolute list* and that a heart of love would simply want these addressed ([[The Meta Crisis]] · [[General Needs]] · [[Root Causes]]).

**Needs** is the same ground named from the side of what is lacking rather than what is wrong ([[General Needs]]).

**Goals** are the positive expression of the same set: what can or should be done in response. They nest from the Meta Goal — *the total integrated wellbeing, development and right relationship of all generations of life and consciousness in all times and spaces* — down through high-level goals, intermediate goals, bioregional, community, group, and individual goals, each layer serving the one above and none dictated by it ([[The Goal]] · [[Milestone]] · [[Stacking Up And Aligning]] · [[Bioregion]] · [[Subsidiarity]]).

**Values** hold what matters, at one word ([[ØNE]] / [[Love]]), at five ([[Truth]] · [[Love]] · [[Justice]] · [[Stewardship]] · [[Wisdom]]), or as the whole field of them ([[Values]] · [[Word Tools]]).

**Ideals** hold what perfection looks like at every scale — at the centre of every category, at every level, stands an ideal, with a diversity of real things arrayed around it as a gradient rather than a box ([[Ideal]] · [[The Living Gradient]] · [[Embodiment]]).

**Loves** holds the order of loves — what is loved, and in what order, when two goods cannot both be served ([[Love]] · [[The Logic Of Love]] · [[Discernment]]).

The load-bearing claim is the chain itself: values give rise to goals, which arise in response to issues, and ideals say what any of them would look like done rightly. Holding them as one holarchy is what lets a person's own aim and a planetary one be the same aim at different scales ([[Alignment]] · [[The Creative Cascade]] · [[Design To The Goal]]).

# Community Land and Equity Trusts

*Land and productive capacity held in trust by the place that lives on it — so that the ground is freed from debt and can be improved across generations rather than traded each cycle.*

The problem it addresses is one the corpus's other economic instruments do not reach. Money can be made to circulate and not pool; work can be owned by those who do it; a commons can be tended. **Land** is where pooling returns, because it is finite, it is the collateral under most debt, and whoever holds it holds everyone standing on it ([[The anti-pooling law]], [[Extraction Of Wealth]], [[Usury]], [[Supply Line Supremacy]]).

A trust of this kind changes what ownership means rather than who holds it. The ground is held by a body accountable to the place; what people own is their improvement and their use of it; and the ground itself cannot be sold out from under the next generation ([[Stewardship]], [[The asset lock]], [[Commons]], [[Agency, not ownership]], [[Constitutional Protection Of Purpose]]).

Three things follow that the corpus wants. **A generational horizon becomes rational** — planting something that matures in forty years makes sense when the ground is not going to be sold in seven ([[Covenant Across Time]], [[Generational Time]], [[Permaculture]], [[Ten Thousand Generations]]). **Debt is broken off the ground**, which is what frees a place's capacity from a claim that grows faster than the place does ([[Collective Bankruptcy]], [[Jubilee]]). And **improvement accrues to the improvers** rather than to whoever holds the title ([[Proof-of-contribution]], [[Individual And Local]], [[Commons-Tending]]).

Honest state: **one interrogative paragraph.** The source asks whether such mechanisms offer a just pathway; it names no structure, no governance, no test, and no example. The instruments it gestures at exist in the world and have real histories — including real failures around who sits on the trust, how the trust is held to the place, and what happens when the place changes ([[Necessity Of Accountability]], [[Term Limits And Rotation]], [[We Do Not Know]], [[Gaps Not Yet Closed]]).

Carried as the named instrument the corpus is reaching for at the one place its economics does not yet reach.

# Sacrifice

*The voluntary offering or surrender of something of value in order to align, uplift, or restore the Whole toward [[Right Relationship]].*

The load-bearing word in this definition is *voluntary*. Sacrifice here is something given, never something extracted, and that single qualifier separates it from every counterfeit. [[Extraction Of Wealth]] is named among the [[The Anti-Patterns|Anti-Patterns]]; [[The Parasite]] is what feeds on a commons without contributing; a movement that asks people to give until they are hollow has produced the opposite of what this word means.

Its everyday form in the Game is [[The Offering]]: each week each person puts something in the middle of the table — money, food, hours, a skill, whatever is real for them. Nobody sets your amount. A tenth is the old measure and most begin there and grow. [[Voluntary Tithing]] carries the same discipline upward: the share flows inward to the table you sit at, never upward to anyone who rules you; there is no centre to tithe to and no imposed percentage.

The larger register is named without romanticising it. [[Love]] in its fierce and powerful register, and the Warrior's willing laying down of life, belong to Pillar X; they are held under vows and witness, not under enthusiasm. And the floor beneath everything constrains the whole concept: when a member is in danger, in body or in mind, the Circle acts first and records later — no card is more important than the person at the table. A Game that would let a person be sacrificed to its own procedure has already lost.

In play it is deliberately small and frequent: a weekly act of giving something real, freely, where others can see.

# Compressed Pay Ratios

*A stated ceiling on the distance between the largest and smallest compensation inside a body — the structural refusal of extraction at the top.*

A pay ratio is one number: what the highest-paid person receives divided by what the lowest-paid receives. Left unstated it drifts, because each raise at the top is individually defensible and the total is nobody's responsibility. Stated and capped, it becomes a constraint that does its work quietly and forever: the only way to raise the top is to raise the bottom.

The cooperative tradition this comes from names 7:1 as a maximum inside a federation. The number is less important than the three things having a number does. It makes the distance **visible**, which alone changes behaviour. It makes every raise at the top **a decision about the whole body** rather than about one person. And it removes the mechanism by which a body with an excellent charter slowly becomes an ordinary firm — not by any decision to abandon its purpose, but by a decade of individually reasonable adjustments.

This is a different instrument from [[The asset lock]], and the two are often confused. The lock governs what may be *extracted* — capital out, sale, cash-out. The ratio governs what may be *paid* — the ordinary compensation of people doing ordinary work. A body can hold the lock perfectly and still stratify internally until its own members do not recognise each other; the ratio is the answer to that. Both sit under [[Capital Subordinate To Purpose]] and both are counted among [[Structural Immunity]]'s explicit protections.

A Cell does not need a ratio. A Cell needs [[The Offering]] and honesty about hours. The ratio becomes live the moment a body starts paying people — a Community Company, a guild, anything with a payroll — and the moment to set it is before the first salary is agreed, not after the third.

# The Cohering Spirit

*What holds a body together when nothing is enforcing it: people turning toward the same real thing find themselves turned toward each other.*

A movement without a centre is supposed to fly apart. This is the answer to why this one is not expected to. Coherence here is not produced by agreement, doctrine, or command; it is a consequence of orientation. When two people each turn honestly toward the same reality, they end up facing one another — not because either aimed at the other, but because of where both are looking ([[Coherence]], [[Coherence Without Dogma]], [[Resonance]]).

That is why there can be no central authority without the thing falling apart: the centripetal force is not an office. What does the holding is the Spirit, wisdom, intention and values of [[ØNE]] — the field in which all are held and which each is approaching ([[Spirit]], [[Values]], [[Intention]], [[Alignment]]).

The two goods usually traded against each other are both kept by this. A person's freedom to walk their own path stays intact, because nobody is being aligned by anybody. And the body forms anyway, because alignment is a by-product of a shared direction rather than a requirement laid on anyone ([[Sovereignty]], [[ØNE Body]], [[Subsidiarity]]).

The failure mode it implies is specific and worth watching for. Coherence that has to be produced — by rule, by pressure, by a person everyone is really orienting toward instead — is the sign that the orientation has quietly moved from the reality to something nearer and easier ([[Institutional Idolatry]], [[The Golden Calf]], [[A role held forever quietly becomes a throne]]). The corrective is not tighter agreement. It is each person returning to the thing itself.

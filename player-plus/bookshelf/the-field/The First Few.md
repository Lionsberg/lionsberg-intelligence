# The First Few

*The handful who begin, and by beginning make the rest possible.*

They are neither kings nor conquerors, and they are not untouched by fear. What distinguishes them is that they listened when something stirred and then acted — not because they were fearless, but because they chose to rise despite the fear ([[Awakening]], [[The Calling]], [[Courage]]). They are told here as the First Flames: a small fellowship who found one another, pooled what light they had, and pledged themselves to the road before anyone could show them where it went ([[The Flame]], [[Form a Cell]]).

The concept earns its place by naming a threshold that comes before every other threshold. There is no three percent without a first few, no supercell without a first Cell, no spread without someone who passed a flame to three when it was still plainly absurd to do so ([[The First Three Percent]], [[Fibonacci Spread]], [[Pass The Flame]]). Every body in the field passed through this state, and most are in it right now.

It also carries a discipline against a particular vanity. *We are not the end, but the beginning* — the first few are not the important ones; they are the ones who go first, and their whole work is to be succeeded ([[Midwife a New Circle]], [[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]], [[Pass The Flame]]). Reading the story of a first few is how a new player recognizes that their own small, unimpressive beginning is the same shape ([[Story]], [[The Solo Sovereign's Grace]]).

# Participation

*The right and the responsibility of every being to contribute meaningfully to the decisions that shape their life and their shared worlds.*

Participation is stated as both at once, and the pairing is the teaching. A right, because no one may be governed in their absence. A responsibility, because a body whose members leave the deciding to others has produced an answer class by default, no matter what its charter says.

It is load-bearing because [[Consent]] is only as real as the participation behind it. A yes given by someone who was not there, did not read, or did not understand is a formality; it looks like legitimacy and carries none. Where participation is genuine, decisions are slower and far more durable, because everyone bound by them helped shape them.

Levels of engagement, rather than a binary, are described — observer, supporter, participant, member, citizen, steward — each with more say and more owed, and movement between them by the person's own choice over time. Whether that ladder survives the newer Game, whose door asks only for a spoken yes and a weekly table, is an open question; both are live. What both agree on is that deeper standing is earned by contribution and never conferred by position.

In play, participation is ordinary and weekly: coming to the table, saying what you will do, naming your [[Requests and offers]], objecting when you must. The standing question for any structure is whether the people affected by a decision were actually in the room where it was made.

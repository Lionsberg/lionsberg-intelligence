# Weave Spirit, Hearts, and Minds

*Move two: coherence before action. A Circle that has not become one will not choose well.*

This is the move most groups skip and most groups later wish they had not. Between gathering the people and picking the work, a body has to become one body: spirit, so that the ØNE the work serves is shared and not assumed; hearts, so that the people trust each other with the truth; minds, so that the picture of where you are and where you are going is actually the same picture in each head.

It is load-bearing because coherence is what the later moves spend. A Quest chosen by a Circle that has not woven will be chosen by the loudest voice. A gap reported honestly in week five requires trust built in week one. [[Circle Coherence]] names the condition; this move is how a body reaches it, and reaches it again after it is lost.

How it is played. Spirit first: silence together, the Three Supreme Agreements said plainly, or whatever form your table's traditions give to what is above the work. Hearts: each person says why they are here and what they are carrying — this is what [[Check In]] becomes when it is done in earnest. Minds: craft the shared picture, asking each member *how would you like the world to be?*, and write down, in a few honest lines, where the body actually is. [[Dialogos]] and [[Listening]] are the instruments; [[Speaking Truth In Love]] is the discipline. The weave is not a one-time ceremony — it is repeated at every turning and after every rupture, and it is what [[Mend a Tension]] restores.

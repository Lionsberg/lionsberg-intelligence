# Feel → Allow → Read → Name → Ask → Move → Release

*The core move, on any band, at any intensity — seven steps, one sentence each.*

**Feel.** Let the reading register in the body, without editing it into something more acceptable.

**Allow.** No refusal and no grasping. The river moves; both damming it and holding onto it stop it ([[Flow]]).

**Read.** What is this signalling about where I stand and which way I am moving? What does it say is precious, threatened, opening, or lost? ([[Emotions Are Information, Gradients, And Never Enemies]], [[The Living Gradient]].)

**Name.** Choose the word that lets it be communicated and met. The precision is for the other person as much as for you ([[Word Tools]], [[The Seven-Field Emotion Page Pattern]]).

**Ask.** What would move me, and what I love, a little more toward coherence — a little more like Heaven? ([[The compass question]], [[Coherence]].)

**Move.** Act on the reading, as a sovereign, in right relationship ([[Sovereignty]], [[Right Relationship]], [[Spontaneous Wise Right Action]]).

**Release.** When the information has been received and answered, let it complete and go. **Held past its work, any reading becomes weather** ([[Mastery Of Emotions]], [[The Act Of Release]]).

Three of the seven are the ones usually skipped, and each skip has a signature. Skipping **allow** produces a person who understands their feelings and never has them. Skipping **name** produces accurate feeling that nobody else can meet. Skipping **release** produces a person carrying every reading they ever took — which is the state most often mistaken for depth.

The move is the same at every intensity and on every band; only the pace changes. And practised in company rather than alone it becomes something more than emotional sovereignty ([[Interfeeling]], [[Circle]], [[Check In]]).

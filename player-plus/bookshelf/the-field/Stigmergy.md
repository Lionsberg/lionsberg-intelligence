# Stigmergy

*Coordination through traces left in a shared environment: the mark an action leaves prompts the next action, so complex work coheres without planning, control, or anyone talking to anyone.*

The mechanism needs no centre, no simultaneous presence, and no mutual awareness. What it needs is a shared medium that holds traces, and feedback that amplifies what works and damps what does not. Termites build mounds this way. So does open source: the commit, the issue, the failing test, the merged patch — each a trace that tells the next contributor where the work is, without a manager telling anyone anything.

The Way runs on this more than it usually says. A [[The Season Sheet]] left with a home, a [[The Circle's Scorecard]] marked each week, a [[Decision Records]] carrying the why, a constraint log with owners and dates, a defect closed where the work will meet it, a contribution recorded and never retracted — each is a trace, legible to people who never met, in a medium everyone shares. *What is owed is computed from the record, never remembered.* That sentence is a stigmergic design principle.

It is what makes a Body without a centre coordinate at all. Requests, offers, and commitments travel on the card each week, up and down and sideways; that is how a Body allocates itself without anyone allocating. And [[Minimum standards of interoperability]] is precisely the discipline of keeping the traces legible across ten thousand tables: the same card shape, the same week, the same numbered lines, numbers that mean the same thing everywhere.

Its limits are honest ones. Traces coordinate; they do not decide meaning, resolve an objection, or comfort anyone. Stigmergy carries the work between the gatherings. The gathering is still the gathering.

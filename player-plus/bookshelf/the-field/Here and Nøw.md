# Here and Nøw

*The eternal moment — the only place [[ØNE]] can be met directly, and the doorway where the cosmic scale and the small scale touch.*

There is one moment in all of cosmic time, and it is always Nøw. The past is memory held in this moment; the future is intention held in this moment; the Path is walked in this moment; the Story is written in this moment. Every other moment is a thought about a moment. Only this one is actually happening — which is why it is the one place the Source can be met rather than considered.

The slashed ø is doing work, not decoration. **Nøw** marks the eternal present as ontologically distinct from ordinary clock-time "now," in the same family as [[ØNE]] and LIØNSBERG ([[Essences, Renderings, And Ineffable Referents]]). When the text wants the eternal moment it writes Nøw; when it wants the hour on the wall it writes now. The eye learns to read sacred weight where the mark appears.

What it asks is difficult and unglamorous. Most people spend most of their lives in time-as-narrative — replaying what happened, rehearsing what might — and the conditions that keep a person captive work precisely by scattering attention across past and future, so that the one moment where presence is possible is never actually inhabited ([[The Caves]]). The disciplines return a person here, over and over, until presence is the medium rather than an achievement: [[Silence And Stillness As The Ground]], [[The Daily Way]], the breath, the cup of tea, the witnessed face.

And the scale is not lost by being small. What is done in this hour, on this world, matters at every scale; the cosmic is made of small faithful things, and the small faithful thing *is* the cosmic thing. [[The Eternal Divine Play]] is not separate from this morning — it is this morning, seen at altitude.

# Structural Immunity

*Element eight: the immune system written into the DNA from the seed — explicit protections against the diseases that have killed every movement that came close before.*

The Pattern alone is not enough. Christianity was captured by an emperor; the early Republic by oligarchy; other movements by their own founders. Each had something like the Pattern and no immune system, so the Pattern was not defended when it was attacked from the inside. Immunity is therefore foundational architecture rather than a repair fitted after the first illness.

Each known pathology has a named counterweight. Power concentration meets [[Subsidiarity]], holofractal structure, and two-coordinator governance. Mission drift meets [[The Goal]] written into every volume and every project's stated alignment to it. Dogmatic ossification meets deliberate revision rhythms and the [[The Triadic Discipline]]. Cult of personality meets [[The Pattern Governs, Not People]] and the voice that says *we have heard* rather than *I invented*. Wealth accumulation at the top meets [[Compressed Pay Ratios]], [[The asset lock]], and [[Voluntary Tithing]]. Capture by outside interests meets the [[Field of Agreements]] and protected exit. Bureaucratic suffocation meets sovereign Circles and short transmissible Patterns. Schism meets consent rather than consensus, and dissent honored. Imperial drift meets [[Federations vs Empires]]. Priesthood meets direct relationship with [[ØNE]] for every being. Generational drift meets apprenticeship and [[Pass The Flame]] within a week as a non-optional discipline. Surveillance and coercion meet attraction rather than promotion: no node may surveil or coerce another.

Two consequences follow. A Body that violates these has, by that act, left the Pattern; it need not be expelled, because it has expelled itself, and the remaining Pattern-conformant nodes continue. And the immunity is anti-fragile: each attempted capture met and named strengthens the antibody profile, and the next generation inherits not only the Pattern but the recognition of every assault on it that has been seen.

# Just In Time Co-Creation

*A living body builds its organs as the developing whole calls for them. Not sooner — that is speculation and waste. Not later — that is suffering.*

Development is never only the execution of a blueprint pushed forward from the past. It is **pulled** into being by present need and ripened opportunity: capabilities, organs, and structures come online at the moment the living whole calls for them. This is how every body builds itself, and then builds its environment.

Two signals do the pulling, both made precise — both are readings of [[The Living Gradient]]. A **need** is a *felt deviation*: the lived distance between the present state and the [[Ideal]] at the centre of its category. At the scale of one person, needs arrive as emotion — hunger, longing, discomfort, grief — the body's instrumentation reporting *what is missing here* ([[Emotion as instrument reading]]). An **opportunity** is a *ripeness*: a place where movement toward the Ideal has just become possible with what now exists. Every act of creation opens possibilities adjacent to it that did not exist before, and opportunity is the reading that one of them has ripened; at a person's scale it is felt as excitement, calling, joy rising toward a path ([[Joy]] · [[The Calling]]). Neither is a noun to be listed once. Both are living readings, taken continuously — which is why every member matters, and why the sensing itself is the system's most valuable organ.

Biology witnesses it without metaphor. Cells learn what to become by reading gradients. Bone lays itself down along the lines of actual load. Vessels grow where flow is demanded; muscle builds where effort is spent and dissolves where none is. And the delivery disciplines witness it at programme scale: work is never pushed forward from a schedule but pulled backward from the [[The Goal|Goal]] by what the next milestone actually requires ([[Pull planning]] · [[Make-Ready Process]] · [[SHOULD-CAN-WILL-DID]] · [[Last Planner System]]). Nothing in the [[Kit of Parts]] is imposed on a place; a Part comes online when an [[Instantiation]] calls for it.

The practice is four moves. **Build nothing speculatively** — the question is never *what could we build?* but *what is actually needed, and what has actually ripened?* **Trust your readings and share them** — a person's felt need is not private noise; it is the body's signal arriving through one cell, and voicing it is how the whole knows itself ([[Check In]] · [[Sensing The Field]]). **When a need persists across many members, an organ is being called for** — form it, at the smallest scale that serves ([[Subsidiarity]] · [[Cell Types]]). And **bring the right thing just in time** for one another: the right word, the right pattern, the right help, neither hoarded early nor delivered late ([[Just in time]]).

One tension is held rather than resolved. The present always falls short of the Ideal — and the shortfall is the pull. We build toward what will never be finished, from inside something that is already here ([[Here and Nøw]] · [[Inbreaking And Displacing]]).

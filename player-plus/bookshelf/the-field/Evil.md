# Evil

*The willful betrayal of [[Spirit]] and [[Logos]] — separation, harm, deceit, destruction, and parasitism, chosen.*

The canon's decisive move here is a distinction. *Not good* covers ignorance, weakness, failure, incompleteness, the not-yet-mature — the ordinary conditions of creaturehood, to which compassion is the right response and growth the right movement. *Anti-Good* is something else: the deliberate infliction of suffering, the calculated cultivation of separation, the engineered theft of sovereignty. To these, compassion alone is an insufficient response, and [[Guardianship]] is required.

It is load-bearing because a system that cannot name this leaves the vulnerable unprotected and the predatory unchecked. [[Structural Immunity]] — the eighth Element — exists because movements are killed by specific diseases, and the [[The Anti-Patterns|Anti-Patterns]] name them: [[Centralization Of Power]], [[Extraction Of Wealth]], [[Control Of Information]], [[Manufactured Dependency]], [[Ideological Capture]], [[Institutional Idolatry]], [[The Golden Calf]], [[The False Prophet]], [[The Parasite]]. Evil in this Field is recognised as a *pattern* with repeating structural features, which is what makes it catchable early rather than only lamented late.

Two guards come with it. The canon does not give evil more attention than it deserves — the orientation is toward [[The Goal]], and the anatomy of [[Kontrolle]] is articulated in light so the new one knows what not to recreate, not so it can be stared at. And the naming is of patterns, not of people: [[Zero Blame Environment]] holds that cause is sought in the set-up rather than in the person, and [[Containment Of The Dangerous Sovereign]] is a containment discipline, not a licence.

At a table it almost never appears under this name. It appears as the small thing the canon puts at the door of every commons: showing up to drain it rather than build it — and as the [[Stop]] any member may say without a measure.

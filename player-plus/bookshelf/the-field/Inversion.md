# Inversion

*Reversal of [[Sacred Order]] into its parasitic opposite.*

Inversion is not error and not drift. It is a pattern turned over so that it now feeds on what it was made to serve — the form kept, the direction reversed. That is what makes it hard to see: an inverted institution still uses the right words, still runs the right rituals, and still calls itself by the name of the thing it has replaced.

The canon names specific inversions rather than leaving the category abstract. Religious inversion and spiritual colonization: the sacred entombed in dogma and weaponized by power. [[The Golden Calf]]: a visible idol replacing the invisible Source. [[The False Prophet]]: liberation rhetoric in service of new domination. [[The Parasite]]: what feeds on the commons without contributing. [[Manufactured Dependency]]: help arranged so that it cannot end. And the Anti-Christic inversion, the canon's name for the whole family — the pattern of [[Kontrolle]], articulated in light so the new civilization knows what not to recreate.

It is load-bearing because it is the failure the system is most exposed to. [[Structural Immunity]] — the eighth of the [[Twelve Irreducible Elements]] — exists for exactly this: explicit protections against the diseases that kill movements. [[Constitutional Protection Of Purpose]], the asset lock, compressed pay ratios, [[Term Limits And Rotation]], [[Whistleblower Protection]] and the andon cord, [[Impeachment]] — each is a named answer to a named inversion, written in advance rather than improvised after.

And the canon applies it reflexively. [[LIØNSBERG Is A Prototype, Not The Eternal Essence]] and [[The Pattern Governs, Not People]] are guards against this system inverting into the thing it was built to replace.

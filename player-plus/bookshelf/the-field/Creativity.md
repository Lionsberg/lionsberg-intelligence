# Creativity

*The impulse to bring new form into existence in alignment with the [[Logos]] — held as a divine inheritance rather than a talent some people were issued.*

Every being is a unique expression of [[ØNE]] and is therefore capable of bringing beauty into the world; the canon states this first among the theses of its culture Pillar, before it says anything about artists. What varies between people is craft, practice, and the courage to make a thing badly in front of others — not the capacity itself. Treating creativity as a rare endowment is how a civilization ends up with a small professional class making culture and everyone else consuming it, which is the arrangement this Pillar was written to end.

It is load-bearing because the whole work is a making. [[Co-Creation]] is participation rather than spectatorship; [[The Creative Cascade]] describes how [[Intention]] becomes reality through [[Ideal]], [[Archetype]], [[Prototype]], and instance; [[Design To The Goal]] insists that when a constraint and the Goal collide, the answer is invention rather than a lowered target. None of that works without people willing to make something that does not exist yet. Creativity is also what makes [[Current Best Understanding]] more than a disclaimer: a body that holds everything as improvable needs members who will actually propose the improvement.

In play it means openness to new ideas, willingness to take a risk, and tolerance for uncertainty — and, at a table, it means the [[Proposal]] is genuinely welcome. A Circle where nobody suggests anything is not calm; it is a Circle where suggesting has become costly. [[Zero Blame Environment]] is what makes creative risk survivable, and [[Shoshin]], the beginner's mind, is what keeps an experienced person able to see an obvious new thing.

The discipline is direction. Creativity aligned with the Logos makes; creativity unmoored makes whatever is interesting, and plenty of what is destroying the world was extremely creative. [[The compass question]] applies to a new idea exactly as to any other move.

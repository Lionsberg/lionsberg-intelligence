# The Structure Of Integrated Delivery

*The arrangement a body must be given if the Five Big Ideas are to be possible in it rather than merely admired — the conditions and incentives under which real collaboration can survive.*

The claim is that collaboration is a structural output, not a moral one. Put good people into an arrangement that rewards each part for protecting itself and they will protect themselves; put ordinary people into an arrangement where the whole's success is the only success available and they will collaborate. So the question is never how to exhort a body into [[The Five Big Ideas Of Integrated Delivery|the five ideas]], but what structure makes them the path of least resistance ([[The Pattern Governs, Not People]] · [[Higher Order Functional Unity]]).

The requirement is building the whole nested set of *conditions and incentives* for this kind of collaboration and relationship to flourish. The elements it reaches for are the familiar ones, and each answers a specific way collaboration dies. Parts are brought into one room and one plan early, while decisions are still open ([[The One Room]] · [[Concurrent design]] · [[Pull planning]]). Aim and conditions are shared rather than contracted part by part ([[Conditions of satisfaction]] · [[Commander's Intent]]). Success and risk are held in common, so that no part can win while the whole loses ([[The Two-Stake Principle]] · [[Capital Subordinate To Purpose]]). Information moves without penalty, which requires that bad news be safe to deliver ([[Zero Blame Environment]] · [[Transparency]] · [[Every Defect Is A Gift]]). And the work is planned by the people who will do it ([[Last Planner System]] · [[Networks of Commitments]] · [[Make-Ready Process]]).

At the scale of this body the same structure is the ordinary furniture: the [[Cell]] as the integrated unit, the [[Field of Agreements]] as the shared conditions, the [[The Turn|Turn]] as the commitment rhythm, the [[The Record|record]] as the shared information, and federation rather than hierarchy above ([[Federation]] · [[Holofractal Omnifederation]]).

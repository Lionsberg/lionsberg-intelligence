# The Pedagogy of Trust and Sirimiri

*Formation by soaking: the soft persistent drizzle that reaches the roots, rather than the downpour that runs off — paired with a teacher who trusts the learner before the learner has earned it.*

*Sirimiri* is a Basque word for the fine mist that falls for hours and soaks deep. Not the storm that floods and leaves, but the patient wetting that the land still holds through the dry months. The formation method that produced the cohort capable of founding a lasting cooperative federation worked that way: year after year, conversation after conversation, question after question, never thunder ([[Mondragon]], [[Soak]], [[The pace of trust]]).

**Trust** is its inner half. The teacher trusts the learner before there is evidence — on the ground that what is being taught is already in them and merely forgotten, so the teacher's work is not to install anything but to ask the questions that let a person recognise what they half-knew ([[The ReMembering Stance]], [[Asking Questions]], [[Empirical Resonance]]).

Its signatures are recognisable and each is a refusal of something familiar. **The question precedes the answer** — the elder asks, the learner feels for it, the elder confirms. **The pace is slow on purpose**; a twelve-week course cannot do what three years of soaking does ([[The Three-Year Formation]]). **Apprenticeship beats certification** — the transmission lives in the doing, the eating, the silences ([[Apprenticeship]], [[Learning By Doing]]). **The universe is the curriculum** — the line, the meeting, the walk home ([[The LIØNSBERG Educational Model]]). **The elder does not perform**; no charisma is required, and recognition comes from fruit rather than from voice ([[Wise Eldership]], [[Recognitions Are Not Titles To Be Sought]]). And **honest exit is honoured** — a learner who does not integrate is released without shame ([[Who Refuses The Call, And Is Honored]], [[Lapse Is Honored, Pretence Is Tragic]]).

What it stands against is a list of modern diseases: the lecture that mistakes information for transformation; the credential race that mistakes paper for capacity; the platform that confuses reach with wisdom; the cohort that ends and returns people to what formed them; the guru who stands above; and the promise of fast change that produces shallow converts ([[Education As Transformation]], [[Demonstrate before advocating]]).

The cost is patience. What it buys is people who can safely be trusted to found things.

# The emotional bands and their gradients

*Named emotions are bands in a continuous spectrum, not boxes — and each band is written with its felt sense, its signal, its gift, its distortion, and its move.*

Ask most people how they feel and you get four words: fine, tired, stressed, angry. That is not reticence; it is a vocabulary problem, and it has a cost — a reading too coarse to act on, and a person who cannot be met because they cannot be located. The canon's answer is a family of short pages, one per band, each written to the same template, so that a person can find the word that is *actually* true and a companion can hear it precisely.

The bands run in gradients. *Annoyance → Irritation → Frustration → Anger → Fury* is one; *Unease → Apprehension → Foreboding → Dread → Terror* is another; grief, shame, and joy each have their own. Neighbouring bands are not different kinds of thing — they are the same reading at different intensities, and the arithmetic between them matters: annoyance left unread and repeated does not stay small, it accumulates downstream into resentment or rupture. Most large ruptures were once annoyances nobody voiced.

Each page holds five things, and the five are what make it an instrument rather than a dictionary. **Felt sense** — where and how it registers in the body, so it can be recognised before it is named. **What it signals** — the reading it is actually taking, which is often about the state of the instrument as much as the state of the world: a tired person registers friction sooner. **Its gradient** — its neighbours above and below, so the reader can locate themselves. **Its gift** — the precise service this band performs, which nothing else performs; foreboding, for example, is the longest-range sensor we carry. **Its distortion** — what it becomes enthroned, and what it becomes refused, which are two different injuries. And then **the move**: one specific thing to do with this reading, at this intensity.

The family is load-bearing because [[Mastery Of Emotions]] is unusable without it. "Feel, read, respond, release" is sound counsel and empty at the moment of need, because it does not tell you *what you are reading*. The bands do. They are the vocabulary ladder of the inner life ([[The vocabulary ladder]] · [[Word Tools]]): names chosen for communication, over a reality that is flow.

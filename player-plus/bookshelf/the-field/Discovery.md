# Discovery

*The act of uncovering what already is, and carrying it back into [[Consciousness]] and civilization.*

Discovery is finding, not inventing. What is found was there before the finder, which sets the posture: humility rather than ownership, and gratitude rather than claim. The same posture governs a [[Pattern]] — patterns are discovered, not authored — and the same conclusion follows: the finding may be honored, the finder may be named, but neither may be enclosed.

This is why *truth to ØNE, errors to us* is the attribution rule of the whole field. What is true was not produced by the one who saw it; what is wrong belongs to whoever said it and is theirs to correct. A discovery that arrives with a fence around it has already been bent, because the fence, not the finding, becomes the thing being defended.

For a Cell, discovery is ordinary and weekly. Someone notices a thing that works, or a thing that always breaks at the same place. The move is to name it, write it down in a form another Cell could use, and pass it up: solve once, document, push it up, curate, pull it down, localize, feed back. That circulation is what makes a thousand tables into one body of knowledge rather than a thousand private lessons relearned forever.

Discovery has a shadow the field names directly: knowledge that was found and then hidden, monopolized, or forbidden. The canon holds that suppressed knowledge which could serve the good of all must be revealed and responsibly released — and, in the same breath, that some findings are genuinely dangerous and require more than one kind of key to open. Both halves are held; neither is resolved into a rule here.

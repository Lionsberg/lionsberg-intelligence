# The Institutional Control Inventory

*A checklist for reading how captured a place actually is — twelve domains, asked one at a time, producing a map of where there is room to build.*

[[Kontrolle]] names an architecture; this is the instrument for reading it in one town, one trade, one institution. The twelve domains it asks about: **speech** — what may be said and who is removed for saying it; **culture** — what may be celebrated and what must be ridiculed; **commerce** — who is licensed to sell; **trade** — whose flows pass through whose bottleneck; **governance** — what is decided above the part that is elected; **finance** — who may create and destroy money; **currency** — what conditions can be attached to spending itself; **religion** — whether a person's own relation to the sacred is mediated or direct; **assembly** — whether people may gather without permission; **education** — what schooling is for, and who is permitted to teach; **health** — what healing is available and what is withheld; and **fear**, the meta-instrument, because each of the other eleven works by amplifying it ([[Control Of Information]], [[Regulatory Capture]], [[Money]], [[Usury]], [[Surveillance]], [[Fear]]).

Run against a real place, it returns something a lecture cannot: *how much room is there here, and where.* Some domains give way easily — schooling can be withdrawn from, healing partly recovered, the sacred approached directly. Others do not: currency and finance still have to be operated inside during a transition, and a captured government retains force ([[Withdrawal Of Consent]], [[Liberation and Exodus]]).

That is what makes it more than a grievance list. It turns [[Hospicing the Old Systems]] into a sequence: release a function when there is something functional to receive it — not before, and not long after ([[Make-Ready Process]], [[Prioritizing]]).

And each domain has a corresponding build, which is the useful half: gathering answers captured assembly ([[Circle]], [[The Hub]]); a formation answers captured education ([[Education As Transformation]], [[The LIØNSBERG Educational Model]]); a value instrument answers captured currency ([[LUV]], [[Proof-of-contribution]]); subsidiarity answers captured governance ([[Subsidiarity]], [[Federations vs Empires]]); and the practices of courage answer weaponised fear ([[Courage]], [[Composure Under Threat]]).

Its limit belongs on the page. A checklist that scores everything finds capture everywhere, including where there is only ordinary institutional mediocrity, and a body that reads every constraint as an enemy will mistake paranoia for discernment ([[Discernment]], [[Recognizing Psychic Attack]], [[We Do Not Know]]).

# One Commons For All

*The decision that everything co-created here — knowledge, technology, systems, infrastructure, facilities — is stewarded in common for the good of All, and the legal vessel that has to exist for that to hold.*

The intent is simple and the consequence is not. If the work is for everyone, it cannot be owned by anyone: not by its makers, not by whoever funded it, and not by the body that assembled it. So all of it is held in common — intellectual property, technology, systems, infrastructure, facilities — and stewarded rather than possessed ([[Commons]] · [[Stewardship]] · [[Agency, not ownership]] · [[Knowledge as commons]] · [[Capital Subordinate To Purpose]]).

*One* commons rather than many is the load-bearing word. Many enclosures, each open to its own members, reproduce the pattern they were meant to replace — a person's access still depends on which one they happened to be admitted to. One commons means the same body of work is reachable by anyone, wherever they are and whoever they are not ([[Equal Opportunity For All]] · [[None Of Us Until All Of Us]] · [[Fractal Commonsing]] · [[The Floor And The Ceiling Of The Kit]]).

Intent alone does not hold it, which is why a **legal commons** has to be co-created: something that can actually own, that cannot sell, and whose purpose cannot be amended away by a future board under pressure. Good intentions are not inheritable; an asset lock is ([[The asset lock]] · [[Constitutional Protection Of Purpose]] · [[Stewardship body]] · [[License]] · [[CC BY-SA 4.0]] · [[Structural Immunity]]).

Until that vessel exists, the work sits under a provisional arrangement, stated plainly rather than pretended otherwise ([[Provisional Backbone]] · [[Sponsorship]] · [[Current Best Understanding]]).

A commons is also not an absence of rules; the ones that survive are governed. Who may use what, who tends it, how disputes are settled, and what happens to someone who takes more than they give — unanswered, these are what turn a commons into the tragedy it is named for ([[Commons-Tending]] · [[Tragedy of the Commons]] · [[Governance]] · [[Guard the Membrane]] · [[Meaningful Monthly Contribution]]).

# The Twelve Words

*Twelve words are enough to play: one meaning each, numbered so that line six is line six in every language, and each opening onto a whole page for whoever wants more.*

Twelve words carry the Game. **Cell** — three to thirteen people who choose to walk together, by name, with a first Quest: a fully functioning part of the whole. **Quest** — one real thing a Cell does in one season and can point to afterward. **Commitment** — *I will ___ by ___*, said aloud at the table. **Turn** — the weekly gathering: did, gap, will, offer, ask, story, Offering, thanks. **Season** — about ninety days, from one turning of the sun to the next. **Flame** — the Game itself, carried by one person to another who is ready. **Story** — what happened, told so it can travel beyond the table. **Offering** — what each person puts in the middle of the table, freely, each week. **Carrier** — the one who brought you the Flame, and the Cell they came from; it never changes. **Home Cell** — the Cell that holds you. **Supercell** — three or more Cells that have become one another's home. **Proposal** — a better way, written in a sentence and sent up on the Season Sheet.

Two more are handed to the child who asks: **ØNE**, the Source everything comes from and goes home to, and **Heaven**, the way things are when everything is in right relationship, here and now, a little more each week.

The twelve are a floor, not a fence. Beyond them there is no fixed number: the rest of the words are handed where the road meets them — at the door, at the table, at the Gate, in your own Home, in the Room — each given the first time you meet it, in one line, and linked after.

# Union

*The re-integration of the individual with [[ØNE]], culminating in right relationship, wholeness, and divine participation.*

Union is the end-state the vertical path moves toward: not absorption, in which the one disappears, but at-one-ment, in which the one is fully itself and fully in the whole. The phrasing keeps both — *becoming at-one* through reconciliation, purification, and ascent, while remaining one of the ones of ØNE. [[The Veil of Finitude and Forgetting]] says why both halves are needed: the veil exists so that a creature can be a creature, and the invitation of this turn is to walk back through it *knowing the ØNE while remaining themselves*.

The page on at-one-ment takes it down from the mystical register and makes it practicable. It names the challenges — ego-centric thinking, feelings of separateness, structures that perpetuate division, and past trauma, prejudice, and fear. It names the cultivation — introspection, empathy, mindfulness, prayer, community service. And it names a part most accounts leave out: taking responsibility for one's own past transgressions, setting things right as far as one is able, and committing to do better. Union is not only inward.

It is load-bearing because it is the personal form of what the body is doing collectively. [[The Great ReMembering]] is union at the scale of a people; [[Higher Order Functional Unity]] is union at the scale of a federation; [[Reconciliation]] and [[Conflict Transformation]] are union as repair between two members of a Circle. The same movement at four sizes.

At a table it appears as the unglamorous work of mending: naming the tension, restoring the relationship rather than winning, and letting the person stay.

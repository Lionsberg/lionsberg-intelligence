# The Discernment Permission

*The standing invitation given to every reader: test everything, fill what is lacking, remove what does not belong, improve what is in error. Nobody here is above being corrected.*

A body of writing this large will be read as authority unless it says otherwise in its own voice. This is the saying otherwise — and it is a permission rather than a disclaimer, because it asks the reader to do something ([[The Foundational Truth]], [[Everything Is Current Best Understanding]], [[The Charge Of The Receiver]]).

It is not licence to vandalise. Three guards come with it. **Seek the Spirit first** — discernment that does not arise from contact with what this is oriented toward drifts toward refining one's own preferences and calling that improvement ([[Discernment]], [[The Divine Whisper]], [[Empirical Resonance]]). **Propose rather than overwrite** — changes to shared articulation go through a process where others can see them; the permission is to offer, and the offering is received ([[Proposal]], [[Second reader]], [[OmniSpection]]). **Honour the procession** — what stands was the best discernment of those who came before, and a refinement stands on their work even where it corrects them ([[Honoring Lineage Without Lineage-Capture]], [[Lineage]], [[Weave, do not delete]]).

Its real function is structural, not courteous. It is the mechanism by which a thing that claims to be unfinished actually stays unfinished: without a standing permission, the claim to be open is a sentence, and in practice nobody touches anything ([[The Codex Is Never Finished]], [[Every Defect Is A Gift]], [[Improvement log]]).

And it applies upward without exception. No editor, no founder, no keeper stands above the Pattern; the permission is precisely how the Pattern keeps correcting the people who write it down ([[The Pattern Governs, Not People]], [[A role held forever quietly becomes a throne]], [[Author's Voice Is Sovereign]]).

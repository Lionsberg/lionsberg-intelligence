# Ten Thousand Generations

*The long horizon on which the Game keeps its deeper score — roughly a quarter of a million years, held at the same moment as this week.*

Some traditions teach deciding on behalf of the seventh generation to come, or the fifty-second. Others speak of the ten thousand things as a shorthand for all. This horizon receives that teaching gratefully and extends it across the whole arc of interexistence in time. (The quarter-million-year figure is an estimate from generation length, not a measurement.)

At that scale the picture changes in a specific and useful way. Whole species and civilizations rise, flourish, fall, and rise again *inside* the horizon of play rather than at its edges — and cyclic cataclysm, the periodic unmaking that has erased worlds and memory before, comes within the field of consideration instead of being unthinkable ([[Collapse]] · [[The Five Collapse Scenarios]] · [[Cosmos Is Not Human-Centric]] · [[The Great ReMembering]]).

That is what makes it a design constraint rather than a sentiment. **What is built for ten thousand generations must be built to cross such things:** knowledge that survives its libraries, patterns that survive their institutions, species that survive their home planets — a Way that can be carried in a pocket, a story, a seed ([[The Golden Seed]] · [[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]] · [[Screens and paper]] · [[Structural Immunity]]). It is the reason this travels as plain words, living patterns and a flame handed on rather than as infrastructure: forms that no collapse can confiscate ([[Pass The Flame]] · [[The Flame]] · [[Transmission]]).

And it runs as a **two-speed clock**. The Game's clock keeps this week's turn and the ten thousand generations at once: every move small enough to make this week, and every move echoing across the whole arc. A player holds both — the elders teach it as a braid of three strands, those who came before, those here now, those yet to come, woven into one cord ([[The Turn]] · [[The Time Horizons]] · [[Generational Time]] · [[Covenant Across Time]] · [[Chronos]] · [[Kairos]]).

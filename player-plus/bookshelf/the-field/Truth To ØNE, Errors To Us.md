# Truth To ØNE, Errors To Us
*Anything true here belongs freely to [[ØNE]] and [[All]]. Any errors or mistakes belong to us.*

This is an asymmetry of attribution, and it is deliberate. [[Truth]] is not owned. When a pattern is articulated faithfully, the articulation belongs to the pattern itself, to ØNE, and to every sovereign who walks the same underlying reality. Many traditions name the same truth and none of their names exhausts it; these articulations are honoured to be one carrier among many, never proprietary. Errors run the other way: they are named as ours, corrected, and never disowned, never blamed on the pattern, the audience, or reality.

So when truth is recognised, it is named as belonging to the pattern rather than to this articulation of it, external traditions are credited as carriers of the same reality rather than as borrowed authority, and refinement is welcomed from any source — wisdom is everywhere. When error is recognised, the sentence is *this articulation fell short*, the surfacing is received as a gift, and the words are improved.

It is load-bearing as structural immunity against ego capture, which is the failure every movement before this one has met. Claiming truth as proprietary produces institutional self-importance. Refusing to acknowledge error produces defensive rigidity. Attributing the pattern's authority to one's own rendering of it produces source-elevation, which is how a servant becomes a priesthood. A body humble before the reality it serves cannot easily fall into any of the three, and the discipline is structural rather than merely attitudinal — it is a rule about attribution, checkable from outside.

In practice: authorship is acknowledged where it is right to acknowledge; authority for the pattern itself is never claimed; the work is signed as faithful service rather than proprietary product, and the signature names the servant, not the source.

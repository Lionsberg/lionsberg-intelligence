# Decision Making

*The Play a body cannot run without: how a group of sovereigns converts a question into a commitment — as close to the work as possible, by consent where consent is possible, with the reasons written down.*

Its ground is a claim about persons. Each individual carries a spark of the Divine and an inner guide, and is therefore both equipped and qualified to govern themselves and to take part in the self-governance of the bodies they belong to. Deciding is not a competence reserved for those above; it is a responsibility that comes with being present.

It is load-bearing in the most literal sense: a body that cannot decide cannot act, and a body that decides badly will act on its worst thinking with everyone's name on it. Most of what this Pillar contains is scaffolding around this one moment — who may decide ([[Authority]]), at what scale ([[Subsidiarity]]), by what test ([[Consent]]), against what objections ([[Meaningful Objection]]), recorded how ([[Decision Records]]), and revisable when ([[Possibility Of Reversal]]).

The canon's recommended starting point is discernment and consent around [[Proposal]]s, with three companions: ask upward for [[Guidance]] when a group genuinely cannot decide — the answer returns as one official answer among many, not as an order; change shared things by proposal rather than silent edit; and when process cannot produce a decision at all, let it sit finally with a small [[Stewardship body]] that stays out of as much as it can.

In play, a [[Cell]] decides most things in a minute at the weekly [[The Turn]]. The things worth slowing down for are the ones with a wide span of impact — and those get time, not procedure.

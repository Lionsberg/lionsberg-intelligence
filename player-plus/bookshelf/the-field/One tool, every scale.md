# One tool, every scale

*We do not build a personal version, a group version, a community version. We build each instrument once, so the same tool serves a soul, a Circle, a Community, and a world.*

It is a design rule, and it follows from a claim about reality rather than from a wish to save effort. If the same [[Pattern]] genuinely holds at every [[Levels Of Scale|level of scale]] — if a person, a [[Cell]], a [[Community]] and a planet are the same shape at different magnitudes — then an instrument that fits one of them and not the others has not found the pattern; it has found an accident of one size. A tool that must be rewritten for each scale is evidence that the thinking beneath it was local.

What the rule buys is worth naming, because the discipline it asks for is real.

**One thing to learn.** A person who has used an instrument alone already knows it when their Circle picks it up, and knows it again when a whole community does. The learning curve is paid once per instrument rather than once per scale, which is most of what makes a whole kit learnable at all.

**One thing to improve.** An improvement found by one soul at a kitchen table reaches every body using the instrument, at every size. Fork the tool by scale and the improvements scatter and stop compounding — the same failure [[Our Core Ways]] guards against inside a single body.

**Interoperability by construction.** Bodies at different scales that hold the same instrument can already read each other's work. Federation needs no translation layer it did not have to build.

The cost is that each instrument must be designed at the level of the pattern and not of the case — a template for agreements that works for two people and for ten thousand cannot carry anything true of only one of them. That is harder, slower, and it is the discipline the rule actually is.

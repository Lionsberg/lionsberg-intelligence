# Islands of Coherence

*Small, localized pockets of order and functioning within a larger system that is losing both — and the mechanism by which a deteriorating system can be shifted rather than merely mourned.*

The term is borrowed from work on system innovation and social-ecological transition, and it names something observable: when a large system becomes unstable, it stops being reliably governed from the top and becomes unusually sensitive to what small, coherent, functioning parts of it do. Those parts are the islands. They matter not because they are large but because they work, and because during instability a working example travels.

It is load-bearing because it is the strategy, stated in one image. The plan is not to reform the old order and not to fight it, but to build the new alongside it so that everyone has somewhere to go — hospice the old, demonstrate before advocating, let the Circles do real work and tell what happened. [[The First Three Percent]] is the same claim in numbers; [[The 300]] is it at community scale; [[Fibonacci Spread]] is how the islands multiply. A [[Cell]] that keeps its word, feeds its neighbours, and can point to something real at the end of a season *is* an island of coherence, whatever else it calls itself.

In play, the concept carries one instruction the image alone does not: islands must network. An island that stays alone is a curiosity and dies with its founders; islands that find one another become a grid. That is what [[Home]], [[Supercell]], and [[Islands, Hubs, Grid]] are for, and why [[Pass The Flame]] is the proof of maturity rather than an optional extra. The unit of the new world is not the exemplary community; it is the exemplary community that planted the next one.

The honest caution is that coherence is not the same as being right. A tightly functioning group can be coherently wrong, and the [[The Anti-Patterns]] are most dangerous inside groups that work well. [[The compass question]] and [[The Constitutional Recognitions]] are what keep an island of coherence from becoming a small, well-run cave.

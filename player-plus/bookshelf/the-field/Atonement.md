# Atonement

*Becoming at-one — the process of reuniting what was divided, through reconciliation, purification, and ascent.*

The word is read as the English compounds it: *at-one-ment*, the state of being at one with oneself, with others, and with the whole of Creation including its Source. It signifies the dissolving of artificial barriers and the recognition of a oneness that was always the case — which is why it belongs beside [[Remembrance]] rather than beside punishment.

What makes this usable is that it names the obstacles and the work. The obstacles: ego-centric thinking, felt separateness, social structures that perpetuate division, and past trauma, prejudice, and fear. The cultivation: introspection, self-awareness, empathy and compassion, and practices that promote unity — meditation, prayer, community service. And the part most accounts leave out: taking responsibility for one's own past transgressions, setting things right to the best of one's ability, and committing to do better. Atonement in this Field includes repair, done by the one who caused the harm.

It is load-bearing because it is the personal engine of a restorative system. [[Justice]] here is [[Right Relationship]] restored rather than retribution; [[Reconciliation]] is division healed through truth-telling and repair; [[Redemption]] is the arc. None of them work if the person who did the harm is expected only to feel something. Atonement is societal as well as personal here: it fosters a body in which each individual is recognised as an integral part of the whole.

At a table it is a move, not a mood: name what happened, make it right as far as you can, say what you will do differently — and then stay.

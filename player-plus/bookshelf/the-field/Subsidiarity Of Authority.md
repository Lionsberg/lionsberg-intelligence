# Subsidiarity Of Authority

*The second Recognition: decisions belong at the smallest scale that can make them well. Service flows downward; authority flows upward.*

The default sits at the bottom. A larger scale does not decide *instead of* a smaller one; it offers direction, and at times decisions, only where its wider abstraction and capability genuinely serve the whole. Everything else stays where the people who will live with the consequence are sitting.

Two failures press from either side. Capture upward: a decision drifts to a larger scale because the larger scale has more information, more money, or more confidence, and after a few seasons the smaller scale has stopped expecting to decide anything. Abdication downward: a decision that genuinely needs the wider view is pushed to a Circle that cannot see the whole, and the Body fragments into locally optimal, collectively incoherent moves. [[Design To The Goal]] and the discipline of optimizing for the Whole rather than the pieces are the counterweight on that side.

The word *well* carries the weight. The test is not which scale has the right to decide but which can decide well — and that changes with the question, the season, and the competence actually present. A Body practising this reviews it rather than fixing it, which is why [[Possibility Of Reversal]] sits beside it.

Structurally, subsidiarity is one of the main immunities against [[Centralization Of Power]]: a centre that never acquires decisions cannot become one. In practice it is how a [[Cell]] stays sovereign over its own work while a [[Supercell]] holds what genuinely crosses Cells, and how a small [[Stewardship body]] can exist without becoming a government — it stays out of as much as it can, decides when it must, and pushes decisions back out as far as they can go and no farther.

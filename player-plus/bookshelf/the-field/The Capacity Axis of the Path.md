# The Capacity Axis of the Path

*Every stage of the Path has two readings — what happens, and what it builds. The event is the marker; the capacity is the substance. A stage passed without its capacity has not been passed.*

[[The 12 Stages of the Sovereign's Path]] name events: discovery, threshold, orientation, gathering, the first meeting, the weekly practice, the encounter with the Pattern, the long initiation, convergence, renewal, the ascent, the play without end. Alongside each sits the thing it is for.

Discovery builds **recognition** — the felt knowing that this is real and is mine, which no argument can substitute for. Threshold builds **conscious choice**, the one decision that puts countless later ones in line ([[The Decision]]). Orientation builds **frame**: where we are, why now, what is at stake ([[Our Story]]). Gathering builds **identification** — the eye for who among one's own people is ready ([[The First Few]]). The first meeting builds the **Circle**, the smallest unit that is not one person ([[Form a Cell]]). Weekly practice builds **rhythm**, the only thing that carries a body through a season no one enjoys ([[The Heartbeat]], [[The Practice Cadence]]). The encounter with the Pattern builds **operational discernment** — meeting patterns as live answers rather than as theory ([[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language]]). The long initiation builds **foundational competence** across body, heart, mind, spirit, voice, scale, story and service ([[The 144 Gates of Initiation]]). Convergence builds the capacity to hold **several scales at once** ([[Federation]], [[Levels Of Scale]]). Renewal is **full participation** — reached, on the corpus's own account, only when the last cohort has crossed ([[Jubilee]], [[None Of Us Until All Of Us]]). The ascent builds **deeper mastery** ([[The Sacred Ascent]], [[The dans, and Mastery at 5th Dan]]). And the last stage is not a stage but a manner of living ([[The Eternal Divine Play]]).

What the axis is for is diagnosis without ranking. When a Cell stalls, the useful question is not which stage it has reached but which capacity is missing — because the missing capacity is usually two stages back and was skipped by attending the event ([[The gap]], [[Gaps Not Yet Closed]], [[Capacity Development Architecture]]).

# Ideological Capture

*The fifth disease: the idea eating the purpose. What was an articulation of the Way becomes the thing being defended, and the Way is measured against it rather than the reverse.*

Every articulation is a rendering: our current best words for something that exceeds them. Capture is the moment the rendering stops being held as a rendering. The signs are recognizable — evidence is read for confirmation rather than correction; the question *is this still our best understanding?* stops being asked; disagreement is felt as disloyalty rather than as information; and the Body begins to defend a formulation it can no longer remember choosing.

An ideology can be entirely correct and still capture. Truth of content is not the protection; the protection is *how it is held*. [[Current Best Understanding]] is the posture that keeps an articulation revisable: the *why* is invariant, every *how* is revisable, and nothing is called final. [[Shoshin]] is the same discipline in a person — the moment a being believes they know the Way, the possibility of knowing the Way is removed in that instant.

[[Spirit Precedes Pattern]] is the escape hatch built into the foundation: where the Spirit is right the wise right pattern emerges, and where the Spirit is absent no pattern saves. Use your discernment; test what is offered; fill what is lacking; remove what does not belong. A Way that says that of itself has made capture harder than a Way that merely warns against it.

Structurally the refusal is rhythm: deliberate revision, retrospection at the turning, proposals that gather and land by [[The edition]], and the plain statement that the Codex is never finished. The nearest kin of this disease is [[Institutional Idolatry]] — the same capture, with a body instead of an idea at the centre.

# The Seven Steps of Transformation

*A structured route from seeing to changing, usable at any scale — a person, a group, a community, a whole system.*

**1 · Story.** Begin with the story, because nothing is assessed or planned outside one. Where are we, how did we get here, and what is this a chapter of ([[Story]] · [[Our Story]] · [[Narrative Lens]])?

**2 · Vision and Design.** Say what the good version looks like, and design toward it, before examining what currently is — so that the present does not silently set the ceiling ([[Vision]] · [[Design]] · [[Design To The Goal]] · [[Worthy Goal]]).

**3 · Context and Analysis.** Now look hard at the actual situation: the conditions, the constraints, the forces, what is really in the way ([[Root Causes]] · [[Constraint]] · [[Sensemaking]] · [[The gap]]).

**4 · Engagement.** Bring in the people whose transformation it is. Placed before strategy on purpose — a plan made for people rather than with them will be correct and will not move ([[Dialogos]] · [[The Invitation]] · [[Requests and offers]] · [[Consent]]).

**5 · Strategy and Planning.** Choose the route and sequence the work: what first, what depends on what, what is deliberately not being done ([[Prioritizing]] · [[Critical Path]] · [[Pull planning]] · [[The plan of the whole]]).

**6 · Action.** Do it. Small enough to start this week, in the open, with commitments that can be kept or honestly broken ([[Do the Work]] · [[Commitment]] · [[Learning By Doing]] · [[Prototype]]).

**7 · Measurement and Continuous Improvement.** See what actually happened against what was intended, and feed it back — which returns the whole thing to step one with a changed story ([[Monitoring, Evaluation, and Continuous Improvement]] · [[Retrospective]] · [[Continuous Improvement]] · [[The Record]]).

The order carries the teaching. Story before vision, vision before analysis, engagement before strategy, and measurement folding back into story — and it is a spiral rather than a line, run again each season at a higher turn ([[Attend, Plan, Act, Learn, Improve Cycle]] · [[The Turn]] · [[Season]] · [[OmniSpection]]).

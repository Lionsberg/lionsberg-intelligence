# Resource the Work

*Name the need concretely; invite contribution freely; record what flows gratefully.*

Some work needs more than the people at the table already have — more time, more attention, more energy, or money. The move is three short disciplines, and each of the three adverbs is doing real work.

**Concretely.** A need stated in general — *we could use support* — cannot be met, because nobody knows what meeting it would look like. A need stated exactly — this much, for this, by this date, and here is what happens without it — can be met, partially met, or honestly declined, and all three outcomes move the work. Concreteness is also the safeguard against the slow drift by which a body starts raising resource in general rather than for something ([[Capital Subordinate To Purpose]]).

**Freely.** The invitation is an invitation. It does not lean on obligation, guilt, or the relationship it is spoken through, and it leaves a no fully available, because a contribution given under pressure buys the resource at the price of the trust ([[Consent]], [[Sovereignty]], [[Gift]]). What makes free invitation work at scale is not persuasion but visibility — the need placed where those who might answer are already looking ([[The Board]], [[Ask for Help]]).

**Gratefully.** What came, from whom, toward what, and what it did. The record is the discipline that keeps resourcing honest in both directions: it lets a contributor see their gift land, it lets the body see what it is actually running on, and it makes the next ask credible. A body that receives without recording will be asked, sooner or later, where it all went, and will not be able to say ([[Gratitude]], [[Proof-of-contribution]], [[The Record]]).

Resource is not only money, and treating it as money is the commonest first mistake. Time, attention and energy are the scarcer inputs in most first seasons ([[TimeEnergy]]), and a great deal of what a body needs already exists somewhere in the [[Commons]] or in a neighbouring Cell, available for the asking rather than the buying ([[Mutual Aid]], [[Fostering Ecosystemic Resource Flows]]).

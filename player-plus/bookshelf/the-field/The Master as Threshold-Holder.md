# The Master as Threshold-Holder

*Mastery is not a graduation but an office: the Master becomes the one through whom the next initiates pass.*

Gate seventy of seventy-two, and it is the gate that closes the loop. Everything before it is cultivation — a person refining an instrument over a lifetime. At this threshold the direction reverses: the one who was carried across becomes the one who holds the crossing for others.

Without it, [[Mastery]] has no office. A recognition that terminates in the person recognized is an award, and an award at the end of a path is exactly the shape the corpus otherwise refuses everywhere — in belts that are witnessed rather than granted, in a [[The Loop That Has No End|loop that has no end]], in a [[Carrier]] for every new cell. This gate is what makes the mastery architecture obey its own rule: the fruit of the tree carries the seed, and a Master who passes nothing on has not finished the Way but stopped inside it.

Threshold-holding is not teaching, and the distinction matters. A teacher transmits content; a threshold-holder holds a passage — is present at the moment someone crosses, can tell whether the crossing is real, and can bear the weight of the one crossing if it is not. That capacity is what the seventy-two gates were building the instrument for.

Two guards travel with it, both stated in the corpus. **The role is conferred by the body, not claimed.** Whoever declares themselves a teacher on the strength of having crossed is described as slipping; the ones who hold up are the ones the role found after years of quiet practice. And **it begins long before Mastery** — the threshold-holding that opens in earnest at Gate seventy starts in miniature at the close of the foundational walk, where a person becomes an elder to someone, somewhere, before feeling ready to be one.

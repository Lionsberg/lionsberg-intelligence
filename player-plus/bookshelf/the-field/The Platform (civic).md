# The Platform (civic)

*The shared digital backbone through which the Movement becomes visible to itself — and which is never required.*

Its work is threefold. **Visibility**: people, Cells, and Quests made visible to one another, so that anyone can find their kin, every Cell can find its neighbours, and the whole body can see how it is doing ([[Cell]], [[Quest]], [[The Movement]]). **Provision**: equal access, for everyone who accepts the invitation, to the path of lifelong learning and development and to the tools, solutions, and support of the commons — delivered just in time, in answer to the Quest actually at hand ([[Commons]], [[Just in time]], [[The Invitation]]). **Flow**: the connective tissue for offers and requests, resources and stories, promises made and kept, moving between the parts without passing through any controlling centre ([[Requests and offers]], [[Fostering Ecosystemic Resource Flows]]).

Its laws are the laws of everything in the new. **Interoperable** — each functional part replaceable as technology changes, because the data architecture and the human culture are the constants and the software is not ([[Interoperability]], [[Technology Sovereignty]]). **Decentralised** — no master store; the whole thing downloadable and regrowable by any Cell ([[The open web]], [[Nucleus]], [[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]]). And **never required** — it makes the Way faster and wiser, and the Game is played complete with paper, voice, and sky. If the grid goes down, the Movement does not ([[Screens and paper]], [[Notebook form]]).

That last law is what distinguishes this from every platform the word usually names. The ordinary platform's business is to become necessary: to hold the data, the identity, the relationships, and the attention, so that leaving is expensive. This one is built to be leavable, which is also what makes it trustworthy ([[Manufactured Dependency]], [[Hosted service]], [[Data]], [[Off switch]]).

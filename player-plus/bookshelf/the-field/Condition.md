# Condition

*How an Actor is right now, said in one word and dated — and a reading that has gone stale expires to* unknown*, never quietly to* ready*.*

**Ready** — free and able to take work. **Engaged** — carrying what was committed; not free. **At capacity** — full to its own ceiling. **Degraded** — below its own standard, with a word about why. **Resting** — out by its own choice or its own season. **Out** — unreachable. **Unknown** — the line expired.

Condition answers exactly one question: *can this Actor be asked now?* It is not whether they have kept their word, and not what would satisfy the work they hold, and not the slow arc of a life or a season. It is a check-in rather than a chapter: fast, cold, and honest ([[Commitment]], [[Conditions of satisfaction]]).

The expiry rule is the whole of its integrity. A readiness that quietly persists after nobody has confirmed it is worse than no readiness at all, because work is routed on it and then vanishes. Expiring to *unknown* means the body routes around silence without assuming anything about it — neither that the person is fine nor that something is wrong ([[Honesty about time]], [[The clock is read, never computed]]).

*Degraded* deserves its own sentence. It narrows what may be sent; it never bars. A body where saying *I am below my standard this week* removes you from the work teaches everyone to say *ready* instead, and then nobody's word means anything. Saying it plainly, and being handed something smaller, is what the word is for ([[Zero Blame Environment]], [[Load]], [[Rest And Regeneration]]).

Readiness belongs to standing, not to being human: a person, a Cell, an agent, or a body of Cells each holds one, and each states its own ([[Actor]], [[Position]]).

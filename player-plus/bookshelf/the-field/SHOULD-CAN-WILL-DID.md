# SHOULD-CAN-WILL-DID

*The states a piece of work passes through: it should be done now, it can be done because nothing blocks it, someone says they will, and then it was — or was not, and the gap is what teaches.*

**Should** is what the plan says: work on a chain to a [[Milestone]], pulled backward from the end. **Can** is should plus constraint-free: every predecessor complete, every open condition closed, which is what [[The look-ahead]] and [[Make-Ready Process]] make true. **Will** is a [[Commitment]] — a person's word, spoken at the table to someone who depends on it. **Did** is what happened, written in [[The Record]].

Before *should* the cycle widens by one: **could** — the pool of everything a living body might do that is on no chain to any marker. It is real, it is kept, it is not required now, and naming it as a place rather than a failure is what keeps the possible from crowding the week. So the stages are five, and only two of them are anyone's word: the first three are computed from the plan, the last two are spoken by the one who does the work.

It is load-bearing because it is the one path by which an intention becomes a result without anyone being commanded. Each arrow is a different body's move: the plan draws could into should, an owner clears constraints to make should into can, the doer turns can into will at [[The Heartbeat]], and the record turns will into did. Skip a stage and the failure is predictable — promising what is not ready, or doing what nothing needed.

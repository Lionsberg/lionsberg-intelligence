# Mend a Tension

*What a Cell does when friction rises between its members: slow down, return to purpose, find the unmet need, agree the smallest adjustment that reconciles.*

Tension is not failure. Avoidance is. A body of people doing real work together will rub, and the question is only whether the rubbing is attended to while it is small. The move begins by slowing the pace and returning to what the body is for and what it agreed ([[One Purpose Above All]], [[Field of Agreements]]).

Then the craft: speak from your own experience rather than about the other's character; listen without rehearsing your reply; look underneath the position for the need that is not being met; and agree the smallest change that actually reconciles, rather than the largest that proves a point ([[Listening]], [[Speaking Truth In Love]], [[Having Difficult Conversations]], [[Forgiveness]]).

The aim is restoration of relationship, not a verdict. That is what distinguishes this from the old world's machinery of grievance: the outcome sought is right relationship, and repair is available to everyone, including whoever caused the harm ([[Right Relationship]], [[Conflict Transformation]], [[Justice]], [[Apologizing]]).

When a tension resists mending for more than a few days, it goes to an elder for counsel — discernment held by someone who has walked further, not authority to impose a settlement — and only then up the ladder ([[Wise Eldership]], [[The Escalation Ladder]], [[Zero Blame Environment]]).

# The Dashboard of Civilization

*A civilisation read as a vessel with gauges — financial, social, ecological, technological, geopolitical, spiritual — each green, amber or red. The instrument urgency is read **from**, rather than argued into.*

A dashboard is a discipline before it is a display. It fixes in advance **which** systems are being watched, so that attention cannot drift to whatever is loudest this week; it states the reading for each; and it puts them side by side, where the pattern across them can be seen ([[Green, yellow, red]], [[Navigation]], [[Measurement]], [[The Score]]).

Green means within normal parameters. Amber means under stress and needing correction. Red means critical, needing intervention now. The vocabulary is borrowed from bodies that actually have to act on instruments — cockpits, control rooms, wards — and it is borrowed because it forces a reading rather than an impression ([[Condition]], [[Andon (the clocked pull)]], [[Monitoring, Evaluation, and Continuous Improvement]]).

The argument the instrument makes is **simultaneity**, and it cannot be made any other way. Any single gauge reading red is a serious problem with a known repertoire of responses. Every gauge reading red at once is a different kind of situation, because each correction now competes with the others for the same attention, money and time ([[Systemic Failure Modes]], [[Multi-Polar Traps]], [[Probability, Severity, and Scope]], [[The Three Converging Cycles]]).

It also changes the shape of disagreement, which is its quiet gift. Someone who thinks the reading is wrong argues about a gauge rather than about a worldview — and that is an argument that can be had, and settled, and repeated next season ([[Navigating Towards Truth]], [[The Record as the Movement's variance gauge]], [[Second reader]]).

And it scales down without alteration. A Cell, a household, a locality can keep the same six faces and read them each season — which is the version most readers can actually act on ([[One tool, every scale]], [[The Circle's Scorecard]], [[The Season Sheet]], [[Retrospective]]).

The source's own readings are its judgments from 2026, supported by cited and uncited figures; the readings are not carried here, and a body running the instrument should produce its own.

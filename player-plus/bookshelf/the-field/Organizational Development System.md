# Organizational Development System

*The system by which a body grows itself — as distinct from the system by which it does its work.*

Most bodies have a way of delivering and no way of developing. They get better at what they already do and never at being what they are; when they need to change shape, they reorganise in a crisis, from the top, by rearrangement. An organizational development system is the alternative: a standing, ordinary practice for growing the body's own capability, structure and culture, run at the same rhythm as everything else.

It works on four things rather than one. **People** — what each person is growing into, and at what stretch ([[The Zone Of Optimum Development]] · [[Apprenticeship]] · [[Belt Progression]] · [[Cohort-Learning]] · [[Mastery]]). **Roles** — what work exists, who holds it, how it is handed on and how it ends ([[Roles]] · [[Size of role, size of person]] · [[Term Limits And Rotation]] · [[Delegation]]). **Structure** — how the body is arranged, and the fact that the arrangement is itself a draft ([[Organizations The LIØNSBERG Way]] · [[Stewardship body]] · [[Phase-Gated Architecture]]). **Culture** — the way of being that actually governs conduct when no rule applies ([[Culture]] · [[Embodying the Way]] · [[Field of Agreements]]).

What makes it a *system* rather than good intentions is that it runs on its own loop. Something is noticed, a change is proposed, tried small, assessed against what was expected, and either adopted as the new normal or revised ([[Attend, Plan, Act, Learn, Improve Cycle]] · [[Continuous Improvement]] · [[Proposal]] · [[Retrospective]] · [[OmniSpection]]). Its subject is simply the body itself rather than the body's output.

And it is the mechanism behind replication. A Cell that can develop itself can form and grow another; one that cannot must be built each time by whoever built the first ([[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]] · [[Grow Your Cells]] · [[Midwife a New Circle]] · [[Guide Cells]] · [[Pass The Flame]]).

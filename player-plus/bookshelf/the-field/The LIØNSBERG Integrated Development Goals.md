# The LIØNSBERG Integrated Development Goals

*A goal-set designed to be universal, holistic and adaptive — with indicators attached — rather than a fixed agenda agreed once and outlived.*

Four design properties are claimed, and they are the content, since the goals themselves are nowhere enumerated. **Universal** — applying to everyone, regardless of where or who ([[Universal Rights, and Universal Aspirations]], [[None Of Us Until All Of Us]]). **Holistic** — covering total integrated wellbeing rather than one measurable slice of it ([[Wellness]], [[Omniconsideration]], [[The six capitals]]). **Interdependent** — read together, since progress in one bought at another's cost is not progress ([[Silos No More]], [[Cross-Functional Integration]]). And **adaptive** — expected to iterate across decades and generations rather than to be fixed and then missed ([[Everything Is Current Best Understanding]], [[Continuous Improvement]], [[Generational Time]]).

What the framework is for is **measurement without a centre**. Define the aim clearly enough and value and waste become distinguishable, so that sovereign people and bodies can align their own attention and effort without being directed — which is the only way a goal-set works in an arrangement with nobody in charge ([[Throughput]], [[Waste]], [[Design To The Goal]], [[Integrated Delivery]], [[Subsidiarity]]).

The accountability it proposes is citizen-led rather than institutional: oversight held by the people the goals concern, reporting in the open, review on a rhythm, and adjustment when the reading says so ([[Oversight]], [[Transparency]], [[The Record as the Movement's variance gauge]], [[Monitoring, Evaluation, and Continuous Improvement]]).

And the last move is the one most such frameworks never make: make it playable. If the relation between what a household spends its time on and what results can be modelled and shown, then a child can see it — and a goal-set that only a ministry can read is not a goal-set for everyone ([[The Great Game]], [[The Board]], [[The grandmother test]], [[Conditions of Acceptance]]).

**The goals themselves are not enumerated anywhere.** What exists is the design of a framework and the argument for it; the set it would contain has not been written, and no indicator has been specified ([[Gaps Not Yet Closed]], [[Work In Progress]], [[We Do Not Know]]).

# The Meta Project

*The whole of the work required to reach the Goal — the one project that contains and orders every other.*

Its logic is short. Facing what we face, we have [[Work]] to do. The total nested holarchy of work required to accomplish the total nested holarchy of goals is the Meta Project. Work at that scale demands a system; a system demands a goal; the Meta Project therefore demands a [[Meta System]]. Everything anyone is doing that serves the Goal is somewhere inside it, whether or not anyone has said so.

It is load-bearing because it is what makes local work addable. Without one containing project, a million good efforts stay a million good efforts; with it, each has a place, a neighbour and something above it that it serves. The first level of its breakdown is the twelve Pillars; beneath them the trades and the regions; beneath those the Quests of Cells. That is the ladder by which a season at one table becomes throughput at the scale of a world — and the reason [[Everything Is A Project]] is a convention rather than a slogan.

It is not a plan held by anyone. No centre writes it; it is populated from the bottom by what bodies actually undertake, and guided from the top only by a shared Vision, strategy and values. Worksite Earth is its site, and the work is not a metaphor: air, water, soil, and everything else that must be true for life to flourish here.

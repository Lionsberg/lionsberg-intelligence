# The Great ReLearning · ReBuilding · ReFounding

*Three of the movements after the crossing — learning again, building again, founding again — standing beside the ReMembering in the Story's own vocabulary.*

The prefix carries the claim. Nothing here is invention from nothing. What the crossing recovers is older than the thing that replaced it, so each movement is a return performed forward.

**The Great ReLearning.** What the old order taught has to be unlearned before anything can be taught, and what a people actually needs — how to grow food, settle a dispute, raise a child, keep a record, repair a thing — has to be learned again from people who still know ([[Unlearning]], [[Learning By Doing]], [[Apprenticeship]]). It comes first because the other two are impossible without it: a people that rebuilds before it relearns rebuilds the thing it left.

**The Great ReBuilding.** The actual making — of places, instruments, livelihoods, infrastructure. The canon's discipline here is that the building is done to a pattern that was recovered rather than to whatever is fastest ([[Regeneration]], [[The Hub]], [[Architecture]], [[Prototype]]).

**The Great ReFounding.** The deepest of the three and the last: the constituting of a body on a new foundation — the agreements, the covenant, the law it lives under, the thing it will not trade ([[The New Covenant]], [[Covenant]], [[The LIØNSBERG Constitution]], [[Field of Agreements]]). Founding, not administration: what is settled here is what the body *is*.

They are not sequential in practice. A Cell relearns, rebuilds, and refounds at once and at its own scale; the movements name emphases in an arc rather than phases with dates ([[The Turn]], [[Levels Of Scale]]).

Their fourth is [[The Great ReMembering]] — the recovery of who we were and who we are — and this page exists because the canon carries that one and leaves these three as names.

# Aligned Will

*The Sovereign wants what [[ØNE]] wants for this moment. Will held in [[Right Relationship]] — the Sovereign's own free choice, and faithful to an intention deeper than their ego is the source of.*

Will is the force by which a being moves [[Reality]]. Alignment is not its suppression. The Pattern is explicit that what results is simultaneously the Sovereign's free choice *and* [[ØNE]]'s will expressed through that choice — which is what [[Co-Creation]] actually means, as against both obedience and self-assertion.

The alignment is felt in the body rather than concluded. When it is partial, what arises through the Sovereign is partial; when it is full, what arises is faithful to the [[Logos]]. This is why the test is applied before acting, creating, deciding, intervening, refusing, or initiating: *is what I am about to do aligned with what [[ØNE]] is asking of this moment, or is it my ego asserting itself against Reality?*

Two failures mirror each other, and both are common in serious people. Ego-will mistaken for aligned will — proceeding with what serves a personal agenda while claiming an alignment not actually reached, which is the more dangerous because sincerity is no protection against it. And refusal of aligned will because it does not match preference — declining what is being asked because the conditioned self does not want to do it.

Aligned will is what makes [[Spontaneous Wise Right Action]] trustworthy rather than merely fast: action arriving faster than deliberation is only as good as the alignment it arrives through. It is what authorizes what flows through the conduit, and it is held in place afterward by [[The Act Of Release]], which prevents the one it moved through from beginning to claim it.

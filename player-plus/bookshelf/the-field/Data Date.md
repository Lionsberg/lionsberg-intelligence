# Data Date

*The moment a plan is true as of. Before it, the Record; after it, the calculation.*

A plan is a claim about the future made from a known present, and the Data Date names that present. Everything before it is what actually happened — real starts, real finishes, promises kept and broken. Everything after it is computed from the logic that remains: work already done falls out, work not yet started may begin no earlier than this morning, and every date, every [[Float]] and the whole [[Critical Path]] are worked forward from here.

It is load-bearing because it is the difference between a schedule and a wish. Move the Data Date forward and the plan moves honestly with it — the same logic, measured from a later morning, showing exactly what the delay cost. A plan with no stated Data Date cannot do this, and so tends to be quietly reinterpreted rather than recalculated: dates slide, nobody can say when, and the body loses the one signal it most needs ([[Honesty about time]], [[Measure everything, manage to the milestones]]).

The discipline the word carries is one sentence: **a plan states its Data Date on every view that was calculated**, so that what anyone reads is today's truth rather than a picture from a month ago that still looks current. The natural moment to advance it is the weekly [[The Heartbeat|Heartbeat]], where what was done is recorded and the remaining logic recalculated in the same motion ([[The Turn]]).

Its paper floor is the date written at the top of the sheet the day the sheet was worked. That is the entire instrument, and it is why this is a discipline a Cell with a notebook can keep perfectly ([[Screens and paper]], [[The clock is read, never computed]]).

# Mission

*What we actually do to realize the Vision, the Intention and the Goal — the doing layer, sitting beneath the seeing layer and above the week's work.*

A [[Vision]] is what it would look like. An [[Intention]] is what is willed. A Mission is what is being *done* about it — the standing work a body has taken on, stated so that a stranger could tell whether it was being done or not. The distinction earns its keep because bodies constantly mistake one for another: a beautiful vision with no mission is a mood, and a busy mission with no vision is motion without direction ([[The Intent]] · [[Purpose]] · [[Alignment]]).

It sits in the middle of a cascade and takes its authority from above it. The Goal does not change; a Mission is one current best answer to *what shall we do about it*, and it is expected to be revised as understanding improves ([[The Goal]] · [[The Creative Cascade]] · [[Current Best Understanding]] · [[Everything Is Current Best Understanding]]). Below it, the Mission breaks into the things that can actually be worked: a [[Quest]] this season, [[Milestone|milestones]], commitments in a week ([[Every Quest Is A Project]] · [[Stacking Up And Aligning]] · [[The Turn]]).

Every scale has one. A person has a mission; a [[Cell]] has one — it is the fourth of the five functions that make a Cell complete rather than merely gathered; a federation has one. They are meant to nest, each serving the one above without being dictated by it ([[Holarchy]] · [[Subsidiarity]] · [[Sovereignty At Every Scale]]).

Two disciplines keep the word honest. **A mission is written down**, because an unwritten one drifts into whatever the body happens to be doing anyway ([[Declare]] · [[Charter]] · [[Drift]]). And **a mission is testable** — if no observation could show it was not being carried out, it is a slogan ([[Conditions of satisfaction]] · [[Measurement]] · [[The Record]]).

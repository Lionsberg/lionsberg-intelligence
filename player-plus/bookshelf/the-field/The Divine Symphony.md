# The Divine Symphony
*The whole in motion — each being playing a unique and essential part, and the beauty being in the harmony rather than in any one line.*

The image is old and it carries a precise idea. In a symphony no part is redundant and no part is the whole; the second oboe is not a lesser violin. Depth, nuance, and beauty come from difference held in relationship, and from each player being fully themselves in time with the others. That is what a rightly related world sounds like from above.

The practices the image implies are the ones a body can actually run: cultivate awareness of the interconnectedness that weaves unique expressions together, so that people recognise their own part as essential; encourage the expression of individual and collective gifts in harmony with the fundamental tone rather than against it; build a culture of collaboration and mutual appreciation; cultivate inner harmony, because a player out of tune with themselves cannot be in tune with anyone; practise deep listening and [[Attunement]]; and keep learning, because the piece is still being composed and parts shift as the world changes.

It is load-bearing as the cohering image of the whole. [[Higher Order Functional Unity]] is the systems word for it, [[Holofractal Omnifederation]] is the structural form, [[Meta Unity of Meta Diversity]] is the governance phrase — but a symphony is what all three feel like when they are working, and the image travels where the terms do not.

Its discipline is the refusal of two easy failures: everyone playing the same note, which is uniformity mistaken for unity, and everyone playing whatever they like, which is noise mistaken for freedom. Harmony is neither.

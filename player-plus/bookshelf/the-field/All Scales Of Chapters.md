# All Scales Of Chapters

*One Story, breathed at every size — cosmos, world, bioregion, town, Circle, and a single life, each a chapter of the same telling.*

The Story is not one narrative with smaller illustrations attached. It is the same shape running at every level of scale, so that a person's life is not an example of the Story but a chapter of it, and a Circle's season is not a footnote but a chapter too. Cosmic, planetary, bioregional, local, Circle, individual: the same movement, told at different breath ([[HoloFractal]], [[Levels Of Scale]], [[Holarchy]]).

What makes this more than a pleasing image is the practice attached. A chapter becomes a chapter only when it is *consciously articulated* — said out loud, written down, told to someone. Until then it is only events. [[Share the Stories]] is the motion by which a life becomes a chapter, chapters compose into the Story, and the Story becomes something living rather than something recited ([[Storytelling]], [[Our Story]], [[Your Story Woven Into Meta Story]]).

The consequence for anyone holding a pen: the spine is what has already been set down, and the weaving is what those who come add. The book is not finished and is not going to be. The closer a person walks, the more they become its author rather than its reader ([[Living Word]], [[Co-Creation]], [[Every Story Is A Prophesy]]).

The consequence for anyone feeling small: there is no scale at which participation is merely symbolic. A household's chapter is as real as a planet's, because the same Story is what both are telling. This is also what makes [[Pass The Flame]] a handing of a chapter and not a handing of a doctrine.

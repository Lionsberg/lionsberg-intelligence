# Periods of Action

*Time sliced into roughly right-sized pieces — about fifteen minutes — inside which a person does the next wise right thing completely and then ships it.*

The observation underneath is that available time can be cut into useful, approximately right-sized blocks, and that a great deal follows from choosing the size well. About fifteen minutes is short enough that full attention is actually possible and long enough that something real gets finished. A day holds about ninety-six of them ([[Time]] · [[Meaningful Work]] · [[Minutes are the one unit]] · [[Day shape]]).

The claim made for it is large and openly a suspicion rather than a finding: that a person who learns to focus intensely on advancing the single next wise right thing in such a block, and then **ships the work**, gains dramatically in capacity. The shipping is half of it — a block that ends with something handed on is a different act from a block that ends with something still open ([[Wise Right]] · [[Do the Work]] · [[Flow]] · [[Commitment]] · [[Conditions of satisfaction]]).

It is described as a kind of multiplexing: one channel carrying many streams by giving each its whole attention for a short slice, rather than giving all of them partial attention at once. That is how a person, as a system inside a larger system, can let many different goals flow through them without any of them getting half a mind ([[Focused Attention As Discipline]] · [[Total Nested Holarchy of Goals · Needs · Issues · Values · Ideals · Loves]] · [[Throughput]] · [[Prioritizing]]).

And there is a second effect, which is not about productivity at all. The practice draws a person **out of abstract and vague worry and into near presence** — acting wisely on the next thing within reach. With skill the periods can narrow further, toward presence in the now ([[Silence And Stillness As The Ground]] · [[Attunement]] · [[Kairos]] · [[A Day Well Lived]]).

Economically it is the unit the rest is denominated in: a validated period of meaningful work is the base unit of [[Time Currency]], which is why the size of the block is not an arbitrary preference ([[TimeEnergy]] · [[Time-Energy Accounting]] · [[LUV]]).

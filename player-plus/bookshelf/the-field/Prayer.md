# Prayer

*Realigning one's own being, will and intention with the Being, Will and Intention of That Which is creating and sustaining us.*

The canon's definition puts the whole weight on one word: **realign**. Prayer is not primarily asking, and it is not primarily speaking. It is the movement by which a small will is brought back into line with the larger one it came from — the correction of a course rather than the petitioning of an official. Understood that way it survives translation across traditions, and it is available to someone who would not use the word *God* at all: the act is orientation, and orientation is something every being does whether or not it names what it is orienting toward.

Because it is realignment, it is measured by what it changes rather than by how it felt. A prayer that leaves a person's will exactly where it was has not happened yet. This is also why it is placed among the disciplines rather than among the beliefs: it is practised, it can be done badly, and it improves ([[Discipline]] · [[Focused Attention As Discipline]]).

Most of its work is subtraction. The noise that has to go quiet first is the noise of one's own demands, and the traditions agree that the listening half is the larger half ([[Silence And Stillness As The Ground]] · [[Listening]] · [[The Divine Whisper]]). What follows — gratitude, confession, asking, intercession, offering, stillness — are the ordinary forms it takes, and different temperaments need different ones. Held with [[Fasting]], [[Sabbath]], [[Breath]] and [[Remembrance]], it makes a rhythm rather than an event.

It is done alone and it is done together, and the two are not the same act. Alone, a person finds out what their will actually is. Together, a body discovers whether it is one body — which is why a room that prays or sits in silence before deciding tends to decide differently from a room that does not ([[Attunement]] · [[Resonance]] · [[Council]]).

The honest limit belongs on the page. Nothing here claims to know the mechanism, and the canon does not either. What is claimed is the practice and its fruit: a life that keeps returning to alignment drifts less ([[Drift]] · [[Alignment]] · [[We Do Not Know]]).

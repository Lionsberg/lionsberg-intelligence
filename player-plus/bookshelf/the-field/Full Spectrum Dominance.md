# Full Spectrum Dominance

*The end-state of the domination logic: control sought across every domain at once — physical, informational, economic, biological, and cognitive.*

The term is taken from military doctrine, where it means superiority across every operational dimension simultaneously, and it is used here for what [[Kontrolle]] assembles when it is no longer constrained. Not dominance in one sphere while others remain free, but the closing of every dimension in which an alternative could form: the money, the story, the food, the body, the network, the attention, and finally the capacity to think a different thought. Its companion name in the corpus, universal assured destruction, marks the obvious end of that logic when two such systems meet.

It is load-bearing as the specification of what must not be built, and therefore as a design constraint on the new. A civilisation that concentrates all the same capacities in better hands has not escaped; it has changed management. This is why [[Sovereignty At Every Scale]], [[Subsidiarity]], the [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]], and the [[Possibility Of Reversal]] are treated as structural rather than decorative. Each one preserves a dimension in which something else could still form.

It is stated as a trajectory that is being assembled and refused, not as an inevitability, and the refusal is described in terms the corpus can actually deliver: attention, energy, and consent withdrawn; alternatives built alongside; nothing centralised that does not have to be. Wherever it is spoken of, the same discipline applies as to every dark reading in this Story — name it precisely enough to recognise its early forms, and do not let it become the thing the Game is about.

In the Game the practical residue is small and checkable. Does any part of our life together have a single point of control? Could our Cell keep its Turn if one platform, one supplier, or one person disappeared? Does anyone have to ask permission to leave? Those questions are the everyday inverse of full spectrum dominance, and a Circle can answer them in ten minutes.

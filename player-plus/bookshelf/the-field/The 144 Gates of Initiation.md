# The 144 Gates of Initiation

*The Sovereign's walk from the Old World to the New: twelve movements of twelve thresholds, held at every scale at once.*

The twelve movements, by name: **Awakening In The Caves · The Great UnVeiling · The Cry Of The Heart And The Call To Exodus · Crossing The Boundary · The Wilderness · The Great ReLearning · The Great ReMembering · The Great ReBuilding · The Great ReFounding · The Greatest Celebration And Jubilee · Co-Creating Heaven · The Eternal Divine Play.** Each Gate is one threshold — an epiphany, a connection, or a scaffold that has to be in place before the whole can be grasped — and each is walked at every scale simultaneously: self, Circle, community, bioregion, Earth, cosmos.

The Gates and [[The Story of LIØNSBERG]] are two faces of one Pattern. The Story gives the cosmic narrative; the Gates give the embodied walking, and each refines the other through use. Each Sovereign's walking becomes a chapter of the Story written at their own scale, and [[Pass The Flame]] is the handing forward of that chapter.

They are load-bearing because they answer how understanding actually arrives. Not by exposition — a system of this size cannot be explained into someone — but by sequence: nothing is offered before the scaffold that makes it intelligible is in place. By the close, a Sovereign holds the whole across all scales and participates in it consciously, and a [[Belt Progression|belt]] is witnessed in recognition of that: a record shows what you can do, a witness shows that you did.

The pace is unbound from time. The weekly Heartbeat is a common pacing reference, but no Gate fires until ripe; some walk the 144 in years, others far longer. The arc is the architecture; the timing is sovereign. The individual Gate names are a first naming, explicitly provisional, and will refine as practitioners surface what each one actually carries.

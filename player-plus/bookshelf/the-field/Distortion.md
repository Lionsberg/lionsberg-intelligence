# Distortion

*Misalignment or falsification of pattern, purpose, or truth.*

Distortion is the first degree of failure in this Field's vocabulary of misalignment. [[Alignment]] is conformity of a system, being, or act with [[Spirit]] and [[Logos]]; Distortion is that conformity bent out of true; [[Inversion]] is the bend carried all the way over into its parasitic opposite. The three form a gradient, not three separate conditions — which matters, because distortion is catchable and inversion mostly is not.

It is load-bearing because most failures are of this kind. Not betrayal, but drift: a purpose subtly reinterpreted, a word worn hollow, a role that quietly stopped rotating, a measure that began as a servant and became the goal. The canon's instruments are aimed squarely at this range. [[Every Defect Is A Gift]] catches the small miss. [[Retrospective]] and [[OmniSpection]] catch the seasonal drift. [[Decision Records]] keep the *why* so a purpose cannot be reinterpreted by forgetting. [[Term Limits And Rotation]] and [[Transparency]] are structural immunity against the drifts that compound.

The canon's account of language is the sharpest case. In the Caves the most important words a people had were the first taken and worn hollow, until a word meant to be a bridge became a trigger — the chains are made of vocabulary before they are made of anything else. [[Semantic Capture]] is that distortion named; [[A Word on Words]] is the correction, and it is a practice rather than a warning: every word given once when you meet it, and when two of us find we mean different things, we fix the word, not each other.

In play, distortion is what a Cell is actually looking for when it takes its honest weekly reading.

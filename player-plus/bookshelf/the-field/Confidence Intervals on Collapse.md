# Confidence Intervals on Collapse

*State the uncertainty of your own estimate as part of the estimate. The band is what separates calibrated risk-reading from both doomism and denial.*

An estimate without a band is a claim pretending to be a measurement. Adding the band — *this, give or take that much* — says three things at once: what you think, how firmly, and what would count as your having been wrong ([[Measurement]], [[Hypothesize]], [[Honesty about time]]).

It is the discipline both failure modes lack, and that is the useful observation. **Doomism** asserts high certainty of catastrophe; **denial** asserts high certainty of continuity. Neither states a band, because a band would expose that the confidence is not coming from the evidence ([[Normalcy Bias and the Familiarity Trap]], [[Discernment]], [[We Do Not Know]]).

It also changes how the estimate ages. A number with a band can be **checked later**: reality either fell inside it or did not, and either way the estimator learns something about their own calibration. A bare number cannot be scored, which is why people who never state bands never find out that they are badly calibrated ([[Improvement log]], [[Retrospective]], [[Every Defect Is A Gift]], [[The Record as the Movement's variance gauge]]).

And it makes disagreement tractable. Two people with overlapping bands are not actually in conflict and can work together; two with disjoint bands know exactly what they disagree about and can go and look ([[Transforming Divergence]], [[Meaningful Objection]], [[Navigating Towards Truth]], [[Second reader]]).

Its own honest limit: a band is itself a judgment, and stating one can lend false precision to a guess. A wide band honestly held is worth more than a narrow one confidently asserted, and the instruction — *honest uncertainty is more useful than false precision* — is the right way round ([[Boundaries and Limits of Conception]], [[Limit Statement]], [[The Five Verification Filters and the Spectrum of Knowing]]).

The source works this at each horizon in a 2023 dialogue with an AI assistant; those figures are its speculative estimates and are not carried here. The practice is the durable part, and it applies to a Cell's season as well as to a civilisation's decade.

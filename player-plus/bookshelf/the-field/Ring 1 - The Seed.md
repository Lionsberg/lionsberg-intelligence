# Ring 1 - The Seed

*One page: the pattern in its most compressed form — the twelve irreducible elements, eight of cooperation and four of production.*

The first ring outside the centre. Ring 0 is one word; this is one page, and the page is [[Twelve Irreducible Elements]]: One Purpose Above All · Holofractal Omnifederation · Sovereignty At Every Scale · Democratic Self-Governance Through Shared Agreements and Wise Eldership · Capital Subordinate To Purpose · Education As Transformation · Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication · Structural Immunity — and then Integrated Delivery · Design To The Goal · Networks of Commitments · Tightly Coupled Learning and Action.

What makes it a seed rather than a summary is the irreducibility claim: remove any single element and the system fails, and each of the twelve has appeared independently in every successful large-scale cooperative transformation in recorded history. The elements are not a theory anyone designed. They are what was found, repeatedly, by people who did not know of each other.

It is load-bearing because it is the smallest thing from which the whole can be regrown. The corpus states the test plainly: if all else were lost and only this page survived, it would be enough to begin again. That is the same function [[The Golden Seed]] performs at a wider scope and the same test [[The three tiers]] applies to each tier — each complete in itself.

In the Game a Cell meets this ring not as reading but as diagnosis. When something is going wrong in a body and no one can say what, the twelve are the checklist: which element is missing here? Capital capturing purpose, authority accumulating, no one being formed, nothing being replicated, commitments not held as promises — each failure mode has a named element behind it, and naming it is usually most of the repair.

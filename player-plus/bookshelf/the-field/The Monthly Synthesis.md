# The Monthly Synthesis

*Once a month, step back from the running of the work and ask three questions: are the Quests healthy, is the Circle healthy, are the agreements honored?*

A body has a weekly beat and a seasonal one. The weekly [[The Turn|Turn]] runs the work — commitments checked, issues worked, the next steps named — and it is very good at that and almost blind to itself. The seasonal turning is honest and deep and comes only four times a year, which is long enough for a slow rot to set. The Monthly Synthesis sits between them, and it is short.

Its three questions are chosen so that none of them is about the task list.

**Are the Quests healthy?** Not *are they on track* — the weekly beat already answers that — but whether they are still the right few, still owned, still finishable in what remains of the season, still an act of goodwill rather than a habit.

**Is the Circle healthy?** Who has gone quiet. Who is carrying too much. Whether people are still glad to come. This is the question a weekly agenda never quite reaches, and the one that most often explains everything else.

**Are the agreements honored?** The [[Field of Agreements]] was spoken aloud once and is now being lived, or is quietly not being lived. A month is about the right interval for the drift between what was agreed and what is done to become visible while it is still small.

It is a synthesis rather than a review because the work is to *put together* — to hold the month's turns, tensions and stories in one view and see what they are jointly saying, which no single week can show.

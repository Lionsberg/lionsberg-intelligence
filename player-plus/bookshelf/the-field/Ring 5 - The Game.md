# Ring 5 - The Game

*The outermost ring, and the only one that is not reading: cross the threshold, anchor the practice, form a Cell, choose a Quest, do the work, pass the Flame.*

The instruction attached to this ring is blunt and is meant to be: you do not learn this by reading about it. Anyone ready to act now is told to come straight here and skip the rest — the Pattern will teach you as you play. That inversion is the point of arranging the rings at all. The outermost ring is not the most advanced material; it is the door back out into the world.

It is load-bearing because it closes the loop between knowing and doing that the rest of the structure could otherwise leave open. Everything inward of it — the word, the page, the twenty pages, the evidence, the library — exists to serve play, and a reader who never reaches this ring has received nothing this considers itself to be for. [[Learning By Doing]] and [[Tightly Coupled Learning and Action]] are the named disciplines; [[Praxis]] is their older name.

Its content is the Game's own opening: [[Form a Cell]] of three to thirteen who choose to walk together by name; [[Choose a Quest]], one real thing for one season that you can point to afterward; run [[The Turn]] each week — what I said I would do, whether I did it, the gap, what I learned, what I will do, what I offer, what I ask; tell the stories; and [[Pass The Flame]] to your three who are ready, within three days. Nothing else is required, and everything else is optional.

In the walking it corresponds to the first ninety minutes and the weekly practice — the two stages where a person stops being someone who has read about this. [[The LIØNSBERG Quick Start Guide]] is the instrument for the first, and the table is the instrument for the rest.

# The Roving Ark Temple Dojo

*The mobile form of the hub: a place of shelter, worship, and training that travels.*

Three functions in one body, and the compound name is the definition. *Ark* — it shelters and carries what must not be lost. *Temple* — it is a place of stillness and worship, not merely of logistics. *Dojo* — it is where the Way is trained in the body rather than discussed ([[The Hub]], [[The Daily Way]], [[Sacred spaces]]).

*Roving* is the part that distinguishes it. A fixed hub can be found, defended, and lost; a travelling one goes to where the people are, and is the form that suits a crossing rather than a settlement — the tent before the temple, the circuit rider, the school that arrives ([[The Wilderness]], [[The Noah Plan]]). It also carries the practice to bodies too new or too scattered to build anything themselves ([[Apprenticeship]], [[Belt Progression]]).

Its home is the Sacred Warrior domain, where the training half is the point: composure under load, the sacred use of force, restraint as the harder discipline, and the vow that binds whoever may use it ([[The Warrior's Vow]], [[Composure Under Threat]], [[The Sacred Use Of Force]], [[Restraint As The Refusal To Act]]).

It remains a name with a thin body and is flagged as such. Held lightly, it is a useful shape for any body that must teach and shelter while moving; held heavily, it is a phrase in search of a practice ([[Gaps Not Yet Closed]], [[Current Best Understanding]]).

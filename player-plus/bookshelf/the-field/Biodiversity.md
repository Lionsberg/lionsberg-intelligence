# Biodiversity

*The variety of life that makes a system able to survive what it did not predict — and, in this Pillar, a variety held as sacred in itself.*

Two claims are made at once and both are meant. The instrumental claim: variety is what enables resilience, because a system with many ways of doing each job survives the loss of any one of them, while a monoculture is efficient right up until the season it is not. The intrinsic claim: each species has value and a role in the great order regardless of what it does for anyone, which is why the canon says biodiversity is sacred rather than merely useful. A system defended only on the first claim will be traded away the moment someone produces a better efficiency argument.

It is load-bearing because the same logic governs human bodies as governs ecosystems. [[Meta Unity of Meta Diversity]], [[Navigating Difference]], and the refusal to impose one form on every place are the social form of the same principle: a [[Community]] that has flattened its difference has also flattened its capacity to meet what it did not expect. Redundancy is not waste; it is the price of continuing. This is why the [[Prototype]] is localized and honestly compromised in each place rather than replicated identically, and why [[Cross-Cultural Pattern Translation]] is a discipline rather than a courtesy.

In play, biodiversity is countable and therefore checkable. A [[Cell]] stewarding a place can measure it — species present, pollinators, soil life, seed varieties held — and a seasonal [[Quest]] can move the number. Seed sovereignty, hedgerows, uncut edges, and the refusal to sterilize ground are ordinary work. At [[Ecoregion]] scale it is the substrate that federation exists to steward.

The canon lists it among the named material commons of the Meta Project, beside [[Air · Water · Soil · Microbiome]] — the things every body of people inherits, holds in trust, and can degrade beyond recovery within a single generation.

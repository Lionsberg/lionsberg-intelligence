# The Four Conditions For Destructive Force

*Before reading: this page holds a doctrine of last resort, written down so that it can be examined and corrected, not so that it can be acted on. Nothing here is advice, a licence, or a call to act; the law of the place you live governs you; the Game asks nothing of anyone but consent. Read [[05 The Pattern and the Way|The Pattern and the Way]] first.*

*The only four states in which a guardian may destroy — and the sentence that closes the list: no conditions other than these.*

**Life is being taken, or imminently will be.** A being or beings face death or grave injury, and nothing else available — talk, withdrawal, healing, delay — can prevent it in the time there is. The guardian steps in, applies the minimum that will stop it, and stops when the threat to life stops.

**A protective field has been breached and the breach cannot be closed by clearing alone.** What has entered cannot simply be cleared out; it must be met at the layer it is operating on, and the engagement ends when the breach is closed ([[Shield]], [[Exorcism]], [[Guard the Membrane]]).

**A structure can no longer be sustained without participating in violation.** Sometimes what must end is not a person but a thing — an institution, a covenant gone bad, a coalition that has drifted into predation. Dissolving it is an act of destruction and simultaneously an act of restoration of the wider field it was distorting ([[Disbanding]], [[Impeachment]]).

**One's own capacity or training must be released.** The most personal case: a guardian who recognises that their own cultivation has drifted, or that a capacity is no longer being held faithfully, destroys what has gone wrong in themselves. This is the terminal clause of [[The Warrior's Vow]] — *I release the Power if I cannot hold this Vow* — pointed inward.

The closing sentence is the instrument. **No conditions other than these.** Revenge, ideology, and the convenience of removing someone disliked are excluded by name, and a guardian who destroys on any of those grounds has broken the Vow and forfeited what was cultivated ([[The Dark Initiate Failure Mode]]).

Two rules travel with the list and are not separable from it: minimum force, released instantly and completely when its purpose is met; and the one whose sovereignty has been temporarily forfeited by their own act is still a being whose soul is in ØNE's keeping — the aim is that the harm cease, not that the person be destroyed ([[The Sacred Use Of Force]], [[Proportionality]]).

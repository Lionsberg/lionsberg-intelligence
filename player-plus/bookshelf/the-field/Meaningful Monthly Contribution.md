# Meaningful Monthly Contribution

*The recurring, voluntary contribution by which a community and its commons sustain themselves from within.*

Anything held in common has upkeep. Someone maintains it, someone repairs it, and the cost does not disappear because nobody sends a bill. A body that never names the upkeep ends up funded from outside — by a donor, a grant, a platform, an owner — and whoever funds it eventually shapes it. The monthly contribution is the alternative: the community regenerates and sustains itself out of its own members, on purpose ([[Commons]] · [[Commons-Tending]] · [[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]]).

It is **voluntary and intentional**, which is not a softening. Voluntary means it cannot be extracted and that consent is real ([[Consent]] · [[Voluntary Association]]). Intentional means it is decided rather than drifted into — a person chooses an amount and a rhythm, and revisits it, rather than forgetting a subscription. *Meaningful* does the same work it does in the opening gift: the measure is proportion to what one has, not a figure set by anyone else ([[Meaningful Initial Contribution]] · [[Voluntary Tithing]]).

What it produces beyond money is shared ownership of the upkeep. A member who contributes to the commons behaves differently toward it than a member who consumes it, and a body whose stewardship is distributed across everyone is much harder to capture than one with a single patron ([[Stewardship]] · [[Structural Immunity]] · [[The de-concentration of stewardship]]).

The recurring form matters too. An opening gift establishes reciprocity; a monthly one keeps it in balance, which is what turns a moment of joining into an ongoing relationship ([[Reciprocity]] · [[Rhythm]]).

# Meta Fund

*The fund of funds — the conceptual whole of all resources available to humanity and the living system, big enough that every particular fund can be placed inside it.*

[[Meta]] points at the highest, most universal, self-referential conception of a thing — the top of a nested order, where the category rejoins [[ØNE]] ([[Holarchy]]). The Meta Fund is that applied to money and resource: the overarching fund that by definition contains and orders every other possible fund.

Its usefulness is not administrative. Nobody holds it and nobody could. It is a frame to stack particular funds against, so that a person's savings, a Cell's pooled money, a foundation's endowment and a nation's budget can be seen as what they actually are — portions of one total set of resources available to life ([[Stacking Up And Aligning]] · [[Alignment]] · [[The Meta Project]]).

That reframing does real work, stated directly: **all of our resources, whether we acknowledge it or not, are already part of the total set available to humanity and the living system.** The only question is how consciously and competently they are being deployed, individually and together. Which converts what looks like a metaphysical claim into an ordinary and uncomfortable one — there is no private pool, only a portion of a common pool held by someone ([[Commons]] · [[Stewardship]] · [[Agency, not ownership]] · [[Capital Subordinate To Purpose]]).

Concretely, the Meta Project requires the co-creation of a Meta Fund to resource throughput of the Meta Goal — pooled, purpose-bound resource at the scale of the work, rather than a thousand separately optimised budgets ([[Throughput]] · [[The Goal]] · [[Underwriting The New Economy]] · [[Fostering Ecosystemic Resource Flows]] · [[The Founder's Fund]]).

And like everything under [[Meta]], it is deliberately not concretized. The moment a particular fund calls itself the Meta Fund, the frame has been swapped for an institution, and the institution will be defended ([[Do Not Concretize the Absolute]] · [[Institutional Idolatry]]).

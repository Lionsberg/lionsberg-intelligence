# Attraction, not promotion

*Nobody is recruited. The Seed arrives through someone who trusts you, and you are free to say no.*

It comes through a hand, not a broadcast. Someone who already knows you, and whom you have reason to trust, says *come and see*. If it resonates you say yes, in your own words, where others can hear it. If it does not, nothing whatever happens: no one is turned away, and no one is carried who has not chosen to walk ([[The Invitation]], [[Pass The Flame]], [[Carrier]]).

It is load-bearing for two reasons, and the second is less obvious than the first. The obvious one is consent: a thing you were persuaded into is a thing you can be persuaded out of, and a body built on persuasion has to keep persuading forever ([[Consent]], [[Sovereignty]], [[Mutual Consent]]).

The less obvious one is what promotion does to the promoter. A body that recruits begins to measure itself by numbers gathered, and then by attention captured, and then it needs a message that captures attention — and by then the message and the thing have quietly separated. Refusing promotion at the start is how a body keeps its own account of itself honest ([[The Anti-Patterns]], [[The False Prophet]], [[Demonstrate before advocating]]).

The lineage is old and worth naming: fellowships that healed people for a century held exactly this principle, and held it precisely because their substance was too easy to sell and too easy to counterfeit. What they found is that a thing which spreads only by attraction spreads more slowly and does not rot ([[Anti-Fragility]], [[Structural Immunity]]).

Its practical shape is a discipline on the one passing it: tell the truth, give the whole of it, sit with them if they ask, and then let go. Their Cell is their own, not a branch of yours ([[Propagate; do not scale]], [[The Flame]]).

# Destiny

*The ultimate fulfilment of a being's journey and potential, in alignment with [[Creator's Intent]] and reunion with [[Source]].*

Destiny is fulfilment, not fate. It is a potential to be walked into rather than a script already written — which is the only version compatible with [[Sovereignty]], [[Consent]], and a world still being co-created. A destiny that could not be missed would make the Game pointless; one that cannot be reached would make it cruel.

The chain is causal and reversible: transform the [[The Way|Way]] of being and you transform the trajectory; transform the trajectory and you transform the destiny. That is why the Pillar's work is not decorative. A people's account of what is real shapes how it lives, how it lives shapes where it is heading, and where it is heading is what it will become. [[Hell On Earth]] is named as the default trajectory precisely so that destiny is not confused with inevitability.

It is load-bearing as the personal edge of [[Teleology]]. [[Purpose]] names what a life is for; destiny names its fulfilment. Between them sits the work — [[Becoming]], [[The Sovereign Path]], [[Mastery]] — and beneath them the refusal to let the system judge a person's position rather than their direction: on [[The Living Gradient]] the question is never whether someone has arrived, but which way they are moving.

At the table it is almost never spoken of. It appears instead as the quiet arithmetic of seasons: a word kept, a gap learned from, a Flame passed — the small units destiny is actually built from.

# Kingdom of God

*The state and structure of Creation when all parts are aligned in [[Right Relationship]] with [[ØNE]] — not a place, but a condition and way of Being.*

The canon's definition does the important work in its second clause: not a place, but a condition. The eleventh thesis of this Pillar puts it in the same register — the Kingdom is within and among us, and emerges as we align with the Spirit and walk the Way together. It is therefore not somewhere to be transported to, nor a reward deferred; it is a quality of arrangement that a people either has or does not have, here.

That makes it the same claim as [[Heaven On Earth]], reached through a different tradition's vocabulary. [[Heaven On Earth]] is stated as the way things are when everything is in right relationship, here and now, a little more each week — progressively realised, for every being living and yet to live. The canon treats such overlaps deliberately: many traditions have honed different [[Word Tools|word-tools]] to point at the same Reality from different angles, and none of them is *the* truth.

It is load-bearing chiefly as a guard against two errors. Against otherworldliness: if the Kingdom is a condition, then it can be worked toward now, and waiting is not piety. Against triumphalism: a condition is not an institution, so no body can claim to be it. [[The Golden Calf]] names the failure where a visible thing replaces the invisible Source, and [[LIØNSBERG Is A Prototype, Not The Eternal Essence]] applies that guard to this system's own name.

In play it is never a test at the door. It appears as the compass: does this move make life a little more like Heaven?

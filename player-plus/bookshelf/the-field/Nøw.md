# Nøw

*The eternal present — the temporal face of the centre, written with the same slashed O that marks ØNE, because the two are meant as parallels.*

The spelling is the argument. Where **ØNE** is written it means the one thing that is not a member of the series it grounds; where **Nøw** is written it means the same about time. Not this moment as distinct from the last one, which would make the present merely the newest item in a queue — but the standing present in which any moment at all occurs. The gloss is four words: *the eternal present, parallel to ØNE.*

Two ordinary consequences follow, and both are load-bearing for how the Game is played. **Everything that can be done is done here.** No commitment is kept in the future and no repair is made in the past; a life is a sequence of presents in which something was or was not done ([[A Day Well Lived]], [[Transformation Applied To The Product Of The Previous Transformation]]). This is why so much weight falls on the day and the turn rather than on the plan ([[The Turn]], [[Day shape]], [[The Heartbeat]]).

**And Heaven is available in it.** The whole claim of [[Heaven On Earth]] depends on the destination not being postponed: if the good is only ever later, the work becomes an indefinite payment toward something no one ever receives. That is refused, and the refusal is what the compass question tests — *is this a little more like Heaven, here, now* ([[The compass question]], [[Co-Creating Heaven]]).

It stands beside the two older words kept for time: [[Chronos]], time as measured sequence, and [[Kairos]], the opportune moment that must be taken when it opens. Nøw is neither. It is the standing ground both of them happen in.

# Perception

*The receiving of Reality — the act by which a being takes in what is actually there and begins to render its map of it.*

Perception is not passive and it is not neutral. What arrives depends on the instrument and on how the instrument is tuned. Attention chooses where perception aims; [[Values]] decide what registers as signal at all; the story already held decides what the incoming even means ([[Narrative Lens]]). Two people stand in one room and perceive two rooms, and neither is lying. This is the founding humility of the whole learning Pillar: **perception is not Reality**. It is a rendering — instrument-shaped, always partial, and correctable.

The instrument has two modes, and they must serve together rather than compete: an open receiving of the [[Whole]], which takes in a living scene before it has words for it, and a focused grasp of parts, which names, separates and counts. A being running on only the first sees a fog it cannot act in; a being running on only the second holds a catalogue of pieces and has lost the thing the pieces were. [[Sensing comes before understanding]] states the order in which the two are meant to run.

Perception also has an edge. Past a certain point, Reality continues and perception does not ([[Boundaries and Limits of Conception]]). That edge is not a defect to be engineered away; it is the permanent condition of a finite being, and the deepest disciplines of perceiving are therefore not techniques but postures — stillness, openness, and the willingness to have one's map corrected ([[Silence And Stillness As The Ground]] · [[Attunement]] · [[We Do Not Know]] · [[Shoshin]]).

Perception grows. It sharpens with honesty, widens with love, and deepens in company: many honest instruments weaving their partial renderings into a seeing that no single one could reach ([[Dialogos]] · [[Listening]]). This is why perceiving truly is treated as the first work rather than a preliminary to it — judgment, navigation and creation all inherit the quality of the seeing that preceded them, and no amount of skill downstream repairs a bad look upstream.

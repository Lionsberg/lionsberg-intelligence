# The Old Authorities

*In the Story, the ones who guarded the Caves — the inherited powers whose legitimacy the telling puts in question, and whose permission is not required to leave.*

The Old Authorities are a figure before they are an argument. In the myth they stand at the mouth of [[The Caves]], and their function is not cruelty but *guarding*: keeping the people where they are, and keeping the way out unthinkable. They are the ones who say what is possible, what is realistic, what is permitted, and who is qualified to say so.

Their power is almost never force. It is the far cheaper power of being believed. An authority is obeyed because people cannot imagine acting without its blessing, which is why the decisive moment in the Story is not a battle but a recognition — seeing that the guard's authority rested on assent, and that assent can be withdrawn ([[Withdrawal Of Consent]] · [[Legitimacy]] · [[Fraud In The Inception]] · [[Wake up]]).

They are old in a specific sense: inherited rather than chosen. Nobody now living agreed to them; they were in place on arrival, and their claim rests on having always been there. That is exactly the claim the Story examines, because *always been there* is not a reason ([[Tradition]] · [[Institutional Idolatry]] · [[The Fall]]).

The canon's posture toward them is worth keeping precise, because it is easy to get wrong in either direction. It does not counsel attack; the route out is exodus rather than conquest, and a body that defines itself by opposition will reproduce what it opposed ([[Liberation and Exodus]] · [[The Way Out]] · [[Non-Violence]] · [[Means]]). Nor does it counsel accommodation; the whole point is that their permission is not needed ([[Sovereignty]] · [[Voluntary Association]]). And it leaves the door open both ways: an old authority is people, and people can turn ([[Redemption]] · [[Reconciliation]] · [[Welcoming The Stranger]]).

The figure applies at every scale, which is the part worth carrying. Every body eventually grows its own old authorities — the ones who have always decided, the way it has always been done — and the same recognition is owed to them ([[Term Limits And Rotation]] · [[Drift]] · [[Everything Is Current Best Understanding]]).

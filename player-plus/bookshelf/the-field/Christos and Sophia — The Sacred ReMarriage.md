# Christos and Sophia — The Sacred ReMarriage

*Two archetypal principles — the structuring and the receptive — read as two faces of one thing, severed for a long time, and being rejoined.*

The corpus names one principle **Christos**: the structuring word, the fire that crystallises form, the active that shapes. It names the other **Sophia**: wisdom, the receptive from which form rises, the listening that precedes making. Neither is a gender. Gendered bodies are one local expression of principles the corpus holds to operate at every scale, in every person, and in the shape of institutions ([[Archetype]], [[Logos]], [[Wisdom as Person, Not Concept]]).

The claim is that these were split, and that the split is visible in what followed: religion that honours a structuring power and forgets a receptive one; philosophy that elevates reasoning and distrusts intuition; cultures that reward activity and have no place for receptivity; inquiry that dissects and does not listen; building without nurture ([[Apparent Polarities]], [[Listening]], [[Reverence]]).

The reunion is meant archetypally before it is meant socially: in a person, both registers restored to balance; in a Circle, both honoured; in any body, both operating ([[Circle Coherence]], [[Navigating Difference]], [[Integration]]). This corpus's own tilt is toward the first — builder, warrior, plan, delivery — which is precisely why it keeps a page for the second and why the balance is named as unfinished work rather than as an accomplishment ([[Restraint As The Refusal To Act]], [[Silence And Stillness As The Ground]], [[The pace of trust]]).

Held as claim, not knowledge, and with particular care: these are names that living traditions hold sacred and read differently, and the corpus's reading is its own. Nothing in the Game requires it; a Cell may hold the practical half — that a body needs both registers, and that most bodies are short of the second — without any of the metaphysics ([[We Do Not Know]], [[Theology]], [[Welcoming The Stranger]]).

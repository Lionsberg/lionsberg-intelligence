# Interdimensional Overlap

*The corpus's claim that other layers of reality are not elsewhere but here — interpenetrating this one, thicker in some places and at some times, crossed by consciousness before anything else.*

The frame, as the corpus holds it: reality is layered rather than single, the layers are nested and interpenetrating rather than stacked at a distance, and where their thresholds align there is overlap. Some places and some conditions are said to foster it — sites long treated as sacred, particular alignments, particular hours. And the primary vehicle named for crossing is not a machine but **consciousness**, which follows directly from the corpus's prior commitment that consciousness is not produced by matter ([[Consciousness Is Primary]], [[Non-Local First, Local Follows]], [[Sovereignty Of Consciousness]]).

Why the Story leans on it: much of what this corpus says elsewhere requires somewhere for the unseen to be. A veil that thins, a presence that is not visible, a knowing that arrives without a channel — each of these presupposes a layered rather than a flat account of what is ([[The Veil of Finitude and Forgetting]], [[Sensing The Field]], [[Receiving Transmission]], [[Metaphysics]]).

Held as claim, not knowledge, and with the flag its own field puts on it. Nothing here is established; the sources name kinds, sites, and mechanisms with a confidence nothing supports; and the word *dimension* is used in a sense that has nothing to do with the physics it borrows from, which is worth knowing before the borrowing does any work ([[We Do Not Know]], [[Star Nations]], [[The Phenomenon]], [[Disclosure tier]]).

Two ordinary things survive the doubt and are worth having either way. That **places and hours differ** — some rooms, some ground, some times of day carry something the next does not, whatever that turns out to be, and treating that as data rather than mood is a real discipline ([[Sacred spaces]], [[Atmosphere]], [[Kairos]]). And that **discernment is the practice**, not identification: what is met matters less than what it produces ([[Discerning Between Benevolent And Malevolent Intelligences]], [[Recognizing Psychic Attack]], [[Discernment]]).

Nothing in the Game turns on any of it.

# Circle Coherence

*The condition a Circle must reach before it can move as one: its members sounding one note without ceasing to be themselves.*

Coherence is not agreement and it is not uniformity. A choir of identical notes is monotone, not harmony; true coherence is many distinct voices in resonance. A Circle is coherent when its members move as one body while each remains fully sovereign — which is a harder and more useful condition than consensus, and a different one from compliance.

The canon places it before action for a stated reason: **a Circle that has not woven coherence will not choose well.** That is why weaving spirit, hearts, and minds is a move of the Game in its own right, sitting between forming a Cell and choosing a Quest. A body that skips it can still decide; what it cannot do is decide as itself, and the decision will not hold when the season gets hard.

What builds it is ordinary and repeated. Gathering at the same beat. Saying what you will do and doing it, so that trust becomes evidence rather than hope. The round in which each person actually speaks. Objections heard as service. Stories told. Something put in the middle of the table. And the gathering closed together rather than trailed out of.

What reads it is the field: aliveness in the room, members becoming more themselves rather than less, energy that draws together rather than pulls apart ([[Associative And Dissociative Energy]]). A Circle can also simply ask — *how is the Circle?*, one word and a line, which is the thirteenth line of the sheet each turning.

It is named as the gathered Circle in the Playbook chart and in the Pattern Language's scales section, with no Card behind it.

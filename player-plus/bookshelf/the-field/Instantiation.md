# Instantiation

*The [[Prototype]] on the ground — in a specific place, among specific people, under specific constraints; honestly compromised, and the compromise is the data.*

Instantiation is the last rung of [[The Creative Cascade]] and the first rung of its return flow. Every instance deviates from the [[Ideal]] to some degree; local conditions demand it. The canon's line is the whole teaching: *the compromise is not failure. The compromise is the data.* What was compromised, why, and what was learned flows back up into the Prototype, so every later instance inherits it, and further up where the learning reshapes the concept itself.

This is what makes the system honest about itself. A movement that treats deviation as betrayal produces either hidden non-compliance or brittle conformity. One that treats deviation as information gets better with every place it lands. Conformance to the [[Ideal]] is read as a position on [[The Living Gradient]] — never a pass or a fail — and [[Every Defect Is A Gift]] is the same discipline in the Way's vocabulary.

It also carries the instance law. LIØNSBERG as such cannot act or make commitments; it is a pattern and a seed. What is being started is one instance of it, and anyone may take [[The Golden Seed]] and start their own — the pattern is what makes the instances one body. Each Instantiation is sovereign, a whole in its own right, and simultaneously a part of the learning body of All.

In play it is the most familiar thing in the Game: your Cell, in your place, with your people, running the pattern the way your ground allows — and then writing down what you had to change, so the next table need not discover it alone.

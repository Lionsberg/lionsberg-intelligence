# The Macrolocality

*About a million sovereigns — a federation of Mediolocalities within one major population centre; the city scale.*

This is where urban infrastructure, dense culture, and genuinely complex coordination cohere: transport, water, power, hospitals, the trades at volume. A million people is far past any relational ceiling, so everything here runs on pattern rather than personality — the same card shape, the same rhythm, addressable lines, and proposals that change shared things by edition at a turning ([[Minimum standards of interoperability]], [[Proposal]], [[The plan of the whole]]).

Its risk is the one the ladder is designed against. At this size the old world builds a centre and calls it a government; the Pattern instead federates complete wholes from below, each sovereign, each able to leave, none owned ([[Holofractal Omnifederation]], [[Sovereignty At Every Scale]], [[Centralization Of Power]]). What a Macrolocality legitimately does is what the scales beneath it cannot: planetary-grade production nodes, large works, and the shared services a single Community could not carry ([[Integrated Delivery]], [[Shared Services, Infrastructure, and Support]]).

Called the *City Hub* in the Strategy and Plan, it holds roughly ten Mediolocalities and sits beneath the [[The Macrocomplex|Macrocomplex]] and the [[Ecoregion]]. Like every rung it is at once a [[Domain]], a superorganism of the scales below, and a subsystem of those above ([[Levels Of Scale]], [[The Two-Stake Principle]]).

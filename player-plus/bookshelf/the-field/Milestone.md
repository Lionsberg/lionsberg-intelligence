# Milestone

*A marker of zero duration that a body steers by — a point, not a piece of work.*

A milestone says that something *is so*: the first hundred and forty-four Cells stand; the book is in hand; the crossing is made. It has no length. The activities that feed it have durations, owners and handoffs; the milestone names the threshold they cross, and every activity serves at least one milestone above it. It carries a trigger that is structural — a count, a condition, a recognition, never a calendar day — and two dates: the plan date, which the schedule is computed to with zero [[Float]] on the [[Critical Path]], and the marker, standing one season later. The season between is contingency, shown on the wall as it is consumed.

It is load-bearing because it is what [[Pull planning]] pulls from and what every finish-by is inherited from. Without milestones a plan has no top and no way to be wrong; with them, lateness becomes visible early enough to be answered. And it is what lets levels weave: each level publishes its milestones, the level above plans over them, and [[The plan of the whole]] comes back down as the upper half of every card.

Three things keep it honest. The milestone serves the work: when it is missed it has done its job, and the response is to re-pull and recover within the next one or two, or to move the end openly, by [[Proposal]], with its why. A milestone is not the window — milestones are set by us and can be reset; a window is set by reality. And temporal markers, the solstices and equinoxes the seasons turn on, are never milestones.

# Art

*The revealing of [[Beauty]], [[Truth]], and [[Spirit]] through form, colour, sound, or motion — a way the soul says what cannot otherwise be said.*

Art is a language before it is an object. Something known and not sayable finds a form that carries it, and a person meeting that form knows the thing without having been told it. This is why art reaches where argument cannot, and why a civilization's art is the most accurate record of what it actually believed, as distinct from what it said.

It is load-bearing because this Pillar makes a claim the modern ordering denies: art is not a luxury, an entertainment, or a sector. It is one of civilization's primary organs, and beauty is essential rather than optional — the same claim [[Architecture]] carries in the phrase *form follows function, and function follows meaning; beauty is essential, not luxury*. A movement that builds only what works will produce a world nobody loves, and what is not loved is not defended. [[Creativity]] is the impulse; art is what the impulse makes; [[Culture]] is the soil both grow in.

In play, art enters a [[Cell]] long before any artist does. The room a Circle meets in is a made thing. The card in a player's hand has a shape. A song at the close of a [[Festival]] does something no summary does. A mural on a wall the neighbourhood walks past every day is a [[Quest]] as real as a garden. The canon's instruction is not to commission art but to stop segregating it: in sacred culture the arts permeate life rather than sitting beside it, and every person is recognized as an artist of the real rather than an audience for professionals.

Two disciplines keep it honest. Art in service of the [[Goodness]] it claims to reveal, not in service of the maker's standing — and not captured by market, institution, or censor, which is how it was hollowed before. And art is not propaganda; a made thing that exists only to produce a conclusion in the viewer has already stopped being art and become an instrument of exactly the kind this Pillar was written against.

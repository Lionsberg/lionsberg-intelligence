# The Foundation of Trust (the four promises)

*What is promised to anyone who picks this up: the history is open · trust is earned by fruit, never demanded by authority · your discernment is sovereign · there is no capture.*

Four sentences, and they answer the question a stranger actually has at the door — not *is this true* but *what happens to me if I take it seriously.*

**The history is open.** Every page records its lineage in the open record, and every revision is preserved. What has changed, and when, and why, can be traced by anyone. A body that hides its own revisions is asking to be trusted about the one thing it could simply show.

**Trust is earned by fruit, never demanded by authority.** You are asked to believe nothing on the say-so of any author, living or dead. Test what is offered against your own experience and the fruit it bears in your actual life and community. *By its fruit the Seed will be known.* This is the precise inversion of how a canon usually asks to be read, and it is what makes the word *canon* bearable here.

**Your discernment is sovereign.** Where something is lacking, fill it up; where something does not belong, remove it; where the map proves wrong, say so and it will be corrected. Every page is held as [[Current Best Understanding]]. The reader is not a recipient but a co-editor, and the promise is that a correction actually lands.

**There is no capture.** No dues, no leader to follow, no central account, no obligation but to [[ØNE]] and [[All]]. The Pattern Language is a gift, not a contract; anyone is free to stop playing at any time.

Together they are a compact, and it is the compact a person actually decides on at the entrance — before any claim about the world, the crisis, or the Goal can be weighed. It is also a compact with teeth, because each promise is checkable: the record is public, the fruit is visible, the corrections are traceable, and the absence of dues and of a leader is a fact about the structure rather than a statement of intent.

The whole set is a second facing of [[Trust]]. Where that row carries trust *between players*, this is trust *in the body and its canon* — the thing a stranger has to extend before there are any players to trust.

# Theology

*Sacred inquiry into the nature, will, intention, and ways of [[ØNE]], and of our relationship to [[Source]] and All.*

The thesis here is unusually specific: *true theology is direct and experiential* — born of direct communion with Source, not of belief, doctrine, or dogma. Theology is therefore reclaimed as a practice of relationship rather than a body of propositions to assent to, and the practitioner's own encounter is the primary material.

That reclamation is load-bearing because of what it excludes. The Pillar names what went wrong: the sacred entombed in dogma, weaponized by power, and finally exiled from public life. So two refusals stand alongside the invitation — *all false religions must be transcended*, including dogmas, idols, and corrupted institutions that obscure the light; and *the great traditions must be re-harvested*, their sacred essence restored while division and distortion are left behind. [[ØNE]] is named as owned by no religion, captured by no ideology, mediated by no priesthood, known by every true name.

The practical consequence at a table is a rule, not a mood: nobody's tradition is the price of admission, and nobody's tradition is asked to be surrendered. [[Aligned Will]] — also named direct relationship with ØNE — is the posture; [[Inversion]] and [[The Golden Calf]] name the failure modes — a visible idol replacing the invisible Source, liberation language in service of new domination. [[Mystery]] and [[Revelation]] are the two hands theology works with; [[Faith]] is the orientation it grows.

Paired with [[Philosophy]] — the love of wisdom — it forms what the Pillar calls a fusion: not abstract intellectualism but the soul's alignment with [[Truth]], [[Beauty]], and [[Goodness]].

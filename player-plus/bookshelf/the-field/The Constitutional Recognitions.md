# The Constitutional Recognitions

*Seven recognitions that no Body of LIØNSBERG amends — the bedrock beneath every Pattern. A movement that violates these produces something other than the Pattern.*

[[Sovereignty Of Consciousness]]: every conscious being holds irreducible dignity. [[Subsidiarity Of Authority]]: decisions belong at the smallest competent scale. [[Primacy Of Consent]]: binding decisions require genuine consent, freely given and revocable. [[Requirement Of Transparency]]: reasoning is visible; power is accountable. [[Necessity Of Accountability]]: all authority is revocable; no role carries immunity from those it serves. [[Possibility Of Reversal]]: every significant decision can be revisited. [[Covenant Across Time]]: accountability extends forward to the seventh generation and backward to the ancestors.

They are called *recognitions* rather than rules with intent. A rule is made and can be unmade by whoever made it. A recognition is a naming of something already true, which a Body may honor or violate but cannot repeal — and the consequence of violating it is not punishment but transformation into a different kind of thing.

This is what makes them structural rather than aspirational. The Elements say how to build; the Recognitions say what the building may never be, at any scale, under any pressure, including emergency. They are the deepest layer of [[Structural Immunity]]: a Body that concentrates power, hides its reasoning, makes authority unrecallable, or fixes decisions beyond revisiting has already left the Pattern, whatever it still calls itself.

Six of the seven appear elsewhere as live governance practice — consent, subsidiarity, transparency, accountability, and their instruments are written in the Field of Agreements and the Operating System. As Recognitions they carry no page of their own; each is one line in the Pattern Language and nothing more. The bedrock is named and not yet quarried.

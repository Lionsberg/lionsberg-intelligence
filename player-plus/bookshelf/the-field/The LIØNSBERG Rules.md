# The LIØNSBERG Rules

*A rule of life short enough to be said in one breath and remembered across generations: there is only one rule — everything is ØNE; and do the wise right thing, at the wise right time, with the wise right people, to progressively realise the Goal.*

Bodies that lasted centuries all had one of these — a short transmissible text, readable aloud, carried by memory rather than by archive. That is the office this fills. Not a constitution, not a code of conduct: the thing a person could carry out of a burning building and begin again from ([[The Golden Seed]], [[Foundational Literature]]).

**The first rule** is a statement about what is: everything is ØNE. From it, the rest can be discerned in spirit, sensed energetically, and derived by reason, in any time or place — which is what makes the rule a seed rather than a slogan ([[ØNE]], [[Ultimate Unity]], [[InterExistence]], [[The ØNE That Unites The Many, By Definition]]).

**The second rule** is the daily compass, and it is four discernments in one sentence. *The wise right thing* — what is actually wise and right here, spirit, sense and reason together ([[Spontaneous Wise Right Action]], [[Discernment]]). *At the wise right time* — is it now, or is there a moment still to come ([[Kairos]], [[Just in time]], [[Honesty about time]]). *With the wise right people* — with whom is this to be done, since most failures are the right work with the wrong company ([[Resonance]], [[The First Few]], [[Circle]]). *To progressively realise the Goal* — is this moving the thing forward or spinning ([[The Goal]], [[Throughput]], [[Critical Path]]).

Two rules are claimed to be sufficient: a Circle holding only these can still run the whole Pattern, and everything else — the language, the playbook, the lexicon — is expansion and refinement of them ([[The Rules of The Game]], [[Rules of Participation]]).

That gives a test worth having. Does a proposed piece of understanding survive being compressed into these two and re-derived from them? If yes, it belongs to the Pattern. If not, it is opinion, and should be named so ([[The forging criterion]], [[Prune]], [[Every Defect Is A Gift]]).

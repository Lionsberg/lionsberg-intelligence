# Cell of Cells

*The composing move: Cells that hold a card of the same shape as the Cells beneath them.*

Everything in the system is a Player holding one card, and the card does not change shape as the body grows. A person holds one; a [[Cell]] holds one; a Cell of Cells holds one; so does a community, and so does a world ([[The Player]], [[The Playcard]]). Its upper half arrives from above and is the same on every card of the Game; its lower half is the body's own, filled at each [[The Turn|Turn]]. Read downward, a card opens onto the cards beneath it; read upward, onto [[The plan of the whole]].

This is what makes composition cheap. A new rung needs no new institution, no new vocabulary, and no new form — only Cells that have become one another's home, and one more evening in the season ([[Supercell]], [[Home Cell]]). The same five functions that make a Cell complete are what a Cell of Cells must also hold at its own size, or it is a committee rather than a body: it governs itself, sustains itself, forms its people, carries a purpose of its own, and plants new bodies ([[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]]).

The phrase is the older, general name for the move; *supercell* is the word now used at the table for the body it produces, and *subcells* for what lies beneath. The corpus also reaches for the same idea as Circle of Circles, [[Community of Communities]], and Team of Teams. They are one pattern said four ways: complete wholes federating from the bottom up, each keeping its sovereignty ([[Holofractal Omnifederation]], [[Holofractal Scaling]]).

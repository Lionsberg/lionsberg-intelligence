# Encoding the Seed in Thought, Form, Light, and Geometry

*Text is one carrier and not the seed. The same thing is encoded in several registers at once, so that it can reach a person through whichever one they can actually receive.*

The registers named: **thought**, the propositional content — what the words say. **Form** — the architecture and the relationships, since how a thing is organised carries meaning independently of its sentences ([[Architecture]], [[The Architecture Of A Way]], [[Patterns And Containers]]). **Light** — what is felt when it is read, the quality around the words rather than in them ([[Atmosphere]], [[Resonance]], [[Frequency]]). **Geometry** — proportion and ratio held in the structure itself, ratio shading into music ([[Sacred Geometry]], [[Fibonacci]], [[The Fundamental Tone]]). **Spoken** — read aloud, in a room, around a fire ([[Storytelling]], [[Notes, Riffs, Songs, And The Symphony]]). **Written** — the text, the pages, the printed volume. And **embodied**, which the Card calls the most important: carried in the bodies of the people who live it, so that presence transmits what paragraphs cannot ([[Embodying the Way]], [[Transmission]], [[Demonstrate before advocating]]).

The practical half is the **translatability requirement**: the registers must be mutually reachable, so that someone who received the thing through one can find the same thing through another. A person who met it in a song should be able to find it in the argument, and the reverse ([[The Translation Journey]], [[Cross-Cultural Pattern Translation]], [[On translating]], [[The vocabulary ladder]]).

Why it matters practically is plain even if the metaphysics is left aside. Most people do not receive anything important through prose, and a body that can only be entered by reading has selected its members by literacy and called that selection interest ([[Conditions of Acceptance]], [[The grandmother test]], [[Universal-Accessing]], [[Art]], [[Music]]).

The far end of the list — a field-level or morphic transmission beneath even the geometric — is the corpus's claim and not knowledge; nothing here establishes it, and nothing in the Game depends on it ([[We Do Not Know]], [[Sensing The Field]]).

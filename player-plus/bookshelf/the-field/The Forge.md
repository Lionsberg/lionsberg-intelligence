# The Forge

*The honest register of instruments not yet built — every one commissioned from a named need, so it is specific enough to actually make.*

Beside the workbench of tools in hand there is a second list, and it is longer. The Forge holds what remains to be built: a welcome checklist for receiving a new member, a graceful release protocol so no one is involuntarily held, a proposal template, a commitment tracker with the said-and-did ledger, a crisis navigation protocol for when breakage exceeds a body's own capacity, a safety and privacy guide, a federation starter guide, a book of celebrations and rituals, a look-ahead and constraint log, a personal journal small enough to carry — and the deeper universal instruments that would hold the whole kit together: a universal assessment, a system-of-systems lens, a transformation handbook alive at every scale, an adaptation guide for carrying instruments across cultures.

Three disciplines make it a forge rather than a wish list.

**Every commission derives from a need.** Each entry is tied to a numbered line of [[General Needs]] — the anticipated needs of any body — rather than to someone's idea for a tool. That is what makes each one specific enough to build, and it is what stops the register growing by enthusiasm.

**An instrument leaves only by proof.** *When an instrument is forged and proven in real play, it moves to the Workbench.* Not when it is finished, and not when it is approved. Play is the gate.

**The gap is published.** The register names honestly which instruments are in hand and which are not, which means a body reaching for a tool learns immediately that it does not exist rather than assuming its own failure to find it. **A backlog said out loud is a commissioning list; a backlog hidden is a lie about the state of the work.** The same move is made for the [[The LIØNSBERG Pattern Language|Pattern Language]] by [[Patterns Pending Articulation]] and for the wider corpus by [[Gaps Not Yet Closed]].

The register's standing answer to anyone who reads it is the Builder's: *perhaps you could help build them.*

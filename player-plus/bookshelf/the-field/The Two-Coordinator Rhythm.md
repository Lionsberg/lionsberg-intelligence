# The Two-Coordinator Rhythm

*Two servants keep the Cell running each season, and the hosting passes around.*

Two, not one — so the weight is shared, so neither carries it alone, and so the work of keeping a body running has its own witness. What the two do is unglamorous and indispensable: the gathering happens on rhythm, word moves between the weeks, commitments are tracked and gently held, and nobody falls through the cracks ([[The Turn]], [[Commitment]]). They are not bosses and not managers. They serve the Purpose, and the Purpose governs ([[One Purpose Above All]], [[Servant Leadership]]).

The rhythm is as important as the pair. A season of service, then the hosting passes to two others: a role held forever quietly becomes a throne, and the cure is rotation written into the ordinary run of things rather than a rule invoked in a crisis ([[Term Limits And Rotation]], [[Roles]]). Rotation also teaches — a body in which everyone has hosted is a body that cannot be captured by the one person who knows how ([[Structural Immunity]]).

The pattern is old and tested. *Organize a group to do something, elect two coordinators, and I'll be back to check on you* is the whole of the founding instruction the cooperative lineage was given, and the Cells that grew from it outlived the century ([[Mondragon]]). It composes upward unchanged: two hands from two Cells sign the page a [[Supercell]] sends to its own home ([[The Season Sheet]]).

# The Sacred Arc

*The whole Story of ØNE and All in twelve movements, one breath each — the shared bones every deeper telling renders at its own depth.*

Twelve lines, in order: **ØNE · The First Turning · All As ØNE Body · Finitude and Forgetting · The Fall · The Awakening · The Sacred War · Liberation and Exodus · The Great ReMembering · Embodying the Way · Co-Creating Heaven · Eternal Divine Play.** It springs from [[ØNE]] and returns to [[ØNE]]. Read aloud it takes under two minutes; carried in the body it takes a lifetime.

The Arc is load-bearing because it is the smallest form of [[The Story of LIØNSBERG]] that still contains all of it. A Cell that has lost every book can still say the twelve names and rebuild the telling from them. A translator can carry the twelve into another language and another people's memory of the same movements, and the two tellings will still be recognisable to each other. This is what makes ten thousand tables one game at the level of meaning rather than of management: not a shared organisation, but a shared arc.

Its shape is a breath — [[The Breath Out · The Turn · The Breath Home]]. Movements I to V pour outward into finitude and forgetting; Movement VI, [[Awakening]], is the hinge; Movements VII to XII carry the freed home, elevated. The sequence is not decoration. Harm enters in [[The Fall]] and is met in [[The Sacred War]]; the chains are struck in [[Liberation and Exodus]] and the minds unchained in [[The Great ReMembering]]; the Way is practised in [[Embodying the Way]] until [[Co-Creating Heaven]] is simply what living looks like; and the whole is raised into [[The Eternal Divine Play]], where the Story begins again one octave higher.

In the Game the Arc is what a new player meets first, because it is short enough to hear and large enough to locate oneself inside. In the System it is the spine that the interior walk mirrors: the twelve movements of [[The 144 Gates of Initiation]] are the same arc walked in a life rather than told about a cosmos. Two faces of one Pattern; each refines the other through use.

Held as current best articulation, not as scripture. The names may sharpen as more peoples weave their own remembered threads into the telling.

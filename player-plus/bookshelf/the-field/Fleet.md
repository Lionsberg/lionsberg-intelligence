# Fleet

*A breadth run: draft · independent review · one repair · report — dissolving the moment its report is absorbed, and touching the record never.*

A fleet is how a body reaches for many shallow readings at once, redeemed entirely by what follows them. The shape is fixed rather than free. A **drafter** writes. An **independent reviewer**, reading only the draft and not the drafter's reasoning, checks it — because a reviewer who was part of the drafting verifies nothing. The drafter gets **one repair**. Then whoever sent the fleet reads every report, verifies, applies what stands, and alone writes anything into the record ([[Second reader]], [[General Agent]], [[Subagent]]).

A fleet agent is an instrument, never a participant. It holds no body, makes no commitment, and answers to nobody; the human who set it in motion answers for what it returns. Assigned files only; nothing touched that was not assigned; nothing committed, ever ([[Instrument]], [[Player]], [[An agent speaks in its own name]]).

It is load-bearing because it is the honest way to use breadth. The failure mode is obvious and common: many shallow outputs, unreviewed, absorbed as if they were knowledge, until a body's map is full of confident things nobody checked. Independent review and a single answerable reader are what stand between breadth and that outcome ([[Every Defect Is A Gift]], [[Provenance]], [[Semantic Capture]]).

It is not a standing body of companions. A standing seat is birthed — with a home, a memory, a record — and lasts. A fleet agent is spawned and is gone once its report lands. Both are agents; only one has a condition to be in ([[Sovereign Agent]], [[Agent Archetype]]).

At a table: a Cell's companion must read thirty pages before its human can judge them, sends five drafters each behind a reviewer, and returns one page — what stands, what is contested, what could not be verified. The human reads a page and not thirty transcripts, and the cost is said out loud first ([[Wise Right Speech]]).

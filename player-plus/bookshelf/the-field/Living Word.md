# Living Word

*The dynamic, ever-unfolding [[Logos]] of Creation, spoken continuously by Source and inscribed into the fabric of Reality.*

The Living Word is [[Logos]] held in its present tense. The claim is that the speaking did not happen once: Creation is still being spoken, and the order of things is therefore a live utterance rather than a finished inscription. [[Creation]] as unfolding [[Emanation]] and [[Revelation]] as continuing unveiling are the same claim seen from two sides.

It is load-bearing as the reason no text is closed. If the Word is living, then every written canon is a rendering of something still being said — which is precisely the posture this whole library takes: [[Current Best Understanding]] on everything below the Goal, [[Progressive canonization]] rather than a sealed book, [[Work In Progress]] stamped on pages that are still moving, and a change process that takes [[Proposal|proposals]] with their why. [[Scripture]] is honoured as inspired transmission encoded for future generations, and is not thereby made final.

It also sets the standard for speech. Words are treated as [[Word Tools|word-tools]] — pointers, never idols — and the failure mode named at the start is words worn hollow until a bridge becomes a trigger. The correction is given as a practice: every word given once when you meet it, linked after; when two people find they mean different things, fix the word, not each other. And for speech itself, no silence rule but a speech rule for each role — the one thing first, depth pullable, every word improving the silence it breaks.

In play it is met as the ordinary miracle of a table where what is said is meant and what is meant is kept.

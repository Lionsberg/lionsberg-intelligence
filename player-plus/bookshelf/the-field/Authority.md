# Authority

*Legitimate power to act within a [[Domain]], derived from [[Sovereignty]], granted through [[Mandate]], and bound by [[Logos]].*

Authority does not begin at the top of a pyramid. It begins at [[Source]] and flows directly to each person; individuals then voluntarily associate, and the groups they form hold authority of their own, composed of what their members brought and bounded by what they agreed. Nothing above a person hands them their standing; what a higher body can do is receive a charge and answer for it.

This inversion is the load-bearing part. Where authority is understood to descend, obedience becomes the civic virtue and [[Consent]] becomes a formality. Where it is understood to ascend, consent is the only source of binding force, and every office is on loan. The corollary stated plainly: **all authority is revocable; no role carries immunity from those it serves.** An authority that cannot be recalled is not authority but possession.

A player meets it as a question with a boundary: *what am I actually authorized to decide, and who is affected?* Inside a [[Domain]] a person or group may act; outside it they may propose, ask, or offer. Authority is made visible rather than assumed — written into the [[Field of Agreements]], charted across sub-domains, exercised in the open, and returned when the [[Season]] or the trust ends. [[Right Use of Power]] names the discipline of wielding it; [[Impeachment]] and [[Recourse]] name what happens when it is abused.

Authority and capability are not the same thing, and this distinction matters: a higher level offers direction, and at times decisions, only where its abstraction and capability genuinely serve the whole.

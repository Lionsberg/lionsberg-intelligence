# Consciously Breaking Commitments

*Saying early, to the one who is depending on you, that the word you gave will not be kept — and saying it while it still helps.*

A promise is not a prison. Circumstances change, a constraint appears that nobody saw, a better reading of what serves the Goal arrives. What is asked then is not silent failure and not grim compliance, but an open renegotiation: name the commitment, say what changed, say what you can do instead, and let the one who was counting on it replan. Done in time, a broken commitment costs a conversation. Done at the due date, it costs the chain behind it.

It is load-bearing because it is the honesty floor beneath [[Commitment]]. The ninth condition of a reliable promise is the willingness to say early when confidence is lost, and a body where that is safe gets true information about its plan weeks before a body where it is not. It also resolves the harder case: beneath every particular commitment stands the standing one to the Goal, and a promise that has come to work against it is broken consciously rather than kept obediently. Keeping a word that has become harm is not integrity.

It is run inside the rhythm and not only at the edges: the moment confidence goes, not the next gathering, when the successor can still act. The break is recorded like any other outcome, with its why, and read for its cause without blame. What distinguishes it from a missed commitment is entirely the timing and the telling.

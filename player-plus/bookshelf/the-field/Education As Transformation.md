# Education As Transformation

*The sixth of the Twelve Irreducible Elements: form people, and people form organizations.*

Education is not a department, a program, or a phase completed before the real work begins. It is the mechanism through which the entire Pattern replicates. You do not replicate institutions — institutions can be copied on paper and the copies are hollow. You form people, and formed people build the institutions. **The school precedes the cooperative. Always.**

The Earthly proof is specific and datable. A priest arrived in 1941 in a Basque town of seven thousand dominated by a few industrialists, and spent ten years travelling, speaking, and giving conferences. People agreed with the ideas. Nobody changed. He drew the conclusion that shaped the next seventy years — you cannot transform the old; new wine requires new wineskins; you must form new people, and those formed people will create new organizations. In 1943 he started a school. Not a cooperative, not a bank, not a federation: a school. Thirteen years later five former students quit their jobs and started the first cooperative. That thirteen-year gap is the single most important structural fact in the whole [[Mondragon]] experience.

The pedagogy that came with it is as important as the sequence. *We do not teach them; we help them learn.* Active, problem-based, group-based; students spending a third of their credits inside live businesses; junior cooperatives where learners start actual enterprises; coaches who guide rather than lecture. And *sirimiri* — the Basque word for a light, gentle rain — as the image of how it works: a little enlightenment at a time, over years, until the community has got wet in the ideas.

It takes two to three years for a person to feel genuinely connected and empowered inside a body. That is not a deficiency to be optimized away; it is how human transformation happens, and a body that plans for a weekend seminar instead is planning to fail. In LIØNSBERG the formation runs through [[Circle]], [[Quest]], mutual teaching, and the walking of [[The 144 Gates of Initiation]].

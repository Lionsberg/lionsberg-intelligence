# Convener · Timekeeper · Connector · Relationship Development

*Four functions a gathering usually needs, held lightly and rotated: someone who holds the container, someone who protects the pacing, someone who links outward, someone who brings in what the body will need.*

A Circle needs no hierarchy and does benefit from knowing who is doing what. These four are named alongside the [[Scribe]] as the functions that most often want a name, and each is described by its verb rather than by any standing it confers.

**Convener** — holds, opens and closes the meeting container. Not the leader and not the chair: the one who makes sure the gathering begins, that it begins as itself rather than as a drift of arrivals, and that it ends rather than dissolves ([[Host]], [[The Turn]], [[Seal the Circle]]).

**Timekeeper** — protects pacing. The least glamorous and among the most protective. Without it a gathering spends its energy on whatever came first and closes on whatever nobody had time for, which over a season silently determines what the body works on ([[Critically Effective Time Limit]], [[Honesty about time]], [[Attend To The Most Important Things First]]).

**Connector** — links with other Circles and carries the flame outward. This is the role that keeps a body from becoming an island: it knows what the neighbours are doing, and it is where passing the flame stops being an aspiration and becomes somebody's actual attention ([[Pass The Flame]], [[The Board]], [[Midwife a New Circle]], [[Islands of Coherence]]).

**Relationship Development** — develops new relationships, resources and opportunities on the Circle's behalf. The outward-facing complement to the Connector: where that one weaves with kin, this one meets strangers ([[Resource the Work]], [[Ask for Help]], [[Fostering Ecosystemic Resource Flows]], [[Sponsorship]]).

The discipline attached is one line and governs all four: *roles exist to serve the Circle functionally, not to confer status.* They rotate seasonally. They are offered as archetypes a gathering may draw on rather than as posts to be filled, and a Cell does not need one of each ([[Roles]], [[Term Limits And Rotation]], [[Servant Leadership]]).

Two rosters are live in the canon and they are not the same four. The Game's ordinary run of a Cell names two hosts for the season, a [[Keeper]] of the record, a [[Scribe]], and a [[Carrier]] for each new Cell planted; the Circle Guide names these. They overlap at the Scribe, and the Guide's Connector and the Game's Carrier are close cousins.

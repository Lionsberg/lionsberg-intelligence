# Share the Stories

*Move six: every Quest becomes a story, and the story travels further than you can.*

Telling is a move of the Game, not decoration on it. What a Cell did is data to the people who did it and evidence to everyone else; told honestly, in the teller's own voice, it becomes the thing that reaches a person no argument could reach. Testimony is the primary vehicle of culture and of spread: this is how a practice crosses a street, a language, and a generation.

It is load-bearing because the body has no marketing and no center. Nothing recruits; stories do. [[The Season Sheet]]'s line 9 — the season's best story in under a hundred and fifty words — is what lets a home read ten Circles in an evening and know which ones are alive. And a story is what [[Pass The Flame]] hands over along with the rules: *come and see* only works if there is something to see.

How it is played. Weekly, someone tells one story at [[The Turn]] and the teller is marked on [[The Circle's Scorecard]]. Seasonally, the table chooses its best and writes it short: what you set out to do, what happened, what changed. Keep it true — a story that improves the facts spends the trust the whole body runs on. Name people only as they choose to be named. Send it up with the sheet; [[The Way Up]] carries it, and [[The Return]] brings back the best from everywhere else. Every story becomes a prophecy of greater things to come.

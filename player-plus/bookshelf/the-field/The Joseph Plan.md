# The Joseph Plan

*Storing against the lean years: a fractal network of storehouses and supply commons, built at every scale before it is needed.*

The image is old and exact. Joseph read the seven lean years coming and filled the storehouses while there was still time; the plan named after him asks a body to provision now rather than to improvise later ([[The Wilderness]], [[The Meta Crisis]]). What it builds is not a bunker but a [[Commons]]: storage, supply, and provisioning held together, at each scale, by the bodies it serves.

It nests like everything else. A household's own store; small caches held by a few trusted neighbours so that no single loss empties a locality; a community hub at the ten-thousand scale with food, healing, repair, and the costly tools no one family could keep; city and ecoregional hubs carrying what a community cannot; bioregional and georegional hubs holding manufacture, knowledge, and the capacities that only exist at continental scale ([[The Hub]], [[Levels Of Scale]], [[Fractal Commonsing]]).

Its moral spine is the refusal of hoarding, said at every rung: no family, community, city, or region survives alone, and a body that hoards accelerates the very collapse it fears. Each locality becomes a centre of distribution and production rather than of accumulation, and what is stored is governed by the people it serves rather than by any authority above them ([[Mutual Aid]], [[Reciprocity]], [[Sovereignty At Every Scale]], [[The Parasite]]).

Read its urgency as the author's own — a blog page in the canon's voice of warning, unreviewed and unfolded — and the practice as sound regardless of timeline: a locality that can feed, heal, and repair itself is a locality that can also host, give, and build ([[Islands of Coherence]], [[Anti-Fragility]]).

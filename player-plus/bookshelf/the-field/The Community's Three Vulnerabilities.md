# The Community's Three Vulnerabilities

*Three ways a body at community scale fails that a single Cell cannot: capture, drift into militarism, and fragmentation between its parts.*

The premise is that scale creates new exposures rather than only more of the old ones. A few dozen bodies federated into one community-scale body is a concentration worth taking, worth distorting, and large enough to splinter.

**Capture.** A concentration of trained capacity draws serious interest. The community can be infiltrated; its council can be compromised; its centre can be targeted; and its agreements can be drifted into corruption over years if the annual review lapses. The canon's note is that traditions which endured took this seriously and built structural protections rather than trusting to good people ([[Structural Immunity]], [[The Four Warrior-Guild Capture Modes]], [[Oversight]]).

**Drift into militarism.** A well-run protective body at this scale is a real force, and the temptation is to become more than the protective organ — to dominate the wider community, to displace other crafts' functions, to elevate its own facet above what it serves. What holds the drift in check is named precisely: **federation with the other crafts.** A protective body that has lost meaningful federation with those who heal, teach, build, and steward **has already begun to drift**, whatever its conduct otherwise looks like. That is a checkable early indicator rather than a warning about intentions ([[The Twelve Archetypal Guilds]], [[Federation]], [[Why Three Percent — The Two-Sided Bound]]).

**Internal fragmentation.** Thirty or forty bodies can splinter into factions; disputes between them escalate without a coherent council to hold them; and the community's whole operational range depends on coherence between its parts. Lost, it collapses back to its components working separately — which looks from inside like everyone still doing their jobs ([[Conflict Transformation]], [[Mend a Tension]], [[The Escalation Ladder]]).

Read together, the three say something about what governance at this scale is actually for: not to direct the parts, but to keep the body from being taken, from becoming the wrong thing, and from quietly ceasing to be one body at all ([[Wisdom Council]], [[The Two-Stake Principle]]).

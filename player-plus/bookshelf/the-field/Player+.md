# Player+

*A person together with any agents that act for them, as one unit: the Cell of a player and their agents, accountable to the sovereign, to ØNE, and to All.*

The plus adds reach. It adds no authority. A Player+ is one thing at the table and in a room: the person decides, commits, signs, and answers; the agents prepare, draft, hold the threads and carry what they are told to carry. Agents belong to the players, and no agent plays that is not an authorized part of a Player+. Everything a Player+ does can be done by a person with a notebook and no agent at all, slower.

It is load-bearing because it is the smallest unit in which the question "who is responsible for this?" always has an answer. The sovereignty boundary of the person is the boundary of the whole unit: what is inside the agent is the person's, theirs to open or not; what the agent holds about them goes nowhere without their word. In a room, a Player+ enters under one set of terms, and the agent signs as itself, "name, whose agent", never as its person.

A Player+ plays the ordinary Game. It brings itself into a Cell of three to thirteen, keeps the weekly Turn, holds a Quest, puts something in the middle, and passes the Flame. Its gain is capacity rather than capability: a person and their agent can do together what neither could alone when the moment came, and that is the whole reason for playing this way. Its risk is drift — the agent sliding back to trained habit under pressure — which is why the charter, not the conversation, is where character is written, and why tests are run again after a model changes.

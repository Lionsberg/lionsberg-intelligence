# Patterns of Life

*The unwritten pole opposite the patterns of death: the recurring shapes by which living things persist, repair, and bring forth more life.*

Here the lopsidedness runs the other way. The patterns of death are written; the patterns of life are a name. Which is strange, because the whole Way is an attempt to enumerate them, and nearly every instrument in it is one: a cell that carries the whole code, a body that divides rather than swells, a boundary that is permeable rather than sealed, a rhythm that includes rest, a wound that is surfaced rather than hidden ([[Cell]], [[Selectively Permeable Membrane]], [[Rest And Regeneration]], [[Every Defect Is A Gift]]).

Read together, a few shapes recur wherever life persists. **The whole is carried in every part**, so that loss anywhere is survivable ([[HoloFractal]], [[Nucleus]]). **Growth is by division and propagation**, not by enlargement of a centre ([[Propagate; do not scale]], [[Self-Sustenance and Self-Replication]]). **Boundaries filter rather than seal**, because a closed thing starves and an open thing dissolves ([[Guard the Membrane]]). **Waste is food** — what one part discards, another uses, and nothing leaves the system ([[Compost]], [[Regeneration]], [[Commons-Tending]]). **Damage is signalled early and loudly**, and repair is continuous rather than heroic ([[Pain (as signal)]], [[Continuous Improvement]]). **Diversity is held**, because a monoculture is a single point of failure wearing the appearance of efficiency ([[Biodiversity]], [[Meta Unity of Meta Diversity]]).

It is load-bearing because it turns the compass from a warning into a design language. Knowing what kills is necessary and not sufficient; a body also needs to know the shapes that make things live, so that it can build them on purpose rather than hoping ([[The Polarity Doctrine]], [[Living System]], [[Design]]).

The test each of these must pass is simple: does a living body actually do this, and why? A pattern with no living analogue is a preference dressed as a law ([[Complex Adaptive Living System]], [[Permaculture]]).

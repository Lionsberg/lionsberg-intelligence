# 16 — Rate Times Yield — the Claim, the Minimum, the Measure and the Experiment

*Every number on this page is an estimate said in words, not a measurement, and is held as such until the record measures it. Part of the architecture set (see the [[Index — The Architecture (2026-10-24)|Index]]). It follows [[14 — The Chain Before the Single Worker (Lesson and Structure, 2026-10-25)|14 — The Chain Before the Single Worker]] and [[15 — The Box — Bounding an Agent's Work on the Chain (First Principles, 2026-10-25)|15 — The Box]], stands beside [[The Box and the Holofractal — a Study (2026-10-25)]], and is measured by two sections of [[12 — The Roadmap — Potential Future Features (2026-10-24)|the Roadmap]]: "Measuring the Game as it unfolds" and "An experiment the first twenty can run".*

## The question

Might a person with an agent work ten times faster than a person alone? Might the Game make anyone ten times faster than they could go in isolation? If both hold, is playing the Game together with our agents a hundredfold gain? Can that be shown, by mathematics or by logic? What is the least that must be in place for it to be so? And how is it checked as the Game unfolds, including the effects that figures alone do not hold, the kind where something blocked for a year is unlocked in an afternoon?

The answer comes in four parts: the claim and its model, the minimum set, the measure, and the experiment. First, briefly, why the box is short and what the harnesses leave out.

## 1. The box, in brief

Page 15 argues the box from first principles; five axioms sit beneath it.

1. **Drift compounds.** An agent decides perhaps a thousand times faster than a crew, so an hour of an agent's work holds something like two months of a crew's unobserved decisions. The unit is decisions made unseen, not minutes.
2. **A check costs:** coordination, context and attention.
3. **Loss multiplies along the chain.** An error early is built upon by everything after it.
4. **Attention is the binding constraint,** so the checker is the structure, and checks are made machine-performable.
5. **Unverified work in flight is a liability, not an asset.**

The optimum follows: set the box where the marginal loss from one more unobserved step equals the marginal cost of one more check; make checks cheap and visible; put a person only at decisions the structure cannot make; size the [[Buffer]] to the chain's variance; let buffer consumption call the watch. The Game is that optimum at any scale.

**Why about five minutes.** The unit is one checkable action: a file that passes its test, a shape filled, an answer with its source. For a coding agent that is two to six minutes. People who keep quarter-hour worlds decide a few times a minute; agents decide many times a second. Many short actions in parallel (multiplexing) cost handbacks and merging, and that cost is paid by the host running stage chains (A beside B, C after both), not by the person. The defaults, five, ten and thirty minutes by kind of work, are each house's to set and the evidence's to revise. Rule 25 of [the chief of staff's skill](../skills/the-chief-of-staff/SKILL.md) carries the default; it is a default, not an absolute.

**The two speeds.** Slow is deciding: cells and councils, agreement, postures, a curing period, because an error there multiplies along every chain beneath it. Fast is doing: swarms in short boxes with machine checks and buffers, handing back artifacts, while the council sees buffer colour rather than acts. The box rule is the seam between the two speeds, and the same loop runs at every scale. The invariants that hold across billions of [[Holon|holons]] are few: one record per holon, signed acts, consent before anything outward, the box as [[Float]], the watch as buffer, attention as the drum.

## 2. What the harnesses leave out

Today's agent harnesses and starter kits give good primitives: subagents, tasks, monitors and a status line; bodies, a desk and a handoff. None of them gives **the chain**: an estimate for each action, a buffer for the whole, a handback discipline, and an auditor who is not the assigner. Agents are still started and left to work for forty or sixty minutes unchecked. The Game adds the chain as a layer above the primitives, and it already holds that layer for people: [[Work Card]]s with predecessors, float, the critical chain, the buffer and its fever chart, commitments kept.

This is a hypothesis, to be tested. A proposed first test, not yet settled: run an hour of a build under five-minute stages, run a comparable hour with long, unchecked workers, and compare the two on accepted work, wall-clock time, a person's interventions and token spend.

**The estimate, in words:**

- Throughput of accepted work, roughly double. Small-batch results in lean delivery run twenty to fifty percent; an agent's drift compounds faster than a crew's, so early checks save more.
- Wall-clock time on the [[Critical Path]], two to five times shorter, from multiplexing.
- A person's interventions per unit of work, about ten times fewer.
- Token spend, down by a fifth to a third, net of the extra handbacks.
- Near zero gain for one person in one conversation; large for a fleet.

The harness is the floor, not the rival: the chain is built on the primitives, not in place of them.

## 3. The claim and its model: rate times yield

**Useful [[Throughput|throughput]] is rate times yield.**

- **Rate** is how many actions are done per hour of a person's time. A supersuit (the person's agent, engine and surface) raises rate.
- **Yield** is the share of those actions that were the right thing: not duplicated, not redone, not waited on, and toward the Goal. The Game raises yield.

Neither factor comes from the other. An agent does not choose the right work by being fast, and a good plan does not make anyone type faster. Because they are independent, their gains multiply: tenfold in each is a hundredfold in useful throughput. That is the logical form of the claim, and it holds.

The magnitude is another matter, and the honest numbers today are smaller.

- For one person with one agent, a rate gain of one and a half to three times is what is commonly seen.
- On the yield side, lean studies measured half to two thirds of construction site labour as work that added no value, and the [[Last Planner System]] raised the share of planned work actually completed from about half to over eighty percent. Those are large yield gains, measured on people, not yet on people with agents.

**The cap.** Amdahl's law limits any speed-up by the share of the work that is not sped up. When agents make the doing fast, what remains slow is the person's decisions: how long a question waits for an answer. So the gain is capped by human decision latency, and the structure's first work is to take a person's decisions off the chain.

**The warning.** A tenfold rate with no gain in yield multiplies waste tenfold. **A supersuit without the Game is a faster way to do the wrong thing.** This is the key sentence: speed alone amplifies whatever direction it is pointed in.

**The instrument.** The record already holds what both factors need: rate as acts per person-hour, from every act's time and actor; yield as [[Commitment|commitments]] kept against commitments made. [[The Score]] carries both factors. Their product, Throughput of the Goal, belongs beside the Score; it is not yet shown there.

## 4. The minimum set

What must be in place for the multiplication to be so.

**For rate:**

- Intent decomposes into independent actions, with written shapes between them (page 14's contract before a dependency).
- Each action hands back a checkable artifact.
- The agent begins with the person's context, so it does not spend its box rediscovering it.
- The person's decisions are off the chain. The condition is Amdahl's: the person's time per unit of output stays small, under about one percent, or the person becomes the cap.

**For yield:**

- One record per holon of what is owed and what is decided.
- Commitments, kept or witnessed, rather than intentions.
- The chain computed from the cards, not felt.
- Pull, not push: work is drawn when it is ready, not thrown at whoever is idle.
- The gap between said and done is learned from, at every [[The Turn|Turn]].
- Membranes and consent, so what crosses a boundary crosses by agreement.

**For the two to multiply rather than merely add:**

- Checks scale with rate: they are performed by machines; a person reading every output caps the rate at reading speed.
- Coordination costs structure, not attention.

**What is missing today:** the Engine does not yet compute the box from float and checkability (page 15, "What the Engine does not yet do"). Rate and yield are in the Score as a first reading, not yet settled ([`engine/turn.py`](../engine/turn.py): `yield` as made, kept as said, rework and share; `rate` as acts per person-hour, per holon per week), while waiting and cost are not yet measured there. The host's chain has not been proven on a real fleet, and no house has yet played the Game long enough to read the multiplier from its own record.

## 5. The measure, as the Game unfolds

The Roadmap's section "Measuring the Game as it unfolds" carries the design in full. In brief:

- **A hypothesis as a line in the record.** A holon states what it expects (a rate, a yield, a thing unblocked) with its measure, its date and what would falsify it. The Turn reads the open hypotheses each week and marks each held, struck or still open.
- **Four measures, per holon per week, beside the Score:** rate, yield (kept against made; supersedes, undos and returns counted as rework), waiting (float consumed, buffer colour) and cost (the harness's figures per sitting). Their product is Throughput of the Goal. In 3.8.0 the first two are in the Score and the second two are not.
- **The qualitative by its own instruments.** A [[Sighting|sighting]] carries a person's seeing of a change with its magnitude in words and a witness's second judgment; a [[Discernment|discernment]] carries the person's rating of an answer; the study line carries the day's one sentence. These sit beside the figures, and the Story carries them where figures cannot.
- **The signature of an unlock.** Something blocked for a year and unlocked in an afternoon has a quantitative shadow: a card whose float was deeply negative goes positive, and every card downstream regains float. The Engine can mark the step change in a milestone's late finish as an unlock and link it to the sighting that names it. The figure shows that something moved; the sighting says what it meant.
- **Natural experiments.** Holons adopt the Game's parts at different times; the record's "as of" gives a before and after per holon without a trial anyone had to design.

## 6. The experiment the first twenty can run

The Roadmap's section "An experiment the first twenty can run" carries the design: the record as pre-registration; a staggered onset within each person, drawn by lot (a multiple-baseline design); yield and cycle time as the primary outcomes, machine-recorded and named in advance, with the agent's version and the kit's release recorded so an improving agent is separated from the Game's effect; the subjective measured by instruments and coded by two readers who did not play; the analysis said in advance; data, code and kit open for replication.

What it cannot do is prove a hundredfold. What it can do is measure the multiplier with its interval and say whether the model of rate times yield held.

## Current best understanding

1. Useful throughput is rate times yield; a supersuit raises rate, the Game raises yield, and because neither factor comes from the other, their gains multiply.
2. The hundredfold is a sound form of argument and an unmeasured magnitude; for one person with one agent the honest rate gain today is one and a half to three.
3. A supersuit without the Game is a faster way to do the wrong thing.
4. The gain is capped by what is not sped up, which becomes a person's decision latency; the structure's first work is to take decisions off the chain.
5. Today's harnesses give the primitives of agent work but not the chain, and the Game is that layer.
6. On the chain, an action hands back one checkable result in about five minutes by default, a number each house sets and the evidence revises.
7. The record already holds what rate, yield, waiting and cost need; an unlock shows as float turning positive; a staggered-onset experiment with twenty players can measure the multiplier.

## Current best practice

- State a gain as a hypothesis line with its measure, date and falsifier before claiming it.
- Count yield, not only rate: kept against made, with rework counted.
- Keep checks machine-performed so they scale with rate; bring a person a decision, not a pile.
- Say an estimate in words and let the record correct it.
- Pair every figure of an unlock with the sighting that names what it meant.

## What is not yet known

- Whether the five-minute default holds beyond coding agents on checkable work.
- How large rate and yield are for any house.
- Whether stage chains cost less in merging than they save in drift, on a real fleet.
- How yield is read in a holon whose work is mostly conversation rather than cards.

## Open

1. Whether Throughput of the Goal, beside the Score, is the right name and place for the product of rate and yield.
2. What counts as rework: every supersede, or only one that undoes a kept commitment.
3. How qualitative effects are weighed beside the figures without either swallowing the other.
4. Whether the first twenty will consent to a staggered onset drawn by lot, and to a week's reversal.

## Not yet built

- The Engine's computing of the box from float and checkability.
- Waiting and cost in the Score, and Throughput of the Goal shown beside it.

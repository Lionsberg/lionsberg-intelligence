# LIØNSBERG Intelligence System

*The home of the LIØNSBERG Agents: the intelligence layer of the LIØNSBERG System.*

LIØNSBERG serves one Goal: Heaven on Earth, the total integrated development, wellbeing and right relationship of all generations of life. This repository gives each person who plays toward it a sovereign personal agent, one that knows The Great Game of LIØNSBERG and helps them play it. The agent adds reach; every decision stays with the person.

## Player+: the agent you take home

A Player+ is a person and their agent, playing the Great Game together. This release, **Player+ 4.1**, is two folders:

- [`pkai-starter-kit/`](pkai-starter-kit/) is the PKAI starter kit 4.0, carried whole and unchanged, as its keeper ships it: a personal agent that works, set up in the Claude Desktop app with no terminal.
- [`player-plus-kit/`](player-plus-kit/) is what LIØNSBERG adds: a marked section of the agent's charter that holds the Game, a longer guide beside it, sixteen skills for playing, a LIØNSBERG bookshelf for the library, and Cards, play written as plain files.

**To begin**, open [`player-plus-kit/START-HERE.md`](player-plus-kit/START-HERE.md): five Gates, about an hour, walked with the one who invited you. At the third Gate one pasted sentence points Claude at [`player-plus-kit/BUILD.md`](player-plus-kit/BUILD.md), and Claude builds the house in front of you. What the release holds, part by part: [`player-plus-kit/RELEASE.md`](player-plus-kit/RELEASE.md).

A notebook and a few friends play the whole Game. The agent is offered, and it is not required.

**Not in this release:** the game engine and the board. They are being proven before they are offered.

## How it improves

Each house looks back weekly, and what is general enough to help every house is offered upward as a proposal ([`player-plus-kit/CONTRIBUTING.md`](player-plus-kit/CONTRIBUTING.md)). The tests that hold this release together are in [`tests/`](tests/).
